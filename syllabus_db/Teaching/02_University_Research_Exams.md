# University / Research Exams in India — Comprehensive Study Guide

India runs a vast, multi-tier examination ecosystem covering teaching/research eligibility (NET-family), scientific fellowships (CSIR/ICMR/DBT), engineering (GATE/JEE), management (CAT/XAT/MAT), agriculture (ICAR), central university admissions (CUET) and more. The guide below collates purpose, eligibility, exam pattern, detailed syllabus and best resources for each.

---

## 1. UGC NET (National Eligibility Test)

### Purpose
Determines eligibility for **Assistant Professorship** and **Junior Research Fellowship (JRF)** in Indian universities/colleges, and PhD admission (UGC NET June 2024 onwards added a "PhD only" category). Conducted by **NTA** on behalf of **UGC** in CBT mode twice a year (June, December). Listed among India's top 10 toughest exams (JRF success ≈ 0.7%, AP ≈ 6–7%).

### Eligibility
- Master's degree or equivalent with **55% (General/EWS), 50% (SC/ST/OBC-NCL/PwD/Transgender)**, final-year PG students also eligible.
- No age limit for Assistant Professor; JRF upper age = **30 years** (relaxation for reserved categories).

### Exam Pattern
| Paper | Qs | Marks | Duration | Type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Paper I (General, common)** | 50 | 100 (2 marks each) | 1 hour | MCQ |
| **Paper II (Subject-specific)** | 100 | 200 (2 marks each) | 2 hours | MCQ |
| **Total** | **150** | **300** | **3 hours** | No negative marking |

### Detailed Syllabus — Paper I (10 Units)
1. **Teaching Aptitude** — Teaching nature, objectives, characteristics, basic requirements; factors affecting teaching; methods (lecture, discussion, seminar, workshop, microteaching, team teaching); evaluation (formative, summative, diagnostic, criterion-referenced); innovative practices (ICT, blended, flipped classroom); continuous & comprehensive evaluation.
2. **Research Aptitude** — Research definition, types (basic/applied/qualitative/quantitative/action), methods, hypothesis, sampling, tools; thesis/article writing; application of ICT in research; research ethics, plagiarism.
3. **Reading Comprehension** — A passage with questions testing literal, inferential and critical comprehension.
4. **Communication** — Types (verbal/non-verbal, formal/informal); communication models (Shannon-Weaver, Schramm, Berlo); barriers; classroom communication; mass media & society; effective communication.
5. **Mathematical Reasoning** — Number series, letter series, codes, relationships; syllogism; Venn diagrams; analogy; classification; statement-conclusion; data sufficiency.
6. **Logical Reasoning** — Premises, conclusions, fallacies; Indian logic (Nyaya, Mimamsa, Buddhist, Jain); structure of Pramanas (Pratyaksha, Anumana, Shabda, Upamana, Arthapatti, Anupalabdhi).
7. **Data Interpretation** — Tables, bar/line/pie charts, graphs, caselets, data sufficiency.
8. **ICT (Information & Communication Technology)** — Digital initiatives (DIKSHA, SWAYAM, MOOCs, e-Pathshala, SWAYAM Prabha, NPTEL, virtual labs); basics of computers, internet, e-governance; cyber security.
9. **People & Environment** — Natural resources, ecosystems, biodiversity, environmental pollution, climate change, sustainable development, environmental policies (National Environmental Policy, Environment Protection Act, Wildlife Protection Act, Forest Conservation Act).
10. **Higher Education System** — Ancient (Takshashila, Nalanda, Vikramashila) to modern (Wood's Despatch 1854, Hunter Commission 1882, Radhakrishnan Commission 1948, Kothari Commission 1964-66, NPE 1986/1992, NEP 2020); institutions (UGC, AICTE, NAAC, NBA, AIU, NUEPA); governance.
11. **Polity, Governance & Administration** — Constitution (preamble, fundamental rights/duties, DPSP, federalism, parliamentary system); constitutional amendments; Panchayati Raj, Municipalities; RTI Act 2005; right to education; consumer awareness.

### 83 Subject-specific Papers (Paper II)
Major subjects include: **Education, Teaching, Special Education, Physical Education, Yoga, Law, Commerce, Management, Economics, Political Science, History, Philosophy, Sociology, Psychology, Anthropology, Social Work, Public Administration, Home Science, Geography, English, Hindi, Sanskrit, Urdu, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi, Assamese, Manipuri, Arabic, Persian, Linguistics, Library & Information Science, Mass Communication & Journalism, Computer Science & Applications, Electronic Science, Environmental Sciences, Forensic Science, Geography, Geology, Earth/Atmospheric/Ocean/Planetary Sciences, Home Science, Human Rights, Labour Welfare, Law, Linguistics, Music, Dance, Public Administration, Performing Arts, Visual Arts, Archaeology, Museology, Buddhist/Jaina/Gandhian Studies, Tourism Administration, Women Studies, Comparative Literature, Sanskrit Traditional Subjects, Defence & Strategic Studies, Physical Education, Yoga, Indian Knowledge System, Bodo, Dogri, Kashmiri, Konkani, Maithili, Nepali, Odia, Santhali, Sindhi, Tribal & Regional Languages, etc.**

### Best Books & Resources
- **Paper I**: NTA UGC NET/SET/SLET Paper 1 by KVS Madaan (Pearson/Standard Exam Guide); Trueman's UGC NET Paper 1; Disha's UGC NET Paper 1.
- **Paper II**: Subject-specific UGC NET guides (Standard Exam Guide, Trueman's, Disha, Pearson).
- Online: NTA Abhyas app, NTA MOOC, SWAYAM, Testbook, Gradeup, Unacademy.

### Official Link
- **ugcnet.nta.nic.in** (conducting body: NTA)
- **ugc.ac.in** (UGC)

## 2. CSIR NET (Council of Scientific & Industrial Research NET)

### Purpose
Awards **JRF and Lectureship (LS)** in five Science streams: **Life Sciences, Chemical Sciences, Earth, Atmospheric, Ocean & Planetary Sciences, Mathematical Sciences, Physical Sciences**. Conducted twice a year in CBT mode (June, December) jointly by CSIR-HRDG and NTA.

### Eligibility
- M.Sc./Integrated B.Sc.-M.Sc./B.E./B.Tech./B.Pharma/MCA or equivalent with **55% (General/EWS/OBC), 50% (SC/ST/PwD/female)**.
- **JRF upper age = 28 years** (relaxation as per Govt. of India norms); **No age limit for LS**.

### Exam Pattern
| Stream | Part A (Gen Aptitude) | Part B (Subject) | Part C (Higher) | Total | Marks | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Life Sciences** | 20 Qs | 50 Qs | 75 Qs (attempt 35) | 145 | 200 | 3 hrs |
| **Chemical/Physical/Mathematical/Earth Sciences** | 20 Qs | 40 Qs | 60 Qs | 200 | 200 | 3 hrs |

- **Part A** (common): General aptitude, logical reasoning, graphical analysis, numerical ability, GK.
- **Part B**: Subject-related MCQs (standard level).
- **Part C**: Higher-order analytical/application-based MCQs.
- Negative marking: -0.25 for Part A, -0.5 for Parts B & C of LS; -1 for wrong answers in other streams.

### Detailed Syllabus (Life Sciences — 14 units)
1. Molecules and their Interaction Relevant to Biology
2. Cellular Organization
3. Fundamental Processes
4. Cell Communication and Cell Signalling
5. Developmental Biology
6. System Physiology — Plant
7. System Physiology — Animal
8. Inheritance Biology
9. Diversity of Life Forms
10. Ecological Principles
11. Evolution and Behaviour
12. Applied Biology
13. Methods in Biology
14. Quantitative Biology & Bioinformatics

(Other streams: Chemical — organic/inorganic/physical chemistry; Physical — classical, quantum, statistical mechanics, electromagnetism, optics, condensed matter, nuclear, particle, atomic, molecular; Mathematical — algebra, analysis, geometry, topology, ODE/PDE, numerical methods, mechanics, fluids, mathematical physics, statistics; Earth Sciences — geology, geophysics, meteorology, oceanography, planetary sciences.)

### Best Books
- **Pathfinder Academy** CSIR NET Life Sciences/Chemical Sciences (most popular).
- **Standard Exam Guide** CSIR NET Solved Papers.
- **MTG** CSIR NET.
- Trueman's CSIR NET.

### Official Link
- **csirhrdg.res.in** (CSIR-HRDG)
- **csirnet.nta.nic.in** (NTA portal)

## 3. ICMR JRF (Indian Council of Medical Research JRF)

### Purpose
Awards **JRF** to young researchers in **biomedical/health sciences** for pursuing PhD, conducted by **PGIMER, Chandigarh** on behalf of ICMR once a year.

### Eligibility
- M.Sc./M.A. or equivalent in **Life/Biological Sciences / Biotechnology / Biochemistry / Microbiology / Pharmacology / Zoology / Botany / Chemistry / Nursing / Nutrition / Genetics / Medical Lab Tech** with **55% (50% SC/ST)**.
- **Upper age = 28 years** (relaxation for SC/ST/PwD/female).

### Exam Pattern
- **Single paper, CBT, 2 hours (120 min)**.
- **150 MCQs (4 marks each = 600 total), 25% negative marking (-1 per wrong)** → max **200 effective marks** (after penalty).
- Sections (life-science oriented): **Aptitude (10 Qs), Biochemistry (15), Microbiology (15), Pharmacology (15), Physiology (15), plus questions from Cell Biology, Molecular Biology, Immunology, Genetics, Biotechnology, Biostatistics, Nutrition, Health Research Methodology.**

### Detailed Syllabus
- **General Aptitude**: Reasoning, numerical, English comprehension, data interpretation.
- **Biochemistry**: Structure & function of biomolecules, enzymes, metabolism (carbohydrate, protein, lipid, nucleic acid), clinical biochemistry.
- **Microbiology**: General, medical, applied; virology, mycology, parasitology, immunology.
- **Pharmacology**: General pharmacology, drug action, ANS, CNS, autacoids, chemotherapy, toxicology.
- **Physiology**: Cell, nerve-muscle, blood, cardiovascular, respiratory, renal, GI, endocrine, reproduction, neurophysiology.
- **Biostatistics & Research Methodology**: Sampling, probability, tests of significance, study designs, ethics, biostatistical software.

### Best Books
- **ICMR JRF Life Sciences** — Standard Exam Guide, Pathfinder, MTG.
- **Anatomy & Physiology** — Tortora, Guyton.
- **Pharmacology** — KD Tripathi, Katzung.
- **Microbiology** — Ananthanarayan, Prescott.

### Official Link
- **icmr.gov.in**
- **pgimer.edu.in** (exam portal)

## 4. ICAR AIEEA (All India Entrance Examination for Admission)

### Purpose
Admission to **UG, PG and PhD (JRF/SRF)** programmes in agriculture and allied sciences at **State Agricultural Universities, ICAR deemed-to-be universities, and other AUs** (75+ institutes). Conducted by **NTA**.

### Eligibility
- **UG (AIEEA-UG)**: 12th with PCB/PCM/PCMB/Agri from recognised board; age **16 years min**.
- **PG (AIEEA-PG)**: Bachelor's in agriculture/related field; age **19 years min**.
- **JRF/SRF (AICE-JRF/SRF)**: Master's in agriculture/related field; age limit varies.

### Exam Pattern
| Level | Qs | Marks | Duration | Negative Marking |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **UG** | 150 (50 per subject × 3) | 600 (4 marks each) | 2.5 hrs | -1 per wrong |
| **PG** | 120 | 480 (4 marks each) | 2 hrs | -1 per wrong |
| **JRF/SRF (PhD)** | 150 | 600 | 2 hrs | -1 per wrong |

### Detailed Syllabus (UG)
- **Stream A (PCB)**: Physics (Mechanics, Optics, Modern Physics, Thermodynamics, Electricity & Magnetism), Chemistry (Physical, Inorganic, Organic), Biology (Botany + Zoology, Ecology, Genetics, Human Physiology, Plant Physiology, Cell Biology, Diversity, Evolution).
- **Stream B (PCM)**: Physics + Chemistry + **Mathematics** (Algebra, Trigonometry, Coordinate Geometry, Calculus, Statistics & Probability, Vectors, Linear Programming).
- **Stream C (Agri)**: Agriculture Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics; Crop Production, Horticulture, Animal Husbandry, Dairy, Fisheries, Forestry, Sericulture, Agri-Engineering.
- **PG**: Subject-specific — Agronomy, Plant Breeding & Genetics, Soil Science, Plant Pathology, Entomology, Horticulture, Veterinary, Dairy Science, Fisheries, Agri-Engineering, Home Science, etc.

### Best Books
- **ICAR AIEEA (UG)**: Standard Exam Guide, Standard Exam GuideChapterwise, Standard General Knowledge CompendiumGeneral Science.
- **ICAR AIEEA (PG)**: Subject-specific — Jain Brothers, Kalyani, Mangal Deep.

## 5. DBT JRF (Department of Biotechnology JRF)

### Purpose
Awards **JRF** to candidates for pursuing **PhD in Biotechnology and related areas** in Indian institutions. Conducted by **DBT, Ministry of Science & Technology** (now under **BET — Biotechnology Eligibility Test**, an umbrella renaming).

### Eligibility
- M.Sc./M.Tech./B.Tech. (Biotechnology/Bioinformatics/Life Sciences/related) with **60% (Gen/EWS/OBC), 55% (SC/ST/PwD)**.
- **Upper age = 28 years**.

### Exam Pattern
- **CBT, 3 hours, 200 Qs (100 MCQ + 100 MSQ)**.
- **Multiple-Select Questions (MSQ)**: marks only if all correct options chosen, partial credit may apply.
- **Total 300 marks**, -0.5 negative for MCQs (some patterns vary).

### Detailed Syllabus (Biotechnology)
1. **Biochemistry & Biophysics**: Biomolecules, enzymology, metabolism, bioenergetics, spectroscopy.
2. **Cell & Molecular Biology**: Cell structure, organelles, replication, transcription, translation, regulation, signalling, cancer biology.
3. **Genetics & Genomics**: Mendelian, molecular genetics, population genetics, genome editing (CRISPR), NGS.
4. **Microbiology & Immunology**: Bacteriology, virology, mycology, parasitology, innate & adaptive immunity, MHC, antibodies, complement, hypersensitivity, vaccines.
5. **Recombinant DNA Technology & Genetic Engineering**: Cloning vectors, restriction enzymes, PCR, blotting, sequencing, expression systems, transgenic animals & plants.
6. **Plant & Animal Biotechnology**: Tissue culture, somatic embryogenesis, hybridoma technology, stem cells, gene therapy.
7. **Bioinformatics & Computational Biology**: Sequence alignment (BLAST, Clustal), phylogenetics, protein structure prediction, molecular docking, databases (NCBI, PDB, UniProt).
8. **Bioprocess Engineering & Industrial Biotechnology**: Fermenter design, upstream/downstream processing, microbial products, biofuels.
9. **Environmental Biotechnology**: Bioremediation, phytoremediation, biodegradation, GMOs in environment.
10. **Bioethics, Biosafety & IPR**: GMO regulations, ethical issues, biosafety levels, patents, IP.
11. **Research Methodology & Quantitative Methods**: Statistics, experimental design, biostatistics, ethics in research.

### Best Books
- **DBT-JRF BET** — Standard Exam Guide, Pathfinder, Dreamtech.
- **Molecular Biology** — Lodish, Alberts, Watson.
- **Immunology** — Janeway, Kuby.
- **Recombinant DNA** — Primrose & Twyman, Brown.

### Official Link
- **dbtindia.gov.in**
- **rcb.res.in** (Regional Centre for Biotechnology, exam conducting)

## 6. GATE (Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering)

### Purpose
Admission to **M.E./M.Tech./M.Arch./PhD** with MoE scholarship; PSU recruitment; foreign university admission. Conducted jointly by **IISc & 7 IITs** on behalf of **NCB-GATE, Dept. of Higher Education, MoE**.

### Eligibility
B.E./B.Tech./B.Arch./B.Planning (3–4 yr after 10+2), or Master's in Science/Math/Stats/CS/IT, prefinal & final year UG/PG students, or equivalent AMIE/AMIETE. **No age limit.**

### Exam Pattern
- **CBT, 3 hours, 65 Qs, 100 marks**.
- **General Aptitude (GA)**: **15 Qs (5 tryout + 10 scored) = 25 marks**.
- **Engineering Mathematics**: 13-15 Qs = 25–30 marks (not all papers).
- **Core Subject**: 55-70 Qs = 70-85 marks.
- **Question types**: MCQ (single correct), MSQ (one or more correct), NAT (numerical).
- **Negative marking** for MCQs only: -1/3 for 1-mark, -2/3 for 2-mark; no negative for MSQ/NAT.

### 30 Subjects
**AE** Aerospace, **AG** Agricultural, **AR** Architecture & Planning, **BM** Biomedical, **BT** Biotechnology, **CE** Civil, **CH** Chemical, **CY** Chemistry, **CS** Computer Science & IT, **DA** Data Science & AI (new), **EC** Electronics & Communication, **EE** Electrical, **ES** Environmental, **EY** Ecology & Evolution, **GE** Geomatics (new), **GG** Geology & Geophysics, **IN** Instrumentation, **MA** Mathematics, **ME** Mechanical, **MN** Mining, **MT** Metallurgical, **NM** Naval Architecture & Marine (new), **PE** Petroleum, **PH** Physics, **PI** Production & Industrial, **ST** Statistics, **TF** Textile & Fibre, **XE** Engineering Sciences (multi-section), **XL** Life Sciences (multi-section), **XH** Humanities & Social Sciences (multi-section).

### Detailed Syllabus Highlights (Core Subjects)
- **CS**: Programming (C, C++, Java, Python), Data Structures, Algorithms, OS, DBMS, CN, Compilers, TOC, Discrete Maths, Digital Logic, Computer Architecture, Software Engineering, Web Tech, ML, AI, Cloud, Cybersecurity.
- **EE**: Electric Circuits, EM Fields, Signals & Systems, Electrical Machines, Power Systems, Power Electronics, Control Systems, Measurements, Analog & Digital Electronics, Power System Protection.
- **ME**: Mechanics (Statics, Dynamics), Strength of Materials, Theory of Machines, Vibrations, Design, Fluid Mechanics, Thermodynamics, Heat Transfer, Manufacturing, Industrial Engineering, Engineering Materials.

- **EC**: Networks, Signals & Systems, Electronic Devices & Circuits, Analog & Digital Circuits, Control Systems, Communication (Analog, Digital), EM Theory, VLSI, Microprocessors, Digital Signal Processing.

## 7. CAT (Common Admission Test)

### Purpose
Admission to **MBA/PGDM** at IIMs and 1000+ B-schools (IITs, IISc, MDI, SPJIMR, IMT, XIM, TAPMI, BIMTECH etc.). Conducted by one of the **IIMs** on rotation.

### Eligibility
Bachelor's degree with **50% (45% for SC/ST/PwD)** or equivalent CGPA. Final year students eligible.

### Exam Pattern
- **CBT, 2 hours total, 40 min per section**, 3 sections in fixed order (VARC → DILR → QA). No switching.
- **No negative marking for TITA (Type-In-The-Answer)**; -1 for wrong MCQ; +3 for correct.
- **Total 68 Qs, 204 marks** (CAT 2024-25).
- **VARC (24 Qs)**: 8 Verbal Ability (para summary, odd-one-out, para completion) + 4 RC passages × 4 Qs.
- **DILR (22 Qs)**: 5 sets (4-5 Qs each) of caselets, games, tournaments, Venn, bar/line graphs, arrangements, puzzles.
- **QA (22 Qs)**: Arithmetic, Algebra, Geometry & Mensuration, Number System, Modern Math (Probability, Set Theory, Logs, Matrices).

### Detailed Syllabus
- **VARC**: Reading Comprehension (Factual, Literary, Narrative, Critical), Para Jumbles, Summary, Vocabulary, Grammar, Fill-in-the-blanks.
- **DILR**: Data Tables, Bar/Pie/Line/Caselet, Venn, Network, Binary Logic, Games & Tournaments, Ordering, Quant-based DI, Set Theory.
- **QA**: Arithmetic (Ratios, Percentages, Profit-Loss, SI/CI, TSD, Work, Mixtures, Averages), Algebra (Linear/Quadratic, Inequalities, Functions, Log, Mod, Sequences, Binomial, Matrices), Geometry/Mensuration, Number System, Modern Math (P&C, Probability, Set Theory).

### Best Books
- Arun Sharma (McGraw-Hill) — Quantitative Aptitude, DILR, VARC.
- Nishit Sinha — Logical Reasoning & DI.
- Sarvesh K. Verma — Quantitative Aptitude.
- Word Power Made Easy — Norman Lewis.
- 10 Year Solved Papers — Disha/Standard Exam Guide.

### Official Link
- **iimcat.ac.in**

## 8. XAT (Xavier Aptitude Test)

### Eligibility
Bachelor's degree (final year eligible). No minimum %.

### Pattern
- **CBT, 3 hours 10 minutes, ~100 Qs (varies)**, 4 sections:
  - **Part 1**: Verbal & Logical Ability (VA-LR) — 26 Qs
  - **Part 1**: Decision Making (DM) — 21 Qs
  - **Part 1**: Quantitative Ability & Data Interpretation (QA-DI) — 28 Qs
  - **Part 2**: General Knowledge (GK) — 25 Qs (1 mark each, no negative)
- **+1 for correct, -0.25 for wrong MCQ; 0 for TITA.**
- **Essay Writing** (250-word, 8 minutes) for shortlist.

### Detailed Syllabus
- **VALR**: RC, Critical Reasoning, Para Completion, Vocabulary, Para Jumbles, Analogies, Logic-based questions, Poetry, Grammar.
- **DM**: Business scenarios, ethics, financial decisions, caselets, situational judgement.
- **QA-DI**: Arithmetic, Algebra, Geometry, Number System, Modern Math; Data Tables, Caselets, Venn, Games, Bar/Line charts.
- **GK**: Static GK + Current Affairs (Business, Economics, Politics, Sports, Awards, Books, Authors, History, Geography, Science).

### Best Books
- XLRI Previous Year Papers (Standard Exam Guide/Disha).
- Arun Sharma QA/DILR.
- XAT Study Guide by Chandresh Agrawal.

### Official Link
- **xatonline.in**

## 10. JEE Main, JEE Advanced, NEET, CUET

### CUET (Common University Entrance Test)
**Purpose**: UG/PG admission in **45+ Central Universities** (BHU, DU, JNU, AMU, JMI, BBAU, Allahabad, Hyderabad, Pondicherry, etc.), Deemed, State, Private universities. Part of NEP 2020. Conducted by **NTA** in CBT/IBT mode.

**Eligibility (UG)**: Class 12 pass; no age limit (per latest bulletin).

**Pattern (CUET-UG)**:
| Section | Content | Qs | Time | Marks | Negative |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1A** | 13 Languages (English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali, Assamese, Punjabi, Odia, Urdu) | 50 | 60 min (per language) | 250 | -1 for wrong |
| **1B** | 20 Other Languages | 50 | 60 min | 250 | -1 for wrong |
| **2** | 27 Domain-Specific Subjects (STEM, Commerce, Humanities, Arts, Sports) | 50 per subject | 60 min per subject | 250 per subject | -1 for wrong |
| **3** | General Test (GK, Current Affairs, Mental Ability, Numerical, Quantitative, Logical, Analytical Reasoning) | 50 | 60 min | 250 | -1 for wrong |

- Max: **2 languages + 6 domain subjects (or 3 + 5) + General Test**.
- **CUET-PG**: 72+ test papers; 100 Qs, 90 min, MCQ.

**Detailed Syllabus**:
- **Languages**: Reading Comprehension (Factual/Literary/Narrative passages, central theme, word meaning), Literary Aptitude, Vocabulary (synonyms, antonyms), Verbal Ability, Grammar, Rearranging, Vocabulary Writing.
- **Domain**: As per **latest NCERT syllabus** of Class 12 (Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Math, History, Geography, Polity, Economics, Sociology, Psychology, etc.).
- **General Test**: GK & Current Affairs, General Mental Ability, Numerical Ability, Quantitative Reasoning (Arithmetic, Algebra, Geometry, Mensuration, Statistics, Probability), Logical & Analytical Reasoning.

**Best Books**: NCERT Class 11 & 12 textbooks (primary source), Standard Exam GuideCUET UG, Oswaal CUET, Disha CUET.

**Official Link**: **cuet.samarth.ac.in**, **cuet.nta.nic.in**

**Pattern (Paper 1 – B.E./B.Tech)**:

| C | Mathematics | 25 (20 MCQ + 5 NV) | 100 |

**Paper 2 (B.Arch)**: Mathematics + Aptitude + Drawing.
**Paper 2B (B.Planning)**: Mathematics + Aptitude + Planning.

**Syllabus** (PCM as per **reduced NCERT Class 11 & 12 syllabus** — matches NCERT):
- **Physics**: Units, Kinematics, Laws of Motion, Work/Energy/Power, Rotation, Gravitation, Solids/Fluids, Thermodynamics, Kinetics, Oscillations/Waves, Electrostatics, Current Electricity, Magnetic Effects, EM Induction, Alternating Current, EM Waves, Optics, Dual Nature, Atoms/Nuclei, Electronic Devices, Communication Systems.

### JEE Advanced
**Eligibility**: Top 2,50,000 JEE Main rankers.

**Pattern (2 papers, 3 hours each, MCQ + Numerical)**:
- **Paper 1**: 60 Qs (total varies), **180-306 marks**.
- **Paper 2**: 60 Qs.
- **Total 306 marks** (subject to change).
- Question types: Single correct, multiple correct, numerical, integer.
- **Marking**: variable per question, partial marks possible, no overall negative.

**Syllabus**: Same as JEE Main (PCM) but with **higher depth and application**.

**Best Books**: Pathfinder (Standard Exam Guide), Cengage, Wiley, ML Khanna (Maths), Irodov (Physics — optional), OP Tandon, Morrison Boyd (Chemistry).

**Official Link**: **jeeadv.ac.in** (rotates among IITs)

### NEET (National Eligibility cum Entrance Test – UG)
**Purpose**: Admission to **MBBS, BDS, AYUSH (BAMS, BHMS, BUMS, BNYS), Nursing, Veterinary** in India. Single entrance since 2020. Conducted by **NTA**.

**Eligibility**: Class 12 (PCB + English) with **50% (Gen/EWS), 40% (SC/ST/OBC), 45% (PwD)**; age **17+**.

**Pattern**:
- **Offline (pen & paper)**, 3 hours 20 min, **200 Qs (attempt 180)**, **720 marks**, **+4 for correct, -1 for wrong**.
- 4 marks per correct: Physics (180), Chemistry (180), Biology-Botany (180), Biology-Zoology (180).

**Syllabus**: NCERT-based Class 11 & 12 (Physics, Chemistry, Biology — Botany & Zoology).

**Best Books**: NCERT 11 & 12, Trueman's Biology, MTG NEET, DC Pandey, Pradeep, OP Tandon, MS Chauhan (Organic).

**Official Link**: **neet.nta.nic.in**

## 11. IGNOU B.Ed Entrance

### Purpose
Admission to **B.Ed programme** of **IGNOU (Indira Gandhi National Open University)** through **Open and Distance Learning (ODL)** mode. Conducted by **NTA** in CBT.

### Eligibility
- **Graduation/Bachelor's degree with 50% (45% SC/ST/OBC/PwD)** from a recognised university.
- Also teachers in service under Govt./NGO with 2 years experience may apply.
- **Age**: No upper limit, but minimum as per NCTE norms (19 years).

### Exam Pattern
- **CBT, 2 hours, 100 MCQs, 100 marks**.
- Sections:
  1. **General English Comprehension** — 10 Qs
  2. **Logical & Analytical Reasoning** — 10 Qs
  3. **Educational Aptitude & Teaching** — 25 Qs
  4. **General Awareness / GK** — 15 Qs
  5. **Computer Knowledge / ICT** — 10 Qs
  6. **Optional Subject (any one)** — 30 Qs (choose from English, Hindi, Sanskrit, Mathematics, Science, Social Studies, Commerce, Economics, Polity, History, Geography, etc.)

### Detailed Syllabus
- **General English**: Reading comprehension, vocabulary, grammar, sentence correction, antonyms/synonyms.
- **Reasoning**: Series, analogy, coding-decoding, blood relations, syllogism, Venn, statement-conclusion.
- **Educational Aptitude**: Teaching-learning process, methods, evaluation, classroom management, inclusive education, NCF 2005, NEP 2020, assessment.
- **GK**: Current affairs, Indian polity, history, geography, economics, science.
- **ICT**: Basics of computer, MS Office, internet, digital initiatives in education, MOODLE, SWAYAM, e-content.
- **Optional Subject**: Graduation level subject chosen by the candidate.

### Best Books
- IGNOU B.Ed Entrance — Standard Exam Guide, Disha, KVS Madaan.
- **TGT/PGT books** (Pratiyogita Darpan, Upkar).
- Standard General Knowledge Compendium.

### Official Link
- **ignou.ac.in** (University)
- **nta.ac.in** (test portal)

### Notes for Aspirants
- Begin with **NCERT foundations** for any subject-based exam.
- Solve **previous year papers (last 10 years)** of each exam.
- Attempt **sectional + full-length mock tests** under timed conditions.
- For NET-family exams, **Paper I preparation is common and reusable** across subjects.
- **Current Affairs** is critical for CUET, UGC NET, MAT, CMAT, XAT.
- **Logical Reasoning and Quantitative Aptitude** form the core of CAT, XAT, MAT, CMAT, SNAP, NMAT, ATMA.
- **Subject specialization** (Paper II of NET / GATE Core) requires UG-level mastery.
