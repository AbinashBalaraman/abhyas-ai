# Teaching Examinations in India
## CTET, KVS, NVS, DSSSB & State TETs

---

## 1. CTET (Central Teacher Eligibility Test)

**Conducted by:** Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE)
**Official site:** https://ctet.nic.in/
**Frequency:** Twice a year (CBT, 20 Indian languages)
**Note (2021):** Validity extended from 7 years to **lifetime**.

### Eligibility
| Level | Qualification |
|-------|---------------|
| **Paper-I (Classes 1-5)** | 12th (50%) + D.Ed / D.El.Ed / B.El.Ed / B.Ed |
| **Paper-II (Classes 6-8)** | Bachelor's (50%) + B.Ed / B.El.Ed (Final year appearing allowed) |

### Exam Pattern (2 Papers)
#### Paper-I (Classes I-V)
| Section | Questions | Marks |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| Child Development & Pedagogy (CDP) | 30 | 30 |
| Language I (compulsory) | 30 | 30 |
| Language II (compulsory) | 30 | 30 |
| Mathematics | 30 | 30 |
| Environmental Studies (EVS) | 30 | 30 |
| **Total** | **150** | **150** |

- **Duration:** 150 minutes (no negative marking)

#### Paper-II (Classes VI-VIII)
| Section | Questions | Marks |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| CDP | 30 | 30 |
| Language I | 30 | 30 |
| Language II | 30 | 30 |
| Mathematics & Science OR Social Studies | 60 | 60 |
| **Total** | **150** | **150** |

### Detailed Syllabus
#### Child Development & Pedagogy (CDP)
- **Child Development:** Principles of growth & development; stages (Infancy, Early Childhood, Middle Childhood, Adolescence); Theories — Piaget (Cognitive), Vygotsky (Socio-cultural), Erikson (Psycho-social), Kohlberg (Moral), Bandura (Social Learning).
- **Learning:** Behaviorist, Cognitive, Constructivist approaches; Factors affecting learning; Motivation.
- **Pedagogy:** Teaching methods (lecture, discussion, activity-based); Classroom management; Assessment (Formative & Summative, CCE); Evaluation.
- **Inclusive Education & Special Education:** CWSN, individual differences, gifted/slow learners.
- **Action Research, Curriculum:** NCF 2005; NEP 2020 perspectives.

#### Languages (Hindi / English / regional)
- Comprehension (unseen passages)
- Pedagogy of Language Development
- Grammar, Vocabulary, Writing skills
- Language acquisition theories

#### Mathematics (Paper I)
- **Content:** Number System; Algebra; Geometry; Mensuration; Data Handling.
- **Pedagogy:** Approaches (Inductive, Deductive, Analytic); Methods; Remedial teaching.

#### EVS (Paper I)
- Family, Food, Shelter, Water, Travel
- Things We Make & Do, Plant & Animal life
- Natural Resources, Environment, Ecology

#### Mathematics & Science (Paper II)
- Number System, Algebra, Geometry, Mensuration, Data Handling, Statistics
- **Physics:** Motion, Force, Energy, Light, Sound
- **Chemistry:** Matter, Acids/Bases/Salts, Metals & Non-metals
- **Biology:** Cell, Nutrition, Health, Diseases, Ecosystem

#### Social Studies (Paper II)
- **History:** Ancient, Medieval, Modern India; World History
- **Geography:** Physical, Economic, Resources
- **Polity:** Constitution, Democracy, Rights
- **Economics:** Money, Banking, Trade, Resources

### Qualifying Marks
- **UR/OBC:** 60%
- **SC/ST/Differently-abled:** 55%

### Salary Impact
CTET is the minimum eligibility for KVS, NVS, DSSSB, Aided Schools, and most State teaching posts. Not a direct recruitment exam.

## 2. KVS (Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan)

**Website:** https://kvsangathan.nic.in/
**Schools:** 1,289+ across India + 3 abroad (Kathmandu, Moscow, Tehran)
**Affiliated to:** CBSE
**Salary:** 7th CPC Pay Level 6–8 (₹35,400–₹1,12,400 approx)

### Posts Offered
TGT (Trained Graduate Teacher), PGT (Post Graduate Teacher), PRT (Primary Teacher), TGT (Special Educator), Librarian, Music Teacher, Art Teacher, Sports Coach, Counselor, Vice-Principal, Principal.

### Eligibility
| Post | Qualification |
|------|---------------|
| **PRT** | 12th (50%) + D.El.Ed / B.El.Ed + CTET |
| **TGT** | Bachelor's (50%) + B.Ed + CTET + Subject knowledge |
| **PGT** | Master's (50%) + B.Ed + CTET + Subject knowledge |
| **PRT (Music/Art)** | Bachelor's in relevant subject |

### Exam Pattern (Recruitment)
| Section | Questions | Marks |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| General English | 10 | 10 |
| General Hindi | 10 | 10 |
| GK & Reasoning | 10 | 10 |
| Computer Literacy | 10 | 10 |
| Pedagogy & Teaching Aptitude | 20 | 20 |
| Subject Knowledge (post-specific) | 90 | 90 |
| **Total** | **150** | **150** |

- **Duration:** 2 hours 30 minutes
- **Negative marking:** 0.25 per wrong answer

### Detailed Syllabus
#### Common Sections
- **English:** Comprehension, Vocabulary, Grammar, Sentence Correction
- **Hindi:** Vyakaran, Muhavre, Comprehension, Letter/Paragraph writing
- **GK & Reasoning:** Current affairs, History, Polity, Geography, Reasoning (verbal & non-verbal), Number series, Coding-Decoding, Blood relations
- **Computer:** Fundamentals, MS Office, Internet, Networking basics
- **Pedagogy:** Teaching methods, Learning theories (Piaget, Vygotsky, Bruner), Assessment, Classroom management, Curriculum development, Inclusive education

#### Subject Knowledge (90 Qs)
Detailed post-specific syllabus for: Hindi, English, Sanskrit, Mathematics, Science, Social Studies, Computer Science, Commerce, Economics, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, History, Geography, Political Science, Home Science.

### Pay Structure (7th CPC)
| Post | Pay Level | Approx Basic Pay |
|------|-----------|-------------------|
| PRT | Level 6 | ₹35,400 |
| TGT | Level 7 | ₹44,900 |
| PGT | Level 8 | ₹47,600 |
| Vice-Principal | Level 8 | ₹47,600+ |
| Principal | Level 12 | ₹78,800 |

## 3. NVS (Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti)

**Website:** https://navodaya.gov.in/
**Schools:** 661 JNVs (functional residential schools, classes VI-XII)
**Affiliated to:** CBSE
**Salary:** 7th CPC Pay Level 6–8 (similar to KVS)

### Posts Offered
PGT, TGT, Principal, Vice-Principal, Counselor, LDC, Stenographer, Catering Assistant, Electrician, Plumber, etc.

### Eligibility
| Post | Qualification |
|------|---------------|
| **TGT** | Bachelor's (50%) + B.Ed + CTET + Subject |
| **PGT** | Master's (50%) + B.Ed + CTET + Subject |
| **Principal** | Master's + B.Ed + 10+ years experience |
| **LDC** | 12th + Typing speed |

### Exam Pattern (TGT/PGT)
| Section | Questions | Marks |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| Reasoning & Numerical Ability | 20 | 20 |
| General English | 10 | 10 |
| General Hindi | 10 | 10 |
| General Knowledge | 10 | 10 |
| Computer Knowledge | 10 | 10 |
| Subject Knowledge | 90 | 90 |
| **Total** | **150** | **150** |

- **Duration:** 2 hours 30 minutes
- **Negative marking:** 0.25 per wrong answer

### Detailed Syllabus
- **Reasoning:** Analogy, Series, Syllogism, Coding-Decoding, Direction, Blood relations
- **Numerical Ability:** Number system, Percentage, Profit-Loss, Time-Distance
- **English:** Comprehension, Vocabulary, Grammar
- **Hindi:** Grammar, Comprehension, Writing
- **GK:** Indian History, Geography, Polity, Economics, Current Affairs
- **Computer:** Fundamentals, MS Office, Internet
- **Subject Knowledge:** Same subjects as KVS (90 Qs, post-specific, detailed)

### Salary
Similar to KVS — Pay Level 6 (₹35,400) to Pay Level 12 (Principal: ₹78,800).

## 4. DSSSB (Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board)

**Website:** https://dsssb.delhi.gov.in/ | https://dsssbonline.nic.in/
**Established:** 1997 (50th year of Independence)
**Recruits for:** MCD, NDMC, Delhi Govt Departments

### Posts Offered
TGT, PGT, PRT (Asst. Teacher Primary), PGT (all subjects), Domestic Science Teacher, Librarian, Music/Art Teacher, Special Educator, Counselor, Patwari, ASI, Section Officer, Grade-IV, JE, AE, etc.

### Exam Pattern (TGT/PGT/PRT)
**Two Tiers** (one for most posts; both for some):

#### One-Tier (Objective CBT) — TGT/PGT/PRT
| Section | Questions | Marks |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| General Awareness | 20 | 20 |
| General Intelligence & Reasoning | 20 | 20 |
| Numerical Ability | 20 | 20 |
| English Language | 20 | 20 |
| Hindi Language | 20 | 20 |
| Subject-specific (post-related) | 100 | 100 |
| **Total** | **200** | **200** |

- **Duration:** 2 hours
- **Negative marking:** 0.25 per wrong answer

### Detailed Syllabus (Section A — Common)
- **General Awareness:** History, Polity, Geography, Economics, Science, Current Affairs
- **Reasoning:** Verbal/Non-verbal, Analogy, Series, Coding, Syllogism
- **Numerical Ability:** Number system, Simplification, Percentage, Profit-Loss, SI/CI, Geometry
- **English:** Comprehension, Grammar, Vocabulary, Sentence Correction
- **Hindi:** Vyakaran, Muhavre, Letter/Paragraph writing, Comprehension

### Subject-Specific Syllabus (100 Qs)
Detailed syllabus for: TGT (Hindi, English, Sanskrit, Mathematics, Science, Social Studies), PGT (all subjects), PRT (CDP + Teaching Aptitude), Librarian (Library Science), Special Educator, etc.

### Salary (7th CPC)
| Post | Pay Level | Basic Pay |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| Grade-IV | Level 1 | ₹18,000 |
| Patwari/LDC | Level 3 | ₹21,700 |
| TGT / PRT | Level 7 | ₹44,900 |
| PGT | Level 8 | ₹47,600 |
| Section Officer | Level 8 | ₹47,600 |

## 5. State TETs (Brief Overview)

All State TETs follow the **same pattern as CTET** (Paper I — Primary; Paper II — Upper Primary). Validity mostly lifetime (most extended from 7 years after 2021). Minimum 60% (55% for SC/ST) qualifying.

| State TET | Conducting Body | Pattern | Syllabus |
|-----------|-----------------|---------|----------|
| **UPTET** (Uttar Pradesh) | UPBEB (updeled.gov.in) | 2 papers, 150 Qs each, 2.5 hrs | Same as CTET |
| **REET** (Rajasthan) | RBSE | Level 1 & 2, 150 Qs, 2.5 hrs, no negative marking | Lifetime validity since 2022 |
| **HTET** (Haryana) | BSEH (bseh.org.in) | 3 Levels: PRT/TGT/PGT, 150 Qs each | 7-year validity; can retake |
| **PSTET** (Punjab) | Punjab School Education Board | 2 papers, 150 Qs each | Same as CTET |
| **MAHATET** (Maharashtra) | MSCE Pune | 2 papers, 150 Qs each | Same as CTET |
| **OTET** (Odisha) | BSE Odisha | Paper I & II, 150 Qs | Same as CTET |
| **KTET** (Kerala) | Kerala PSC / Pareeksha Bhavan | 4 categories (I–IV), 150 Qs | Specific to Kerala syllabus |
| **TNTET** (Tamil Nadu) | TRB Tamil Nadu | Paper I & II, 150 Qs | Same as CTET |
| **APTET** (Andhra Pradesh) | AP Commissioner of School Education | 2 papers, 150 Qs | Same as CTET; 20% weightage in DSC |
| **TSTET** (Telangana) | Telangana Commissioner School Education | 2 papers, 150 Qs | Same as CTET |
| **MPTET** (Madhya Pradesh) | MPPEB (Bhopal) | 2 papers, 150 Qs | Same as CTET |
| **HPTET** (Himachal Pradesh) | HPBoSE | JBT, TGT (Arts/Med/Non-Med), LT, Shastri | Lifetime validity |
| **UTET** (Uttarakhand) | UBSE | 2 papers, 150 Qs | Same as CTET |
| **CG TET** (Chhattisgarh) | CGSOS/SCERT | 2 papers | Same as CTET |
| **BTET / STET** (Bihar) | BSEB | STET for Classes 9–12 | Similar pattern |
| **JHTET** (Jharkhand) | JAC | 2 papers | Same as CTET |
| **WB TET** (West Bengal) | WBBSE / WBCSSC | Primary & Upper Primary | Same as CTET |
| **KTET** (Karnataka) | KSEEB | 2 papers, 150 Qs | Same as CTET |
| **Goa TET** | Goa Board | 2 papers | Same as CTET |
| **NE State TETs** (Manipur, Nagaland, Sikkim, Arunachal, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Tripura, Assam) | Respective state boards | 2 papers, 150 Qs | Same as CTET |

**Common Pattern:** All have CDP (30), Language I (30), Language II (30), Maths/Science/EVS/Social Studies (60). Duration: 150 min. Most valid lifetime. Salary impact: eligibility for state teaching posts (₹25,000–₹40,000 approx for fresh appointees).
