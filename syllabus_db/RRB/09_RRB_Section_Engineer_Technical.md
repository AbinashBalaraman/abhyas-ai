## 1. RRB Section Engineer / Senior Section Engineer (SSE)

### 1.1 Posts Offered (Senior Technical Posts in Various Departments)

**Civil Engineering Stream:**
- Senior Section Engineer (P-Way) - Permanent Way / Track maintenance
- Senior Section Engineer (Bridge) - Bridge inspection & maintenance
- Senior Section Engineer (Track Machine) - Track machine operations
- Senior Section Engineer (Works) - Civil works & construction
- Senior Section Engineer (Estimator) - Project cost estimation
- Senior Section Engineer (Drawing & Design) - Civil design office

**General Streams:**
- Senior Section Engineer (Civil Engineering)
- Senior Section Engineer (Mechanical Engineering)
- Senior Section Engineer (Electrical Engineering)
- Senior Section Engineer (Electronics Engineering)
- Senior Section Engineer (Telecommunication Engineering)
- Senior Section Engineer (Signal Engineering)

**Mechanical Specializations:**
- SSE (Mech / Mech-Traction / Diesel) - Locomotive traction
- SSE (Mech-Thermal / AC) - Air-conditioning systems
- SSE (Mech-Workshop) - Workshop engineering
- SSE (Mech-C&W) - Carriage & Wagon

**Electrical Specializations:**
- SSE (Electrical General)
- SSE (Electrical TRD) - Traction Distribution
- SSE (Electrical TRC) - Traction Rolling stock
- SSE (Electrical Workshop)
- SSE (Electrical TRS) - Traction Rolling Stock

**Signal & Telecommunication (S&T) Specializations:**
- SSE (S&T)
- SSE (S&T Workshop)
- SSE (S&T-Cabin) - Cabin signaling operations

### 1.2 Eligibility

**Educational Qualifications (any one):**
- 4-year Bachelor's Degree in Engineering (B.E / B.Tech) in relevant discipline
- 3-year Diploma in Engineering + 2 years relevant work experience
- B.Sc (Engineering) - older qualification still accepted

**Age Limit:**
- General: 20 - 34 years
- OBC (Non-Creamy Layer): 20 - 37 years (3 years relaxation)
- SC/ST: 20 - 39 years (5 years relaxation)
- PwD: 10-15 years additional relaxation as per category
- Ex-Servicemen: up to 3 years after military service deduction
- Note: Final year students are not eligible; degree must be complete at time of application

### 1.3 Exam Pattern

#### CBT-1 (Computer Based Test - Stage 1)
| Parameter | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Sections | 4 (General Awareness, Reasoning, Quantitative Aptitude, General Science) |
| Total Questions | 100 |
| Total Marks | 100 |
| Duration | 90 minutes (120 minutes for PwD with scribe) |
| Marking | +1 mark for correct, -1/3 for wrong |
| Language | English, Hindi & 13 regional languages |

#### CBT-2 (Computer Based Test - Stage 2)
| Parameter | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Type | Post-specific Technical |
| Total Questions | 150 |
| Total Marks | 150 |
| Duration | 120 minutes |
| Marking | +1 for correct, -1/3 for wrong |

#### Document Verification (DV)
- After CBT-2 shortlisting
- Original certificates verification
- Medical fitness test as per railway standards

### 1.6 Marking Scheme

| Event | Marks |
|-------|-------|
| Correct Answer | +1 mark |
| Wrong Answer | -1/3 mark (0.33) |
| Unattempted | 0 marks |

- No normalization in CBT-1 & CBT-2
- Merit based on CBT-2 score only (CBT-1 is qualifying)
- Section-wise cut-off + Overall cut-off

## 2. RRB Other Senior Posts

### 2.1 Assistant Station Master (ASM)
- **Department:** Operating
- **Eligibility:** Graduate (any discipline) + typing knowledge desirable
- **Age:** 18-32 (relaxation for reserved)
- **Exam:** NTPC (Non-Technical Popular Categories) under RRB
- **Pay Level:** Level 6 (Rs 35,400 - 1,12,400)
- **Duties:** Station operations, train passing, ticket checking, signal control

### 2.2 Traffic Apprentice (TA)
- **Department:** Traffic / Transportation
- **Eligibility:** Bachelor's Degree (any stream)
- **Age:** 18-30
- **Exam:** NTPC category
- **Pay Level:** Level 6 (Rs 35,400 - 1,12,400)
- **Duties:** Train scheduling, traffic planning, dispatching

### 2.3 Assistant Loco Pilot (ALP)
- **Department:** Mechanical / Electrical
- **Eligibility:** ITI in relevant trade OR Diploma in Engineering OR Graduation
- **Age:** 18-28
- **Exam:** 2-stage CBT (Technical + Aptitude) + Medical
- **Pay Level:** Level 2 (Rs 19,900 - 63,200)
- **Duties:** Assisting Loco Pilot in train operations

### 2.4 Goods Guard
- **Department:** Transportation / Operating
- **Eligibility:** Bachelor's Degree (any stream)
- **Age:** 18-32
- **Exam:** NTPC category
- **Pay Level:** Level 6 (Rs 35,400 - 1,12,400)
- **Duties:** Managing goods trains, safety, documentation

### 2.5 Section Officer
Various sub-categories:
- **Section Officer (Finance):** - B.Com/M.Com with relevant experience, Pay Level 7
- **Section Officer (Personnel):** - Degree in Personnel Management / HR, Pay Level 7
- **Section Officer (S&T):** - Engineering in S&T discipline, Pay Level 7
- **Section Officer (Civil):** - Civil Engineering degree, Pay Level 7
- **Section Officer (Electrical):** - Electrical Engineering degree, Pay Level 7

**Common:** Recruitment through RRB (Ministerial & Isolated Categories) or departmental exams. Age 20-32.

### 2.6 Material Manager
- **Department:** Stores / Material Management
- **Eligibility:** Degree in Engineering OR Master's in Materials Management OR MBA (Materials)
- **Age:** 18-32
- **Pay Level:** Level 7 (Rs 44,900 - 1,42,400)
- **Duties:** Inventory management, procurement, vendor management

### 2.7 Office Superintendent
- **Department:** Administrative / Ministerial
- **Eligibility:** Bachelor's Degree
- **Age:** 18-32
- **Pay Level:** Level 6 (Rs 35,400 - 1,12,400)
- **Duties:** Office administration, staff management, record keeping

### 2.8 Personnel Officer
- **Department:** Human Resources
- **Eligibility:** Master's in Personnel Management / HRM / Social Work OR equivalent
- **Age:** 18-32 (relaxation as per category)
- **Pay Level:** Level 7 (Rs 44,900 - 1,42,400)
- **Duties:** Staff welfare, recruitment, disciplinary matters, employee relations

### 3.4 Preparation Tips

1. **Know the Pattern:** Understand CBT-1 and CBT-2 structure thoroughly
2. **Time Management:** Practice with timer; aim for accuracy over attempts
3. **Previous Year Papers:** Solve last 10 years' papers
4. **Mock Tests:** Take at least 50-100 mock tests before the exam
5. **Revision:** Make short notes for last-minute revision
6. **Current Affairs:** Read daily for at least 6 months before exam
7. **Technical Focus:** CBT-2 is technical-heavy; focus on core engineering subjects
8. **Negative Marking:** Avoid wild guessing; only attempt with >70% confidence
9. **Physical & Mental Health:** Regular exercise and proper sleep schedule
10. **Stay Updated:** Follow official RRB notifications and updates
