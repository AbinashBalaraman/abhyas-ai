# Indian Railway PSUs, Subordinate Services & Other Major Railway Exams

Comprehensive guide to recruitment exams conducted by Indian Railway Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs), subordinate organisations, and other railway establishments (excluding RRB JE/ALP/NTPC/Group-D covered in separate files).

---

## 1. RITES Ltd (Rail India Technical and Economic Service)

**Overview:** RITES Limited is a Navratna Central Public Sector Enterprise (CPSE) under the Ministry of Railways, Government of India. Incorporated on 26 April 1974, headquartered in Gurugram (Haryana). Listed on NSE/BSE since 2018. Provides consultancy, engineering, export, leasing, and turnkey construction services across railways, highways, metros, airports, ports, ropeways, and urban infrastructure. Operates in 55+ countries.

### Posts
- **Engineer** (Civil / Electrical / Mechanical / Computer Science / Electronics / Metallurgy)
- **Site Inspector**
- **Project Officer / Project Engineer**
- **Manager / Assistant Manager** (various disciplines)
- **Technical Assistant / Draftsman**
- **Apprentice** (Graduate / Diploma / ITI)

### Eligibility
| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Education | B.E. / B.Tech / M.E. / M.Tech in relevant discipline (Diploma for some posts) |
| Age Limit | 21 to 32 years (relaxation: OBC +3, SC/ST +5, PwD as per Govt. rules) |
| Nationality | Indian Citizen |
| Experience | 0–3 years (post-qualification, depending on post level) |

### Exam Pattern
**Stage 1 – Written Test (CBT):**
- **Technical Section:** 70% weightage (discipline-specific, GATE-level)
- **General Aptitude:** Reasoning, English, Quantitative Aptitude, General Awareness (30%)
- Duration: 2 hours | Negative marking: 0.25 or 0.33

**Stage 2 – GD / Interview:**
- Group Discussion (topic on current affairs / technical)
- Personal Interview (technical + HR)
- Weightage ratio varies: 80:20 or 70:30 (Written:Interview)

### Detailed Syllabus

**Technical (GATE-Level):**
- **Civil Engineering:** Structural Analysis, RCC, Steel Design, Soil Mechanics, Foundation, Fluid Mechanics, Hydraulics, Survey, Transportation, Environment, Irrigation
- **Electrical:** Power Systems, Machines, Control Systems, Power Electronics, Signals, Measurements, Network Theory, EMTL
- **Mechanical:** Thermodynamics, RAC, IC Engines, Fluid Mechanics, Machine Design, Manufacturing, Industrial Engineering, Strength of Materials
- **CSE:** Data Structures, Algorithms, OS, DBMS, CN, Software Engineering, Discrete Maths, Programming (C/C++/Java)

**General Aptitude:**
- **Reasoning:** Coding-decoding, blood relations, syllogism, seating arrangement, puzzles
- **English:** Grammar, vocabulary, comprehension, error spotting
- **Quantitative Aptitude:** Number system, algebra, geometry, mensuration, trigonometry, statistics
- **General Awareness:** Indian Railways, current affairs, static GK, Indian polity, geography, history

**Official Link:** https://www.rites.com/ → Careers → Vacancies

## 2. IRCON International Limited

**Overview:** Navratna PSU under Ministry of Railways, incorporated on 28 April 1976 as Indian Railway Construction Company Limited. Headquartered in New Delhi (CIN: L45203DL1976GOI008171). Specializes in turnkey construction of railways, highways, bridges, buildings, electrical, signaling/telecom, and mechanical projects in India and abroad. 405 domestic + 130 international projects completed.

### Posts
- **Site Engineer** (Civil / Electrical / Mechanical / S&T)
- **Project Engineer / Project Manager**
- **Manager / Deputy Manager / Assistant Manager**
- **Executive (Finance, HR, Legal)**
- **Apprentice / Technician**
- **Company Secretary, Security Officer**

### Eligibility
| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Education | Diploma / B.E. / B.Tech / M.Tech in relevant engineering discipline (CA/ICWA for Finance, MBA for HR) |
| Age Limit | 30 to 55 years (varies by post) |
| Experience | 1–10 years (depending on post) |

### Exam Pattern
**Recruitment Modes:**
- **Direct Recruitment:** Written Test (Technical + General) + Interview
- **Contract Basis:** Walk-in interviews / shortlisting based on merit
- **Deputation:** From other government organisations

**Written Test (when conducted):**
- Technical questions (discipline-specific): 70%
- General aptitude + English: 30%
- Duration: 2 hours

**Interview:** 20–30% weightage (technical expertise + personality)

**Official Link:** https://www.ircon.org/ → HR & Career

## 4. CRIS (Centre for Railway Information Systems)

**Overview:** Autonomous organisation under Ministry of Railways, established 1986. Headquartered in New Delhi. Develops and maintains mission-critical IT systems for Indian Railways – Passenger Reservation System (PRS), Unreserved Ticketing System (UTS), Freight Operations, FOIS, HRMS, Accounting, Asset Management, RTIS, eDrishti, eProcurement, etc. Handles 2 crore+ daily ticket bookings.

### Posts
- **Software Engineer / Programmer**
- **Technical Assistant**
- **Assistant Personnel Officer (APO)**
- **Senior Project Manager / Project Manager**
- **System Analyst / Network Engineer**
- **Junior Engineer (IT)**

### Eligibility
| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Education | B.E. / B.Tech (CS/IT) / MCA / M.Sc (CS/IT) |
| Age Limit | 22 to 35 years (relaxation as per rules) |
| Skills | Programming (Java, Python, .NET, React, Node, Angular, SQL), DBMS, OS, CN, AI/ML, Big Data, Cloud (AWS/Azure) |

### Exam Pattern
**Written Test (CBT):**
- **Technical:** Programming, DSA, DBMS, OS, CN, Software Engineering, Web Technologies (70%)
- **General Aptitude:** Reasoning, English, Quantitative, GA (30%)
- Duration: 2 hours

**Interview:** Technical + HR

**Final Selection:** Written (80%) + Interview (20%)

**Official Link:** https://www.cris.org.in/ → Career section

## 5. DFCCIL (Dedicated Freight Corridor Corporation of India Ltd)

**Overview:** Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) under Ministry of Railways, established 2006. Building dedicated freight corridors (Eastern: Ludhiana–Kolkata; Western: Mumbai–Delhi). CPSE, Navratna status.

### Posts
- **Executive** (Civil, Electrical, HR, Finance, Operations, S&T, Mechanical, IT)
- **Junior Executive** (multiple disciplines)
- **Multi-Tasking Staff (MTS)**
- **Assistant Manager / Manager**

### Eligibility
| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Education | Diploma (3 years) / B.E. / B.Tech / MBA / CA / PG (depending on post) |
| Age Limit | 18 to 30 years (relaxation: OBC +3, SC/ST +5) |

### Exam Pattern
**CBT (Computer Based Test) – 2 hours:**
- **For Executive/JE (Technical):** Technical (75%) + General (25%) – 100 Questions
- **For Executive (Non-Technical):** Reasoning, GA, English, Quantitative, Technical awareness
- **Negative marking:** 1/3 for each wrong answer
- Sections: General Awareness, Reasoning, English, Quantitative, Technical

**For MTS:** CBT Descriptive / Objective mix

**Document Verification → Medical → Final Selection**

### Detailed Syllabus

**General Section (All Posts):**
- **Reasoning:** Analogies, coding-decoding, series, Venn diagrams, syllogism, seating
- **General Awareness:** Indian Railways, current affairs, static GK, polity, history, geography
- **English Language:** Grammar, comprehension, fill in blanks, antonyms/synonyms, sentence correction
- **Quantitative Aptitude:** Number system, simplification, ratio, percentage, profit/loss, time-work, geometry

**Technical Section (Discipline-Specific):**
- **Civil:** SOM, RCC, Steel, Soil, Fluid Mechanics, Survey, Transportation, Environment, Concrete Tech
- **Electrical:** Power Systems, Machines, Control, Power Electronics, Signals, Measurements
- **S&T:** Signaling, Telecom, Networks, Digital Electronics, Microprocessors
- **Mechanical:** Thermodynamics, RAC, IC Engines, Machine Design, Manufacturing, Industrial Engg

**Official Link:** https://dfccil.com/ → Careers

## 7. IRCTC (Indian Railway Catering and Tourism Corporation)

**Overview:** Schedule 'A' Miniratna PSU under Ministry of Railways, incorporated 1999. Listed on NSE/BSE. Handles catering, tourism, packaged drinking water (Rail Neer), internet ticketing, and travel services.

### Posts

- **Executive (Operations / Tourism / IT)**
- **Supervisor (Catering)** – now under Indian Railways
- **Apprentice** (Graduate / Diploma / ITI)

### Eligibility
| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Education | Graduation / Post-Graduation / MBA / CA / B.E. / B.Tech (depending on post) |
| Age Limit | 20 to 35 years (relaxation as per rules) |
| Skills | Tourism, Hospitality, IT, Marketing, Finance, HR |

### Exam Pattern
**Online Test (CBT):**
- **Section A:** Professional/Technical Knowledge (50%)

- Negative marking applicable

**Interview:** Document Verification → Medical → Final Selection

**Official Link:** https://www.irctc.com/ → Careers

## 10. RCF (Rail Coach Factory) Apprentices – Kapurthala & Sonipat

**Overview:** RCF Kapurthala (Punjab) and RCF Sonipat (Haryana) are production units of Indian Railways manufacturing LHB coaches. Both offer apprenticeship training under the Apprentices Act 1961.

### Posts
- **Trade Apprentice** (Fitter, Welder, Electrician, Machinist, Painter, Carpenter)
- **Technician Apprentice** (Diploma holders)
- **Graduate Apprentice** (Engineering Degree holders)

### Eligibility
| Post | Qualification | Age |
|---|---|---|
| Trade Apprentice | 10th + ITI (NCVT/SCVT) in relevant trade | 15–24 years |
| Technician Apprentice | Diploma in Engineering | 18–25 years |
| Graduate Apprentice | B.E. / B.Tech in Engineering | 18–26 years |

**Selection Process:**
- **Merit-Based:** Based on 10th/ITI/Diploma/Degree marks (no separate exam)
- Document verification
- Medical fitness
- Reservation as per Government rules

**Application:** Through RCF official portal and Apprenticeship India (https://www.apprenticeshipindia.gov.in/)

**Official Link:** https://www.rcf.indianrailways.gov.in/

## 11. RPF / RPSF Tradesman & Other Railway Jobs

### RPF Tradesman Recruitment
- **Posts:** Constable (Tradesman) in Railway Protection Force
- **Eligibility:** 10th pass + ITI in relevant trade OR 10th + relevant experience
- **Exam:** CBT (100 Qs, 90 min) → PET/PMT → DV
- **Syllabus:** General Awareness, Reasoning, Arithmetic, Basic Technical Trade Knowledge
- **Conducted by:** RRB on behalf of RPF

### Other Subordinate Railway Posts
- **Catering Staff:** Base Kitchen Helper, Catering Inspector, Cook (recruitment transferred to IRCTC/Indian Railways)
- **Ticket Checking Staff:** Travelling Ticket Examiner (TTE) – promoted from Group-D or direct
- **Station Master (Goods/Assistant):** Graduation + aptitude test (RRB)
- **Train Guard / Loco Pilot (Goods/Shunting):** through RRB ALP
- **Pointsman / Signal Maintainer / Track Maintainer:** Group-D posts
- **Commercial Clerk / Booking Clerk:** Graduation + RRB NTPC
- **Enquiry-cum-Reservation Clerk:** Graduation + typing
- **Paramedical Staff:** Staff Nurse, Pharmacist, Lab Technician (RRB Paramedical)
- **Ministerial & Isolated:** Stenographer, Junior Translator, Law Assistant, Traffic & Accounts (RRB M&I)

### Key Exam Bodies
- **RRB (Railway Recruitment Boards):** All India railway recruitment
- **RRCB (Railway Recruitment Control Board):** Apex body of RRBs
- **Zonal Railways:** Direct recruitment for specific project-based posts

## Preparation Strategy
1. **Strong Technical Foundation** – Equal emphasis on GATE-level concepts
2. **General Aptitude** – Daily 1 hour for Reasoning, Quant, English
3. **Current Affairs** – Focus on Indian Railways, government schemes, economy
4. **Previous Papers** – Solve last 5–10 years' papers of respective PSUs
5. **Group Discussion / Interview** – Practice for RITES, IRCON, CRIS-style interviews
6. **GATE Preparation** – Most PSU exams mirror GATE syllabus; preparing for GATE helps in multiple PSU exams
