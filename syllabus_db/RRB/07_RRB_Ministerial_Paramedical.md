# RRB Ministerial & Isolated Categories and RRB Paramedical Examinations

Comprehensive study guide for the Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) Ministerial & Isolated Categories (M&I) and Paramedical recruitment examinations conducted under the Ministry of Railways, Government of India.

---

## Part 1: RRB Ministerial & Isolated Categories (M&I)

### Overview

The RRB Ministerial & Isolated Categories examination recruits non-gazetted Group C posts in Indian Railways for administrative, teaching, stenographic, translation, and welfare functions. The recruitment is conducted centrally through 21 RRBs (Ahmedabad, Ajmer, Prayagraj, Bangalore, Bhopal, Bhubaneswar, Bilaspur, Chandigarh, Chennai, Gorakhpur, Guwahati, Jammu & Kashmir, Kolkata, Malda, Mumbai, Muzaffarpur, Patna, Ranchi, Secunderabad, Siliguri, Thiruvananthapuram) under the apex Railway Recruitment Control Board (RRCB), established 27 April 1998, headquartered at Rail Bhavan, New Delhi.

**Latest Cycle:** CEN 07/2024 (M&I) — CBT held 10–12 September 2025; Result/Translation Test 2025–2026; DV-1 from 04 June 2026. CEN 08/2025 (Isolated Categories) released for 311 vacancies.

### Eligibility (Summary)

- **Nationality:** Indian Citizen (or subject of Nepal/Bhutan, Tibetan refugee with eligibility certificate)
- **Age:** 18 to 30 years (general), up to 33 for some posts; relaxations: SC/ST +5 yrs, OBC +3 yrs, PwD +10 yrs, Ex-Servicemen as per rules
- **Educational:** 10+2 / Graduation / Post-Graduation / B.Ed / Professional Diploma depending on post
- **Typing/Steno:** Specific WPM norms for steno and translator posts
- **Medical Fitness:** As per prescribed standard for the post

### Selection / Exam Pattern

```
Stage 1:  Computer Based Test (CBT)             — Qualifying + Merit
Stage 2:  Skill Test (Steno / Translation / TST)— Qualifying in nature
Stage 3:  Document Verification (DV)              — Final verification
Stage 4:  Medical Examination (ME)                — Fitness check
```

#### CBT Pattern (Objective Type)

| Section | No. of Questions | Marks | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Professional Ability (post-specific) | 50 | 50 | 90 minutes (120 min for PwD) |
| General Awareness | 15 | 15 | |
| General Intelligence & Reasoning | 15 | 15 | |
| Mathematics | 10 | 10 | |
| General Science | 10 | 10 | |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** | |

- **Marking:** +1 for correct; −1/3 negative marking for wrong answers
- **Medium:** English, Hindi, and 13 regional languages for CBT

#### Skill Test Details

**Stenography Skill Test** (for Steno posts)
- 10× vacancies called for Shorthand Test
- 300 marks, computer-based evaluation
- 80 WPM dictation; transcription time:
  - English: 50 minutes
  - Hindi: 65 minutes (KrutiDev/Mangal fonts required)
- Marking: −1 for major error, −0.5 for minor error; 10% error tolerance

**Translation Test** (for Junior Translator/Hindi)
- 10× vacancies called
- Minimum 60% required to qualify
- Tested separately from main CBT; score not added to main merit

**Performance Test / Teacher Skill Test (TST)** (for TGT, PGT, PRT, PTI, Music, Dance, Art)
- Performance Test: Music/Dance teachers assessed on performance
- TST: Topics chosen on the spot; evaluates teaching method, communication, classroom handling

### Detailed Syllabus

#### 1. General Awareness
- **Indian History & Culture:** Ancient, Medieval, Modern India; Freedom Struggle; Post-Independence
- **Polity & Constitution:** Indian Constitution, political system, Panchayati Raj, public policy, governance
- **Geography:** Physical features of India, world geography, climate, soil, rivers, mountains
- **Economics:** Basic economic principles, Indian economy, five-year plans, budget, banking, RBI
- **General Science:** Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Environmental Science (up to 10th CBSE)
- **Current Affairs:** National & international events, sports, awards, important days, summits

#### 2. General Intelligence & Reasoning
- Analogies (word/number)
- Alphabetical & Number Series
- Coding-Decoding
- Blood Relations
- Directions (Distance/Compass)
- Venn Diagrams
- Syllogism
- Seating Arrangement & Puzzles
- Classification
- Data Interpretation (charts, graphs, tables)
- Statement-Arguments, Assumptions, Conclusions
- Mathematical Operations
- Similarities & Differences

#### 3. Mathematics / Arithmetic
- Number System (Integers, Fractions, Decimals, BODMAS, LCM, HCF)
- Percentages, Averages
- Ratio & Proportion
- Profit & Loss, Discount
- Simple & Compound Interest
- Time & Work, Time & Distance, Pipes & Cistern
- Algebra (Simplification, Factorisation, Linear Equations)
- Geometry (Lines, Angles, Triangles, Circles, Quadrilaterals)
- Mensuration (Area, Volume, Perimeter)
- Trigonometry (basic ratios & applications)
- Statistics & Probability (Mean, Median, Mode, SD)
- Square Root, Age Calculations, Calendar & Clock

#### 4. English Language (where applicable for specific posts)
- Vocabulary (Synonyms, Antonyms, Idioms, Phrases)
- Grammar (Tenses, Voice, Narration, Articles, Prepositions)
- Sentence Correction / Error Spotting
- Fill in the blanks, Cloze test
- Comprehension Passages
- One-word Substitution
- Spelling mistakes

#### 5. Professional Ability (post-specific)
- **Stenographer posts:** English/Hindi shorthand, transcription, computer typing
- **Teacher posts (TGT/PGT/PRT):** Pedagogy, child psychology, subject knowledge, B.Ed syllabus topics, NEP-2020
- **Hindi Translator:** Hindi/English grammar, vocabulary, translation techniques, terminology
- **Catering/Hospitality:** Food production, hygiene, kitchen management, Indian Railway Catering rules
- **Library Science:** Classification (DDC, UDC), cataloguing, reference service, library management
- **Law Assistant:** Indian Constitution, Civil/Criminal Law, Labour Law, Railway Act
- **SSE (Engineering):** Technical subjects (Civil/Mechanical/Electrical/Signal/Tele)

## Part 2: RRB Paramedical

### Overview

RRB Paramedical recruitment is conducted for medical, technical, and allied health posts in Indian Railway Hospitals and Health Units. Cycle conducted under CEN 04/2024 and CEN 03/2025 (CBT 11–13 March 2026).

### Eligibility (Summary)

- **Age:** 18–30 years (general), 18–33 for some posts; relaxations per category
- **Nationality:** Indian
- **Qualification:** Strictly post-specific (B.Sc Nursing / GNM, B.Pharm, DMLT, BASLP, BPT, B.Sc MLT, Diploma in respective paramedical discipline, etc.)
- **Registration:** Valid registration with State/National Council where applicable (Nursing Council, Pharmacy Council)
- **Medical Fitness:** As per Railway medical standards (C1/C2/PwD)

### Selection / Exam Pattern

```
Stage 1: Computer Based Test (CBT)        — 100 marks
Stage 2: Document Verification (DV)        — Eligibility & certificates
Stage 3: Medical Examination (ME)          — Fitness (Aye-one or higher)
```

### Quick Preparation Strategy

1. **Read the official CEN notification** completely – pay attention to eligibility, age relaxations, exam city preference, document upload requirements.
2. **Master the Professional/Technical section first** – carries 50% (M&I) to 70% (Paramedical) weightage.
3. **Build daily GK habit** – The Hindu, PIB, Pratiyogita Darpan monthly.
4. **Solve 5+ years of PYQs** topic-wise.
5. **Take 15–20 full-length mock tests** in timed conditions.
6. **For Skill Tests (Steno/Translation/TST):** Practice daily on computer with proper font setup.
7. **Keep originals + 3 sets of self-attested photocopies** ready from Day 1 for DV.
