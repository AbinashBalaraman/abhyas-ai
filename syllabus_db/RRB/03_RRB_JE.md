# RRB JE (Junior Engineer) Examination

## 1. Overview

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Full Name** | Railway Recruitment Board – Junior Engineer, Depot Material Superintendent (DMS), Chemical & Metallurgical Assistant (CMA) and allied posts |
| **Conducting Body** | Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) under the **Railway Recruitment Control Board (RRCB)**, Ministry of Railways, Government of India. There are 21 RRBs (Ahmedabad, Ajmer, Prayagraj, Bangalore, Bhopal, Bhubaneswar, Bilaspur, Chandigarh, Chennai, Gorakhpur, Guwahati, J&K, Kolkata, Malda, Mumbai, Muzaffarpur, Patna, Ranchi, Secunderabad, Siliguri, Thiruvananthapuram) |
| **Latest Cycle** | CEN 05/2025 (CBT-1 held 19, 20 & 25 Feb 2026; CBT-2 tentatively scheduled 02 Jul 2026) |
| **Application Mode** | Online via [rrbapply.gov.in](https://www.rrbapply.gov.in/) |
| **Selection Stages** | CBT-1 → CBT-2 → Document Verification (DV) → Medical Examination (ME) |
| **Frequency** | Generally every 2-3 years as Centralised Employment Notice (CEN). Earlier cycles: CEN 03/2024, CEN 03/2018, CEN 02/2015 |

## 3. Eligibility Criteria (as per CEN 05/2025)

### 3.1 Educational Qualification

| Post | Required Qualification |
|---|---|
| **JE (Civil)** | 3-year Diploma in Civil Engineering / Civil Engineering (Transportation) / B.E./B.Tech in Civil Engineering **OR** B.Sc (with Physics & Maths) + 2-year experience in Civil Engineering works (for certain posts) |
| **JE (Mechanical)** | 3-year Diploma in Mechanical / Production / Automobile / Fabrication / Industrial / Welding Engineering **OR** B.E./B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering |
| **JE (Electrical)** | 3-year Diploma in Electrical / Electronics & Communication / Instrumentation & Control / Computer Science & Engineering **OR** B.E./B.Tech in same |
| **JE (Workshop)** | Diploma/B.Tech in Mechanical/Electrical/Industrial/Electronics |
| **JE (Track Machine)** | Diploma/Degree in Mechanical/Civil/Electrical Engineering |
| **JE (IT)** | B.Sc (Computer Science / IT) **OR** 3-year Diploma in Computer Science / IT / Computer Engineering **OR** B.E./B.Tech in Computer Science / IT |
| **DMS** | 3-year Diploma in Engineering (any discipline) **OR** B.Sc (with Physics, Chemistry, Maths) |
| **CMA** | Bachelor's Degree in Science with **Physics & Chemistry** as main subjects |

### 3.2 Age Limit (as on 01.01.2026 for CEN 05/2025)

| Category | Age |
|---|---|
| **General** | 18 – 33 years |

## 4. Exam Pattern

### 4.1 CBT-1 (Stage 1) – Common for All Posts

| Subject | No. of Questions | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| Mathematics | 30 | 30 |
| General Intelligence & Reasoning | 25 | 25 |
| General Awareness | 15 | 15 |
| General Science (Physics, Chemistry, Life Sciences up to 10th std) | 30 | 30 |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** |

- **Duration:** 90 minutes (PwBD candidates with scribe: 120 minutes)
- **Negative Marking:** 1/3 mark deducted for every wrong answer
- **Sectional Cut-off:** Yes (25% sectional + 25% overall for UR; relaxed for reserved)
- **Shortlisting Ratio:** Approximately **15-20 times** the vacancies for CBT-2

### 4.2 CBT-2 (Stage 2) – Technical / Professional Ability

| Section | No. of Questions | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| General Awareness | 10 | 10 |
| Physics & Basic Science | 15 | 15 |
| Basics of Computers & Applications | 10 | 10 |
| **Technical Ability (discipline-specific)** | **100** | **100** |
| **Total** | **135** (in some cycles 150) | **150** |

- **Duration:** 120 minutes (PwBD with scribe: 160 minutes)
- **Negative Marking:** 1/3 mark deduction
- **Qualifying:** Sectional cut-off + overall cut-off (varies category-wise)

> Note: Some prior cycles (e.g., CEN 03/2024) had CBT-2 with 135 questions and 150 marks. The latest CEN 05/2025 pattern is 150 Qs / 150 Marks.

### 4.3 Document Verification (DV)

- Based on merit in CBT-2, candidates are called for DV in a 1:1 ratio (subject to medical fitness).
- Candidates upload certificates via the OIRMS portal: <https://oirms-ir.gov.in/rrbdv/>
- Medical Examination follows at designated Railway Hospitals (e.g., Ambala Cantt, Delhi/Northern Railway Hospital).

## 5. Detailed Syllabus – CBT-1 (Common for All)

### 5.1 Mathematics (30 Questions)

- **Number System:** Natural, whole, rational, irrational, real numbers; divisibility; HCF/LCM; remainders
- **BODMAS & Simplification:** Order of operations; simplification of fractions
- **Fractions, Decimals, Percentages, Surds & Indices**
- **Ratio & Proportion:** Simple, compound, partnership
- **Percentage:** Profit & loss; discount; successive percentage change
- **Average:** Simple & weighted average
- **Mensuration (2D & 3D):** Areas and volumes of square, rectangle, triangle, circle, cylinder, cone, sphere, cube, cuboid, frustum
- **Time & Work:** Pipes & cisterns; work & wages
- **Time & Distance / Speed:** Trains, boats, streams, races
- **Simple Interest & Compound Interest**
- **Profit & Loss:** True discount, bank discount
- **Algebra:** Identities; linear & quadratic equations; polynomials
- **Geometry:** Triangles (congruence, similarity); circles (tangent, chord); quadrilaterals
- **Trigonometry:** Ratios; identities; heights & distances
- **Statistics & Probability:** Mean, median, mode; basic probability
- **Algebraic Identities, Factorization, Polynomials**

### 5.2 General Intelligence & Reasoning (25 Questions)

- **Analogies:** Word, number, alphabet, semantic
- **Series:** Number, alphabet, alpha-numeric, figural
- **Coding & Decoding:** Letter shifting, sentence coding, conditional coding
- **Mathematical Operations:** BODMAS-based; symbol substitution; balance
- **Similarities & Differences**
- **Relationships:** Blood relations (family tree, coded relations)
- **Analytical Reasoning:** Ranking, seating arrangement, circular, linear, square
- **Syllogism:** Two/three statements, Venn diagram based
- **Jumbling:** Sentence re-arrangement, paragraph order
- **Venn Diagrams:** 2-set and 3-set
- **Puzzle:** Scheduling, assignment, comparison
- **Data Sufficiency:** Two statements
- **Statement & Conclusion / Assumption / Course of Action**
- **Decision Making / Passage Based**
- **Maps:** Direction sense (8-point compass)
- **Interpretation of Graphs / Charts / Tables**
- **Mirror & Water Images, Paper Folding, Paper Cutting**
- **Embedded Figures, Figure Counting, Cube & Dice**

### 5.3 General Awareness (15 Questions)

- **History:** Indian History (Ancient, Medieval, Modern) – emphasis on Freedom Struggle
- **Polity:** Indian Constitution, Parliament, President, PM, Judiciary, Fundamental Rights/Duties
- **Geography:** Indian Geography (rivers, soils, climate, agriculture, minerals), World Geography basics
- **Economics:** Indian Economy, Planning, Budget basics, GST, RBI
- **Science (everyday):** Basic Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Environment
- **Sports:** Cricket, Olympics, Trophies
- **Awards:** National (Padma series) & International (Nobel, Booker)
- **Books & Authors, Capitals, Currencies**
- **Current Affairs:** Last 6 months (national/international) – summits, schemes, appointments, defence, science

### 5.4 General Science (30 Questions)

- **Physics (10th level):** Units & dimensions; motion; force & laws; work-energy-power; gravitation; sound; light (reflection, refraction); electricity & magnetism; heat & thermodynamics; modern physics basics
- **Chemistry (10th level):** Atomic structure; periodic table; chemical bonding; acids/bases/salts; metals & non-metals; carbon compounds; everyday chemistry
- **Life Sciences (Biology):** Cell; nutrition; respiration; blood & circulation; plant & animal kingdom; disease & immunity; nutrition & health

## 6. CBT-2 Technical Syllabus (Discipline-wise)

### 6.1 Civil Engineering

- **Engineering Mechanics:** Statics – forces, equilibrium, friction, centroid, moment of inertia
- **Strength of Materials:** Stress, strain, bending moment, shear force, deflection, torsion
- **Building Construction & Materials:** Stones, bricks, cement, aggregates, timber; building components (foundation, walls, roofs, floors); finishing works
- **Concrete Technology:** Mix design, workability, strength, durability, admixtures, NDT
- **Surveying:** Chain, compass, plane table, theodolite, levelling, traverse, curves, GPS/GIS basics
- **Soil Mechanics:** Index properties, classification, permeability, compaction, shear strength, bearing capacity, earth pressure, slope stability
- **Hydraulics & Hydrology:** Fluid properties, hydrostatics, Bernoulli's theorem, flow measurement, open channel flow, pipes
- **Irrigation Engineering:** Canals, dams, headworks, water-logging, drainage
- **Transportation Engineering:** Highway planning, geometric design, traffic engineering, pavement design, railway engineering basics
- **Environmental Engineering:** Water supply, sewage treatment, air pollution, noise pollution
- **Structural Engineering:** RCC & steel structures; design of beams, columns, slabs, footings
- **RCC Design:** Limit state & working stress method (IS 456)
- **Steel Design:** Tension/compression members, beams, columns, column bases (IS 800)
- **Estimation & Costing:** Rate analysis, valuation, specifications
- **Construction Management:** Project planning, CPM/PERT, contracts, tenders
- **AutoCAD basics; Building codes (NBC, IS codes)**

### 6.2 Electrical Engineering

- **Basic Concepts:** SI units, work, power, energy, conversion
- **Circuit Law:** Ohm's law, Kirchhoff's laws, nodal/mesh analysis, network theorems (Thevenin, Norton, Superposition, Maximum Power Transfer)
- **Magnetic Circuit:** MMF, flux, reluctance, hysteresis, eddy current
- **AC Fundamentals:** Single & 3-phase, phasor, power factor, resonance
- **DC Machines:** Generator & motor – construction, EMF equation, characteristics, starting, speed control
- **Transformers:** Single & 3-phase, equivalent circuit, regulation, efficiency, autotransformer, instrument transformers
- **Synchronous Machines:** Alternator & motor – construction, EMF, regulation, parallel operation, V-curves
- **Induction Motors:** 3-phase & 1-phase, equivalent circuit, starting methods, speed control
- **Generation, Transmission & Distribution:** Power plants, line parameters, sag, insulation, corona, HVDC, underground cables, distribution systems, substations
- **Switchgear & Protection:** Circuit breakers, relays, fuses, protection schemes
- **Estimation & Costing:** Wiring, materials, lighting
- **Utilization:** Electric heating, welding, traction, illumination, drives
- **Basic Electronics:** Diodes, transistors, amplifiers, oscillators, OP-AMP, digital electronics (logic gates, flip-flops)
- **Power Electronics:** SCR, MOSFET, IGBT, converters, inverters
- **Microprocessors & PLC basics**

### 6.3 Mechanical Engineering

- **Engineering Mechanics:** Equilibrium, friction, kinematics of particles/rigid bodies
- **Strength of Materials:** Stress, strain, SFD, BMD, deflection, columns, springs
- **Theory of Machines:** Kinematic pairs, mechanisms, cams, gears, flywheel, governors, balancing
- **Vibrations & Machine Design:** Design of shafts, keys, couplings, bearings, brakes, clutches
- **Thermodynamics:** Laws of thermodynamics, ideal gases, cycles (Otto, Diesel, Rankine, Brayton), entropy
- **Heat Transfer:** Conduction, convection, radiation, heat exchangers
- **Fluid Mechanics & Machinery:** Properties, fluid statics, kinematics, dynamics, Bernoulli's equation, turbines, pumps
- **Production Engineering:** Casting, forming, machining, welding, metrology, casting defects
- **Engineering Materials:** Ferrous, non-ferrous, alloys, heat treatment, composites
- **Industrial Engineering:** Work study, ergonomics, quality control, TQM
- **Power Plant Engineering:** Steam, gas, diesel, nuclear, renewable (solar, wind, hydro)
- **IC Engines:** Combustion, performance, emissions
- **Refrigeration & Air Conditioning:** Vapour compression, absorption systems, psychrometry
- **CAD/CAM/CIM basics**

### 6.4 Information Technology (IT) – For JE (IT) & CMA branches

- **Operating Systems:** Process, thread, memory, file system (Linux, Windows)
- **DBMS:** ER model, relational algebra, SQL, normalization, transactions
- **Computer Networks:** OSI/TCP-IP, routing, switching, HTTP/DNS/DHCP, security
- **Software Engineering:** SDLC, Agile, testing, UML, design patterns
- **Data Structures:** Arrays, linked lists, stacks, queues, trees, graphs, sorting & searching
- **Algorithms:** Time/space complexity, greedy, dynamic programming
- **Programming:** C, C++, Java (OOP concepts, inheritance, polymorphism, exception handling)
- **Web Technologies:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP basics, REST APIs
- **Information Security:** Cryptography, encryption, hashing, attacks, firewalls
- **Computer Organization & Architecture:** ALU, CPU, memory hierarchy, I/O
- **Discrete Mathematics:** Logic, sets, relations, functions, graph theory
- **Cloud Computing, IoT, AI/ML basics**

## 11. Key Tips for Aspirants

1. **CBT-1 Strategy:** Focus on Mathematics & Reasoning (55 Qs) to clear sectional cut-offs comfortably. Practice previous year papers (2015, 2018, 2019, 2024).
2. **CBT-2 Focus:** Technical section carries ~75% weightage (100/150). Master the relevant engineering discipline textbook.
3. **Time Management:** Allocate first 30 min to General Awareness & Science (easier scoring); dedicate remaining time to technical.
4. **Mock Tests:** Take at least 50+ full-length mocks before exam. Use the **official RRB mock test** link to familiarise with the interface.
5. **Cut-off Awareness:** Previous UR cut-offs for CBT-1 hover around 55-65 / 100 for JE; CBT-2 cut-offs ~90-110 / 150.
6. **Document Readiness:** Keep Degree/Diploma certificate, marksheets, caste certificate (if applicable), PwBD certificate, photo & signature ready before DV.
7. **Age & Qualification:** Ensure diploma/degree completion certificate is dated on or before the application closing date.
8. **Negative Marking:** Avoid blind guessing; only attempt when confident (>50% sure).
