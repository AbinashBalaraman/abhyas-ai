# RRB Paramedical Technical Categories Examination

## 1. Overview

| Particular | Details |
|---|---|
| **Full Name** | Railway Recruitment Board Paramedical Technical Categories Examination |
| **Conducting Body** | Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) under the Ministry of Railways, Government of India |
| **Frequency** | Periodic (typically once in 1–2 years based on vacancy) |
| **Mode of Exam** | Online Computer-Based Test (CBT) followed by Document Verification & Medical Examination |
| **Selection Process** | Single Stage CBT (100 Marks) → Document Verification (DV) → Medical Examination (ME) |
| **Negative Marking** | **1/3 mark** deducted for each wrong answer |
| **Official Website** | https://www.rrbcdg.gov.in/ (and other regional RRB portals) |

---

## 2. Key Posts Offered & Qualifications

Recruitment covers a wide range of specialized medical and technical support staff:

*   **Staff Nurse:**
    *   *Qualification:* B.Sc. Nursing from a recognized institution **OR** General Nursing and Midwifery (GNM) certificate with registration in the Indian/State Nursing Council.
*   **Pharmacist (Grade III):**
    *   *Qualification:* 10+2 in Science + Diploma/Degree in Pharmacy (D.Pharm / B.Pharm) with registration as a Pharmacist under the Pharmacy Act.
*   **Lab Superintendent / Lab Assistant:**
    *   *Qualification:* B.Sc. in Medical Laboratory Technology (BMLT) **OR** B.Sc. in Science + Diploma in Medical Laboratory Technology (DMLT).
*   **Radiographer / X-Ray Technician:**
    *   *Qualification:* 10+2 in Science + Diploma/Certificate in Radiography / Science Graduate with Diploma in Radiography.
*   **Health & Malaria Inspector (Grade III):**
    *   *Qualification:* B.Sc. in Chemistry / Life Sciences + One-year Diploma in Sanitary Inspector / Health Inspector.

## 3. Exam Pattern (Single CBT)

*The Computer-Based Test consists of 100 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs).*

| Section | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---|:---:|:---:|---|

| General Awareness | 10 | 10 | |
| General Arithmetic | 10 | 10 | |
| General Intelligence & Reasoning | 10 | 10 | |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** | **90 Minutes** |

## 4. Detailed Syllabus

### 4.1 Professional Ability (70 Marks - Post-wise)

#### A. Staff Nurse
*   **Anatomy & Physiology:** Skeletal system, muscular system, circulatory, respiratory, digestive, nervous, excretory, endocrine, and reproductive systems.
*   **Nutrition & Biochemistry:** Balanced diet, macronutrients, vitamins, mineral deficiencies, metabolic pathways.
*   **Microbiology:** Bacterial classification, viral replication, sterilization methods, clinical pathology, immunology.
*   **Nursing Foundations:** Nursing principles, vital signs checking, drug administration, patient hygiene, first aid.
*   **Medical-Surgical Nursing:** Common diseases of organ systems, critical care nursing, oncology, perioperative nursing.
*   **Community Health Nursing:** Epidemiology, health indicators, national health programs, vaccination schedule.
*   **Midwifery & Obstetrical Nursing:** Pregnancy stages, labor management, newborn care, complications in pregnancy.

#### B. Pharmacist
*   **Pharmaceutics:** Dosage forms (tablets, capsules, syrups, parenterals), sterilization, prescription reading, calculations.
*   **Pharmacology:** Drug classification, mechanism of action, side effects, drug interactions, pharmacokinetics (absorption, distribution, metabolism, excretion).
*   **Pharmaceutical Chemistry:** Inorganic pharmaceuticals, organic compounds structure, IUPAC naming, assay methods.
*   **Pharmacognosy:** Natural drug sources, identification of crude drugs (alkaloids, glycosides, resins).
*   **Biochemistry & Clinical Pathology:** Urine analysis, blood biochemistry, liver function tests, renal parameters.
*   **Hospital & Community Pharmacy:** Drug inventory management, patient counseling, cold chain storage.
*   **Pharmacy Jurisprudence:** Drugs and Cosmetics Act 1940, Pharmacy Act 1948, Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act.

#### C. Lab Superintendent
*   **Hematology:** Blood collection, cell counting, hemoglobin estimation, coagulation studies, blood banking (cross-matching, typing).
*   **Clinical Biochemistry:** Glucose estimation, lipid profile, cardiac markers, liver/kidney function tests, blood gas analysis.
*   **Medical Microbiology & Parasitology:** Culture media preparation, staining techniques (Gram, Ziehl-Neelsen), identification of parasites (malaria, amoeba).
*   **Histopathology & Cytology:** Tissue processing, microtomy, staining (H&E), Pap smear analysis.

---

### 4.2 Non-Technical Sections (30 Marks Combined)

#### A. General Arithmetic (10 Marks)
- Number System, Fractions, Decimals, HCF/LCM.
- Ratio and Proportion, Percentages.
- Profit & Loss, Discount, Averages.
- Simple & Compound Interest.
- Time & Work, Speed, Distance, and Time.
- Mensuration (2D & 3D).

#### B. General Intelligence & Reasoning (10 Marks)
- Analogies, Series (Numerical/Alphabetical).
- Coding-Decoding.
- Mathematical Operations, Venn Diagrams, Syllogism.
- Blood Relations, Direction Sense.
- Decision Making and puzzles.

#### C. General Awareness (10 Marks)
- **Current Affairs:** National & international news, sports, summits, awards.
- **Science & Geography:** Basic Environmental Science, space/nuclear programs, Indian geography.
- **Polity & History:** Indian Constitution, local governance, Indian freedom struggle.

## 5. Salary Structure

- **Staff Nurse:** Pay Level-7 (₹44,900 starting basic). In-hand: **₹60,000 – ₹70,000/month**.
- **Pharmacist:** Pay Level-5 (₹29,200 starting basic). In-hand: **₹40,000 – ₹48,000/month**.
- **Lab Superintendent:** Pay Level-6 (₹35,400 starting basic). In-hand: **₹48,000 – ₹55,000/month**.
- *Allowances:* DA, HRA, Transport Allowance, Laundry allowance, Nursing allowance (for nurses).
