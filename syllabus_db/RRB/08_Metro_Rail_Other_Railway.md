### 1.3 Exam Pattern

DMRC recruitment is in two Computer-Based Tests (CBT), followed by **Psycho / Aptitude Test** (for SC/TO), **Medical Fitness**, and **Document Verification**.

#### A) CBT-1 — Common for All Posts (90 min)

| Section | Questions | Marks | Negative Marking |
|---------|-----------|-------|------------------|
| General Awareness | 30 | 30 | – 1/3 |
| General Intelligence & Reasoning | 30 | 30 | – 1/3 |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 30 | 30 | – 1/3 |
| English / Hindi (bilingual) | 30 | 30 | – 1/3 |
| **Total** | **120** | **120** |  |

* CBT-1 is **qualifying**; marks are NOT counted for final merit.  
* 90 minutes (120 for PwD with scribe). 0.33 negative marking.

#### B) CBT-2 — Technical / Professional Paper (90 min)

| Post | Subjects | Q | Marks |
|------|----------|---|-------|
| JE / SE (Civil) | Civil Engg. (Structural, Geotech, Transportation, Env., Hydraulics, Survey, RCC, Steel, Construction Mgmt) | 75 | 75 |
| JE / SE (Electrical) | Power Systems, Machines, Switchgear, Measurements, Control, Power Electronics | 75 | 75 |
| JE / SE (Electronics) | Analog, Digital, Microprocessor, Signals, Communication, VLSI, Embedded | 75 | 75 |
| JE / SE (Mechanical) | Theory of Machines, Thermodynamics, Fluid Mech, Heat Transfer, Manufacturing, Industrial Engg. | 75 | 75 |
| Maintainer | ITI Trade (Electrician / Fitter / Mechanic / Refrigeration / Electronic) + Basic Engg. Science | 75 | 75 |
| SC/TO | General + Reasoning + Quant + English + Basic Engineering + Metro Operations Awareness | 75 | 75 |
| CRA | General Awareness + Reasoning + English + Customer Behaviour + Computer Awareness | 75 | 75 |
| Accounts Assistant | Accounting + Auditing + Taxation + Commercial Laws + General + Reasoning + Quant + English | 75 | 75 |
| Office Assistant | General + Reasoning + Quant + English + Hindi + Computer Awareness | 75 | 75 |
| Stenographer | Shorthand Dictation + Transcription + Typing + General | 75 | 75 |

#### C) Psycho / Aptitude Test (SC/TO only)
* Tests reaction time, spatial perception, attention, eye-hand co-ordination, multitasking, psychomotor ability. **Qualifying** (no marks).

#### D) Medical Fitness (Aye-1 / Aye-2 / Aye-3)
* **Aye-1:** Train Operator / Station Controller / Maintainer / JE. Distance vision 6/9, 6/9 (with/without glasses). No colour blindness.  
* **Aye-2:** Office-based posts. Less stringent.  
* **Aye-3:** Tracks / workshop posts.

#### E) Document Verification & Final Merit
* 100 % weightage on **CBT-2 marks** (merit list). DV + Medical + Police verification before joining.

### 1.4 Detailed Syllabus

#### (a) General Awareness
* Indian History, Geography, Polity (Constitution, Fundamental Rights/Duties, President/PM/Governor), Economy (5-yr plans, Budget basics, RBI, GDP, Inflation), Current Affairs (last 6 months).

* Static GK: Awards (Nobel, Bharat Ratna, Padma), Books & Authors, Capitals, Currencies, First in India, Important Days, UN bodies, Defence, Space (ISRO), Sports.

#### (b) General Intelligence & Reasoning
Analogies, Classification, Series (number/letter), Coding-Decoding, Blood Relations, Direction Sense, Ranking, Puzzles (seating, floor, scheduling), Syllogism, Venn Diagrams, Statement & Conclusion, Cause & Effect, Mirror/Water Images, Paper Folding/Cutting, Embedded Figures, Cubes & Dice, Calendar, Clock, Mathematical Operations, Matrix, Word formation.

#### (c) Quantitative Aptitude
Number System, HCF/LCM, Simplification, Approximation, Surds & Indices, Percentage, Profit & Loss, Discount, SI/CI, Ratio & Proportion, Partnership, Average, Ages, Time & Work, Pipe & Cistern, Time-Speed-Distance, Trains, Boats & Streams, Allegation, Mixture, Mensuration (2D/3D), Geometry (Triangles, Circles, Quadrilaterals), Trigonometry (basic), Height & Distance, Probability, Permutation & Combination, Data Interpretation (Table, Pie, Bar, Line, Caselet), Algebra (linear, quadratic, identity), Statistics (Mean, Median, Mode, Variance).

#### (d) English / Hindi
* **English:** Reading Comprehension, Cloze Test, Fillers, Error Spotting, Sentence Correction, Para Jumbles, Vocabulary (Synonyms/Antonyms, One-word substitution, Idioms & Phrases), Active-Passive, Direct-Indirect, Tenses, Articles, Prepositions.  
* **Hindi:** संधि, समास, उपसर्ग, प्रत्यय, पर्यायवाची, विलोम, अनेकार्थक, मुहावरे-लोकोक्ति, वाक्य शुद्धि, अपठित गद्यांश, पत्र लेखन, निबंध, अंग्रेजी-हिंदी अनुवाद।

#### (e) Technical Syllabus (Summary — See Section 5.2.5 for full details)
* **(e-1) Civil:** Structural Engg. (IS 456, IS 800, Limit State, Prestressed, Concrete Tech, EQ Engg), Geotechnical (Soil Mechanics, Foundations), Transportation (Highway, Railway, Airport, Tunnel, Pavement), Environmental (Water Supply, Sewerage, Air, Noise, SWM), Hydraulics & Hydrology, Surveying, Construction Mgmt, Irrigation.  
* **(e-2) Electrical:** Power Systems (Generation, Transmission, Distribution, Protection, Switchgear, Stability, Fault Analysis, HVDC, FACTS, Indian Electricity Rules), Electrical Machines (DC, Transformer, Induction, Synchronous, Special), Power Electronics (Rectifiers, Choppers, Inverters, Cycloconverter, PWM, Drives), Control Systems (TF, Bode, Nyquist, PID, State-Space), Measurements & Instrumentation (Bridges, Transducers, CRO, CT/PT), Renewable Energy, Traction (25 kV AC OHE, 750 V DC third rail).  
* **(e-3) Electronics:** Electronic Devices & Circuits, Analog Electronics (BJT, FET, Op-amp, oscillators, 555, PLL, VCO, filters), Digital Electronics (Logic gates, combinational & sequential circuits, FSM, PLD, FPGA, VHDL/Verilog), Signals & Systems, Microprocessors (8085, 8086) & Microcontrollers (8051, ARM, Arduino, ESP32, Raspberry Pi), Communication (AM/FM/PM, PCM, ASK/FSK/PSK/QAM, OFDM, GSM, LTE, 5G NR, Optical Fibre, Satellite, Mobile), Networks (OSI, TCP/IP, IPv4/IPv6, Cryptography), VLSI & Embedded, Antenna & Microwave, Signal Processing.  
* **(e-4) Mechanical:** Theory of Machines, Strength of Materials, Design of Machine Elements, Thermodynamics, Fluid Mechanics & Machines, Heat Transfer, Manufacturing Technology, Industrial Engineering & Management, Refrigeration & AC, IC Engines, Power Plant Engineering, Engineering Materials, Mechatronics & Robotics, Auto / Vehicle Engineering.  
* **(e-5) Maintainer (ITI Trades):** Electrician, Fitter, Electronic Mechanic, Mechanic (Diesel/MV/RAC/Tractor), Mason, Carpenter, Painter, Plumber — trade theory + safety + measurements + materials.

#### (f) SC/TO — Aptitude & Reasoning Specific
* Spatial Visualization, Visual Memory, Figure Classification, Decision Making, Problem Solving, Visual Comparison, Speed, Attention, Reaction Time, Multitasking.  
* **Basic Metro Operations:** Signalling (ATP, ATC, ATO, ATS, CBTC), Traction (25 kV AC OHE, 750 V DC third rail), OHE maintenance, Permanent Way (Track, ballast, sleepers, rail, fastenings, points & crossings), Tunnels, Viaducts, Stations, OCC, CCOE, Rolling Stock (Bogies, traction motor, brake, door, ventilation, PIS, PA, CCTV), Safety rules, First Aid, Fire fighting, Emergency Evacuation.

### 1.5 Selection Process Flow
Notification → Online Application → Admit Card → CBT-1 (qualifying) → CBT-2 (merit) → Psycho Test (SC/TO) → Medical Fitness → Document Verification → Police Verification → Final Merit List → Appointment Letter → Induction Training at DMRA (Shastri Park) → Posting.

### 1.6 DMRC Salary, Perks & Career Growth
* Basic + DA + HRA + CCA + Medical + LTC + PF + Gratuity + PRP + Metro Travel Pass.  
* **Career (Executive):** JE → Section Engineer → Assistant Manager → Manager → Senior Manager → AGM → GM → Director.  
* **Career (Non-Executive):** Maintainer → Senior Maintainer → JE → Section Engineer → Assistant Manager.  
* **Promotion:** LDCE (Limited Departmental Competitive Exam), Seniority-cum-fitness, GATE-out recruitment, Senior Management selection.

### 1.7 DMRC Preparation Strategy
* **CBT-1:** 30 days of Standard General Knowledge Compendium + RS Aggarwal Reasoning + RS Aggarwal / Fast Track Objective Arithmetic + Plinth to Paramount (English) + 2 months current affairs.  
* **CBT-2:** Made Easy / GATE Academy / Kreatryx + 10-year DMRC previous papers + RRB JE previous papers.  
* **Psycho Test:** Practice reaction-time apps, Stroop test, 2D rotation puzzles.  
* **Mock Tests:** Testbook, Gradeup (BYJU's Exam Prep), Oliveboard DMRC test series.
