# RPF (Railway Protection Force) - Constable & Sub-Inspector (SI) Examination Guide

**Conducting Body:** Railway Protection Force (RPF) / Ministry of Railways, Government of India
**Governing Acts:** RPF Act, 1957; Railway Property (Unlawful Possession) Act, 1966
**Headquarters:** New Delhi
**Current DG:** Sonali Mishra (IPS)
**Workforce:** ~75,000 active personnel
**Motto:** यशो लभस्व (Attain Honour)

---

## 1. RPF CONSTABLE

### Posts Offered
**Constable (Executive)** in Railway Protection Force — responsible for protection of railway property, passenger security, and enforcement of the Railway Property (Unlawful Possession) Act 1966. Recruited across 18 railway zones.

### Eligibility
| Criteria | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Educational Qualification | **10th Pass (Matriculation)** from a recognized board |
| Age Limit | **18 to 25 years** (as on cut-off date) |
| Age Relaxation | SC/ST: 5 years; OBC: 3 years; Ex-Servicemen: as per rules |
| Nationality | Indian Citizen |
| Medical Fitness | Physically and mentally fit as per RPF standards |

### Exam Pattern

#### Computer-Based Test (CBT) — Stage 1
- **Total Questions:** 120
- **Total Marks:** 120
- **Duration:** 90 minutes
- **Negative Marking:** 1/3 mark per wrong answer
- **Language:** 15 languages including English, Hindi, and regional
- **Difficulty Level:** 10th Standard

| Section | Questions | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| General Awareness | 50 | 50 |
| Arithmetic | 35 | 35 |
| General Intelligence & Reasoning | 35 | 35 |
| **Total** | **120** | **120** |

#### Physical Efficiency Test (PET) & Physical Measurement Test (PMT) — Stage 2
Qualifying in nature. Candidates must qualify to be eligible for Document Verification.

#### Document Verification — Stage 3
Original certificates, identity proof, and category documents verified.

### Detailed Syllabus

#### General Awareness (50 Qs)
- **Indian History:** Ancient, Medieval, Modern India; Freedom Struggle
- **Art & Culture:** Visual Arts, Performing Arts, Literature, Music, Dance, Festivals
- **Geography:** Physical Geography, World Geography, Environmental Issues
- **Indian Polity & Constitution:** Preamble, Fundamental Rights/Duties, Directive Principles, Amendments
- **Economics:** Macro & Microeconomics, Economic Reforms, Poverty, Unemployment
- **Physics, Chemistry, Biology:** General Science up to 10th standard
- **Computer & Technology:** Inventions, Space, Astronomy
- **Sports:** National/International Events, Famous Personalities
- **Current Affairs:** National, International, Awards, Appointments, Important Days
- **Indian Railways:** History, Achievements, Current Developments

#### Arithmetic (35 Qs)
- **Number System:** Integers, Fractions, Decimals, Simplification, LCM/GCD
- **Percentages:** Basic calculation, Increase/Decrease, Applications
- **Ratio & Proportion:** Simple & Compound Ratios
- **Average:** Simple, Weighted, Mean, Median, Mode
- **Interest:** Simple Interest, Compound Interest
- **Profit, Loss & Discount:** CP, SP, Marked Price
- **Time & Work:** Work Efficiency, Wages, Pipes & Cisterns
- **Time & Distance:** Speed, Relative Speed, Trains & Boats
- **Mensuration:** Area, Perimeter, Volume, Surface Area
- **Algebra:** Basic Expressions, Linear Equations
- **Data Interpretation:** Tables, Bar/Line Graphs, Pie Charts

#### General Intelligence & Reasoning (35 Qs)
- **Analogies:** Word & Number Analogies
- **Series:** Number Series, Non-Verbal Series
- **Coding-Decoding:** Patterns, Message Deciphering
- **Blood Relations**
- **Direction Sense:** Maps, Spatial Relationships
- **Venn Diagram**
- **Syllogism:** Evaluation, Logical Conclusions
- **Statement & Conclusion:** Drawing Inferences, Logical Deduction
- **Puzzles:** Seating Arrangement, Classification
- **Data Sufficiency**
- **Spatial Visualization, Orientation, Visual Memory**

## 2. RPF SUB-INSPECTOR (SI)

### Posts Offered
**Sub-Inspector (Executive)** in Railway Protection Force — supervisory rank with greater investigative powers, leadership in operations, and authority to exercise powers under NDPS Act (2019). Non-Gazetted officer rank with promotion path to Inspector and above.

### Eligibility
| Criteria | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Educational Qualification | **Bachelor's Degree** from a recognized university |
| Age Limit | **20 to 25 years** (as on cut-off date) |
| Age Relaxation | SC/ST: 5 years; OBC: 3 years; Ex-Servicemen: as per rules |
| Nationality | Indian Citizen |
| Medical Fitness | Physically and mentally fit |

### Exam Pattern

#### Computer-Based Test (CBT) — Stage 1
- **Total Questions:** 160
- **Total Marks:** 160
- **Duration:** 120 minutes
- **Negative Marking:** 1/3 mark per wrong answer
- **Language:** English, Hindi, and regional languages
- **Difficulty Level:** Graduation Level

| Section | Questions | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| General Awareness | 50 | 50 |
| Arithmetic | 35 | 35 |
| General Intelligence & Reasoning | 35 | 35 |
| English (Language & Comprehension) | 40 | 40 |
| **Total** | **160** | **160** |

#### Physical Measurement Test (PMT) & Physical Efficiency Test (PET) — Stage 2
Qualifying in nature.

#### Document Verification — Stage 3
Original certificates verified.

### Detailed Syllabus (similar to Constable + English)

#### General Awareness (50 Qs) — Same as Constable
- Indian History, Art & Culture, Geography, Polity, Economics, Science, Current Affairs, Indian Railways

#### Arithmetic (35 Qs) — Same as Constable
- Number System, Percentages, Ratio & Proportion, Average, Interest, Profit/Loss, Time & Work, Time & Distance, Mensuration, Algebra, Geometry, Data Interpretation

#### General Intelligence & Reasoning (35 Qs) — Same as Constable
- Analogies, Series, Coding-Decoding, Blood Relations, Direction, Venn Diagram, Syllogism, Statement & Conclusion, Puzzles, Data Sufficiency

#### English (40 Qs)
- **Vocabulary:** Synonyms, Antonyms, One-word Substitution
- **Grammar:** Spotting Errors, Sentence Correction, Tenses, Active/Passive Voice
- **Idioms & Phrases**
- **Comprehension:** Reading Passages
- **Cloze Test / Fill in the blanks**
- **Sentence Rearrangement / Para Jumbles**

## 3. RPF — KEY DIFFERENCES (Constable vs SI)

| Parameter | RPF Constable | RPF SI |
|---|---|---|
| Education | 10th Pass | Bachelor's Degree |
| Age | 18–25 years | 20–25 years |
| Total Qs | 120 | 160 |
| Total Marks | 120 | 160 |
| Duration | 90 minutes | 120 minutes |
| Sections | 3 (GA, Arith, GI&R) | 4 (+ English) |
| Pay Level | Level 3 | Level 6 |
| Pay Scale | ₹21,700–69,100 | ₹35,400–1,12,400 |
| Rank | Non-gazetted (Constable) | Non-gazetted (SI) |
| Promotion Path | HC → ASI → SI | Inspector → Asst. Commandant |

## 4. RPF — RECRUITMENT PROCESS (Common to Both)

1. **Notification** released by RPF/Ministry of Railways (RRB conducts CBT)
2. **Online Application** via RRB portal
3. **Computer-Based Test (CBT)**
4. **Physical Measurement Test (PMT)** — qualifying
5. **Physical Efficiency Test (PET)** — qualifying
6. **Document Verification (DV)**
7. **Medical Examination**
8. **Final Merit List** & Appointment

## 7. PREPARATION STRATEGY

### Recommended Study Plan (3–4 Months)
| Week | Focus Area | Hours/Day |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | GA: History, Polity, Constitution basics | 2 hrs |
| 3–4 | Arithmetic: Number System, Percentages, Ratio | 2–3 hrs |
| 5–6 | Reasoning: Analogies, Series, Coding | 2 hrs |
| 7–8 | GA: Geography, Economics, Science | 2 hrs |
| 9–10 | Arithmetic: Profit/Loss, SI/CI, Mensuration | 2–3 hrs |
| 11–12 | Reasoning: Puzzles, Syllogism, Blood Relations | 2 hrs |
| 13–14 | Full revision + Mock tests (weekly) | 3 hrs |
| 15–16 | Previous year papers + time management | 3–4 hrs |

### Daily Routine
- **Morning:** 1 hr Physical Training (for PET)
- **Afternoon:** 2–3 hrs Subject Study
- **Evening:** 1 hr Current Affairs + Revision
- **Night:** 1 hr Mock Test / Practice Questions

### Physical Preparation
- Start running 5 km/day at least 2 months before exam
- Practice long jump and high jump
- Maintain proper diet and sleep

## 8. CAREER PATH & PROSPECTS

### Rank Structure (Non-Gazetted)
**Constable → Head Constable → ASI → SI → Inspector**

### Promotions
- **Time-bound promotions** available
- **Departmental exams** for career advancement
- **Opportunity** to become Gazetted Officer (Assistant Commandant/ASC) via UPSC IRPFS

### Job Profile
- Security of railway property and passengers
- Anti-terrorism operations
- Rescue operations (Nanhe Farishtey — child rescue)
- Cyber crime investigation
- VIP security duties
- Disaster response

### Benefits
- **Government accommodation** (Railway quarters)
- **Railway passes** for free travel
- **Medical facilities** for self and family
- **Pension** under NPS (after 7th CPC)
- **Insurance** under RPF welfare schemes
- **Canteen facilities** (CPC canteens)
