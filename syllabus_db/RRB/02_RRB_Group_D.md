# RRB Group D (Level 1 Posts) - Complete Examination Guide

## 1. Overview

| Particular | Details |
|------------|---------|
| **Full Name** | Railway Recruitment Board - Group D (Level 1 Posts) |
| **Conducting Body** | Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) - 21 RRBs across India under Ministry of Railways, Government of India |
| **Purpose** | Recruitment of eligible Indian citizens to various Level 1 posts (formerly Group D) in Indian Railways across multiple departments (Track, Mechanical, Electrical, S&T, etc.) |
| **Examination Level** | 10th Standard (Matriculation) / ITI level |
| **Mode of Exam** | Computer Based Test (CBT) followed by Physical Efficiency Test (PET), Document Verification, and Medical Fitness Test |
| **Frequency** | As and when vacancies arise (no fixed annual cycle); conducted through Centralized Employment Notice (CEN) — e.g., CEN 02/2018, CEN RRC 01/2019, CEN 08/2024, CEN 09/2025 |
| **Pay Scale** | 7th CPC Pay Level 1 (Rs. 18,000 basic) — initial pay Rs. 18,000 plus DA, HRA, and other allowances |
| **Latest Notice** | CEN 09/2025 (Level-1) with Corrigendum-4 issued on 02-03-2026 (revised notified dates) |

## 2. Posts Offered (Level 1 Posts in 7th CPC Pay Matrix)

The RRB Group D / Level 1 recruitment covers various non-gazetted, non-ministerial posts in different technical and non-technical departments of Indian Railways:

### 2.1 Track & Civil Engineering Department
- **Track Maintainer Grade IV** — Inspection, maintenance, and repair of railway tracks
- **Gateman** — Operating level crossings and managing gate operations
- **Pointsman** / **Assistant Pointsman** — Operating railway signals, points/switches, and assisting in train movements

### 2.2 Mechanical Department
- **Helper / Assistant (Mechanical)** — Workshop, diesel loco shed, electric loco shed, carriage & wagon
- **Helper (C & W)** — Carriage and Wagon maintenance
- **Helper (Diesel / Electrical)** — Assisting technicians in loco sheds

### 2.3 Electrical Department
- **Helper / Assistant (Electrical)** — TRD (Traction Rolling Distribution), OHE (Overhead Equipment)
- **Helper (Electrical / Power)** — Electrical maintenance and operations
- **Helper (AC Mechanic)** — Air-conditioning and refrigeration

### 2.4 Signalling & Telecommunications (S&T) Department
- **Helper / Assistant (S&T)** — Signal and Telecommunication equipment maintenance
- **Helper (Telecom)** — Telecom equipment installation and upkeep

### 2.5 Other Level 1 Posts in Technical Departments
- **Helper (Bridge)** — Bridge inspection and maintenance
- **Helper (Workshop)** — Various workshop support roles
- **Helper (Stores)** — Stores and inventory management
- **Helper (Loco Shed)** — Loco shed operations support
- **Helper (Track Machine)** — Track machine operations
- **Assistant (Various)** — Department-specific support staff

> **Note:** Posts are allocated RRB-wise and zone-wise based on vacancies. Candidates can indicate post preferences during the application process.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

### 3.1 Educational Qualification
Candidates must possess **ANY ONE** of the following:
- **10th Pass (Matriculation)** from a recognized board (CBSE, ICSE, State Boards, or equivalent)
- **ITI (Industrial Training Institute)** certificate from a recognized institution (NCVT/SCVT) in relevant trades
- Equivalent qualification recognized by the Government of India

> Candidates appearing for the qualifying examination are NOT eligible; they must possess the certificate on the date of document verification.

### 3.2 Age Limit (As on Date of Notification)
| Category | Age Limit |
|----------|-----------|
| **General / UR** | 18 to 33 years |
| **OBC (Non-Creamy Layer)** | 18 to 36 years (3 years relaxation) |
| **SC / ST** | 18 to 38 years (5 years relaxation) |
| **PwBD (Persons with Benchmark Disabilities)** | 18 to 43 years (10 years relaxation) |
| **Ex-Servicemen** | Up to additional relaxation as per rules |
| **Candidates from J&K (1980-89)** | Up to 5 years relaxation (subject to domicile certificate) |

> Minimum age is **18 years** as on the date mentioned in the notification. No age relaxation is provided to SC/ST/OBC candidates applying against unreserved (UR) vacancies.

### 3.3 Nationality
- Citizen of India, OR
- Subject of Nepal/Bhutan, OR
- Tibetan refugee (permanently settled in India before 01-01-1962), OR
- Person of Indian origin migrated from specified countries with intent to settle permanently in India

### 3.4 Medical Fitness
- Candidates must be medically fit as per the standards prescribed for the relevant post (A-1, A-2, A-3, B-1, B-2, C-1, C-2 medical categories)
- Visual acuity and other physical standards apply as per Railway Medical Rules

## 4. Exam Pattern

### 4.1 Selection Process Stages
1. **Computer Based Test (CBT)** — Single stage qualifying + merit-based
2. **Physical Efficiency Test (PET)** — Qualifying in nature
3. **Document Verification (DV)** — Final stage
4. **Medical Fitness Test** — Post DV

### 4.2 CBT (Computer Based Test) Pattern

| Section | Subject | No. of Questions | Marks |
|---------|---------|------------------|-------|
| 1 | **Mathematics** | 25 | 25 |
| 2 | **General Intelligence & Reasoning** | 30 | 30 |
| 3 | **General Science** | 25 | 25 |
| 4 | **General Awareness on Current Affairs** | 20 | 20 |
| | **Total** | **100** | **100** |

**Key CBT Details:**
- **Duration:** 90 minutes (120 minutes for PwBD candidates with scribe)
- **Question Type:** Multiple Choice Questions (MCQ)
- **Marking Scheme:** 1 mark for each correct answer
- **Negative Marking:** 1/3 (0.33) mark deducted for each wrong answer
- **Minimum Qualifying Marks:** 40% for UR, 30% for SC/ST/OBC/PwBD (relaxation as per norms)
- **Language:** Hindi, English, and 13 regional languages (Assamese, Bengali, Gujarati, Kannada, Konkani, Malayalam, Manipuri, Marathi, Odia, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, Urdu)

> Note: There is no sectional cutoff; the overall merit is based on total CBT score normalized across sessions.

| **Time & Work** | Work efficiency, pipes & cistern, man-days, wages |
| **Time & Distance** | Speed = Distance/Time, relative speed, trains, boats & streams |
| **Simple & Compound Interest** | SI formula, CI formula (annual/half-yearly/quarterly), difference between SI & CI |
| **Profit & Loss** | CP, SP, profit %, loss %, discount, marked price |
| **Algebra** | Linear equations, quadratic equations, factorization, polynomials, identities |

| **Square Root** | Finding square root of numbers (prime factorization, long division method) |

| **Calendar & Clock** | Days calculation, leap year, clock angles, mirror & water image problems |
| **Pipes & Cistern** | Inlet/outlet pipes, tank filling problems |

## 6. Physical Efficiency Test (PET) — Qualifying Only

PET is of **qualifying nature** — candidates must pass all events; no marks are awarded for PET. Failure to complete any event results in disqualification.

### 6.1 Male Candidates
| Event | Requirement |
|-------|-------------|
| **Weight Lifting & Carrying** | Lift and carry 35 kg weight for 100 meters in 2 minutes |
| **Running** | Run 1000 meters (1 km) in 4 minutes 15 seconds |

### 6.2 Female Candidates
| Event | Requirement |
|-------|-------------|
| **Weight Lifting & Carrying** | Lift and carry 20 kg weight for 100 meters in 2 minutes |
| **Running** | Run 1000 meters (1 km) in 5 minutes 40 seconds |

> **Note:** There is **no separate PET for PwBD candidates** who are declared unfit for PET. They will be considered for alternative posts/railways as per availability and medical fitness. Ex-servicemen are exempted from PET.

## 7. Marking Scheme

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Correct Answer** | +1 mark |
| **Wrong Answer** | -1/3 mark (0.33 negative) |
| **Unattempted Question** | 0 marks (no penalty) |
| **Multiple Responses** | Marked wrong, negative marking applied |
| **Normalization** | Marks are normalized across multiple sessions using equi-percentile method |
| **Sectional Cutoff** | No sectional cutoff |
| **Overall Qualifying** | UR: 40%, OBC/SC/ST/PwBD: 30% (relaxation applicable) |

## 11. Preparation Strategy & Tips

1. **Know the Syllabus Thoroughly** — Print the official syllabus and tick topics as you cover them
2. **Focus on Reasoning (30 marks)** — Highest weightage; practice daily for 30-45 minutes
3. **NCERT Class 10 Science is Bible** — Cover Physics, Chemistry, Biology chapters from Class 10 NCERT line-by-line
4. **Daily Current Affairs** — Read 1 national newspaper + 1 monthly magazine capsule
5. **Mathematics Practice** — Shortcuts, tricks, Vedic maths; practice 20-25 questions daily
6. **Previous Year Papers** — Solve at least last 5 years' RRB Group D papers (CEN 02/2018, CEN RRC 01/2019, CEN 08/2024)
7. **Mock Tests** — Take 2-3 full-length mock tests weekly; analyze mistakes
8. **Time Management** — 90 min for 100 Qs = 54 sec/question; don't get stuck on any question
9. **Revision is Key** — Revise formulas (Maths), scientific facts (Science), GK facts weekly
10. **Physical Fitness** — Start running 1 km daily 2-3 months before PET to meet the qualifying time
11. **Avoid Negative Marking** — Don't guess blindly; only attempt when 80%+ confident
12. **Use the Language Option** — Appear in your regional language if more comfortable (better comprehension)

## 12. Key Takeaways

- **Most Popular Railway Exam** — RRB Group D attracts crores of applicants for lakhs of vacancies
- **10th Pass Eligibility** — Lowest qualification among major govt exams, hence massive competition
- **CBT Only (Single Stage)** — No multiple stages of CBT; single CBT determines merit
- **Normalization Applied** — Final score is normalized across shifts/sessions
- **Zone/Post Preference** — Choose wisely; RRB-wise, then post-wise preference
- **Three Attempts Allowed Per Cycle** — Candidates can apply for multiple RRBs
- **Reserved Category Benefits** — OBC/SC/ST have separate cutoffs, age relaxation, and fee concession
- **Career Growth** — Initial Level 1; can rise to Level 6+ (Officer rank) through departmental promotions and LDCE exams over 20-30 years of service
- **Job Security** — Central government job under Ministry of Railways; permanent, pensionable (NPS), with strong union protections
- **All India Posting Liability** — Selected candidates can be posted anywhere across India; no guarantee of home state
