## 2. Posts Offered

The RRB ALP recruitment covers the following posts:

### A. Assistant Loco Pilot (ALP)
- Operates locomotives under the supervision of a Loco Pilot (Goods/Mail/Express).
- Pay Level: 2 (7th CPC).

### B. Loco Pilot (LP) — Various Grades
- **Loco Pilot (Goods)** — operates freight trains.
- **Loco Pilot (Link)** — links between goods/passenger runs.
- **Loco Pilot (Express)** — operates express passenger trains.
- **Loco Pilot (Mail)** — operates mail/express services.
- Pay Level: 6 (7th CPC).

### C. Motor Car Driver
- **Motor Car Driver (Diesel)** — operates diesel-powered shunting/motor cars in yards.
- **Motor Car Driver (Electric)** — operates electric motor cars for shunting operations.

## 4. Exam Pattern (4 Stages)

The selection process comprises **four stages**:

```
CBT-1 (Screening)  →  CBT-2 (Merit)  →  CBAT (Aptitude)  →  DV & Medical
```

### Stage 1: CBT-1 (Screening) — Common for All Candidates
- **Purpose:** Screening/Qualifying (marks not counted in final merit)
- **Total Questions:** 75
- **Total Marks:** 75
- **Duration:** 60 minutes
- **Negative Marking:** 1/3 mark per wrong answer

| Section | Questions | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| Mathematics | 20 | 20 |
| General Intelligence & Reasoning | 25 | 25 |
| General Science (Physics, Chemistry, Life Sciences — 10th level) | 20 | 20 |
| General Awareness on Current Affairs | 10 | 10 |
| **Total** | **75** | **75** |

### Stage 2: CBT-2 (Merit) — Post-Specific
- **Purpose:** Merit-based; Part A marks counted (50% weightage) in final merit
- **Total Questions:** 175
- **Total Marks:** 175
- **Duration:** 150 minutes (Part A: 90 min + Part B: 60 min)
- **Negative Marking:** 1/3 mark per wrong answer

| Part | Section | Questions | Marks |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Part A** | Mathematics | 25 | 25 |
| | General Intelligence & Reasoning | 25 | 25 |
| | Basic Science & Engineering | 40 | 40 |
| | General Awareness on Current Affairs | 20 | 20 |
| | **Sub-Total (Part A)** | **100** | **100** |
| **Part B** | Technical Ability / Trade (post-specific) | 75 | 75 |
| | **Grand Total** | **175** | **175** |

### Stage 3: Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBAT)
- **Applicable for:** ALP & Loco Pilot posts only
- **Purpose:** Tests psychological/cognitive fitness for operating a locomotive
- **Minimum Qualifying Score:** 42 marks in each test battery
- **Weightage in Final Merit:** 50% (combined 50:50 with CBT-2 Part A)
- **Duration:** ~71 minutes

### Stage 4: Document Verification (DV) & Medical Fitness Test
- **DV:** Verification of original certificates (educational, age, category, etc.)
- **Medical:** A1-category medical standards (detailed below)

- Elementary Statistics (Mean, Median, Mode)
- Data Interpretation (Tables, Bar Graphs, Pie Charts)
- Mixture & Allegation

### B. General Intelligence & Reasoning (25 Questions)

- Statements, Arguments, Assumptions, Conclusions

- Non-Verbal Reasoning (Mirror/Water images, Paper folding, Embedded figures)

### C. General Science (20 Questions — 10th Level)

- Prominent Personalities (National/International)

## 7. CBAT (Computer Based Aptitude Test)

The CBAT consists of **five test batteries** designed to assess locomotive-operating aptitude. Each battery requires a **minimum score of 42 marks** to qualify.

| # | Battery | What It Tests |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Memory Test** | Short-term memory, recall of visual/auditory patterns |
| 2 | **Following Directions** | Ability to follow multi-step instructions precisely under time pressure |
| 3 | **Depth Perception** | Judging distance, speed, and spatial relationships (critical for signalling) |
| 4 | **Concentration** | Sustained attention over prolonged periods |
| 5 | **Reasoning / Planning** | Logical reasoning, planning routes, decision-making under simulated locomotive scenarios |

**Note:** CBAT is **not a knowledge-based test**; it assesses innate psychological/cognitive abilities. No prior preparation can "crack" it, but mock tests help acclimatize candidates to the interface.

## 8. Medical Standards (A1 Category)

Selected candidates must meet **A1 medical fitness standards** prescribed by Indian Railways:

### A. Visual Standards
| Parameter | Standard |
|---|---|
| Distance Vision | 6/6, 6/6 (both eyes, with or without correction) |
| Near Vision | Snellen 0.6, 0.6 (both eyes) |
| Colour Vision | Must be **Normal** (no colour blindness) — tested by Ishihara test |
| Field of Vision | Full — no field defect |
| Night Blindness | Must not be present |
| Squint | No squint allowed |
| Spectacles | Permitted; limits on power as per latest RRB instructions |

### B. Hearing Standards
- **Good hearing** in both ears (no conductive or mixed hearing loss)
- Whispered voice test must be passed at specified distance

### C. Physical Fitness
| Parameter | Standard |
|---|---|
| Height | Minimum 152 cm (relaxable for certain categories) |
| Chest | Minimum 81 cm (with 5 cm expansion) — for males |
| Weight | Proportionate to height (BMI acceptable range) |
| Speech | Clear, no major stammering |
| General Health | Free from cardiovascular, respiratory, neurological diseases; no hernia; no varicose veins |

### D. Disqualifying Conditions
- Colour blindness (any type)
- Night blindness
- Squint / strabismus
- Severe refractive errors beyond limit
- Heart disease, hypertension (uncontrolled)
- Epilepsy, mental disorders
- Active tuberculosis
- Major musculoskeletal deformities
- Hearing loss beyond prescribed limits

## 10. Salary Structure

Pay is governed by the **7th Central Pay Commission (7th CPC)** with applicable Dearness Allowance (DA), House Rent Allowance (HRA), and other allowances.

### A. Assistant Loco Pilot (ALP)
| Component | Details |
|---|---|
| **Pay Level** | Level 2 |
| **Pay Band (Old)** | PB-1: ₹5,200 – ₹20,200 |
| **Grade Pay (Old)** | ₹1,900 |
| **Pay Matrix (7th CPC)** | **₹19,900 – ₹32,400** (entry basic ₹19,900) |
| **Approximate In-hand Salary** | ₹30,000 – ₹35,000 per month (basic + DA + allowances, varies by location) |
| **Allowances** | DA (revised quarterly), HRA, Transport Allowance, Running Allowance, Night Duty Allowance, Medical |

### B. Loco Pilot (Goods / Link / Express / Mail)
| Component | Details |
|---|---|
| **Pay Level** | Level 6 |
| **Pay Band (Old)** | PB-2: ₹9,300 – ₹34,800 |
