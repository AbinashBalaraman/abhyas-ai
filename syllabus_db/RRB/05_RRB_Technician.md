# RRB Technician Examination - Comprehensive Guide

## 1. Overview

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Full Name** | Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) Technician Recruitment Examination |
| **Conducting Body** | Railway Recruitment Boards (under Ministry of Railways, Government of India) |
| **Posts** | Technician Grade I (Signal) and Technician Grade III |
| **Recurring Notice** | Centralized Employment Notice (CEN) – published as CEN 02/2024, CEN 02/2025, etc. |
| **Frequency** | Approximately every 1–2 years (based on vacancy requirement) |
| **Latest Cycle** | CEN 02/2025 (Technician-1 & Technician-3) – CBT completed; DV in progress as of June 2026 |
| **Total Vacancies (Latest)** | 6,565 posts |
| **Application Portal** | https://www.rrbapply.gov.in/ |
| **Selection Stages** | Computer-Based Test (CBT) → Document Verification (DV) → Medical Examination (ME) |

The RRB Technician exam recruits technical staff for various trades in the Indian Railways across 21 Railway Recruitment Boards. The exam is held in Computer-Based Test (CBT) mode in 15 languages (Hindi, English, and 13 regional languages).

## 2. Posts Offered

### 2.1 Technician Grade I Signal (Higher Grade)

| Parameter | Details |
|---|---|
| **Pay Level** | Level 5 (7th CPC) |
| **Initial Pay** | ₹29,200 (Pay Band ₹5,200–20,200 + GP ₹2,800 pre-revised) |
| **Approx. Gross Salary** | ₹35,000–₹40,000 per month (including DA, HRA, allowances) |
| **Role** | Installation, maintenance, and troubleshooting of railway signalling systems, telecommunications, and electronic interlockings |
| **Posting** | Open-line, construction, or RE (Research Engineering) units |

**Eligibility Pathways (any one):**
- B.Sc. in Physics / Electronics / Computer Science / Information Technology (OR)
- 3-year Diploma in Engineering in relevant stream (Electrical, Electronics, Instrumentation, IT) (OR)
- B.E. / B.Tech in relevant discipline (OR)
- B.Sc. (Hons.) in relevant subjects with Physics and Maths

### 2.2 Technician Grade III (Various Trades)

- Turner

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

### 3.1 Age Limit (as on the cut-off date in the CEN)

| Post | Minimum Age | Maximum Age | Age Relaxation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Technician Grade I Signal** | 18 years | 30 years | OBC-NCL: +3 yrs; SC/ST: +5 yrs; PwBD: +10 yrs; Ex-Servicemen as per rules |
| **Technician Grade III** | 18 years | 33 years | OBC-NCL: +3 yrs; SC/ST: +5 yrs; PwBD: +10 yrs; Ex-Servicemen as per rules |

### 3.2 Educational Qualification (Detailed)

#### Grade I Signal — Acceptable Qualifications
- **B.Sc. (Physics / Electronics / Computer Science / IT)** from a recognized university
- **3-year Engineering Diploma** in any of: Electrical, Electronics, Instrumentation, Communication, Computer, IT
- **B.E. / B.Tech** in relevant engineering discipline
- **B.Sc. (Hons.)** with Physics, Maths, and one of Electronics/Computer Science/IT as a subject

#### Grade III — Acceptable Qualifications (Trade-wise)
| Trade | Required Qualification |
|---|---|
| Electrician / Wireman / Winder (Armature) / Refrigeration & AC Mechanic | 10th + ITI in relevant trade OR 10th + Diploma |
| Fitter / Turner / Machinist / Mechanic Diesel / Mechanic Motor Vehicle / Heat Engine / Millwright | 10th + ITI in relevant trade OR 10th + Diploma in Mechanical/Automobile |
| Electronics Mechanic / Mechanic Radio & TV / Instrument Mechanic | 10th + ITI in relevant trade OR 10th + Diploma in Electronics |
| Tractor Mechanic | 10th + ITI in Tractor Mechanic trade |

### 3.3 Nationality
Indian citizen (subject to eligibility certificates for other categories as per Government of India rules).

### 3.4 Medical Fitness
Candidates must be in good mental and bodily health and free from any physical defect likely to interfere with discharge of duties. Medical standards are classified (A1, A2, A3, B1, B2, C1, C2) per Railway Medical Manual.

### 4.3 Selection Process Stages

1. **Computer-Based Test (CBT)** — Single-stage objective test (100 MCQs, 100 marks, 90 minutes)
2. **Document Verification (DV)** — Shortlisted candidates (1:1 ratio, approximately) called for original certificate verification
3. **Medical Fitness Test (ME)** — Final medical examination by Railway Administration doctors at designated Railway Hospitals (e.g., Ambala Cantt, Delhi)

> **Note:** There is NO skill test / trade test / typing test in RRB Technician exam. Final merit is based on CBT marks + DV clearance + Medical fitness.

### 4.4 Minimum Qualifying Marks (CBT)

| Category | Minimum Marks (%) |
|---|---|
| Unreserved (UR) | 40% |
| OBC-NCL / SC | 30% |
| ST | 25% |

(Note: PwBD candidates also follow category-based cut-offs.)

### 4.5 Medium of Examination
Bilingual — Question paper available in **Hindi, English, and 13 regional languages** (Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi, Bengali, Odia, Assamese, Manipuri, Konkani, Urdu).

## 5. Detailed Syllabus (CBT — Common Section)

### 5.1 Mathematics (25 Qs — Grade III; 20 Qs — Grade I)

- **Number System** — Integers, Rational & Irrational numbers, Real numbers
- **BODMAS** rule and simplification
- **Decimals and Fractions** — Conversion, operations
- **LCM and HCF** — Calculations, applications
- **Ratio and Proportion** — Direct/indirect proportions, mixtures
- **Percentages, Profit and Loss** — Discount, successive discount
- **Simple Interest and Compound Interest**
- **Mensuration** — Areas (square, rectangle, triangle, circle, trapezium), Volumes (cube, cuboid, cylinder, cone, sphere)
- **Time and Work** — Work rates, pipelines
- **Time and Distance** — Speed, relative speed, trains
- **Algebra** — Linear equations, quadratic equations, polynomials, identities
- **Arithmetic Progression** and series
- **Geometry** — Triangles (similarity, congruence, Pythagoras theorem), Circles (tangents, chords), Quadrilaterals
- **Trigonometry** — Ratios, identities, heights and distances
- **Coordinate Geometry** — Distance formula, section formula
- **Statistics** — Mean, median, mode, range, mean deviation, variance, standard deviation (grouped & ungrouped)
- **Probability** — Occurrence of events, exhaustive events, mutually exclusive events
- **Pipes and Cisterns**
- **Age Calculations**
- **Calendar and Clock** — Hands positions, leap year, day calculations
- **Square Root** — Estimation methods
- **Sets** — Representation, union, intersection, complement, Venn diagrams

### 5.2 General Intelligence & Reasoning (25 Qs — Grade III; 15 Qs — Grade I)

- **Analogies** — Word, number, letter analogies
- **Alphabetical and Number Series** — Completion of series
- **Coding and Decoding** — Letter shifts, number codes, condition-based
- **Mathematical Operations** — Symbolic operations, simplification
- **Similarities and Differences** — Pattern recognition
- **Relationships** — Blood relations, family tree
- **Analytical Reasoning** — Seating arrangements, ranking
- **Syllogism** — Logical conclusions, venn-diagram based
- **Jumbling** — Sentence/word rearrangement
- **Venn Diagrams** — Set operations
- **Puzzle** — Cross-referential reasoning
- **Data Sufficiency and Interpretation** — Charts, tables, graphs
- **Statement–Conclusion / Statement–Assumption / Statement–Arguments**
- **Decision Making** — Course-of-action problems
- **Maps** — Direction, distance, location
- **Interpretation of Graphs** — Bar, line, pie charts
- **Classification** — Odd one out
- **Directions** — Cardinal/inter-cardinal points, shadows

- Atomic structure

- Chemical bonding
- Acids, bases, salts
- Metals and non-metals

#### Life Sciences (Biology)

- Diversity in organisms
- Nutrition and digestion
- Respiration
- Transportation (blood, circulation)
- Excretion
- Control and coordination
- Reproduction
- Health and hygiene
- Ecology and environment

### 5.4 General Awareness (10–20 Qs — Current Affairs focused)

- **Current Affairs** — National and International, last 6–12 months
- **Science & Technology** — Recent developments, space missions
- **Sports** — Tournaments, awards, records
- **Culture** — Indian heritage, dance, music, festivals
- **Personalities** — Awards, achievers

- **Politics / Indian Polity** — Constitution, Parliament, governance
- **History** — Indian history, freedom struggle

## 6. Trade-Specific Skills Required (for Grade III)

### 6.1 Electrician
- Wiring (house, industrial, conduit, casing-capping)
- AC/DC circuits, series, parallel connections
- Transformers (single-phase, three-phase)
- Generators (AC, DC) and Motors (induction, synchronous)
- Electrical safety, earthing, insulation testing
- Circuit breakers, fuses, relays
- Power factor, energy meters, instruments

### 6.2 Fitter
- Fitting operations (marking, measuring, cutting, filing, drilling)
- Sheet metal work (cutting, bending, joining)
- Drilling, grinding, cutting operations
- Threading (internal/external), tapping
- Assembly and alignment of mechanical components
- Use of hand tools, measuring instruments

### 6.3 Mechanic (Diesel)
- Diesel engine construction (2-stroke, 4-stroke)
- Components — cylinder block, piston, crankshaft, camshaft, valves
- Fuel injection system, governor
- Cooling, lubrication, air intake systems
- Engine maintenance, repair, overhauling
- Troubleshooting, engine testing

### 6.4 Mechanic (Motor Vehicle)
- IC engine construction and working
- Transmission system (clutch, gearbox, propeller shaft, differential)
- Brake system (hydraulic, mechanical, air)
- Steering system (Ackermann, power steering)
- Suspension system
- Electrical system (battery, starter, ignition)
- Vehicle maintenance and diagnostics

### 6.5 Electronics Mechanic
- Electronic components — resistor, capacitor, inductor, diode, transistor
- Circuits — rectifiers, filters, amplifiers, oscillators
- Transistors — biasing, characteristics
- ICs (linear and digital) — op-amps, timers (555), logic gates
- Microcontrollers and microprocessors — basics, programming
- PCB design, soldering, testing
- Signal generation, measurement, CRO usage

### 6.6 Wireman
- Domestic and industrial wiring (single-phase, three-phase)
- Wiring systems — cleat, casing-capping, conduit, concealed
- Earthing (pipe, plate, chemical)
- Electrical installations (main board, sub-board, distribution)
- Testing of wiring installations
- Safety devices, protective equipment
- IS codes, electrical regulations

### 6.7 Refrigeration & AC Mechanic
- Refrigeration cycle, components (compressor, condenser, evaporator, expansion device)
- Refrigerants (types, properties, environmental impact)
- Window AC, Split AC, Central AC systems
- Refrigeration tools and instruments
- Troubleshooting, gas charging, leak detection
- Electrical controls in AC systems

### 6.8 Mechanic Radio & TV
- Radio receiver, transmitter principles
- Audio and video systems
- Antenna systems
- TV technology (CRT, LCD, LED, OLED)
- Modulation/demodulation (AM, FM, PM)
- Communication electronics

### 6.9 Instrument Mechanic
- Measurement and instrumentation principles
- Transducers and sensors
- Pressure, temperature, flow, level measurement
- Calibration and instrumentation standards
- Industrial process control
- PLC basics

### 6.10 Turner / Machinist
- Lathe operations (turning, facing, knurling, threading, parting)
- Machine tools — milling, shaping, drilling, grinding
- Cutting tools, tool materials, geometry
- Speeds, feeds, depth of cut
- CNC basics
- Precision measurement (vernier, micrometer, dial gauge)

### 6.11 Winder (Armature)
- DC machine winding — armature, field coils
- AC machine winding — stator, rotor
- Insulation materials, varnishing
- Winding calculations, connections
- Testing, balancing, commutation
- Rewinding procedures

### 6.12 Tractor Mechanic
- Tractor engine — diesel, working
- Transmission, differential, PTO
- Hydraulic system — three-point linkage
- Tractor electrical system
- Field maintenance, repair

### 6.13 Heat Engine
- Internal combustion engines — construction, working
- Thermodynamics cycles (Otto, Diesel, dual)
- Performance parameters (BSFC, thermal efficiency)
- Engine testing, maintenance

### 6.14 Millwright Maintenance Mechanic
- Industrial machinery installation, alignment
- Bearing maintenance, lubrication
- Couplings, belts, chains
- Pumps, compressors, blowers
- Preventive maintenance, troubleshooting

## 7. Marking Scheme

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| **Correct Answer** | +1 mark |
| **Wrong Answer** | -1/3 mark (0.33 negative marking) |
| **Unanswered** | No penalty (0) |
| **Multiple Marking** | Treated as wrong answer → -1/3 |
| **Total Marks** | 100 |
| **Question Type** | MCQ (Multiple Choice Questions) — Objective type |
| **Duration** | 90 minutes (no sectional time limit) |

### Normalization
Since the exam is held in multiple shifts, RRB uses **normalization of marks** (based on equated percentile method) to ensure fairness across sessions. Final merit is prepared on normalized scores.

### Merit List
- Prepared separately for each post and category
- Based on CBT normalized marks
- Tie-breaking: older candidate by DOB → alphabetical order of name
- Separate lists for PwBD, Ex-Servicemen, CCAA (Course Completed Act Apprentices)

3. **Daily 1 Mock Test** — Use official mock tests and Adda247/Testbook

5. **Current Affairs Daily** — Last 6 months is sufficient
6. **Trade Theory** — Read NIMI publications thoroughly for your trade
7. **Time Management** — Practice completing 100 Qs in 80 minutes
8. **Revision is Key** — Make short notes for formulas, GK facts
9. **Negative Marking Caution** — Avoid blind guessing
10. **Physical Fitness** — Medical exam needs A1/A2 standards for some posts
