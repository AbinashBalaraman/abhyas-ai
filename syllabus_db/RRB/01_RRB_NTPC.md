# RRB NTPC (Non-Technical Popular Categories) - Complete Examination Guide

## 1. Overview

**Full Name:** RRB Non-Technical Popular Categories (NTPC) Examination
**Conducting Body:** Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) under the Ministry of Railways, Government of India
**Purpose:** Recruitment to various non-technical posts across Indian Railways (Zonal Railways & Production Units)
**Frequency:** As and when vacancies arise (typically once every 1-2 years; most recent cycle: CEN 05/2024 (Graduate) & CEN 06/2024 (Undergraduate); CEN 06/2025 & CEN 07/2025 active)
**Scale:** ~1 crore applicants per cycle (one of India's largest recruitment exams)

## 2. Posts Offered

### 2.1 Undergraduate (10+2 / 12th Pass) Posts
- Junior Clerk cum Typist
- Accounts Clerk cum Typist
- Junior Time Keeper
- Trains Clerk
- Commercial cum Ticket Clerk

**Pay Level:** Level 2 (Rs 19,900 initial basic)

### 2.2 Graduate (Any Degree) Posts
- Traffic Assistant
- Goods Guard (Goods Train Manager)
- Senior Commercial cum Ticket Clerk
- Senior Clerk cum Typist
- Junior Account Assistant cum Typist
- Senior Time Keeper
- Commercial Apprentice
- Station Master

**Pay Level:** Level 2 to Level 6 (Rs 19,900 to 35,400 initial basic; Station Master & Traffic Assistant are higher)

---

## 3. Eligibility

### Undergraduate (10+2) Posts
- **Educational Qualification:** 12th Pass (+2) from a recognized Board
- **Age Limit:** 18 to 33 years (relaxation for reserved categories as per government norms: SC/ST +5 yrs, OBC +3 yrs, PwBD +10 yrs)

### Graduate Posts
- **Educational Qualification:** Any Bachelor's Degree from a recognized University
- **Age Limit:** 18 to 33 years (relaxation applicable as above); specific posts like Station Master/Traffic Assistant may have additional requirements

**Note:** Typist posts require typing proficiency; typist posts with Hindi need Devanagari typing knowledge.

## 4. Exam Pattern (Two Stages - Common for Both UG & Graduate)

### Stage 1: CBT-1 (Common for All Posts)
- **Sections:** 4 (3 scored + 1 common)
- **Total Questions:** 100
- **Total Marks:** 100
- **Duration:** 90 minutes
- **Nature:** Qualifying (marks not added to final merit)

| Section | Questions | Marks |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| Mathematics | 30 | 30 |
| General Intelligence & Reasoning | 30 | 30 |
| General Awareness | 40 | 40 |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** |

### Stage 2: CBT-2 (Post-Specific)
- **Sections:** 3 (post-specific)
- **Total Questions:** 100-120 (recent: 120 Qs)
- **Total Marks:** 120
- **Duration:** 90 minutes
- **Nature:** Merit-determining

| Section | Questions | Marks |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| Mathematics | 35 | 35 |
| General Intelligence & Reasoning | 35 | 35 |
| General Awareness | 50 | 50 |
| **Total** | **120** | **120** |

### Stage 3: Skill Tests
- **Typing Skill Test (TST):** For all typist posts (Junior Clerk cum Typist, Senior Clerk cum Typist, Junior Account Assistant cum Typist, Accounts Clerk cum Typist)
  - English: 30 wpm on PC
  - Hindi: 25 wpm on PC (Kruti Dev/Mangal fonts)
- **Computer-Based Aptitude Test (CBAT):** Only for Station Master & Traffic Assistant posts (qualifying)

### Stage 4: Document Verification & Medical Examination

## 6. Typing Skill Test (TST) Details
- **Applicable to:** Junior Clerk cum Typist, Senior Clerk cum Typist, Junior Account Assistant cum Typist, Accounts Clerk cum Typist
- **English Typing:** 30 words per minute (wpm) on Personal Computer
- **Hindi Typing:** 25 wpm on Personal Computer
- **Hindi Fonts Available:** Kruti Dev and Mangal
- **Nature:** Qualifying only (not counted in merit)
- **PwBD Exemption:** Candidates with permanent disability (blindness, low vision, cerebral palsy, locomotor disability with 40%+ disability) may be exempted
- **Shortlisting:** 8x the number of vacancies are called for TST based on CBT-2 scores

---

## 7. Computer-Based Aptitude Test (CBAT)
- **Applicable to:** Station Master and Traffic Assistant posts only
- **Nature:** Qualifying (T1 - T5 scoring system; must score minimum qualifying marks in each test battery)
- **Tests Cover:** Memory, Analytical reasoning, Psychomotor skills, Decision-making, Attention
- **Bilingual:** Available in Hindi and English
- **No negative marking**

---

## 8. Marking Scheme
- **Correct Answer:** +1 mark
- **Incorrect Answer:** -1/3 (0.33) mark negative marking
- **Unattempted:** 0 marks
- **Same marking scheme applies to both CBT-1 and CBT-2**

## 9. Salary Structure
- **Undergraduate Posts:** Pay Level 2 - Rs 19,900 (initial basic pay) + DA + HRA + other allowances
- **Graduate Posts:** Pay Level 2 to 6 - Rs 19,900 to 35,400 (initial basic) + DA + HRA + allowances
- **Station Master & Traffic Assistant:** Pay Level 6 (Rs 35,400)
- **In-hand Salary (Approximate):**
  - Level 2: Rs 25,000 - 30,000/month
  - Level 6 (Station Master/TA): Rs 40,000 - 50,000/month
- **Perks:** Free/Railway Pass, CGHS medical, accommodation (subject to availability), pension, increments, promotion prospects

## Quick Reference Summary
- **Stages:** CBT-1 (screening) → CBT-2 (merit) → Skill Test (Typing/CBAT) → DV/ME
- **Negative Marking:** 1/3 for wrong answers
- **Duration:** 90 min per CBT
- **Selection:** Purely on CBT-2 merit + qualifying TST/CBAT
- **Difficulty:** Easy to Moderate (NCERT Class 10-12 level)
- **Best Strategy:** Cover NCERTs (6-12), previous year papers, daily current affairs, and time-bound mock tests
