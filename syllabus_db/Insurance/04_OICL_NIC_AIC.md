# Insurance PSU Exams - OICL, NIC, AIC, PMFBY & IRDA

Comprehensive study guide for Public Sector Undertaking (PSU) recruitment exams in the Indian insurance sector: Oriental Insurance (OICL), National Insurance (NIC), Agriculture Insurance Company (AIC), PMFBY-related schemes, and Insurance Regulatory Development Authority (IRDA/IRDAI).

#### Agriculture Insurance (Specialized Topics for Professional Knowledge)

##### 1. Crop Insurance Schemes
- **PMFBY (Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana)**: Launched 18 Feb 2016; covers all food/oilseed/annual commercial/horticultural crops; premium 2% Kharif, 1.5% Rabi, 5% commercial/horticulture for farmers; remaining subsidized by Centre and State (50:50)
- **Weather Based Crop Insurance Scheme (WBCIS)**: Uses weather parameters as proxy for crop yield
- **Coconut Palm Insurance Scheme (CPIS)**
- **National Agricultural Insurance Scheme (NAIS)** - discontinued but historical
- **Modified NAIS (MNAIS)** - discontinued
- **Restructured Weather Based Crop Insurance Scheme (RWBCIS)**

##### 2. PMFBY Key Features
- **Coverage risks**:
  - Prevented Sowing/Planting/Germination Risk
  - Standing Crop (Sowing to Harvesting): Drought, dry spell, flood, inundation, pests/diseases, landslides, fire, lightning, storm, hailstorm, cyclone
  - Post-Harvest Losses (up to 2 weeks from harvesting)
  - Localized Calamities: Hailstorm, landslide, inundation, cloud burst, natural fire
- **Unit of insurance**: Village/Village Panchayat level
- **Voluntary** for all farmers since 2020 (Kharif onwards)
- **Implementing agencies**: AIC + 10 empanelled general insurance companies (ICICI Lombard, HDFC ERGO, IFFCO-Tokio, Cholamandalam MS, Bajaj Allianz, Reliance General, Future Generali, TATA AIG, SBI General, Universal Sompo)
- **Calendar**:
  - Kharif: Loaning Apr-Jul, Cut-off 31 July
  - Rabi: Loaning Oct-Dec, Cut-off 31 December

##### 3. Crop Production & Protection
- Major crops of India: Rice, Wheat, Maize, Millets, Pulses, Oilseeds, Cotton, Sugarcane, Tea, Coffee, Rubber, Horticulture crops
- Cropping seasons: Kharif (June-Oct), Rabi (Oct-Mar), Zaid (Mar-Jun)
- Soil types, soil health, conservation
- Irrigation methods, water management
- Fertilizers (Urea, DAP, MOP, micronutrients)
- Plant protection: Pesticides, fungicides, herbicides
- Integrated Pest Management (IPM)
- Agricultural inputs, seed varieties (HYV, Hybrid, GM crops)
- Agricultural marketing: MSP, APMC, e-NAM
- Climate change impact on agriculture
- Agricultural finance, Kisan Credit Card (KCC)
- Crop cutting experiments (CCE)
- Area-yield based insurance

## 4. PMFBY (Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana) - Related Scheme Exams

### About PMFBY
- **Launched**: 18 February 2016 by PM Narendra Modi
- **Ministry**: Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare
- **Objective**: Provide comprehensive insurance cover against crop failure, stabilize farmer income, encourage modern farming practices, ensure credit flow to agriculture
- **Replaces**: NAIS and Modified NAIS
- **Crop covered**: Food crops, Oilseeds, Annual Commercial/Horticultural crops
- **Voluntary since 2020** Kharif
- **Implementing agency**: AIC is the leading implementing agency

### CSC (Common Service Centre) VLE Recruitment

The **Common Service Centres (CSC)** are the access points for delivery of various government services including PMFBY enrollment to farmers in rural areas.

#### Post: Village Level Entrepreneur (VLE) / CSC Operator
- **Eligibility**: 12th Pass (some states require Graduation)
- **Age**: 18-35 years (relaxation as per state rules)
- **Selection**: State-level competitive exam / interview

| Quantitative Aptitude | 15 | 15 |

| Agriculture Awareness | 10 | 10 |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** |

- Time: 90 minutes
- Some states also include descriptive test

#### Syllabus
- General Awareness (Indian Polity, History, Geography, Economy)
- Computer Fundamentals, MS Office, Internet
- Banking and Insurance basics
- Government Schemes (PMFBY, PM-KISAN, KCC, MGNREGA)

### Key Note on PMFBY Recruitment
- AIC is the **leading implementing agency** (over 50% market share in crop insurance)

- Banks (Cooperative, RRBs, Commercial) also distribute PMFBY through their network

## 7. Other Insurance Sector Exams

### A. Insurance Foundation Course (IIBF - Indian Institute of Banking & Finance)
- **Conducted by**: IIBF (formerly Insurance Institute of India)
- **Courses**:
  - **Junior Associate of Indian Insurance (JAII)** - Foundation course
  - **Associate of Indian Insurance (AII)** - Intermediate
  - **Fellow of Indian Insurance (FII)** - Advanced
  - **Licentiate (Insurance)**, **Associateship**, **Fellowship** awarded by III
- **Eligibility**: 10+2 pass for foundation courses
- **Recognition**: Recognized by GOI, IRDAI, insurers and UK/Canada/US institutes
- **91 associated institutes** across India + Sri Lanka Insurance Institute, Sri Lanka Insurance Academy, RICB Insurance Institute Bhutan
- **Exams**: Conducted twice a year (May/June & Nov/Dec)
- **Syllabus**: Principles of Insurance, Practice of Insurance (Life/General/Health), Underwriting, Claims, Insurance Law, Reinsurance
- **Use**: Required for promotion, licensing of agents/surveyors

### B. Actuarial Common Entrance Test (ACET)
- **Conducted by**: Institute of Actuaries of India (IAI)
- **Eligibility**: 10+2 pass (no age limit)
- **Exam Pattern**:
  - 70 Multiple Choice Questions
  - 100 marks
  - 2 hours duration
  - Online mode
  - Negative marking: No
  - Conducted twice a year (June & December)
- **Syllabus**:
  - Mathematics (Algebra, Calculus, Probability, Statistics, Linear Algebra, Numerical Methods)
  - English (Grammar, Vocabulary, Comprehension, Business Writing)
  - Logic & Reasoning
  - Data Interpretation
- **Purpose**: Entry-level exam for the Indian Actuarial Profession
- **After ACET**: Clear CT (Core Technical) series papers:
  - **CS (Core Series)**: CS1 (Actuarial Statistics), CS2 (Risk Modelling & Survival Analysis), CM1 (Actuarial Mathematics), CM2 (Financial Economics & CM)
  - **CB (Core Business)**: CB1 (Business Finance), CB2 (Business Economics), CB3 (Business Analytics)
- **Body**: 13,000+ members and students, headquartered in Mumbai

### C. IRDA Insurance Brokers Exam
- **Conducted by**: NIA (National Insurance Academy) / III on behalf of IRDAI
- **For**: Licensing as Insurance Broker (Direct, Reinsurance, Composite)
- **Eligibility**:
  - Minimum age 21 years
  - Graduation with 50% (relaxable for SC/ST)
  - Pre-licensing training (mandatory 100 hours)
- **Exam Pattern**:
  - 100 Objective Questions
  - 100 marks
  - 2 hours
  - Online
  - Negative marking: 0.25
  - Passing: 60% (50% for SC/ST)
- **Syllabus**:
  - **Principles of Insurance** (15%)
  - **Practice of Insurance - Life** (15%)
  - **Practice of Insurance - General** (25%)
  - **Practice of Insurance - Health** (10%)
  - **Insurance Law** (15%)
  - **Insurance Finance, Accounts & Investment** (10%)
  - **Insurance Marketing, Sales & Customer Service** (5%)
  - **Insurance Surveyors, Loss Assessors, TPAs** (5%)
- **Broker Types**: Direct Broker (Life or General), Reinsurance Broker, Composite Broker
- **License**: Issued by IRDAI, valid 3 years, renewable

### D. IRDA Insurance Agents Exam (IC-38)
- **For**: Becoming an Insurance Agent
- **Conducted by**: III (Insurance Institute of India)
- **Eligibility**: 10+2 pass, Age 18+
- **Pattern**: 50 MCQ, 90 minutes, 35% passing
- **Syllabus**:
  - Introduction to Insurance
  - Insurance Products
  - Proposal Stage
  - Policy Servicing
  - Claims Management
  - Grievance Redressal

### E. IRDA Surveyor & Loss Assessor Exam
- **Eligibility**: Graduation + relevant technical qualification
- **Exam**: 100 MCQ, 2 hours
- **Syllabus**: Surveyor practice, Loss assessment, Insurance law, Claims

### F. IRDA Web Aggregator Exam
- **For**: License to operate Insurance Web Aggregator platform

### G. NIA (National Insurance Academy) Courses
- **Post Graduate Diploma in Insurance Business Management (PGDM-IBM)**
- **Fellow Programme in Risk & Insurance Management (FPRIM)**

## Summary

The Insurance PSU sector offers multiple career opportunities through OICL, NIC, AIC, and IRDA examinations. The most common recruitment is for **Administrative Officer (AO)** and **Management Trainee (MT)** posts through a **3-stage process**: Preliminary Exam (Objective), Mains Exam (Objective + Descriptive), and Interview. Common syllabus includes Reasoning, English, Quantitative Aptitude, and General Awareness with a strong focus on Insurance knowledge. The **AIC exam uniquely adds Agriculture/Crop Insurance specialization** including PMFBY (Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana) coverage. Salaries range from **Rs 50,000 to Rs 70,000+ basic pay** with excellent perks, allowances, and career growth in the insurance sector.
