# Central PSU Examinations - Syllabus & Pattern Guide

**Coverage:** Major Maharatna/Navratna Central Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs) - ONGC, BHEL, IOCL, NTPC, GAIL, SAIL, HPCL, BPCL, PFC, REC, NMDC, NLC India, NALCO

---

## 1. ONGC (Oil and Natural Gas Corporation) Examination

**Status:** Maharatna PSU (since November 2010)
**Website:** https://www.ongcindia.com/ | **HQ:** Deendayal Urja Bhawan, New Delhi
**Workforce:** ~24,000+ professionals

### Posts
- **Graduate Trainee (GT)** - Engineering (Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics, Civil, Instrumentation, Chemical), Geo-Science (Geology, Geophysics, Chemistry), Other (Finance, HR, Materials Management, CSR)
- **Medical Officer**
- **Law Officer**
- **Assistant Executive Engineer (AEE)**

### Eligibility
- **Engineering:** B.E./B.Tech in relevant discipline with **60% marks** (55% for SC/ST/PWD)
- **Geo-Science:** M.Sc./M.A./M.Tech (Geology/Geophysics/Chemistry) with 60%
- **Law:** LLB with 60%
- **Age:** 18-30 years (relaxation for reserved categories as per govt. norms)

### Exam Pattern

**Stage 1: Computer Based Test (CBT)**
| Section | Topic | Questions | Marks |
|---------|-------|-----------|-------|
| Section A | General (Reasoning, English, GA, Quant Aptitude) | 50 | 50 |
| Section B | Technical/Professional (Concerned Discipline) | 50 (or 100) | 50 (or 100) |
| Section C | Concerned Subject (post-specific) | Variable | Variable |

- **Duration:** 3 hours (composite)
- **Negative Marking:** -0.25 for each wrong answer
- **Normalization** applied for multi-shift exams

**Stage 2:** Group Discussion (GD) / Group Task (qualifying)
**Stage 3:** Personal Interview (weightage ~70-80%)

### Detailed Syllabus (Common + Technical)

**Common Section (Section A):**
- **Reasoning:** Analogies, Coding-Decoding, Blood Relations, Syllogisms, Series Completion, Direction Test, Venn Diagrams, Statement & Conclusion, Puzzles
- **English:** Reading Comprehension, Vocabulary, Grammar (Error Spotting, Sentence Correction), Cloze Test, Para Jumbles, Synonyms/Antonyms
- **General Awareness:** Current Affairs (National/International), PSU-related, Oil & Gas Sector, Indian Polity, Geography, History, Economics
- **Quantitative Aptitude:** Number System, Algebra, Geometry, Trigonometry, Mensuration, Statistics, Profit-Loss, SI/CI, Time-Work, Speed-Distance

**Technical (Section B/C) - Discipline-wise:**
- **Petroleum Engineering:** Drilling, Reservoir Engineering, Production, Well Logging, EOR Methods, Petroleum Geology
- **Mechanical:** Thermodynamics, Fluid Mechanics, Heat Transfer, Manufacturing, Machine Design, IC Engines
- **Electrical:** Power Systems, Machines, Control Systems, Power Electronics, Electrical Measurements
- **Instrumentation:** Process Control, Transducers, Industrial Instrumentation, Analytical Instruments
- **Civil:** Structural Analysis, RCC, Steel Structures, Soil Mechanics, Fluid Mechanics, Surveying
- **Geology:** Mineralogy, Petrology, Structural Geology, Stratigraphy, Paleontology, Hydrogeology
- **Geophysics:** Seismic Methods, Gravity/Magnetic methods, Well Logging, Earth Sciences
- **Chemistry:** Organic/Inorganic/Physical Chemistry, Spectroscopy, Reaction Kinetics

### Salary
- **Basic Pay:** Rs 60,000 - 1,80,000 (E2 scale)
- **CTC:** Rs 12-16 LPA (approximate, including DA, HRA, perks)
- **Benefits:** HRA, DA, Perks (35% of basic), Medical, LTC, PF, Gratuity

## 2. BHEL (Bharat Heavy Electricals Limited) Examination

**Status:** Maharatna PSU
**Website:** https://www.bhel.com/ | **HQ:** BHEL House, Siri Fort, New Delhi
**Workforce:** ~27,800 employees

### Posts
- **Engineer Trainee (ET)** - Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics (ECE), Civil, Chemical, Metallurgy, Ceramic
- **Supervisor** (Diploma Engineers)
- **Artisan/Trade Apprentice**

### Eligibility
- **B.E./B.Tech** in relevant discipline with **60% aggregate** (55% for SC/ST)
- **Age:** 18-27 years (upper age limit may extend to 30 for some posts)

### Exam Pattern

**Stage 1: Online Test (CBT)**
| Part | Subject | Questions | Marks |
|------|---------|-----------|-------|
| Part I | General Aptitude (Reasoning, English, Numerical Ability, GA) | 50 | 50 |
| Part II | Technical (Concerned Engineering Discipline) | 50 | 50 |
| **Total** | | **100** | **100** |

- **Duration:** 2 hours
- **Negative Marking:** -0.25 per wrong answer

**Stage 2:** Personal Interview (qualifying nature)

### Detailed Syllabus

**General Aptitude (Part I):**
- **Reasoning:** Verbal/Non-Verbal, Analytical, Logical, Number Series, Coding
- **English:** Grammar, Vocabulary, Comprehension, Sentence Correction
- **Numerical Ability:** Arithmetic, Algebra, Geometry, Data Interpretation
- **GA:** Current Affairs, Indian History, Polity, Geography, Economics, BHEL-related

**Technical (Part II) - Discipline-wise:**
- **Mechanical:** Thermodynamics, SOM, Fluid Mechanics, Heat Transfer, Manufacturing, Theory of Machines, Machine Design, IC Engines, RAC
- **Electrical:** Network Theory, Machines, Power Systems, Control Systems, Power Electronics, Electrical Measurements, Analog/Digital Electronics
- **Electronics:** Analog Circuits, Digital Electronics, Signals & Systems, Microprocessors, Communication Systems, VLSI, Embedded Systems
- **Civil:** Structural Engineering, Geotechnical, Surveying, Fluid Mechanics, Environmental, Transportation, RCC/Steel Design
- **Chemical:** Reaction Engineering, Mass Transfer, Heat Transfer, Process Control, Thermodynamics, Plant Design

### Salary
- **Basic Pay (ET):** Rs 50,000 - 1,80,000 (E1/E2 scale)
- **CTC:** Rs 10-13 LPA (approximate)
- **Benefits:** DA, HRA, Perks (30% basic), Medical, PF, Gratuity, Performance Pay

## 3. IOCL (Indian Oil Corporation Limited) Examination

**Status:** Maharatna PSU
**Website:** https://www.iocl.com/ | **HQ:** New Delhi | **Registered:** Mumbai
**Workforce:** ~30,321 employees

### Posts
- **Officer (Engineers/Officers/Assistant Officer)** - Chemical, Mechanical, Electrical, Instrumentation, Civil, Computer Science, Electronics & Telecommunications, Metallurgy
- **Junior Engineering Assistant (JEA)** - Diploma Engineers
- **Trade Apprentice** - ITI holders
- **Officer (HR/Finance/Marketing/Legal)**

### Eligibility
- **B.E./B.Tech** in relevant discipline with **60% marks** (55% for SC/ST/PWD)
- **Age:** 18-30 years (relaxation as per rules)
- For some positions, MBA/CA/ICWA may be required

### Exam Pattern

**Stage 1: Computer Based Test (CBT)**
| Section | Topic | Questions | Marks |
|---------|-------|-----------|-------|
| Section A | General (Reasoning, English, Quantitative Aptitude, GA) | 50 | 50 |
| Section B | Technical (Concerned Discipline) | 50 | 50 |
| **Total** | | **100** | **100** |

- **Duration:** 2.5 hours
- **Negative Marking:** -0.25 per wrong answer

**Stage 2:** Group Discussion (GD) + Group Task (qualifying)
**Stage 3:** Personal Interview (technical + HR)
**Stage 4:** Medical Fitness Test

### Detailed Syllabus

**General Section:**
- **Reasoning:** Similar to ONGC pattern; emphasis on Data Sufficiency, Statement-Conclusion
- **English:** Reading Comprehension, Fill in the Blanks, Spotting Errors, Para Jumbles
- **Quantitative Aptitude:** Arithmetic, Algebra, Probability, Statistics, Mensuration
- **GA:** Current Affairs (Oil Sector, PSU, Indian Economy, International), Static GK

**Technical Section:**
- **Chemical Engineering:** Chemical Reaction Engineering, Mass Transfer, Heat Transfer, Fluid Mechanics, Process Control, Petroleum Refining, Petrochemicals
- **Mechanical:** Same as BHEL pattern
- **Electrical:** Same as BHEL pattern
- **Instrumentation:** Transducers, Process Control, Industrial Instrumentation, Control Systems
- **Civil:** Same as BHEL pattern

### Salary
- **Basic Pay (Officer):** Rs 50,000 - 1,60,000 (E1/E2)
- **CTC:** Rs 12-16 LPA (approximate)
- **Benefits:** DA, HRA, Perks, Medical, Petrol Allowance, PF, Gratuity, PRBS

## 6. SAIL (Steel Authority of India Limited) Examination

**Status:** Maharatna PSU (since May 2010)
**Website:** https://www.sail.co.in/ | **HQ:** New Delhi
**Plants:** Bhilai, Rourkela, Durgapur, Bokaro, Burnpur (5 integrated)

### Posts
- **Management Trainee (Technical, HR, Finance)**
- **Operator-cum-Technician (OCT)** - Diploma Engineers
- **Attendant-cum-Technician (ACT)** - ITI holders
- **Medical Officer, Law Officer**

### Eligibility
- **MT (Technical):** B.E./B.Tech with 60% (55% SC/ST)
- **MT (HR):** MBA/PGDBM with 60%
- **MT (Finance):** CA/ICWA/MBA (Finance) with 60%
- **OCT:** Diploma in Engineering (60%)
- **ACT:** ITI in relevant trade
- **Age:** 18-28 years (relaxation as per rules)

### Exam Pattern

**Stage 1: Computer Based Test (CBT)**
- For MT: General Aptitude + Technical (similar to other PSU pattern)
- For OCT/ACT: Trade Test + Aptitude

**Stage 2:** Personal Interview (Group Discussion for some)

### Detailed Syllabus

**General Aptitude:** Reasoning, English, Quantitative, GA

**Technical (MT):**
- **Mechanical:** Same as BHEL pattern + Steel Plant specific (Rolling Mills, Blast Furnace, Steel Making)
- **Electrical:** Same as BHEL pattern + Steel Plant Electrical Systems
- **Metallurgy:** Physical Metallurgy, Extractive Metallurgy, Iron & Steel Making, Mechanical Metallurgy
- **Chemical:** Chemical Processes, Pollution Control, Water Treatment
- **Civil:** Same as BHEL pattern + Industrial Structures

### Salary
- **Basic Pay (MT):** Rs 50,000 - 1,60,000 (E1 scale)
- **CTC:** Rs 10-14 LPA (approximate)
- **Benefits:** DA, HRA, Perks, Medical, PF, Gratuity, House Building Advance

## 8. Common Preparation Strategy

### General Aptitude Topics (Common Across PSUs)

**Reasoning:**
- Analogies, Classification, Series
- Coding-Decoding, Direction Sense
- Blood Relations, Syllogisms, Venn Diagrams
- Statement & Conclusion, Statement & Assumption
- Puzzles, Seating Arrangement
- Data Sufficiency, Input-Output

**English Language:**
- Reading Comprehension
- Vocabulary (Synonyms/Antonyms, One-Word Substitution)
- Grammar (Spotting Errors, Sentence Correction)
- Cloze Test, Fill in the Blanks
- Para Jumbles, Sentence Rearrangement
- Idioms & Phrases

**Quantitative Aptitude:**
- Number System, HCF/LCM
- Percentages, Profit & Loss, SI/CI
- Ratio & Proportion, Averages
- Time & Work, Time-Speed-Distance
- Geometry, Mensuration
- Algebra, Trigonometry
- Data Interpretation (Tables, Charts, Graphs)
- Probability & Statistics

**General Awareness:**
- Current Affairs (National/International)
- Indian Polity, Constitution
- History (Ancient, Medieval, Modern)
- Geography (India & World)
- Indian Economy, Budget
- Science & Technology
- PSU-related news, Government Schemes
- Sports, Awards, Books & Authors
