# EXIM Bank, NHB, FCI & Coal India Limited Examinations

Comprehensive syllabus guide for four major PSU/specialized institution examinations in India.

---

## 1. EXIM Bank (Export-Import Bank of India) Officer

### Overview
Established in 1982, EXIM Bank is India's premier financial institution engaged in financing, facilitating, and promoting India's international trade. Headquartered at Centre One Building, World Trade Centre Complex, Cuffe Parade, Mumbai.

### Posts
- **Administrative Officer (AO)**
- **Specialist Officer** (various streams: Finance, Legal, IT, HR, Treasury, Forex)

### Eligibility
- **Educational Qualification**: Post-Graduation / MBA / CA / ICWA / Professional qualification (e.g., CFA, CS, LLB) in relevant discipline
- **Age Limit**: 21–30 years (relaxation for SC/ST/OBC/PwD as per Govt. norms)
- **Experience**: 2–5 years preferred for specialist roles (varies by post)

### Exam Pattern
**Stage 1: Online Written Test**
| Section | Questions | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| Reasoning Ability | 50 | 50 |
| English Language | 50 | 50 |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 50 | 50 |
| General Awareness (with focus on Economy, Finance, Banking) | 50 | 50 |
| Professional Knowledge (Trade Finance, Forex, EXIM Policies, EXIM Bank Schemes) | 50 | 50 |
| **Total** | **250** | **250** |

- **Duration**: 2 hours (120 minutes)
- **Negative Marking**: 0.25 per wrong answer

**Stage 2: Interview** (15–20% weightage)

### Detailed Syllabus

**Reasoning**: Puzzles, Seating Arrangement, Coding-Decoding, Syllogism, Blood Relations, Direction Sense, Inequalities, Data Sufficiency.

**English**: Reading Comprehension, Cloze Test, Vocabulary, Sentence Correction, Para Jumbles, Grammar.

**Quantitative Aptitude**: DI, Number Series, Simplification, Profit & Loss, SI/CI, Time-Work, Speed-Distance, Mensuration, Probability.

**General/Financial Awareness**: Indian Economy, RBI policies, Foreign Trade Policy, EXIM policies, WTO, IMF, World Bank, Balance of Payments, Trade Finance instruments.

**Professional Knowledge**:
- Trade Finance (LCs, Bank Guarantees, Export/Import financing)
- Forex Markets (Exchange Rates, Currency Derivatives, Hedging)
- EXIM Bank schemes (Lines of Credit, Buyer's Credit, Pre/Post-shipment Credit, Project Finance, Ubharte Sitaare Programme)
- EXIM Policies and Foreign Trade Policy (FTP)
- International Trade Documentation (Bill of Lading, Invoices, Certificates of Origin)
- FEMA and RBI guidelines for cross-border transactions

### Salary
- **Basic Pay**: Rs 44,500/- (starting) in JMGS-I
- **Gross Salary**: Approx Rs 70,000–80,000 per month (including DA, HRA, CCA, perks)
- **CTC**: Approx Rs 10–12 LPA
- **Allowances**: HRA, DA, Medical, LTC, Conveyance, Newspaper allowance, Furnishing allowance

## 2. NHB (National Housing Bank) Officer

### Overview
Set up in 1988 under the National Housing Bank Act, 1987, NHB is the apex financial institution for housing in India. Apex DFI for housing, principal agency to promote HFCs, and provider of low-cost long-term liquidity. Located at Core 5-A, India Habitat Centre, Lodhi Road, New Delhi.

### Posts
- **Assistant Manager**
- **Manager**
- **Senior Manager**
- (Also: Deputy General Manager, General Manager, Chief General Manager)

### Eligibility
- **Educational Qualification**: Graduation (60%) / Post-Graduation (55%) / MBA / CA / ICWA / Professional qualification in relevant field
- **Age Limit**: 21–30 years (relaxation as per Govt. norms)
- **Age Relaxation**: SC/ST +5 yrs, OBC +3 yrs, PwD +10 yrs

### Exam Pattern
**Stage 1: Online Examination**
| Section | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reasoning Ability | 50 | 50 | Composite |
| English Language | 50 | 50 | Composite |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 50 | 50 | Composite |
| General Awareness (Banking, Economy, Finance) | 50 | 50 | Composite |
| **Total** | **200** | **200** | **2 Hours** |

- **Negative Marking**: 0.25 per wrong answer

**Stage 2: Descriptive Paper / Interview**

### Detailed Syllabus

**Reasoning**: Verbal & Non-Verbal Reasoning, Puzzles, Coding-Decoding, Syllogism, Seating Arrangement, Direction Sense.

**English**: Reading Comprehension, Grammar, Vocabulary, Cloze Test, Sentence Rearrangement, Error Spotting.

**Quantitative Aptitude**: DI, Simplification, Profit-Loss, SI/CI, Time-Work, Mensuration, Algebra, Probability.

**Housing Finance Knowledge**:
- NHB Act, 1987; RBI regulations for HFCs
- Housing Finance Companies (regulation & supervision)
- PMAY (Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana) – PMAY-U & PMAY-G
- Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme (CLSS)
- Affordable Housing, Reverse Mortgage, Refinance mechanisms
- NHB Residex (Housing Price Index), NHB's Refinance Schemes
- Securitisation, Mortgage-Backed Securities
- Priority Sector Lending in housing
- Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 (RERA)

**Banking Awareness**: RBI, SEBI, IRDA, Banking regulations, NBFC sector, Basel norms.

### Salary
- **Basic Pay**: Rs 36,400/- (Assistant Manager)
- **Gross Salary**: Rs 55,000–65,000 per month (approx)
- **CTC**: Rs 8–10 LPA (approx)
- **Allowances**: DA, HRA, CCA, Medical, LTC, Conveyance, Pension (NPS)

## 3. FCI (Food Corporation of India) Examination

### Overview
FCI, set up under the Food Corporations Act, 1964, ensures food security through procurement, storage, distribution, and buffer management of food grains. Plays a key role in implementing PDS.

### Posts

**A. Assistant Grade III (AG III)**:
- Technical
- General
- Depot
- Accounts
- Stenographer Grade II

**B. Manager (Category II)**:
- General
- Engineering (Civil / Electrical / Mechanical)
- Technical (Food Science / Agriculture / Biotech)
- Accounts
- Hindi (for Rajbhasha)

### Eligibility

**Assistant Grade III**:
- Graduation with 60% (55% for SC/ST/PwD)
- Age: 18–27 years
- Stenographer: Graduation + Steno speed 80/40 w.p.m.

**Manager**:
- Graduation / Engineering / M.Sc. (Food Tech) / MBA / CA / Professional qualification
- Age: 18–28 years (relaxation applicable)

### Exam Pattern

#### Phase-I (Common for All Posts)
| Section | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| English Language | 25 | 25 | 15 min |
| Reasoning Ability | 25 | 25 | 15 min |
| Numerical Ability | 25 | 25 | 15 min |
| General Studies | 25 | 25 | 15 min |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** | **60 min** |

#### Phase-II (Post-Specific)

**Manager (General)**:
| Paper | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paper I (Post Specific) | 120 | 120 | 90 min |
| Paper II (Post Specific) | 60 | 120 | 90 min |

**Manager (Hindi) – Paper III & IV**: 120 questions + Descriptive (translation, essay, précis) totaling 120 marks.

**Assistant Grade III – Phase-II**:
| Section | Questions | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| English | 30 | 30 |
| Reasoning | 35 | 35 |
| Numerical Ability | 35 | 35 |
| General/Economic/Banking Awareness/Computer | 60 | 60 |
| **Total** | **160 (Manager: 180)** | **160** |

#### Phase-III (Manager only)
- **Descriptive Test**: English Essay / Letter Writing / Technical Description (60 min)
- **Interview**: 50 marks weightage

### Detailed Syllabus

**Common Sections (Phase-I)**:
- **English**: RC, Cloze Test, Vocabulary, Grammar, Sentence Improvement, Para Jumbles, One Word Substitution.
- **Reasoning**: Puzzles, Seating Arrangement, Coding-Decoding, Syllogism, Blood Relation, Inequalities, Direction Sense.
- **Numerical Ability**: DI, Number Series, SI/CI, Profit-Loss, Time-Work, Speed-Distance, Mensuration, Probability, Quadratic Equations.
- **General Studies**: Current Affairs, History, Geography, Polity, Science.

**Phase-II General/Economic Awareness**:
- Indian Economy, GDP, Inflation, Monetary Policy
- Food Security, PDS (Public Distribution System)
- FCI Schemes and operations
- FSSAI (Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006)
- PFA Act, 1964
- Agriculture – Cereals, Pulses, MSP (Minimum Support Price)
- Buffer Stock, Procurement Policy
- Food Grain Storage and Preservation

**Computer Knowledge**:
- History and Generations of Computers
- MS Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint)
- Internet, Email, Browsers
- Computer Memory, Operating Systems
- Networking basics

**General/Hindi for Hindi Medium**: Translation, Precis Writing, Hindi Essay, Rajbhasha policies.

**Technical Syllabus (Discipline-wise)**:
- **Accounts**: Contract Act, Companies Act, Negotiable Instrument Act, Income Tax, GST, Auditing, Budgeting, Capital Budgeting, Ratio Analysis, MS Office basics.
- **Technical (Food/Agriculture)**: Microbes, Genetic Engineering, Entomology, IPM, PFA/FSSAI Act, Food Microbiology, Post-Harvest care, Food Grain Protection.
- **Civil Engineering**: Surveying, Soil Mechanics, Structural Analysis, RCC Design, Steel Structures, Highway Engineering, Building Materials.
- **Electrical/Mechanical**: Thermodynamics, Heat Transfer, Machine Design, Theory of Machines, Power Systems, EM Theory, Network Theorems.

### Salary
- **Assistant Grade III**: Basic Rs 28,200/-, Gross Rs 45,000–55,000/-, CTC Rs 6–7 LPA
- **Manager**: Basic Rs 40,000–50,000/-, Gross Rs 65,000–80,000/-, CTC Rs 9–11 LPA
- **Allowances**: DA, HRA, CCA, LTC, Medical, Pension (NPS), Performance Related Pay

### Marking Scheme
- Correct Answer: **+1 mark**
- Wrong Answer: **−0.25 marks** (negative marking)
- Unanswered: 0 marks

## 4. Coal India Limited (CIL) Examination

### Overview
Coal India Limited, a Maharatna PSU established in 1973, is the largest coal producer globally. Headquarters at Coal Bhawan, Newtown, Rajarhat, Kolkata. Operates 8 subsidiaries (ECL, BCCL, CCL, NCL, WCL, SECL, MCL, NEC) plus CMPDI.

### Posts

**A. Management Trainee (MT)**:
- Mining, Mechanical, Electrical, Civil, Chemical/Mineral, Electronics & Telecom, Computer Science, Finance, Marketing, Personnel & HR, Legal, Company Secretary.

**B. Medical Executive**:
- Medical Specialist, Sr. Medical Officer, Sr. Medical Specialist.

**C. Other Posts**:
- Accountant
- Operator
- Mining Sirdar / Overman

### Eligibility

**Management Trainee (MT)**:
- B.E./B.Tech/B.Sc (Engineering) — 60% (55% for SC/ST/PwD)
- Or CA/ICWA/MBA/PG for non-technical
- Age: 18–30 years (relaxation applicable)

**Medical Executive**:
- MBBS + PG Degree / Diploma
- Age: 18–30 years (relaxation applicable)

**Other Posts**:
- ITI / Diploma / Graduation (depending on post)
- Age: 18–30 years

### Exam Pattern

#### For Management Trainee (MT)

**CBT (Computer Based Test)** – Sectional Cutoff & Overall Cutoff:

| Section | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Section A** (Common) | | | |
| — Reasoning | 20 | 20 | |
| — English | 20 | 20 | |
| — Quantitative Aptitude | 20 | 20 | Composite 3 hours |
| — General Awareness | 20 | 20 | |
| **Section B** (Technical/Discipline) | 90 | 90 | |
| **Total** | **170** | **170** | **3 hours** |

- **Negative Marking**: 0.25 marks per wrong answer
- **Selection**: CBT + Personal Interview (10–20% weightage)

#### For Other Posts (Operator / Accountant / Mining Sirdar)

**Trade/Technical Test (Offline/Online)**:
- Discipline-specific technical questions
- 100 questions, 100 marks

**Aptitude Test (Common)**:
| Section | Questions | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| Reasoning | 25 | 25 |
| English | 25 | 25 |
| Numerical Ability | 25 | 25 |
| General Awareness | 25 | 25 |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** |

### Detailed Syllabus

**Common Section (For MT)**:
- **Reasoning**: Puzzles, Coding-Decoding, Syllogism, Direction Sense, Blood Relations, Number Series, Pattern Recognition, Series Completion.
- **English**: RC, Grammar, Vocabulary, Cloze Test, Para Jumbles.
- **Quantitative Aptitude**: DI, Number System, Algebra, Geometry, Mensuration, Profit-Loss, SI/CI, Ratio & Proportion, Percentage.
- **General Awareness**: Current Affairs, Indian Economy, Geography, History, Polity, Science, Coal Industry updates.

**Technical (Discipline-wise for MT)**:

**Mining Engineering**:
- Mine Planning, Mine Development, Underground & Opencast Mining
- Mining Methods (Longwall, Room & Pillar)
- Drilling & Blasting
- Mine Ventilation, Mine Safety
- Rock Mechanics, Strata Control
- Coal Handling & Preparation Plants
- Mine Surveying, Mine Legislation

**Mechanical Engineering**:
- Thermodynamics, Heat Transfer
- Theory of Machines, Machine Design
- Strength of Materials
- Production Engineering, Industrial Engineering
- Fluid Mechanics, Hydraulic Machines
- Manufacturing Technology (Casting, Welding, Machining)
- Refrigeration & Air-conditioning

**Electrical Engineering**:
- Electrical Circuits, Network Theorems
- Electrical Machines (DC, AC, Transformers, Induction Motors)
- Power Systems (Generation, Transmission, Distribution)
- Power Electronics & Drives
- Control Systems, Signals & Systems
- Electrical Measurements, Instrumentation

**Civil Engineering**:
- Structural Analysis, Design of RC & Steel Structures
- Geotechnical Engineering, Soil Mechanics
- Surveying, Transportation Engineering
- Environmental Engineering
- Hydraulics, Fluid Mechanics
- Construction Management

**Computer Science**:
- Data Structures & Algorithms
- Operating Systems, DBMS
- Computer Networks, Network Security
- Software Engineering, Object-Oriented Programming
- Digital Electronics, Microprocessors
- Programming Languages (C, C++, Java, Python)

### Salary
- **MT (E-2 Grade)**: Basic Rs 50,000 – 1,60,000 (E2-E7 pay scale)
- **Starting Gross**: Rs 60,000–70,000 per month (approx)
- **CTC**: Rs 11–13 LPA (approx, including perks)
- **Allowances**: DA, HRA, Coal Allowance, Medical, LTC, Performance Related Pay (PRP), Executive Pension Scheme
- **Mining Sirdar / Overman**: Basic Rs 30,000–45,000 (T&S Grade)
