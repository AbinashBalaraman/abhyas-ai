# IPPB & India Post / GDS Examinations - Comprehensive Study Guide

> **Source basis**: indiapost.gov.in, ippbonline.bank.in (official sites), en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India_Post_Payments_Bank, en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India_Post, and standard syllabus notifications from SSC / IBPS / Department of Posts / IPPB.

The Department of Posts (DoP) / India Post is a statutory body under the Ministry of Communications, GoI, with 1,64,999 post offices (Mar 2025) - the widest postal network in the world. India Post Payments Bank (IPPB) is a division of India Post, headquartered in New Delhi, started as pilot on 30 Jan 2017, launched by PM Modi on 1 Sept 2018; as of Aug 2025 it has 12 crore+ customers and Rs 20,000+ crore deposits. Recruitments are conducted by the Department of Posts directly (for Postman, Mail Guard, MTS, PA/SA, GDS) and by IBPS (for IPPB scale posts).

## 1. IPPB (India Post Payments Bank) - Assistant / Officer

### 1.1 IPPB Assistant (Scale I)

- **Posts**: Assistant in IPPB (Junior level clerical/assistant post in the payments bank).
- **Conducting body**: IBPS (Institute of Banking Personnel Selection), CRP pattern.
- **Eligibility**:
  - Graduation in any discipline from a recognized university.
  - Minimum 60% (55% for SC/ST/PwBD) aggregate marks.
  - Age: 20-28 years (relaxation: SC/ST 5 yrs, OBC 3 yrs, PwBD 10-15 yrs).
  - Proficiency in the official language of the state/UT applied for.
- **Exam Pattern (2 Tiers)**:
  - **Preliminary (Qualifying)** - 100 Qs / 100 marks / 60 min, sectional timing:
    - English Language - 30 Qs / 30 marks
    - Reasoning Ability - 35 Qs / 35 marks
    - Quantitative Aptitude - 35 Qs / 35 marks
  - **Mains (Merit)** - 150 Qs / 150 marks / 120 min, sectional timing:
    - Reasoning - 50 Qs / 50 marks
    - English Language - 40 Qs / 40 marks
    - Quantitative Aptitude - 50 Qs / 50 marks
    - Banking & Payment System Awareness + General Awareness - 10 Qs / 10 marks
  - Negative marking: 0.25 deducted for each wrong answer.
  - Prelims qualifying; Mains merit ranking; final selection = Mains + LPT (Language Proficiency Test).
- **Detailed Syllabus**:
  - **English**: Reading Comprehension, Cloze Test, Fillers, Error Spotting, Para Jumbles, Sentence Correction, Synonyms/Antonyms, Vocabulary, Grammar, Idioms & Phrases.
  - **Reasoning**: Puzzles (Seating/Linear/Floor), Syllogism, Inequality, Coding-Decoding, Series, Direction Test, Blood Relations, Order & Ranking, Alphanumeric, Data Sufficiency, Statement & Conclusion/Assumption.
  - **Quantitative Aptitude**: Simplification, Number Series, Data Interpretation (Table, Pie, Bar, Line), Quadratic Equations, Arithmetic (Percentage, Profit-Loss, SI/CI, Ratio, Time-Work, Speed-Distance, Pipes, Ages, Partnership, Mixture), Mensuration, Probability, Permutation & Combination.
  - **Banking & Payment System Awareness** (specific to IPPB & payments bank concept):
    - History & functioning of IPPB, payments bank model, RBI guidelines for payments banks.
    - RBI - structure, functions, monetary policy, repo/reverse repo, CRR/SLR.
    - Digital Banking - UPI, IMPS, NEFT, RTGS, AEPS, RuPay, QR Card, PoS, mBanking, Internet Banking, Mobile Wallets, CBDC, e-RUPI, Bharat BillPay, DBT.
    - Banking Regulation Act, Negotiable Instruments Act, BCSBI codes, KYC/AML, NPCI.
    - Financial Inclusion, Jan Dhan Yojana, MGNREGS, Social Security Schemes (PMJJBY, PMSBY, APY).
    - Government schemes: Sukanya Samriddhi, PMJDY, Mudra Yojana, Stand-Up India, PSL norms.
    - Basics of Indian Economy, NITI Aayog, GDP, Inflation, Repo Rate, Fiscal Policy.
- **Salary**: Basic Pay Rs 29,956 (Pay Scale 720-1930 in IPPB pattern). In-hand ~ Rs 35,000 - 40,000 (approx.) including DA, HRA, CCA and other allowances.

### 1.2 IPPB Officer (Scale I / JMGS-I)

- **Posts**: Officer (Probationary Officer / Junior Management Grade Scale-I) in IPPB.
- **Eligibility**:
  - Graduation / Post-Graduation from a recognized university.
  - Age: 21-30 years (relaxation as per category).
  - Knowledge of computers and proficiency in local language.
- **Exam Pattern**: Online Examination + Interview.
  - Online Test: Reasoning, Quantitative Aptitude, English, General Awareness / Banking Awareness.
  - Descriptive Paper (Letter Writing / Essay).
  - Personal Interview (10-15% weightage).
- **Detailed Syllabus**: Quant (DI-heavy, advanced arithmetic), Reasoning (high-level puzzles, LR), English (RC, vocabulary, grammar), GA (Banking, Economy, Static GK, Current Affairs). 
- **Salary**: Basic Pay Rs 41,960 (Pay Scale 1000-2450 in IPPB pattern). Gross in-hand ~ Rs 50,000-60,000 including allowances.

## 2. India Post (Department of Posts) Examinations

All posts are recruited through SSC (Staff Selection Commission) under the Ministry of Communications, with Computer Based Examination (CBE). Pay matrix follows 7th CPC.

### 2.1 Postman

- **Eligibility**: 10th Pass (Matriculation) from a recognized board; age 18-27 years (relaxation: SC/ST 5 yrs, OBC 3 yrs, PwBD 10 yrs, Ex-Servicemen as per rule).
- **Knowledge of local language** of the applied state/circle is mandatory.
- **Exam Pattern (Single CBE)** - 100 Qs / 100 marks / 120 min (160 min for PwBD):
  - **General Knowledge** - 30 Qs / 30 marks
  - **Mathematics** - 30 Qs / 30 marks
  - **English** - 30 Qs / 30 marks
  - **Regional Language (local)** - 10 Qs / 10 marks
  - Negative marking: 0.25 deduction per wrong answer.
- **Detailed syllabus**:
  - **GK**: Indian History, Geography, Polity, Economy, General Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology up to 10th), Current Affairs, Sports, Awards, Books, Capitals, First in India, Abbreviations, Computer basics.
  - **Mathematics**: Number System, HCF/LCM, Simplification, Decimals & Fractions, Ratio & Proportion, Percentage, Profit & Loss, Simple & Compound Interest, Average, Time & Work, Speed & Distance, Mensuration, Algebra basics, Geometry, Trigonometry basics (10th level).
  - **English**: Comprehension, Sentence Completion, Error Spotting, Synonyms/Antonyms, One-word substitution, Idioms, Articles, Prepositions, Tenses, Voice, Narration, Spelling, Vocabulary.
  - **Regional Language**: Grammar, Comprehension, Sentence formation, Vocabulary (class 10th level).
- **Skill Test**: No skill test for Postman; only merit-based.
- **Salary**: Pay Level 3 (Rs 21,700 - Rs 69,100) + DA + HRA + other allowances. In-hand ~ Rs 25,000-32,000.

### 2.2 Mail Guard

- **Eligibility, exam pattern, syllabus, salary** - same as Postman (10th Pass, 18-27 yrs, 4-section CBE, 100 Qs/100 marks).
- Role involves guarding mail in transit (Railway Mail Service / Road transport), postal sorting and transmission of mail bags between post offices, mail offices and railway stations.
- **Salary**: Pay Level 3 (Rs 21,700 - Rs 69,100).

### 2.3 MTS (Multi-Tasking Staff) in Post

- **Eligibility**: 10th Pass (Matriculation); age 18-25 years (relaxation as per category).
- **Exam Pattern (Single CBE)** - 100 Qs / 100 marks / 90 min (120 min for PwBD):
  - **General Intelligence & Reasoning** - 25 Qs / 25 marks
  - **Numerical Aptitude** - 25 Qs / 25 marks
  - **General English** - 25 Qs / 25 marks
  - **General Awareness** - 25 Qs / 25 marks
- **Detailed syllabus**:
  - **Reasoning**: Analogies, Coding-Decoding, Classification, Series, Blood Relation, Direction, Ranking, Syllogism, Venn Diagrams, Matrix.
  - **Numerical Aptitude**: Simplification, Percentage, Ratio, Average, Profit & Loss, SI/CI, Time-Work, Speed-Distance, Mensuration, Data Interpretation (basic).
  - **English**: Fill in the blanks, Synonyms/Antonyms, Spellings, Sentence Correction, Error Spotting, Comprehension, Grammar.
  - **GA**: History, Geography, Polity, Economics, General Science, Current Affairs, Static GK.
- **Salary**: Pay Level 1 (Rs 18,000 - Rs 56,900) + allowances. In-hand ~ Rs 22,000-28,000.

### 2.4 Postal Assistant (PA) / Sorting Assistant (SA)

- **Eligibility**: 12th Pass (10+2) from a recognized board; age 18-27 years.
- **Exam Pattern (CBE + Skill Test)** - 100 Qs / 100 marks / 120 min (160 min for PwBD):
  - **General Knowledge** - 30 Qs / 30 marks
  - **Mathematics** - 30 Qs / 30 marks
  - **English** - 30 Qs / 30 marks
  - **Regional Language** - 10 Qs / 10 marks
- **Syllabus**: Same as Postman for these 4 sections (10th standard topics).
- **Skill Test**: Data Entry Skill Test on computer - typing 7,200 key depressions per hour (30 wpm) in English OR Hindi, within 10 minutes. Qualifying in nature.
- **Salary**: Pay Level 4 (Rs 25,500 - Rs 81,100) + DA + HRA. In-hand ~ Rs 30,000-38,000.
- **Sorting Assistant (SA)**: Same eligibility and exam pattern; PA/SA are recruited together through SSC CGL / CHSL in some cycles and through dedicated SSC MTS / Post Office exams in others. Both fall under Pay Level 4.

## 3. Gramin Dak Sevak (GDS) - through India Post

The largest single recruitment by India Post; conducted by India Post itself (not SSC).

### 3.1 Posts
- **Branch Postmaster (BPM)**
- **Assistant Branch Postmaster (ABPM)**
- **Dak Sevak**

### 3.2 Eligibility
- **Educational**: 10th Pass (Matriculation) from a recognized board.
- **Marks**: Minimum 50% aggregate in 10th (relaxation of 5% i.e. 45% for SC/ST candidates).
- **Age**: 18-40 years (relaxation as per government norms; no upper-age relaxation for SC/ST usually).
- **Local language**: Candidate must have studied the local language (state's official language) at least up to 10th standard as a compulsory/elective subject.
- **Domicile**: Must be a permanent resident / domiciled in the applied state / postal circle.
- **Computer knowledge**: Basic computer certificate (60 hours / 3 months course from a recognized institute) is required at the time of engagement.

### 3.3 Selection Process - **No Entrance Exam**
- Selection is **purely on the basis of 10th class merit**:
  - **100% weightage** to Class 10 (Matriculation) marks.
  - No separate written examination, no interview.
  - Merit list prepared state/circle-wise, category-wise.
  - Tie-breaking: Higher aggregate marks in 10th -> higher marks in a specific subject -> date of birth (older preferred).
- **Merit List** is published on the official India Post GDS recruitment portal.
- **Document Verification** is then conducted for shortlisted candidates.
- **Basic computer training certificate** is mandatory before engagement (relaxation for candidates who have completed 10th from a state-run school with computer as a subject in some cycles).

### 3.4 Detailed Eligibility & Selection Criteria
- Candidate should not be a government servant.
- Only one application per candidate is allowed.
- Selection is done circle-wise (e.g., Andhra Pradesh, Bihar, Gujarat, Karnataka, Kerala, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Odisha, Punjab, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, UP, Uttarakhand, West Bengal, etc.).
- BPM carries higher responsibility and an additional TRCA (Time Related Continuity Allowance) slab.
- Engaging authority may conduct a simple local language proficiency test in some cases.
- Medical fitness as per DoP norms.
- Cycle-wise vacancies: **Cycle 1 (2018-19, ~41,000 posts)**, **Cycle 2 (2019-20, ~6,000 posts)**, **Cycle 3 (2020-21, ~3,831 posts)**, **Cycle 4 (2022-23, ~98,830+ posts - one of the largest)**, **Cycle 5 (2025, ~21,000+ posts)**, and further cycles announced periodically. State-wise break-ups available on indiapost.gov.in.

### 3.5 Salary
- **BPM (Branch Postmaster)**: TRCA Rs 12,000 - Rs 14,500/month (Category A/B branches), in-hand ~ Rs 14,000-16,000.
- **ABPM / Dak Sevak**: TRCA Rs 10,000 - Rs 12,000/month, in-hand ~ Rs 12,000-14,000.
- Allowances: DA, Medical, Travel, Bonus may be applicable per the cycle's notification.
- No regular Government servant pay, NPS benefits, or HRA; but the job is a Group-D equivalent engagement.
