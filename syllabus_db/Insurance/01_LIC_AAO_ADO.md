# LIC (Life Insurance Corporation of India) – AAO & ADO Examination

> **About LIC**: India's largest insurance company and largest institutional investor, established 1 September 1956 through nationalisation of 245+ insurance companies and provident societies. Headquarters: Mumbai. Owner: Government of India (96.5%). Listed on BSE (543526) and NSE (LICI) since May 2022. Operates 8 zonal offices, ~98,661 employees, and ~29 crore policyholders. Subsidiaries include LIC Housing Finance, LIC Mutual Fund, LIC Pension Fund, LIC Cards Services.
>
> **Source**: [licindia.in](https://www.licindia.in/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Life_Insurance_Corporation)

## 1. LIC AAO (Assistant Administrative Officer)

### Posts & Specialisations
- **AAO (Generalist)** – Administrative / operational roles across branches
- **AAO (IT)** – Information Technology
- **AAO (Chartered Accountant)** – Finance & Accounts
- **AAO (Legal)** – Legal & Compliance
- **AAO (Actuarial)** – Actuarial Sciences
- **AAO (Rajbhasha)** – Official Language (Hindi)

### Eligibility (All Posts)
| Post | Qualification | Age |
|------|---------------|-----|
| **Generalist** | Bachelor's in any discipline (60% for SC/ST/PwBD; 55% for others) | 21–30 |
| **IT** | B.Tech / B.E. / MCA (60% / 55%) | 21–30 |
| **Chartered Accountant** | Bachelor's + ICAI CA qualification | 21–30 |
| **Legal** | Bachelor's in Law (60% / 55%) + Bar Council enrolment | 21–30 |
| **Actuarial** | Bachelor's + 5+ Actuarial papers cleared (IAI/IAIS) | 21–30 |
| **Rajbhasha** | PG in Hindi/English with Hindi/English at graduation (60% / 55%) | 21–30 |

*Age relaxation as per Govt. of India rules for reserved categories (SC/ST/OBC/PwBD/Ex-servicemen).*

### Exam Pattern (3 Stages)

#### Stage 1: Preliminary Examination (Qualifying)
| Section | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---------|-----------|-------|----------|
| Reasoning Ability | 35 | 35 | 20 min |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 35 | 35 | 20 min |
| English Language | 30 | 30 | 20 min |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** | **60 min** |

- No sectional cut-off for Generalists (minimum qualifying marks: 16–18 for SC/ST/PwBD; 18 for others; 9–10 in English).
- **No negative marking** in Prelims (per latest 2026 notification).

#### Stage 2: Main Examination (Merit-Building)
**For Generalist Post:**
| Section | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---------|-----------|-------|----------|
| Reasoning Ability | 30 | 90 | 40 min |
| General Knowledge & Current Affairs | 30 | 60 | 20 min |
| Data Analysis & Interpretation | 30 | 90 | 40 min |
| Insurance & Financial Market Awareness | 30 | 60 | 20 min |
| **Objective Total** | **120** | **300** | **120 min** |
| English Language Descriptive (Email/Report/Precis) | 2 | 25 | 30 min |

**For Specialist Post:**
| Section | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---------|-----------|-------|----------|
| Reasoning Ability | 30 | 30 | 20 min |
| General Awareness (Insurance/Financial) | 20 | 20 | 10 min |
| Professional Knowledge | 50 | 100 | 30 min |
| **Objective Total** | **100** | **150** | **60 min** |
| Professional Knowledge Descriptive (4 of 6) | – | 50 | 60 min |
| English Descriptive | 2 | 25 | 30 min |

#### Stage 3: Interview – 60 marks
- Qualifying marks: 30 (UR/EWS/OBC), 27 (SC/ST/PwBD).
- **Final Merit = Mains Marks + Interview Marks**.
- Tie-breakers: higher qualification → senior in age.

### Detailed Syllabus (Common Sections)

#### Reasoning Ability
- Puzzles, Seating Arrangements, Coding-Decoding, Blood Relations
- Direction Test, Order & Ranking, Input-Output, Inequalities
- Syllogism, Alpha-Numeric-Symbol Series, Statement & Conclusion
- Analytical & Logical Reasoning, Machine Input-Output

#### Quantitative Aptitude
- Simplification, Number Series, HCF/LCM, Ratio & Proportion, Percentage
- Profit & Loss, SI/CI, Time & Work, Speed-Distance-Time
- Probability, Permutation & Combination, Average, Mixtures
- Mensuration, Quadratic Equations, Data Sufficiency
- Data Interpretation (Bar, Line, Pie, Tabular, Radar/Web)

#### English Language
- Reading Comprehension, Cloze Test, Fillers, Para-jumbles
- Error Spotting/Correction, Sentence Improvement
- Active/Passive Voice, Direct/Indirect Speech, Idioms & Phrases
- Antonyms/Synonyms, Vocabulary, Word Usage, Phrase Connectors

#### General Awareness, Insurance & Financial Market
- **Insurance**: Principles of Insurance, types (life/general/health), IRDAI regulations, LIC schemes (Jeevan Labh, New Endowment, New Jeevan Tarang, etc.), underwriting, claims, reinsurance
- **Financial Market**: Capital market (equity, debt, IPOs), Money market instruments, Mutual funds, Derivatives, SEBI/RBI
- **Banking**: History of banking, repo rate, monetary policy, NBFC, financial institutions
- **Current Affairs**: National/International events, Govt. schemes, Awards, Sports, Books & Authors, Science & Tech, Committees & Reports, Important Days

#### Professional Knowledge (Specialist-specific)
- **CA**: Corporate/Cost Accounting, Ind-AS, Auditing, Taxation, Corporate Law
- **Legal**: Industrial/Labour Laws, Insurance Act, Contract Act, CPC/CrPC, Companies Act 2013
- **Actuarial**: Life Insurance Maths, Demography, Risk Models, Probability & Statistics
- **IT**: DBMS, Networking, Software Engineering, Cyber Security, Data Structures

### Marking Scheme
- **No negative marking** in Prelims or Mains (as per latest 2026 notification).
- For descriptive papers – qualifying in nature.
- Old pattern used 0.25 negative (now discontinued).

### Salary Structure
- **Basic Pay**: Rs 53,600 (Pay Level 8 as per 7th CPC)
- **Gross In-hand**: ~Rs 60,000–65,000/month (Basic + DA + HRA + CCA + other allowances)
- **City Compensatory Allowance**: Varies by metro/urban/rural
- **Perks**: LTC, medical, pension, group insurance, vehicle/furniture loans, housing
- **CTC**: ~Rs 10–12 lakh per annum at initial scale

## 2. LIC ADO (Apprentice Development Officer)

### Post Description
- **Post**: Apprentice Development Officer (Life Insurance)
- **Role**: Field development officer; recruits, trains, and supervises insurance agents; achieves business targets
- **Tenure**: 2-year apprenticeship/training, then absorbed as Development Officer

### Eligibility
- **Education**: Bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognised university (no percentage bar mentioned in most notifications)
- **Age**: 18–30 years (relaxation for reserved categories as per Govt. norms)
- **Note**: Candidates employed in LIC (Grade III) or LIC agents are eligible under separate quotas (Employee/Agent category)

### Selection Process (3 Stages)
1. **Written Examination** (Prelims + Mains – both online)
2. **Interview**
3. **Medical Examination**

### Exam Pattern

#### Preliminary Examination (Qualifying – 1 hour)
| Section | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---------|-----------|-------|----------|
| Reasoning Ability | 35 | 35 | 20 min |
| Numerical Ability | 35 | 35 | 20 min |
| English Language | 30 | 30 | 20 min |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** | **60 min** |

#### Main Examination (Merit – 2 hours)

**For Open Market Category:**
| Section | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---------|-----------|-------|----------|
| Reasoning Ability & Numerical Ability | 50 | 50 | Combined 120 min |
| GK, Current Affairs & English (Grammar & Vocabulary) | 50 | 50 | – |
| Insurance & Financial Market Awareness (with special focus on Life Insurance) | 60 | 60 | – |
| **Total** | **160** | **160** | **120 min** |

**For Agents/Employee Category:** Same structure but 100 Qs/160 marks (different topic weightage with focus on insurance marketing/principles).

**Note**: Recent notification specifies combined 150 Qs/150 marks for Open category with separate sectional allocations.

#### Interview – 40 marks
- Final selection = Mains Marks + Interview Marks
- Medical fitness test mandatory

### Detailed Syllabus

#### Reasoning Ability
- Syllogism, Inequalities, Coding-Decoding, Blood Relation
- Direction & Distance, Order & Ranking, Input-Output
- Alphanumeric Series, Puzzles, Seating Arrangement

#### Numerical Ability
- Average, Ages, Ratio & Proportion, Percentage
- Profit & Loss, Speed/Time/Distance, Time & Work
- Boats & Streams, SI/CI, Probability
- Permutation & Combination, Partnership, Mixture & Allegation, Mensuration

#### English Language
- Reading Comprehension, Cloze Test, Antonym/Synonym
- Sentence Rearrangement, Phrase Replacement
- Idioms & Phrases, Fillers, Error Detection

#### General Knowledge & Current Affairs
- National & International current affairs, Awards & Honours
- Indices & Reports, Capital & Currency, Obituaries
- Union Budget, Monetary Policy, Government schemes

#### Insurance & Financial Market Awareness
- **Insurance Basics**: Principles of insurance, types of insurance (life, general, health), underwriting, claims management, reinsurance
- **LIC Products**: Endowment, Money-back, Term, ULIP, Pension plans (Jeevan Anand, New Jeevan Labh, etc.)
- **IRDAI**: Role, regulations, protection of policyholders' interests
- **Financial Markets**: Capital market, money market, mutual funds, derivatives
- **Insurance Marketing**: Bancassurance, distribution channels, agency model
- **Acts & Laws**: Insurance Act 1938, LIC Act 1956, IRDA Act 1999

### Marking Scheme
- **Negative Marking**: 0.25 (1/4) deduction per wrong answer in both Prelims and Mains
- English section of Mains may be qualifying

### Salary Structure
- **During Training (2 years)**: Stipend ~Rs 51,000–55,000/month (revised periodically; some notifications mention Rs 35,650–37,000)
- **After Confirmation (Development Officer)**: 
  - Basic Pay: Rs 36,400 (Pay Level 7) + DA + HRA + allowances
  - **In-hand**: ~Rs 45,000–55,000/month
  - Plus performance-based incentives on business targets
  - CTC: ~Rs 8–10 LPA including incentives

## 3. LIC HFL (LIC Housing Finance Limited) – Separate Recruitment

LIC HFL is a subsidiary of LIC, conducts its own recruitment for housing finance roles.

### Posts
- **Assistant**
- **Associate**
- **Senior Associate**

### Eligibility
- **Assistant**: Graduation (any discipline), Age 21–30
- **Associate**: Graduation + 1–3 years experience, Age 21–35
- **Senior Associate**: Graduation + 3–5 years experience, Age 21–35

### Exam Pattern
- **Online Written Test** (Reasoning, English Language, Numerical Ability, General Awareness) – 2 hours
- **Interview**
- **Document Verification & Medical**

### Detailed Syllabus
- **Reasoning**: Puzzles, seating arrangement, coding-decoding, syllogism, inequality, blood relations, direction test
- **English Language**: Reading comprehension, grammar, vocabulary, cloze test, error detection
- **Numerical Ability**: Simplification, arithmetic, algebra, DI (bar/pie/line/table)
- **General Awareness**: Banking/financial awareness, current affairs, static GK, insurance awareness, housing finance basics

### Salary
- **Assistant**: Rs 25,000–35,000/month
- **Associate**: Rs 35,000–45,000/month
- **Senior Associate**: Rs 45,000–55,000/month
- Plus allowances, medical, PF, performance incentives

## 4. LIC Assistant (Brief Overview)

- **Post**: Assistant (Clerical-cum-Field)
- **Eligibility**: Graduation (any discipline), Age 18–30 (relaxation for reserved)
- **Selection**: Prelims (Qualifying) → Mains (Merit) → Regional Language Test → Medical
- **Prelims**: 3 sections – Reasoning (35), Quant (35), English (30) – 100 Qs/100 marks/60 min
- **Mains**: 4 sections – Reasoning (40), Quant/GA (40), English (40), Computer Knowledge (40) – 200 Qs/200 marks
- **Salary**: Basic Rs 30,905 (Pay Level 3) + allowances; In-hand ~Rs 35,000–40,000
