# GIC, EPFO & ESIC Examinations – Comprehensive Study Guide

> **Parent Ministries**: Ministry of Finance (GIC) & Ministry of Labour and Employment (EPFO, ESIC)
> **Conducted By**: GIC Re (own), NTA (EPFO SSA/Steno), ESIC (own), UPSC (EPFO EO/AO)

---

## 1. GIC (General Insurance Corporation of India) – Scale I Officer (GIC AO)

### 1.1 About the Organisation
- **Incorporated**: 22 November 1972, Mumbai (re-notified as Indian Reinsurer in Nov 2000)
- **Type**: Public sector reinsurance company; "GIC Re" – 11th largest global reinsurer
- **Ownership**: Govt of India 85.78%
- **Listed on**: BSE (540755) and NSE (GICRE)
- **Subsidiaries (former)**: NICL, UIIC, OICL, NIACL (delinked 21 Mar 2003)
- **Motto**: "Āpatkāle Rakṣisyāmi" – "I shall protect you in times of distress"
- **Recruitment Body**: GIC Re conducts its own recruitment (GIC Re website: https://www.gicre.in)

### 1.2 Post & Vacancy
| Post | Scale | Approx CTC |
|------|-------|-----------|
| GIC AO (Administrative Officer) | Scale I | ~Rs 8.5–9 LPA |

### 1.3 Eligibility
- **Education**: Graduation (60% for Gen/OBC, 55% for SC/ST) in any discipline
- **Age**: 21 to 30 years (relaxation: SC/ST +5, OBC +3, PWD +10)
- **Nationality**: Indian citizen

### 1.4 Salary Structure
- **Basic Pay**: Rs 50,925/-
- **Pay Scale**: Rs 50,925 – 2,500 (4) – 60,925 – 2,850 (7) – 80,975
- **Allowances**: DA, HRA, CCA, Special Allowance, Lunch Allowance
- **Other benefits**: LTC, Medical, PF, Gratuity, Performance Linked Incentive
- **Gross Monthly**: Approx Rs 70,000–75,000 (approx CTC Rs 8.5–9 lakh)

### 1.5 Exam Pattern (2 Stages – Online)

#### **Stage 1 – Online Objective Test (200 Marks, 120 min)**
| Section | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---------|-----------|-------|----------|
| Reasoning Ability | 40 | 40 | Composite |
| English Language | 40 | 40 | Composite |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 40 | 40 | Composite |
| General Awareness (with Insurance focus) | 40 | 40 | Composite |
| **Total** | **160** | **160** | **120 min** |

- Negative marking: 0.25 per wrong answer
- Qualifying in each section + overall merit

#### **Stage 1 – Descriptive Test (separately on same/next day)**
| Component | Marks | Duration |
|-----------|-------|----------|
| Essay Writing (1 topic, ~250 words) | 20 | 30 min |
| Letter Writing (formal/informal) | 20 | (combined) |
| **Total** | **40** | **30 min** |

- Qualifying in nature; evaluated only for those who qualify objective

#### **Stage 2 – Interview (60 Marks)**
- Candidates shortlisted in ratio (usually 1:5 to 1:3) called
- Final merit = Objective (200) + Interview (60) = 260 marks

### 1.6 Detailed Syllabus

#### **Reasoning Ability (40 Qs)**
- Puzzles (Seating, Floor, Box, Scheduling)
- Seating Arrangements (Linear, Circular, Year-based)
- Syllogism, Coding-Decoding, Direction Sense
- Blood Relations, Order & Ranking, Alphanumeric Series
- Input-Output, Inequalities, Data Sufficiency
- Statement & Conclusion/Assumption

#### **English Language (40 Qs)**
- Reading Comprehension, Cloze Test
- Error Spotting / Sentence Correction
- Fill in the Blanks, Para Jumbles / Sentence Rearrangement
- Sentence Improvement, Active-Passive
- Direct-Indirect Speech, Idioms & Phrases
- Vocabulary: Synonyms, Antonyms, One-word Substitution

#### **Quantitative Aptitude (40 Qs)**
- Number System, Simplification, Approximation
- Percentage, Profit-Loss, SI/CI
- Ratio, Proportion, Partnership, Mixture-Alligation
- Time & Work, Pipes & Cisterns
- Speed-Time-Distance, Trains, Boats
- Mensuration (2D, 3D), Geometry
- Algebra, Trigonometry (basic), Probability
- Data Interpretation (Bar, Pie, Table, Line, Caselet)
- Data Sufficiency, Quadratic Equations

#### **General Awareness + Insurance Awareness (40 Qs)**
- **Banking**: RBI, monetary policy, fiscal policy, banking terms, NPA
- **Economics**: Indian Economy, Five Year Plans, NITI Aayog, GST, Budget highlights
- **Insurance Awareness** (HIGH WEIGHTAGE):
  - Principles of Insurance (Utmost good faith, Insurable interest, Indemnity, Proximate cause, Contribution, Subrogation)
  - Types: Life, General, Health, Marine, Fire, Motor
  - Insurance regulatory framework (IRDAI, Insurance Act 1938)
  - Reinsurance concepts (Treaty, Facultative)
  - GIC Re, LIC, public sector insurers
  - Premium calculation, Underwriting, Claims
- **Current Affairs**: National, International, Awards, Sports, Books
- **Static GK**: Indian Polity, Geography, History, Science

#### **Descriptive (Essay + Letter)**
- Topics: Insurance awareness, social issues, economy, finance, current affairs
- Letter formats: To editor, Complaint, Application, Business letter

### 1.7 Best Books
- **Reasoning**: R.S. Aggarwal, M.K. Pandey (Analytical Reasoning)
- **English**: Standard Objective English, Plinth to Paramount, Wren & Martin
- **Quant**: R.S. Aggarwal, Arun Sharma
- **GA + Insurance**: Standard Exam Guide's Banking & Insurance Awareness, Pratiyogita Darpan
- **Insurance Specific**: "Principles & Practice of Insurance" by P. Periasamy, IIIB Diploma books

## 2. EPFO (Employees' Provident Fund Organisation)

### 2.1 About the Organisation
- **Established**: 4 March 1952 (under EPF&MP Act 1952)
- **Headquarters**: East Kidwai Nagar, New Delhi
- **Parent**: Ministry of Labour & Employment
- **Statutory Body**: Central Board of Trustees (CBT) chaired by Union Labour Minister
- **Coverage**: 20+ crore members; Rs 15+ lakh crore assets
- **Three Schemes**: EPF 1952, EDLI 1976, EPS 1995
- **Structure**: 20 Zonal Offices, 149 Regional Offices, 113 District Offices
- **Manpower**: 20,000+ employees

### 2.2 EPFO SSA (Social Security Assistant) – Most Common Recruitment

#### **Eligibility**
- **Education**: 12th Pass (any stream)
- **Age**: 18 to 27 years (relaxation as per govt norms)
- **Typing Speed**: 35 wpm English / 30 wpm Hindi on computer (for skill test)

#### **Exam Pattern (2 Stages)**
| Stage | Component | Details |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| **I – CBE (Prelims + Mains combined)** | 4 sections, 100 Qs, 200 marks, 90 min | English, Reasoning, Quant, GA |
| **II – Skill Test** | Typing on computer | Qualifying |

#### **Detailed Pattern (CBE)**
| Section | Qs | Marks |
|---------|-----|-------|
| English Language | 30 | 60 |
| Reasoning | 30 | 60 |
| Numerical Ability (Quant) | 30 | 60 |
| General Awareness (with EPFO focus) | 20 | 40 |
| **Total** | **100** | **200** |

- 90 min composite, negative marking 0.33
- Sectional + overall cut-off; merit based on marks

#### **Detailed Syllabus – SSA**
- **English**: Reading Comprehension, Cloze, Error Detection, Sentence Improvement, Para Jumbles, Vocabulary
- **Reasoning**: Puzzles, Seating Arrangement, Coding-Decoding, Syllogism, Inequalities, Blood Relations, Direction, Data Sufficiency
- **Quant**: Number Series, Simplification, DI (Table, Bar, Pie), Percentage, Average, Ratio, SI/CI, Time-Work, Time-Distance, Mensuration, Algebra
- **GA**: Indian Polity, History, Geography, Economics, Current Affairs; **EPFO Schemes** (EPF, EPS, EDLI), UAN, EPF calculation, contribution rates, Pension scheme, latest initiatives, UAN, ABKRY, ECR

### 2.3 EPFO Stenographer (Grade 'C')

#### **Eligibility**
- **Education**: 12th Pass
- **Age**: 18 to 27 years
- **Skill**: Dictation 10 min @ 80 wpm; Transcription 50 min (English) / 65 min (Hindi)

#### **Exam Pattern (2 Stages)**
- **Stage I – CBE**: 100 Qs, 200 marks – English (60), Reasoning (60), GA (40), Quant (40) – 90 min
- **Stage II – Skill Test (Stenography)**: Qualifying
  - Dictation 10 min @ 80 wpm
  - Transcription: 50 min English (on computer) / 65 min Hindi

#### **Syllabus**: Similar to SSA; English section has higher weightage including comprehension, grammar, vocabulary

### 2.4 EPFO Assistant (Section Supervisor – through LDCE)
- Internal promotion-based Limited Departmental Competitive Examination (LDCE)
- 10% by direct recruitment (when posts notified) – 12th Pass, age 18–27
- Exam conducted by **NTA** (https://exams.nta.ac.in/EPFO-LDCE/)
- Pattern: 4 sections, 100 Qs, 200 marks, 90 min (English, Reasoning, Quant, GA)

### 2.5 UPSC EPFO EO/AO (Enforcement Officer / Accounts Officer)
- **Post**: Group B, Pay Level 8 (Rs 47,600 – 1,51,100)
- **Recruitment**: Union Public Service Commission
- **Eligibility**: Bachelor's degree, age 18–30
- **Exam Pattern**:
  - **Stage I – Pen & Paper**: General Studies + English (Conventional, 100 + 100 marks)
  - **Stage II – General Studies + Economics/Commerce/Law etc.** (MCQ 200 marks)
  - **Stage III – Interview/Personality Test** (100 marks)
- *(Detailed in separate UPSC file)*

### 2.6 Best Books for EPFO
- **Standard General Knowledge Compendium** + EPFO-specific schemes notes
- **NCERT Class 6–12** (History, Geography, Polity, Economics)
- **Standard Exam GuideSSC / Banking** books for Quant, Reasoning, English
- **EPFO Manual & EPF Act 1952** (key sections, amendments)
- **Pratiyogita Darpan** for current affairs
- Specific: "EPFO: Practice Papers" by Disha, "EPFO Social Security Assistant" by Standard Exam Guide---

## 3. ESIC (Employees' State Insurance Corporation)

### 3.1 About the Organisation
- **Established**: 24 February 1952 (under ESI Act, 1948)
- **Headquarters**: New Delhi
- **Parent**: Ministry of Labour & Employment
- **Director General**: Ashok Kumar Singh, IAS
- **Wage Limit**: Rs 21,000/month (contribution @ 4%: 3.25% employer + 0.75% employee)
- **Benefits**: Medical, Sickness, Maternity, Disablement, Dependants, Funeral
- **Infrastructure**: 1418 dispensaries, 145 hospitals, 19,387+ beds, 11 medical colleges, 2 dental colleges
- **Zones**: 5 across India; Manpower 21,000+
- **Post Cadres**: UDC, MTS, Stenographer, SSO, Paramedical, IMO (Medical Officers via UPSC CMSE)

### 3.2 ESIC SSO (Social Security Officer / Manager Gr-II)

#### **Eligibility**
- **Education**: Bachelor's degree from recognised university
- **Age**: 21 to 27 years (relaxation as per rules)
- **Desirable**: Knowledge of computer, insurance/social security laws

#### **Exam Pattern (3 Stages)**

##### **Phase I – Preliminary Exam (Qualifying)**
| Section | Qs | Marks | Duration |
|---------|-----|-------|----------|
| Reasoning | 25 | 50 | Composite |
| English | 25 | 50 | Composite |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 25 | 50 | Composite |
| General Awareness (with Insurance) | 25 | 50 | Composite |
| **Total** | **100** | **200** | **60 min** |

- 2 marks per question; negative 0.5
- Only for shortlisting (no sectional cut-off in some years)

##### **Phase II – Main Exam (200 Qs, 200 Marks)**
| Section | Qs | Marks |
|---------|-----|-------|
| Reasoning/Intelligence | 50 | 50 |
| English Language | 50 | 50 |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 50 | 50 |
| **General Awareness + Insurance & Allied Subjects** (Insurance Marketing, Financial Accounting, Cost Accounting, Principles of Insurance) | 50 | 50 |
| **Total** | **200** | **200** |

- 2 hours composite; negative 0.5
- **The Insurance section includes**:
  - Insurance Marketing: concepts, customer service, market segmentation, distribution channels
  - Financial Accounting: Accounting concepts, journal/ledger, trial balance, financial statements
  - Cost Accounting: Cost concepts, elements, methods
  - Principles of Insurance: All principles + IRDAI regulations, types, reinsurance

##### **Phase III – Computer Skill Test + Descriptive (Qualifying)**
- **Computer Skill Test**: 30 marks
  - Preparation of PowerPoint presentation (10)
  - Microsoft Excel (basic formulas, charts) (10)
  - MS Word (formatting, mail merge basics) (10)
- **Descriptive Test**: 30 marks
  - Essay (English) on insurance/social security – 150–200 words
  - Precis writing / Letter writing

#### **Detailed Syllabus – SSO**

- **Reasoning**: Puzzles, seating arrangement, syllogism, coding-decoding, inequalities, data sufficiency
- **English**: RC, cloze, sentence correction, vocabulary, grammar, para jumbles
- **Quant**: All banking-level topics + DI (heavy weightage)
- **GA**: Current affairs + Static GK + **Special focus on:**
  - ESIC Act 1948, EPF Act 1952, Payment of Wages Act, Industrial Disputes Act
  - Insurance principles, types, IRDAI, GIC Re
  - Indian Economy, Budget, RBI policy
  - Social security schemes (PMJJBY, PMSBY, APY, NPS)

#### **Salary – SSO**
- Pay Level 6: Rs 35,400 – 1,12,400 (7th CPC)
- In-hand: ~Rs 40,000–50,000 initially
- Approx CTC: Rs 6–7 LPA

### 3.3 ESIC UDC (Upper Division Clerk)

#### **Eligibility**
- **Education**: 12th Pass (any stream)
- **Age**: 18 to 27 years
- **Typing**: 30 wpm English / 25 wpm Hindi (qualifying)

#### **Exam Pattern (2 Stages)**
- **Stage I – CBE (Prelims + Mains combined)**: 100 Qs, 200 marks, 90 min
  - English (60), Reasoning (60), Quant (60), GA (20)
- **Stage II – Computer Skill Test**: 25 marks (qualifying)
  - Word Processing (10), Spreadsheet (10), PowerPoint (5)

#### **Syllabus**: Similar to EPFO SSA with ESIC focus
- **GA**: ESIC history, ESI Act, benefits, dispensary network, ESIC hospitals/medical colleges, ongoing schemes, recent initiatives

### 3.4 ESIC MTS (Multi Tasking Staff)
- **Eligibility**: 10th Pass (Matriculation) from recognised board
- **Age**: 18 to 25 years
- **Selection**: Direct recruitment or through SSC/ESIC exam
- **Pattern**: CBE – Reasoning, English, GA, Numerical Ability – 100 Qs, 200 marks, 90 min
- **Basic syllabus**: Class 8–10 level

### 3.5 ESIC Stenographer
- **Eligibility**: 12th Pass
- **Age**: 18 to 27 years
- **Pattern**: CBE + Skill Test (Stenography)
- **Skill Test**: 80 wpm dictation (10 min), transcription 50 min English / 65 min Hindi

### 3.6 ESIC Paramedical Posts
- **Posts**: Staff Nurse, Pharmacist, Lab Technician, ECG Technician, Radiographer, Dietician, OT Assistant, etc.
- **Eligibility**: 10+2 (Science) / Diploma / Degree in relevant field, age 18–27
- **Exam**: CBE – 125 Qs, 125 marks, 90 min
  - Technical/Professional (75 Qs, 75 marks)
  - Reasoning (10), English (10), GA (10), Quantitative (10), Computer/IT (10)
- **Pay**: Pay Level 5 (Pharmacist) or 6/7 for staff nurses

### 3.7 ESIC IMO (Insurance Medical Officer)
- **Recruitment**: Through **UPSC Combined Medical Services Examination (CMSE)**
- **Eligibility**: MBBS degree, age 21–30 (relaxation as per rules)
- **Service**: Central Health Service (CHS) equivalent
- **Pay**: Pay Level 10 (Rs 56,100 – 1,77,500)
- *Covered in detail in UPSC / Medical officer file*

### 3.8 Best Books for ESIC
- **GA**: Pratiyogita Darpan, Standard Exam Guide, Standard General Knowledge Compendium- **Insurance**: IIIB (Insurance Institute of India) IC-33, IC-38, IC-46; Principles & Practice of Insurance (Periasamy)
- **Reasoning/Quant/English**: Same as banking books (R.S. Aggarwal, Arun Sharma, Standard Objective English)
- **ESIC-specific**: "ESIC SSO Manager Complete Guide" by Disha/Standard Exam Guide- **ESI Act 1948** & **EPF Act 1952** (bare acts) for policy/legal sections

## 7. Common Preparation Strategy

1. **Foundation (3 months)**: Build concepts in Reasoning, Quant, English using standard banking books
2. **Insurance Domain (1 month)**: Special focus on IRDAI regulations, insurance principles, types, GIC Re, ESIC/EPF schemes – read IIIB IC-33 + Act Bare
3. **Current Affairs (Daily)**: 30 min daily on The Hindu + monthly compilations
4. **Mock Tests (1 month)**: 1 mock daily + analysis; focus on accuracy and speed
5. **Revision (15 days)**: Formulae, static GK, insurance glossary, latest govt schemes
