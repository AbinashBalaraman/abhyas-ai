# PSU Bank & Other Specialized Banking Exams (BOB, PNB, Canara, Indian Bank, etc.)

> Comprehensive syllabus & exam pattern guide for individual PSU bank recruitments conducted outside the IBPS/SBI common framework. This document covers Bank of Baroda (BOB), Punjab National Bank (PNB), Canara Bank, Indian Bank, and other major PSU bank SO/PO/Clerk recruitments.

---

## 1. Bank of Baroda (BOB) – Specialist Officer / Various Posts

### About BOB
- **Founded**: 20 July 1908 by Maharaja Sayajirao Gaekwad III in Baroda (Vadodara), Gujarat.
- **HQ**: Vadodara, Gujarat.
- **Type**: Public Sector Bank (Government of India holds 63.97%).
- **Branches**: 8,400+ in India; 107 branches/offices in 24 countries overseas.
- **Tagline**: "India's International Bank".
- **Subsidiaries**: BOBCARD, BOB Capital Markets, Nainital Bank, IndiaFirst Life Insurance, Baroda BNP Paribas AMC, BarodaSun Technologies.
- **Recent Merger**: 1 April 2019 — Dena Bank + Vijaya Bank merged with BOB (first 3-way PSU bank merger).
- **MD & CEO**: Debadatta Chand.
- **Official**: https://www.bankofbaroda.bank.in | Careers: https://www.bankofbaroda.in/career

### Posts (BOB)
1. **BOB Specialist Officer (SO)** – IT, HR, Marketing, Finance, Treasury, Risk Management, Legal, Agriculture, IT Security, Data Analyst, Cyber Security, Forex, Statistician, Economist, Security Officer.
2. **BOB Probationary Officer (PO)** – (Separate from IBPS PO; rare; through BOB own recruitment).
3. **BOB Clerk / Junior Assistant** – (Separate from IBPS Clerk; BOB-specific).

### Eligibility
| Post | Age (as on cut-off) | Qualification |
|------|---------------------|----------------|
| SO (General) | 21 – 30 years | Degree + relevant PG/professional qualification in the stream |
| SO (Senior/Manager) | 25 – 35/40 years | As above + experience (2–10 years) |
| PO | 20 – 28 years | Any Degree (60% – 55% for SC/ST) |
| Clerk | 20 – 28 years | Any Degree (55% – 50% for SC/ST) |

- Age relaxation: SC/ST +5, OBC +3, PWD +10, Ex-servicemen as per rules.

### Exam Pattern (BOB-Specific)

#### BOB SO (Specialist Officer) – Online Test + Interview
| Section | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---------|-----------|-------|------|
| Reasoning Ability | 50 | 50 | Composite |
| English Language | 50 | 50 | 2 hrs 30 mins |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 50 | 50 | |
| Professional Knowledge (related to post) | 50 | 75 | |
| **Total** | **200** | **225** | **2h 30m** |

- Negative marking: 0.25 per wrong.
- Shortlisting for interview = sectional + aggregate cut-off.
- **Interview**: 50–100 marks (post-specific).

#### BOB PO – Online Test (Tier-I) + Interview
| Section | Qs | Marks | Time |
|---------|----|-------|------|
| Reasoning | 50 | 50 | Composite 2 hrs |
| English | 50 | 50 | |
| Quant | 50 | 50 | |
| General Awareness (with Banking) | 50 | 50 | |
| **Total** | **200** | **200** | |

- Interview: 50 marks (merit = 80% online + 20% interview).

#### BOB Clerk
- Pre: 100 Qs / 100 marks (Eng-30, Num-35, Reas-35). 1 hour.
- Mains: 190 Qs / 200 marks (Eng-40, Num-50, Reas-50, GA/Banking-50). 2 hr 30 m.
- No interview.

### Detailed Syllabus (BOB)

#### Reasoning Ability
- Puzzles, Seating Arrangement, Order/Ranking, Direction Sense, Blood Relations, Syllogism, Coding-Decoding, Machine Input, Inequalities, Data Sufficiency, Statement-Assumption/Conclusion, Alphanumeric Series.

#### English Language
- Reading Comprehension, Cloze Test, Para Jumbles, Fillers, Error Spotting, Sentence Correction, Synonyms/Antonyms, Idioms/Phrases, Vocabulary, Word Substitution.

#### Quantitative Aptitude
- Simplification, Approximation, Number Series, Quadratic Equations, Data Interpretation (Table, Bar, Line, Pie, Caselet), Percentage, Profit-Loss, SI/CI, Ratio, Time-Work, Speed-Distance, Mensuration, Probability, Permutation-Combination, Average, Mixture-Alligation.

#### General / Banking Awareness
- Current Affairs (national + international), Banking Terminology, RBI/Monetary Policy, Budget, Economic Survey, Indian Financial System, Stock Market, Insurance, Govt Schemes, Awards, Summits, Sports, Books & Authors.

#### Professional Knowledge (IT)
- DBMS (SQL, NoSQL), Data Structures, OOP, Operating Systems, Networking, Web Technologies (HTML/CSS/JS), Cyber Security, Cloud Computing, Software Engineering, SDLC, Agile.

#### Professional Knowledge (Finance/Treasury/Risk)
- Financial Management, Accounting Standards (Ind AS/IFRS), Treasury Operations, ALM, Basel Norms, NPA Management, Risk Management (Credit/Market/Operational), Derivatives, Mutual Funds, Balance Sheet Analysis, Ratio Analysis.

#### Professional Knowledge (HR/Marketing)
- HRM principles, Recruitment & Selection, Training, Performance Management, Industrial Relations, Labour Laws, Marketing Mix, Digital Marketing, Brand Management, Consumer Behaviour.

#### Professional Knowledge (Legal)
- Banking Regulation Act, RBI Act, SARFAESI, Companies Act, Contract Act, Consumer Protection, Negotiable Instruments Act, Cyber Law, IPC/CrPC (basics).

#### Professional Knowledge (Agriculture)
- Crop production, Soil Science, Agricultural Finance, KCC, NABARD, Agricultural Marketing, Farm Management, Govt Agri Schemes, Crop Insurance (PMFBY).

## 6. Common Exam Pattern for PSU Bank SO (Standardized)

### Online Test (Objective) – 200 Questions / 200–225 Marks
| Section | Qs | Marks | Duration |
|---------|----|-------|----------|
| Reasoning Ability | 50 | 50 | Composite 2h–2h 30m |
| English Language | 50 | 50 | |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 50 | 50 | |
| Professional Knowledge (SO) / GA (PO) | 50 | 50–75 | |
| **Total** | **200** | **200–225** | **2h–2h 30m** |

### Descriptive Test (50 marks) – optional / for some banks
- Letter Writing
- Essay Writing (Banking, Economy, Current Affairs)
- Precis Writing
- Comprehension
- Duration: 30 minutes.

### Interview / GD-PI
- SO Interview: 50–100 marks.
- PO Interview/GD: 50 marks.
- Final weightage varies:
  - 80% Online + 20% Interview (most common).
  - 70% Online + 30% Interview (some banks).

### Negative Marking
- 0.25 per wrong answer in objective sections.

### Sectional Cut-off + Overall Cut-off
- Must clear sectional + aggregate cut-offs to qualify for interview.

### Standard Detailed Syllabus (Common across PSU Bank SO)

#### Reasoning Ability (50 Qs)
| Topic | Weightage |
|-------|-----------|
| Puzzles & Seating Arrangement | 15–20 Qs |
| Coding-Decoding, Syllogism | 5–7 Qs |
| Inequalities, Data Sufficiency | 5 Qs |
| Blood Relations, Direction Sense | 3–5 Qs |
| Order/Ranking, Series | 3–5 Qs |
| Statement-Conclusion/Assumption | 3–5 Qs |
| Input-Output, Machine Input | 0–2 Qs |

#### English Language (50 Qs)
| Topic | Weightage |
|-------|-----------|
| Reading Comprehension | 10–15 Qs |
| Cloze Test (new pattern) | 5–7 Qs |
| Para Jumbles, Sentence Rearrangement | 5 Qs |
| Error Spotting / Sentence Correction | 5 Qs |
| Fillers (single/double) | 3–5 Qs |
| Vocabulary (Synonyms/Antonyms/Idioms) | 5 Qs |
| Sentence Improvement, Voice/Speech | 3–5 Qs |
| Word Substitution, Spelling | 2–3 Qs |

#### Quantitative Aptitude (50 Qs)
| Topic | Weightage |
|-------|-----------|
| Data Interpretation (Table, Bar, Pie, Line, Caselet) | 15–20 Qs |
| Simplification/Approximation | 5 Qs |
| Number Series, Quadratic Equations | 5 Qs |
| Arithmetic (SI/CI, Profit-Loss, Ratio, Time-Work) | 10–15 Qs |
| Mensuration, Geometry | 2–3 Qs |
| Probability, P&C | 2–3 Qs |
| Data Sufficiency, Caselet | 2–3 Qs |

#### General / Banking Awareness (50 Qs)
| Topic | Weightage |
|-------|-----------|
| Current Affairs (last 6 months) | 15–20 Qs |
| Banking Awareness (RBI, Monetary Policy, Schemes) | 10–15 Qs |
| Indian Economy, Budget, Economic Survey | 5–7 Qs |
| Government Schemes, Reports | 3–5 Qs |
| International News, Summits, Awards | 3–5 Qs |
| Sports, Books, Obituaries | 2–3 Qs |

#### Professional Knowledge (50 Qs) – SO Specific
- **IT Officer**: DBMS (SQL), OOP, OS, Networking, Web, Cloud, Cyber Security, AI/ML, SDLC, Data Structures.
- **Finance/CA**: Accounting Standards (Ind AS), FM, Treasury, ALM, Basel, NPA, Risk, Derivatives, Tax.
- **HR**: HRM, Recruitment, Training, PMS, IR, Labour Laws.
- **Marketing**: 4Ps/7Ps, Digital, Brand, Consumer Behaviour, CRM.
- **Legal**: Banking Reg Act, RBI Act, SARFAESI, NI Act, Companies Act, IT Act, PMLA, Consumer Protection.
- **Agriculture**: Agronomy, Soil, Agri Finance, KCC, NABARD, PMFBY, PM-KISAN, Farm Management.
- **Treasury/Forex**: FEMA, FOREX, Derivatives, ALM, Basel, RBI Master Directions, IFS Code.
- **Risk Management**: Credit/Market/Operational Risk, Basel I/II/III, Stress Testing, ICAAP.
- **Security Officer**: Physical Security, Fire Safety, Vigilance, RBI guidelines, IT Security.

## Summary (Key Takeaways)

1. **Standardized Pattern**: All PSU bank individual recruitments (BOB, PNB, Canara, Indian Bank, UBI, BoM, CBI, UCO, IOB, BoI) follow a **near-identical exam structure**: 200 Qs / 200–225 marks covering Reasoning + English + Quant + Professional Knowledge/GA, plus Interview.
2. **Eligibility**: 21–30 years (Junior SO/PO); 25–35/40 (Manager/Senior); relaxation as per GoI.
3. **Differentiator**: The **Professional Knowledge** section is what separates SO from PO/Clerk. Aspirants must specialize in their stream (IT, Finance, HR, Legal, Agriculture, Treasury, Risk, etc.).
4. **Negative Marking**: 0.25 per wrong answer.
5. **Selection Ratio**: Online Test → Interview (and/or GD) → Final Merit.
6. **Common Resources**: R.S. Aggarwal (Reas+Quant), Standard Objective English (Eng), Standard Exam Guide(Banking Awareness), Standard Previous Years Solved PapersPrakashan (Chapterwise), specialized books for Professional Knowledge.
7. **Key Official Sites**: https://www.bankofbaroda.in, https://www.pnbindia.in, https://www.canarabank.com, https://indianbank.bank.in, plus career pages of UBI, BoM, CBI, UCO, IOB, BoI.

> **Disclaimer**: Exam patterns, eligibility, and syllabus are based on the latest publicly available notifications and may change as per bank's discretion. Always refer to the official notification PDF of the specific recruitment for authoritative information.
