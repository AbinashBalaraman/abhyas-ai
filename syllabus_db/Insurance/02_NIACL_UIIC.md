## 1. NIACL AO (Administrative Officer - Generalist/Specialist)

### About the Organization
The New India Assurance Company Limited (NIACL) is India's largest public-sector general insurer, founded on **23 July 1919** by Sir Dorabji Tata and nationalized in 1973. Headquartered in **Mumbai, Maharashtra**, it is listed on NSE (`NIACL`) and BSE (`540769`). Government of India holds **85.44%** stake. CMD: **Girija Subramanian**. FY 2025-26 Gross Written Premium: **₹47,174 crore** with **12.74%** market share; PAT ₹1,384 crore. Present in **25+ countries** with **1,680+ offices**. Designated as a **Domestic Systemically Important Insurer (D-SII)** by IRDAI. Workforce: ~10,951 (Mar 2025).

### Posts: AO (Scale I)
- Generalist
- Information Technology (IT)
- Finance
- Legal
- Automobile Engineering
- Medical
- Actuarial

### Eligibility
| Stream | Qualification | Age |
|---|---|---|
| **Generalist** | Graduation with **60%** (55% for SC/ST/PwBD) | 21–30 years |
| **IT** | B.E./B.Tech (CS/IT) or M.Tech/MCA | 21–30 years |
| **Finance** | Graduation/PG in Commerce, Finance, Economics, Statistics, or Mathematics (with required subjects) | 21–30 years |
| **Legal** | LLB (Bachelor of Law) | 21–30 years |
| **Automobile Engineer** | B.E./B.Tech in Automobile Engineering | 21–30 years |
| **Medical** | MBBS | 21–30 years |
| **Actuarial** | Graduation + passed Actuarial papers (Institute of Actuaries of India) | 21–30 years |

Age relaxation: SC/ST +5 yrs, OBC +3 yrs, PwBD +10–15 yrs, Ex-servicemen as per rules.

### Exam Pattern (3 Stages)

- **Negative marking**: 0.25 per wrong answer
- **Medium**: English (only English), English/Hindi (Reasoning & Quant)

#### Stage 2 - Mains (Merit) — Generalist
| Section | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reasoning Ability | 50 | 50 | 40 min |
| English Language | 50 | 50 | 40 min |
| General Awareness | 50 | 50 | 30 min |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 50 | 50 | 40 min |
| **Total** | **200** | **200** | **150 min** |

#### Stage 2 - Mains — Specialist
| Section | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reasoning | 40 | 40 | 30 min |
| English | 40 | 40 | 30 min |
| General Awareness | 40 | 40 | 25 min |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 40 | 40 | 30 min |
| Professional/Technical Knowledge | 40 | 40 | 35 min |
| **Total** | **200** | **200** | – |

#### Descriptive Test (online, qualifying for some posts)
| Topic | Marks |
|---|---|
| Essay Writing | 20 |
| Letter Writing | 10 |
| **Total** | **30 marks, 30 min** |

#### Stage 3 - Interview
- **75 marks** (weightage ratio Online Exam : Interview = **80:20**)
- Final merit = Mains + Interview

### Detailed Syllabus

#### English Language
Reading Comprehension, Cloze Test, Fill in the blanks, Error Spotting, Sentence Correction, Para Jumbles, Vocabulary, Grammar, Idioms & Phrases, Word Swap, Sentence Rearrangement, Column-based/Spelling Errors.

#### Reasoning Ability
Puzzles & Seating Arrangement (linear/circular/triangular/uncertain), Syllogism, Coded Inequalities, Coding-Decoding, Blood Relations, Direction/Distance, Ranking/Alphabet Test, Data Sufficiency, Input-Output, Alphanumeric/Number Series, Logical Reasoning.

#### Quantitative Aptitude
Simplification/Approximation, Number Series, Percentage, Ratio & Proportion, Profit & Loss, SI/CI, Time & Work, Time-Speed-Distance, Mensuration (2D & 3D), Permutation/Combination, Probability, Data Interpretation (Table, Pie, Line, Bar, Caselet, Mixed DI), Quadratic/Linear Equations.

#### General Awareness (Insurance & Banking Focus)
- **Current Affairs** (national/international), Summits, Awards, Books & Authors, Sports, Defence, Obituary
- **Banking Awareness**: Indian Financial System, RBI, History of Indian Banking, Monetary & Credit Policy, Union Budget, Capital/Money Market, International Financial Institutions (IMF, World Bank, ADB, BIS), Government Schemes, Economic Terminology, Abbreviations
- **Insurance Focus**: IRDAI, Insurance Act 1938, types of insurance (Life/General/Health), underwriting, claims management, reinsurance, regulatory framework, recent insurance policies & schemes, InsurTech, Bancassurance

### Salary
- **Basic Pay**: ₹50,925 (Pay Level 10/11 of 7th CPC)
- **Gross In-hand**: ~₹60,000–₹65,000/month (City Allowance, DA, HRA, CCA, Medical, LTC, other perks included)
- Perks: HRA/lease accommodation, medical, travel allowance, LTC, pension (NPS), group insurance, housing/vehicle loans at concessional rates.

### Marking Scheme
- 1 mark per correct answer
- **0.25 negative marking** for wrong answers
- No penalty for un-attempted questions

## 2. NIACL Assistant (Class III)

### Posts & Eligibility
- **Post**: Assistant (Class III)
- **Qualification**: Graduation with **60%** (55% for SC/ST/PwBD)
- **Age**: 21–30 years (relaxation as per norms)

### Exam Pattern (2 Tiers)

#### Tier 1 - Preliminary (Qualifying)
| Section | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| English Language | 30 | 30 | 20 min |
| Reasoning Ability | 35 | 35 | 20 min |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 35 | 35 | 20 min |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** | **60 min** |

- Negative marking: 0.25; Medium: English/Hindi

#### Tier 2 - Mains (Merit)
| Section | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reasoning | 40 | 50 | 30 min |
| English | 40 | 50 | 30 min |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 40 | 50 | 30 min |
| General Awareness | 40 | 50 | 25 min |
| English Language (Descriptive – Letter/Essay) | – | 50 | 30 min |
| **Total** | **160** | **250** | **145 min** |

#### Regional Language Test (LPT)
- Qualifying in nature
- Candidate must read/write/speak the regional language of the state applied for
- Failure = disqualification even after final selection

### Detailed Syllabus
Syllabus is similar to NIACL AO but slightly lighter. Key topics:
- **English**: RC, Cloze Test, Para Jumbles, Fillers, Error Detection, Vocabulary
- **Reasoning**: Puzzles, Seating Arrangement, Syllogism, Coding-Decoding, Blood Relations, Input-Output, Alphanumeric Series
- **Quantitative Aptitude**: Simplification, Number Series, DI, Arithmetic (Profit/Loss, SI/CI, Ratio, %, TSD, Time & Work), Mensuration
- **GA**: Current Affairs, Banking, Insurance basics, IRDAI, Static GK
- **Descriptive English**: Letter (formal/informal), Essay (150–200 words)

### Salary
- **Basic Pay**: ₹32,795 (Pay Level – as per 7th CPC)
- **Gross In-hand**: ~₹40,000–₹45,000/month
- Perks: DA, HRA, Medical, CCA, Transport Allowance, NPS, festival advance, housing loan, vehicle loan at subsidized rates

## 3. UIIC AO (United India Insurance AO)

### About the Organization
**United India Insurance Company Limited (UIICL)** was incorporated on **18 February 1938** in Chennai and nationalized in **1972** under the General Insurance Business (Nationalisation) Act. Headquartered in **Chennai, Tamil Nadu**. Chairman & MD: **Bhupesh Sushil Rahul**. After GIBNA Amendment Act 2002, UIIC became independent (no longer GIC subsidiary). Operates **30 regional offices** and **1,400+ operating offices** nationwide with **~9,306 employees** (2023-24). Revenue: ₹17,644 crore (FY 2022-23). Listed PSU general insurer.

### Posts: AO (Scale I) – Generalist & Specialist
Same streams as NIACL: Generalist, IT, Finance, Legal, Automobile Engineering, Medical, Actuarial.

### Eligibility
- **Qualification**: Graduation with **60%** (55% for SC/ST/PwBD)

## 4. UIIC Assistant — Brief Overview

- **Post**: UIIC Assistant (Class III)
- **Qualification**: Graduation with **60%** (55% for reserved)
- **Age**: 21–30 years
- **Exam Pattern**: 2 Tiers (Prelims + Mains) + LPT
  - **Prelims**: 100 Qs, 100 marks, 60 min (English 30, Reasoning 35, Quant 35)

- **Salary**: Pay Level 7 — Basic ~₹32,795; In-hand ~₹40,000+

## 5. Other General Insurance Companies (PSUs)

### National Insurance Company Limited (NIC)
- Founded **1906**, nationalized 1972
- Headquarters: **Kolkata**
- Subsidiaries: NIC Asia (Nepal), LIC-NIC joint ventures
- Products: Motor, Health, Fire, Marine, Crop, Personal Accident
- Website: nationalinsurance.nic.co.in
- Recruitment: NIC AO, NIC Assistant (Class III), NICL HEO (Handsome Executive Officer)
- **Note**: As part of PSU disinvestment/merger discussions, NIC, OICL, UIIC were to be merged into a single entity, but the **merger was demerged in 2018**; the three continue to operate separately.

### Oriental Insurance Company Limited (OICL)
- Founded **1947** in Delhi, nationalized 1972
- Headquarters: **New Delhi**
- Products: Motor, Health, Fire, Marine, Crop Insurance, Overseas Mediclaim
- Website: orientalinsurance.org.in
- Recruitment: OICL AO (Administrative Officer), OICL Assistant
- D-SII candidate; large global footprint

### GIPSA & Restructuring Context
- **GIPSA** (General Insurance Public Sector Association) — earlier umbrella of 4 PSU insurers (NIC, NIA, OICL, UIIC) for common services like TPA, surveyor empanelment.
- In 2018, government proposed **mega-merger** of these three (excluding NIA) into a single entity, but the proposal was shelved/deferred.
- All four PSUs remain **independent** today, each with its own recruitment, branding, and operations.
