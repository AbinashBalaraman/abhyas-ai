# Specialized Banking & Currency Printing Roles: RBI, NABARD, BoI & SPMCIL

Comprehensive guide covering specialized posts in Reserve Bank of India (RBI), National Bank for Agriculture & Rural Development (NABARD), Bank of India (BoI), and Security Printing & Minting Corporation of India Ltd (SPMCIL – CNP Nashik / BNP Dewas).

---

## 1. RBI Grade C (Manager – Specialized Streams)

RBI periodically recruits Grade C Managers in specialized streams — **Legal, Accounts/Finance, IT, Protocol & Security, Library, Rajbhasha (Hindi), etc.** Structure mirrors Grade B but is stream-specific.

### Posts
- Manager (Legal) – LLB required
- Manager (Accounts) – CA / ICWA / MBA (Finance)
- Manager (IT) – B.Tech / M.Tech / MCA
- Manager (Protocol & Security)
- Manager (Library) – Master's in Library Science
- Manager (Rajbhasha) – Master's in Hindi with English as a subject at degree level

### Eligibility
- **Age**: 21–35 years (relaxation as per GOI for SC/ST/OBC/PwBD)
- **Nationality**: Indian Citizen
- **Qualification**: Bachelor's degree / Post-Graduation / Professional qualification (varies by stream). For Manager-IT: First Class B.E./B.Tech/MCA.
- **Experience**: 3+ years' experience in the relevant field (preferable; some streams require it).

### Exam Pattern (Online Test + Interview)
| Stage | Type | Marks | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paper I – English | Descriptive (Precis, RC, Essay) | 100 | 90 min |
| Paper II – Stream-Specific (Professional Knowledge) | Objective + Descriptive | 100 | 120 min |
| Interview | Personality Test | 50 | — |

Final Merit = Paper II (50%) + Interview (50%). English Paper is qualifying (needs 25%).

### Detailed Syllabus

#### Paper I – English (Common to all streams)
- Essay Writing (250–500 words)
- Precis Writing
- Comprehension (Passage + Questions)
- Business / Official Correspondence

#### Paper II – Professional Knowledge (Stream-Specific)

**Manager (Accounts/Finance)**
- Accounting Standards (Ind AS & IFRS)
- Auditing (Internal, Statutory, Concurrent, Revenue)
- Taxation (Direct & Indirect)
- Balance Sheet Analysis, Ratio Analysis
- Treasury, Risk Management (Credit, Market, Operational)
- RBI Guidelines on Banks, Basel Norms
- Treasury / Investment Policy of RBI
- Budget & Fiscal Policy

**Manager (Legal)**
- Banking Regulation Act, 1949
- RBI Act, 1934
- Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881
- SARFAESI Act, 2002
- Consumer Protection Act, 2019
- Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA)
- Insolvency & Bankruptcy Code (IBC), 2016
- Contract Law, Company Law, Arbitration Law
- Constitutional Law – Fundamental Rights, Writs
- Cyber Law & IT Act, 2000

**Manager (IT)**
- Software Engineering (SDLC, Agile, DevOps)
- DBMS (Normalization, SQL, NoSQL)
- Networking (OSI/TCP-IP, Routing, Firewalls, VPN)
- Cyber Security (Cryptography, OWASP, NIST)
- Data Warehousing, ETL, OLAP
- Cloud Computing (AWS/Azure/GCP basics)
- AI/ML & Big Data (RDBMS, Hadoop, Spark)
- Programming: Java, Python, C++
- Operating Systems (Linux/Windows)
- Information Security & Cyber Forensics

### Salary
- **Basic Pay**: Rs 55,200/- (Pay Level 10 of 7th CPC, revised as per RBI 2024 Bipartite Settlement)
- **Gross Initial Monthly Emoluments**: ~Rs 1,00,000–1,10,000/-
- Perks: HRA (or leased accommodation), DA, Medical, LTC, PF, Gratuity, Newspaper allowance, Bank accommodation.

## 2. RBI Security Guard

### Posts
- Security Guard (Class IV/Group 'C') across RBI offices/zonal centres.

### Eligibility
- **Age**: 18–28 years (relaxation: SC/ST +5, OBC +3, Ex-Servicemen +3)
- **Education**: Class 10th Pass (50% for Ex-Servicemen)
- **Physical Standards**:
  - **Male**: 165 cm height, 50 kg weight (relaxable for hill tribes)
  - **Female**: 153 cm height, 45 kg weight

### Exam Pattern
| Stage | Type | Marks | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Online Test | Objective (Reasoning, GA, English) | 100 | 90 min |
| Physical Test | Running, Long Jump, High Jump | Qualifying | — |
| Biometric & Document Verification | — | — | — |

### Detailed Syllabus (Online Test)

#### Reasoning – 40 Qs
- Number Series, Analogy, Coding-Decoding
- Blood Relations, Direction Sense
- Syllogism, Statement & Conclusion
- Puzzles (basic), Seating Arrangement
- Order & Ranking

#### General Awareness – 40 Qs
- Current Affairs (national)
- Banking & RBI basic awareness
- Static GK (History, Polity, Geography)
- Awards, Sports, Schemes

#### English – 20 Qs
- Reading Comprehension
- Grammar (Tenses, Articles, Prepositions)
- Synonyms/Antonyms, Idioms
- Fill in the Blanks, Spotting Errors

#### Physical Test
| Test | Male | Female |
|---|---|---|
| Running (800 m) | Under 3 minutes | Under 4 minutes |
| Long Jump | 1.5 m | 1.0 m |
| High Jump | 1.2 m | 0.9 m |
| Push-ups / Sit-ups | 20 | 15 |

### Salary
- **Basic Pay**: Rs 19,000/- (Pay Level 1 of 7th CPC)
- **Gross Initial Monthly Emoluments**: ~Rs 33,000–36,000/-
- DA, HRA, Transport Allowance, Medical benefits, PF, Gratuity.

## 3. RBI Office Attendant (Class IV/Group 'C')

### Posts
- Office Attendant (Peon-cum-Housekeeping) — RBI offices nationwide.

### Eligibility
- **Age**: 18–25 years (relaxation as per GOI norms)
- **Education**: Class 10th Pass (any board recognised by GOI)
- **Domicile**: Must be a domicile of the state applied for
- **Skills**: Basic knowledge of the local language of the state

### Exam Pattern
| Stage | Type | Marks | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Online Test (CBT) | Objective | 120 | 90 min |
| Language Proficiency Test (LPT) | Read/Write/Speak | Qualifying | — |

### Detailed Syllabus (Online Test)

#### Reasoning – 30 Qs
- Puzzles & Seating Arrangement
- Syllogism, Coding-Decoding
- Blood Relations, Directions
- Number Series, Inequalities
- Input-Output, Data Sufficiency

#### General English – 30 Qs
- Reading Comprehension
- Cloze Test, Fill in the Blanks
- Error Detection, Sentence Improvement
- Para Jumbles, Vocabulary, Synonyms/Antonyms
- Idioms & Phrases

#### General Awareness – 30 Qs
- Current Affairs (National & International)
- Banking & Financial Awareness
- RBI Functions & Role
- Static GK (Indian Polity, History, Geography, Economy)

#### Numerical Ability – 30 Qs
- Simplification, Approximation
- Number Series, Quadratic Equations
- Data Interpretation (basic)
- Percentage, Ratio & Proportion, Average
- Profit & Loss, SI & CI
- Time & Work, Time-Speed-Distance

Candidates must demonstrate ability to **read, write, and speak** the local language of the applied state (e.g., Gujarati for Ahmedabad, Marathi/Konkani for Mumbai, Telugu for Hyderabad, Tamil for Chennai, Hindi for North-Indian offices). LPT is qualifying.

### Salary

- **Gross Monthly Emoluments**: ~Rs 36,000–40,000/-
- Perks: DA, HRA (or accommodation), Medical, LTC, PF, Gratuity, Festival advance.

## 4. NABARD Assistant (Grade A – Information Technology)

NABARD recruits Grade A Assistant Managers across multiple streams — General, Agriculture, Animal Husbandry, Fisheries, Forestry, IT, Legal, Finance, etc. The IT stream is highly sought-after.

### Posts
- **Assistant Manager (Grade A) – IT**: Network/System Administrator, Software Developer, Database Admin, IT Security roles at NABARD offices.

### Eligibility
- **Age**: 21–30 years (relaxation: SC/ST +5, OBC +3, PwBD +10)
- **Education**:
  - **General/OBC**: B.Tech / B.E. / MCA (minimum **60%**) OR Post-Graduation in CS/IT/Electronics with **55%**
  - **SC/ST/PwBD**: B.Tech / B.E. / MCA (minimum **55%**)
- **Experience**: Not mandatory (preferred: 2 years for some streams)

### Exam Pattern (3 Phases)

| Phase | Component | Type | Marks | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 | Reasoning, English, Quant, Computer Knowledge, Decision Making, GA, ESI, ARD | Objective (MCQ) | 200 | 120 min |
| Phase 2 (Mains) | Paper I – General English (Descriptive) | Online Descriptive | 100 | 90 min |
| Phase 2 (Mains) | Paper II – IT Stream-Specific (Objective + Descriptive) | Mixed | 100 | 120 min |
| Phase 3 | Interview | Personality Test | 50 | — |

Final Merit = Phase 2 (Paper I + Paper II) + Interview. Phase 1 is qualifying.

### Detailed Syllabus

#### Phase 1 (Prelims) – Common Subjects

| Subject | Qs | Marks | Key Topics |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reasoning Ability | 20 | 20 | Puzzles, Seating Arrangement, Syllogism, Coding-Decoding, Inequalities, Input-Output, Data Sufficiency |
| English Language | 30 | 30 | Reading Comprehension, Cloze Test, Error Spotting, Sentence Rearrangement, Para Jumbles, Vocabulary, Fillers |
| Computer Knowledge | 20 | 20 | Generations, DBMS, MS Office, Networking, Input-Output Devices, Internet, Shortcuts |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 20 | 20 | DI, Number Series, Quadratic Equations, Simplification, Arithmetic, Probability, P&C |
| Decision Making | 10 | 10 | Ethical dilemma, situational judgment |
| General Awareness | 20 | 20 | Current Affairs, Banking & Economy, Insurance |
| Economic & Social Issues (ESI) – Rural focus | 40 | 40 | Indian Economy, Poverty, Agriculture, Social Justice, Population |
| Agriculture & Rural Development (ARD) – Rural focus | 40 | 40 | Agronomy, Soil, Water Resources, Plantation, Animal Husbandry, Fisheries, Forestry, Climate Change |

#### Phase 2 (Mains) Paper I – General English (Descriptive)
- Essay Writing (250–500 words)
- Precis Writing
- Comprehension (with word limit)
- Letter/Report Writing

#### Phase 2 (Mains) Paper II – IT Stream Specific

**Objective Section (50 marks – 30 Qs)**
- Object-Oriented Programming (C++, Java, Python)
- Data Structures & Algorithms (Arrays, Linked List, Trees, Graphs, Sorting/Searching)
- DBMS & RDBMS (Normalization, SQL, ER Diagrams, Transactions, ACID)
- Operating Systems (Process Scheduling, Memory Management, Deadlocks)
- Computer Networks (OSI/TCP-IP, Routing, TCP/UDP, HTTP, DNS)
- Software Engineering (SDLC, Agile, Waterfall, Testing)

**Descriptive Section (50 marks – 4 of 6 Qs)**
- Software Architecture & Design Patterns
- Web Technologies (HTML, CSS, JS, REST APIs, MVC)
- Database Management – Advanced (NoSQL, MongoDB, Cassandra)
- Data Warehousing & Data Mining (OLAP, ETL, BI)
- Cyber Security (Cryptography, Network Security, OWASP, Ethical Hacking)
- Cloud Computing (AWS, Azure, GCP, Virtualization, Containers)
- Big Data & Analytics (Hadoop, Spark, HDFS, MapReduce)
- AI / Machine Learning (Neural Networks, NLP, Deep Learning basics)
- Mobile Application Development (Android/iOS, Flutter, React Native)
- IT Governance & Information Security (ISO 27001, NIST, COBIT)

### Salary
- **Basic Pay**: Rs 44,500/- (Pay Level 10 of 7th CPC)
- **Gross Monthly Emoluments**: ~Rs 80,000–90,000/-
- Perks: HRA / Bank accommodation, DA, Medical, LTC, PF, Gratuity, Vehicle allowance (Senior), Furniture allowance.

## 6. Bank of India (BoI) Probationary Officer (JMGS-I)

BoI conducts standalone PO recruitment through IBPS-like patterns (separate from IBPS PO).

### Posts
- Probationary Officer (Junior Management Grade Scale – I / JMGS-I)

### Eligibility
- **Age**: 21–30 years (relaxation: SC/ST +5, OBC +3, PwBD +10)
- **Education**:
  - **General/EWS**: Graduation with minimum **60%** marks
  - **SC/ST/PwBD**: Graduation with **55%** marks
- Professional degrees (CA, ICWA, MBA) may have relaxed percentage criteria.

### Exam Pattern
| Stage | Type | Marks | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Online Test (Objective) | MCQ | 200 | 180 min |
| English Descriptive | Letter Writing + Essay | 25 | 30 min |
| Group Discussion (GD) | Qualifying | 25 | — |
| Personal Interview | Final | 60 | — |

Final Merit = Online Test (200) + GD (25) + Interview (60) = 285 marks. English Descriptive is qualifying (must score 15+ for Gen, 12.5+ for SC/ST/OBC).

### Detailed Syllabus (Online Test)

| Section | Qs | Marks | Time | Topics |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| English Language | 35 | 40 | 40 min | RC, Error Detection, Para Jumbles, Cloze Test, Fillers, Vocabulary, Word Rearrangement, Idioms |
| Reasoning & Computer Aptitude | 45 | 60 | 60 min | Puzzles, Seating Arrangement, Syllogism, Coding-Decoding, Machine Input-Output, Inequalities, **Computer Aptitude**: Hardware, Networking, MS Office, Internet, DBMS, Shortcuts |
| General/Economy/Banking Awareness | 40 | 40 | 35 min | Current Affairs, RBI Circulars, Banking Reforms, PSL, SEBI, NABARD, Basel Norms, SARFAESI, Banking Committees |
| Data Analysis & Interpretation | 35 | 60 | 45 min | Tabular Graph, Line Graph, Pie Chart, Bar Graph, Caselet DI, Radar Graph, Probability, P&C, Data Sufficiency |

#### English Descriptive (Qualifying – 25 marks)
- Letter Writing (formal/informal)
- Essay (banking/economy/current topics) – 150–250 words

### Salary
- **Basic Pay**: Rs 41,960/- (Pay Level 8 of 7th CPC, revised as per 2020 IBA Bipartite Settlement)
- **Gross Monthly Emoluments**: ~Rs 70,000–80,000/- (including DA, HRA, CCA)
- Perks: Medical, LTC, Housing loan at concessional rate, PF, Gratuity, Newspaper, Briefcase allowance.

## 7. Currency Note Press (CNP) Nashik / Bank Note Press (BNP) Dewas / SPMCIL Units

**SPMCIL** (Security Printing & Minting Corporation of India Ltd) is a Miniratna CPSE under the Ministry of Finance. It operates 9 units — 4 Mints (Mumbai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Noida), 2 Currency Note Press (Nashik + BNP Dewas), 2 Security Presses (Nashik + Hyderabad), and 1 Security Paper Mill (Narmadapuram). Each unit recruits separately.

### Posts in SPMCIL Units (CNP Nashik / BNP Dewas / IGMs / SPs)

| Post | Pay Level | Qualification |
|---|---|---|
| Junior Office Assistant (JOA) | Level 4 | Graduate + Typing (Hindi/English) |
| Office Attendant | Level 1 | Class 10th Pass |
| Supervisor (Technical) | Level 6 | Diploma / B.Tech in relevant field |
| Assistant Manager (Technical) | Level 8 | B.Tech / M.Tech |
| Junior Technician | Level 2-3 | ITI / Diploma |

### Eligibility (Typical)
- **Junior Office Assistant**: Graduate with typing speed (30 wpm English / 25 wpm Hindi)
- **Office Attendant**: Class 10th Pass (50% minimum)
- **Supervisor (Printing/Mechanical/Electrical)**: Diploma/B.Tech in respective branch
- **Age**: 18–28 years (relaxation as per norms)

### Exam Pattern

#### For Junior Office Assistant
| Stage | Type | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| Online Test (CBT) | MCQ | 200 |
| Skill Test (Typing) | Qualifying | — |

| Subject | Qs | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| Reasoning | 40 | 40 |
| English Language | 40 | 40 |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 40 | 40 |
| General Awareness | 40 | 40 |
| Technical Knowledge (Computer / Printing) | 40 | 40 |
| **Total** | **200** | **200** |

Negative marking: 0.25 marks per wrong answer.

#### For Office Attendant
- Online Test: 100 Qs / 100 Marks / 90 min
- Subjects: Reasoning (30), English (20), GA (30), Quant (20)
- NO negative marking in some notifications
- **LPT (Local Language)** may be required

#### For Supervisor (Technical)
- Online Test: Objective (200 marks) + Descriptive / Technical Section
- Ratio for Technical : Non-Technical is 70:30

### Detailed Syllabus

#### Reasoning
- Coding-Decoding, Puzzles, Seating Arrangement
- Blood Relations, Direction & Distance
- Syllogism, Inequalities, Input-Output
- Order & Ranking, Alphanumeric Series

#### English Language
- Reading Comprehension
- Error Detection, Sentence Correction
- Fill in the Blanks, Cloze Test
- Vocabulary, Synonyms/Antonyms
- Letter Writing (for JOA)

#### Quantitative Aptitude
- Number System, Simplification
- Percentage, Ratio & Proportion, Average
- Profit & Loss, SI & CI
- Time & Work, Speed-Time-Distance
- DI (Tables, Bar Graphs, Pie Charts)

#### General Awareness
- Current Affairs
- Indian Banking, RBI, NBFC basics
- Static GK – History, Polity, Geography
- General Science
- SPMCIL / Printing / Currency Notes / Security features

#### Technical Knowledge (post-specific)
- **Printing Technology**: Offset, Intaglio, Letterpress
- **Mechanical**: Thermodynamics, Hydraulics, Workshop tech
- **Electrical**: Circuit theory, Machines, Power systems
- **Computer (for JOA)**: MS Office, Networking, Internet

### Salary
| Post | Basic Pay | Gross Monthly (approx.) |
|---|---|---|
| Office Attendant | Rs 19,900 | Rs 35,000–38,000 |
| Junior Office Assistant | Rs 25,500 | Rs 45,000–50,000 |
| Supervisor (Tech) | Rs 35,400 | Rs 60,000–65,000 |
| Assistant Manager | Rs 47,600 | Rs 80,000+ |

Plus DA, HRA, Perks, Medical, PF, Gratuity, Performance Related Pay (PRP).

### Preparation Tips
1. **Tier-1 / Online Test**: Focus on speed and accuracy. Attempt 80+ mocks before exam.
2. **Descriptive**: Practice 1 essay + 1 letter daily (10–15 min). Read editorial of The Hindu / Indian Express.
3. **Banking/Economy**: Read RBI Annual Report, Economic Survey, Union Budget highlights, monthly RBI Bulletins.
4. **IT-Specific (NABARD/SBI)**: Cover DBMS + Networking + OS + Software Engineering thoroughly – these fetch maximum marks.
5. **Current Affairs**: Last 6 months focus – appointments, schemes, banking mergers, monetary policy.
6. **Physical Tests (Security Guard)**: Practice 800m running and long jump daily for 60 days.

Each section includes detailed exam pattern, syllabus, and salary structure. Best preparation books include R.S. Aggarwal, Wren & Martin, Standard General Knowledge Compendium, and stream-specific technical books. Official notifications appear on rbi.org.in, nabard.org, bankofindia.co.in, and spmcil.com respectively.
