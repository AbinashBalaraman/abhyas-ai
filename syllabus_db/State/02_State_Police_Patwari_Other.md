## 1. UTTAR PRADESH POLICE (UPPRPB) — uppbpb.gov.in

**Conducting Body:** Uttar Pradesh Police Recruitment & Promotion Board (UPPRPB)
**Posts:** Constable (Civil Police), Armed Police, PAC, Jail Warder, Fireman, Driver, SI (Civil Police), Platoon Commander (PAC), SI (Confidential)

### UP Police Constable 2025
- **Eligibility:** 12th Pass (Intermediate) from recognized board; Age 18-22 (relaxation for reserved categories as per UP Govt)
- **Selection Process:** Written Exam → Document Verification & PST → PMT
- **Exam Pattern (Constable 2024-25):**
  | Subject | Questions | Marks |
  |---|---|---|
  | General Knowledge | 38 | 76 |
  | Numerical Ability & Quantitative Aptitude | 38 | 76 |
  | Reasoning & General Intelligence | 37 | 74 |
  | General Hindi | 37 | 74 |
  | **Total** | **150** | **300** |
  - Duration: 2 Hours | Mode: Offline OMR | Negative Marking: 0.5 per wrong answer
- **Syllabus (Detailed):**
  - **GK:** Gandhi Era, Modern History, Buddhism, Nobel Prizes, Boundary Lines, Revolutions, Indian Constitution (Amendments, Parts, Schedule, Articles, Parliament, President Powers), UP Culture/Social Customs, Revenue & Police Administration of UP, Women/Child Protection, ICT Basics, Environmental Studies.
  - **Hindi:** Hindi & Indian Languages, Alphabet, Tatsam-Tadbhava, Paryayvachi, Vilom, Anekarthak, Sentence correction, Ling-Vachan-Karak, Sarvanam, Visheshan, Kriya, Kaal, Vachya, Avyay, Upsarg, Pratyay, Sandhi, Samas, Viraam-Chinh, Muhavre-Lokokti, Ras-Chhand-Alankar, Apathit Bodh, Famous authors and works, Hindi language awards.
  - **Math:** Simplification, Percentage, Average, Profit/Loss, Number System, Discount, SI/CI, Time-Distance, Time-Work, Mensuration, Ratio-Proportion.
  - **Reasoning:** Analysis & Judgment, Arithmetic Reasoning, Syllogism, Similarities-Differences, Analogies, Relationship, Verbal/Figure classification, Decision-making, Arithmetical Number Series, Observation.
- **Physical Standards (PST/PET):**
  - **Male (General/OBC):** Height 168 cm, Chest 79-84 cm; Running 4.8 km in 25 min
  - **Male (SC/ST):** Height 160 cm
  - **Female:** Height 152 cm, Weight 40 kg (min); Running 2.4 km in 14 min
- **Salary:** Level-3 (₹21,700 - ₹69,100) + DA + allowances ≈ ₹30,000-₹35,000 in-hand initially
- **Best Books:** Standard General Knowledge Compendium, Standard Exam GuideUP Police Constable, Standard Previous Years Solved PapersUP Police, Standard Objective English ReferenceHindi, RS Aggarwal Math & Reasoning
- **Official Link:** https://uppbpb.gov.in

### UP Police SI (Civil/Platoon Commander) 2025
- **Eligibility:** Bachelor's Degree; Age 21-28
- **Exam Pattern:** 2 Papers — Paper I (General Hindi, GK, Quantitative Aptitude — 400 marks) + Paper II (Law/Constitution/General Knowledge — 200 marks) + Physical + Interview
- **Syllabus:** Hindi essay/grammar, Indian Polity, History, Geography, Economics, General Science, Indian Penal Code, CrPC, Evidence Act, Constitution
- **PST/PET:** Same as Constable; Long-distance running + hurdle race
- **Salary:** Level-7 (₹44,900 - ₹1,42,400) ≈ ₹50,000-₹60,000 in-hand

## 2. BIHAR POLICE (CSBC & BPSSC)

**Conducting Body:** Central Selection Board of Constable (CSBC) for Constables; Bihar Police Subordinate Services Commission (BPSSC) for SI
**Posts:** Constable (Armed/Unarmed), Driver, SI (Civil Police/Sergeant/Platoon Commander)

### Bihar Police Constable 2025 (CSBC)
- **Eligibility:** 12th Pass (Intermediate); Age 18-25
- **Selection:** Offline OMR Written (Qualifying) → PET/PST (100 marks, only basis for merit) → Document Verification
- **Exam Pattern:**
  - 100 MCQ | 100 marks | 2 hours | No negative marking (qualifying — 30 marks min)
  - **Subjects:** Hindi (50 Q) — literature & grammar; English/GA/Current Affairs/Social Science (25 Q); Science — Phy/Chem/Zoology/Botany (25 Q)
- **Syllabus:**
  - **Hindi:** Prose & verse, works of Premchand, Satyajit Ray, Kabir, Mirabai (lists available in official PDF)
  - **English:** Comprehension, passage-based, letter/notice writing, grammar, précis, reports
  - **Arithmetic:** Sets, relations, functions, quadratic equations, trigonometry, geometry, 3-D, statistics
  - **History:** Pre-history, ancient civilizations, nationalist movements (1918-47), 1857 revolt, Indian awakening
  - **Geography:** Physical, human, economic, resources, environment, Bihar/India geography
  - **Polity:** State theories, sovereignty, law/liberty/equality, DPSP, Parliament, state legislature, judiciary
  - **Physics:** Measurement, kinematics, laws of motion, work/energy/power, gravitation, thermodynamics, optics, atomic physics
  - **Chemistry:** Atomic structure, periodic table, chemical bonds, organic chemistry, environmental chemistry
  - **Biology:** Biodiversity, morphology, cell biology, plant physiology, zoology, genetics, biotechnology
  - **Economics:** Scope, data collection/interpretation, Bihar/India economic development, micro/macro
- **PST/PET:**
  - Male (Gen/BC): Height 165 cm, Chest 81-86 cm, Running 1.6 km in 6 min, Shot Put 16 ft (16 lb ball), High Jump 4 ft
  - Female: Height 155 cm, Weight 48 kg, Running 1 km in 5 min, Shot Put 12 ft (12 lb ball), High Jump 3 ft
  - PET Scoring: Running 50 + Shot Put 25 + High Jump 25 = 100 marks
- **Salary:** Level-3 (₹21,700 - ₹69,100) ≈ ₹26,000-₹30,000 in-hand
- **Best Books:** Bihar Police Constable by Standard Exam Guide, Standard General Knowledge Compendium, Standard Previous Years Solved PapersHindi, Standard Previous Years Solved Papers
- **Official:** https://csbc.bihar.gov.in, https://bpssc.bih.nic.in

### Bihar Police SI (BPSSC)
- **Eligibility:** Bachelor's Degree; Age 18-37 (M) / 18-40 (F)
- **Exam Pattern:** Prelims (150 Q, 2 hrs) + Mains (Descriptive) + PET + Interview
- **Syllabus:** GK, Math, Reasoning, Hindi/English, Bihar Special (History, Culture, Geography, Polity, Economy), Law (IPC, CrPC, Evidence), Current Affairs
- **Salary:** Level-7 (₹44,900 - ₹1,42,400)

## Common Study Strategy

1. **Daily Time Table:** 6-8 hours for competitive exam prep
2. **Section-wise Preparation:** Divide 60% time to weak areas, 40% to strong
3. **Mock Tests:** Minimum 30 full-length mocks per subject
4. **Current Affairs:** Daily 30 minutes (The Hindu, Indian Express, AIR, PIB)
5. **Previous Year Papers:** Last 5-10 years essential
6. **State GK:** Compulsory — refer to state board textbooks (Class 6-12)
7. **Physical Fitness:** For Police/Forest — daily 1 hour (running, exercises)
8. **Negative marking awareness:** Calculate risk before guessing

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Ignoring state-specific GK
- Skipping physical preparation
- Not reading official notification carefully (eligibility, age, fees)
- Relying only on one source (book/coaching)
- Ignoring current affairs
- Late start of preparation
- Skipping revision
