# Judicial Services & Law Exams – India (State & Central)

> **Scope:** Civil Judge / JMFC (Lower Judiciary) for all major State High Courts, plus Central legal examinations (AIBE, CLAT, AILET, LSAT, MAH-LLB CET, PSU/Banking Law Officer posts, and High Court non-judicial posts). Compiled from official High Court notifications, the Department of Justice (DoJ), Supreme Court of India, Bar Council of India, CLAT Consortium, and recognised coaching sources.

---

## Quick Reference – Judicial Service Hierarchy in India

| Tier | Court | Posts | Recruitment |
|------|-------|-------|-------------|
| Apex | Supreme Court of India | Judges (AOR) | Collegium system |
| Highest State | High Courts (25) | District Judge, Additional DJ | HJS / Promotion |
| Subordinate | District & Sessions Court | Civil Judge (Sr/Jr Div), JMFC, Metropolitan Magistrate | State Judicial Service Exam (PCS-J) |
| Tribunals / Central | NGT, ITAT, CAT, etc. | Presiding Officer | UPSC / DoJ |

Common **3-stage** selection process: **Preliminary (Objective, Qualifying) → Mains (Descriptive) → Viva-Voce**.

# CATEGORY A – STATE JUDICIAL SERVICES EXAMINATIONS (CIVIL JUDGE / JMFC)

## A.1 Uttar Pradesh – Civil Judge (Junior Division) – Allahabad High Court

- **Conducting Body:** High Court of Judicature at Allahabad
- **Post:** Civil Judge (Junior Division) — `https://www.allahabadhighcourt.in/`
- **Eligibility:**
  - LLB (3-year / 5-year) from a BCI-recognised university
  - Age: **22 – 35 years** (relaxation for SC/ST/OBC as per UP rules)
  - Must be enrolled with a State Bar Council
- **Pay:** Pay Level 10 (₹ 47,600 – 1,51,100) + DA
- **Pattern:**

| Stage | Type | Marks | Duration | Negative Marking |
|-------|------|-------|----------|-----------------|
| Preliminary | MCQ | 150 | 2 hrs | 1/3 per wrong |
| Mains (5 papers) | Descriptive | 800 (150 + 150 + 200 + 200 + 100) | 3 hrs each | No |
| Interview | Viva | 100 | – | – |

- **Mains Papers & Detailed Syllabus:**
  1. **Paper I – English (150 marks):** Essay, Precis, Comprehension, Translation (English↔Hindi), Idioms & Phrases, Grammar
  2. **Paper II – General Knowledge (150 marks):** Indian Polity, History (ancient/medieval/modern), Geography, Economics, Current Affairs, Science, UP Special GK
  3. **Paper III – Law Paper I (200 marks):** Constitutional Law, Jurisprudence, Administrative Law, Law of Contract, Specific Relief, Indian Easements Act
  4. **Paper IV – Law Paper II (200 marks):** Law of Evidence (IEA), Criminal Procedure Code, Indian Penal Code (BNS), Code of Civil Procedure, Transfer of Property, Limitation
  5. **Paper V – Law Paper III (100 marks):** Hindu Law, Muslim Law, Personal Law, Revenue Law (UP Zamindari Abolition), Local Laws
- **Pre Syllabus (MCQ):** Same five subjects, but the focus is the Code of Civil Procedure, CPC order-wise, Code of Criminal Procedure, Indian Evidence Act, Indian Penal Code, Family Law, Constitutional Law, plus general legal awareness and GK.
- **Official:** `https://www.allahabadhighcourt.in/` → Recruitment Section. Latest Direct Recruitment (HJS) Advt 14-03-2024 (`advt_event_18029_14-03-2024.pdf`); HJS-2023 portal at `https://apps.allahabadhighcourt.in/hjs/`.

# CATEGORY B – CENTRAL JUDICIAL / LEGAL SERVICES

## B.1 AIBE – All India Bar Examination

- **Purpose:** Certificate of Practice (CoP) to advocate in any court in India.
- **Conducting Body:** **Bar Council of India (BCI)** — `https://allindiabarexamination.com/`
- **Eligibility:**
  - LLB (3-yr/5-yr) from BCI-recognised university
  - Enrolled with a State Bar Council
  - No upper age limit
  - 2024 amendment: final-year LLB students also eligible (subject to enrolment on passing)
- **Fee:** ₹ 3,500 (General) / ₹ 2,500 (SC/ST)
- **Exam Pattern:**
  - Mode: **Open-book** Offline (pen & paper) – CBT introduced gradually
  - Duration: **3 hours 30 min**
  - Questions: **100 MCQs** (all compulsory, no negative marking)
  - Total Marks: 100 (passing: 45% General, 40% OBC, 35% SC/ST)
  - Centres: 53 cities across India
  - Languages: English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, Odia, Assamese, Punjabi
- **Detailed Syllabus (19 Topics):**
  1. **Constitutional Law** – Fundamental Rights, DPSP, Federal structure, Judiciary
  2. **Family Law I (Hindu Law)** – Marriage, Divorce, Maintenance, Succession, Adoption
  3. **Family Law II (Muslim Law)** – Nikah, Talaq, Mehr, Dower, Wills
  4. **Civil Procedure Code (CPC)**
  5. **Criminal Procedure Code (CrPC)**
  6. **Indian Penal Code (IPC) – now BNS (Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023)**
  7. **Indian Evidence Act – now BSA (Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023)**
  8. **Indian Contract Act & Specific Relief Act**
  9. **Transfer of Property Act & Indian Easements Act**
  10. **Law of Torts (incl. Motor Vehicles Act, Consumer Protection)**
  11. **Labour & Industrial Law (ID Act, Trade Unions, ESI, EPF, Gratuity)**
  12. **Company Law (Companies Act 2013)**
  13. **Administrative Law (RTI, Tribunals)**
  14. **Tax Law (Income Tax, GST)**
  15. **Environmental Law (EPA, Wildlife Protection, Forest Conservation)**
  16. **Cyber Law (IT Act 2000)**
  17. **Intellectual Property Law (Patents, Copyrights, Trademarks)**
  18. **Land & Property Law (Rent Control, Tenancy, Registration, Stamps)**
  19. **Public Interest Litigation (PIL) & Legal Ethics**

- **Conducting:** Consortium of NLUs (rotational) — `https://consortiumofnlus.ac.in/`

- **Eligibility (UG):** 10+2 (45% / 40% SC/ST); No age limit
- **Pattern (UG, since 2020):** Online MCQ – 150 Qs – 150 marks – 2 hrs
  - English (incl. Comprehension) – 22–26 Qs
  - Current Affairs + GK – 28–32 Qs
  - Legal Reasoning – 42–48 Qs
  - Logical Reasoning – 42–48 Qs

- **Pattern (PG):** Descriptive (Essay, Legal Reasoning, Current Affairs, Law questions) – 120 marks

- **Purpose:** Admission to various private law schools (Jindal Global Law School, Amity, UPES, etc.)
- **Pattern:** Online remote-proctored – ~92 Qs – ~138 marks
  - Logical Reasoning (≈24 Qs), Analytical Reasoning (≈24 Qs), Reading Comprehension (≈24 Qs), Games/Logic Games
  - **No negative marking**
- **Eligibility:** 10+2 (50%) for UG; LLB for PG

## E.4 MAH-LLB CET (Maharashtra 3-year LLB)

- **Conducting:** State CET Cell, Maharashtra — `https://cetcell.mahacet.org/`
- **Eligibility:** Graduation (45% / 40% reserved) or appearing
- **Pattern:** Online MCQ – 120 Qs – 120 marks – 90 min
  - Legal Aptitude (40), GK (30), Mental Ability (30), English (20)
  - No negative marking
