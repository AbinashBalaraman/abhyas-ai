# State Public Service Commission (PSC) Examinations - Comprehensive Guide

> **Overview**: State PSCs are constitutional bodies established under Articles 315-323 of the Indian Constitution. They recruit candidates for state civil services through competitive examinations. India has 28 State PSCs (Jammu & Kashmir PSC was merged in 2019).

---

## 1. Uttar Pradesh (UPPSC)

**Full Name**: Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission  
**Headquarters**: Prayagraj (Allahabad)  
**Established**: 1 April 1937  
**Website**: https://uppsc.up.nic.in

### Posts Offered
- **PCS (Combined State/Upper Subordinate Service)** - Deputy Collector, DSP, Tehsildar, Block Development Officer (BDO)
- **RO/ARO** - Review Officer / Assistant Review Officer
- **Assistant** (various departments)
- **Lower PCS**
- **ACF/FRO** (Assistant Conservator of Forest / Forest Range Officer)
- **UP Judicial Services (PCS-J)**
- **Assistant Prosecuting Officer (APO)**

### Eligibility
- **Age**: 21-40 years (relaxation for reserved categories)
- **Education**: Bachelor's degree from a recognized university

### Exam Pattern
- **Prelims**: General Studies - **150 marks**, 2 hours, objective type (MCQs)
  - Paper I: General Studies I (200 questions)
  - Paper II: General Studies II (CSAT - Qualifying, 33%)
- **Mains**: **8 papers - 1500 marks total** (Descriptive)
  - General Hindi (150 marks)
  - Essay (150 marks)
  - GS Paper I (200 marks) - History, Geography, Society
  - GS Paper II (200 marks) - Polity, Governance, IR
  - GS Paper III (200 marks) - Economy, Environment
  - GS Paper IV (200 marks) - Ethics, Integrity, Aptitude
  - GS Paper V (200 marks) - UP Special GK
  - GS Paper VI (200 marks) - UP Special Industry, Trade, Culture
  - Optional Subject (2 papers - 200 marks each)
- **Interview**: **100 marks**

### Key Syllabus Topics
- **History**: Ancient, Medieval, Modern Indian History; Freedom Struggle; UP History
- **Geography**: Indian & World Geography; UP Geography; Climate, Resources
- **Polity**: Indian Constitution, Governance, Panchayati Raj
- **Economy**: Indian Economy, Planning, UP Economy
- **Environment & Ecology**
- **Current Affairs**: National & UP-specific
- **Ethics, Integrity, Aptitude**
- **UP Special**: History, Culture, Economy, Geography, Polity, Literature, Arts

### State-specific GK Weightage
**~25-30%** of GS papers dedicated to UP-specific topics

### Salary
- **Deputy Collector/SDM**: ₹56,100 - ₹1,77,500 (Level 10)
- **DSP/Tehsildar**: ₹44,900 - ₹1,42,400 (Level 7-8)
- **RO/ARO**: ₹44,900 - ₹1,42,400 (Level 7)

## 2. Bihar (BPSC)

**Full Name**: Bihar Public Service Commission  
**Headquarters**: Patna  
**Established**: 1 April 1948  
**Website**: https://www.bpsc.bih.nic.in

- **PCS (Bihar State Service / Bihar Administrative Service - BAS)**
- **BPSC 67th, 68th, 70th CCE** (Combined Competitive Examination)
- **Deputy Collector, DSP, Block Development Officer, Sub-Divisional Officer**
- **Range Officer, Jail Inspector, Supply Inspector**

### Eligibility
- **Age**: 20-37 years (General), 22-40 (Female), relaxation for reserved
- **Education**: Bachelor's degree

### Exam Pattern
- **Prelims**: **150 marks** (General Studies - objective)
- **Mains**: **4 papers - 900 marks** (Descriptive)
  - General Hindi (100 marks)
  - General Studies Paper 1 (300 marks) - History, Polity, Geography
  - General Studies Paper 2 (300 marks) - Economy, Science, Environment
  - Optional/Optional Subject (300 marks)
- **Interview**: **120 marks**

### Key Syllabus Topics
- **History**: Indian National Movement, Bihar's role in Freedom Struggle
- **Geography**: Bihar Geography - rivers, plains, climate
- **Polity**: Indian Constitution, Bihar Governance
- **Economy**: Bihar's agriculture, industry, migrant labor issues
- **General Science**, **Environment**
- **Current Affairs**: National + Bihar-specific
- **Mental Ability, Comprehension**

### State-specific GK Weightage
**~20-25%** of Mains dedicated to Bihar-specific topics

### Salary
- **Deputy Collector/SDM**: ₹56,100 - ₹1,77,500
- **DSP**: ₹53,100 - ₹1,67,800

**Full Name**: Uttarakhand Public Service Commission  
**Headquarters**: Haridwar  
**Established**: 2000  
**Website**: https://ukpsc.gov.in

### Posts Offered
- **UKPCS (Uttarakhand Provincial Civil Service)**
- **Deputy Collector, DSP, Tehsildar**
- **UKPCS Combined Civil Services**

### Eligibility
- **Age**: 21-42 years
- **Education**: Bachelor's degree

### Exam Pattern
- **Prelims**: **300 marks** (Objective - 2 papers)
- **Mains**: **7 papers - 1500 marks**
  - Hindi (Compulsory - 150 marks)
  - English (150 marks)
  - GS Paper I to V (200-300 marks each)
- **Interview**: **100 marks**

### Key Syllabus Topics
- **Uttarakhand History**: Garhwal, Kumaon dynasties
- **Uttarakhand Geography**: Himalayas, rivers, climate
- **Uttarakhand Culture**: Folk dances (Langvir Nritya)
- **State Movement**: Uttarakhand Andolan
- **Current Affairs**

### State-specific GK Weightage
**~35%** for Uttarakhand-specific topics

### Salary
- **UKPCS Officer (Dy. Collector)**: ₹56,100 - ₹1,77,500

## 20. Kerala (KPSC)

**Headquarters**: Thiruvananthapuram  
**Established**: 1956  
**Website**: https://keralapsc.gov.in

### Posts Offered
- **KAS (Kerala Administrative Service)**
- **Kerala Civil Service, Kerala Police Service**
- **Various Gazetted posts**
- **KPSC Civil Services Exam**

### Eligibility
- **Age**: 19-40 years
- **Education**: Bachelor's degree

### Exam Pattern
- **Prelims**: **200 marks** (Objective - 1 paper)
- **Mains**: **6 papers - 1200 marks**
  - Malayalam (Compulsory - 100 marks)
  - English (100 marks)
  - GS Paper I to IV (250-300 marks each)
- **Interview**: **100 marks**

### Key Syllabus Topics
- **Kerala History**: Chera dynasty, medieval, modern
- **Kerala Geography**: Western Ghats, backwaters, rivers
- **Malayalam Literature**: Kalidasa, classical literature
- **Kerala Renaissance**: Sree Narayana Guru, Chattambi Swami
- **Current Affairs**

### State-specific GK Weightage
**~30%** for Kerala-specific topics

### Salary
- **KAS Officer (Dy. Collector)**: ₹56,100 - ₹1,77,500

## 21. Assam (APSC)

**Full Name**: Assam Public Service Commission  
**Headquarters**: Guwahati  
**Established**: 1937  
**Website**: https://apsc.nic.in

### Posts Offered
- **Assam Civil Service (ACS)**
- **APS, AFS, AJS** (Police, Forest, Judicial Service)
- **APSC Combined Competitive Examination (CCE)**

### Eligibility
- **Age**: 21-38 years
- **Education**: Bachelor's degree

### Exam Pattern
- **Prelims**: **300 marks** (Objective - 2 papers)
  - GS Paper I (200 marks)
  - GS Paper II (200 marks - CSAT Qualifying)
- **Mains**: **6 papers - 1500 marks**
  - Assamese/Bengali/Hindi (Compulsory - 100 marks)
  - English (100 marks)
  - GS Paper I to IV (200-300 marks each)
- **Interview**: **180 marks**

### Key Syllabus Topics
- **Assam History**: Ahom dynasty, Koch, Koch-Hajo
- **Assam Geography**: Brahmaputra, Barak Valley, hills
- **Assam Polity**: Sixth Schedule, Autonomous Districts
- **Assam Culture**: Bihu, folk dances, music
- **Current Affairs**

### State-specific GK Weightage
**~30%** for Assam-specific topics

### Salary
- **ACS Officer (Dy. Commissioner)**: ₹56,100 - ₹1,77,500

## Key Common Syllabus Topics Across All PSCs

1. **History**: Ancient, Medieval, Modern India; State-specific History; Freedom Struggle
2. **Geography**: India and World Geography; State-specific; Physical, Economic, Social
3. **Polity & Constitution**: Indian Constitution; State Polity; Panchayati Raj
4. **Economy**: Indian Economy; State Economy; Agriculture, Industry, Trade
5. **General Science**: Physics, Chemistry, Biology; Environment
6. **Current Affairs**: National + International + State-specific
7. **Reasoning & Mental Ability**
8. **Ethics, Integrity, Aptitude**
9. **State Language Paper** (in many PSCs - compulsory)

## Selection Process (Common)

1. **Preliminary Examination** (Objective - Qualifying in most states)
2. **Mains Examination** (Descriptive - Counting for merit)
3. **Interview / Personality Test** (75-200 marks)
