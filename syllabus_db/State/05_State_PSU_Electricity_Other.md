## Quick Reference: State Power Sector Structure (India)

Following the Electricity Act 2003, all State Electricity Boards (SEBs) were unbundled into separate Generation, Transmission, and Distribution companies. Each state typically has 3-5 successor entities (Genco / Transco / 2-5 Discoms + Holding Company). Recruitments are conducted by the respective successor entities (often through the Holding Co.) via Computer Based Test (CBT) followed by Document Verification (DV).

**Common Exam Pattern (most State Power PSUs):**
- CBT: 200 Questions / 200 Marks (or 100 Q / 100 M), Objective MCQ
- Sections: Technical (Diploma/B.Tech syllabus - 75%), General Hindi (5-10%), General English (5-10%), Reasoning & General Awareness (10-15%)
- Duration: 2-3 hours; Negative marking: 0.25 per wrong answer
- DV: Certificate verification + Medical fitness

## PREPARATION STRATEGY (Universal for State PSU Exams)

1. **Technical Subject (75-80% weightage)** — Focus on core discipline syllabus. Solve last 10 years' papers. Refer to standard textbooks (mentioned above). For Electrical: priority to Power Systems, Machines, Basic Electrical.
2. **General Hindi** — Standard General Knowledge CompendiumHindi + daily newspaper reading. Focus on अपठित गद्यांश, व्याकरण, मुहावरे-लोकोक्ति, संधि-समास।
3. **General English** — Wren & Martin + 1 grammar book. Focus on Comprehension, Error Spotting, Tense, Voice.
4. **Reasoning** — R.S. Aggarwal (Verbal & Non-Verbal) — Practice daily 1 set of 25 Q.
5. **General Awareness** — Daily 30-min current affairs (AffairsCloud/Adda247). Monthly magazine. State GK specific (refer to Standard Exam GuideState GK or MP Specific GK for MP exams).
6. **Numerical Aptitude** — R.S. Aggarwal Quantitative Aptitude. Focus on Speed Maths.
7. **Mock Tests** — Take minimum 30 mocks before exam. Analyze each one. Time management crucial.
8. **State GK** — Each state has specific topics. Refer to state PSC GK material.
