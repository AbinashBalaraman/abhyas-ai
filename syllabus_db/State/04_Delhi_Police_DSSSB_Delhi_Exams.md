## 1. SSC CPO Delhi Police Sub-Inspector (SI)

**Conducted by:** Staff Selection Commission (SSC) — covered in the SSC master file in brief.
- **Posts:** Sub-Inspector (GD) in CAPF, Delhi Police, BSF, CISF, ITBP, SSB; Sub-Inspector (Executive) in Delhi Police.
- **Eligibility:** Bachelor's degree from a recognized university; 20–25 years (relaxation as per rules for reserved categories); Physical standards as prescribed.
- **Exam Pattern:** Paper-I (Objective MCQ — 200 Qs, 200 marks, 2 hrs, −0.50 negative), Paper-II (English Comprehension — 200 Qs, 200 marks, 2 hrs, −0.50); followed by Physical Endurance & Measurement Test (PE&MT), Medical.
- **Syllabus (Paper-I):** General Intelligence & Reasoning, GK & General Awareness, Quantitative Aptitude, English Comprehension (Class 10+2 level).
- **Syllabus (Paper-II):** Error recognition, Fill in the blanks, Vocabulary, Spellings, Grammar, Sentence Structure, Synonyms/Antonyms, Idioms & Phrases, Passage comprehension.
- **Physical (Male):** 100 m race in 16 sec, 1.6 km in 6.5 min, Long jump 3.65 m, High jump 1.2 m, Shot put 4.5 m.
- **Physical (Female):** 100 m in 18 sec, 800 m in 4 min, Long jump 2.7 m, High jump 0.9 m.
- **Salary:** Pay Level-6 (₹35,400–₹1,12,400) + allowances.
- **Official:** https://ssc.nic.in/

## 2. SSC GD Delhi Police Constable

**Conducted by:** SSC — covered in SSC master file in brief.
- **Post:** Constable (General Duty) in BSF, CISF, CRPF, SSB, ITBP, AR, SSF, NIA, Delhi Police (Executive) — male & female.
- **Eligibility:** 10th (Matriculation) pass; 18–23 years (relaxation applicable).
- **Exam Pattern:** CBT — 100 Qs, 100 marks, 90 min, 4 sections (GK/GS, Reasoning, Math, English/Hindi), −0.25 negative.
- **Syllabus:** GK (History, Polity, Geography, Economics, Science), Reasoning (Analogies, Coding, Series, Syllogism), Math (Number system, Percentages, Ratio, Profit-loss, Time-work, Distance), English/Hindi.
- **Physical:** Male — 5 km in 24 min; Female — 1.6 km in 8.5 min.
- **Salary:** Pay Level-3 (₹21,700–₹69,100).
- **Official:** https://ssc.nic.in/

## 3. SSC Head Constable AWO/TPO Delhi Police

**Conducted by:** SSC — covered in SSC master file in brief.
- **Posts:** Head Constable (Assistant Wireless Operator/AWO and Telecommunication Police Operator/TPO) in Delhi Police.
- **Eligibility:** 12th (+2) with Science/Physics/Math/Computer; 18–27 years; typing/operational skills may be required.
- **Exam Pattern:** CBT (100 Qs, 100 marks, 90 min, −0.25 negative) — covers GK, Reasoning, Quantitative, English/Hindi + technical/science questions (Physics, Electronics, Computers, Networking).
- **Syllabus:** GK (current affairs, history, geography, polity), Reasoning (verbal/non-verbal), Quantitative Aptitude, English/Hindi comprehension, Science/Computers (Electrostatics, Current Electricity, Magnetic Effects, EMI, Optics, Modern Physics; MS Office, Networking, Internet).
- **Physical:** Same standards as Delhi Police HC (PET + Measurement).
- **Salary:** Pay Level-4 (₹25,500–₹81,100).
- **Official:** https://ssc.nic.in/

## 4. Delhi Police — Direct Recruitment Posts

**Authority:** Delhi Police / GNCTD (operational posts); SSC for Constables & HC (AWO/TPO). Commissioner of Police: Satish Golcha (IPS, 2026). Sanctioned strength: ~83,762. Headquarters: Jai Singh Marg, New Delhi-110001.

### 4.1 Delhi Police Constable (Executive) — covered in SSC file
Recruited via SSC; CBT-based selection; posts ~7,565 in the 2026 cycle. Physical standards: Male height 170 cm (chest 81–85, 4 cm expansion), Female 157 cm. PE&MT: 1600 m race, Long Jump, High Jump. Pay Level-3 (₹21,700–₹69,100).

### 4.2 Delhi Police Head Constable (Ministerial)
- **Recruiter:** Delhi Police (own recruitment notice).
- **Eligibility:** 12th pass; 18–25 years; typing speed 30 wpm English / 25 wpm Hindi; basic computer knowledge.
- **Exam Pattern:** Tier-I (MCQ — GK, Reasoning, Quantitative, English, Computer) 100 Qs, 100 marks, 90 min, −0.25; Tier-II (Descriptive English/Hindi + Typing Test).
- **Syllabus:** GK & Current Affairs; Reasoning; Numerical Ability; English/Hindi grammar; Computer fundamentals (MS Office, Internet); Typing test on computer.
- **Salary:** Pay Level-4 (₹25,500–₹81,100).
- **Selection:** CBT → Typing Test → PE&MT → Medical.

### 4.3 Delhi Police Head Constable (AWO/TPO) — covered
Recruited through SSC; 12th Science; physical + technical exam; pay Level-4.

### 4.4 Delhi Police Sub-Inspector (GD) — covered
Recruited through SSC CPO; graduation mandatory; age 20–25; PE&MT, Medical; pay Level-6.

### 4.5 Delhi Police Sub-Inspector (Armed) — Rare
- **Recruiter:** Delhi Police limited departmental notice.
- **Eligibility:** Graduation; 20–27 years; physical standards as prescribed.
- **Exam:** One-tier written (GK, Reasoning, Quant, English) + PE&MT + Medical.
- **Syllabus:** Same as SI (GD) with emphasis on law, fire-arms, and tactical knowledge.
- **Salary:** Pay Level-6 (₹35,400–₹1,12,400) + special allowance.

### 4.6 Delhi Police Subedar (Inspector/ACP) — Promotional
- **Recruiter:** Promoted from Inspectors via Departmental Promotion Committee (DPC) + limited departmental exam; not direct recruitment.
- **Eligibility:** Existing Inspectors/Sub-Inspectors with qualifying service and ACRs.
- **Exam:** Limited Departmental Competitive Examination (LDCE) — GK, Indian Penal Code, CRPC, evidence, leadership.
- **Salary:** Pay Level-7 (ACP) to Pay Level-8 (Inspector rank with selection grade).

### 4.7 Delhi Police Photographer
- **Recruiter:** Delhi Police (own notice, infrequent).
- **Eligibility:** 12th with diploma/certificate in Photography from a recognized institute; 18–27 years; experience preferred.
- **Exam Pattern:** Written (GK, Reasoning, English) + Practical Photography Test (portfolio + on-spot assignment) + PE&MT.
- **Syllabus (Written):** GK, Reasoning, English, Basics of Photography (aperture, shutter, ISO, lighting, composition, photo-editing in Photoshop/Lightroom).
- **Practical:** Camera handling, studio & outdoor shoots, post-processing.
- **Salary:** Pay Level-4 (₹25,500–₹81,100).

### 4.8 Delhi Police Motor Mechanic
- **Recruiter:** Delhi Police.
- **Eligibility:** 10th pass + ITI in Motor Mechanic/Diesel Mechanic/Automobile Engineering; 18–27 years.
- **Exam Pattern:** Written (GK, Reasoning, Technical) + Trade Test + PE&MT.
- **Syllabus:** GK, Reasoning, Technical (Engine parts, fuel system, lubrication, brakes, transmission, electricals, diagnostics, tools, safety).
- **Salary:** Pay Level-2 to Level-4 depending on designation.

### 4.9 Delhi Police Driver
- **Recruiter:** Delhi Police (own notice; very common, large annual intake).
- **Eligibility:** 10th pass + valid LMV/HMV driving license; 3 years driving experience; 18–27 years.
- **Exam Pattern:** Written (GK, Reasoning, Traffic Rules, Technical) + Driving Test (road + HMV/LMV) + PE&MT.
- **Syllabus:** GK, Reasoning, Traffic & Road Safety (Motor Vehicle Act, signs, signals, offences, penalties), Vehicle maintenance (engine, brakes, tyre care, breakdown handling).
- **Salary:** Pay Level-2 to Level-4.
- **Official:** https://delhipolice.gov.in/ (Recruitments section).

## 5. Delhi Fire Service (DFS)

**Authority:** Delhi Fire Service, GNCTD (Home Dept). Director: Chief Fire Officer. Website: https://delhifire.nic.in/ (often unstable; mirror: delhi.gov.in → Fire Service).

### Posts & Eligibility
- **Fireman (Fire Operator):** 10th (Matric) pass; 18–27 years; height 165 cm (male)/152 cm (female), chest 78–83 (male).
- **Leading Fireman:** 12th + 5 years service as Fireman; departmental promotion.
- **Sub Officer:** B.Sc. (Science) with Physics/Chem/Math OR BE/B.Tech; 18–30; physically fit.
- **Station Officer:** Graduation in any discipline + relevant experience (or Sub Officer promotional); 18–30; physical standards.
- **Driver Operator / Fire Engine Driver:** 10th pass + HMV license + experience; 18–27.

### Exam Pattern
1. **Written Exam (Objective MCQ):** 100 Qs, 100 marks, 90 min, −0.25 negative.
   - General Knowledge & Current Affairs (25)
   - Reasoning & General Intelligence (25)
   - Quantitative Aptitude (25)
   - English/Hindi Language (15)
   - Fire Service awareness / Technical (10)
2. **Physical Efficiency Test (PET):** Male — 1.6 km in 6 min, Long jump 3.65 m, High jump 1.2 m, Rope climbing; Female — 800 m in 4 min, Long jump 2.7 m.
3. **Physical Measurement (PMT):** Height/chest; weight proportional to height.
4. **Medical Fitness:** Eyes (6/6), no color blindness for technical posts.
5. **Document Verification.**

### Detailed Syllabus
- **GK:** Indian History, Polity, Geography, Economics, General Science, Current Affairs.
- **Reasoning:** Analogies, Coding, Series, Blood Relations, Direction, Venn, Syllogism, Puzzles.
- **Quantitative:** Number System, Simplification, HCF/LCM, Percentages, Ratio, Profit-Loss, SI/CI, Time-Work, Distance, Geometry, Trigonometry (basic), Statistics.
- **English/Hindi:** Grammar, Comprehension, Vocabulary, Sentence correction.
- **Fire Service/Technical (Class 10+2 Science level):** Fire science basics, types of fire, extinguishers, fire-fighting equipment, first aid, rescue techniques, hydraulics, hazardous materials awareness, building fire safety.

### Salary
- Fireman: Pay Level-3 (₹21,700–₹69,100)
- Leading Fireman: Pay Level-4 (₹25,500–₹81,100)
- Sub Officer: Pay Level-6 (₹35,400–₹1,12,400)
- Station Officer: Pay Level-7 (₹44,900–₹1,42,400)

### Physical Standards (Male)
- Height: 165 cm (5 cm relaxation for hill-area/ST)
- Chest: 78–83 cm (4 cm expansion)
- Weight: proportional to height

### Official Link
- https://delhifire.nic.in/
- Vacancy notifications appear on https://delhi.gov.in/ (Recruitment section) and DSSSB for some posts.

## 6. Delhi Jail Staff

**Authority:** Department of Prisons, GNCTD (Home Dept). Posts typically advertised through DSSSB or Delhi Prison Dept.

### 6.1 Jail Prahari (Ward-er)
- **Eligibility:** 12th pass from recognized board; 18–27 years (relaxation as per rules).
- **Height:** Male 170 cm / Female 157 cm (relaxation for hill/ST).
- **Chest (Male):** 81–85 cm with 4 cm expansion.

### 6.2 Matron (Female)
- **Eligibility:** 12th pass; 18–27 years; female candidates only; physical standards for female.

### 6.3 Other posts
- Assistant Superintendent (Jail), Jail Welfare Officer, Craft Instructor — Graduation / relevant diploma.

### Exam Pattern
1. **Online Written Exam:** 100 Qs, 100 marks, 90 min, −0.25 negative.
   - General Awareness (25)
   - Reasoning (25)
   - Numerical Ability (25)
   - English/Hindi Language (15)
   - Concerned subject / professional knowledge (10)
2. **Physical Endurance Test (PET):** Running 1600 m, Long Jump, High Jump.
3. **Physical Measurement (PMT).**
4. **Medical Fitness Test.**
5. **Document Verification.**

### Detailed Syllabus
- **General Awareness:** History (India & Delhi — Sultanate, Mughal, British, Freedom struggle, post-independence), Polity (Indian Constitution, Parliament, State, Fundamental Rights/Duties), Geography, Indian Economy, General Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology up to 10+2), Current Affairs (national & international).
- **Reasoning:** Verbal & Non-verbal — analogies, similarities, differences, spatial visualization, problem-solving, analysis, judgment, decision making, visual memory, discrimination, observation, relationship concepts, arithmetic reasoning, verbal & figure classification, arithmetic number series, syllogism, coding-decoding.
- **Numerical Ability:** Number System, Decimals & Fractions, HCF/LCM, Ratio & Proportion, Percentages, Profit & Loss, Simple & Compound Interest, Time & Work, Distance, Averages, Mensuration, Geometry, Trigonometry (basic), Statistics.
- **English Language:** Comprehension, Spotting errors, Fill in the blanks, Synonyms/Antonyms, Sentence correction, Tenses, Voice, Narration, Idioms & Phrases.
- **Hindi Language:** वाक्य रचना, व्याकरण, पर्यायवाची, विलोम, मुहावरे-लोकोक्तियाँ, अपठित गद्यांश, अशुद्धि सुधार।
- **Professional (where applicable):** Prison management, Indian Penal Code basics, CRPC, rehabilitation, human rights, first aid, fire safety.

### Salary
- Jail Prahari / Matron: Pay Level-3 (₹21,700–₹69,100)
- Higher posts: Pay Level-4 to Pay Level-6.

### Official
- https://delhi.gov.in/ → Department of Prisons (recruitment notices)
- https://dsssb.delhi.gov.in/ (most prison posts now via DSSSB)

## 7. DSSSB (Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board)

**Authority:** DSSSB, GNCTD. Conducts exams for Group-B & Group-C posts across Delhi departments (MCD, NDMC, DOE, DHB, DJB, DUSIB, Transport, Health, Prison, Audit, NDMC, etc.). Headquarters: FC-18, Institutional Area, Karkardooma, Delhi-110092. Helpline: 011-22378382. Apply: https://dsssbonline.nic.in. See "DSSSB_Teaching_Officer_etc" master file for TGT/PGT/PRT details.

### DSSSB TGT, PGT, PRT — covered in teaching file (brief)
- **TGT:** Graduation + B.Ed + CTET (Paper-II); Tier-I (200 marks, GK + Reasoning + Numerical + English + Hindi) + Tier-II (concerned subject, 200 marks).
- **PGT:** Master's degree + B.Ed; pattern similar to TGT; concerned subject Tier-II.
- **PRT:** 12th + D.El.Ed / B.El.Ed; Tier-I (General) + Tier-II (concerned teaching aptitude / subject).
- **Pay:** TGT/PRT — Level-7; PGT — Level-8.

### 7.1 DSSSB Patwari
- **Post:** Patwari (Revenue Dept, GNCTD).
- **Eligibility:** Graduation; 18–27; Hindi/English typing preferred.
- **Exam Pattern:** Tier-I (MCQ 200 marks — GK 20, Reasoning 20, Numerical 20, English 20, Hindi 20, Concerned 100) + Tier-II (where applicable). Negative −0.25.
- **Syllabus:** GK (Delhi & India), Reasoning, Quant, English/Hindi, Land revenue system, surveying, Delhi Land Reforms, Record of Rights, Mutation, Jamabandi, agriculture basics, computer knowledge.
- **Salary:** Pay Level-3 (₹21,700–₹69,100).

### 7.2 DSSSB Section Officer (Horticulture, Audit, etc.)
- **Eligibility:** Graduation; departmental experience for SO (Horticulture) — 5 years.
- **Exam:** Tier-I (MCQ) + Tier-II (Descriptive/Subject) for some posts.
- **Syllabus:** Section A (GK, Reasoning, Quant, English/Hindi — common) + Section B (Subject — Horticulture: plant propagation, soil science, pest control, landscaping, irrigation; Audit: principles of auditing, accounting standards, public accounts, CAG guidelines; etc.).
- **Salary:** Pay Level-7 (₹44,900–₹1,42,400).

### 7.3 DSSSB Stenographer (Steno Grade-II/III)
- **Eligibility:** 12th pass; English shorthand 80 wpm / typing 40 wpm OR Hindi shorthand 70 wpm / typing 30 wpm.
- **Exam:** Tier-I (MCQ 100 marks — GK, Reasoning, English/Hindi, Computer) + Skill Test (Shorthand dictation 10 min @ 80/70 wpm, transcription 50/65 min, Typing 40/30 wpm).
- **Syllabus:** GK, Reasoning, English/Hindi language, MS Office, Shorthand dictation, Transcription, Typing.
- **Salary:** Pay Level-4 (Steno-III) / Level-6 (Steno-II).

### 7.4 DSSSB LDC (Lower Division Clerk)
- **Eligibility:** 12th pass; typing 35 wpm English / 30 wpm Hindi.
- **Exam:** Tier-I (MCQ 100 marks — GK, Reasoning, Quant, English/Hindi, Computer) + Tier-II (Skill/Typing).
- **Syllabus:** GK (Delhi & India), Reasoning, Quant, English/Hindi, MS Word, Excel, Internet, Email.
- **Salary:** Pay Level-2 (₹19,900–₹63,200).

### 7.5 DSSSB Junior Engineer (JE) — Civil / Electrical / Mechanical
- **Eligibility:** Diploma in Engineering in relevant discipline; 18–27.
- **Exam:** Tier-I (MCQ 200 marks — Section A: GK/Reasoning/Quant/Eng/Hindi 100 + Section B: Technical 100) + Tier-II (Technical).
- **Syllabus:** Section A: GK, Reasoning, Quant, English, Hindi, Computer; Section B: Civil (Surveying, Building Materials, RCC, Steel, Soil, Hydraulics, Estimation, Irrigation, Environment, Transportation) / Electrical (Circuits, Machines, Power Systems, Measurements, Control Systems, Power Electronics, Switchgear) / Mechanical (Thermodynamics, Strength of Materials, Manufacturing, Fluid Mechanics, Heat Transfer, Theory of Machines).
- **Salary:** Pay Level-6 (₹35,400–₹1,12,400).

### 7.6 DSSSB Pharmacist (Allopathic / Homeopathic / Ayurvedic)
- **Eligibility:** 12th (Science) + Diploma/degree in Pharmacy (D.Pharm / B.Pharm) for Allopathic; equivalent for Homeo/Ayurved.
- **Exam:** Tier-I (MCQ 200 marks — Section A general 100 + Section B Pharmacist 100) — HBA, drug classification, pharmacology, pharmaceutics, drug store management, hospital pharmacy, anatomy, physiology, community pharmacy, law.
- **Syllabus:** Section A common; Section B — Pharmacognosy, Pharmaceutics, Pharmaceutical Chemistry, Pharmacology, Biochemistry, Hospital & Clinical Pharmacy, Drug Laws, First Aid, Anatomy & Physiology.
- **Salary:** Pay Level-5 (₹29,200–₹92,300).

### 7.7 DSSSB Staff Nurse
- **Eligibility:** B.Sc (Nursing) / GNM + Delhi Nursing Council registration; 18–32.
- **Exam:** Tier-I MCQ (general 100 + nursing 100) + Tier-II (nursing technical).
- **Syllabus:** Anatomy, Physiology, Medical-Surgical Nursing, Pediatric Nursing, OBG, Community Health, Mental Health, Nutrition, First Aid, Pharmacology, Nursing Administration, Microbiology.
- **Salary:** Pay Level-7 (₹44,900–₹1,42,400).

### 7.8 DSSSB Assistant Teacher (Primary)
- **Eligibility:** 12th + D.El.Ed / B.El.Ed; CTET; 18–30.
- **Exam:** Tier-I + Tier-II (concerned — pedagogy, language, math, EVS, child development).
- **Syllabus:** GK, Reasoning, Quant, English/Hindi, Child Development & Pedagogy, Language (Hindi/English), Mathematics, EVS, ICT.
- **Salary:** Pay Level-6 (Assistant Teacher Primary).

### 7.9 DSSSB Computer Teacher
- **Eligibility:** B.Tech (CS/IT) OR BCA + B.Ed; OR equivalent; CTET preferred.
- **Exam:** Tier-I + Tier-II (Computer — fundamentals, programming, networking, DBMS, internet, multimedia, MS Office, pedagogy).
- **Salary:** Pay Level-6 to Level-7.

### 7.10 DSSSB Drawing Teacher
- **Eligibility:** 5-year Diploma in Drawing/Painting (BFA equivalent) + B.Ed; OR BFA + B.Ed.
- **Exam:** Tier-I general + Tier-II (Drawing — fundamentals, sketching, painting, art history, pedagogy, materials & techniques).
- **Salary:** Pay Level-6.

### 7.11 DSSSB Music Teacher
- **Eligibility:** Degree/Diploma in Music (Vocal/Instrumental) from recognized university; B.Ed preferred.
- **Exam:** Tier-I + Tier-II (Music Theory, Raga, Taal, History of Indian Music, Gharana, Pedagogy).
- **Salary:** Pay Level-6.

### 7.12 DSSSB Librarian
- **Eligibility:** B.Lib.I.Sc / M.Lib.I.Sc; 18–30.
- **Exam:** Tier-I + Tier-II (Library classification — Dewey Decimal, Colon, AACR-2/RDA, cataloging, reference, library management, ICT in libraries, information science).
- **Salary:** Pay Level-6.

### 7.13 DSSSB PET (Physical Education Teacher)
- **Eligibility:** Bachelor's in Physical Education (B.P.Ed) or equivalent; CTET.
- **Exam:** Tier-I + Tier-II (Physical Education — anatomy, physiology, sports training, rules of games, fitness, first aid, sports psychology, pedagogy).
- **Physical:** Fitness test.
- **Salary:** Pay Level-6.

### 7.14 DSSSB Lab Assistant
- **Eligibility:** 12th (Science) with relevant subjects; OR BSc (1st year); 18–27.
- **Exam:** Tier-I + Tier-II (Lab techniques, equipment handling, safety, basic chemistry/physics/biology practicals, recording, computer basics).
- **Salary:** Pay Level-2 to Level-3.

### 7.15 DSSSB Crafts Teacher
- **Eligibility:** 10th/12th + ITI in craft trade (weaving, pottery, book-binding, etc.) OR Diploma in relevant craft; B.Ed preferred.
- **Exam:** Tier-I + Tier-II (Trade theory, materials, tools, pedagogy, safety).
- **Salary:** Pay Level-6.

### DSSSB Common Exam Pattern (indicative, post-2018)
- **Tier-I (One-Tier for most posts):** 200 marks, 2 hours, MCQ, −0.25.
  - Section A: GK (20), Reasoning (20), Numerical (20), English (20), Hindi (20).
  - Section B: Concerned/Professional subject (100).
- **Tier-II (where applicable):** Subject-specific descriptive/MCQ.
- **Skill Test:** Typing, Shorthand, Driving (where applicable).
- **Document Verification + Medical (where applicable).**

### DSSSB Indicative Syllabus Sections
- **Section A (General):** GK (India & Delhi — History, Polity, Geography, Economy, General Science, Current Affairs), Reasoning, Quantitative Aptitude, English Language, Hindi Language, Computer basics.
- **Section B (Subject-specific):** As per post requirement.

### Official
- https://dsssb.delhi.gov.in/
- Apply: https://dsssbonline.nic.in

## 8. MCD (Municipal Corporation of Delhi) Exams

**Authority:** MCD (now bifurcated into MCD + NDMC after 2022 trifurcation reorganisation). Most MCD posts — Sanitary Inspector, Health Inspector, JE, UDC, LDC, ASO, Section Officer — are recruited through DSSSB.

### 8.1 MCD Sanitary Inspector
- **Eligibility:** 12th (Science) + Diploma/Certificate in Sanitary/Public Health; 18–27.
- **Exam:** DSSSB Tier-I (MCQ 200 marks — General 100 + Sanitary/Public Health 100).
- **Syllabus:** Section A common; Section B — Public Health, Epidemiology, Sanitation, Waste Management, Water Supply, Food Hygiene, Vector Control, Health Education, First Aid, Anatomy, Physiology, Microbiology, Indian Constitution, MCD Acts.
- **Salary:** Pay Level-4 (₹25,500–₹81,100).

### 8.2 MCD Health Inspector
- **Eligibility:** Bachelor's in Public Health / Sanitary Science / B.Sc (Bio) + Diploma in Health Inspector; 18–30.
- **Exam:** DSSSB Tier-I + Tier-II (Health Inspection).
- **Syllabus:** Public Health, Epidemiology, Communicable & Non-communicable diseases, Maternal & Child Health, Nutrition, Immunization, Health Statistics, MCD health schemes, Environmental sanitation, First Aid.
- **Salary:** Pay Level-5 (₹29,200–₹92,300).

### 8.3 MCD Junior Engineer (Civil/Electrical/Mechanical)
- **Eligibility:** Diploma in relevant Engineering; 18–27.
- **Exam:** DSSSB Tier-I (General 100 + Technical 100) + Tier-II.
- **Syllabus:** Section B — Civil (Building Materials, Survey, RCC, Steel, Soil Mechanics, Hydraulics, Estimation, Transportation, Environment); Electrical (Circuits, Machines, Power Systems, Measurements, Power Electronics); Mechanical (Thermodynamics, SOM, Fluid Mechanics, Manufacturing, Heat Transfer, TOM).
- **Salary:** Pay Level-6.

### 8.4 MCD UDC (Upper Division Clerk)
- **Eligibility:** Graduation + typing 35/30 wpm; 18–27.
- **Exam:** DSSSB Tier-I (200 marks) + Skill Test.
- **Syllabus:** GK, Reasoning, Quant, English/Hindi, Computer (MS Office, Internet), Typing, Office Procedures.
- **Salary:** Pay Level-4.

### 8.5 MCD LDC
- **Eligibility:** 12th pass + typing 35/30 wpm.
- **Exam:** DSSSB Tier-I + Skill Test.
- **Salary:** Pay Level-2.

### 8.6 MCD ASO (Assistant Section Officer)
- **Eligibility:** Graduation; 18–27.
- **Exam:** DSSSB Tier-I (200 marks) + Tier-II (descriptive + office procedure).
- **Syllabus:** GK, Reasoning, Quant, English/Hindi, Office Management, Noting & Drafting, CCS Rules, RTI, General Financial Rules, MCD administration.
- **Salary:** Pay Level-7 (₹44,900–₹1,42,400).

### 8.7 MCD Section Officer
- **Eligibility:** Graduation; 5 years MCD service for internal; or direct via DSSSB competitive.
- **Exam:** DSSSB Tier-I + Tier-II.
- **Salary:** Pay Level-7 to Level-8.

### Official
- MCD notices: https://mcdonline.nic.in/ (often redirects to https://edistrict.delhi.gov.in/ or DSSSB)
- https://dsssb.delhi.gov.in/ (most MCD recruitment)

## 9. NDMC (New Delhi Municipal Council) Exams

**Authority:** NDMC (Constitution under NDMC Act 1994). Jurisdiction: Lutyens' Delhi & surrounding areas.

### NDMC Various Posts (brief)
- **Recruiter:** DSSSB for most Group-C posts; NDMC's own notice for some (e.g., Medical Officer, Teaching Faculty, Sanitary Inspector, Section Officer).
- **Common posts:** Sanitary Inspector, Health Inspector, Junior Engineer (Civil/Electrical), Pharmacist, Staff Nurse, LDC, UDC, ASO, Section Officer (Horticulture), TGT/PGT/PRT, Driver, Conductor, Mali.
- **Eligibility:** 10th/12th/Graduation/Diploma/Degree in relevant discipline; age 18–27/30 (relaxation applicable).
- **Exam Pattern:** DSSSB Tier-I (MCQ 200 marks — General + Subject) or NDMC's own pattern for direct posts.
- **Syllabus:** Common Section A (GK Delhi & India, Reasoning, Quant, English/Hindi, Computer) + Section B (Subject-specific).
- **Salary:** Pay Level-2 to Level-8 depending on post.
- **Official:** https://ndmc.gov.in/

## 10. Delhi Cantonment Board (DCB)

**Authority:** Delhi Cantonment Board (under Ministry of Defence, with elected members). Conducts own recruitment for Group-C & Group-D posts; some via DSSSB.

### Common Posts
- **Primary Teacher / TGT:** Graduation + B.Ed + CTET.

## Tips for Delhi Exams

1. **Delhi GK is critical** — most DSSSB/MCD/DDA/Delhi Police exams include a substantial chunk of Delhi-specific GK (Delhi history, Sultanate, Mughal, British, post-1947, GNCTD schemes, geography of Delhi, MPD 2041, Delhi Sultanate monuments, important acts).
2. **Hindi is mandatory** — paper is bilingual (Hindi & English) and Section A always has Hindi section.
3. **Tier-I + Tier-II pattern is now standard** for DSSSB — Section A is common, Section B is subject-specific (100 marks each).
4. **Physical is mandatory** — for all uniformed posts (Constable, HC, Fireman, SI, Jail staff) — be prepared for running, jumping, and medical standards from day 1.
5. **Document verification** is stringent — 10th marksheet, 12th, graduation, caste certificate, Delhi domicile, character certificate, photo, ID proofs.
6. **Negative marking** of 0.25 (DSSSB, Delhi Police, DDA, DTL) means unattempted > random guessing.
7. **Speed vs accuracy** — DSSSB papers have 200 marks in 120 minutes = ~36 sec per question.
8. **Practice on DSSSB's official DigiExam mock test** (free) — very close to actual interface.
9. **Solve previous year papers** (DSSSB & Delhi Police) — questions are often repeated with modifications.
10. **Combine SSC + Delhi-specific prep** — many topics overlap (GK, Reasoning, Quant, English), so prepare them together.
