# SSC GD Constable (General Duty) Examination

## 1. Overview

- **Full Name**: Constable (General Duty) in CAPFs, NIA, SSF, and Rifleman (GD) in Assam Rifles Examination
- **Common Name**: SSC GD Constable
- **Conducting Body**: Staff Selection Commission (SSC), Government of India
- **Headquarters**: Block No. 12, CGO Complex, Lodhi Road, New Delhi - 110003
- **Website**: https://ssc.gov.in/ (also https://ssc.nic.in/)
- **Frequency**: Annual (once a year)
- **Mode of Exam**: Online Computer Based Examination (CBE)
- **Parent Organization**: Department of Personnel and Training (DoPT), Ministry of Personnel, Public Grievances and Pensions

---

## 2. Posts Offered

The recruitment is conducted for the following posts across various Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs) and other organizations:

### 2.1 Constable (General Duty)
- **Border Security Force (BSF)**
- **Central Industrial Security Force (CISF)**
- **Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF)**
- **Indo-Tibetan Border Police (ITBP)**
- **Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB)**
- **National Investigation Agency (NIA)**
- **Secretariat Security Force (SSF)**

### 2.2 Constable/Driver
- **ITBP Constable/Driver**
- **CISF Constable/Driver** (in select forces)

### 2.3 Rifleman
- **Rifleman (General Duty) in Assam Rifles**

> Note: Both **Male and Female** candidates are eligible for all posts (except where explicitly mentioned otherwise in the official notification).

---

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

### 3.1 Educational Qualification
- **Minimum**: Matriculation (10th Class) Pass from a recognized Board/University.
- No higher qualification is required; candidates with higher qualifications are also eligible.

### 3.2 Age Limit
- **Minimum Age**: 18 years
- **Maximum Age**: 23 years
- **Age Relaxation** (as per Government of India norms):
  - SC/ST: 5 years
  - OBC: 3 years
  - Ex-Servicemen: 3 years (after deduction of military service)
  - Children/Journalist affected by 1984 riots: 5 years
- Age is calculated as per the cutoff date mentioned in the official notification (typically January 1 of the examination year).

### 3.3 Nationality
- Must be a citizen of India.

### 3.4 Gender
- Both **Male and Female** candidates are eligible.

---

## 4. Exam Pattern

The selection process consists of four stages:

### Stage 1: Computer Based Examination (CBE)

| Section | Subject | No. of Questions | Marks | Time |
|---------|---------|------------------|-------|------|
| Part-A | General Intelligence & Reasoning | 20 | 20 | - |
| Part-B | General Knowledge & General Awareness | 20 | 20 | - |
| Part-C | Elementary Mathematics | 20 | 20 | - |
| Part-D | English/Hindi | 20 | 20 | - |
| **Total** | **4 Sections** | **100** | **100** | **90 minutes** |

- **Medium**: Bilingual (English & Hindi) — except for the English language section in Part-D.
- **Question Type**: Objective Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs).
- **Duration**: 90 minutes (120 minutes for PwD candidates eligible for scribe).
- **Marking**: +1 for correct answer; -0.25 for incorrect answer (negative marking).

### Stage 2: Physical Standard Test (PST)
- Qualifying in nature — candidates are screened on physical measurements.

### Stage 3: Physical Efficiency Test (PET)
- Qualifying in nature — candidates must complete specific physical tasks within the time limit.

### Stage 4: Medical Examination
- Candidates who qualify PST/PET undergo a Detailed Medical Examination (DME).

## 5. Detailed Syllabus

### 5.1 General Intelligence & Reasoning (20 Questions)
- Analogical Reasoning
- Number Series
- Coding-Decoding
- Blood Relations
- Direction Sense
- Venn Diagrams
- Syllogism
- Pattern Completion
- Non-Verbal Reasoning
- Classification (Odd One Out)
- Similarities and Differences
- Statement & Conclusion
- Ranking
- Mirror Image
- Embedded Figures
- Paper Folding & Cutting

### 5.2 General Knowledge & General Awareness (20 Questions)
- **History**: Indian History (Ancient, Medieval, Modern), World History
- **Polity**: Indian Constitution, Political System, Panchayati Raj
- **Geography**: Indian Geography, World Geography, Physical, Social, Economic Geography
- **Economics**: Indian Economy, Budget, Five Year Plans, Banking, Finance
- **Science**: Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Computer Basics
- **Sports**: National and International sports events, awards, trophies
- **Awards**: National and International awards (Nobel, Bharat Ratna, etc.)
- **Books & Authors**: Important books and their authors
- **Current Affairs**: National and International events of past 6 months

### 5.3 Elementary Mathematics (20 Questions)
- Number System
- Percentages
- Ratio & Proportion
- Averages
- Simple & Compound Interest
- Profit & Loss
- Discount
- Time & Work
- Time & Distance (Speed, Trains, Boats)
- Mensuration (Area, Volume, Surface)
- Algebra (Basic)
- Geometry (Basic)
- Trigonometry (Basic)
- Data Interpretation (Tables, Charts, Graphs)

### 5.4 English/Hindi (20 Questions)
- **Comprehension**: Reading comprehension passages
- **Vocabulary**: Synonyms, Antonyms, One-word substitution
- **Grammar**: Parts of Speech, Tenses, Voice, Narration
- **Fill in the Blanks**
- **Error Spotting/Correction**
- **Idioms & Phrases**
- **Sentence Improvement**
- **Active/Passive Voice**

## 6. Physical Standard Test (PST) Requirements

### 6.1 For Male Candidates

| Category | Height | Chest (Unexpanded - Expanded) |
|----------|--------|-------------------------------|
| General/OBC/SC | 170 cm | 81 cm - 85 cm |
| ST (All States) | 162.5 cm | 76 cm - 80 cm |
| Garhwali, Kumaoni, Gorkha, Dogra, Maratha, Assamese, Kashmiri Manipuri, etc. | 165 cm | 78 cm - 82 cm |

- **Weight**: Proportionate to height and age (as per medical standards).

### 6.2 For Female Candidates

| Category | Height |
|----------|--------|
| General/OBC/SC | 157 cm |
| ST (All States) | 150 cm |
| Garhwali, Kumaoni, Gorkha, Dogra, Maratha, Assamese, Kashmiri Manipuri, etc. | 155 cm |

- **Weight**: Proportionate to height and age.

> Note: Relaxation applicable for candidates from hill states and specific categories as per official notification.

---

## 7. Physical Efficiency Test (PET) Requirements

### 7.1 For Male Candidates
- **Race**: 5 km in **24 minutes**
- **Squats**: 20 (in one go) — for Ladakh region candidates
- **Push-ups**: 20 (in one go) — for Ladakh region candidates

### 7.2 For Female Candidates
- **Race**: 1.6 km in **8 minutes 30 seconds (8.5 min)**
- **Squats**: 15 (in one go) — for Ladakh region candidates

> Note: PET is qualifying in nature. No marks are awarded. Pregnant female candidates are exempted from PET and may be considered for deferred recruitment as per rules.

## 8. Marking Scheme

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Correct Answer | **+1 mark** |
| Incorrect Answer | **-0.25 marks** (Negative Marking) |
| Unattempted | 0 marks |
| Multiple Answers | Treated as incorrect (negative marking) |
| Total Marks | 100 |
| Total Questions | 100 |

- There is no negative marking for un-attempted questions.
- CBE is conducted in multiple shifts; normalization is applied to adjust for difficulty variation.

## 9. Salary Structure

- **Pay Level**: Pay Level-3 (7th Central Pay Commission)
- **Pay Band**: Rs. 21,700 - Rs. 69,100
- **Grade Pay**: Rs. 2,000 (pre-revised structure)
- **Starting Basic Pay**: Rs. 21,700
- **Gross Salary (Approx.)**: Rs. 25,000 - Rs. 35,000 per month (including allowances)
- **Allowances**: Dearness Allowance (DA), House Rent Allowance (HRA), Transport Allowance, Ration Money, Special Duty Allowance, etc.
- **Other Benefits**: Free accommodation (or HRA), uniform, medical facilities, LTC, pension (NPS), and other government perks.
