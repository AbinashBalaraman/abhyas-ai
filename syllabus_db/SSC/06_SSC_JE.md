The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) is an attached office under the Department of Personnel and Training (DoPT), Government of India, constituted on 4 November 1975. SSC conducts various competitive examinations for recruitment to Group B (Non-Gazetted) and Group C posts across Central Government ministries, departments, and subordinate offices. SSC JE is one of the most popular engineering recruitment exams in India.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

### A. Nationality

Candidate must be:
- A citizen of India, OR
- A subject of Nepal/Bhutan, OR
- A Tibetan refugee who came to India before 1 January 1962 with the intention of permanent settlement, OR
- A person of Indian origin who has migrated from Pakistan, Burma, Sri Lanka, Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Zambia, Malawi, Zaire, Ethiopia, or Vietnam with the intention of permanent settlement in India.

## 4. Exam Pattern

The SSC JE examination comprises **two papers** - Paper-I (CBE) and Paper-II (Offline Descriptive). Both papers are mandatory; the final merit is based on combined performance.

### Paper-I (Computer-Based Examination)

| Section | Questions | Marks | Duration | Negative Marking |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **General Intelligence & Reasoning** | 50 | 50 | 2 Hours (120 min) | 0.25 marks per wrong answer |
| **General Awareness** | 50 | 50 | (combined) | 0.25 marks per wrong answer |
| **General Engineering (Civil & Structural / Electrical / Mechanical)** | 100 | 100 | (combined) | 0.25 marks per wrong answer |
| **Total** | **200** | **200** | **2 Hours** | **Yes** |

> **Updated Pattern Note (2024-25 onwards):** As per the latest SSC JE 2024/2025 notification, Paper-I now contains 200 questions (50 Reasoning + 50 General Awareness + 100 General Engineering) for 200 marks total. The earlier legacy pattern (per the user's brief) was 100 Qs / 100 marks covering only General Intelligence + General Awareness; candidates preparing should follow the current 200-Q pattern.

### Paper-II (Offline Descriptive / Pen-and-Paper)

| Section | Questions | Marks | Duration | Negative Marking |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Part A:** Civil & Structural Engineering **OR** | 100 | 300 | 2 Hours (120 min) | 1 mark per wrong answer |
| **Part B:** Electrical Engineering **OR** | 100 | 300 | 2 Hours | 1 mark per wrong answer |
| **Part C:** Mechanical Engineering | 100 | 300 | 2 Hours | 1 mark per wrong answer |

> **Permissible Aids in Paper-II:** Slide-Rule, Calculator, Logarithm Tables, and Steam Table are **allowed** in Paper-II only. They are **not permitted** in Paper-I.
> **Normalization:** Marks in Paper-I are normalized; final merit is based on Paper-I + Paper-II performance.

### Selection Process

1. **Paper-I (CBE)** - 200 marks (qualifying-cum-screening for Paper-II)
2. **Paper-II (Descriptive)** - 300 marks
3. **Document Verification** - Original documents check
4. **Medical Examination** - Physical fitness test

## 5. Detailed Syllabus

### A. General Intelligence & Reasoning

- Analogies (word / letter / number)
- Similarities and Differences
- Space Visualization
- Problem Solving
- Analysis and Judgment
- Decision Making
- Visual Memory
- Discrimination and Observation
- Relationship Concepts
- Arithmetical Reasoning
- Verbal and Figure Classification
- Arithmetical Number Series
- Abstract Ideas and Symbols
- Coding-Decoding
- Venn Diagrams
- Direction and Distance
- Blood Relations
- Seating Arrangements
- Syllogism
- Statement & Conclusion
- Paper Folding Method
- Matrix
- Word Formation
- Series Completion

### B. General Awareness

- History (Indian History - Ancient, Medieval, Modern)
- Polity (Indian Constitution & Political System)
- Geography (Indian & World)
- Economics (Basic Economics, Indian Economy)
- General Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
- Current Affairs (National & International)
- Static General Knowledge
- Books and Authors
- Important Government Schemes
- Prominent Personalities in News
- Sports
- Culture
- Awards and Honors
- Important Days/Events

### C. Civil Engineering (Paper-I + Paper-II Detailed)

#### 1. Building Materials
- Physical properties of building materials (stone, brick, timber, cement, aggregate, mortar, concrete)
- Timber, Plywood, Paints, Varnishes, Distempers
- Lime, Cement, Aggregates: properties, tests, classifications

#### 2. Surveying
- Chain surveying, compass surveying, leveling
- Theodolite, traverse surveying
- Triangulation, plane table surveying
- Contouring, curve setting
- Area and volume computation
- Modern surveying instruments (Total Station, GPS, GIS basics)

#### 3. Soil Mechanics / Geotechnical Engineering
- Index properties of soil
- Soil classification (IS classification)
- Permeability, seepage, effective stress
- Compaction, consolidation
- Shear strength of soils
- Earth pressure theories (Rankine, Coulomb)
- Bearing capacity, shallow & deep foundations
- Stability of slopes

#### 4. Hydraulics / Fluid Mechanics
- Fluid properties, fluid statics
- Fluid kinematics, dynamics
- Flow measurement (weirs, notches, venturi, orifice meter)
- Flow through pipes (laminar & turbulent, Darcy-Weisbach, Hazen-Williams)
- Open channel flow
- Dimensional analysis and similitude

#### 5. Hydrology & Irrigation Engineering
- Hydrological cycle, rainfall, runoff
- Flood frequency analysis
- Water requirement of crops
- Canal design (Kennedy's, Lacey's theory)
- Headworks, cross-drainage works
- Dams (gravity, earth, arch)
- Spillways, canals, irrigation methods
- Waterlogging and drainage

#### 6. Transportation Engineering
- Highway planning and development
- Geometric design of highways
- Traffic engineering
- Pavement design (flexible & rigid)
- Highway materials, construction, maintenance
- Railway engineering (track, sleepers, ballast, points and crossings)
- Bridge engineering basics
- Tunnel engineering basics

#### 7. Environmental Engineering
- Water supply engineering (sources, quality, treatment, distribution)
- Sewerage systems, sewage treatment
- Air pollution, noise pollution
- Solid waste management
- Environmental impact assessment

#### 8. Structural Engineering
- **Theory of Structures:** Bending moment and shear force diagrams, determinate & indeterminate structures
- **Concrete Technology:** Mix design, workability, strength, durability
- **RCC Design:** Working stress and limit state method, design of beams, slabs, columns, footings, retaining walls, water tanks
- **Steel Design:** Connections, tension & compression members, beams, column bases, roof trusses
- **Structural Analysis:** Trusses, arches, cables, influence line diagrams
- **Stability of structures**

#### 9. Estimation, Costing & Valuation
- Quantity estimation
- Rate analysis
- Valuation of buildings
- Specifications

#### 10. Water Supply Engineering
- Water treatment (sedimentation, filtration, disinfection)
- Water distribution systems
- Plumbing systems

### D. Electrical Engineering (Paper-I + Paper-II Detailed)

#### 1. Basic Electrical & Circuit Theory
- Basic concepts (Ohm's law, Kirchhoff's laws)
- Circuit law (DC and AC circuits)
- Network theorems (Thevenin, Norton, Superposition, Maximum Power Transfer)
- Resonance, coupled circuits
- Three-phase circuits, star-delta transformation

#### 2. Magnetic Circuit
- Magnetic materials, B-H curve
- Magnetic circuits, hysteresis
- Electromagnetic induction

#### 3. AC Fundamentals
- Single-phase and three-phase AC circuits
- Power factor, real/apparent/reactive power
- Phasor diagrams, complex power

#### 4. DC Machines
- DC generator (working principle, types, characteristics, armature reaction, commutation)
- DC motor (types, characteristics, speed control, starting, applications)

#### 5. Transformers
- Single-phase and three-phase transformers
- Equivalent circuit, phasor diagram, regulation, efficiency
- Auto-transformer, instrument transformers (CT, PT)

#### 6. Synchronous Machines
- Alternators (construction, EMF equation, regulation, synchronizing)
- Synchronous motors (operation, V-curves, starting methods, applications)

#### 7. Induction Motors
- Three-phase and single-phase induction motors
- Construction, working principle, slip, torque, speed control, starting methods
- Fractional Kilowatt Motors and Single-Phase Induction Motors

#### 8. Generation, Transmission & Distribution
- Power generation (thermal, hydro, nuclear, renewable)
- Transmission line parameters, line insulators
- Underground cables
- Distribution systems (radial, ring, interconnected)
- Substations, switchgear, protection
- HVDC transmission basics

#### 9. Estimation & Costing
- Electrical installations, wiring systems
- IE rules, safety
- Cost estimation of electrical works

#### 10. Utilization of Electrical Energy
- Electric heating, welding
- Illumination (lighting schemes, lux calculations)
- Electric traction
- Electrolysis, electric drives

#### 11. Basic Electronics
- Semiconductor diodes, transistors
- Amplifiers, oscillators
- Power electronics basics (SCR, TRIAC, MOSFET, IGBT)
- Digital electronics fundamentals

#### 12. Measurement & Measuring Instruments
- Voltmeter, ammeter, wattmeter, energy meter
- Megger, potentiometer
- CRO basics
- Transducers and sensors

### E. Mechanical Engineering (Paper-I + Paper-II Detailed)

#### 1. Theory of Machines & Machine Design
- Kinematics of machines (mechanisms, cams, gears, belts, chains)
- Dynamics of machines (flywheel, governors, balancing)
- Vibrations (free, forced, damped)
- Machine design (design of shafts, keys, couplings, brakes, clutches, springs, bearings, gears)
- Stress analysis, theories of failure
- Design of machine elements (riveted/welded joints, power screws)

#### 2. Strength of Materials / Engineering Mechanics
- Stress, strain, elastic constants
- Bending moment and shear force
- Bending and torsion of circular shafts
- Deflection of beams
- Combined stresses, principal stresses
- Columns and struts (Euler's, Rankine's)
- Thin and thick cylinders, spheres

#### 3. Engineering Materials
- Classification, properties, applications of engineering materials
- Heat treatment of steel (annealing, normalizing, hardening, tempering)
- Alloys, cast iron, non-ferrous metals
- Composite materials basics

#### 4. Production Engineering
- Casting processes (sand, die, investment, centrifugal)
- Forming processes (forging, rolling, extrusion, drawing, sheet metal)
- Welding (arc, gas, resistance, TIG/MIG, soldering, brazing)
- Machining (turning, drilling, milling, grinding)
- Machine tools and operations
- Metrology and inspection
- Powder metallurgy basics
- Jigs and fixtures

#### 5. Industrial Engineering & Management
- Work study, time and motion study
- Production planning and control
- Inventory control (EOQ, ABC analysis)
- Quality control (SQC, TQM, Six Sigma basics)
- Operations research (linear programming, queuing theory)
- Engineering economics, cost analysis
- Management principles

#### 6. Thermodynamics
- Zeroth, 1st and 2nd laws of thermodynamics
- Thermodynamic processes and cycles
- Air standard cycles (Otto, Diesel, Dual, Brayton)
- Properties of pure substances
- Entropy, availability
- Refrigeration cycles (Vapour compression, absorption)

#### 7. Heat Transfer
- Conduction (1D, fins)
- Convection (free, forced)
- Radiation (black body, grey body)
- Heat exchangers

#### 8. Fluid Mechanics & Hydraulic Machines
- Properties and classification of fluids
- Fluid statics, manometry
- Fluid kinematics, dynamics
- Bernoulli's equation and applications
- Flow measurement
- Boundary layer theory
- Hydraulic turbines (Pelton, Francis, Kaplan)
- Centrifugal and reciprocating pumps
- Nozzles and steam turbines

#### 9. Power Plant Engineering
- Steam power plants (Rankine cycle, boilers, condensers)
- Internal Combustion (IC) engines - performance, combustion, cooling, lubrication
- Gas turbine plants
- Hydroelectric power plants
- Nuclear power plants
- Renewable energy sources (solar, wind, biomass)

#### 10. Refrigeration & Air Conditioning
- Refrigerants, refrigeration cycles
- Refrigeration equipment (compressors, condensers, evaporators)
- Psychrometrics
- Air conditioning systems, comfort conditions, load calculations
- Cold storage

#### 11. Auto Mobile Engineering
- Vehicle layout, transmission, steering, suspension, braking
- Engine components, fuel supply, ignition, cooling, lubrication systems
- Electric and hybrid vehicles basics

## 10. Quick Preparation Tips

1. **Know the Pattern First:** Start with the latest SSC JE notification and exam pattern. Paper-I has 200 Qs; Paper-II is descriptive.
2. **Strong Fundamentals:** Master core engineering subjects (Civil / Electrical / Mechanical) for Paper-II - this carries 60% of the total weightage (300/500 marks).
3. **Daily Reasoning Practice:** 30-45 minutes daily for General Intelligence & Reasoning.
4. **Stay Updated:** Read newspaper / monthly magazines for current affairs; revise static GK topics weekly.
5. **Solve PYQs:** Solve the last 10 years' SSC JE previous year papers.
6. **Mock Tests:** Take weekly full-length mock tests to build time management and accuracy.
7. **Permissible Aids:** Practice using calculator / log tables / steam tables for Paper-II.
8. **Time Management:** Devote ~40% time to Paper-II engineering, ~25% to Reasoning, ~20% to GA, ~15% to revision/mocks.
9. **Revision Notes:** Maintain short notes for formulae, design codes (IS 456, IS 800), and shortcuts.
10. **Health & Consistency:** Maintain 6-8 hours of focused study daily for 4-5 months consistently.

## 11. Key Exam-Day Guidelines

- Carry **Admit Card** + **Photo ID** (Aadhaar / PAN / Voter ID / Passport) to the examination centre.
- **Reporting Time:** Usually 1-1.5 hours before the exam start.
- **Permitted Items in Paper-I:** Blue/black ballpoint pen, Admit Card, ID Proof. (No calculator, log table, or mobile phone allowed.)
- **Permitted Items in Paper-II:** Calculator, Slide-Rule, Logarithm Table, Steam Table, Ballpoint pen, Admit Card, ID Proof.
- **Prohibited Items:** Mobile phones, smart watches, electronic gadgets, books, notes, food items, bags.
- **No:** Smoking, alcohol, or intoxicating substances in/around the exam centre.
- **Read Instructions Carefully:** Before attempting, read the OMR / online instructions thoroughly.
