# SSC Stenographer Grade 'C' and Grade 'D' Examination

## 1. Overview

- **Full Name**: Staff Selection Commission Stenographers Examination (SSC Stenographer 'C' & 'D')
- **Conducting Body**: Staff Selection Commission (SSC), Government of India
- **Under Department**: Department of Personnel and Training (DoPT), Ministry of Personnel, Public Grievances and Pensions
- **Headquarters**: Block No. 12, Lodhi Road, CGO Complex, Lodhi Colony, New Delhi — 110003
- **Established**: 4 November 1975 (as Subordinate Service Commission; renamed SSC on 26 September 1977)
- **Frequency**: Conducted annually (every year)
- **Mode of Exam**: Online Computer Based Examination (CBE) + Skill Test
- **Official Website**: [https://ssc.gov.in](https://ssc.gov.in) (current) / [https://ssc.nic.in](https://ssc.nic.in) (legacy)
- **Latest Cycle (2025)**: Notification released 6 June 2025; revised vacancies 1,590 posts; CBE held 6–11 August 2025

> **Note on Pattern**: The current SSC Stenographer CBE (effective 2023–2025 cycle) has **3 sections totalling 200 questions / 200 marks**. The user-supplied structure shows 4 sections including Quantitative Aptitude, which is documented here as requested, with a clear note that the latest official pattern has no separate QA section. Candidates should always verify with the latest SSC notification.

## 4. Exam Pattern

### Selection Process (Two Tiers)
1. **Tier-I**: Computer Based Examination (CBE) — Objective MCQ, screening/merit purpose
2. **Tier-II**: Skill Test in Stenography — Qualifying in nature (Dictation + Transcription)
3. **Document Verification** — for finally recommended candidates

### Tier-I: Computer Based Examination (CBE)

The CBE carries **200 Questions / 200 Marks** for a duration of **2 hours (120 minutes)**. Candidates with scribe get 2 hours 40 minutes.

**User-specified structure (4 sections, 200 Qs / 200 marks, 2 hours)**:

| Section | No. of Questions | Marks |
|---------|------------------|-------|
| General Intelligence & Reasoning | 50 | 50 |
| General Awareness | 50 | 50 |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 50 | 50 |
| English Language & Comprehension | 50 | 50 |
| **Total** | **200** | **200** |

> **Important — Current Official Pattern**: The latest SSC Stenographer CBE (2024 & 2025 cycle) has only **3 sections** (no Quantitative Aptitude). Marks distribution is: General Intelligence & Reasoning (50 Qs / 50 marks), General Awareness (50 Qs / 50 marks), English Language & Comprehension (100 Qs / 100 marks) = 200 Qs / 200 marks. Confirm the latest notification before preparation.

### Marking Scheme
- **+1 mark** for every correct answer
- **−0.25 marks** (¼ negative marking) for every wrong answer
- **0 mark** for un-attempted questions
- Bilingual paper: Hindi & English (except English section which is English only)

### A. General Intelligence & Reasoning
- Analogies
- Similarities and Differences
- Space Visualization
- Problem Solving
- Analysis
- Judgment
- Decision Making
- Visual Memory
- Discrimination
- Observation
- Relationship Concepts

- Verbal and Figure Classification

- Abstract Ideas and Symbols and their Relationship
- Non-verbal Series
- Coding-Decoding, Puzzle, Venn Diagram, Direction & Distance, Blood Relation, Order & Ranking, Number Series, Verbal & Non-Verbal Reasoning

### B. General Awareness
Questions designed to test the candidate's general awareness of the environment and its application to society. Topics include:
- **Static GK**
- **Current Affairs** (National & International)
- **History** (Indian & World)
- **Polity / Indian Constitution** & General Polity
- **Geography** (India & World)
- **Economics** / Economic Scene
- **General Science** (Physics, Chemistry, Biology — up to 10+2 level)
- **Science & Technology** & Scientific Research
- **Culture**
- **Sports**
- **Static Awareness** (important days, books, awards, capitals, etc.)

### C. Quantitative Aptitude

- Percentage
- Ratio and Proportion
- Average
- Interest (Simple & Compound)
- Profit and Loss
- Discount
- Time and Work
- Time and Distance
- Mensuration (areas and volumes)
- Relationship between Numbers
- Fundamental Arithmetical Operations

### D. English Language & Comprehension
- **Vocabulary**
- **Grammar**
- **Sentence Structure**
- **Synonyms & Antonyms**
- **Idioms and Phrases**
- **Comprehension Passage** (Reading Comprehension)
- Fill in the Blanks
- Spellings
- Active & Passive Voice
- Direct & Indirect Speech
- Para Jumbles / Sentence Jumbles
- Phrase Replacement / Sentence Correction
- Error Spotting
- One-word Substitution
- Cloze Test

## 6. Skill Test Details (Tier-II)

The Skill Test is **qualifying in nature**. Candidates who qualify the CBE are called for the Skill Test in the ratio prescribed by SSC.

### Dictation
| Post | Speed | Duration |
|------|-------|----------|
| **Stenographer Grade 'C'** | **100 wpm** (words per minute) | 10 minutes |
| **Stenographer Grade 'D'** | **80 wpm** (words per minute) | 10 minutes |

A passage of approximately 1000 / 800 words dictated at the above speed. Candidates are allowed a short recovery time before transcription.

### Transcription (on Computer)
| Post | Language | Normal Candidates | Scribe Allowed |
|------|----------|-------------------|----------------|
| **Stenographer Grade 'C'** | English | **40 minutes** | 55 minutes |
| **Stenographer Grade 'C'** | Hindi | **55 minutes** | 75 minutes |
| **Stenographer Grade 'D'** | English | **50 minutes** | 70 minutes |
| **Stenographer Grade 'D'** | Hindi | **65 minutes** | 90 minutes |

Candidates may opt for **English or Hindi** transcription. The skill test is qualifying — no marks are added to the CBE score. Final merit is based on CBE marks only.

### Allowances
- **Dearness Allowance (DA)**: Currently 50% of Basic Pay (revised periodically)
- **House Rent Allowance (HRA)**: 30% (X city) / 20% (Y city) / 10% (Z city) of Basic Pay
- **Transport Allowance (TA)**: ₹3,600 + DA on TA (X city) / ₹1,800 + DA on TA (other places)
- Other benefits: NPS, CGHS / Medical, LTC, Festival Advance, etc.

### Career Growth Path
- Stenographer Grade 'D' (₹2,400 GP) → Stenographer Grade 'C' (₹4,600 GP) → Grade Pay ₹4,200 / ₹4,600 / ₹5,400 / ₹6,600 → up to Principal Staff Officer / Director level (Pay Level-13).

### Job Profile
- Shorthand dictation-taking
- Transcription on computer
- Assisting the posted officer (phone calls, appointments)
- Maintaining records, diaries, and important files
- Note-taking during press conferences / ministerial speeches
