## 1. SSC Head Constable (AWO/TPO) in Delhi Police

### 1.1 Overview

| Parameter | Details |
|---|---|
| Conducting Body | Staff Selection Commission (SSC) |
| Recruiting Force | Delhi Police |
| Posts | Head Constable (Assistant Wireless Operator / AWO) & Head Constable (Tele-Printer Operator / TPO) |
| Department | Delhi Police – Communication / Wireless Wing |
| Pay Level | Level-4 (7th CPC), ₹25,500 – ₹81,100 + DA + allowances |
| Official Website | https://ssc.gov.in / https://delhipolice.gov.in |
| Notification Page | https://ssc.gov.in/api/attachment/uploads/masterData/NoticeBoards/Notice_of_DPHCT_2025.pdf |

### 1.2 Vacancies (2025 Cycle – Referenced)

| Category | Male | Female | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| General | 158 | 78 | 236 |
| EWS | 37 | 18 | 55 |
| OBC | 94 | 47 | 141 |
| SC | 48 | 23 | 71 |
| ST | 33 | 16 | 49 |
| **Total** | **370** | **182** | **552** |

### 1.3 Eligibility

**Educational Qualification** (any one):
- 10+2 (Senior Secondary) passed with **Science & Mathematics** as subjects from a recognized Board, **OR**
- **NTC (National Trade Certificate)** in Mechanic-Cum-Operator Electronic Communication System from a recognized institute.

**Age Limit (as on 01/07/2025)**:
- Minimum: 18 years
- Maximum: 27 years
- Age relaxation as per Government rules (SC/ST +5 yrs, OBC +3 yrs, Ex-Servicemen, etc.)

**Gender**: Both Male and Female

**Physical Standards**:

| Standard | Male | Female |
|---|---|---|
| Height | 170 cm | 157 cm |
| Chest (Male only) | 81 cm (unexpanded) – 85 cm (expanded) | N/A |
| Eye sight | 6/6 (corrected) | 6/6 (corrected) |

**Note**: Must be a citizen of India.

### 1.4 Selection Process

1. **Computer Based Examination (CBE)** – 100 marks (screening)
2. **Physical Endurance & Measurement Test (PE&MT)** – qualifying
3. **English Typing Skill Test on Computer** – 1000 key depressions in 15 minutes (qualifying)
4. **Document Verification & Medical Examination**

### 1.5 Exam Pattern (CBE)

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Mode | Online (Computer Based) |
| Type | Objective MCQ |
| Total Questions | 100 |
| Total Marks | 100 |
| Duration | 90 minutes (1.5 hours) |
| Negative Marking | **0.33 marks** deducted per wrong answer |
| Medium | English & Hindi |
| Sections | 5 (General Awareness, Reasoning, Quantitative Aptitude, English, Computer Fundamentals) |

**Section-wise Distribution** (approximate):

| Section | Questions | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| General Awareness | 25 | 25 |
| Reasoning | 25 | 25 |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 25 | 25 |
| English Comprehension | 25 | 25 |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** |

*Note: Section-wise weightage may vary; refer to official notification for latest cycle.*

### 1.6 Detailed Syllabus

#### A) General Awareness (25 Qs)
- Indian History (Ancient, Medieval, Modern – Freedom Struggle)
- Indian Polity & Constitution
- Indian Geography (Physical, Economic, Social)
- Indian Economy & Budget basics
- General Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology – 10th/12th level)
- Static GK – Awards, Books, Capitals, First events
- Current Affairs (national & international)
- Defence & Police-related GK
- Science & Technology developments

#### B) General Intelligence & Reasoning (25 Qs)
- Analogies, Similarities & Differences
- Space visualization, Spatial orientation
- Visual memory, Discrimination
- Observation, Relationship concepts
- Arithmetical reasoning, Figural classification
- Arithmetic number series, Non-verbal series
- Coding-Decoding
- Syllogism, Venn diagrams
- Blood Relations, Directions, Ranking
- Seating Arrangements, Puzzles
- Input-Output, Statement-Conclusion

#### C) Quantitative Aptitude (25 Qs)
- Number System, Simplification
- HCF, LCM, Percentage, Average
- Ratio & Proportion, Partnership
- Profit & Loss, Discount
- Simple & Compound Interest
- Time & Work, Time & Distance
- Mensuration (2D & 3D)
- Algebra (basic identities, linear equations)
- Geometry (Triangles, Circles, Quadrilaterals)
- Trigonometry (basic identities, height & distance)
- Data Interpretation (Tables, Bar graphs, Pie charts, Line graphs)

#### D) English Comprehension (25 Qs)
- Reading comprehension passages
- Vocabulary – Synonyms, Antonyms, One-word substitution
- Grammar – Spotting errors, Sentence correction
- Sentence completion, Fill in the blanks
- Idioms & Phrases
- Active/Passive voice, Direct/Indirect speech
- Cloze test
- Para jumbles
- Prepositions, Articles, Tenses

#### E) Computer Fundamentals (Included within sections)
- History & generations of computers
- Hardware, Software, Operating Systems
- MS Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint)
- Internet, Networking basics
- Database fundamentals
- Cyber security basics
- Shortcut keys
- Input/output devices

### 1.7 Skill Test (English Typing on Computer)

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Speed | 1000 key depressions |
| Duration | 15 minutes |
| Mode | On computer (qualifying only) |
| Language | English |
| Note | Marks are not counted for merit; failure disqualifies the candidate |

|---|---|---|
| Race | 1600 m in 7½ min | 800 m in 5 min |
| Long Jump | 12 ft (3 chances) | 9 ft (3 chances) |

### 1.9 Salary & Career

- **Basic Pay**: ₹25,500 (Level-4)
- **Gross Salary (approx.)**: ₹38,000 – ₹45,000/month (including DA, HRA, transport)
- **Job Profile**: Operate wireless / teleprinter communication equipment for Delhi Police; maintain radio networks.
- **Career Progression**: Head Constable → Assistant Sub-Inspector → Sub-Inspector → Inspector

### 1.10 Official Links

## 2. SSC Scientific Assistant (IMD) – India Meteorological Department

### 2.1 Overview

| Parameter | Details |
|---|---|
| Conducting Body | Staff Selection Commission (SSC) |
| Recruiting Department | India Meteorological Department (IMD), Ministry of Earth Sciences |
| Post | Scientific Assistant (Group 'B', Non-Gazetted) |
| Pay Level | Level-6 (7th CPC), ₹35,400 – ₹1,12,400 |
| Headquarters | Mausam Bhavan, Lodi Road, New Delhi |
| Official Websites | https://ssc.gov.in / https://mausam.imd.gov.in |

### 2.2 About IMD
Established 15 January 1875, IMD is the principal agency responsible for meteorological observations, weather forecasting, and seismology in India. Operates under the Ministry of Earth Sciences. One of six Regional Specialised Meteorological Centres of WMO for the Indian Ocean region.

### 2.3 Eligibility

**Educational Qualification** (as on the cut-off date):
- Bachelor's Degree in Science (B.Sc.) with **Physics** as one of the subjects **AND**
- **Computer Science / Information Technology / Electronics** as one of the subjects, **OR**
- Bachelor's Degree with **Physics, Mathematics/Statistics, and Electronics** as subjects, **OR**
- Diploma in Electronics & Telecommunication / Electronics & Communication Engineering from a recognized institution.

**Age Limit**:
- Minimum: 18 years
- Maximum: 30 years (relaxation for reserved categories as per Govt. rules)

### 2.4 Selection Process
1. **Computer Based Examination (CBE)** – 200 Qs, 200 marks
2. **Document Verification**
(No interview, no skill test)

### 2.5 Exam Pattern (CBE)

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Mode | Online (Computer Based) |
| Type | Objective MCQ |
| Total Questions | 200 |
| Total Marks | 200 |
| Duration | 2 hours (120 minutes) |
| Negative Marking | 0.25 marks deducted per wrong answer |
| Medium | English & Hindi |

**Section-wise Distribution**:

| Section | Questions | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| General Intelligence & Reasoning | 25 | 25 |
| General Awareness | 25 | 25 |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 25 | 25 |
| English Comprehension | 25 | 25 |
| Computer Knowledge | 25 | 25 |
| Technical Subject (Physics/Electronics/CS) | 75 | 75 |
| **Total** | **200** | **200** |

### 2.6 Detailed Syllabus

#### A) General Intelligence & Reasoning
- Analogies, Similarities, Differences
- Spatial visualization, Orientation
- Visual memory, Discrimination
- Relationship concepts, Arithmetical reasoning
- Verbal & non-verbal series
- Coding-Decoding
- Syllogism, Venn diagrams
- Puzzles, Seating arrangements
- Blood relations, Directions

#### B) General Awareness
- Indian History, Polity, Geography
- Economics, General Science
- Current Affairs – National & International
- Meteorological / Scientific developments
- Awards, Books, Capitals
- Government schemes related to earth sciences

#### C) Quantitative Aptitude
- Number System, HCF, LCM
- Percentage, Average, Ratio & Proportion
- Profit-Loss, Discount, SI/CI
- Time & Work, Time-Speed-Distance
- Algebra, Geometry, Trigonometry
- Mensuration
- Data Interpretation (Table, Chart, Graph)

#### D) English Comprehension
- Reading comprehension
- Vocabulary (Synonyms, Antonyms)
- Grammar (Error spotting, Sentence correction)
- Fill in the blanks
- Idioms & Phrases
- One-word substitution
- Active/Passive, Direct/Indirect

#### E) Computer Knowledge
- Computer fundamentals, generations
- Hardware, Software, OS (Windows, Linux)
- MS Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint)
- Internet, Networking, Protocols
- DBMS basics
- Cyber security concepts
- Programming basics (logic only)

#### F) Technical Subject (Physics / Electronics / Computer Science / IT) – 75 Qs
The technical portion is degree-level and includes:

**Physics (Core)**:
- Mechanics, Optics, Waves
- Thermodynamics, Heat
- Electricity, Magnetism
- Modern Physics, Nuclear Physics
- Electromagnetic Theory
- Solid State Physics
- Quantum Mechanics basics
- Mathematical Methods in Physics

**Electronics & Telecommunication**:
- Electronic Devices & Circuits (Diodes, Transistors, Amplifiers)
- Digital Electronics (Logic gates, Boolean algebra, Flip-flops)
- Signals & Systems
- Communication Systems (AM, FM, Modulation)
- Microprocessors & Microcontrollers
- Network Theory
- Control Systems basics
- Electromagnetic Field Theory

**Computer Science / IT**:
- Data Structures (Arrays, Linked Lists, Trees, Graphs)
- Algorithms (Sorting, Searching, Complexity)
- Operating Systems (Process, Memory, File management)
- Database Management Systems (SQL, Normalization)
- Computer Networks (OSI, TCP/IP)
- Object-Oriented Programming (C++/Java)
- Software Engineering basics
- Web Technologies basics

*Note: The exact subject weightage depends on the candidate's qualifying degree; SSC allows candidates to attempt questions from the relevant domain.*

### 2.7 Salary & Career

- **Basic Pay**: ₹35,400 (Level-6)
- **Gross Salary (approx.)**: ₹50,000 – ₹58,000/month
- **Job Profile**: Meteorological observations, weather data collection, instrument maintenance, analysis & forecasting support
- **Career Path**: Scientific Assistant → Met. Grade-II → Met. Grade-I → Assistant Meteorologist → Meteorologist

### 2.8 Official Links
- SSC: https://ssc.gov.in
- IMD: https://mausam.imd.gov.in
- Ministry of Earth Sciences: https://moes.gov.in

### 3.2 About NCB
Established 17 March 1986, the Narcotics Control Bureau is the Indian federal law enforcement and intelligence agency tasked with combating drug trafficking and illegal substance use under the NDPS Act, 1985. Operates under the Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India.

### 3.3 Eligibility

**Educational Qualification**:
- Bachelor's Degree (Graduation) from a recognized University or equivalent.

**Age Limit**:
- Minimum: 18 years
- Maximum: 30 years
- Relaxation as per Government rules.

### 3.4 Selection Process
1. **Paper-I**: Computer Based Examination (CBE) – 200 marks
2. **Paper-II**: Descriptive English Paper – 200 marks
3. **Physical Endurance Test (PET) / Physical Standard Test (PST)** – qualifying
4. **Document Verification & Medical Examination**

- Puzzles, Seating arrangement

- Decision making, Problem solving

#### B) General Awareness
- Indian History (Ancient, Medieval, Modern)
- Indian Polity, Constitution, Governance
- Indian Geography, Environment
- Indian Economy, Planning, Budget
- General Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
- Static GK – Books, Awards, Capitals, First events
- Current Affairs – National & International
- Indian Laws (especially Criminal Law, NDPS Act, IPC)

- International Organizations (UN, WCO, INTERPOL, UNODC)

#### C) Quantitative Aptitude
- Number System, Simplification, Approximation
- HCF, LCM, Percentage, Average

## 8. Quick Strategy Tips

### For HC AWO/TPO
- Focus on **Computer Fundamentals** (rare topic) – read Standard General Knowledge CompendiumComputer chapter
- Practice **typing** on computer (online typing tutors) – 1000 keys in 15 min
- PET preparation: Daily 1600m run + Long jump + High jump practice
- Speed matters – 100 Qs in 90 min means <1 min per question

### For Scientific Assistant (IMD)
- Strong grasp on **B.Sc. Physics & Electronics fundamentals**
- Practice numericals in Physics, especially Electricity, Magnetism, Optics
- 200 Qs in 2 hrs = 36 sec/Q – speed + accuracy essential
- Cover **NDPS Act**, meteorological terms, atmospheric science basics in GK

### For NCB SI
- Focus on **English Descriptive** (200 marks Paper-II is decisive)
- Study **NDPS Act 1985, IPC, CrPC** for General Awareness
- Strong GK on Law Enforcement, International drug conventions (UN, WCO, INCB)
- Daily 1 essay + 1 letter practice for Paper-II
