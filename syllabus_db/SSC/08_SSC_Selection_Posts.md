# SSC Selection Posts (Phase-XII) Examination

## 1. Overview

| Particular | Details |
|---|---|
| **Full Name** | Staff Selection Commission Selection Posts Examination |
| **Conducting Body** | Staff Selection Commission (SSC), Government of India |
| **Established** | 4 November 1975 (as Subordinate Services Commission; renamed SSC on 26 September 1977) |
| **Headquarters** | Block No. 12, Lodhi Road, CGO Complex, Lodhi Colony, New Delhi - 110003 |
| **Frequency** | Annually (conducted in Phases) |
| **Current Phase** | Phase-XII (2024-25 cycle) — Subsequent phases are Phase-XIII/XIV |
| **Total Vacancies (Phase XII approx.)** | 3,049+ vacancies (varies by phase) |
| **Exam Mode** | Computer-Based Examination (CBE) |
| **Official Website** | https://ssc.gov.in/ |
| **Parent Organization** | Department of Personnel and Training (DoPT), Ministry of Personnel, Public Grievances and Pensions |
| **Purpose** | Recruitment to Group 'B' (Non-Gazetted) and Group 'C' (Non-Technical) posts under various Ministries/Departments of Govt. of India |

## 3. Eligibility

### Educational Qualification (as per post)

| Level | Required Qualification |
|---|---|
| **Matriculation Posts** | Passed 10th (Matriculation) from a recognized Board |
| **Higher Secondary Posts** | Passed 12th (Higher Secondary) from a recognized Board |
| **Graduation & Above Posts** | Bachelor's Degree from a recognized University; some posts require specific disciplines (e.g., Statistics for Statistical Investigator, Engineering for JE) |

### Age Limit

| Category | Age Range |
|---|---|
| **General / EWS** | 18 to 30 years (varies by post; some posts allow 18-27, 20-30, 18-25) |
| **SC / ST** | 5 years relaxation (i.e., up to 35 years) |
| **OBC (Non-Creamy Layer)** | 3 years relaxation (i.e., up to 33 years) |
| **PwD (Persons with Disabilities)** | 10 to 15 years relaxation |
| **Ex-Servicemen** | Up to 3 years after deduction of military service |
| **Departmental Candidates (Central Govt.)** | Up to 40 years (for some posts) |

> **Note:** Crucial date for age reckoning is as mentioned in the official notification. Some posts like Inspector of Income Tax have a higher upper age limit (up to 30 years) while others like JE have different ranges.

### Nationality
- Citizen of India, OR
- Subject of Nepal / Bhutan, OR
- Tibetan refugee (with intent of permanent settlement in India), OR
- Person of Indian Origin (PIO) migrated from Pakistan, Burma, Sri Lanka, East African countries, etc.

## 4. Exam Pattern

### Computer-Based Examination (CBE)

| Part | Subject | No. of Questions | Max Marks | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | General Intelligence & Reasoning | 25 | 50 | 15 Minutes |
| **B** | General Awareness | 25 | 50 | 15 Minutes |
| **C** | Quantitative Aptitude (Basic Arithmetic Skill) | 25 | 50 | 15 Minutes |
| **D** | English Language (Basic Comprehension) | 25 | 50 | 15 Minutes |
| | **Total** | **100** | **200** | **60 Minutes** |

**Key Points:**
- All questions are **objective-type (MCQ)** with four options.
- Exam conducted in **Hindi and English** only (except English Language section).
- **Scribe candidates** get an additional **20 minutes** (total 80 minutes).
- The exam pattern is **same for all three levels** (Matric, 12th, Graduation) but **difficulty level varies** based on the qualification required for the post.
- There is **negative marking** of **0.50 marks** for every wrong answer.
- Each correct answer carries **2 marks**.

### Skill Test (Qualifying in Nature)
- Applicable for specific posts (e.g., JSA, Stenographer, LDC) where typing / shorthand is essential.
- Conducted as per post requirement (Hindi/English typing, Stenography).
- Skill test marks are **not added** to the final merit; it is qualifying only.

### Document Verification
- Candidates qualifying in CBE (and Skill Test, where applicable) are called for **Document Verification (DV)**.
- Original documents checked: Educational certificates, caste certificate, ID proof, NOC (for govt. employees), etc.

## 5. Detailed Syllabus (Topic-Wise)

### A. General Intelligence & Reasoning

**Matriculation Level:**
- Non-verbal type questions
- Similarities and differences
- Space visualization
- Problem-solving and analysis
- Judgment, decision making
- Visual memory
- Discriminating observation
- Relationship concepts
- Figure classification
- Arithmetical number series
- Non-verbal series

**Higher Secondary Level (12th):**
- Both verbal and non-verbal questions
- Semantic Analogy, Symbolic operations, Symbolic/Number Analogy
- Trend, Figural Analogy, Space orientation
- Semantic classification, Venn Diagram, Symbolic/Number Classification
- Drawing inferences, Figural Classification
- Semantic Series, Figural Pattern – folding and completion
- Number Series, Embedded figures, Figural Series
- Critical Thinking, Problem Solving
- Emotional Intelligence, Word Building
- Social Intelligence, Coding and Decoding

**Graduation & Above Level:**
- Analogies, Space visualization, Spatial orientation
- Judgment and decision-making
- Visual memory, Discrimination and Observation
- Relationship concepts
- Arithmetical reasoning and figural classification
- Arithmetic number series, Non-verbal series
- Coding and decoding, Statement conclusion
- Syllogistic reasoning
- Semantic Analogy, Symbolic/Number Analogy, Figural Analogy
- Semantic Classification, Symbolic/Number Classification, Figural Classification
- Semantic Series, Number Series, Figural Series
- Problem-Solving, Word Building
- Numerical Operations, Symbolic Operations
- Trends, Space Orientation and Visualisation
- Venn Diagrams, Drawing inferences
- Punched hole/pattern – folding & unfolding
- Figural Pattern – folding and completion
- Indexing, Address matching, Date & city matching
- Classification of centre codes/roll numbers
- Small & Capital letters/numbers coding, decoding
- Embedded Figures, Critical thinking
- Emotional Intelligence, Social Intelligence

### B. General Awareness

Topics common across all levels:
- **History & Culture** – Indian History, Freedom Struggle, World History
- **Geography** – Indian and World Geography, Physical, Social, Economic
- **Economic Scene** – Indian Economy, Budget, Five Year Plans, Economic Terminology
- **General Polity** – Indian Political System, Governance
- **Indian Constitution** – Fundamental Rights, Duties, Constitutional Bodies
- **Scientific Research** – General Science, Inventions, Discoveries
- **Awards & Honours** – National and International (Nobel, Bharat Ratna, etc.)
- **Sports** – Tournaments, Players, Records
- **Current Events** – National & International importance
- **General awareness of the environment** and its application to society
- **Books & Authors**
- **Important Days** and their significance

### C. Quantitative Aptitude

**Matriculation Level:**
- Number Systems
- Decimals and Fractions and Relationship Between Numbers
- Fundamental arithmetical operations
- Computation of Whole Numbers
- Averages, Interest (Simple), Percentages
- Ratio and Proportion
- Profit and Loss, Discount
- Mensuration
- Time and Distance, Ratio and Time, Time and Work
- Use of Tables and Graphs

**Higher Secondary Level (12th):**
- Arithmetic, Number Systems, Computation of Whole Numbers
- Decimal and Fractions, Relationship between numbers
- Fundamental arithmetical operations
- Percentages, Ratio and Proportion, Square roots
- Averages, Interest (Simple and Compound)
- Profit and Loss, Discount, Partnership Business
- Mixture and Allegation
- Time and Distance, Time and Work
- **Algebra:** Basic algebraic identities of School Algebra and Elementary surds (simple problems) and Graphs of Linear Equations
- **Geometry:** Triangle and its various kinds of centres; Congruence and similarity of triangles; Circle and its chords, tangents, angles subtended by chords; common tangents to two or more circles
- **Mensuration:** Triangle, Quadrilaterals, Regular Polygons, Circle, Right Prism, Right Circular Cone, Right Circular Cylinder, Sphere, Hemispheres, Rectangular Parallelepiped, Regular Right Pyramid with triangular or square Base
- **Trigonometry:** Trigonometric ratios, Complementary angles, Height and distances (simple problems), Standard Identities
- **Statistical Charts:** Use of Tables and Graphs, Histogram, Frequency polygon, Bar-diagram, Pie-chart

**Graduation & Above Level:**
- All topics of 12th level plus:
- Degree and Radian Measures
- Advanced algebraic identities
- All geometric concepts in greater depth
- Heights and Distances (advanced problems)

### D. English Language

**Matriculation Level:**
- Basics of English Language
- Vocabulary, Grammar
- Synonyms
- Sentence Structure
- Antonyms and their correct usage

**Higher Secondary Level (12th):**
- Spot the Error
- Fill in the Blanks
- Synonyms / Homonyms
- Antonyms
- Spellings / Detecting Misspelt words
- Idioms & Phrases
- One-word substitution
- Improvement of Sentences
- Active / Passive Voice of Verbs
- Conversion into Direct / Indirect narration
- Shuffling of Sentence parts
- Shuffling of Sentences in a passage
- Cloze Passage
- Comprehension Passage

**Graduation & Above Level:**
- Tests proficiency in correct English, basic comprehension and writing ability
- Same as 12th level topics with **higher difficulty** (graduation-level standard)

## 7. Marking Scheme

| Component | Marks |
|---|---|
| **Correct Answer** | **+2 marks** |
| **Incorrect Answer** | **-0.50 marks** (negative marking) |
| **Unattempted** | 0 marks (no penalty) |
| **Total Marks** | 200 (100 questions × 2) |
| **Normalized Marks** | Yes — normalization is applied if exam is held in multiple shifts |

### Important Notes on Marking:
- Negative marking applies to MCQs only.
- For every **3 wrong answers, you lose 1.5 marks** (3 × 0.5).
- **Multiple responses** for the same question are treated as **wrong** and penalized.
- No marks are deducted for questions left un-attempted.

- Dearness Allowance (DA) – revised twice a year

- Travel Allowance (TA)
- Leave Encashment
- LTC, Festival Advance, and other Central Govt. benefits

### Probation Period:
- **2 years** from the date of joining for most posts.
- Performance during probation determines future career progression.
