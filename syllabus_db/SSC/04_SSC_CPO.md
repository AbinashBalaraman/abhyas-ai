# SSC CPO (Staff Selection Commission - Central Police Organisation) Examination

## 1. Overview

- **Full Name**: Staff Selection Commission - Central Police Organisation (Sub-Inspector) Examination
- **Conducting Body**: Staff Selection Commission (SSC), Government of India
- **Frequency**: Annually (once a year)
- **Purpose**: Recruitment of Sub-Inspectors in CAPFs and Delhi Police
- **Examination Level**: National
- **Mode of Exam**: Computer Based Test (CBT - Online) + Physical Tests
- **Official Website**: https://ssc.gov.in/
- **Parent Organization**: Department of Personnel and Training (DoPT), Ministry of Personnel, Public Grievances and Pensions
- **Headquarters**: Block No. 12, Lodhi Road, CGO Complex, Lodhi Colony, New Delhi - 110003

---

## 2. Posts Offered

The SSC CPO recruits Sub-Inspectors (SI) to the following posts:

### Central Armed Police Forces (CAPF)
1. **Border Security Force (BSF)** - Sub-Inspector
2. **Central Industrial Security Force (CISF)** - Sub-Inspector
3. **Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF)** - Sub-Inspector
4. **Indo-Tibetan Border Police (ITBP)** - Sub-Inspector
5. **Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB)** - Sub-Inspector

### Other Organisations
6. **Sashastra Seema Bal (SSF)** - Sub-Inspector
7. **Delhi Police** - Sub-Inspector (Executive)
8. **Delhi Police** - Sub-Inspector (Male only)

---

## 3. Eligibility

### Age Limit
- **Minimum Age**: 20 years
- **Maximum Age**: 25 years
- **Age Relaxation**: As per Government of India norms for reserved categories (SC/ST - 5 years, OBC - 3 years, etc.)

### Educational Qualification
- **Bachelor's Degree** (Graduation) from a recognized university or equivalent
- For Delhi Police SI: Graduation in any discipline
- For CAPF SI: Graduation in any discipline

### Physical Standards
- Must meet prescribed Physical Standard Test (PST) requirements
- Both Male and Female candidates must satisfy height, chest, and weight criteria
- Detailed standards given in Section 6 below

### Nationality
- Citizen of India
- Subject of Nepal/Bhutan (with certificate of eligibility)
- Tibetan refugee who permanently settled in India before January 1, 1962

## 4. Exam Pattern

The SSC CPO selection process has 3 stages:

### Stage 1: Paper-I (Computer Based Examination)
- **Mode**: Online (CBT)
- **Duration**: 2 hours (120 minutes)
- **Total Questions**: 200 (50 per section)
- **Total Marks**: 200
- **Sectional Timing**: Yes (30 minutes per section - newly introduced)
- **Negative Marking**: 0.25 marks per wrong answer

| Section | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---------|-----------|-------|------|
| General Intelligence & Reasoning | 50 | 50 | 30 min |
| General Knowledge & General Awareness | 50 | 50 | 30 min |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 50 | 50 | 30 min |
| English Comprehension | 50 | 50 | 30 min |
| **Total** | **200** | **200** | **2 Hours** |

### Stage 2: Physical Standard Test (PST) & Physical Efficiency Test (PET)
- Conducted for candidates who qualify Paper-I
- Qualifying in nature (no marks)

### Stage 3: Paper-II (Computer Based Examination)
- **Mode**: Online (CBT)
- **Subject**: English Language & Comprehension
- **Total Questions**: 200
- **Total Marks**: 200
- **Duration**: 2 hours (120 minutes)
- **Negative Marking**: 0.25 marks per wrong answer

### Stage 4: Medical Examination
- Conducted after qualifying Paper-I and Paper-II
- Tests physical fitness including vision standards

### Stage 5: Interview/Personality Test
- **Note**: As per DOPT guidelines, Interview/Personality Test is no longer conducted (marks withdrawn)
- Final selection is based on Paper-I + Paper-II performance

### Qualifying Marks
- General: 30%
- OBC/EWS: 25%
- SC/ST/Other: 20%

## 5. Detailed Syllabus

### A. General Intelligence & Reasoning (Paper-I)
**Verbal Reasoning Topics:**
- Analogy (word, letter, number)
- Series completion (number, letter, alpha-numeric)
- Syllogism
- Coding-Decoding
- Direction Sense Test
- Blood Relations
- Venn Diagrams
- Matrix arrangements
- Pattern completion
- Order and Ranking
- Sitting Arrangement (Circular, Linear, Double Line-up)
- Scheduling
- Input-Output
- Data Sufficiency
- Statement & Conclusion
- Statement & Assumptions
- Statement & Courses of Action
- Decision Making

**Non-Verbal Reasoning Topics:**
- Mirror Images
- Water Images
- Paper Folding/Cutting
- Figure Counting
- Embedded Figures
- Pattern Completion
- Classification
- Cube & Dice

### B. General Knowledge & General Awareness (Paper-I)
- **History**: Indian History (Ancient, Medieval, Modern), World History, Freedom Struggle
- **Polity**: Indian Constitution, Political System, Panchayati Raj, Public Policy, Rights
- **Geography**: Physical, Economic, World Geography, Indian Geography
- **Economics**: Indian Economy, Budget, Five Year Plans, Economic Terms
- **Science**: Physics, Chemistry, Biology (up to 10+2 level)
- **Current Affairs**: National, International, Awards, Books, Sports, Important Days
- Static GK: Awards & Honours, Books & Authors, Scientific Research, Obituaries

### C. Quantitative Aptitude (Paper-I)
- **Number System**: HCF, LCM, Divisibility, Remainders
- **Algebra**: Quadratic Equations, Linear Equations, Polynomials
- **Geometry**: Triangles, Circles, Quadrilaterals, Polygons
- **Trigonometry**: Heights & Distances, Identities
- **Mensuration**: Area, Volume, Surface Area
- **Data Interpretation (DI)**: Bar Graph, Pie Chart, Table, Line Graph
- **Arithmetic**:
  - Percentage
  - Ratio & Proportion
  - Profit & Loss
  - Simple & Compound Interest
  - Time & Work
  - Speed, Distance & Time
  - Problems of Ages
  - Number Series
  - Data Sufficiency
  - Average
  - Mixture & Alligation

### D. English Comprehension (Paper-I)
- Reading Comprehension
- Error Spotting/Correction
- Fill in the Blanks
- Synonyms & Antonyms
- Idioms & Phrases
- One-Word Substitution
- Spelling Correction
- Para Jumbles
- Active/Passive Voice
- Direct/Indirect Speech
- Cloze Test

### E. Paper-II English Language & Comprehension
1. Error Recognition
2. Fill in the Blanks
3. Vocabulary
4. Spellings
5. Grammar
6. Sentence Structure
7. Synonyms
8. Antonyms
9. Sentence Completion
10. Phrases and Idiomatic Use of Words
11. Comprehension (Passages)

## 8. Marking Scheme

### Paper-I
- **Total Questions**: 200 (50 per section × 4 sections)
- **Marks per Question**: 1 mark
- **Total Marks**: 200
- **Negative Marking**: 0.25 marks (1/4) for each wrong answer
- **Duration**: 2 hours (120 minutes) with sectional timing of 30 minutes
- **Normalisation**: SSC applies normalisation formula for score calculation

### Paper-II
- **Total Questions**: 200
- **Marks per Question**: 1 mark
- **Total Marks**: 200
- **Negative Marking**: 0.25 marks (1/4) for each wrong answer
- **Duration**: 2 hours (120 minutes)

### Scoring Formula
- Correct Answer: +1 mark
- Wrong Answer: -0.25 mark
- Unattempted: 0 (no penalty)

### Bilingual Nature
- Questions in Parts I, II, and III of Paper-I will be set in both Hindi and English
- Part IV (English Comprehension) will be in English only

## 9. Salary Structure

### Pay Scale
- **Pay Level**: Level-6 (7th Central Pay Commission)
- **Pay Band**: Rs. 35,400 - Rs. 1,12,400
- **Grade Pay**: Rs. 4,200 (pre-revised)
- **Basic Pay**: Rs. 35,400 (starting)

### In-Hand Salary (Approximate)
- **Starting Basic Pay**: Rs. 35,400
- **Dearness Allowance (DA)**: As applicable (~50% of basic)
- **House Rent Allowance (HRA)**: 8% to 24% of basic (city-dependent)
- **Travel Allowance (TA)**: As per rules
- **Special Duty Allowance**: Applicable for CAPF personnel
- **Gross Monthly Salary**: Rs. 44,000 - Rs. 55,000 (approx.)
- **Net In-Hand Salary**: Rs. 35,000 - Rs. 45,000 (approx.)

### Additional Benefits
- **Perks**: Medical facilities, accommodation, ration allowance
- **Pension**: As per NPS (National Pension System)
- **Promotion Path**: SI → Inspector → Deputy Superintendent → Superintendent
- **Insurance and risk allowances** for CAPF personnel

## Summary

SSC CPO is a national-level competitive examination conducted annually by the Staff Selection Commission to recruit Sub-Inspectors in CAPFs (BSF, CISF, CRPF, ITBP, SSB, SSF) and Delhi Police. The selection process comprises Paper-I (200 marks, 4 sections), Physical Test (PST/PET), Paper-II (200 marks, English), and Medical Examination. Candidates aged 20-25 with a graduation degree are eligible. Successful candidates receive Pay Level-6 salary (Rs. 35,400 - Rs. 1,12,400) with additional allowances and benefits.
