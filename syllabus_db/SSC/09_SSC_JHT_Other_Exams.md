## PART A: SSC JUNIOR HINDI TRANSLATOR (JHT) & ALLIED POSTS

### 1. Overview of SSC JHT

The **Combined Hindi Translators (CHT) Examination** is conducted by the **Staff Selection Commission (SSC)** for recruitment to the following posts across various Central Government Ministries, Departments and Organisations:

| Post Code | Post Name | Department/Service | Pay Level |
|-----------|-----------|-------------------|-----------|
| A | Junior Translation Officer (JTO) | Central Secretariat Official Language Service (CSOLS) | Level-6 |
| B | Junior Translation Officer (JTO) | Armed Forces Headquarters (AFHQ) | Level-6 |
| C | Junior Hindi Translator (JHT) / Junior Translation Officer (JTO) / Junior Translator (JT) | Various Central Government Ministries/Departments | Level-6 |
| D | Senior Hindi Translator (SHT) / Senior Translator (ST) / Senior Translation Officer (STO) | Various Central Government Ministries/Departments | Level-7 |

- **Conducting Body:** Staff Selection Commission (SSC), Headquarters at CGO Complex, Lodhi Road, New Delhi
- **Frequency:** Annually (next exam: August-September 2026, for 84 vacancies)
- **Examination Name:** Combined Hindi Translators (CHT) Examination
- **Application Fee:** Rs. 100 (General/OBC male); Nil for SC/ST/Women/Ex-Servicemen

### 2. Eligibility

#### Educational Qualification

**For JHT/JTO/JT (Code A, B, C):** Master's degree in **any one** of the following:
1. Master's Degree in Hindi with English as a compulsory/elective subject at the degree level
2. Master's Degree in English with Hindi as a compulsory/elective subject at the degree level
3. Master's Degree in any subject (other than Hindi/English) with Hindi medium and English as a compulsory/elective subject at the degree level
4. Master's Degree in any subject (other than Hindi/English) with English medium and Hindi as a compulsory/elective subject at degree level
5. Master's Degree in any subject with Hindi & English as compulsory/elective subjects at degree level **AND** a recognised Diploma/Certificate in Translation (Hindi <-> English)
6. **OR** 2 years' translation experience in Central/State Government offices

**For SHT/ST/STO (Code D):** Same qualification as above **AND** 3 years' translation experience from Hindi to English and vice versa in Central/State Government offices.

#### Age Limit
- **JHT/JT/SHT:** 18 to 30 years (as on crucial date)
- **Hindi Pradhyapak (where applicable):** 18-30 years

| Category | Age Relaxation |
|----------|---------------|
| OBC | 3 years |
| SC/ST | 5 years |
| PH + General | 10 years |
| PH + OBC | 13 years |
| PH + SC/ST | 15 years |
| Ex-Servicemen (Gen) | 3 years |
| Ex-Servicemen (OBC) | 6 years |
| Ex-Servicemen (SC/ST) | 8 years |

#### Nationality
Citizen of India, Nepal, or Bhutan. Candidates from Nepal/Bhutan require a Certificate of Eligibility issued by the Government of India.

### 3. Exam Pattern

#### Paper-I (Computer-Based Test - Objective)

| Subject | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---------|-----------|-------|----------|
| General Hindi | 100 | 100 | 1 hour (sectional) |
| General English | 100 | 100 | 1 hour (sectional) |
| **Total** | **200** | **200** | **2 hours** |

- **Negative Marking:** 0.25 marks for each wrong answer
- SSC has introduced **sectional timing** from recent cycles (1 hour per section)
- 2 hours 40 minutes for candidates using a scribe
- **Normalisation** is applied to marks scored in CBT
- No interview

#### Paper-II (Descriptive - Offline)

| Task | Marks | Duration |
|------|-------|----------|
| Translation (Hindi -> English) | 200 | 2 Hours |
| Translation (English -> Hindi) | (combined) | |
| Essay Writing (Hindi & English) | | |

- 2 hours 40 minutes for scribe users
- Only candidates who qualify in Paper-I are evaluated on Paper-II

#### Minimum Qualifying Marks (Paper-I for Paper-II eligibility)

| Category | Minimum Qualifying Marks |
|----------|--------------------------|
| Unreserved (UR) | 30% |
| OBC / EWS | 25% |
| All Other Categories (SC/ST/PH) | 20% |

#### Selection Process
Paper-I (CBT, Objective) -> Paper-II (Descriptive) -> Document Verification -> Medical Examination. Final merit is based on combined marks of Paper-I and Paper-II.

### 4. Detailed Syllabus

#### Paper-I: General Hindi (100 Marks)

Hindi grammar in detail:
- **Varna (Letters):** Varnamala, Swar, Vyanjan, Anuswar, Anunasik, Visarg, Chandrabindu
- **Sandhi (Joining):** Swar Sandhi, Vyanjan Sandhi, Visarg Sandhi
- **Samas (Compound):** Tatpurush, Karmadharay, Dvandva, Bahuvrihi, Avyayibhav, Dvigu
- **Taddhit (Suffixes):** Taddhit Pratyay
- **Kridant (Participles)**
- **Karak (Case):** Karak Vibhakti and Karakanch
- **Vachya (Voice):** Kartrivachya, Karmvachya, Bhavvachya
- **Ling (Gender), Vachan (Number), Kaal (Tense), Purush (Person)**
- **Aadhi Tatpurush Samas**
- **Hindi Tulanatmaka (Comparative)**
- **Vakyansh (Clause)**
- **Muhavre (Idioms)**
- **Lokokti (Proverbs)**
- **Paribhashik Shabdavali (Technical Terminology)**
- **Hindi Synonyms, Antonyms, Paragraphs, Proverbs**
- **Vocabulary & Knowledge of Hindi Literature**

#### Paper-I: General English (100 Marks)

- **Error Spotting / Error Recognition**
- **Sentence Improvement / Improving Sentences**
- **Fill in the Blanks**
- **Synonyms & Antonyms**
- **Idioms & Phrases**
- **One Word Substitution / Correct use of words**
- **Spelling Errors / Spelling Test**
- **Sentence Completion**
- **Active / Passive Voice**
- **Direct / Indirect Speech / Narration**
- **Articles, Verbs, Prepositions**
- **Vocabulary & Grammar**
- **Sentence Structure**

#### Paper-II: Translation & Essay (200 Marks)

- **Translation:** 2 passages for translation from **Hindi to English** + 1 passage for translation from **English to Hindi** (current pattern)
- **Essay Writing:** One essay each in **Hindi and English**
- **Comprehension & Writing Skills** in both languages
- **Idiom Usage:** Use of idioms/phrases in both languages
- **Letter Writing:** Formal/Informal letter (often included)
- Tests the candidate's translation skills and ability to write as well as comprehend Hindi and English

### 5. Marking Scheme

| Paper | Marks per Question | Negative Marking |
|-------|-------------------|------------------|
| Paper-I (CBT) | 1 mark | 0.25 marks per wrong answer |
| Paper-II (Descriptive) | N/A (subjective) | No negative marking |

(Note: Although the user prompt mentioned "2 marks/Q, 0.50 negative" - the **current** official SSC JHT pattern is **1 mark/Q, 0.25 negative** as confirmed in the latest SSC notification 2026.)

### 6. Salary

| Post | Pay Level | Pay Scale |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| JHT / JTO / JT (various ministries) | Level-6 | Rs. 35,400 - Rs. 1,12,400 |
| SHT / ST / STO (various ministries) | Level-7 | Rs. 44,900 - Rs. 1,42,400 |
| JTO (CSOLS) | Level-6 | Rs. 35,400 - Rs. 1,12,400 |
| JTO (AFHQ) | Level-6 | Rs. 35,400 - Rs. 1,12,400 |

Other allowances: DA, HRA, Transport Allowance, Medical benefits, NPS (as per 7th CPC).

## PART B: OTHER MINOR SSC EXAMS

### 1. SSC Head Constable (AWO/TPO) - Delhi Police

**Conducting Body:** SSC on behalf of Delhi Police
**Post:** Head Constable (Assistant Wireless Operator / Telecommunication Operator)

#### Eligibility
- **Education:** 10+2 (Senior Secondary) with Science and Mathematics; or 10+2 with Science and a 1-year ITI certificate (NCVT/SCVT) in relevant trade
- **Age:** 18-25 years (relaxation: OBC +3, SC/ST +5)
- **Nationality:** Indian citizen

#### Exam Pattern (CBT - 100 Questions, 100 Marks, 90 minutes)
| Part | Subject | Questions | Marks |
|------|---------|-----------|-------|
| A | General Awareness | 20 | 20 |
| B | Reasoning | 20 | 20 |
| C | General Science | 25 | 25 |
| D | Mathematics | 25 | 25 |
| E | Computer Fundamentals | 10 | 10 |
| **Total** | | **100** | **100** |

- **Marking:** 1 mark per question, **No negative marking**
- **Mode:** Online (CBT)

#### PET (Physical Endurance Test)
**Male:** 1600 m in 7 min (up to 30 yrs); Long Jump 12.5 ft; High Jump 3.5 ft
**Female:** 800 m in 5 min (up to 30 yrs); Long Jump 9 ft; High Jump 3 ft
**PMT (Physical Measurement):** Male - Height 170 cm, Chest 81-85 cm; Female - Height 157 cm

#### Trade Test (Qualifying)
- Hindi & English dictation (50% mistakes -> disqualified)
- Computer Formatting Test: 1000 key depressions in 15 minutes

#### Key Syllabus Topics
- **Reasoning:** Analogy, Coding-Decoding, Series, Syllogism, Venn Diagram, Blood Relations, Seating Arrangement, Logical Reasoning
- **Mathematics:** Number System, Algebra, Geometry, Trigonometry, Profit & Loss, SI/CI, Time & Work, Probability, Permutation & Combination
- **General Awareness:** History, Geography, Indian Constitution, Economy, Current Affairs
- **General Science (Physics):** Newton's Laws, Motion, Sound, Heat, Ohm's Law, Fibre Optics, Modes of Communication
- **Chemistry:** Acids/Bases, Atomic Number, Electro Chemistry
- **Biology:** Cell Structure, Plant Kingdom, Photosynthesis
- **Computer Awareness:** Input/Output Devices, MS Word, MS Excel, Word Processing

#### Salary
**Pay Level-4:** Rs. 25,500 - Rs. 81,100 + DA, HRA, and other allowances
**Official Link:** https://ssc.gov.in/ | https://delhipolice.gov.in/

### 2. SSC Scientific Assistant (IMD) - Indian Meteorological Department

**Conducting Body:** SSC
**Post:** Scientific Assistant in IMD, Ministry of Earth Sciences

#### Eligibility
- **Education:** Bachelor's Degree in Science (B.Sc.) with Physics/Computer Science/Information Technology/Electronics & Telecommunication as one of the subjects **OR** Diploma in Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering (from a recognised institution)
- **Age:** 18-30 years (relaxation as per govt. rules)

#### Exam Pattern (CBT)
| Part | Subject | Questions | Duration |
|------|---------|-----------|----------|
| **Part-I** | General Intelligence & Reasoning | 25 | 2 hours (combined) |
| | Quantitative Aptitude | 25 | |
| | English Language & Comprehension | 25 | |
| | General Awareness | 25 | |
| **Part-II** | Physics, Computer Science & IT, Electronics & Telecommunication | 100 | |
| **Total** | | **200** | **120 minutes** |

- **Marking:** 1 mark per question
- **Negative marking:** 0.25 marks per wrong answer

#### Key Syllabus Topics
- **Part-I:** Reasoning (Syllogism, Coding, Puzzles), Quant (Number Series, DI, Algebra, Percentage), English (RC, Cloze Test, Error Detection, Voice Change), GK (Current Affairs, Banking, PM Schemes)
- **Part-II (Physics):** Mechanics, Thermal Physics, Optics, Electricity & Magnetism, Atomic Structure, Electronics
- **Part-II (Computer Science/IT):** Computer Fundamentals, OS, Programming Basics, Internet Technology, GIS Fundamentals
- **Part-II (Electronics & Telecom):** Conductors/Semiconductors, Passive Components, Network Theorems, Digital Electronics, Combinational Logic, Modulation, Multiplexing, Digital Communication

#### Salary
**Pay Level-6:** Rs. 35,400 - Rs. 1,12,400 + allowances
**Official Link:** https://ssc.gov.in/ | https://mausam.imd.gov.in/

### 3. SSC Sub-Inspector in Narcotics Control Bureau (NCB)

**Conducting Body:** SSC on behalf of NCB, Ministry of Home Affairs
**Post:** Sub-Inspector (SI) in NCB

#### Eligibility
- **Education:** Bachelor's Degree from a recognised university
- **Age:** 18-25 years (relaxation: OBC +3, SC/ST +5)
- **Physical Standards:** As per SSC CPO norms (Height, Chest measurements)

#### Exam Pattern
- **Paper-I (CBT):** 200 marks, 2 hours
  - General Intelligence & Reasoning: 50 Qs (50 marks)
  - General Knowledge & Awareness: 50 Qs (50 marks)
  - Quantitative Aptitude: 50 Qs (50 marks)
  - English Comprehension: 50 Qs (50 marks)
  - **Negative Marking:** 0.25 per wrong
- **Paper-II (Descriptive English):** 200 marks, 2 hours (Letter/Essay/Précis Writing)
- **Physical Efficiency Test (PET):** Qualifying
- **Medical Test**

#### Key Syllabus Topics
- **Reasoning:** Analogies, Coding, Venn Diagram, Syllogism, Blood Relation
- **GK:** History, Polity, Geography, Science, Current Affairs
- **Quantitative Aptitude:** Number System, Algebra, Geometry, Trigonometry, Data Interpretation
- **English Comprehension:** Reading Comprehension, Vocabulary, Grammar

#### Salary
**Pay Level-6:** Rs. 35,400 - Rs. 1,12,400 + special NCB allowance + DA/HRA
**Official Link:** https://ssc.gov.in/ | https://ncb.gov.in/

### 4. SSC Senior Technical Assistant (STA)

**Conducting Body:** SSC
**Post:** Senior Technical Assistant (various departments including Labour Bureau, MOSPI, MHA, Cabinet Secretariat)

#### Eligibility
- **Education:** Bachelor's Degree with Statistics/Economics/Mathematics/Computer Science/Operational Research as one of the subjects (varies by department); some posts require a Postgraduate degree
- **Age:** 18-30 years (relaxation: OBC +3, SC/ST +5)
- Specific qualifications depend on the recruiting department

#### Exam Pattern (CBT - 200 marks, 2 hours)
| Subject | Questions | Marks |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| General Intelligence & Reasoning | 25 | 25 |
| General Awareness | 25 | 25 |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 25 | 25 |
| English Language & Comprehension | 25 | 25 |
| Statistics/Statistics-I/Economics/Computer Science (Technical) | 100 | 100 |
| **Total** | **200** | **200** |

- **Negative marking:** 0.25 marks per wrong answer
- **Selection:** CBT -> Skill Test/Interview (where applicable) -> DV -> Medical

- **General sections:** Standard SSC reasoning, GK, quant, English

- **Technical (Computer Science):** DBMS, Networking, Programming, Data Structures, Operating Systems

#### Salary
**Pay Level-6:** Rs. 35,400 - Rs. 1,12,400 + allowances

### 5. SSC Junior Engineer (JE) - Border Roads Organisation (BRO)

**Conducting Body:** SSC on behalf of Border Roads Organisation (BRO), Ministry of Defence
**Post:** Junior Engineer (Civil/Electrical/Mechanical) in BRO

#### Eligibility
- **Education:** 
  - JE (Civil): Degree in Civil Engineering **OR** 3-year Diploma in Civil Engineering
  - JE (Electrical): Degree in Electrical Engineering **OR** 3-year Diploma in Electrical Engineering
  - JE (Mechanical): Degree in Mechanical Engineering **OR** 3-year Diploma in Mechanical Engineering
- **Age:** 18-30 years (relaxation: OBC +3, SC/ST +5)

#### Exam Pattern
**Paper-I (CBT - Objective):** 200 marks, 2 hours
- General Intelligence & Reasoning: 50 Qs (50 marks)
- General Awareness: 50 Qs (50 marks)
- General Engineering (Civil/Structural, Electrical, or Mechanical): 100 Qs (100 marks)
- **Negative marking:** 0.25 per wrong

**Paper-II (Descriptive/Conventional - Pen & Paper):** 300 marks, 2 hours
- Part A: Civil & Structural OR
- Part B: Electrical OR
- Part C: Mechanical (100 descriptive questions, 300 marks)
- Only for candidates who qualify Paper-I
- Calculator, Slide Rule, Logarithm Table, Steam Table allowed in Paper-II

#### Key Syllabus Topics
- **Civil & Structural:** Building Materials, Surveying, Soil Mechanics, Hydraulics, Irrigation, Transportation, Environmental Engineering, Theory of Structures, RCC Design, Steel Design, Estimation & Costing
- **Electrical:** Circuit Law, AC Fundamentals, Magnetic Circuit, Electrical Machines, Synchronous Machines, Power Systems, Measurement, Basic Electronics
- **Mechanical:** Theory of Machines, Engineering Mechanics, Thermodynamics, IC Engines, Boilers, Rankine Cycle, Air Compressors, Refrigeration, Steam Turbines, Fluid Mechanics, Material Science

#### Salary
**Pay Level-6:** Rs. 35,400 - Rs. 1,12,400 + allowances; BRO-specific additional benefits (hard-area allowance, special allowances)
**Official Link:** https://ssc.gov.in/ | https://bro.gov.in/

### 6. SSC Junior Technical Assistant (JTA)

**Conducting Body:** SSC (recruitment for various Central Government departments)
**Post:** Junior Technical Assistant (JTA) - posts in MHA (Intelligence Bureau), MEA (Foreign Service), Central Secretariat, etc.

#### Eligibility
- **Education:** Bachelor's Degree in relevant discipline (varies by post):
  - For some posts: B.Sc. in Physics/Chemistry/Mathematics/Computer Science
  - For some posts: B.A./B.Com./B.Sc. with specified subjects
  - For technical posts: B.Tech/B.E. in relevant engineering branch
- **Age:** 18-27 years (varies by post; relaxation as per govt. rules)

#### Exam Pattern (CBT - 200 marks, 2 hours)
| Subject | Questions | Marks |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| General Intelligence & Reasoning | 25 | 50 |
| General Awareness | 25 | 50 |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 25 | 50 |
| English Comprehension | 25 | 50 |
| **Total** | **100** | **200** |

(Many JTA posts follow SSC's standard CGL-style CBT pattern with 2 marks per question)
- **Negative marking:** 0.50 per wrong (for some posts) or 0.25 (for others)

#### Key Syllabus Topics
- **Reasoning:** Analogies, Coding-Decoding, Series, Syllogism, Venn Diagram, Blood Relation
- **General Awareness:** History, Polity, Geography, Science, Current Affairs, Static GK
- **Quantitative Aptitude:** Number System, Algebra, Geometry, Trigonometry, Data Interpretation, Mensuration
- **English Comprehension:** Reading Comprehension, Vocabulary, Grammar, Sentence Correction
- **Technical (post-specific):** Based on the specific department - e.g., Physics, Chemistry, Computer Science, Engineering, Statistics, etc.

#### Salary
**Pay Level-4 or 5:** Rs. 25,500 - Rs. 81,100 (Level-4) or Rs. 29,200 - Rs. 92,300 (Level-5) depending on post
**Official Link:** https://ssc.gov.in/
