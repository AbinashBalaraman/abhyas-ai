## 1. Exam Overview

| Particular | Details |
|---|---|
| **Full Name** | Staff Selection Commission - Combined Graduate Level Examination (SSC CGL / CGLE) |
| **Conducting Body** | Staff Selection Commission (SSC), under Department of Personnel and Training (DoPT), Ministry of Personnel, Public Grievances and Pensions, Government of India |
| **Purpose** | Recruitment of Group "B" (Gazetted/Non-Gazetted) and Group "C" (Non-Technical) officers/posts in various Central Government Ministries, Departments, Attached & Subordinate Offices, and Statutory bodies. |
| **Year Started** | 1975 (50+ years of legacy) |
| **Frequency** | **Annual** (single cycle per year) |
| **Mode of Exam** | Online (Computer-Based Test - CBT) |
| **Medium** | English and Hindi |
| **Application Fee** | Rs.100 (Gen/OBC); Exempted for Women, SC, ST, PwBD, ESM |
| **Annual Test Takers** | ~30-38 lakh applicants; ~14-15 lakh appear for Tier 1 |
| **Official Website** | https://ssc.gov.in/ |
| **Headquarters** | Block No. 12, CGO Complex, Lodhi Road, New Delhi - 110003 |

## 3. Eligibility

### 3.1 Nationality
Candidate must be:
- A citizen of **India**, **Nepal**, or **Bhutan**; OR
- A **Tibetan refugee** who came to India before 1 January 1962 with intention of permanent settlement; OR
- A **person of Indian origin** migrated from Pakistan, Burma, Sri Lanka, East African countries (Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Zambia, Malawi, Zaire, Ethiopia) or Vietnam with intention of permanent settlement.

### 3.2 Educational Qualification (Post-wise)
| Post | Qualification |
|---|---|
| **Assistant Audit Officer (AAO) - Central Cadre** | Bachelor's Degree from recognised University/Institute. **Desirable:** CA/CMA/CS/M.Com/MBA (Finance)/Business Economics |
| **AAO - State Cadre** | Bachelor's Degree in any discipline + proficiency in regional/official language of the State at matriculation level |
| **Assistant Accounts Officer** | Bachelor's Degree + proficiency in regional/official language of the State at matriculation level |
| **Junior Statistical Officer (JSO)** | Bachelor's Degree with **60% in Mathematics in Class 12** OR Bachelor's Degree with **Statistics as a subject** at degree level |
| **Statistical Investigator Grade-II** | Bachelor's Degree in **Statistics / Mathematics / Economics / Demography / Population Studies / Operation Research / IT / Computer Science / Computer Engineering / Computer Technology / Computer Application / Data Science / AI** |
| **All Other Posts** | **Bachelor's Degree in any discipline** from a recognised University or equivalent |

### 3.3 Age Limit (as on cut-off date)
| Post Group | Age Range |
|---|---|
| Most Group B posts | **18 - 30 years** |
| Sub-Inspector (CBI) & ASO (CSS/Railways/MEA/AFHQ) | **20 - 30 years** |
| Junior Statistical Officer | **18 - 32 years** |
| Group C posts (Auditor, Accountant, Tax Assistant, Postal Assistant, UDC, etc.) | **18 - 27 years** |

### 3.4 Age Relaxation
| Category | Relaxation |
|---|---|
| SC / ST | 5 years |
| OBC | 3 years |
| PwBD (General) | 10 years |
| PwBD (OBC) | 13 years |

| Ex-Servicemen (OBC) | 6 years |
| Ex-Servicemen (SC/ST) | 8 years |

| Widows / Divorced / Judicially separated women | Up to 35 years (Gen) / 40 years (SC/ST) |

### 3.5 Number of Attempts
**No fixed limit** - candidates can appear as long as they are within the age limit and meet eligibility.

## 4. Exam Pattern - Tier I (Preliminary)

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Mode | Online (CBT) |
| Type | Objective MCQ |
| Sections | 4 (English, GA, Reasoning, Quant) |
| Total Questions | **100** (25 per section) |
| Total Marks | **200** (2 marks per question) |
| Duration | **60 minutes** (1 hour) - 15 minutes **per section** (new sectional timing from 2026) |
| Negative Marking | **0.50 marks** per wrong answer |
| PwD Scribe Time | 1 hour 20 minutes |

### Section-wise Distribution
| Section | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| English Comprehension | 25 | 50 | 15 min |
| General Awareness | 25 | 50 | 15 min |
| General Intelligence & Reasoning | 25 | 50 | 15 min |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 25 | 50 | 15 min |
| **Total** | **100** | **200** | **60 min** |

> **Note:** Tier 1 is **qualifying**; marks are not added to final merit. Cut-off based on normalized scores.

## 6. Detailed Syllabus

### 6.1 Quantitative Aptitude (Tier I & Tier II)

#### Number System & Arithmetic
- Whole numbers, Decimals, Fractions
- Relationships between numbers
- Percentage
- Ratio & Proportion
- Square roots, Averages
- Simple & Compound Interest
- Profit, Loss & Discount
- Partnership business
- Mixture & Alligation
- Time & Distance
- Time & Work

#### Algebra
- Basic algebraic identities
- Elementary surds
- Graphs of Linear Equations

#### Geometry
- Triangles and their centres
- Congruence & similarity of triangles
- Quadrilaterals and polygons
- Circles (chords, tangents, angles, common tangents)
- Regular polygons

#### Mensuration
- Right Prism, Right Circular Cone, Right Circular Cylinder
- Sphere, Hemispheres
- Rectangular Parallelepiped
- Regular Right Pyramid
- Heights & Distances

#### Trigonometry
- Trigonometric ratios, degree and radian measures
- Standard identities (sin^2 + cos^2 = 1, etc.)
- Complementary angles

#### Data Interpretation
- Tables, Histograms, Frequency Polygon
- Bar Diagram, Pie Chart
- Mean, Median, Mode
- Standard Deviation, Basic probability

### 6.6 Statistics - Paper II (For JSO Post Only)

1. **Collection, Classification & Presentation of Data**
   - Primary & Secondary data
   - Methods of data collection
   - Tabulation, Graphs & Charts
   - Frequency distributions, Diagrammatic presentation

2. **Measures of Central Tendency**
   - Mean, Median, Mode
   - Quartiles, Deciles, Percentiles

3. **Measures of Dispersion**
   - Range, Quartile deviation, Mean deviation, Standard deviation
   - Relative dispersion

4. **Moments, Skewness & Kurtosis**
   - Types of moments
   - Measures of skewness & kurtosis

5. **Correlation & Regression**
   - Scatter diagram, Simple correlation coefficient
   - Regression lines, Spearman's rank correlation
   - Association of attributes
   - Multiple & partial correlation (3 variables)

6. **Probability Theory**
   - Meaning & definitions, Conditional & compound probability
   - Independent events, Bayes' theorem

7. **Random Variable & Probability Distributions**
   - Random variable, Probability functions
   - Expectation & variance, Higher moments
   - Binomial, Poisson, Normal, Exponential distributions
   - Joint distribution of two discrete variables

8. **Sampling Theory**
   - Population & sample, Parameter & statistic
   - Sampling & non-sampling errors
   - Simple random, Stratified, Multistage, Multiphase, Cluster, Systematic, Purposive, Convenience, Quota sampling
   - Sampling distribution & sample size

9. **Statistical Inference**
   - Point & interval estimation
   - Properties of good estimator
   - Methods: Moments, Maximum likelihood, Least squares
   - Hypothesis testing: Z-test, t-test, Chi-square, F-test, Confidence intervals

10. **ANOVA (Analysis of Variance)**
    - One-way and two-way classified data

11. **Time Series Analysis**
    - Components, Trend measurement, Seasonal variation

12. **Index Numbers**
    - Types, Different formulae
    - Base shifting & splicing, Cost of living index
    - Uses of index numbers

---

### 6.7 Finance & Economics - Paper III (For AAO Post Only)

#### Part A: Finance and Accounts (80 Marks)
- Nature and scope, Limitations of Financial Accounting
- Basic concepts and conventions, GAAP
- Single & double entry, Books of Original Entry
- Bank Reconciliation, Journal, Ledgers, Trial Balance
- Rectification of Errors
- Manufacturing, Trading, Profit & Loss Appropriation Accounts
- Balance Sheet, Distinction between Capital & Revenue Expenditure
- Depreciation Accounting, Valuation of Inventories
- Non-profit organisations Accounts
- Receipts & Payments and Income & Expenditure Accounts
- Bills of Exchange, Self Balancing Ledgers

#### Part B: Economics and Governance (120 Marks)
1. **Comptroller & Auditor General (CAG)** - Constitutional provisions, Role
2. **Finance Commission** - Role & functions
3. **Basic Concepts of Economics & Micro Economics** - Definition, Scope, Central problems, PPC
4. **Theory of Demand & Supply** - Law of demand/supply, Elasticity, Consumer behaviour (Marshallian & Indifference curve)
5. **Theory of Production & Cost** - Factors of production, Laws of variable proportions & returns to scale
6. **Forms of Market & Price Determination** - Perfect competition, Monopoly, Monopolistic, Oligopoly
7. **Indian Economy** - Agriculture, Industry, Services, National Income, Population, Poverty, Unemployment, Infrastructure
8. **Economic Reforms in India** - Liberalisation, Privatisation, Globalisation, Disinvestment (post-1991)
9. **Money & Banking** - Monetary & Fiscal policy, RBI, Commercial banks, RRB, Payment Banks, Budget, BoP, FRBM Act 2003
10. **Role of IT in Governance**

|---|---|---|---|

- **No marks** for un-attempted questions
- **Penalty** also applies to multiple-marked responses

## Quick Reference: Key Takeaways

1. **Exam is annual**, conducted online in two tiers (Tier 1 qualifying; Tier 2 merit-deciding).
2. **Graduation (Bachelor's Degree)** is the minimum eligibility.
3. **Age:** 18-27 (Group C), 18-30 (most Group B), 20-30 (CBI/ASO), 18-32 (JSO).
4. **No interview** - selection is purely exam-based.
5. **No descriptive paper** - all objective MCQ.
6. **Sectional timing** introduced in 2026.
7. **Negative marking:** 0.50 (Tier 1) / 1 mark (Tier 2).
8. **DEST** is qualifying for posts like Tax Assistant.
9. **Highest post:** Assistant Audit Officer (AAO) - Pay Level 8 (~Rs.70,000+ gross in X-cities).
10. **12,256 vacancies** announced in 2026 cycle.
