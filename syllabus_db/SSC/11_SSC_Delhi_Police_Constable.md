# SSC Delhi Police Constable (Executive) Examination

## 1. Overview

| Particular | Details |
|---|---|
| **Full Name** | Constable (Executive) Male and Female in Delhi Police Examination |
| **Conducting Body** | Staff Selection Commission (SSC) on behalf of Delhi Police |
| **Parent Ministry** | Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA), Government of India |
| **Frequency** | Periodic (typically once in 1–2 years based on vacancy) |
| **Mode of Exam** | Online Computer-Based Test (CBT) followed by Physical Endurance & Measurement Test (PE&MT) |
| **Vacancies (Recent)** | ~7,500+ vacancies per cycle |
| **Official Websites** | https://ssc.gov.in/ (SSC) and https://delhipolice.gov.in/ (Delhi Police) |

---

## 2. Posts Offered

- **Constable (Executive) - Male**
- **Constable (Executive) - Female**

*Recruited directly into Delhi Police (Group "C" Non-Gazetted post).*

---

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

### 3.1 Educational Qualification
- **10+2 (Senior Secondary)** pass from a recognized Board.
- **Male candidates** must possess a valid **Driving License (L.M.V. - Motorcycle or Car)** on the date of PE&MT. Learner License is NOT acceptable.
- *Relaxation:* Up to 11th pass for children/widows of Delhi Police personnel/Multi-Tasking Staff (MTS) of Delhi Police.

### 3.2 Age Limit (as on the cut-off date)
- **Minimum:** 18 years
- **Maximum:** 25 years
- **Age Relaxation:**
  - SC/ST: 5 years (up to 30 years)
  - OBC: 3 years (up to 28 years)
  - Sportsmen/Sportswomen (National/International representation): 5–10 years
  - Wards of Delhi Police Personnel: Up to 30 years
  - Widows/Divorced/Judicially separated women (not re-married): Up to 30 years (General) / 35 years (SC/ST)

### 3.3 Nationality & Physical Standards
- Must be a citizen of India.
- **Physical Standards (Minimum):**
  - **Height (Male):** 170 cm (Relaxable by 5 cm for ST and Hill area residents).
  - **Height (Female):** 157 cm (Relaxable by 2 cm for SC/ST and 5 cm for wards of Delhi Police).
  - **Chest (Male only):** 81 cm with a minimum of 4 cm expansion (i.e. 81–85 cm).

## 4. Exam Pattern

The recruitment consists of two main stages:
1.  **Computer-Based Examination (CBT)** - 100 Marks (Merit-deciding)
2.  **Physical Endurance & Measurement Test (PE&MT)** - Qualifying in nature

### 4.1 CBT Exam Structure

| Part | Section | Questions | Marks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Part-A | General Knowledge / Current Affairs | 50 | 50 |
| Part-B | Reasoning | 25 | 25 |
| Part-C | Numerical Ability | 15 | 15 |
| Part-D | Computer Fundamentals (MS Excel, MS Word, Internet, WWW, etc.) | 10 | 10 |
| **Total** | **4 Sections** | **100** | **100** |

- **Duration:** 90 minutes
- **Question Type:** Objective Multiple Choice Questions (MCQ)
- **Negative Marking:** **0.25 marks** per wrong answer
- **Medium:** English and Hindi

---

## 5. Detailed Syllabus

### 5.1 General Knowledge & Current Affairs (50 Questions)
*This is the highest-weightage section, designed to test the candidate’s awareness of current developments and environment.*
- **Current Affairs:** National and international developments (last 8–10 months), government schemes, summits, new policies.
- **History:** Ancient, Medieval, and Modern India (freedom struggle, major events).
- **Polity & Constitution:** Preamble, Fundamental Rights/Duties, Directive Principles, Parliamentary system, Judiciary, Constitutional Amendments.
- **Geography:** Physical geography, rivers, soils, crops, climate of India, major mineral reserves, world geography basics.
- **Economy:** Indian Economy basics, five-year plans, GDP, inflation, budget highlights, RBI monetary policy.
- **General Science:** Basic Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (up to 10th standard CBSE/NCERT level).
- **Static GK & Culture:** Indian art forms, folk dances, festivals, music, monuments, books & authors, awards & honors, sports events, capital & currencies.

### 5.2 Reasoning (25 Questions)
- Analogies (Semantic, Figural, Symbolic)
- Number and Alphabetical Series
- Coding-Decoding
- Blood Relations
- Direction Sense
- Venn Diagrams
- Syllogism (Basic cases)
- Similarities and Differences
- Spatial Visualization & Orientation
- Non-Verbal Reasoning (embedded figures, mirror/water images, paper folding/cutting, pattern completion)
- Clocks & Calendars basics

### 5.3 Numerical Ability (15 Questions)
- Number System (Integers, Decimals, Fractions, LCM/HCF)
- VBODMAS & Simplification
- Percentage
- Ratio and Proportion
- Averages
- Profit, Loss & Discount
- Simple & Compound Interest
- Time and Work
- Time, Speed & Distance
- Mensuration (2D & 3D - Area, Perimeter, Volume, Surface Area of basic shapes)

### 5.4 Computer Fundamentals (10 Questions)
- **Word Processing (MS Word):** Text creation, formatting, editing, page setup, shortcuts.
- **Spreadsheets (MS Excel):** Cells, formulas, functions (Sum, Average, Count), sorting, formatting.
- **Internet & Communication:** Web browsing, searching, uploading/downloading, Email (sending, receiving, attachments).
- **World Wide Web (WWW):** Web browsers (Chrome, Edge), Search engines, URLs, basic internet protocols (HTTP, FTP).

## 6. Physical Endurance Test (PET) — Qualifying Only

Candidates must pass all the following events to qualify for the next stage (Measurement Test).

### 6.1 Male Candidates (Age-wise)

| Age | 1600m Race | Long Jump | High Jump |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Up to 30 years** | 6 minutes | 14 Feet | 3'9" (3 Feet 9 Inches) |
| **30 to 40 years** | 7 minutes | 13 Feet | 3'6" |
| **Above 40 years** | 8 minutes | 12 Feet | 3'3" |

### 6.2 Female Candidates (Age-wise)

| Age | 1600m Race | Long Jump | High Jump |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Up to 30 years** | 8 minutes | 10 Feet | 3'0" (3 Feet) |
| **30 to 40 years** | 9 minutes | 09 Feet | 2'9" |
| **Above 40 years** | 10 minutes | 08 Feet | 2'6" |

## 7. Salary & Career Progression

- **Pay Scale:** 7th CPC Pay Level-3
- **Pay Band:** ₹21,700 – ₹69,100
- **Starting Basic Pay:** ₹21,700
- **In-hand Monthly Salary (Initial):** ~₹35,000 – ₹40,000 (inclusive of HRA for Delhi 'X' Class city, DA, Transport allowance, Ration money).
- **Career Path:** Constable → Head Constable → Assistant Sub-Inspector (ASI) → Sub-Inspector (SI) → Inspector.
