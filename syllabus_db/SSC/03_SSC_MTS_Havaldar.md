# SSC MTS (Multi Tasking Staff) & Havaldar Examination

## 1. Overview

- **Full Name:** Staff Selection Commission Multi-Tasking (Non-Technical) Staff Examination, commonly known as SSC MTS, conducted along with Havaldar in CBIC and CBN
- **Conducting Body:** Staff Selection Commission (SSC), under the Department of Personnel and Training (DoPT), Government of India. SSC was constituted on 4 November 1975 (renamed from Subordinate Services Commission in 1977) and is headquartered at CGO Complex, Lodhi Road, New Delhi
- **Frequency:** Annually
- **Examination Mode:** Computer-Based Test (CBT) conducted in 15 languages (Hindi, English, and 13 regional languages: Assamese, Bengali, Gujarati, Kannada, Konkani, Malayalam, Manipuri, Marathi, Odia, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, Urdu)
- **Conducting Authority Note:** SSC Chairman (incumbent as of 2026): Shri Rakesh Ranjan, IAS (since 29 May 2024)
- **Latest Cycle (2026):** Exam held 4–19 February 2026 (re-exam 20 February 2026); answer key released 3 March 2026

## 2. Posts Offered

### A. Multi Tasking Staff (MTS) - Non-Technical, Non-Gazetted, Group C
Appointed in various Central Government Ministries, Departments, and Attached/Subordinate Offices. Common designations include:
- Peon
- Daftary
- Jamadar
- Junior Gestetner Operator
- Chowkidar
- Safaiwala
- Mali

### B. Havaldar
Recruited specifically in:
- **Central Board of Narcotics (CBN)** under Department of Revenue (age 18–25)
- **Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC)** under Department of Revenue (age 18–27)

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

### Nationality
Must be a citizen of India, or subject of Nepal/Bhutan, or a Tibetan refugee who came to India before 1 January 1962, or a Person of Indian Origin (PIO) migrated from specified countries (Pakistan, Burma, Sri Lanka, etc.) with an eligibility certificate issued by the Government of India.

### Age Limit
| Post | Minimum Age | Maximum Age |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| MTS (under CBN) | 18 years | 25 years |
| Havaldar (under CBIC) | 18 years | 27 years |

### Age Relaxation (Upper Limit Only)
| Category | Relaxation |
|----------|------------|
| SC/ST | 5 years |
| OBC | 3 years |
| PwD (UR) | 10 years |
| PwD (OBC) | 13 years |
| PwD (SC/ST) | 15 years |

| Widows / Divorced / Judicially separated women | Up to 35 years (40 for SC/ST) |

### Educational Qualification
- Must have passed **Matriculation (Class 10th)** or equivalent from a recognized Board/University/Institute
- Candidates appearing in the qualifying exam are also eligible to apply provisionally
- For distance learning degrees, approval from Distance Education Bureau/UGC is mandatory

## 4. Exam Pattern - Computer Based Examination (CBE)

The SSC MTS 2026 exam is conducted in **two compulsory sessions** (both must be attempted; missing either leads to disqualification).

### Session-I (45 minutes)
| Subject | No. of Questions | Marks | Negative Marking |
|---------|------------------|-------|-------------------|
| Numerical and Mathematical Ability | 20 | 60 | None |
| Reasoning Ability and Problem-Solving | 20 | 60 | None |
| **Total** | **40** | **120** | — |

### Session-II (45 minutes)
| Subject | No. of Questions | Marks | Negative Marking |
|---------|------------------|-------|-------------------|
| General Awareness | 25 | 75 | -1 mark each |
| English Language and Comprehension | 25 | 75 | -1 mark each |
| **Total** | **50** | **150** | — |

### Descriptive Paper (Havaldar only — qualifying in nature)
- **Essay:** 200–250 words (in English or Hindi)
- **Letter/Application:** 150–200 words (in English or Hindi)

### Total Marks: 270

### Note on Revised Pattern
The pattern underwent a major revision. Earlier versions (pre-2024) had 100 MCQs in one session of 90 minutes (25 each in Reasoning, Numerical, English, GA) with 1/3 negative marking, plus a descriptive paper in Session-II. The 2026 pattern is split into two distinct sessions with no Session-I negative marking.

## 5. Detailed Syllabus

### A. Numerical and Mathematical Ability (20 Qs)
- Number System (Integers, Fractions, Decimals, Surds, Indices)
- HCF and LCM
- Percentage, Average
- Ratio and Proportion
- Mixture and Alligation
- Profit and Loss, Discount
- Simple Interest (SI) and Compound Interest (CI)
- Time and Work, Pipe and Cistern
- Speed, Time and Distance, Problems on Trains, Boats and Streams
- Mensuration (2D and 3D: Triangle, Circle, Quadrilateral, Sphere, Cylinder, Cone)
- Geometry (Basic theorems, Triangles, Circles)
- Trigonometry (Basic identities, Heights and Distances)
- Algebra (Basic identities, Factorization, Equations)
- Data Interpretation (Tables, Bar Graphs, Pie Charts, Line Graphs)
- Square and Square Roots, Cube and Cube Roots
- Number Series

### B. Reasoning Ability and Problem-Solving (20 Qs)
- Number and Alphabetical Series
- Coding-Decoding
- Analogy (Word/Letter/Number)
- Classification (Odd One Out)
- Syllogism
- Direction Sense and Distance
- Ranking and Ordering
- Blood Relations
- Mirror Image, Water Image
- Paper Folding and Cutting
- Embedded Figures, Figure Counting
- Matrix
- Venn Diagrams
- Word order as per dictionary
- Mathematical Operations
- Non-Verbal Reasoning (Pattern completion, Figure series)

### C. English Language and Comprehension (25 Qs)
- Spot the Error
- Fill in the Blanks
- Synonyms and Antonyms
- Spelling / Detecting Misspelt Words
- Idioms and Phrases
- One Word Substitution
- Sentence Improvement / Correction
- Active-Passive Voice
- Direct-Indirect Speech
- Reading Comprehension Passages
- Cloze Test

### D. General Awareness (25 Qs)
- Indian Constitution and Polity
- Indian History (Ancient, Medieval, Modern) and Culture
- Indian Geography (Physical, Economic)
- Indian Economy and Five Year Plans
- General Science — Physics, Chemistry, Biology (up to Class 10 level)
- Inventions and Discoveries
- Current Affairs (National and International)
- Static GK — Books and Authors, Awards and Honours, Important Days
- Sports
- Important Financial and Banking Awareness
- Computer Awareness (basics)

### E. Descriptive Paper (Havaldar)
- **Essay Writing (200–250 words):** Topics on national issues, social issues, economy, governance, science & technology, environment
- **Letter/Application Writing (150–200 words):** Formal letter, application, complaint, suggestion to authorities

## 6. Physical Standard Test (PST) and Physical Efficiency Test (PET) — Havaldar Only

The PET/PST is conducted only for Havaldar posts in CBIC and CBN after qualifying the CBE.

### Physical Standards

| Particulars | Male | Female |
|-------------|------|--------|
| **Height** | 157.5 cm (relaxable by 5 cm for Garhwalis, Assamese, Gorkhas, and Scheduled Tribes) | 152 cm (relaxable by 2.5 cm for Garhwalis, Assamese, Gorkhas, and Scheduled Tribes) |
| **Chest (only for males)** | 76 cm (unexpanded); Minimum expansion 5 cm | Not applicable |
| **Weight (only for females)** | Not applicable | 48 kg (relaxable by 2 kg for Garhwalis, Assamese, Gorkhas, and Scheduled Tribes) |

### Physical Efficiency Test (Running)
- **Male:** 1.6 km run in 8 minutes (earlier tests included long jump and high jump)
- **Female:** 1 km run in 8 minutes

Note: The latest revised standards may vary; refer to the official SSC notification for the current cycle.

## 8. Salary Structure (7th Pay Commission)

- **Pay Level:** Level-1
- **Pay Band:** Pay Band-1 (Rs. 5,200 – Rs. 20,200)
- **Grade Pay:** Rs. 1,800
- **Basic Pay:** Rs. 18,000
- **In-Hand Salary Range:** Rs. 16,915 to Rs. 20,245 (depending on city category X/Y/Z)
- **Gross Salary (City X):** ~Rs. 23,670

### Salary Slip Components
- Basic Pay: Rs. 18,000
- House Rent Allowance (HRA): Rs. 1,440 (Z) – Rs. 4,320 (X)
- Transport Allowance (TA): Rs. 900 – Rs. 1,350
- Dearness Allowance (DA): Revised periodically
- **Deductions:** NPS (Rs. 1,800), CGEGIS (Rs. 1,500), CGHS (Rs. 125), Income Tax, PF

### Career Growth (Promotion Path)
- **First Promotion (after 3 years):** Grade Pay Rs. 1,900
- **Second Promotion (after 3 more years):** Grade Pay Rs. 2,000
- **Third Promotion (after 5 more years):** Grade Pay Rs. 2,400
- **Final:** Can rise up to Grade Pay Rs. 5,400 (Section Officer level by retirement)

## 12. Quick Tips for Preparation

1. **Quantitative Aptitude:** Focus on arithmetic (Percentages, Ratio, SI/CI, Time-Work) and practice previous year papers
2. **Reasoning:** Practice puzzles, analogies, coding-decoding, and classification daily
3. **English:** Read newspapers; focus on vocabulary (synonyms/antonyms), grammar, and comprehension
4. **General Awareness:** Stay updated with daily current affairs; revise static GK (History, Polity, Geography, Science) regularly
5. **Havaldar Aspirants:** Begin physical fitness training early — focus on running practice (1.6 km for male, 1 km for female)
