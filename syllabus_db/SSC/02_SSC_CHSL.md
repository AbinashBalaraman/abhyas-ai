# SSC CHSL (Staff Selection Commission - Combined Higher Secondary Level Examination)

## 1. Exam Overview

| Particulars | Details |
|---|---|
| **Full Name** | Staff Selection Commission - Combined Higher Secondary Level Examination (SSC CHSL) |
| **Conducting Body** | Staff Selection Commission (SSC) |
| **Exam Level** | National |
| **Frequency** | Annually (once a year) |
| **Mode of Examination** | Online (Computer-Based Test) |
| **Selection Process** | Tier-I (CBT) → Tier-II (CBT) → Skill/Typing Test → Document Verification |
| **Official Website** | https://ssc.nic.in / https://ssc.gov.in |
| **Posts Offered** | LDC, JSA, PA, SA, DEO, DEO Grade 'A' |
| **Pay Commission** | 7th Central Pay Commission (CPC) |
| **Pay Levels** | Level 2, Level 4, Level 5 |

---

## 2. Posts Offered & Pay Levels

| Post | Pay Level | Pay Scale | Basic Pay |
|---|---|---|---|
| **LDC (Lower Division Clerk) / JSA (Junior Secretariat Assistant)** | Level 2 | ₹19,900 – ₹63,200 | ₹19,900 |
| **PA (Postal Assistant) / SA (Sorting Assistant)** | Level 4 | ₹25,500 – ₹81,100 | ₹25,500 |
| **DEO (Data Entry Operator)** | Level 4/5 | ₹25,500 – ₹81,100 / ₹29,200 – ₹92,300 | ₹25,500 |
| **DEO Grade 'A'** | Level 4 | ₹25,500 – ₹81,100 | ₹25,500 |

**Job Profiles:**
- **LDC/JSA:** Clerical work, maintaining data, files, and documents; email registration and data entry.
- **DEO:** Office computer handling, typing, working with MS Excel, Word, and PowerPoint.
- **PA/SA:** Mail disposal, data maintenance, customer support and query handling.

## 3. Eligibility

| Criteria | Details |
|---|---|
| **Nationality** | Indian Citizen; or subject of Nepal/Bhutan; Tibetan refugee (per Govt. of India rules) |
| **Age Limit** | 18 to 27 years (as on the cut-off date) |
| **Age Relaxation** | SC/ST: 5 years; OBC: 3 years; PwD: 10–15 years; Ex-Servicemen: as per rules |
| **Educational Qualification** | 12th Pass (Higher Secondary / 10+2) from a recognized Board or University |
| **Other Requirements** | Computer proficiency may be required for certain posts |

## 4. Exam Pattern - Tier-I (Computer-Based)

| Section | No. of Questions | Marks | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| General Intelligence & Reasoning | 25 | 50 | — |
| General Awareness | 25 | 50 | — |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 25 | 50 | — |
| English Comprehension | 25 | 50 | — |
| **Total** | **100** | **200** | **60 minutes (80 min for PwD)** |

- **Negative Marking:** 0.50 marks deducted for each wrong answer.
- **Mode:** Online (CBT)
- **Question Type:** Objective (MCQ)
- **Medium:** English & Hindi
- **Normalisation:** Applied for multi-shift exams.

## 5. Exam Pattern - Tier-II (Computer-Based)

Tier-II is conducted in **two sessions** (Session I & Session II).

### Session I (2 hours 15 minutes)

| Section | Module | Subject | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Section 1 | Module-I | Mathematical Abilities | 30 | 90 | 1 hour |
| Section 1 | Module-II | Reasoning & General Intelligence | 30 | 90 | 1 hour |
| Section 2 | Module-I | English Language & Comprehension | 40 | 120 | 1 hour |
| Section 2 | Module-II | General Awareness | 20 | 60 | 1 hour |
| Section 3 | Module-I | Computer Knowledge Test | 15 | 45 | 15 minutes |
| **Total** | | | **135** | **405** | **2 hr 15 min** |

### Session II (Skill/Typing Test - Qualifying)

| Module | Post | Test Type | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Module-II Part A | DEO in Department/Ministry | Skill Test | 15 minutes |
| Module-II Part B | DEO except in Department/Ministry | Skill Test | 15 minutes |
| Module-II Part C | LDC/JSA | Typing Test | 10 minutes |

**Negative Marking (Tier-II):** 1 mark deducted for each wrong answer in Section 1, Section 2, and Module-I of Section 3.

## 6. Detailed Syllabus

### A. General Intelligence & Reasoning (Tier-I & Tier-II)

- Analogy (Semantic, Symbolic, Number, Figural)
- Classification (Semantic, Symbolic/Number, Figural)
- Series (Number, Figural, Semantic)
- Coding-Decoding
- Syllogism
- Venn Diagrams
- Direction and Distance
- Ranking
- Blood Relations
- Seating Arrangement & Puzzle
- Word Formation
- Paper Folding Method
- Matrix
- Embedded Figures
- Critical Thinking / Problem Solving
- Emotional & Social Intelligence
- Numerical & Symbolic Operations
- Trends, Space Orientation
- Word Building
- Drawing Inferences
- Punched hole / Folding

### B. English Language (Tier-I)

- Spotting Errors
- Fill in the Blanks
- Synonyms / Antonyms / Homonyms
- Idioms & Phrases
- One-word Substitution
- Reading Comprehension
- Cloze Test
- Spellings
- Sentence Correction
- Para Jumbles
- Active/Passive Voice
- Direct/Indirect Narration

### C. English Language & Comprehension (Tier-II)

- Vocabulary, Grammar, Sentence Structure
- Fill in the Blanks
- Idioms & Phrases
- Improvement of Sentences
- Direct/Indirect Narration
- Passage Analysis
- Comprehension Passage
- Synonyms / Antonyms / Homonyms
- Spot the Error
- Spellings / Misspelt words
- One-word Substitution
- Active/Passive Voice
- Sentence Shuffling
- Cloze Passage

### D. Quantitative Aptitude / Mathematical Abilities

- **Number System:** Computation of Whole Numbers, Decimals, Fractions, Relationship between numbers
- **Arithmetic:** Percentages, Ratio & Proportion, Square Roots, Averages, Simple & Compound Interest, Profit & Loss, Discount, Partnership, Time & Distance, Time & Work
- **Algebra:** Basic algebraic identities, Elementary surds, Graphs of linear equations
- **Geometry:** Elementary geometric figures, Triangles, Congruence & similarity, Circles & tangents, Chords, Common tangents
- **Mensuration:** Triangle, Quadrilaterals, Regular Polygons, Circle, Right Prism, Right Circular Cone, Cylinder, Sphere, Hemispheres, Rectangular Parallelepiped, Regular Right Pyramid
- **Trigonometry:** Trigonometric ratios, Complementary angles, Height & Distances, Standard identities
- **Statistics & Probability:** Tables & Graphs, Histogram, Frequency Polygon, Bar-Diagram, Pie-Chart, Mean/Median/Mode, Standard Deviation, Simple Probabilities
- **Data Interpretation**
- **Simplification**
- **Speed, Distance & Time**

### E. General Awareness

- **History:** Ancient, Medieval, Modern (Indian History)
- **Polity / Constitution:** Indian Polity, General Policy
- **Geography:** Indian and World Geography
- **Economics:** Economic Scene, Indian Economy
- **General Science:** Physics, Chemistry, Biology
- **Computer Knowledge**
- **Current Affairs**
- **Static GK**
- **Books & Authors**
- **Awards & Honours**
- **Sports**
- **Important Schemes & Portfolios**
- **People in the News**
- **Culture**
- **India and its Neighbours**

### F. Computer Knowledge (Tier-II Section 3 Module-I)

- **Computer Basics:** Organization of a computer, CPU, Input/Output devices, computer memory, memory organization, backup devices, ports, Windows Explorer, keyboard shortcuts
- **Software & Applications:** Windows OS, MS Word, MS Excel, PowerPoint
- **Internet & Email:** Web Browsing & Searching, Downloading & Uploading, Managing email, e-banking
- **Networking & Cybersecurity:** Networking devices and protocols, Network and information security threats (hacking, viruses, worms, Trojans), preventive measures

## 7. Skill / Typing Test

| Post | Speed Required | Duration | Document Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Data Entry Operator (DEO)** | 8,000 Key Impressions/hour | 15 minutes | English document with 2,000–2,200 strokes/key depressions |
| **DEO in Office of C&AG** | 15,000 key depressions/hour | 15 minutes | Printed English matter with ~3,700–4,000 key depressions |
| **LDC/JSA and PA/SA** | 10,500 key depressions/hour (English) | 15 minutes | Printed English matter with ~9,000 key depressions/hour |
| **Typing Test (LDC/JSA)** | English 35 wpm / Hindi 30 wpm | 10 minutes | — |

**Note:** The skill/typing test is **qualifying in nature** but mandatory.

## 14. Tie-Breaking Criteria

In case of tied scores, merit is resolved by:
1. **Total marks in Tier-II**
2. **Total marks in Tier-I**
3. **Date of birth** (older candidate ranked higher)
4. **Alphabetical order of first names**

## 15. Tips for Preparation

- Review the **SSC CHSL syllabus and exam pattern** thoroughly before starting preparation.
- Solve **previous year question papers** (at least last 5 years) to understand question trends.
- Take **daily mock tests** for all four Tier-I subjects.
- For English, focus on **comprehension passages, vocabulary, and grammar rules**.
- For Quantitative Aptitude, master **Arithmetic (Time & Work, Profit & Loss, Percentage)** as most questions come from these.
- For Reasoning, practice **puzzles, seating arrangements, and syllogisms** regularly.
- For General Awareness, read **daily current affairs and revise Standard General Knowledge Compendium** weekly.
- Practice **typing regularly** to meet skill test requirements.
- Plan a **revision schedule** in the last 30 days before the exam.
