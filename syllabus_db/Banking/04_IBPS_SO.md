# IBPS SO (Specialist Officer) - Comprehensive Guide

## 1. Overview

**Full Name:** Institute of Banking Personnel Selection - Specialist Officer (IBPS SO) Examination

**Conducting Body:** Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS)
- Established: 19 December 1975
- Headquarters: Mumbai, India
- Parent Organization: Ministry of Finance, Government of India
- Chairman: Shri Rajneesh Karnatak
- Operates as an autonomous central recruitment agency

**Frequency:** Annually (Common Recruitment Process - CRP Specialist Officers)

**Mode of Examination:** Online (Computer Based Test - CBT)

**Selection Process:** Preliminary (Qualifying) → Mains (Merit) → Interview

**Participating Banks (Public Sector Banks - PSBs):**
Allahabad Bank (now Indian Bank), Andhra Bank (merged), Bank of Baroda, Bank of India, Bank of Maharashtra, Canara Bank, Central Bank of India, Corporation Bank (merged), Dena Bank (merged), Indian Bank, Indian Overseas Bank, Oriental Bank of Commerce (merged), Punjab & Sind Bank, Punjab National Bank, Syndicate Bank (merged), UCO Bank, Union Bank of India, United Bank of India (merged), Vijaya Bank (merged), and IDBI Bank (some years).

> Note: Following bank mergers in 2019-2020, the active PSBs participating in IBPS SO recruitment are now approximately 12 banks.

**Official Website:** [https://www.ibps.in](https://www.ibps.in)

## 2. Posts Offered (Specialist Officer Posts - Scale I)

The IBPS SO recruitment is conducted for the following 6 specialist posts (all at Junior Management Grade Scale-I):

| Post Code | Post Name | Scale |
|-----------|-----------|-------|
| 01 | I.T. Officer (Scale I) | I |
| 02 | Agricultural Field Officer (AFO) (Scale I) | I |
| 03 | Rajbhasha Adhikari (Scale I) - Hindi Officer | I |
| 04 | Law Officer (Scale I) | I |
| 05 | HR/Personnel Officer (Scale I) | I |
| 06 | Marketing Officer (Scale I) | I |

**Note on other posts:**
- **Treasury Manager (Scale I):** Was previously included in some IBPS SO notifications but has been discontinued in recent cycles. Candidates should refer to the latest notification.
- **Probationary Officer (Scale I):** This is a separate recruitment under **IBPS PO/MT CRP**, not part of IBPS SO. The IBPS PO exam is conducted separately.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

### 3.1 Nationality / Citizenship
A candidate must be one of the following:
1. A citizen of India, or
2. A subject of Nepal, or
3. A subject of Bhutan, or
4. A Tibetan Refugee who came to India before 1st January 1962 with the intention of permanently settling in India, or
5. A person of Indian origin who has migrated from Pakistan, Burma, Sri Lanka, East African countries of Kenya, Uganda, United Republic of Tanzania, Zambia, Malawi, Zaire, Ethiopia, and Vietnam.

### 3.2 Age Limit (as on the cut-off date)
- **Minimum Age:** 20 years
- **Maximum Age:** 30 years
- Candidate must have been born between **2 July 1995 and 1 July 2005** (both dates inclusive) for the 2026 cycle.

**Age Relaxation:**

| Category | Age Relaxation |
|----------|----------------|
| SC/ST | 5 years |
| OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) | 3 years |
| PWD (Person with Disability) | 10 years |
| Ex-Servicemen / Commissioned Officers (ECOS/SSCO with 5+ years service) | 5 years |
| Persons affected by 1984 riots | 5 years |

### 3.3 Educational Qualification (Post-wise)

#### IT Officer (Scale I) - Post Code 01
- 4-year Engineering/Technology degree in **Computer Science / Information Technology / Computer Application / Electronics & Communication Engineering / Electronics & Telecommunication / Electronics & Instrumentation**, OR
- Post Graduate degree in the above-mentioned fields, OR
- Graduates who have passed the **DOEACC 'B' level** examination.

#### Agricultural Field Officer (AFO) (Scale I) - Post Code 02
- 4-year graduation (B.Sc./B.Tech./B.E.) in **Agriculture / Horticulture / Animal Husbandry / Veterinary Science / Dairy Science / Agricultural Engineering / Fishery Science / Pisciculture / Agri Marketing and Cooperation / Co-operation and Banking / Agro-Forestry**.

#### Rajbhasha Adhikari (Scale I) - Post Code 03
- **Post Graduate degree in Hindi** with English as a subject at the graduation/degree level, OR
- **Post Graduate degree in Sanskrit** with English and Hindi as subjects at the graduation level.

#### Law Officer (Scale I) - Post Code 04
- A **Bachelor's degree in Law (LLB)** (3-year or 5-year integrated course) from a recognized university, AND
- **Enrolled as an advocate with the Bar Council**.

#### HR/Personnel Officer (Scale I) - Post Code 05
- **Graduation** (in any discipline), AND
- **Full-time Post Graduate degree or Full-time Diploma** in Personnel Management / Industrial Relations / HR / HRD / Social Work / Labour Law.

#### Marketing Officer (Scale I) - Post Code 06
- **Graduation** (in any discipline), AND
- **Full-time MMS (Marketing) / MBA (Marketing) / PGDBA / PGDBM** with specialization in Marketing.

### 3.4 Computer Literacy (for non-IT posts)
For posts other than IT Officer Scale I, candidates should have a Certificate/Diploma/Degree in computer operations / Language OR have studied Computer / Information Technology as one of the subjects in High School / College / Institute.

### 3.5 Number of Attempts
There is **no limit on the number of attempts** as long as candidates meet the age criteria (maximum age of 30 years, with relaxation for reserved categories).

## 4. Exam Pattern (3 Stages)

### 4.1 Preliminary Examination (Qualifying - Online Objective)

**Total:** 150 Questions | 125 Marks | 120 Minutes

The Preliminary exam has two slightly different patterns based on the post:

**Pattern A: For IT Officer, AFO, Marketing Officer, and HR/Personnel Officer**

| Section | No. of Questions | Max Marks | Duration |
|---------|------------------|-----------|----------|
| English Language | 50 | 25 | 40 minutes |
| Reasoning Ability | 50 | 50 | 40 minutes |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 50 | 50 | 40 minutes |
| **Total** | **150** | **125** | **120 minutes** |

**Pattern B: For Law Officer and Rajbhasha Adhikari**

| Section | No. of Questions | Max Marks | Duration |
|---------|------------------|-----------|----------|
| English Language | 50 | 25 | 40 minutes |
| Reasoning Ability | 50 | 50 | 40 minutes |
| General Awareness (with special reference to Banking Industry) | 50 | 50 | 40 minutes |
| **Total** | **150** | **125** | **120 minutes** |

> Note: The Preliminary exam is **only qualifying in nature**. Marks obtained in Prelims are not added to the final merit. Candidates must qualify sectionally and overall to be eligible for Mains.

### 4.2 Mains Examination (Merit - Online)

**Pattern A: For Law Officer, IT Officer, AFO, HR/Personnel Officer, and Marketing Officer**

| Section | No. of Questions | Max Marks | Medium | Duration |
|---------|------------------|-----------|--------|----------|
| Professional Knowledge | 60 | 60 | English & Hindi | 45 minutes |
| **Total** | **60** | **60** | | **45 minutes** |

**Pattern B: For Rajbhasha Adhikari**

| Section | No. of Questions | Max Marks | Medium | Duration |
|---------|------------------|-----------|--------|----------|
| Professional Knowledge (Objective) | 45 | 60 | English & Hindi | 30 minutes |
| Professional Knowledge (Descriptive) | 2 | - | - | 30 minutes |
| **Total** | - | **60** | | **60 minutes** |

> The Mains exam is the **merit-deciding stage**. Scores from Mains are used for final shortlisting for the interview.

### 4.3 Interview (100 Marks)
- Candidates shortlisted from the Mains exam are called for an Interview.
- The interview carries **100 marks**.
- **Final Selection Weightage:** Mains (80%) : Interview (20%) = **80:20 ratio**
- Minimum qualifying marks in interview: 40% (35% for SC/ST/OBC/PWD candidates)

## 7. Marking Scheme

| Stage | Correct Answer | Wrong Answer (Negative Marking) |
|-------|---------------|----------------------------------|
| Preliminary | +1 mark (or as per section) | **-0.25 marks** (1/4th deduction) |
| Mains | +1 mark (or as per section) | **-0.25 marks** (1/4th deduction) |
| Interview | As per panel marks | No negative marking |

> **Important:** There is no sectional cut-off in the Preliminary exam (only sectional qualifying criteria may apply). Candidates must qualify overall to be eligible for Mains.

## 10. Important Tips for Preparation

1. **Know Your Post:** Since the Professional Knowledge syllabus differs by post, specialize early.
2. **Prelims Strategy:** Focus on Quant, Reasoning, and English - practice speed and accuracy.
3. **Mains Focus:** Professional Knowledge carries 60 marks and is the merit-deciding stage. In-depth study of post-specific subjects is crucial.
4. **Interview Preparation:** Stay updated on current banking affairs, RBI policies, and basic knowledge of your specialization.
5. **Time Management:** Take sectional and full-length mock tests regularly.
6. **Negative Marking:** Be cautious - 0.25 deduction per wrong answer can significantly impact your score.
