# Specialized Financial & Regulatory Body Examinations: SEBI, PFRDA, IBBI, NHB, IIBF, NISM, NSE/BSE, FRM, CFA, CPA

This document covers specialized examinations conducted by India's financial regulators (SEBI, PFRDA, IBBI, NHB, IIBF) and global certifications (NISM, NSE/BSE, FRM, CFA, CPA), including eligibility, exam pattern, syllabus, salary and official resources.

---

## 1. SEBI Grade A (Officer Grade A - Assistant Manager)

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) — established 12 April 1988 (statutory status 30 January 1992 under SEBI Act, 1992), headquartered at SEBI Bhavan, Bandra Kurla Complex, Mumbai — recruits Grade A Officers (Assistant Managers) through a three-stage selection process.

### 1.1 Streams
- General (most common)
- Legal
- Information Technology (IT)
- Engineering — Civil / Electrical
- Research
- Official Language (Rajbhasha)

### 1.2 Eligibility
- **General**: Master's/Postgraduate in any discipline OR Bachelor's in Law/Engineering OR Professional qualification (CA/CS/CFA/ICWA) OR MBA.
- **Legal**: Bachelor's in Law (LLB).
- **IT**: Bachelor's/Master's in Computer Science/IT/Engineering (CS/IT) or equivalent.
- **Engineering (Civil/Electrical)**: Bachelor's/Master's in relevant engineering discipline.
- **Research**: Master's in Economics/Statistics/Finance/Commerce/MBA (Finance).
- **Official Language**: Master's in Hindi with English as a subject at degree level, or vice versa.
- **Age**: 18 – 30 years (relaxation as per government norms for SC/ST/OBC/PwBD).

### 1.3 Exam Pattern (3 Stages)

#### Phase-I (Online Screening — Screening only; not counted for merit)
| Paper | Stream | Marks | Duration | Cut-off |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Paper-I | All streams: General Awareness, English, Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning | 100 | 60 min | 30% |
| Paper-II | General (Commerce/Finance/Economics/Math/IT/Audit/Insurance), Legal, IT, Research, Engineering, Official Language — subject-specific MCQs | 100 | 40 min | 40% |

Aggregate cut-off for Phase-I: **40%**. Negative marking: 1/4 for wrong answers.

#### Phase-II (Mains — counted for merit; weightage 85%)
| Paper | Subject | Marks | Duration | Cut-off | Weightage |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Paper-I | English Descriptive (essay, précis, comprehension, drafting) — common to all | 100 | 60 min | 30% | 1/3 |
| Paper-II | General/Legal/IT (coding test in C++/Java/Python)/Research/Engineering — subject specific MCQ + descriptive (for Legal: 70 MCQ + 3 descriptive × 10 marks) | 100 | 40–180 min | 40% | 2/3 |

Aggregate cut-off Phase-II: **50%** (Legal: 50%). Negative marking 1/4 in Paper-II (except IT coding).

#### Phase-III: Interview (50 marks; weightage 15%)
Final merit = Phase-II marks (85%) + Interview (15%).

### 1.4 Detailed Syllabus — Phase I, Paper I
- **English**: Reading Comprehension, Cloze Test (fillers), Error Spotting, Vocabulary (Synonyms/Antonyms), Grammar (Active-Passive, Direct-Indirect, Sentence Connectors), Para Completion, Sentence Rearrangement, Idioms & Phrases.
- **Reasoning**: Puzzles, Seating Arrangement, Coding-Decoding, Syllogism, Inequalities, Blood Relations, Direction Sense, Machine Input-Output, Data Sufficiency, Logical Reasoning, Order & Ranking.
- **Quantitative Aptitude**: Simplification, Approximations, Number System, Quadratic Equations, SI/CI, Averages, Ratios, DI (Percentage/Ratios), Time & Work, Pipes & Cisterns, Speed-Distance-Time, Probability, P&C, Series, Mensuration, Profit-Loss.
- **General Awareness**: Current Affairs (National/International), Budget, SEBI-related news, Financial Sector, Awards, Summits, Books & Authors, Science/Technology.

### 1.5 Detailed Syllabus — Phase II, Paper 2 (General Stream)
- **Commerce & Accountancy**: Accounting Standards (Depreciation, Inventories, Revenue Recognition, Fixed Assets, Foreign Exchange, Investments), Cash Flow / Fund Flow / Ratio Analysis, Share Capital, ESOPs, Buy-back, Final Accounts.
- **Finance**: Financial System & regulators, Primary/Secondary markets, Forex, Money/Bond/Equity markets, Derivatives (Forwards/Futures/Swaps), Financial Inclusion, Sources of Finance, Public-Private Partnership, GST, FRBM, Fiscal Policy, Inflation (WPI/CPI).
- **Management**: Principles (Planning/Organizing/Staffing/Directing/Controlling), Managerial roles, Leadership theories, HRD, Motivation, Morale, Communication.
- **Costing**: Cost & Management Accounting concepts, Job/Batch/Contract/Process Costing, Standard Costing, Marginal Costing, Budgetary Control, JIT, Kaizen, 5S, TPM, Six Sigma, BPR.
- **Companies Act**: Chapters III, IV, VIII, X, XI, XII, XXVII of Companies Act 2013.
- **Economics**: Demand-Supply, Market Structures, National Income, Classical & Keynesian, IS-LM, Inflation, Phillips Curve, Business Cycles, BoP, Forex, Monetary & Fiscal Policy, NBFIs.
- **Insurance**: Life/General Insurance basics, IRDAI regulations.

### 1.6 Stream-specific Syllabus (Phase II)
- **Legal**: Constitution of India, Indian Contract Act, CPC, Transfer of Property Act, Arbitration & Conciliation Act, Limitation Act, Administrative Law, Jurisprudence, Law of Torts, Criminal Law (BNS 2023, BNSS 2023, BSA 2023), Companies Act 2013, LLP Act 2008, IBC 2016, Securities Laws (SCRA, SEBI Act, Depositories Act, PFUTP, Takeover, Insider Trading).
- **IT**: DBMS (ER/Relational model, SQL, Normalization, Transactions), Programming (Java/C/C++), Python/R, Algorithms, Data Structures, Networking (OSI/TCP-IP), Cyber Security (CIA triad, attacks), Data Warehousing, Shell scripting. Phase-II is a coding test in C++/Java/Python.
- **Research**: Economics (Micro/Macro/Public/International), Statistics & Econometrics (Central Tendency, Dispersion, Correlation, Sampling, Hypothesis, Regression), Financial Markets (Asymmetric info, Efficiency, Primary/Secondary/Commodity, Mutual Funds, Forwards/Futures/Options).
- **Official Language (Rajbhasha)**: Hindi↔English translation (terms, phrases, sentences), Legal terminology, Administrative/Banking/Capital Market terminology.
- **Engineering (Civil)**: Building Materials, Solid Mechanics, Structural Analysis, Steel/Concrete/Masonry Design, Construction Management, Fluid Mechanics, Hydrology, Environmental, Geotechnical, Surveying, Transportation.
- **Engineering (Electrical)**: Electrical Materials, Circuits & Fields, Measurements, Computer Fundamentals, Basic Electronics, Analog & Digital Electronics, Signals & Systems, Control Systems, Electrical Machines, Power Systems, Power Electronics & Drives.

### 1.7 Salary
- **Basic Pay**: ₹44,500 – ₹89,150 (Pay Level 8, 7th CPC).
- **Gross / In-hand**: ~₹75,000 – ₹1,00,000 per month (including DA, HRA, CCA, perks).
- Perks: LTC, medical, house loan, low-interest loans, newspaper allowance, etc.

## 2. SEBI Grade B (Manager)

A higher managerial cadre; intake is rare and competitive.

### 2.1 Eligibility
- **Master's/Postgraduate** in any discipline / MBA / CA / CS / CFA / CWA / LLB / Engineering, with **21–30 years** age.

### 2.2 Exam Pattern
- Similar to Grade A but with greater difficulty and depth.
- Phase-I (screening) + Phase-II (Mains) + Phase-III (Interview).
- Phase-II typically has heavier descriptive component and advanced subject knowledge.

### 2.3 Detailed Syllabus
- Same subjects as Grade A but more advanced (CFA-level finance, advanced law, deeper economic theory).
- **Finance**: Capital Asset Pricing Model, APT, portfolio theory, derivatives pricing, options, swaps.
- **Economics**: Econometrics, time series, monetary economics, international finance.
- **Management**: Strategic management, organizational behaviour, leadership.
- **Legal**: SEBI regulations in depth, securities law, insolvency law.

### 2.4 Salary
- **Basic Pay**: ₹55,200 – ₹1,40,000 (Pay Level 10, 7th CPC).
- **Gross / In-hand**: ~₹1,00,000 – ₹1,40,000 per month.

## 3. PFRDA Grade A (Assistant Manager)

The Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA) was established as Interim PFRDA on 23 August 2003; statutory under PFRDA Act 2013 (notified 1 February 2014). It regulates NPS, APY, NPS Vatsalya, and Unified Pension Scheme (UPS).

### 3.1 Posts
- Officer Grade A (Assistant Manager) in PFRDA via SEBI-conducted recruitment (PFRDA uses SEBI exam pattern).

### 3.2 Eligibility
- Master's in Economics / Commerce / Finance / Business Administration / MBA; or CA/CS/CFA/CWA.
- Age: 18 – 30 years.

### 3.3 Exam Pattern
- Phase-I (screening) + Phase-II (mains) + Phase-III (interview).
- Pattern broadly mirrors SEBI Grade A.

### 3.4 Detailed Syllabus
- **Pension Sector Knowledge**: Pension reforms globally and in India.
- **National Pension System (NPS)**: Architecture (Subscribers, PoPs, CRA, Pension Funds, Custodian, Trustee Bank, NPS Trust, ASPs), Tier I & II, NPS Vatsalya, UPS, NPS Lite.
- **Atal Pension Yojana (APY)**: Eligibility, age 18–40, guaranteed pension ₹1,000–₹5,000.
- **PFRDA Act 2013**: Powers, functions, intermediaries.
- **Investment**: Asset allocation (E/C/G/A), NAV computation, multiple scheme framework (MSF).
- **Pension Fund Regulations**: PFRDA (Pension Fund) Regulations 2015.
- **General**: Economics, Finance, Quantitative, Reasoning, English, GK — similar to SEBI.

### 3.5 Salary
- Basic: ₹44,500+ (Pay Level 8).
- In-hand: ~₹70,000 – ₹90,000 per month.

## 4. IBBI Grade A (Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India)

IBBI — established under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) 2016 — regulates Insolvency Professionals (IPs), IPAs, Information Utilities (IUs), and Registered Valuers.

### 4.1 Posts
- Officer Grade A (Assistant Manager) in IBBI.

### 4.2 Eligibility
- Master's in Law / Commerce / Economics / Finance / Management / Business Administration / CA / CS / CMA / LLB.
- Age: 18 – 30 years.

### 4.3 Exam Pattern
- SEBI pattern (Phase-I + Phase-II + Interview).

### 4.4 Detailed Syllabus
- **IBC Code 2016**: Preamble, sections 7–33 (CIRP), sections 33–54 (Liquidation), sections 14–26 (Moratorium, CoC, RP), Section 29A (ineligibility), Section 12A (withdrawal), fast-track, pre-pack.
- **Insolvency Professionals (IPs)**: Registration, IPAs, IPEs, code of conduct, disciplinary.
- **Liquidation Process**: Order, liquidator duties, waterfall mechanism under Section 53, avoidance transactions (Sections 43–51).
- **Adjudicating Authority (AA)**: NCLT (corporate debtors), DRT (individuals/partnership firms), NCLAT (appellate), Supreme Court (final).
- **Cross-border Insolvency**: UNCITRAL Model Law adoption.
- **Personal Guarantors**: Sections 94–187.
- **Group**: Pre-packaged insolvency, voluntary liquidation.
- **Valuation**: Valuation standards under IBBI.

### 4.5 Salary
- Basic: ₹44,500+.
- In-hand: ~₹70,000+ per month.

## 5. NHB (National Housing Bank) — Brief

NHB — established under NHB Act 1987, apex housing finance institution, headquartered at Core 5-A, India Habitat Centre, Lodhi Road, New Delhi. Now subsumed under RBI (subsidiary, 2019).

### 5.1 Recruitment (NHB / Now RBI)
- Earlier conducted own Grade A & B recruitment; currently posts are filled through **RBI Grade B (DEPR/DSIM)** or specialist contracts.
- Officers oversee refinancing of HFCs, NHB Residex (housing price index), supervision of HFCs, reverse repo, government schemes (PMAY 2.0).

### 5.2 Topics
- **Housing Finance**: HFCs, NHB Act, refinance, securitisation, NHB Residex, affordable housing.
- **Banking & Finance**: RBI Master Directions, Prudential Norms, Basel III.
- **Economics**: Macro/micro, monetary policy, financial inclusion.
- **General Aptitude**: Quant, Reasoning, English, GK.

### 5.3 Salary
- Similar to RBI Grade B: Basic ₹55,200 – ₹1,40,000; gross ~₹1,00,000+.

## 6. IIBF (Indian Institute of Banking & Finance) Exams

IIBF (est. 1928, headquarters: Kohinoor City, Kurla, Mumbai) is the premier institute offering certifications for bank employees, primarily in India. Most exams are mandatory for in-service bankers under IBA/HRMD circulars.

### 6.1 JAIIB (Junior Associate of IIBF)
- **Eligibility**: Bank employees with at least 6 months of service; member of IIBF.
- **Papers (4)**: Principles & Practices of Banking; Accounting & Finance for Bankers; Legal & Regulatory Aspects of Banking; Retail Banking & Wealth Management.
- **Pattern**: 100 MCQ × 100 marks, 2 hours each; 50% pass; bilingual (English/Hindi).
- **Mandatory**: Probationary Officers and Clerks within 2 years of joining.

### 6.2 CAIIB (Certified Associate of IIBF)
- **Eligibility**: Passed JAIIB; bank employees.
- **Compulsory Papers (3)**: Advanced Bank Management; Bank Financial Management; Risk Management; plus 1 elective.
- **Electives**: Rural Banking, Information Technology, HRM, Treasury Management, International Banking, Insurance, Forex, etc.
- **Pattern**: 100 MCQ × 100 marks; 50% pass.
- **Mandatory**: For promotion to higher scales; promotion to MMG-II / SMG-IV in SBI requires CAIIB.

### 6.3 DBF (Diploma in Banking & Finance)
- For non-member / non-bank candidates (students, professionals).
- 4 papers: Principles & Practice of Banking; Business Mathematics & Statistics; Financial Management; Marketing of Bank Services.
- Pattern: 100 MCQ × 100 marks; 50% pass.

### 6.4 MSB (Microfinance Services and Business)
- One paper of 100 MCQs. Eligibility: IIBF member (or non-member with extra fee).
- Syllabus: Microfinance ecosystem, RBI/NABARD guidelines, SHGs, MFI regulations, micro-insurance.

### 6.5 CAIF (Certificate in Accounting & Finance)
- One paper: 100 MCQs; 60% pass (50% aggregate). Covers basic accounting, financial statements, ratio analysis.

### 6.6 CFAIIB (Chartered Financial Accountant of IIBF)
- Advanced course; eligibility: IIBF member; multiple papers on advanced accounting, auditing, taxation, financial reporting.

### 6.7 SOB (Senior Officer Business)
- Eligibility: Officers in Scale I & II in PSBs.
- Subjects: Business strategy, advanced banking, treasury, risk.

### 6.8 CoB (Certificate of Business)
- Foundation-level certificate for bank staff in business development.

### 6.9 Other Notable IIBF Certifications
- **Certified Treasury Professional (CTP)**
- **Certificate in Risk and Financial Services**
- **Certified Accounting & Audit Professional (CAAP)**
- **Certificate in IT Security**
- **Certified Information System Banker**
- **Certificate Course on Resolution of Stressed Assets (with IBC 2016)**
- **Certificate in Strategic Management & Innovations in Banking**
- **Certificate in Emerging Technologies**
- **Certificate in AML & KYC**
- **Certificate in MSME**
- **Prevention of Cyber Crimes and Fraud Management**
- **Certified Credit Professional (CCP)**
- **Certificate in Foreign Exchange Operations**
- **Certificate in Digital Banking**
- **Certificate in International Trade Finance**
- **DRA (Debt Recovery Agent)** — mandatory under RBI for recovery agents.
- **BCBF (Business Correspondent / Facilitator)** — required for BCs.

### 6.10 Examination Mode
- Online CBT (Computer Based Test) at designated centres; remote proctored for many certificate courses.

## 7. NISM (National Institute of Securities Markets) Certifications

NISM — established by SEBI under SEBI Act 1992; school of excellence in securities markets; campus at Vashi, Navi Mumbai.

### 7.1 Series I: Currency Derivatives
- **Topics**: Currency markets, exchange rate mechanisms, forwards/futures/options on currency, RBI/FEMA, NSE/BSE currency products, trading & settlement.
- **Exam**: 60 MCQ × 60 marks; 60% pass; 2 hours; ₹1,500.

### 7.2 Series V-A: Mutual Fund Distributors
- **Mandatory**: For AMFI-registered Mutual Fund distributors (ARN holders).
- **Topics**: Concept of mutual funds, scheme types, NAV, load, taxation, KYC, suitability, investor rights.
- **Exam**: 50 MCQ × 50 marks; 60% pass; ₹600 (certification valid 3 years).

### 7.3 Series VI: Depository Operations
- **Mandatory**: For persons associated with depository participants (DPs) of NSDL/CDSL.
- **Topics**: Depositories Act 1996, DP operations, dematerialisation, pledging, settlement, risk management.
- **Exam**: 100 MCQ × 100 marks; 60% pass.

### 7.4 Series VII: Securities Operations & Risk Management
- **Mandatory**: For stock-broking operations staff.
- **Topics**: Trading, clearing, settlement, risk management, margins (VaR), investor protection, compliance.
- **Exam**: 100 MCQ × 100 marks; 60% pass.

### 7.5 Series X-A: Investment Adviser (Level 1)
- **Mandatory**: For individual Investment Advisers (IA) registered with SEBI.
- **Topics**: Investment planning, asset allocation, valuation of equity/fixed income/derivatives, taxation, ethics, compliance.
- **Exam**: 100 MCQ × 100 marks; 60% pass; ₹1,500.

### 7.6 Series X-B: Investment Adviser (Level 2)
- **Mandatory**: For compliance officers of IA firms.
- **Topics**: Advanced investment advisory, regulatory requirements, compliance, risk profiling.

### 7.7 Series XV: Research Analyst
- **Mandatory**: For SEBI-registered Research Analysts.
- **Topics**: Financial statement analysis, valuation, economics, industry analysis, equity research report writing, ethics.
- **Exam**: 100 MCQ × 100 marks; 60% pass.

### 7.8 Other Notable NISM Series
- **Series II**: Registrars to an Issue & Share Transfer Agents.
- **Series III**: Issuers Compliance.
- **Series IV**: Intermediaries Compliance (Non-Fund).
- **Series V-B**: Mutual Fund (Advisors) — advanced.
- **Series VIII**: Equity Derivatives.
- **Series IX**: Merchant Banking.
- **Series XI**: Equity Sales.
- **Series XII**: Securities Markets Foundation.
- **Series XIII**: Common Derivatives.
- **Series XIV**: Internal Auditors of Stock Brokers.
- **Series XVI**: Commodity Derivatives.
- **Series XVII**: Retirement Advisors.

### 7.9 Exam Pattern (General)
- Computer-based at NISM test centres.
- Mostly 50–100 MCQs; pass: **60%** (some 70%).
- Negative marking: 25% for some series.
- Validity: 3 years (renewable through CPE).

## 9. FRM (Financial Risk Manager) by GARP

The Global Association of Risk Professionals (GARP), USA administers FRM — globally recognised certification for risk professionals in banks, asset managers, insurance, regulatory bodies.

### 9.1 Eligibility
- Bachelor's degree (or equivalent).
- No mandatory work experience, but **2 years of relevant full-time risk-related work experience** required for FRM certification.

### 9.2 Exam Pattern
Two parts:
- **Part I**: 100 MCQs; 4 hours; topics below.
- **Part II**: 80 MCQs; 4 hours; topics below.
- Both exams on the same day (May/November); computer-based at Prometric centres.
- Pass: performance against GARP's passing score; Part I must be passed before Part II.

### 9.3 Part I Syllabus (Foundations of Risk Management; Quantitative Analysis; Financial Markets & Products; Valuation & Risk Models)
- **Foundations of Risk Management**: Risk types, governance, ERM, Basel Accords (I, II, III), credit ratings, capital.
- **Quantitative Analysis**: Probability, statistics, regression, time series, simulation.
- **Financial Markets & Products**: Forwards, futures, options, swaps, bonds, equities, commodities, FX, structured products.
- **Valuation & Risk Models**: Option pricing (Binomial, BSM), VaR, expected shortfall, fixed income valuation, risk metrics.

### 9.4 Part II Syllabus (Market Risk; Credit Risk; Operational Risk; Liquidity Risk; Risk Management & Investment Management; Current Issues)
- **Market Risk**: VaR (Historical, Variance-Covariance, Monte Carlo), stress testing, expected shortfall, back-testing.
- **Credit Risk**: Default probability (PD), LGD, EAD, credit VaR, credit derivatives (CDS, CDO), counterparty risk, Basel framework.
- **Operational Risk**: OpRisk frameworks, Basel AMA/SMA, scenario analysis, KRIs, BCBS 239.
- **Liquidity Risk**: Funding liquidity, market liquidity, LCR, NSFR, liquidity buffers.
- **Risk Management & Investment Management**: Portfolio theory, CAPM, APT, performance measurement, asset allocation.
- **Current Issues in Financial Markets**: Contemporary topics (crypto, climate risk, AI/ML in risk).

### 9.5 Fees (approx.)
- Part I: USD 700 (early) / USD 1,000 (standard).
- Part II: USD 700 / USD 1,000.
- GARP membership: USD 175/year.

## 10. CFA (Chartered Financial Analyst) by CFA Institute

The CFA Institute (USA) administers the CFA Program — the gold standard for investment professionals globally.

### 10.1 Eligibility
- Bachelor's degree (or in final year), OR
- 4,000 hours of professional work experience accumulated over a minimum of 36 months, OR
- Combination of education + work experience totalling 4,000 hours.
- Valid passport required.

### 10.2 Exam Pattern (3 Levels)
| Level | Format | Duration | Topics |
|---|---|---|---|
| Level I | 180 MCQ (90 × 2 sessions, 2h 15m each) | ~4h 30m | All 10 subjects (introductory) |
| Level II | 88 item sets (vignette + 6 MCQs each) in 2 sessions | ~4h 30m | All 10 subjects (asset valuation) |
| Level III | Constructed response (essay) + 8–12 item sets in 2 sessions | ~4h 30m | All 10 subjects incl. portfolio management |

Pass marks are not fixed; CFA Institute uses MPS (Minimum Passing Score). Attempt rate limited to: 6 attempts per level, max 2 attempts per year window.

### 10.3 Detailed Syllabus (10 Topic Areas)
1. **Ethics & Professional Standards** (~15–20% weight).
2. **Quantitative Methods** — TVM, probability, hypothesis testing, regression, time series.
3. **Economics** — Micro/Macro, monetary & fiscal policy, international trade, currency.
4. **Financial Statement Analysis** — IFRS/GAAP, three statements, ratios, inventories, long-lived assets, taxes, pensions, leases, financial reporting quality.
5. **Corporate Issuers** — Corporate governance, ESG, capital allocation, leverage, working capital.
6. **Equity Investments** — Market structure, indexation, equity valuation models (DDM, FCFE, FCFF, residual income, P/E), industry & company analysis.
7. **Fixed Income** — Bond pricing, yields, duration, convexity, term structure, credit analysis, structured products.
8. **Derivatives** — Forwards, futures, options, swaps, hedging strategies, option pricing.
9. **Alternative Investments** — Real estate, private equity, venture capital, hedge funds, commodities, infrastructure.
10. **Portfolio Management & Wealth Planning** — Portfolio theory (MPT), CAPM, APT, performance evaluation, asset allocation, IPS, behavioural finance.

### 10.4 Fees (approx., 2026)
- One-time enrollment: USD 350 (waived for scholarships).
- Exam registration: USD 1,140 (early) / USD 1,290 (standard).
- Total all 3 levels: ~USD 4,000–4,500.

## 11. CPA (Chartered Public Accountant) by ICAI

Note: The user mentioned "CPA by ICAI" — in India, ICAI (Institute of Chartered Accountants of India) administers the **Chartered Accountant (CA)** course, while "CPA" typically refers to the US CPA (Uniform CPA Exam by AICPA). Below we cover both.

### 11.1 Indian Chartered Accountant (CA) Course by ICAI
- **Foundation (Entry after Class 10 / 12)**: 4 papers — Principles & Practice of Accounting, Business Laws & Business Correspondence, Business Mathematics & Logical Reasoning, Business Economics & Business and Commercial Knowledge.
- **Intermediate**: 6 papers (2 groups of 3) — Accounting, Corporate & Other Laws, Cost & Management Accounting, Taxation, Advanced Accounting, Auditing & Assurance, Financial Management & Economics for Finance, Strategic Management & Performance Management.
- **Final**: 6 papers (2 groups of 3) — Financial Reporting, Strategic Financial Management, Advanced Auditing & Professional Ethics, Corporate & Economic Laws, Strategic Cost Management & Performance Evaluation, Direct Tax Laws & International Taxation, Indirect Tax Laws (GST), Integrated Business Solutions.
- **Pattern**: Descriptive + MCQs; 100 marks per paper; 50% pass.
- **Eligibility**: 12th (Foundation) → Intermediate → 3 years Articleship → Final; or Direct Entry Route (Graduates/Postgraduates skip Foundation).
- **Articleship**: 3 years practical training under practising CA.
- **Fees**: Foundation ~₹9,000; Intermediate ~₹18,000 (Indian); Final ~₹22,000.

### 11.2 US CPA (Uniform CPA Exam by AICPA — reference)
- **Eligibility**: 150 credit hours of education (typically master's), 1-year work experience under CPA.
- **Papers (4 sections)**: Auditing & Attestation (AUD), Business Environment & Concepts (BEC), Financial Accounting & Reporting (FAR), Regulation (REG).
- **Pattern**: MCQs + Task-Based Simulations; 4 hours each; 75% pass per section.
- **Fees**: ~USD 1,500–3,500 total (varies by state).
