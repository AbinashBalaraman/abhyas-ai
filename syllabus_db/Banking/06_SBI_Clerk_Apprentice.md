## 1. SBI Clerk (Junior Associate)

### Posts
**Junior Associate (Customer Support & Sales)** – clerical cadre post in SBI branches handling customer service, cash, accounts, and sales.

### Conducting Body
**State Bank of India (SBI)** – India's largest public sector bank, headquarters at State Bank Bhavan, Nariman Point, Mumbai. ~22,500+ domestic branches, 63,580+ ATMs, ~2.45 lakh employees. Government of India holds 55.52% stake.

### Eligibility
- **Education**: Graduation in any discipline from a recognized university. Integrated degrees also eligible. (Candidates with 12th pass were eligible for some states in older notifications under backlog/SRD; current standard is graduation.)
- **Age**: 20 to 28 years (as on the cut-off date). Relaxation: SC/ST – 5 yrs; OBC (NCL) – 3 yrs; PWD (SC/ST – 15 yrs; OBC – 13 yrs; Gen – 10 yrs); Ex-servicemen, J&K Domicile – as per govt. rules.
- **Local Language**: Must be able to read, write, speak, and understand the local language of the state applied for. Candidates who studied the local language in 10th or 12th are exempted from the Language Proficiency Test (LPT).
- **Nationality**: Indian citizen (or subject of Nepal/Bhutan, Tibetan refugee with intent to settle in India, or person of Indian origin from specified countries).

### Exam Pattern (2 Tiers)

#### Tier-1: Preliminary Examination (Qualifying)
- **Total Questions**: 100
- **Total Marks**: 100
- **Duration**: 60 minutes (1 hour, sectional timing – 20 min per section)
- **Negative Marking**: 0.25 marks per wrong answer (¼ mark deduction)

| Section | No. of Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| English Language | 30 | 30 | 20 mins |
| Numerical Ability (Quantitative Aptitude) | 35 | 35 | 20 mins |
| Reasoning Ability | 35 | 35 | 20 mins |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** | **60 mins** |

Note: Tier-1 is purely qualifying; marks are not added to the final merit.

#### Tier-2: Mains Examination (Merit-Defining)
- **Total Questions**: 190
- **Total Marks**: 200
- **Duration**: 160 minutes (2 hours 40 minutes, sectional timing)
- **Negative Marking**: 0.25 marks per wrong answer (¼ mark deduction)

| Section | No. of Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| English Language | 40 | 40 | 35 mins |
| Quantitative Aptitude (Numerical Ability) | 50 | 50 | 45 mins |
| Reasoning Ability & Computer Aptitude | 50 | 60 | 45 mins |
| General/Financial Awareness | 50 | 50 | 35 mins |
| **Total** | **190** | **200** | **2h 40m** |

#### Tier-3: Language Proficiency Test (LPT) – Qualifying
- Conducted for candidates provisionally selected after Mains.
- Tests ability to read, write, speak, and understand the opted state's local language.
- **Pattern**:
  - Non-Verbal Test (Objective): 3 passages, 15 Qs – 15 marks
  - Subjective: 3 passages, 15 Qs – 15 marks
  - Verbal Test: 20 Qs
- Candidates with 10th/12th certificate showing the local language are EXEMPTED.
- Failing LPT → disqualification (no appointment).

### Detailed Syllabus

#### A. English Language (Prelims & Mains)
- Reading Comprehension (RC) – factual, literary, opinion-based
- Cloze Test (old & new pattern – including multiple fillers)
- Para Jumbles / Sentence Rearrangement
- Para Summary / Odd One Out
- Fill in the Blanks
- Sentence Connectors
- Phrase Replacement / Sentence Improvement
- Error Detection / Error Correction / Multiple Error Correction
- Word Swap / Word Rearrangement
- Vocabulary: Synonyms, Antonyms, Idioms & Phrases, Phrasal Verbs
- Spelling Errors / Misspelled Words
- Inference & Sentence Completion
- Column-based / Paragraph-based questions

#### B. Quantitative Aptitude / Numerical Ability

**Prelims Topics**:
- Simplification, Approximation
- Number Series (Missing, Wrong)
- Quadratic Equations
- Data Interpretation (Bar, Line, Pie, Tabular, Caselet, Radar/Web)
- Data Sufficiency
- Arithmetic: Average, Ratio, Percentage, Profit & Loss
- Simple & Compound Interest
- Time & Work, Pipes & Cisterns
- Speed, Distance & Time
- Problems on Ages
- Permutation & Combination, Probability
- Boat & Stream

**Mains Topics** (in addition to deeper versions of the above):
- Inequalities (Quantity 1 vs Quantity 2)

### Marking Scheme
- **Prelims**: +1 for correct, –0.25 for incorrect; no penalty for un-attempted.
- **Mains**: +1 for correct, –0.25 for incorrect (per official notification; user prompt referenced 0.50 — actual is 0.25 per the latest notification).
- **LPT**: Qualifying in nature; no negative marking.

- **Pay Scale**: ₹24,050 – 1340/3 – 28,070 – 1650/3 – 33,020 – 2000/4 – 41,020 – 2340/7 – 57,400 – 4400/1 – 61,800 – 2680/1 – 64,480
- **Starting Basic Pay** (for graduates, with 2 advance increments): **₹26,730**
- **Gross Monthly Salary**: ~₹46,329 (incl. DA 19.83%, HRA, TA ₹850, Special Allowance ₹7,083.45, Special Pay ₹1,200)

## 2. SBI Apprentice

### Posts
**Apprentice** – contract-based apprenticeship training for 1 year in SBI branches. Apprentices are NOT regular employees and are not entitled to employee benefits/perks.

### Conducting Body
**State Bank of India (SBI)** – recruits apprentices under the Apprentices Act, 1961.

### Eligibility
- **Education**: Graduation in any discipline from a recognized university/institution.
- **Age**: 20 to 28 years (as on cut-off). Standard age relaxations apply (SC/ST: 5 yrs; OBC: 3 yrs; PWD: 10–15 yrs).
- **Local Language**: Must be proficient (read/write/speak/understand) in the designated local language of the state opted for. Candidates with 10th/12th certificate of the local language are EXEMPTED from the Test of Local Language.
- **Nationality**: Indian citizen.

### Duration
**1 year (12 months)** of apprenticeship training. NOT a permanent or contractual position. SBI is not obligated to absorb apprentices into permanent employment.

### Exam Pattern

#### Online Test (Objective MCQ) – Single Stage
- **Total Questions**: 100
- **Total Marks**: 100
- **Duration**: 60 minutes (1 hour, sectional timing – 15 min per section)
- **Negative Marking**: 0.25 marks per wrong answer (1/4th deduction); no penalty for un-attempted
- **Medium**: Bilingual (English & Hindi), except English section

| Section | No. of Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reasoning Ability & Computer Aptitude | 25 | 25 | 15 mins |
| Quantitative Aptitude (Numerical Ability) | 25 | 25 | 15 mins |
| General English | 25 | 25 | 15 mins |
| General / Financial Awareness | 25 | 25 | 15 mins |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** | **1 hour** |

#### Test of Local Language (Qualifying)
- Conducted for candidates who qualify the online test (and didn't provide 10th/12th local language proof).
- **Duration**: 25 minutes
- Tests proficiency in reading, writing, speaking, and understanding the state language.
- **Final Selection Weightage**: 70% Online Test + 30% Test of Local Language

#### Document Verification
- Verification of educational certificates, identity, category, and local language documents.
- Candidates must produce original certificates.

#### Medical Fitness
- Candidates must be medically fit as per SBI's prescribed standards.

### Detailed Syllabus (similar to Clerk but lower depth)

#### A. Reasoning Ability & Computer Aptitude
- Spatial Orientation, Spatial Visualization

- **Computer Aptitude**: MS PowerPoint, MS Excel, MS Word basics, Computer Abbreviations, Modern Technology, Computer Organisation, LAN, Shortcut Keys, Input/Output Devices, Internet, Memory, Modem, Generations of Computer, WAN

#### B. Quantitative Aptitude
- Quadratic Equations
- Area (Mensuration)
- Bar Graph & Pictorial Graph, Pie Chart
- LCM & HCF
- Profit & Loss
- Simple & Compound Interest
- Average
- Time & Speed, Time & Work
- Simplification
- Investment
- Problem on Ages

#### C. General English
- Tenses
- Antonyms & Synonyms

- Reading Comprehension, Unseen Passages
- Fill in the Blanks

#### D. General / Financial Awareness

- National & International Organizations
- Latest Events, Current Affairs

- Abbreviations
- Banking & Financial Awareness (basics)

### Stipend
- **₹15,000 per month** during the 1-year apprenticeship.
- No additional allowances, bonuses, or perks.
- Apprentices are NOT SBI employees and do not get PF, gratuity, medical, or pension.
- After 1 year, the engagement ends (no automatic absorption).

### Final Selection Weightage
- **70%** weightage – Online Test (objective exam) marks
- **30%** weightage – Test of Local Language marks
- LPT is qualifying in nature, but contributes to the final merit ranking.

## 3. SBI Other Junior Posts

SBI regularly recruits across multiple junior-level posts under the same Junior Associate umbrella. Common variants include:

### A. SBI Junior Associate (Special Recruitment Drive – SRD)
- **For**: Reserved categories (SC/ST/OBC/PWD) and specific states/regions with backlog vacancies.
- **Pattern**: Same as the standard SBI Clerk (Prelims + Mains + LPT).
- **Eligibility**: Graduation (relaxations in age and category-specific).
- **Purpose**: To fill reserved/backlog vacancies and improve representation.

### B. SBI Customer Service Associate (CSA)
- **Role**: Specialised customer-service role, often within SBI's digital/call-centre/branch-customer-experience operations.
- **Pattern**: Generally aligned with SBI Clerk (Prelims + Mains) with possible sectional variations emphasising English, Computer, and customer-handling aptitude.
- **Eligibility**: Graduation, 20–28 years (relaxations as per norms).
- **Salary**: Same pay scale as Junior Associate (₹26,730 starting basic).

### C. SBI Junior Agricultural Associate (JAA)
- **Role**: Junior Associate postings in rural/semi-urban branches with a focus on agricultural lending, Kisan Credit Card, crop loans, and agri-related banking products.
- **Pattern**: SBI Clerk pattern (Prelims + Mains + LPT), sometimes with additional questions on agriculture / rural economy in General Awareness.
- **Eligibility**: Graduation (preference sometimes given to candidates with Agriculture/Horticulture background).
- **Salary**: Same as Junior Associate (₹24,050–₹64,480 scale, ~₹26,730 starting basic for graduates).

### D. SBI Junior Statistical Assistant (JSA)
- **Role**: Specialised statistical/data-handling role supporting analytics, MIS, and reporting in SBI's head office / zonal offices.
- **Pattern**: Similar to SBI Clerk with added emphasis on Quantitative Aptitude, Data Interpretation, and Statistics in the Mains exam.
- **Eligibility**: Graduation with Statistics/Mathematics/Economics (or any graduation with strong quantitative background), 20–28 years.
- **Salary**: Same clerical pay scale (₹26,730 starting basic; in-hand ~₹40,000–₹42,000).

### E. SBI Backlog Vacancies
- **For**: Filling unfilled reserved category posts (SC/ST/OBC/PWD/Ex-servicemen) from previous recruitment cycles.
- **Pattern**: Identical to the regular SBI Clerk (Prelims + Mains + LPT) with reservation-specific cut-offs.
- **Eligibility**: Same as Junior Associate (graduation, 20–28 years) with category-specific age relaxations.
- **Note**: Notifications are released as "Special Recruitment Drive" (SRD) or backlog drives when vacancies remain.

> **Note**: All "Junior" posts share the same basic pay scale (₹24,050–₹64,480 with two advance increments for graduates = ₹26,730 starting). Differentiation is in job profile and posting location. The "Specialist" officers (IT, HR, Law, MMGS-II scale) are a separate SBI SO recruitment, NOT covered here.

## 5. Preparation Strategy (Combined)

### Tips
1. **English first** – Easy to score; practise RC, Cloze, Para Jumbles daily.
2. **Quant & Reasoning** – Master basics (percentages, ratios, puzzles) before mocks.
3. **GA/FA** – Read daily current affairs (last 6 months for Mains); focus on RBI, SBI, Budget, Banking terms.
4. **Computer Aptitude** – Cover basics (MS Office, networking, generations, shortcuts).
5. **Mock Tests** – Take at least 30–50 full mocks before the exam; analyse each one.
6. **Time Management** – Sectional timing is strict; practise timed sectional tests.
7. **Previous Year Papers** – Last 5 years are gold for understanding question patterns.
