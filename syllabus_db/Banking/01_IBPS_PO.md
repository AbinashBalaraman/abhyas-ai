# IBPS PO (Institute of Banking Personnel Selection – Probationary Officer)

## 1. Overview

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Full Name** | IBPS Probationary Officer / Management Trainee (CRP PO/MT) |
| **Conducting Body** | Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) — an autonomous body under the **Ministry of Finance, Government of India**, established **19 December 1975**; HQ **Mumbai** |
| **Frequency** | Annual (Common Recruitment Process – CRP PO/MT) |
| **Post Grade** | Junior Management Grade Scale – I (JMGS-I) |
| **Mode of Exam** | Online (CBT) for Prelims, Mains & Descriptive |
| **Language** | English & Hindi (English Language section must be attempted in English) |

**Participating Banks (12 PSU Banks – all Nationalised Banks as on May 2026):**
1. State Bank of India (SBI)
2. Punjab National Bank (PNB)
3. Bank of Baroda (BOB)

12. Punjab & Sind Bank (PSB)

*Note: IDBI Bank is now categorised as a private sector bank (LIC stake, Jan 2019) and is not part of IBPS CRP PO/MT.*

---

## 2. Posts Offered

**Probationary Officer (PO) / Management Trainee (MT)** in participating public sector banks at the rank of **Junior Management Grade Scale-I (JMGS-I)**. After successful completion of the 2-year probation period, candidates are absorbed as Assistant Managers and progress through MMGS-II → MMGS-III → SMGS-IV → Top Executive Grade (TEG).

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

### Educational Qualification
- **Bachelor's Degree** in any discipline from a **recognised University** (or equivalent qualification recognised by the Central Government).
- Must possess a valid mark-sheet / degree certificate proving they are a graduate on the day of registration and also on the day of document verification.
- **Computer Literacy:** Certificate / Diploma in Computer Operation / IT / Computer Science OR working knowledge of computers is mandatory.

### Age Limit (as on the cut-off date in the notification)
- **Minimum:** 20 years
- **Maximum:** 30 years
- Candidate must be born between the dates specified (e.g. for 2026: 02.08.1996 to 01.08.2005)

### Age Relaxation
| Category | Upper Age Relaxation |
|---|---|
| SC / ST | 5 years |
| OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) | 3 years |
| PWD (SC/ST) | 15 years |
| PWD (OBC) | 13 years |
| PWD (General/EWS) | 10 years |
| Ex-Servicemen / Commissioned Officers | 5 years |
| Persons ordinarily domiciled in J&K (1980-89) | 5 years |
| Widows / Divorced Women | 9 years |
| Affected persons of 1984 riots | 5 years |
| Regular employees of participating banks | 5–10 years |

## 4. Exam Pattern (3 Tiers)

### Tier 1 – Preliminary Examination (Qualifying)
- **Total:** 100 Questions, 100 Marks, 60 Minutes
- **Negative Marking:** 0.25 per wrong answer
- **Sectional Time Limit:** 20 minutes per section (cannot switch)
- Marks are NOT counted for final merit — only for qualifying to Mains.

| Section | Questions | Marks | Time | Medium |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| English Language | 30 | 30 | 20 min | English |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 35 | 30 | 20 min | English & Hindi |
| Reasoning Ability | 35 | 40 | 20 min | English & Hindi |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** | **60 min** | |

### Tier 2 – Mains Examination (Counts for Merit)
- **Objective:** 145 Qs / 200 Marks / 160 min
- **Descriptive:** 2 Qs / 25 Marks / 30 min
- **Total Mains Marks:** 225

| Section | Questions | Marks | Time | Medium |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reasoning Ability & Computer Aptitude | 40 | 60 | 50 min | Eng & Hindi |
| General / Economy / Banking Awareness (incl. Digital & Financial, RBI) | 35 | 50 | 25 min | Eng & Hindi |
| English Language | 35 | 40 | 40 min | English |
| Data Analysis & Interpretation | 35 | 50 | 45 min | Eng & Hindi |
| **Objective Total** | **145** | **200** | **160 min** | |
| **Descriptive – Essay & Letter (English)** | **2** | **25** | **30 min** | English |
| **Grand Total Mains** | **147** | **225** | **190 min** | |

### Tier 3 – Interview
- **Total:** 100 marks
- **Qualifying (General/EWS):** 40% (40 marks)
- **Qualifying (SC/ST/OBC/PWBD):** 35% (35 marks)
- Conducted State-wise / UT-wise by the participating organisation, coordinated by Nodal Bank in each state/UT with help of IBPS.
- **Final Merit Weightage:** Mains 80% + Interview 20%

### Final Selection
- Candidates must qualify in Mains (sectional + overall cut-off) AND Interview.
- Final merit list is based on combined Mains (80%) + Interview (20%) score.
- Bank allotment is based on merit rank and candidate's bank preferences.

## 5. Detailed Syllabus (Topic-wise)

### A. English Language
- Reading Comprehension (RC) – Economy, Banking, Social issues, Science
- Cloze Test (New & Old pattern)
- Fill in the Blanks (single/double)
- Sentence Errors / Error Spotting / Error Detection
- Sentence Correction / Sentence Improvement
- Para Jumbles / Sentence Rearrangement
- Para Completion / Paragraph Completion
- Vocabulary: Synonyms, Antonyms, One Word Substitution
- Word Usage, Word-Swap, Column-based Spelling
- Idioms & Phrases
- Phrase Replacement
- Match the Following / Sentence Connectors

### B. Quantitative Aptitude / Numerical Ability
- Number System, HCF, LCM
- Simplification & Approximation
- Surds & Indices, Quadratic Equations
- Percentage, Profit & Loss, Discount
- Simple Interest & Compound Interest
- Ratio & Proportion, Mixture & Alligation
- Average, Age-based problems
- Time & Work, Pipes & Cisterns
- Time, Speed & Distance, Trains, Boats & Streams
- Mensuration (Cylinder, Cone, Sphere, Cuboid)
- Geometry, Trigonometry (basic)
- Permutation, Combination & Probability
- Data Interpretation: Tables, Bar Graphs, Pie Charts, Line Charts, Caselets, Radar, Data Sufficiency
- Number Series (Missing/Wrong)

### C. Reasoning Ability
- Puzzles (Linear, Circular, Square, Box-based, Scheduling, Floor, Day/Month, etc.)
- Seating Arrangement (Linear, Circular, Double Row-up)
- Syllogism (Direct, Reverse, Possibility)
- Inequalities / Code Inequalities
- Blood Relations
- Direction & Distance
- Order & Ranking, Alpha-Numeric Series
- Coding-Decoding (Fictitious Language, Letter shifting)
- Input-Output (Machine-based)
- Statement & Conclusion, Statement & Assumption
- Statement & Course of Action
- Critical Reasoning (Strong/Weak Argument, Cause & Effect)
- Data Sufficiency
- Decision Making

### D. General / Economy / Banking Awareness (incl. Digital & Financial)
- **Banking History:** Nationalisation of banks (1969, 1980), Bank mergers
- **RBI Functions:** Monetary Policy, Repo, Reverse Repo, MSF, Bank Rate
- **Banking Terms / Instruments:** NEFT, RTGS, IMPS, UPI, SWIFT, AEPS, BBPS, NFS
- **Banking Ratios:** CRR, SLR, NPA, CAR/CRAR, Priority Sector Lending
- **Interest Rates:** Repo, Reverse Repo, MCLR, Base Rate, BPLR, Marginal Standing Facility
- **Banking Reforms:** Narasimham Committee I & II, Chavan Committee, Khan Committee
- **Indian Economy:** Budget, Economic Survey, Five Year Plans, NITI Aayog, GST, Inflation (CPI, WPI, CPI-IW)
- **International Organisations:** IMF, World Bank, WTO, ADB, AIIB, NDB (BRICS Bank), BIS
- **Financial Institutions:** RBI, NABARD, SEBI, SIDBI, NHB, EXIM Bank, IRDAI, PFRDA
- **Capital & Money Market:** Shares, Debentures, Bonds, Mutual Funds, ETF
- **Insurance & Pension:** IRDA, LIC, NPS, APY
- **Digital Banking & Fintech:** UPI, BHIM, AEPS, CBDC, Digital Lending
- **Government Schemes:** Jan Dhan Yojana, MUDRA, PMJDY, PMSBY, PMJJBY, APY, Stand-Up India
- **Static GK:** Country, Capital, Currency, Awards, Books, Important Days
- **Current Affairs (last 6–8 months):** National & International, Banking, Economy, Sports, Awards

### E. Computer Aptitude (Mains)
- History & Generations of Computers
- Input / Output Devices
- Memory (RAM, ROM, Cache, Virtual)
- Hardware vs Software
- Operating Systems
- MS Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint)
- Internet, Web Browsers, Email
- Networking Basics (LAN, WAN, MAN, PAN, Topology)
- DBMS (Database Management System) – basics
- Shortcut Keys
- Cyber Security, Malware, Virus, Firewall
- Computer Abbreviations

### F. Descriptive Test (Letter & Essay – 25 marks)
- **Essay Topics:** Economic & Social Issues, Emerging Trends in Banking, Technology, Current Events, Ethics
- **Letter Writing:** Formal, Informal, to Editor, to Bank Manager, Complaint, Application
- Word Limit: ~150-200 words (Essay) and ~150 words (Letter)
- Tested in **English** only

## 9. Preparation Strategy

1. **Know the syllabus & exam pattern** thoroughly (revised in 2026).
2. **Build conceptual clarity** in Quant & Reasoning fundamentals.
3. **Daily 1 Mock Test + 1 Sectional Test** after completing basics.
4. **Analyse mocks** – track accuracy, time spent per question, silly mistakes.
5. **Daily Current Affairs & Banking Awareness** – 30 min/day, 6-month coverage.
6. **Descriptive practice** – 1 essay + 1 letter/week from Mains stage onwards.
7. **Improve typing speed** (35–40 WPM) for the Descriptive paper.
8. **Revise formulas, vocabulary, GK capsules** weekly.

## 10. Career Path & Promotion Hierarchy

```
Probationary Officer (PO) – JMGS-I
        ↓ (2-yr probation / confirmation)
Assistant Manager – JMGS-I
        ↓
Manager – MMGS-II
        ↓
Senior Manager – MMGS-III
        ↓
Chief Manager – SMGS-IV
        ↓
Assistant General Manager (AGM) – TEG-V
        ↓
Deputy General Manager (DGM) – TEG-VI
        ↓
General Manager (GM) – SE-VII
        ↓
Executive Director (ED) – Top Executive Grade
        ↓
Chairman & Managing Director (CMD)
```

Probation period: 2 years. Confirmation subject to passing a confirmation test / satisfactory performance review.
