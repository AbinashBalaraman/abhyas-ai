# IBPS RRB (Regional Rural Banks) Examination 2026

## Overview

The **Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS)** is a central recruitment agency under the Ministry of Finance, Government of India, established on **19 December 1975**, headquartered in **Mumbai**. IBPS conducts the Common Recruitment Process (CRP) for Regional Rural Banks (RRBs) across India for four key posts: Office Assistant (Multipurpose), Officer Scale I, II, and III.

- **Conducting Body:** Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS)
- **Chairman:** Shri Rajneesh Karnatak
- **Participating Banks:** 43 Regional Rural Banks across India
- **Coordinated with:** NABARD (for interview process)
- **Official Website:** https://www.ibps.in
- **Selection Process:** Online Examination (Prelims/Mains) + Interview (for Officer posts)
- **Medium of Exam:** English and Hindi
- **Negative Marking:** 0.25 marks deducted for each wrong answer
- **Sectional Cut-off:** Applicable in all stages

## 1. IBPS RRB Office Assistant (Clerk) - Multipurpose

### Posts
- **Office Assistant (Multipurpose)** in 43 RRBs across India
- Equivalent to Clerk post in National Public Sector Banks

### Eligibility
- **Educational Qualification:** Bachelor's Degree in any discipline from a recognized university
- **Age Limit:** 18 to 28 years (age relaxation for reserved categories as per government norms)
- **Language Proficiency:** Proficiency in the local language of the state/region applied for
- **Computer Knowledge:** Certificate/Diploma in computer operations is desirable

### Exam Pattern

#### Preliminary Examination
| S.No | Section | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|------|---------|-----------|-------|----------|
| 1 | Reasoning | 40 | 40 | 45 minutes (composite) |
| 2 | Numerical Ability | 40 | 40 | - |
| **Total** | | **80** | **80** | **45 min** |

#### Mains Examination
| S.No | Section | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|------|---------|-----------|-------|----------|
| 1 | Reasoning | 40 | 50 | 120 minutes (composite) |
| 2 | English/Hindi Language | 40 | 40 | - |
| 3 | Numerical Ability | 40 | 50 | - |
| 4 | General Awareness | 40 | 40 | - |
| 5 | Computer Knowledge | 40 | 20 | - |
| **Total** | | **200** | **200** | **120 min** |

**Note:** No interview for Office Assistant post. Final selection is based on Mains exam marks only.

### Detailed Syllabus

#### Reasoning Ability
- Odd One Out, Coding-Decoding, Blood Relation
- Causes and Effects, Decision Making, Assertion and Reason
- Analogy, Series Test, Direction Test
- Statement and Assumption, Statement and Conclusion
- Inequalities, Syllogism, Alphabet Test
- Ranking and Time, Sitting Arrangements
- Figure Series, Word Formation, Puzzles

#### Numerical Ability
- Number System, Decimal Fractions, Simplification
- Time and Work, Age Problems, Percentage
- Permutation and Combination, LCM, HCF
- Simple Interest, Compound Interest, Time and Distance
- Ratio and Proportion, Probability, Profit and Loss
- Average, Partnership, Data Interpretation
- Quadratic Equations, Approximation, Number Series

#### English Language
- Reading Comprehension (Conventional and Comprehensive)
- Phrase Rearrangement, Word Swap
- Match the Column, Connectors, Starters
- Fill in the Blanks, Word Usage
- Sentence-based Error Detection
- Phrase Replacement, Spelling Errors
- Idioms and Phrases, Cloze Test
- Sentence Rearrangement, One-word Substitution
- Error Detection, Antonyms, Synonyms

#### Hindi Language (अपठित गद्यांश)
- समानार्थक और विलोम शब्द
- रिक्त स्थान, गद्यांश में रिक्त स्थानों की पूर्ति
- वाक्य में त्रुटि, शब्द त्रुटी या वर्तनी त्रुटी
- वाक्यांश के लिए एक शब्द
- मुहावरे - लोकोक्तियाँ

#### General Awareness (with Rural Banking focus)
- India and International Current Affairs
- Banking Awareness (RBI, NABARD, RRB schemes)
- Countries and Currencies
- National Parks and Wildlife Sanctuaries
- Banking Terms and Abbreviations, Banking History
- Sports, Books and Authors, Agriculture
- Fiscal Policies, Union Budget
- Government Schemes and Policies (especially rural/agrarian)
- State Current Affairs

#### Computer Knowledge
- Computer Fundamentals, History, Generations
- Computer Abbreviations, Shortcut Keys
- Software and Hardware Fundamentals
- Networking, Internet, MS Office
- Database Management, Number System
- Computer Languages, Security Tools
- Input and Output Devices

### Salary Structure
- **Basic Pay:** Rs 19,000 - 22,000 (initial)
- **Gross Salary (with allowances):** Approximately Rs 26,000 - 32,000 per month
- **Allowances:** Dearness Allowance (DA), House Rent Allowance (HRA), Special Allowance, Medical Benefits, Travel Allowance
- **Pay Scale:** Rs 11,765 - 31,540 (approximate)
- **Career Growth:** Promotion to Officer cadre through internal exams after 2-3 years of service

## 2. IBPS RRB Officer Scale I (PO Equivalent)

### Posts
- **Officer Scale I (Assistant Manager)** in Regional Rural Banks
- Equivalent to Probationary Officer (PO) in National Public Sector Banks

### Eligibility
- **Educational Qualification:** Bachelor's Degree in any discipline from a recognized university (50% marks for some RRBs)
- **Age Limit:** 18 to 30 years (age relaxation for reserved categories)
- **Language Proficiency:** Proficiency in the local language of the state applied for
- **Computer Literacy:** Working knowledge of computer systems

### Exam Pattern

#### Preliminary Examination
| S.No | Section | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|------|---------|-----------|-------|----------|
| 1 | Reasoning | 40 | 40 | 45 minutes (composite) |
| 2 | Quantitative Aptitude | 40 | 40 | - |
| **Total** | | **80** | **80** | **45 min** |

#### Mains Examination
| S.No | Section | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|------|---------|-----------|-------|----------|
| 1 | Reasoning | 40 | 50 | 120 minutes (composite) |
| 2 | General Awareness | 40 | 40 | - |
| 3 | Quantitative Aptitude | 50 | 50 | - |
| 4 | English/Hindi Language | 40 | 40 | - |
| 5 | Computer Knowledge | 40 | 20 | - |
| **Total** | | **200-210** | **200** | **120 min** |

#### Interview
- **Total Marks:** 100
- **Conducted by:** Nodal Regional Rural Bank with NABARD assistance
- **Weightage:** Mains (80%) + Interview (20%) approximately
- **Minimum Qualifying Marks:** As per extant guidelines (40% for General, 35% for SC/ST/OBC)

### Detailed Syllabus

#### Reasoning Ability (Same as Clerk Mains but with higher difficulty)
- All reasoning topics covered in Clerk Mains
- Emphasis on Complex Puzzles, Seating Arrangements
- Data Sufficiency (Two or Three statements)
- Input-Output, Logical Reasoning variants

#### Quantitative Aptitude (Same as Clerk + Advanced topics)
- All topics covered in Clerk Mains
- Advanced Data Interpretation (Radar, Caselet, Arithmetic DI)
- Statement-based Quadratic Equations
- Three Statement Data Sufficiency
- Basic Algebra, Mensuration (2D and 3D)

#### General Awareness (Focus on Rural Banking, NABARD, RRB schemes)
- Current Affairs (National + International)
- Banking Awareness with special focus on RRB and NABARD
- Union Budget, Economic Survey
- Government Schemes (MUDRA, Jan Dhan, PM-KISAN)
- Agricultural Credit, Priority Sector Lending
- RBI Monetary Policy
- State Current Affairs

#### English/Hindi Language
- Same as Clerk Mains with slightly advanced grammar
- More Reading Comprehension passages

#### Computer Knowledge
- Same as Clerk Mains syllabus

### Salary Structure
- **Basic Pay:** Rs 36,000 - 44,000 (initial)
- **Gross Salary (with allowances):** Approximately Rs 45,000 - 55,000 per month
- **Pay Scale:** Rs 23,700 - 42,020 (approximate)
- **Allowances:** DA, HRA, Special Allowance, Medical, Travel, Newspaper Allowance
- **Probation Period:** 2 years
- **Career Growth:** Officer Scale II → Scale III → Branch Manager → Senior Management

- **Marketing Officer**

- **HR/Personnel Officer**

## 4. IBPS RRB Officer Scale III

### Posts
- **Officer Scale III (Senior Manager)** in Regional Rural Banks
- Equivalent to Senior Manager post in National Public Sector Banks
- Senior management position handling branch/regional operations

### Eligibility
- **Educational Qualification:** Bachelor's Degree in any discipline with minimum 50% marks from a recognized university
- **Age Limit:** 21 to 40 years (relaxation for reserved categories)
- **Experience:** Minimum 5 years experience as an Officer in a Bank or Financial Institution
- **Language Proficiency:** Working knowledge of local language

### Exam Pattern

#### Single Online Examination
| S.No | Section | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|------|---------|-----------|-------|----------|
| 1 | Reasoning | 40 | 50 | 2 hours (composite) |
| 2 | Quantitative Aptitude & Data Interpretation | 40 | 50 | - |
| 3 | Financial Awareness | 40 | 40 | - |
| 4 | English/Hindi Language | 40 | 40 | - |
| 5 | Computer Knowledge | 40 | 20 | - |
| **Total** | | **200** | **200** | **120 min** |

#### Interview
- **Total Marks:** 100
- **Conducted by:** Nodal RRB with NABARD
- **Weightage:** Mains (80%) + Interview (20%)
- **Focus Areas:** Leadership, Banking Knowledge, Decision-making, Management

### Detailed Syllabus

#### Reasoning
- All topics from Scale I + II with senior management difficulty
- Complex Puzzles, Multi-variable problems
- Critical Reasoning, Logical Deduction
- Data Interpretation with analytical reasoning

#### Quantitative Aptitude & Data Interpretation
- Advanced arithmetic and mensuration
- Complex Data Interpretation (Mixed, Caselet, Radar)
- Probability, Statistics, Advanced Algebra
- Time-Speed-Distance variants, Permutations

#### Financial Awareness
- Advanced Banking and Financial Awareness
- Monetary Policy, RBI Functions
- International Finance, Forex
- Capital Markets, Derivatives
- BASEL Norms, Risk Management
- Banking Reforms, NPAs Resolution
- Priority Sector Lending, Agricultural Finance

#### English/Hindi Language
- Advanced Reading Comprehension
- Business Writing, Email Writing
- Critical Reasoning passages
- Advanced Grammar, Vocabulary

#### Computer Knowledge
- All basics + advanced topics
- Database Management Systems
- Networking, Security
- Emerging Technologies (AI, ML basics)

### Salary Structure
- **Basic Pay:** Rs 55,000+ (initial)
- **Gross Salary (with allowances):** Approximately Rs 70,000 - 85,000 per month
- **Pay Scale:** Rs 42,020 - 51,490 (approximate)
- **Allowances:** DA, HRA, Special Allowance, Medical, Conveyance, Fuel
- **Additional Benefits:** Car loan at concessional rates, Housing loan benefits
- **Career Growth:** Chief Manager → Assistant General Manager → General Manager

## 5. Marking Scheme (Common for All Posts)

| Component | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Negative Marking** | 0.25 marks deducted for each wrong answer |
| **Unattempted Questions** | No penalty (no marks added or deducted) |
| **Sectional Cut-off** | Applicable for each section in Prelims and Mains |
| **Overall Cut-off** | Candidates must clear both sectional and overall cut-offs |
| **Scoring** | 1 mark per correct answer (general rule; some sections have higher weightage) |
| **Mode of Exam** | Online Computer-Based Test (CBT) |
| **Medium** | English and Hindi (except English language section) |
| **Normalization** | Score normalization applied if exam is held in multiple shifts |
| **No. of Attempts** | Limited attempts as per IBPS guidelines |

## Key Differences: IBPS RRB vs Other Bank Exams

| Feature | IBPS RRB | IBPS PO/Clerk |
|---------|----------|---------------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate | Higher |
