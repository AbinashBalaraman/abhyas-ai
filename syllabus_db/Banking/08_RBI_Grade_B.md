# RBI Grade B (Officer Grade 'B' - Direct Recruitment) - Complete Study Guide

## 1. Overview

- **Full Name**: Reserve Bank of India (RBI) - Officer Grade 'B' (Direct Recruitment / DR)
- **Conducting Body**: Reserve Bank of India (RBI) - India's Central Bank
- **Establishment**: RBI established on 1 April 1935 under the RBI Act, 1934; nationalised on 1 January 1949
- **Headquarters**: Mumbai, Maharashtra
- **Current Governor**: Sanjay Malhotra (IAS)
- **Frequency**: Annually (or as per RBI requirement)
- **Selection Process**: Phase-I (Prelims) → Phase-II (Mains) → Phase-III (Interview)
- **Examination Mode**: Online Computer-Based Test (CBT)
- **Negative Marking**: 0.25 marks deducted per wrong answer
- **Languages**: Bilingual (Hindi & English), except English Language test

---

## 2. Posts Offered

- **Officer Grade 'B' (DR) - General**: Generalist banking/regulatory role
- **Officer Grade 'B' (DR) - DEPR**: Department of Economic and Policy Research
- **Officer Grade 'B' (DR) - DSIM**: Department of Statistics and Information Management

The General stream is the most popular. The position is one of the most prestigious entry-level officer roles in India's financial regulatory ecosystem.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

### Educational Qualifications (as on date of application)

**Option 1**: Master's Degree in any discipline from a recognised university
- **55% marks** for UR/OBC candidates
- **50% marks** for SC/ST/PwBD candidates

**Option 2**: Bachelor's Degree in Engineering (B.E./B.Tech) with **60% marks**

**Option 3**: PhD in any discipline

**Option 4**: Professional qualifications: **CA / ICWA / CS / CFA**

### Age Limit (as on cut-off date)
- **Minimum**: 21 years
- **Maximum**: 30 years
- **Age Relaxation**:
  - SC/ST: 5 years
  - OBC (Non-Creamy Layer): 3 years
  - PwBD: 10 years (General), 13 years (OBC), 15 years (SC/ST)
  - Ex-Servicemen: As per government norms
  - RBI staff: Additional relaxation

### Nationality
- Indian citizen, or
- Subject of Nepal/Bhutan, or
- Tibetan refugee (with intent to permanently settle in India), or
- Person of Indian Origin (PIO) who migrated from specified countries

## 4. Exam Pattern (3 Phases)

### Phase-I: Preliminary Examination (Qualifying)
- **Mode**: Online (CBT)
- **Total Questions**: 200 MCQs
- **Total Marks**: 200
- **Duration**: 120 minutes (with sectional timing)
- **Negative Marking**: 0.25 per wrong answer
- **Cut-off**: Must clear both sectional AND overall cut-off

| Section | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---------|-----------|-------|----------|
| General Awareness | 80 | 80 | 25 min |
| English Language | 30 | 30 | 25 min |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 30 | 30 | 25 min |
| Reasoning | 60 | 60 | 45 min |
| **Total** | **200** | **200** | **120 min** |

### Phase-II: Mains Examination (Merit)
- **Mode**: Online (CBT) - Descriptive answers must be typed
- **Total Marks**: 300
- **Three Papers** of 100 marks each

#### Paper-I: Economic & Social Issues (ESI) - 100 Marks (3 Hours)
- **Objective Section**: 30 MCQs × 50 marks (30 min)
- **Descriptive Section**: 6 questions, attempt any 4 (15 marks each × 4 = 60 → 50 marks) (90 min)

#### Paper-II: English (Writing Skills) - 100 Marks (90 Minutes)
- **Descriptive only**: 3 questions (Precis, Reading Comprehension, Essay)

#### Paper-III: Finance & Management (F&M) - 100 Marks (3 Hours)
- **Objective Section**: 30 MCQs × 50 marks (30 min)
- **Descriptive Section**: 6 questions, attempt any 4 (15 marks each × 4 = 60 → 50 marks) (90 min)

### Phase-III: Interview - 75 Marks
- Personality test assessing communication, decision-making, and subject knowledge
- Conducted at RBI offices

## 5. Detailed Syllabus - Phase-I (Prelims)

### 5.1 General Awareness (80 Qs / 80 Marks)
- **Static GK**: Indian History (Ancient, Medieval, Modern), Geography (India & World), Indian Polity & Constitution
- **Indian Economy**: Economic History, Reforms, Five-Year Plans, Economic Survey, Union Budget
- **Science**: General Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology), Defence & Space
- **Current Affairs**: National & International events, Awards, Summits, Appointments, Sports
- **Banking Awareness**: RBI history, functions, monetary policy, financial terms
- **National & International Institutions**: IMF, World Bank, WTO, ADB, UN bodies, BIS, SAARC, BRICS
- **Banking Terms**: CRR, SLR, Repo, Reverse Repo, MCLR, NPA, Basel Norms

### 5.2 English Language (30 Qs / 30 Marks)
- Reading Comprehension
- Cloze Test
- Fill in the Blanks (single & double fillers)
- Vocabulary (Synonyms, Antonyms, Idioms, Phrases)
- Grammar (Error Spotting, Sentence Correction)
- Para Jumbles / Sentence Rearrangement
- Sentence Improvement / Framing
- Active-Passive, Direct-Indirect Speech

### 5.3 Quantitative Aptitude (30 Qs / 30 Marks)
- **Data Interpretation**: Tables, Bar, Pie, Line charts, Caselets
- **Number System**: HCF, LCM, Divisibility, Remainders
- **Simplification & Approximation**
- **Arithmetic**: Ratio & Proportion, Percentage, Average, Profit-Loss, SI/CI, Time & Work, Speed-Distance-Time, Mixture-Alligation, Pipes & Cisterns
- **Algebra**: Linear & Quadratic equations, Inequalities
- **Geometry & Mensuration**: Triangles, Circles, Quadrilaterals, Volumes, Surface Area
- **Trigonometry**: Basic identities, Height & Distance
- **Probability & Permutation-Combination**

### 5.4 Reasoning (60 Qs / 60 Marks)
- **Puzzles**: Floor-based, Box-based, Scheduling, Day-based
- **Seating Arrangement**: Linear, Circular, Square
- **Syllogism**: Direct, Reverse, Possibility
- **Coding-Decoding**
- **Blood Relations**
- **Direction & Distance**
- **Inequalities**: Direct, Coded
- **Order & Ranking**
- **Machine Input-Output**
- **Data Sufficiency**
- **Alphanumeric & Number Series**
- **Verbal Reasoning**: Statement & Assumptions, Arguments, Conclusions, Cause & Effect

## 6. Detailed Syllabus - Phase-II (Mains)

### 6.1 Paper-I: Economic & Social Issues (ESI)

#### A. Economics

**Microeconomics**:
- Demand & Supply analysis
- Consumer theory (utility, indifference curves)
- Production theory (production functions, returns to scale)
- Cost curves (short-run & long-run)
- Market structures (Perfect competition, Monopoly, Oligopoly, Monopolistic competition)
- Pricing under different market conditions
- Welfare economics (Pareto optimality)

**Macroeconomics**:
- National Income accounting (GDP, GNP, NNP, methods of measurement)
- Monetary Policy (Repo, Reverse Repo, CRR, SLR, MSF, LAF, Open Market Operations)
- Fiscal Policy (Revenue expenditure, Capital expenditure, FRBM Act)
- Inflation (WPI, CPI, types - demand-pull, cost-push)
- Money Market (call money, T-bills, CDs, CPs)
- Capital Market (primary, secondary - equity, debt)
- RBI functions and role
- Public Finance (taxation, public debt, fiscal deficit)
- Multiplier, Accelerator, IS-LM Model

**International Trade & Finance**:
- Theories of international trade (Absolute & Comparative Advantage)
- Balance of Payments (Current Account, Capital Account)
- Exchange rate determination (Fixed, Flexible, Managed)
- FOREX market
- IMF, World Bank, ADB, WTO, BIS

**Indian Economy**:
- Economic History (Pre and Post-Independence)
- Planning in India
- Liberalization, Privatization, Globalization (LPG Reforms - 1991)
- Industrial Policy
- Agricultural sector (Green Revolution, MSP, PDS)
- Services sector growth
- Indian Banking System & RBI role
- Economic Survey & Union Budget highlights
- Sustainable Development & Environmental issues
- Demographic trends, Poverty, Unemployment

#### B. Social Issues
- Poverty & Inequality (measurement - Lorenz curve, Gini coefficient)
- Unemployment (types, measurement)
- Inflation impact on society
- Population dynamics
- Social Justice, Affirmative Action
- Education, Health, Gender issues
- Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)
- Government welfare schemes (MGNREGA, PDS, etc.)

### 6.2 Paper-II: English (Writing Skills) - 3 Questions
- **Essay Writing** (150-200 words typically): Current affairs, economic, social topics
- **Precis Writing**: Summarisation of given passage
- **Reading Comprehension**: Passage-based questions
- **Business/Office Correspondence**: Letters, memos, reports
- Focus: Expression, grammar, vocabulary, coherence, comprehension

### 6.3 Paper-III: Finance & Management (F&M)

#### A. Finance

**Financial System & Markets**:
- Structure of Indian Financial System
- Banking System in India (Scheduled, Commercial, Cooperative, RRBs, SFBs, PBs, NBFCs)
- RBI functions, role, monetary policy
- Financial Institutions: SIDBI, EXIM Bank, NABARD, NHB, NABFID, DICGC
- Money Market instruments & functions
- Capital Market (Primary & Secondary)
- Forex Market, Bond Market, Equity Market
- Recent developments in financial markets

**Financial Concepts**:
- Inflation & its management
- Monetary & Fiscal Policy
- Public-Private Partnership (PPP)
- Corporate Governance in banking
- Union Budget concepts
- Accounting basics (debit/credit, balance sheet, P&L)
- Financial Inclusion (PMJDY, Jan Dhan, MUDRA)
- Digital Payments (UPI, AePS, IMPS, NEFT, RTGS)

**International Finance**:
- Global financial system & India's linkages
- International Banking
- Exchange rate management
- BOP management

**Risk Management**:
- Types of financial risks (credit, market, operational, liquidity)
- Basel Norms (I, II, III)
- Derivatives basics (Forwards, Futures, Options, Swaps)
- Asset-Liability Management (ALM)
- NPA classification & resolution (SARFAESI, IBC)

**Insurance**:
- Types: Life, General, Health
- IRDAI role
- Principles of insurance

#### B. Management

**Management Concepts**:
- Introduction & Evolution of Management Thought
- Planning, Organizing, Staffing, Directing, Controlling
- Managerial Roles (Mintzberg)
- Systems Approach
- Nudge Theory

**Organizational Behavior**:
- Personality (Big Five Model)
- Perception, Reinforcement
- Motivation theories (Maslow, Herzberg, Alderfer, McClelland, Vroom)
- Leadership (Trait, Behavioural, Contingency, Charismatic, Transactional, Transformational)
- Emotional Intelligence
- Interpersonal relationships (Transactional Analysis, Johari Window)
- Conflict Management
- Organizational Change & Development

**Communication**:
- Communication Process & Channels (Oral/Written, Verbal/Non-verbal)
- Barriers to Communication
- Upward, Downward, Lateral Communication
- Role of IT in Communication

**Human Resource Management (HRM)**:
- Recruitment, Selection, Training & Development
- Performance Appraisal
- Compensation Management
- Industrial Relations

**Marketing Management**:
- Marketing Mix (4Ps, 7Ps)
- Consumer Behaviour
- Market Segmentation, Targeting, Positioning
- Brand Management
- Digital Marketing

**Strategic Management**:
- SWOT, PESTLE, Porter's Five Forces
- Corporate Strategy formulation
- BCG Matrix, Ansoff Matrix

**Corporate Governance & Ethics**:
- Ethical theories (Utilitarianism, Rights & Duties, Justice & Fairness)
- Code of Ethics & Conduct

## 7. Final Selection Process

The final merit list is prepared as follows:

| Component | Marks | Weightage |
|-----------|-------|-----------|
| Phase-II (Mains) - Paper I (ESI) | 100 | Considered for Merit |
| Phase-II (Mains) - Paper II (English) | 100 | Considered for Merit |
| Phase-II (Mains) - Paper III (F&M) | 100 | Considered for Merit |
| Phase-III (Interview) | 75 | Considered for Merit |
| **Phase-I (Prelims)** | 200 | **Qualifying only** |

- Phase-I is **qualifying in nature** (marks not added to merit)
- Phase-II (300 marks) + Interview (75 marks) = **Combined Final Merit**
- Candidates must clear sectional and overall cut-offs in each phase
- Final selection is based on combined Phase-II + Interview score
- Category-wise merit list is prepared

## Important Tips for Preparation

1. **Phase-I is qualifying** - Focus on sectional cut-offs and overall score
2. **Read The Hindu / Indian Express daily** - Both ESI and English benefit
3. **RBI publications are gold** - Annual Report, Monetary Policy, Financial Stability Report
4. **Current Affairs** - Last 6-8 months, focus on economy, banking, finance
5. **Practice descriptive writing** - Essay, Precis, RC regularly
6. **Take weekly mock tests** - Both Phase-I and Phase-II
7. **Interview preparation** - Start from beginning; stay updated on current economic issues
8. **Time management** - Build stamina for 3-hour papers
9. **NCERT basics** - Build strong foundation in economics
10. **Group discussion & communication** - Work on interview skills

- **Key Committees**: Monetary Policy Committee (MPC), Financial Stability and Development Council (FSDC)
