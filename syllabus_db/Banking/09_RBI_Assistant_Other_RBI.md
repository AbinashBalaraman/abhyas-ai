# RBI Assistant & Other RBI Subordinate Positions

Complete guide to Reserve Bank of India examinations including RBI Assistant, Grade A, B, C, D and specialized posts.

---

## 1. RBI Assistant

### Posts
- **Designation**: Assistant (Clerical Cadre) in Reserve Bank of India
- **Branches**: All RBI offices across India
- **Role**: Clerical/support work in various departments

### Eligibility
- **Educational Qualification**: Bachelor's Degree in any discipline
  - **UR/EWS**: Minimum 50% marks (aggregate)
  - **SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-servicemen**: Pass class (no minimum percentage)
- **Age Limit**: 20 to 28 years (as on the date of notification)
  - **Age Relaxation**:
    - SC/ST: 5 years
    - OBC: 3 years
    - PwBD: 10 years (Gen), 13 years (OBC), 15 years (SC/ST)
    - Ex-servicemen: As per government rules
- **Nationality**: Indian citizen

### Exam Pattern (2 Tiers + LPT)

#### Preliminary Examination (Qualifying)
- **Total Questions**: 100
- **Total Marks**: 100
- **Duration**: 60 minutes (20 minutes per section)
- **Mode**: Online Objective

| Section | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---------|-----------|-------|------|
| English Language | 30 | 30 | 20 min |
| Numerical Ability | 35 | 35 | 20 min |
| Reasoning Ability | 35 | 35 | 20 min |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** | **60 min** |

#### Mains Examination (Merit-Deciding)
- **Total Questions**: 200
- **Total Marks**: 200
- **Duration**: 135 minutes
- **Mode**: Online Objective

| Section | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---------|-----------|-------|------|
| Reasoning Ability | 40 | 40 | 30 min |
| English Language | 40 | 40 | 30 min |
| Numerical Ability | 40 | 40 | 30 min |
| General Awareness | 50 | 50 | 25 min |
| Computer Knowledge | 40 | 40 | 20 min |
| **Total** | **200** | **200** | **135 min** |

#### Language Proficiency Test (LPT) - Qualifying
- Candidates qualifying Mains must appear for LPT
- Tests ability to read, write, speak, and understand the **official/local language** of the state/UT applied for
- **Qualifying in nature** - marks not added to merit

- Reading Comprehension
- Cloze Test
- Para Jumbles / Sentence Rearrangement

- Sentence Errors / Correction
- Vocabulary (Synonyms, Antonyms, Word Meanings)
- One Word Substitution
- Active & Passive Voice
- Sentence Completion
- Phrases and Idioms

#### Numerical Ability / Quantitative Aptitude
- Number System
- Simplification & Approximation
- Decimals & Fractions
- Percentage
- Ratio & Proportion
- Average
- Simple & Compound Interest
- Profit & Loss
- Discount
- Time & Work
- Time, Speed & Distance
- Mensuration (Area, Volume)
- Data Interpretation (Tables, Pie Charts, Bar Graphs, Line Graphs)
- HCF and LCM
- Probability
- Permutation & Combination
- Pipes & Cistern
- Allegation & Comparison
- Algebra
- Geometry
- Trigonometry
- Train and Boat Problems

#### Reasoning Ability
- Puzzles
- Seating Arrangement (Linear, Circular, Square)
- Blood Relations
- Direction Sense
- Order & Ranking
- Coding-Decoding
- Syllogism
- Inequalities
- Number Series
- Analogy
- Odd Man Out
- Statement & Conclusion
- Data Sufficiency
- Pattern and Arrangement
- Symbols

#### General Awareness
- **Current Affairs** (last 6 months - National & International)
- **Banking Awareness**:
  - RBI history, structure, functions
  - Banking terms & concepts
  - NBFC, Scheduled vs Non-Scheduled Banks
  - Government Schemes (Jan Dhan, MUDRA, Atal Pension, etc.)
  - Monetary Policy tools (Repo, Reverse Repo, CRR, SLR)
  - International Financial Institutions (IMF, World Bank, ADB, BIS)
- **Static GK**:
  - Geography, History, Polity
  - Indian Economy basics
  - Important days, awards, books
  - Sports, summits, reports

#### Computer Knowledge
- History of Computers
- Generations of Computers
- Computer Memory (RAM, ROM, Cache)
- Input/Output Devices
- MS Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint)
- Internet & Web Browsers
- Networking Basics (LAN, WAN, MAN, Topology)
- Operating Systems
- Database Management Systems (DBMS)
- Cyber Security basics
- Computer Abbreviations
- Shortcut Keys

### Marking Scheme
- **Correct Answer**: +1 mark (both Prelims and Mains)
- **Incorrect Answer**: -0.25 marks (negative marking)
- **Unanswered**: 0 marks (no penalty)

### Salary Structure
- **Basic Pay**: Rs 29,764/- per month
- **Gross Salary**: Approximately Rs 45,000 - 50,000/-
- **In-hand Salary**: Rs 40,000 - 45,000/-
- **Pay Scale**: 29,764 - 1,12,168 (with DA, CCA, HRA, etc.)
- **Additional Benefits**:
  - House Rent Allowance (HRA)
  - Dearness Allowance (DA)
  - City Compensatory Allowance (CCA)
  - Medical Benefits
  - LTC, LFC
  - Pension & Gratuity (NPS)
  - Loan facilities at concessional rates

### Selection Process
1. Preliminary Examination (Qualifying)
2. Mains Examination (Merit)
3. Language Proficiency Test (Qualifying)
4. Document Verification
5. Medical Fitness Test

## 2. RBI Grade A (Other Specialized Departments)

In addition to General Grade B (Officer), RBI recruits Grade A officers in specialized departments.

### RBI DEPR (Department of Economic and Policy Research)
- **Post**: Manager (Grade A) - Research
- **Eligibility**:
  - PhD in Economics / Master's in Economics (with 55%+) or
  - Master's in Quantitative Economics / Mathematical Economics / Econometrics/Finance
- **Age Limit**: 21-30 years
- **Exam Pattern**:
  - **Paper I**: Objective (General Awareness, English, Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning) - Qualifying
  - **Paper II**: Descriptive Economics (90 min) - Merit
  - **Paper III**: English Descriptive (60 min) - Qualifying
  - Interview

### RBI DSIM (Department of Statistics and Information Management)
- **Post**: Manager (Grade A) - Statistics
- **Eligibility**:
  - Master's in Statistics / Mathematical Statistics / Mathematical Economics / Econometrics / Actuarial Statistics
  - Minimum 55% marks
- **Age Limit**: 21-30 years
- **Exam Pattern**:
  - **Paper I**: Objective (Common) - Qualifying
  - **Paper II**: Descriptive Statistics
  - **Paper III**: English - Qualifying
  - Interview

### RBI Legal Officer (Grade A/B)
- **Post**: Legal Officer / Manager (Legal)
- **Eligibility**:
  - Bachelor's Degree in Law (LLB) from a recognized university
  - Minimum 50% marks
  - **Experience**: 2-5 years of experience in legal practice (preferred)
- **Age Limit**: 21-32 years
- **Selection**: Online test + Interview
- **Areas of Law**: Banking Law, Company Law, Contract Law, SARFAESI, RBI Act, FEMA, etc.

### RBI IT Officer / Cyber Security (Grade A/B)
- **Post**: Manager (IT/Cyber Security)
- **Eligibility**:
  - B.Tech / B.E. in Computer Science / IT / Electronics & Communication OR
  - MCA with Bachelor's degree
  - Minimum 60% marks
- **Age Limit**: 21-30 years
- **Exam Pattern**:
  - Paper I: Objective (Common subjects) - Qualifying
  - Paper II: IT/Specialization
  - Interview
- **Topics**: Programming, Data Structures, DBMS, Networking, OS, Cyber Security, Cryptography, Cloud Computing, AI/ML

### RBI Rajbhasha Officer (Grade A)
- **Post**: Manager (Rajbhasha - Hindi)
- **Eligibility**:
  - Master's degree in Hindi with English as a subject at Bachelor's level OR
  - Master's in Sanskrit / English / Economics / Commerce with Hindi as a subject
  - Minimum 55% marks (50% for SC/ST)
  - Knowledge of Hindi translation
- **Age Limit**: 21-30 years
- **Exam Pattern**:
  - Paper I: Objective (Common) - Qualifying
  - Paper II: Hindi Descriptive (Translation, Essay, Letter Writing)
  - Paper III: English - Qualifying
  - Interview

## 3. RBI Grade C (Manager)

### Overview
- **Post**: Manager (Grade C) - Specialized positions
- **Departments**: Various specialized divisions

### Common Eligibility
- **Education**: Relevant Master's/Bachelor's degree in respective field
- **Age Limit**: Typically 25-35 years (relaxation as per category)
- **Experience**: 3-5 years in relevant field (usually required)

### Selection Process
- **Phase I**: Online Objective Test (Common subjects)
- **Phase II**: Online Objective + Descriptive Test (Specialization)
- **Phase III**: Personal Interview

### Streams (Indicative)
- **Finance & Accounts**: CA/ICWA/MBA (Finance)
- **Legal**: LLB with experience
- **Information Technology**: B.Tech/MCA with experience
- **Human Resource Management**: MBA (HR)
- **Market Intelligence & Statistical Analysis**: Master's in Economics/Statistics
- **Library & Information Science**: Master's in Library Science

### Salary
- **Basic Pay**: Approximately Rs 65,000 - 70,000/-
- **Pay Scale**: 65,000 - 1,50,000 (approx)
- **Gross Monthly**: Rs 1,00,000 - 1,20,000/-

## 4. RBI Grade D (Office Assistant)

### Overview
- **Note**: RBI does not have a separate "Grade D" designation. The entry-level clerical post is "RBI Assistant" covered in Section 1 above.
- The classification in RBI is:
  - **Grade A** (Assistant Manager/Manager)
  - **Grade B** (Manager/General/Officer)
  - **Grade C** (Assistant General Manager/Manager)
  - **Grade D** (Chief General Manager)
  - **Grade E** (Principal Chief General Manager)
  - **Grade F** (Deputy Governor)

For clerical positions, refer to **RBI Assistant** details in Section 1.

## 5. RBI Medical Officer

### Post: Medical Officer (Grade C/B)

### Eligibility
- **Education**: MBBS from MCI-recognized university
- **Postgraduate**: MD/MS in relevant specialty (preferred for senior posts)
- **Experience**: 2-3 years of clinical experience (preferred)
- **Age Limit**: 21-35 years (relaxation for reserved categories)

### Selection Process
- **Screening**: Based on academic qualifications and experience
- **Interview**: Personal interview
- **Medical Examination**: Fitness test

### Specializations (Indicative)
- General Medicine
- Cardiology
- Orthopedics
- Ophthalmology
- ENT
- Gynecology
- Pediatrics
- Psychiatry

### Salary
- **Basic Pay**: Rs 65,000+ (Grade C)
- **Gross Salary**: Rs 1,00,000 - 1,20,000/-
- **Allowances**: Non-practicing allowance, medical allowance

### Duties
- Medical care of RBI employees
- Health check-ups
- Occupational health services
- Emergency medical services

## 6. RBI Security Officer

### Post: Security Officer / Chief Security Officer

### Eligibility
- **Education**: Bachelor's degree in any discipline
- **Service Background** (Essential):
  - **Junior/Senior Security Officer**: Ex-servicemen / Commissioned Officers from Army/Navy/Air Force
  - 5+ years of experience in security/intelligence
- **Age Limit**: 25-40 years (relaxation as per rules)
- **Physical Standards**: As per RBI standards

### Specializations Preferred
- Counter-terrorism operations
- VIP security/protection
- Intelligence gathering
- Fire safety management
- Crisis management

### Selection Process
- Shortlisting based on service record
- Personal Interview
- Medical fitness test
- Background verification

### Salary
- **Basic Pay**: Rs 50,000 - 1,20,000 (depending on grade)
- **Additional**: Risk allowance, special pay for security personnel

### Duties
- Overall security of RBI premises
- Cash vault protection
- Personnel security
- Coordination with central/state security agencies
- Crisis management

## 7. RBI Library Staff

### Post: Library & Information Officer (Grade A/B/C)

### Eligibility
- **Education**:
  - Master's in Library & Information Science (M.Lib.I.Sc.) / Library Science
  - Minimum 55% marks
  - **Additional**: PG Diploma in Library Automation, Digital Library Management (preferred)
- **Age Limit**: 21-30 years (Grade A) / 25-35 years (Grade B/C)
- **Experience**: 2-5 years for higher grades

### Skills Required
- Library management software (KOHA, DSpace)
- Digital library management
- Cataloging & classification
- Information retrieval
- Research methodology
- Knowledge of banking/economic databases

### Selection Process
- Online Objective Test
- Descriptive Test
- Interview

### Salary
- **Basic Pay**: Rs 44,000 - 1,20,000 (depending on grade)
- **Gross Monthly**: Rs 70,000 - 1,50,000

### Duties
- Manage RBI's specialized library
- Maintain archives and records
- Research support to RBI officers
- Subscription management
- Digital content management

## Important Tips for RBI Exams

1. **Prelims is Qualifying** - Only need to clear sectional and overall cutoff
2. **Mains Merit Matters** - Focus maximum preparation on Mains
3. **LPT is Mandatory** - Practice local language reading/writing
4. **Banking Awareness** - Read RBI Annual Report, Monetary Policy documents
5. **Current Affairs** - Focus on last 6 months, especially Banking & Economy
6. **Time Management** - Practice mock tests regularly
7. **Negative Marking** - Avoid blind guessing
8. **Document Readiness** - Keep certificates ready for verification
