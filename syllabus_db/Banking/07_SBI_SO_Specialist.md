## 2. Eligibility (Post-Specific)

**Common nationality rule:** Indian citizen. Age reckoned as on the cut-off date mentioned in the notification (relaxation for SC/ST/OBC/PwD/Ex-Servicemen as per GoI rules).

### 2.1 IT Officer (Assistant Manager – Systems)
- **Qualification:** B.Tech / B.E. in Computer Science / IT / Electronics / Electronics & Telecommunications / Electrical / Electronics & Instrumentation **OR** M.Tech / M.Sc. (CS/IT) **OR** MCA.
- **Age:** 21–30 years (relaxable as per rules).

### 2.2 Law Officer
- **Qualification:** LLB (3-year / 5-year) **OR** LLM.
- **Experience:** 3–5 years of experience as an Advocate / Legal Consultant in a Bank/Financial Institution/PSB/listed entity (post-qualification). Some notifications allow fresh LLBs.
- **Age:** 21–35 years (relaxable).

### 2.3 HR / Personnel Officer
- **Qualification:** MBA (HR / Personnel Management) **OR** PGDBM (HR) **OR** PG in HR / IR / Personnel Management / Industrial Relations.
- **Experience:** 2–5 years in HR/IR/Personnel functions (variance by cadre).
- **Age:** 21–35 years.

### 2.4 Marketing Officer
- **Qualification:** MBA (Marketing) **OR** PGDBM (Marketing) **OR** MMS (Marketing) **OR** PG in Marketing Management.
- **Experience:** 2–5 years in Sales / Marketing / Product Management (preferred).
- **Age:** 21–35 years.

### 2.5 Engineering Officer – Civil
- **Qualification:** B.E. / B.Tech in Civil Engineering.
- **Experience:** 2–5 years in relevant civil construction / maintenance of bank premises (preferred).
- **Age:** 21–35 years.

### 2.6 Engineering Officer – Electrical
- **Qualification:** B.E. / B.Tech in Electrical Engineering / Electrical & Electronics / Power Systems.
- **Experience:** 2–5 years in relevant electrical works.
- **Age:** 21–35 years.

### 2.7 Rajbhasha Officer (Hindi / Official Language)
- **Qualification:** PG in Hindi with English as a subject at UG level **OR** PG in English with Hindi as a subject at UG level **OR** PG in Sanskrit / Economics / Commerce with Hindi and English as subjects.
- **Experience:** 3 years in translation / official language implementation (for higher posts).
- **Age:** 21–35 years.

### 2.8 Fire Officer
- **Qualification:** B.E. (Fire) / B.E. (Fire & Safety) **OR** B.Tech (Safety & Fire Engineering) **OR** equivalent recognised by UGC/AICTE.
- **Experience:** 2–5 years in firefighting / fire safety in large establishments (preferred).
- **Age:** 21–35 years.

### 2.9 Economist
- **Qualification:** M.A. / M.Sc. / Ph.D. in Economics / Econometrics / Mathematical Economics / Finance from a recognised university.
- **Experience:** 3–5 years in research / economic analysis (for higher cadres).
- **Age:** 21–35 years.

### 2.10 Security Officer
- **Qualification:** Graduate in any discipline.
- **Experience:** Commissioned service in Indian Army / Navy / Air Force **OR** equivalent service in paramilitary forces (mandatory).
- **Age:** 21–40 years (relaxable for ex-servicemen).

## 3. Exam Pattern (Standard SBI SO)

The standard SBI SO exam has **two parts** – an **Online Test (Objective)** followed by an **Interview**. Some senior cadres (Chief Manager, AGM) may skip the online test and shortlist directly for interview.

### 3.1 Online Test – General Aptitude (Objective)
- **Duration:** 90 minutes
- **Negative Marking:** 1/4th mark deducted for every wrong answer

| Section | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---------|-----------|-------|----------|
| Test of Reasoning | 50 | 50 | 90 min (combined) |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 35 | 35 | – |
| English Language | 35 | 35 | – |
| **Total** | **120** | **120** | **90 min** |

### 3.2 Online Test – Professional Knowledge (Subject-specific)
- **Duration:** 70 minutes
- **Negative Marking:** 1/4th mark deducted for every wrong answer

| Section | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---------|-----------|-------|----------|
| General IT Knowledge | 25 | 50 | 70 min (combined) |
| Role-Based Knowledge | 50 | 100 | – |
| **Total** | **75** | **150** | **70 min** |

### 3.3 Interview
- Shortlisted candidates (roughly 3x the vacancies) are called for interview.
- **Weightage:** Online Test **70%** : Interview **30%**.

> Final merit = 70% of Online Test score + 30% of Interview score.

## 4. Detailed Syllabus

### 4.1 Common Sections (same as SBI PO)

#### Reasoning
- Coding-Decoding, Blood Relations, Alphabet Test
- Sitting Arrangement (Linear / Circular), Direction Sense
- Mathematical Operations, Arithmetical Reasoning
- Analogy, Classification, Series
- Number, Ranking & Time Sequence
- Eligibility Test, Inserting the Missing Character
- Syllogism, Statement-Assumption, Statement-Conclusion
- Input-Output, Puzzles, Data Sufficiency
- Order & Ranking, Inequalities

#### English Language
- Reading Comprehension, Cloze Test
- Idioms & Phrases, One Word Substitutions
- Prepositions, Tenses, Conjunctions
- Active / Passive Voice, Direct / Indirect Speech
- Synonyms & Antonyms, Spotting Errors
- Sentence Improvement / Correction, Fill in the Blanks
- Para Jumbles, Sentence Rearrangement

#### Quantitative Aptitude

## 5. Circle Based Officer (CBO) – Special Section

CBO is a **dedicated JMGS-I recruitment** for SBI where the selected officer is posted within a specific circle/state and is expected to serve in that circle.

### 5.1 Posts
- Circle Based Officer (CBO) – JMGS-I cadre
- Posted in: Any of the 17 SBI Circles (e.g., Mumbai, Delhi, Chennai, Kolkata, Bangalore, Lucknow, Patna, Hyderabad, etc.)

### 5.2 Eligibility
- **Nationality:** Indian citizen
- **Qualification:** Graduation in any discipline from a recognised university (no percentage cut-off in most notifications)
- **Age:** 21–30 years (relaxation for reserved categories as per GoI)
- **Local Language:** Candidate must be able to read, write and speak the **local language of the circle** they apply for. (Candidates who studied the local language at 10th/12th/graduation level are deemed proficient; otherwise, language proficiency test at the time of joining.)

### 5.3 Exam Pattern
- **Online Written Test (Objective + Descriptive)** – 3 sections
- **Interview**

#### Online Test Structure
| Section | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---------|-----------|-------|----------|
| English Language | 30 | 30 | 20 min |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 30 | 30 | 20 min |
| Reasoning Ability | 30 | 30 | 20 min |
| **Total (Objective)** | **90** | **90** | **60 min** |
| **Descriptive English** (Letter Writing + Essay) | 2 | 50 | 30 min |

- **Negative marking:** 1/4th for every wrong answer.
- **Weightage:** Online Test 70% : Interview 30% (final merit).

### 5.4 Detailed Syllabus for CBO

#### Reasoning
- Puzzles, Seating Arrangement, Order/Ranking
- Syllogism, Inequalities, Coding-Decoding
- Blood Relations, Direction Sense, Series
- Data Sufficiency, Input-Output, Statement-Conclusion
- Alphanumeric Series, Logical Reasoning

#### English Language
- Reading Comprehension, Cloze Test
- Vocabulary: Synonyms, Antonyms, One Word Substitutions, Idioms
- Grammar: Error Spotting, Sentence Correction, Fill in the Blanks
- Para Jumbles, Sentence Rearrangement
- Active/Passive, Direct/Indirect
- **Descriptive:** Letter (formal/informal) + Essay (banking/economy/current topics)

#### Quantitative Aptitude
- Simplification, Number Series
- DI (Tabular, Bar, Pie, Line, Caselet, Missing)
- Arithmetic: Ratio, %, Profit-Loss, SI/CI, Time-Work, Speed-Distance
- Mensuration, Algebra, Geometry
- Probability, Permutation & Combination

#### Banking Awareness (asked in GK/GA component)
- Indian Banking System – Structure, RBI role
- Types of Banks: Commercial, Cooperative, RRBs, SFBs, Payments Banks
- RBI Monetary Policy, Repo, Reverse Repo, CRR, SLR, MSF
- Banking products: Loans, Deposits, NPA, Priority Sector
- Government Schemes: PMJDY, MUDRA, Jan Suraksha, APY
- Digital Banking: UPI, IMPS, AEPS, NETC
- Banking Ombudsman, SARFAESI, DRT
- Latest Banking News, NPAs, Mergers
- Insurance basics, Financial Inclusion

#### Local Language (Circle-specific)
- Reading comprehension
- Sentence formation & grammar
- Translation (English ↔ Local Language)
- Vocabulary of the local language
- (No specific test; proficiency tested at interview / joining)

### 6.1 Marking Scheme (Common to SBI SO & CBO)
- **Objective sections:** +1 mark per correct answer

## 8. Quick Reference – Selection Stages

1. **Release of Notification** (sbi.co.in/careers)
2. **Online Registration & Fee Payment** (₹750 for General/OBC/EWS; nil for SC/ST/PwD; in case of CBO ₹750)
3. **Download of Call Letter (Pre-exam training – for reserved categories in some states)**
4. **Online Objective Test** (Reasoning + Quant + English)
5. **Online Subject Test** (Professional Knowledge + General IT)
6. **Descriptive Test (CBO only)**
7. **Declaration of Online Test Result**
8. **Interview Call Letter**
9. **Interview & Document Verification**
10. **Final Merit List & Provisional Allotment**
11. **Pre-joining Medical Fitness**
12. **Joining & Probation (2 years)**
