# IBPS Clerk (CRP CSA) - Comprehensive Guide

## 1. Overview

- **Full Name:** IBPS Clerk (Institute of Banking Personnel Selection - Customer Service Associate / Clerical Cadre) examination, officially termed **CRP CSA** (Common Recruitment Process for Clerical Cadre)
- **Conducting Body:** Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) — an autonomous central recruitment agency under the **Ministry of Finance, Government of India**, headquartered in **Mumbai**. Established on **19 December 1975** (evolved from Personnel Selection Services in 1984).
- **Current Chairman:** Shri Rajneesh Karnatak
- **Frequency:** Annually (Prelims usually October, Mains usually December)
- **Mode:** Online (Computer-Based Test)
- **Participating Public Sector Banks (11):**
  1. Bank of Baroda
  2. Bank of India
  3. Bank of Maharashtra
  4. Canara Bank
  5. Central Bank of India
  6. Indian Bank
  7. Indian Overseas Bank
  8. Punjab National Bank
  9. Punjab & Sind Bank
  10. UCO Bank
  11. Union Bank of India

---

## 2. Posts Offered

- **Designation:** Clerk / Junior Associate (Clerical Cadre) — also called **Customer Service Associate (CSA)**
- **Cadre:** Group 'C' employee in nationalised public sector banks
- **Work Profile:** Day-to-day banking operations, customer service, cash handling, account opening, query resolution, and basic administrative tasks at branches.

---

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

### Educational Qualification
- **Bachelor's Degree** in any discipline from a university recognized by the Government of India (or equivalent qualification recognized by the Central Government).
- Must possess a valid mark-sheet / degree certificate proving graduation at the time of registration/verification.
- Candidates awaiting results or in the final year are provisionally eligible (must produce proof at document verification).
- **Note:** Some banks in certain states (e.g., Punjab & Sind Bank) may accept 12th pass for specific posts, but for the standard IBPS Clerk CRP, graduation is the minimum.
- **Computer Literacy:** Working knowledge of computer systems is desirable.

### Age Limit
- **Minimum:** 20 years
- **Maximum:** 28 years
- **Age Relaxation (as per Government norms):**
  | Category | Relaxation |
  |-----------|------------|
  | SC/ST | 5 years |
  | OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) | 3 years |
  | PWD (SC/ST) | 15 years |
  | PWD (OBC) | 13 years |
  | PWD (General/EWS) | 10 years |
  | Ex-Servicemen / Disabled Ex-Servicemen | As per Govt. rules (max 50 years) |
  | Widows/Divorced Women | 9 years |
  | Persons ordinarily domiciled in J&K (1980-89) | 5 years |

### Nationality
- Citizen of India, or
- Subject of Nepal/Bhutan, or
- Tibetan refugee (arrived before 1 Jan 1962), or
- Person of Indian Origin migrated from Pakistan, Burma, Sri Lanka, or East African countries (Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Zambia, Malawi, Zaire, Ethiopia, Vietnam) intending to permanently settle in India.
- Candidates in categories (ii)-(v) must hold an eligibility certificate issued by the Government of India.

### Language Proficiency
- Candidates must be able to **read, write, and speak the official regional language** of the state/UT for which they are applying.
- Verified at the **Language Proficiency Test (LPT)** stage (qualifying in nature).
- Exempted if the candidate studied the local language as a subject at the 10th/12th level.

### Application Fee (2024 cycle, indicative)
- **SC / ST / PWBD / EXSM:** Rs. 175 (inclusive of GST)
- **All Others (Gen / OBC / EWS):** Rs. 850 (inclusive of GST)

## 4. Exam Pattern (2 Tiers)

### Tier I — Preliminary Examination (Qualifying)
- **Total:** 100 Questions, 100 Marks, 60 Minutes (sectional timing enabled)
- **Purpose:** Only qualifying; marks not added to final merit.
- **Negative Marking:** 0.25 mark per wrong answer.

| Section | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---------|-----------|-------|------|
| English Language | 30 | 30 | 20 min |
| Numerical Ability | 35 | 35 | 20 min |
| Reasoning Ability | 35 | 35 | 20 min |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** | **60 min** |

> **Note:** Per the latest IBPS Clerk 2026 notification, the Mains pattern was revised. Candidates should check the official notification for the current structure.

### Tier II — Mains Examination (Merit-deciding)
- **Total (per user-provided structure):** 190 Questions, 200 Marks, 160 Minutes
- **Purpose:** Final merit list for bank allotment.
- **Negative Marking:** 0.50 mark per wrong answer (per user spec) / 0.25 per current IBPS rules.

| Section | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---------|-----------|-------|------|
| Reasoning Ability & Computer Aptitude | 50 | 60 | 45 min |
| English Language | 40 | 40 | 35 min |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 50 | 50 | 40 min |
| General / Financial Awareness | 50 | 50 | 25 min |
| **Total** | **190** | **200** | **160 min** |

#### Latest IBPS Clerk Mains Pattern (2025-2026 cycle, as per official sources)
- **Total:** 155 Questions, 200 Marks, 120 Minutes (with sectional timing)
- Negative Marking: 0.25 per wrong answer

| Section | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---------|-----------|-------|------|
| Reasoning Ability & Computer Aptitude | 40 | 60 | 35 min |

| **Total** | **155** | **200** | **120 min** |

### Tier III — State-wise Document Verification & Language Proficiency Test (LPT)
- Shortlisted candidates (≈10× vacancies) are called for document verification.
- LPT assesses the candidate's ability to read, write, and speak the official regional language of the state/UT applied for.
- LPT is of a **qualifying** nature; failure leads to disqualification.

## 5. Detailed Subject-Wise Syllabus

### A. English Language
- Reading Comprehension (with vocab-based questions)
- Cloze Test (story-based / passage-based)
- Fill in the Blanks (single, double, vocab-based)
- Sentence Errors / Error Spotting
- Sentence Improvement / Correction
- Para Jumbles (sentence rearrangement)
- Para Completion / Sentence Completion
- Vocabulary — Synonyms & Antonyms
- Spelling Errors / Misspelt Words
- Idioms & Phrases
- One Word Substitution
- Phrase Replacement / Word Swap
- Active / Passive Voice
- Direct / Indirect Speech
- Tenses, Articles, Prepositions
- Passage Completion

### B. Numerical Ability / Quantitative Aptitude
- Number System (LCM, HCF, divisibility)
- Simplification / Approximation (BODMAS)
- Decimals & Fractions
- Percentage, Ratio & Proportion
- Average, Age-based problems
- Simple & Compound Interest
- Profit & Loss, Discount
- Time & Work, Time & Distance, Speed
- Mensuration (2D & 3D: area, volume, perimeter)
- Data Interpretation (Table, Pie Chart, Bar Graph, Line Graph, Caselet, Radar)
- Probability
- Permutation & Combination
- Number Series (missing/wrong number)
- Quadratic Equations
- Data Sufficiency
- Mixture & Alligation
- Partnership, Boats & Streams, Pipes & Cisterns

### C. Reasoning Ability
- Puzzles (floor, scheduling, box, month/day-based, etc.)
- Seating Arrangement (linear, circular, square, rectangular, uncertain)
- Blood Relations
- Direction Sense & Distance
- Order & Ranking
- Coding-Decoding (alphabet, number, symbol-based)
- Syllogism (direct, reverse, possibility)
- Inequalities (coded, direct)
- Input-Output (machine-based)
- Statement & Conclusion / Argument / Assumption
- Series (numeric, alphanumeric, alpha)
- Analogies
- Classification, Odd One Out
- Data Sufficiency
- Verbal Reasoning
- Assertion-Reason

### D. General / Financial Awareness
- **Banking History & Structure:** Indian banking evolution, nationalisation (1969/1980), reforms
- **Reserve Bank of India (RBI):** History, structure, functions, monetary policy, recent circulars
- **Banking Terms:** Repo rate, Reverse repo, CRR, SLR, MCLR, NPA, CASA, etc.
- **Financial Awareness:** Union Budget, Economic Survey, Five-Year Plans, GDP, inflation
- **Government Schemes:** PMJDY, MUDRA, Jan Dhan, Aadhaar-related, Atal Pension Yojana, PMJJBY, PMSBY, Stand-Up India, Start-Up India
- **Static GK:** Important days, books & authors, awards, headquarters, capitals, currencies
- **Current Affairs:** National & International (last 6 months), summits, sports, summits & conferences
- **Reports & Indices:** NITI Aayog, World Bank, IMF reports
- **Banking & Economy News:** Recent RBI rate decisions, SEBI, IRDA, regulatory changes

### E. Computer Aptitude / Knowledge
- History of Computers (generations: 1st to 5th)
- Computer Fundamentals (hardware vs software, types)
- Memory types (RAM, ROM, Cache, Primary/Secondary)
- Input / Output Devices
- MS Office (MS Word, MS Excel, MS PowerPoint — basic functionalities & shortcuts)
- Operating Systems (Windows basics)
- Internet (terms, services, browsers, URLs, search engines)
- Networking (LAN, MAN, WAN, topologies, protocols)
- Database Management Systems (DBMS basics)
- Cybersecurity — hacking, viruses, malware, security tools
- Shortcut Keys & Abbreviations

| Stage | Correct Answer | Wrong Answer | Unattempted |

- Multiple correct answers lead to negative marking of that question's marks.

| Stage | Post | Description |

## 10. Tie-Breaking Policy

If two or more candidates score equal marks:
1. **Higher age (earlier date of birth) is preferred** — older candidate ranked higher.
2. If still tied, alphabetical order of names is considered.
3. If still tied, performance in specific sections (usually Reasoning/Quant) is considered.

## 11. Exam Medium / Language Options

The exam is **bilingual** (English + Hindi), with additional regional language options in respective states. English Language section is English-only.

Examples:
- **Andhra Pradesh / Telangana:** English, Hindi, Telugu, Urdu
- **Tamil Nadu / Puducherry:** English, Hindi, Tamil (+ Telugu/Malayalam in Puducherry)
- **Maharashtra / Goa:** English, Hindi, Marathi, Konkani
- **Gujarat / Daman & Diu / Dadra & Nagar Haveli:** English, Hindi, Gujarati (+ Marathi, Konkani in DNH/DD)
- **Jammu & Kashmir / UP:** English, Hindi, Urdu
- **North-Eastern / Hindi-belt states:** English, Hindi

(Full state-wise list available in the official notification.)

## 12. Important Tips for Preparation

1. Understand the syllabus & exam pattern thoroughly.
2. Build a strong foundation in Quantitative Aptitude & Reasoning (high-weightage sections).
3. Read newspapers daily (The Hindu, Indian Express) and follow monthly current-affair magazines.
4. Practice sectional tests daily + full-length mock tests weekly.
5. Solve previous year papers (last 5-7 years) under timed conditions.
6. Maintain an error log to identify and revise weak areas.
7. Focus on speed and accuracy — 100% accuracy with smart guessing beats blind attempts.
8. Attempt sectional cut-offs strategically; don't get stuck on tough questions.
9. Take IBPS Clerk 2026 mock tests from authentic platforms regularly.
10. Revise vocabulary, banking terms, and static GK on weekends.
