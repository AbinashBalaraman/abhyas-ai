## 2. Posts Offered & Experience Required

### 2.1 Officer Scale II (Middle Management Grade Scale-II)
This cadre is divided into Generalist and Specialist categories:

*   **General Banking Officer (GBO):** Works as branch manager or operations officer.
    *   *Experience:* Minimum **2 years** as an officer in a Bank or Financial Institution.
*   **Specialist Officers (SO):**
    *   **Information Technology (IT) Officer:** Manage bank networks, servers, database.
        *   *Experience:* 1 year in relevant IT domain.
    *   **Chartered Accountant (CA):** Handle tax audits, financial planning.
        *   *Experience:* 1 year as a practicing CA.
    *   **Law Officer:** Deal with recovery cases, legal drafting.
        *   *Experience:* 2 years as an advocate or law officer in bank.
    *   **Treasury Manager:** Manage bank funds, bonds, cash reserves.
        *   *Experience:* 1 year in treasury operations.
    *   **Marketing Officer:** Product marketing, sales coordination.
        *   *Experience:* 1 year as marketing officer in bank/FI.
    *   **Agricultural Officer:** Crop loans, dairy, agricultural credit appraisal.
        *   *Experience:* 2 years in agricultural credit/loans.

### 2.2 Officer Scale III (Senior Management Grade Scale-III)
*   **Senior Manager:** Lead larger regional branches or administrative offices.
    *   *Experience:* Minimum **5 years** as an officer in a Bank or Financial Institution.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

### 3.1 Educational Qualification
*   **General Banking Officer (Scale II):** Bachelor’s degree in any discipline with a minimum of **50% marks** in aggregate. Preferences for Agriculture, Horticulture, Forestry, Animal Husbandry, Veterinary Science, Agricultural Engineering, Marketing, etc.
*   **Officer Scale III:** Bachelor’s degree in any discipline with a minimum of **50% marks** in aggregate. Preferences for banking, finance, agriculture, business administration.
*   **Specialist Officers (Scale II):** Degree in Engineering (CS/IT/ECE) for IT; LLB for Law; CA certification; MBA Marketing for Marketing Officer; Degree in Agriculture/Horticulture for Agri Officer.

### 3.2 Age Limit (as on the cut-off date)
*   **Officer Scale II:** Above 21 years - Below 32 years.
*   **Officer Scale III:** Above 21 years - Below 40 years.
*   **Age Relaxation:** SC/ST +5 years, OBC +3 years, PwBD +10 years.

## 5. Detailed Syllabus

### 5.1 Financial Awareness (Common for GBO & Scale III)
*This section requires high-level conceptual clarity in banking operations.*
- **Indian Banking System & RBI:** History, regulatory powers under BR Act, NPA guidelines, Prompt Corrective Action (PCA), capital adequacy, SLR, CRR, Basel III.
- **Priority Sector Lending (PSL):** Targets for RRBs (75% of ANBC) vs. Commercial Banks (40%). Agriculture, MSME, housing, education, renewable energy sub-targets.
- **Agricultural Finance:** KCC (Kisan Credit Card) schemes, crop loan scales of finance, interest subvention, collateral-free agricultural loans.
- **Financial Statements Analysis:** Reading bank balance sheets, NPA provisioning, Capital-to-Risk Weighted Assets Ratio (CRAR), Return on Assets (ROA), Net Interest Margin (NIM).
- **Risk Management:** Credit risk, market risk, operational risk, ALM (Asset Liability Management).
- **Government Schemes (Rural):** PMJDY, PMJJBY, PMSBY, APY, Stand-Up India, Mudra loans, PM Fasal Bima Yojana.
- **Latest Current Affairs:** Banking mergers, Union Budget, Economic Survey highlights, RBI circulars from the last 6–8 months.

### 5.2 Professional Knowledge (For Scale II Specialist Streams)
*   **IT Officer:** Operating System concepts, DBMS & SQL, Computer Networks (TCP/IP, OSI layers), Data Structures & Algorithms, Network Security (cryptography, firewalls, cyber laws), Software Engineering (SDLC models).
*   **Law Officer:** Banking Regulation Act, RBI Act, SARFAESI Act, IBC Code, Recovery of Debts due to Banks (DRT), Consumer Protection Act, Indian Contract Act, Transfer of Property Act, Negotiable Instruments Act.
*   **Agriculture Officer:** Crop production, Soil science & fertilizer management, Horticulture & Forestry, Animal Husbandry, Dairy technology, Agricultural economics, Farm mechanization.

### 5.3 Quantitative Aptitude & Data Interpretation
- **Data Interpretation:** Tables, Bar graphs, Line charts, Pie charts, Radar charts, Caselet DI, and arithmetic-based DI (percentage/ratios).
- **Number Series:** Missing and wrong number series.
- **Quadratic Equations:** Comparing variable values (\(x\) and \(y\)).
- **Arithmetic:** Profit & Loss, Simple & Compound Interest, Averages, Percentages, Ratio & Proportion, Partnership, Time & Work, Speed & Distance, Probability, Permutation & Combination.

### 5.4 Reasoning Ability
- **Seating Arrangements:** Circular, linear, square, triangular facing directions.
- **Puzzles:** Floor puzzles, box puzzles, scheduling (day/month/year), double-variable puzzles.
- **Syllogism:** Basic statements, Reverse Syllogism, "Only a Few" qualifiers.
- **Direction Sense, Blood Relations, Input-Output, Coded Inequalities, Data Sufficiency.**

## 6. Salary & Benefits

*   **Officer Scale II (GBO/SO):** Basic Pay of ₹48,170 – ₹69,810. Approximate starting gross salary: **₹75,000 – ₹85,000/month** + perks.
*   **Officer Scale III (Senior Manager):** Basic Pay of ₹63,840 – ₹78,230. Approximate starting gross salary: **₹1,00,000 – ₹1,15,000/month** + perks.
*   **Perks:** Bank accommodation/leased quarters, petrol allowance, news/briefcase allowance, concessional staff loans (vehicle/home).
