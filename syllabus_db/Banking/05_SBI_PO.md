# SBI PO - State Bank of India Probationary Officer Examination

## 1. Overview

- **Full Name**: State Bank of India - Probationary Officer (SBI PO) Examination
- **Conducting Body**: State Bank of India (SBI) - India's largest Public Sector Undertaking (PSU) bank
- **Frequency**: Conducted annually for recruitment of Probationary Officers
- **About SBI**: Largest bank in India with 23% market share by assets, 25% share of total loan and deposits market, 43rd largest bank in the world by total assets, Fortune Global 500 (163rd rank, 2025), 500+ million customers, 23,270+ branches, 63,580+ ATMs, ~245,131 employees, headquartered in Mumbai
- **Significance**: One of the most prestigious and sought-after banking examinations in India, offering entry to Junior Management Grade Scale-I

## 2. Posts Offered

- **Probationary Officer (PO)** - Junior Management Grade Scale-I
- Direct recruitment to Officer cadre
- Probation period of 2 years (with 4 advance increments during training)
- Service bond of Rs. 2 Lakhs (3 years minimum service)
- Career progression: Assistant Manager → Deputy Manager → Manager → Senior Manager → Chief Manager → AGM → DGM → GM → CGM → DMD → MD → Chairman

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

### Educational Qualification
- **Bachelor's Degree** in any discipline from a recognized University (or equivalent qualification)
- Final year students eligible (provisional admission subject to passing)
- Minimum percentage: No specific percentage criteria

### Age Limit
- **Minimum Age**: 21 years
- **Maximum Age**: 30 years
- **Age Relaxation** (as per category):
  - SC/ST: 5 years
  - OBC (Non-Creamy Layer): 3 years
  - PWD (SC/ST): 15 years
  - PWD (OBC): 13 years
  - PWD (General/EWS): 10 years
  - Ex-Servicemen/Commissioned Officers: 5 years
  - Widows/Divorced Women: 9 years (subject to conditions)

### Nationality
- Indian Citizen
- Subject of Nepal/Bhutan
- Tibetan Refugee (permanently settled in India before 1962)
- Person of Indian Origin (PIO) migrated from specified countries

## 4. Exam Pattern (3 Stages)

### Stage 1: Preliminary Examination (Qualifying)
- **Mode**: Online (Objective MCQ)
- **Duration**: 60 minutes (1 hour)
- **Total Questions**: 100
- **Total Marks**: 100
- **Sectional Cut-off**: No (only overall cut-off)
- **Marking**: 1 mark per correct answer, 0.25 negative marking

| Section | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---------|-----------|-------|----------|
| English Language | 30 | 30 | 20 min |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 35 | 35 | 20 min |
| Reasoning Ability | 35 | 35 | 20 min |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** | **60 min** |

### Stage 2: Mains Examination (Merit)
- **Mode**: Online (Objective + Descriptive)
- **Duration**: 180 minutes (3 hours) for objective + 30 min for descriptive
- **Total Marks**: 250 (200 objective + 50 descriptive)
- **Sectional Cut-off**: Yes

**Objective Test (200 marks)**:

| Test | Name of Test | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|------|--------------|-----------|-------|----------|
| I | Reasoning & Computer Aptitude | 40 | 60 | 50 min |
| II | Data Analysis & Interpretation | 30 | 60 | 45 min |
| III | English Language | 35 | 40 | 40 min |
| IV | General/Economy/Banking Awareness | 50 | 40 | 35 min |
| | **Total (A)** | **155** | **200** | **180 min** |

**Descriptive Test (50 marks)**:
- **English Letter Writing + Essay Writing**
- 2 Questions
- 50 marks
- 30 minutes duration
- Online mode
- Topics: Banking, Economy, Social Issues, General Topics
- Descriptive paper evaluated only if candidate qualifies in objective test

### Stage 3: Group Exercises & Interview
- **Psychometric Test**: 50 marks (personality assessment)
- **Group Discussion (GE)**: 20 marks
- **Interview (PI)**: 30 marks
- **Total GD/PI/GE**: 50 marks

## 5. Final Selection Process

- **Prelims**: Qualifying only (marks not counted in final merit)
- **Mains**: 250 marks scaled down to 75 marks (weightage 75%)
- **GD/PI**: 50 marks scaled down to 25 marks (weightage 25%)
- **Final Merit**: 100 marks (Mains 75 + GD/PI 25)
- Category-wise merit list prepared based on combined score
- Candidates must qualify separately in Mains and GD/PI
- Final selection based on overall merit in respective categories

## 7. Marking Scheme

- **Correct Answer**: +1 mark
- **Incorrect Answer (MCQ)**: -0.25 marks (1/4th negative marking)
- **Unanswered**: No penalty
- **Descriptive Test**: No negative marking
- **Sectional Cut-off**: No in Prelims, Yes in Mains
