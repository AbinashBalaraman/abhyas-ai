# IFSCA Grade A (Assistant Manager) Examination

## 1. Overview

| Particular | Details |
|---|---|
| **Full Name** | International Financial Services Centres Authority Officer Grade 'A' (Assistant Manager) Recruitment |
| **Conducting Body** | International Financial Services Centres Authority (IFSCA) - a unified statutory authority established in 2020 |
| **Headquarters** | GIFT City, Gandhinagar, Gujarat, India |
| **Frequency** | Periodic (typically once a year) |
| **Mode of Exam** | Online Computer-Based Test (CBT) - Phase I and Phase II + Phase III (Interview) |
| **Selection Process** | Phase I (Screening) → Phase II (Mains) → Phase III (Interview) |
| **Official Website** | https://www.ifsca.gov.in/ |

## 2. Posts Offered

- **Officer Grade 'A' (Assistant Manager) - General Stream**
- **Officer Grade 'A' (Assistant Manager) - Legal Stream**
- **Officer Grade 'A' (Assistant Manager) - IT Stream**

*Note: The General stream is the most common and popular.*

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

### 3.1 Educational Qualification (General Stream)
Candidates must possess **ANY ONE** of the following:
- **Master's Degree** in any discipline from a recognized University.
- **Bachelor's Degree in Law (LLB)** from a recognized University.
- **Bachelor's Degree in Engineering (B.E. / B.Tech)** from a recognized University.
- **Professional Qualification:** CA / CMA / CS / CFA.

### 3.2 Age Limit (as on the cut-off date)
- **Maximum Age:** 30 years (must not have exceeded 30 years on the cut-off date).
- **Age Relaxation:**
  - SC/ST: 5 years
  - OBC (Non-Creamy Layer): 3 years
  - PwBD: 10 years (General), 13 years (OBC), 15 years (SC/ST)
  - Ex-Servicemen: As per government norms

## 4. Exam Pattern

### Phase I: Online Screening Test (Two Papers - 100 Marks Each)
*Phase I is qualifying in nature. Marks scored in Phase I are not added to final merit.*

#### Paper 1: Common for all streams (100 Marks / 60 Minutes)
- **General Awareness** (especially related to the financial sector): 25 Qs
- **English Language**: 25 Qs
- **Quantitative Aptitude**: 25 Qs
- **Reasoning Ability**: 25 Qs
- **Weightage:** 100% of Paper 1 score. Cut-off: Minimum 30% marks.

#### Paper 2: General Stream (100 Marks / 40 Minutes)
- **Type:** Multiple Choice Questions (MCQ) on:
  - Financial Sector (General overview of Banking, Capital Markets, Insurance, Pension)
  - Management
  - Accounting & Finance basics
  - Companies Act, 2013 basics
  - Economics
  - Financial Markets
- **Weightage:** 100% of Paper 2 score. Cut-off: Minimum 40% marks.
- **Aggregate Cut-off for Phase I:** Minimum 40% overall (Paper 1 + Paper 2 combined).

- **Negative Marking (Phase I):** 1/4th (0.25) marks deduction for both Paper 1 & 2.

---

### Phase II: Online Mains Examination (Two Papers - 100 Marks Each)
*Marks obtained in Phase II are added to the final merit list (85% weightage).*

#### Paper 1: Common for all streams (100 Marks / 60 Minutes)
- **Format:** Online English Descriptive Test (to be typed on computer).
- **Sections:** Essay, Precis Writing, and Comprehension.
- **Weightage:** 1/3rd (33.33%) of the overall Phase II score. Cut-off: Minimum 30% marks.

#### Paper 2: General Stream (100 Marks / 60 Minutes)
- **Format:** Online Multiple Choice Questions (MCQ).
- **Topics:**
  - IFSCA Act, 2019 & GIFT City Operations
  - Union Budget & Economic Survey
  - Banking & Financial System
  - Capital Markets
  - Insurance & Pension Sector
  - Global Financial Centers & Regulatory Frameworks
- **Weightage:** 2/3rds (66.67%) of the overall Phase II score. Cut-off: Minimum 40% marks.
- **Negative Marking (Phase II):** 1/4th (0.25) marks deduction for Paper 2 only. (No negative marking for descriptive Paper 1).

---

### Phase III: Interview (75 Marks)
- Only candidates qualifying Phase II overall are shortlisted for the interview.
- **Final Selection Weightage:** **85% from Phase II** (Paper 1 + Paper 2) and **15% from the Interview**.

## 5. Detailed Syllabus (General Stream)

### 5.1 Phase I & II - Paper 2 Core Subject Syllabus

#### A. International Financial Services Centres Authority (IFSCA)
- **IFSCA Act, 2019:** Objectives, powers, functions, constitution of the Authority.
- **GIFT City (Gujarat International Finance Tec-City):** Concept of Special Economic Zone (SEZ), offshore banking units (OBU), International Financial Services Centre (IFSC) regulations.
- **Global Financial Centres:** Overview of London, New York, Singapore IFSC hubs and their comparison.

#### B. Accounting & Finance basics
- **Double Entry System:** Concept, journal, ledger, trial balance.
- **Financial Statements:** Balance sheet, profit & loss account, cash flow statement, schedules.
- **Financial Ratios:** Liquidity, solvency, activity, profitability ratios.
- **Basics of Costing:** Direct/indirect cost, marginal costing, break-even analysis.

#### C. Management
- **Concepts & Functions:** Planning, organizing, staffing, directing, controlling.
- **Leadership & Motivation:** Major theories (Maslow, Herzberg, Vroom, trait and behavioral leadership).
- **Communication:** Channels, barriers, role of IT in organizational communication.

#### D. Economics
- **Microeconomics:** Demand & supply, market structures (perfect competition, monopoly, oligopoly).
- **Macroeconomics:** National Income (GDP, GNP, NNP calculations), inflation types and control measures.
- **Monetary & Fiscal Policy:** RBI interest rate tools, Union Budget concepts, fiscal deficit, revenue accounts.

#### E. Financial Markets & Financial System
- **Banking System:** Commercial banking, Small Finance Banks, payment systems, NPA resolution.
- **Capital Markets:** Primary & Secondary markets, equity vs. debt, SEBI's role, mutual funds.
- **Insurance:** Life and general insurance basics, IRDAI regulations.
- **Pension Sector:** Pension funds, NPS, APY, PFRDA Act.

## 6. Salary Structure & Benefits

- **Pay Level:** Officer Grade 'A' (Assistant Manager) scale.
- **Starting Basic Pay:** ₹44,900/- per month.
- **Gross Monthly Salary:** ~₹1,00,000 – ₹1,15,000 (inclusive of housing allowance, DA, special allowance, local allowance).
- **Benefits:** Concessional loans, medical reimbursement, leave travel concession (LTC), NPS pension benefits, and official accommodation in Gandhinagar/GIFT City.
