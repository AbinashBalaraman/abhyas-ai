## 1. INDIAN NAVY ENTRIES

The Indian Navy (Bhāratīya Nau Senā) is the maritime branch of the Indian Armed Forces. Headquarters: Nausena Bhawan, Delhi Cantonment, Delhi. Motto: *Shaṁ No Varunaḥ* (May the Lord of Water be auspicious unto us). Navy Day: **4 December**. Officer entries fall under Executive, Engineering, Electrical, and Education branches; sailor entries are routed through Agniveer (MR/SSR/AA) and Artificer Apprentice schemes.

Official portal: https://www.joinindiannavy.gov.in/ and https://indiannavy.gov.in/

---

### 1.1 NDA — Naval Wing (National Defence Academy)

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Posts** | Permanent Commission (PC) in the Executive Branch of the Indian Navy (Naval Armament Inspectorate cadre on merit) |
| **Eligibility** | 12th (10+2) with Physics, Chemistry & Mathematics (PCM); **Unmarried Male**; Age **16.5 – 19.5 yrs**; Indian citizen |
| **Exam** | **NDA** conducted by UPSC (twice a year) — covered in `01_UPSC_NDA_CDS.md` |
| **SSB** | 5-day Services Selection Board interview at Allahabad/Dehradun/Bhopal/Coimbatore/Kapurthala |
| **Training** | 3 yrs at **NDA Khadakwasla (Pune)** → 1 yr at **INA Ezhimala** (Kerala) → afloat training |
| **Pay** | Stipend during training; Pay Level 10 (₹56,100 – ₹1,77,500) + allowances after commissioning |
| **How to Apply** | https://upsc.gov.in → Apply Online |

---

### 1.2 CDS — Naval Wing (Combined Defence Services)

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Posts** | Permanent Commission in Executive (General Service), Engineering, Electrical, Education branches |
| **Eligibility** | B.E./B.Tech (any branch for Engineering/Electrical; any graduation for Executive/Education); **Unmarried Male**; Age **19 – 22 yrs** (varies by branch — Engineering up to 23, Education up to 25 for some) |
| **Exam** | **CDS (II)** by UPSC — covered in UPSC file |
| **SSB** | 5-day SSB |
| **Training** | **Indian Naval Academy (INA), Ezhimala, Kerala** — 4 years (combined degree + naval training) |
| **Pay** | Pay Level 10 + allowances; in-hand ~₹80,000+ |

### 1.3 INET — Indian Navy Entrance Test (Officer Entry)

The flagship direct-entry exam conducted by the Indian Navy twice yearly for short-service and permanent commission officer entries.

#### Posts under INET
- **Executive Branch:** General Service (GS-X), Pilot, Naval Air Operations (Observer), IT, Law, Logistics, Naval Armament Inspectorate (NAI), Hydrography, Sports entry
- **Engineering Branch:** General Service (GS), Marine Engineering, Naval Architecture (NA), IT, Electrical (Power & Control), Engineering Sub-Lieutenant (for serving sailors)
- **Education Branch:** Post Graduate (PG) entry — MSc/MCom/MA; Naval (Technical) entry
- **Medical Branch:** Short Service Commission (SSC) Doctor (MBBS)

#### Eligibility
| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Nationality | Indian |
| Age | **19½ – 25 yrs** (relaxation for some branches — Education up to 29; Law 22-27; Sports up to 27) |
| Marital Status | Unmarried; female candidates eligible for most branches |
| Educational Qualification | B.E./B.Tech (Engineering, Electrical, Naval Architecture, IT) — 60%; B.Sc (Physics/Maths) — 60%; PG (Education); MBBS (Medical); LLB (Law); MBA/CMA/CA (Logistics) |

#### Exam Pattern

**Stage 1 — Computer Based Online Test (CBT)** at designated centres:

| Section | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| English | 50 | 100 | — |
| Reasoning & Numerical Ability | 50 | 100 | — |
| General Knowledge & Current Affairs | 50 | 100 | — |
| **Subject-specific** (Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Computer Science, etc., as applicable to post) | 50 | 100 | — |
| **Total** | **200** | **400** | **2 hours** |

- Each correct answer: +2 marks; Negative marking: -0.5 per wrong answer.

**Stage 2 — SSB Interview (5 days):**
- Day 1: Screening Test (OIR + PPDT) → Stage I
- Day 2–5: Psychological Tests (TAT, WAT, SRT, SD), GTO Tasks (GPE, PGT, HGT, Command Task, Final Group Task, Lecturette), Personal Interview, Conference
- Total ~900 candidates selected for SSB against each vacancy list.

**Stage 3 — Medical Examination** (at designated military hospitals as per IAF/MN medical standards).

#### Detailed Syllabus

**English (100 marks)**
- Grammar: Spotting Errors, Sentence Correction, Active-Passive, Direct-Indirect
- Vocabulary: Synonyms, Antonyms, One-Word Substitution, Idioms & Phrases
- Comprehension: Passage-based questions
- Cloze Test, Fill-in-the-blanks

**Reasoning & Numerical Ability (100 marks)**
- Reasoning: Coding-Decoding, Series, Analogy, Venn Diagrams, Blood Relations, Directions, Syllogism, Seating Arrangement, Puzzles
- Numerical: Arithmetic (Number System, HCF/LCM, Percentage, Profit-Loss, SI/CI, Time & Work, Time-Speed-Distance, Averages, Ratio), Algebra, Geometry (Mensuration, Triangles, Circles), Trigonometry, Statistics

**General Knowledge & Current Affairs (100 marks)**
- Static GK: History (India & World), Geography, Polity, Economics, General Science
- Defence-related: Indian Navy, Armed Forces, Wars, Honours, Defence Awards
- Current Affairs: National & International events, Awards, Summits, Sports, Books & Authors

**Subject-specific (100 marks)** — varies by branch:

| Branch / Post | Subject Syllabus |
|---|---|
| **Pilot / Observer** | Physics (Mechanics, Optics, Modern Physics) + Mathematics (Calculus, Algebra, Matrices, Probability) |
| **Naval Architecture** | Engineering Drawing, Fluid Mechanics, Strength of Materials, Thermodynamics, Naval Architecture basics |
| **IT (Information Technology)** | Computer Science: Data Structures, DBMS, OS, Networking, OOP, Programming (C/Java) |
| **Marine Engineering** | Thermodynamics, Fluid Mechanics, Heat Transfer, IC Engines, Refrigeration & AC, Machine Design |
| **Electrical** | AC/DC Machines, Power Systems, Control Systems, Electrical Measurements, Power Electronics |
| **Education (PG/Naval)** | Subject-specific PG topics (English, Physics, Maths, History, etc. — based on chosen stream) |

#### Training & Pay
- **Training:** 22 weeks (SSC) to 4 years (PC) at **INA Ezhimala**; afloat training on ships
- **Pay:** Pay Level 10 (₹56,100 base) + Military Service Pay (₹15,500) + allowances (DA, Kit, Uniform, House Rent) → in-hand ~₹80,000–1,00,000

### 1.7 Agniveer (MR / SSR) — Navy

The **Agnipath Scheme** recruits young men and women as Agniveers for 4 years of service; 25% retained for regular enrolment post service.

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Agniveer SSR (Senior Secondary Recruit)** | 12th (10+2) with PCM/PCB — 60% aggregate; Age **17 – 21 yrs**; Male & Female |
| **Agniveer MR (Matric Recruit)** | 10th (Matriculation) — 60% aggregate; Age **17 – 21 yrs**; Male only |
| **Agniveer MR (Musician)** | 10th + Music proficiency; Age **17 – 21 yrs**; Male |
| **Selection** | Online CBT (Indian Navy Recruitment website) → Physical Fitness Test (PFT) → Medical |
| **Salary** | First year ₹30,000 → 4th year ₹40,000; ₹11.71 lakh lump-sum Seva Nidhi on exit (tax-free); Life cover ₹48 lakh (incl. govt contribution) |

#### Exam Pattern for Agniveer (SSR / MR)

| Test | Type | Marks / Pass Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| Science / Mathematics / GK | CBT (MCQ) | 100 Qs × 1 mark each = 100 marks; Pass: 50% (UR/EWS), 45% (OBC/SC/ST) |
| Physical Fitness Test (PFT) | Qualifying | 1.6 km run in 7 min, push-ups, sit-ups, chin-ups (relaxation for some categories) |
| Medical | Qualifying | As per Naval medical standards |

#### Detailed Syllabus — Agniveer SSR (Science stream)

**Mathematics (10+2 level)**
- Algebra, Trigonometry, Geometry, Mensuration, Statistics, Probability
- Matrices & Determinants, Coordinate Geometry, Calculus (basic)

**Physics (10+2 level)**
- Mechanics (Laws of Motion, Work-Energy-Power, Gravitation)
- Properties of Matter, Heat, Light, Sound, Electricity & Magnetism, Modern Physics

**English**
- Grammar, Vocabulary, Comprehension, Sentence Improvement, Cloze Test

**GK & Current Affairs**
- History, Geography, Polity, Science, Defence, Awards, Sports

#### Syllabus — Agniveer MR (Matric stream)
- English, Mathematics, Science (up to 10th standard), GK & Current Affairs

## 2. INDIAN AIR FORCE ENTRIES

The Indian Air Force (Bhāratīya Vāyu Senā) is the aerial warfare branch of the Indian Armed Forces. Established 8 October 1932 (Royal Indian Air Force, renamed IAF in 1950). Headquarters: Vayu Sena Bhawan, New Delhi. Motto: *Nabhaḥ Spr̥śaṁ Dīptam* (Touch the Sky with Glory). Air Force Day: **8 October**.

Official portal: https://indianairforce.nic.in/ and https://careerairforce.nic.in/

---

### 2.1 AFCAT — Air Force Common Admission Test

The flagship entry exam conducted by **Air Force Selection Board (AFSB)** twice yearly (February & August) for Flying, Ground Duty (Technical), and Ground Duty (Non-Technical) branches.

- **Conducted by:** Indian Air Force via CDAC (afcat.cdac.in / afcat.edcil.co.in)
- **Fee:** ₹649 (~)
- **Duration:** 2 hours
- **Negative marking:** -1 for each wrong answer (1 mark per correct)
- **Result validity:** 2 years for all branches

#### Posts / Branches

| Branch | Sub-cadre | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Flying Branch** | Fighter Pilot, Transport, Helicopter | Pilot in combat aircraft, transport planes, helicopters |
| **Ground Duty (Technical)** | AE (L) — Electronics, AE (M) — Mechanical | Aeronautical Engineers in maintenance, radar, communications |
| **Ground Duty (Non-Tech)** | Administration, Accounts, Education, Logistics, Meteorology | Ground officers in IAF stations |

#### Eligibility

| Branch | Age (as on joining date) | Qualification |
|---|---|---|
| **Flying Branch** | **20 – 24 yrs** (extendable to 26 with valid Commercial Pilot Licence — CPL) | 10+2 with **50% each in Maths & Physics** + Graduation (3-yr degree in any discipline) / B.E./B.Tech (4-yr) with **minimum 60%** OR qualified Associate Membership of Institution of Engineers/Aeronautical Society of India with 60% |
| **GD (Technical)** — AE(L) | 20 – 26 yrs | 10+2 with 50% in Physics & Maths + 4-yr B.E./B.Tech in Electronics/Computer/Telecom/Electrical disciplines with 60% |
| **GD (Technical)** — AE(M) | 20 – 26 yrs | 10+2 with 50% in Physics & Maths + 4-yr B.E./B.Tech in Mechanical/Aerospace/Automotive/Production/Metallurgy with 60% |
| **GD (Non-Tech)** — Admin/Accts/Edn/Log | 20 – 26 yrs | Graduation (3-yr) with 60%; PG 50% for Education branch |
| **GD (Non-Tech)** — Meteorology | 20 – 26 yrs | PG (50%) in Physics/Maths/Geography/Geo-physics/Statistics/Computer Applications/Environmental Sciences with Maths & Physics at graduation (55%) |

**Marital Status:** Single; Below 25 yrs at time of joining must be unmarried.

#### Exam Pattern

**Stage 1 — AFCAT Online (for all branches):**
| Section | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| English | ~25 | ~25 | — |
| General Awareness | ~25 | ~25 | — |
| Numerical Ability | ~25 | ~25 | — |
| Reasoning & Military Aptitude Test (RAGA) | ~25 | ~25 | — |
| **Total** | **100** | **300** | **2 hours** |

- +3 marks per correct; -1 per wrong (each Q = 3 marks)

**Stage 1a — Engineering Knowledge Test (EKT)** — for **GD (Technical) only**:
| Section | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Subject-specific (Mechanical / Electronics) | 50 | 150 | 1 hour |
| **Total** | **50** | **150** | **1 hour** |

- Mandatory for AE(L) and AE(M); candidates must qualify separately.

**Stage 2 — AFSB (Air Force Selection Board) Interview (5 days)** at **Dehradun, Mysuru (Mysore), Gandhinagar, Varanasi, Guwahati**:
- **Day 1:** Officer Intelligence Rating (OIR — verbal & non-verbal reasoning) + Picture Perception & Discussion Test (PPDT)
- **Day 2:** Psychological Tests (TAT, WAT, SRT, SD)
- **Day 3–4:** Group Tasks (GTO): Outdoor tasks, Group Discussions, Lecturette, Command Task
- **Day 5:** Personal Interview + Conference
- **Flying branch candidates:** Additional **CPSS (Computerised Pilot Selection System)** — INSTA instrument battery (must pass; only one attempt in lifetime)

**Stage 3 — Medical Examination (AFCME/CAMA)** at Air Force Central Medical Establishment (AFCME), New Delhi.

#### Detailed Syllabus — AFCAT

**English**
- Comprehension Passages
- Vocabulary (Synonyms, Antonyms, Idioms & Phrases, One-Word Substitutes)
- Cloze Test, Fill-in-the-blanks, Sentence Completion
- Error Spotting, Sentence Correction, Active-Passive, Direct-Indirect
- Rearrangement of Sentences

**General Awareness**
- History (Indian Freedom Struggle, Ancient, Medieval, Modern — World History)
- Geography (Indian & World — Physical, Economic, Social)
- Polity (Constitution, Parliament, PM/President, Fundamental Rights/Duties, Panchayati Raj)
- Economics (Indian Economy, Five Year Plans, Banking, Inflation, Taxation)
- General Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology — everyday applications)
- Defence-related: IAF history, aircraft, missiles, awards, wars, operations
- Current Affairs: National & International, Awards (Nobel, Bharat Ratna, etc.), Summits, Sports, Books

**Numerical Ability**
- Number System, Simplification, HCF/LCM
- Percentage, Profit & Loss, Discount, Simple & Compound Interest
- Ratio & Proportion, Averages, Partnership, Mixture & Alligation
- Time & Work, Pipes & Cisterns, Time-Speed-Distance, Boats & Streams
- Algebra, Geometry, Mensuration, Trigonometry
- Data Interpretation (Tables, Bar Graphs, Pie Charts, Line Graphs)
- Probability, Statistics

**Reasoning & Military Aptitude Test (RAGA)**
- Verbal Reasoning: Series, Analogy, Coding-Decoding, Blood Relations, Directions, Syllogism, Venn Diagrams, Ranking, Seating Arrangement, Puzzles, Statement-Conclusion
- Non-Verbal Reasoning: Embedded Figures, Pattern Completion, Odd-one-out, Mirror/Water Images, Paper Folding/Cutting
- Military Aptitude: Spatial ability, situational judgement, problem-solving under pressure (military scenarios)

#### Detailed Syllabus — EKT (Engineering Knowledge Test)

**For AE (Mechanical) / Mechanical Engineering candidates:**
- Engineering Mechanics: Statics, Dynamics, Kinetics
- Strength of Materials: Stress, Strain, Bending, Torsion
- Theory of Machines: Kinematics, Dynamics of Machines
- Thermodynamics: Laws, Cycles, IC Engines
- Fluid Mechanics: Bernoulli, Flow measurement, Turbomachinery
- Heat Transfer: Conduction, Convection, Radiation
- Manufacturing: Casting, Forming, Welding, Machining
- Machine Design: Joints, Gears, Bearings

**For AE (Electronics / CS / IT / Electrical) candidates:**
- Engineering Mathematics: Linear Algebra, Differential Equations, Probability
- Networks: AC/DC, Theorems, Transient Analysis
- Electronic Devices & Circuits: Diodes, BJTs, MOSFETs, Amplifiers
- Digital Electronics: Logic Gates, Combinational/Sequential Circuits, Microprocessors
- Signals & Systems: LTI Systems, Fourier, Z-Transform
- Communication: Analog & Digital, Modulation, Antenna, Wave Propagation
- Data Structures & Algorithms (for CS/IT): Arrays, Linked Lists, Trees, Graphs, Sorting, Searching
- Object-Oriented Programming (OOP): C++/Java concepts
- DBMS: Normalization, SQL, Transactions
- Operating Systems: Process, Memory, File Systems, Deadlocks
- Computer Networks: OSI/TCP-IP, Routing, Protocols

#### Training & Pay
- **Flying Branch:** Initial training at **Air Force Academy (AFA), Dundigal, Hyderabad** — 1.5 years (Stage 1 & 2 in Pilatus PC-7, Stage 3 & 4 in Hawk); ~74 weeks total
- **GD (Technical/Non-Tech):** AFA Dundigal — 1.5 years; followed by specialised training at Air Force Technical College/Air Force Administrative College (AFAC)
- **Pay:** Pay Level 10 (₹56,100 base) + MSP ₹15,500 + allowances; in-hand ~₹85,000–1,00,000

### 2.4 Airmen (X & Y Group) — Indian Air Force

Recruitment of **Airmen** in the IAF — technical and non-technical trades.

| Group | Stream | Qualification | Trades |
|---|---|---|---|
| **X Group (Technical)** | Science with PCM | 12th with **PCM, 50% aggregate** + 50% in English | Aircraft Technician, Communication Technician, Electronics Technician, Mechanical System, Propulsion System, Workshop Trade, etc. |
| **Y Group (Non-Tech)** | Any stream | 12th pass with **50% aggregate** + 50% in English | Admin, Accounts, Logistics, Medical Assistant, IAF Police, IAF Security, etc. |
| **Age** | 17 – 21 yrs (as on date of joining); Upper age relaxation for some categories |

#### Selection Process — Airmen

**Phase I — Online Test (CBT):**
| Section | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| English | 20 | 20 | — |
| Mathematics | 25 | 25 | — |
| Physics (for X Group) / RAGA (for Y Group) | 25 | 25 | — |
| **Total** | **70** | **70** | **60 min** |

- Negative marking: -0.25 per wrong answer
- Pass: 50% (UR/EWS), 40% (OBC), 35% (SC/ST)

**Phase II — Physical Fitness Test (PFT)** (qualifying, for those who clear Phase I cut-off):
- 1.6 km run in 6 min 30 sec
- Push-ups: 10 minimum
- Sit-ups: 10 minimum
- Chin-ups: 3 minimum
- Squat (Asian): 20

**Phase III — Adaptability Test:** Assessment of suitability for military life — group tasks, psychometric tests, individual interview.

**Phase IV — Medical Examination:** Conducted by AF Board Medical Officers as per IAF medical standards.

#### Detailed Syllabus — Airmen

**English (10+2 level)**
- Reading Comprehension
- Vocabulary: Synonyms, Antonyms, Idioms, One-Word Substitutes
- Grammar: Spotting Errors, Sentence Correction, Fill-in-the-blanks
- Cloze Test, Para-jumbles

**Mathematics (10+2 level)**
- Number System, HCF/LCM, Ratio, Percentage
- Profit & Loss, SI/CI, Time & Work, Speed & Distance
- Algebra, Geometry, Mensuration, Trigonometry, Statistics

**Physics (X Group) — 10+2 level**
- Units & Measurements, Mechanics (Laws of Motion, Gravitation, Work-Energy-Power)
- Properties of Matter, Sound, Light (Optics), Heat
- Electricity & Magnetism, Modern Physics (Atoms, Nuclei)
- Electronics & Communication basics

**RAGA — Reasoning and General Awareness (Y Group)**
- Reasoning: Verbal & Non-Verbal (Series, Analogy, Coding-Decoding, Blood Relations, Venn Diagrams, Directions, Syllogism, Mirror Images)
- General Awareness: History, Geography, Polity, Economics, General Science, Defence (esp. IAF), Current Affairs

#### Training & Pay
- **Training:** **Basic Military Training School (BMTS), Belagavi** — ~22 weeks; followed by trade-specific training at various IAF stations (~1 year total)
- **Pay:** Group X — 7th CPC Pay Level 5 (₹29,200 – ₹92,300); Group Y — Pay Level 4 (₹25,500 – ₹81,100); in-hand ₹35,000 – ₹55,000
