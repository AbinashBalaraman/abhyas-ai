## 1. BSF (Border Security Force)

**Overview**: The BSF is India's primary border guarding force, established on **1 December 1965** post the Indo-Pak War of 1965. It guards India's borders with Pakistan (2,290 km) and Bangladesh (4,097 km). BSF is the **world's largest border security force** with **193 battalions** and a sanctioned strength of **~2.7 lakh personnel**. Motto: *"Jeevan Paryant Kartavya"* (Duty Unto Death). Current DG: Shri Praveen Kumar, IPS. Annual Budget: ₹29,567.64 crore (2026-27).

**Recruitment Portal**: https://rectt.bsf.gov.in/

### Posts Offered
- **Constable (General Duty)** - 10th Pass
- **Constable (Tradesman)** - 10th + trade certificate
- **Constable (Driver)** - 10th + Heavy Vehicle Driving License
- **Head Constable (Ministerial)** - 12th + typing
- **Head Constable (RO/RM)** - 12th with Science + ITI/Diploma
- **Head Constable (Carpenter, Electrician, etc.)** - 12th + trade
- **Assistant Sub-Inspector (ASI)** - 12th/Graduation
- **Sub-Inspector (SI)** - Bachelor's Degree
- **Inspector** - through LDCE/Promotion
- **Assistant Commandant** - through UPSC CAPF AC Exam

### Eligibility (By Post)
| Post | Education | Age Limit |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| Constable (GD) | 10th Pass (Matriculation) | 18-23 years |
| Constable (Tradesman) | 10th + relevant trade | 18-23 years |
| Constable (Driver) | 10th + HMV License + 3 yrs experience | 18-25 years |
| Head Constable | 12th Pass + relevant qualification | 18-25 years |
| ASI | 12th/Graduation (post-specific) | 18-25 years |
| SI | Bachelor's Degree | 20-25 years |
| AC (CAPF) | Bachelor's Degree | 20-25 years |

### Exam Pattern - Constable (Tradesman)
**Phase 1: Physical Standards Test (PST) & Physical Efficiency Test (PET)**
- Height & Chest measurement
- Document verification
- Detailed Medical Examination (DME)

**Phase 2: Written Examination** (Objective Type - OMR/CBT)
- **General Knowledge/Awareness** - 25 questions
- **Mathematics** - 25 questions
- **Reasoning & General Intelligence** - 25 questions
- **English/Hindi** - 25 questions
- **Total: 100 questions, 100 marks, 2 hours**
- **Negative marking: 0.25 marks** for each wrong answer

**Phase 3: Trade Test** (Qualifying)
- Practical test of the relevant trade

### Detailed Syllabus - Constable

**General Knowledge & Current Affairs**
- Indian History (Ancient, Medieval, Modern)
- Indian Geography (Physical, Economic)
- Indian Polity & Constitution
- Indian Economy
- General Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology - 10th level)
- Current Affairs (National & International)
- Sports, Awards, Books, Authors
- Important Days/Events

**Mathematics (10th Standard)**
- Number System, HCF, LCM
- Simplification, Decimals, Fractions
- Percentage, Average, Ratio & Proportion
- Profit & Loss, Simple & Compound Interest
- Time & Work, Time & Distance
- Mensuration, Geometry
- Algebra, Trigonometry (basic)

**Reasoning & General Intelligence**
- Analogy, Classification, Series
- Coding-Decoding
- Blood Relations, Directions
- Venn Diagrams, Syllogism
- Non-verbal reasoning (Figure counting, Mirror/Water images, Paper folding)
- Statement & Conclusions
- Puzzles, Seating Arrangement

**English/Hindi (10th/12th level)**
- Comprehension
- Grammar (Parts of Speech, Tenses, Voice)
- Vocabulary (Synonyms, Antonyms, Idioms)
- Sentence Correction
- Translation (Hindi to English & vice versa)
- Letter/Paragraph writing

### Physical Standard Test (PST) - BSF
| Category | Height (Male) | Chest (Male) | Height (Female) |
|----------|--------------|--------------|-----------------|
| General/OBC | 170 cm | 80-85 cm | 157 cm |
| SC/ST | 162.5 cm | 77-82 cm | 150 cm |
| Garhwal/Kumaon/Dogras/Marathas | 165 cm | 78-83 cm | 155 cm |
| Candidates from NE states | 162.5 cm | 77-82 cm | 150 cm |

### Physical Efficiency Test (PET)
- **1.6 km run in 6.5 minutes** (qualifying)
- **Long Jump**: 3.65 m (3 attempts) - Male
- **High Jump**: 1.2 m (3 attempts) - Male
- Female: 1.6 km run, Long Jump 2.7 m, High Jump 0.9 m

### Salary Structure
- **Constable (GD/Tradesman)**: Pay Level-3, ₹21,700-69,100 + DA + allowances
- **Head Constable**: Pay Level-4, ₹25,500-81,100
- **ASI**: Pay Level-5, ₹29,200-92,300
- **SI**: Pay Level-6, ₹35,400-1,12,400
- **AC**: Pay Level-10, ₹56,100-1,77,500
- Additional benefits: Ration, Uniform, Quarters, Medical, LTC, Pension

### BSF Tradesman Specific
Trades include: Cook, Water Carrier, Washerman, Barber, Sweeper, Mali, Carpenter, Electrician, etc. Recruitment is through Staff Selection Commission (SSC) or BSF recruitment portal with relaxed PST/PET standards.

### BSF Constable Driver
- **Eligibility**: 10th Pass + Valid HMV Driving License
- Must possess transport vehicle license for 3 years
- Age: 18-25 years
- **Trade Test**: Driving test + basic vehicle maintenance
- Written test covers: Traffic signs, vehicle mechanism, road rules

## 2. CISF (Central Industrial Security Force)

**Overview**: CISF was established on **10 March 1969** under the CISF Act, 1968. It provides security to industrial units, airports (66 airports), seaports, Delhi Metro, Parliament, nuclear plants, and heritage monuments. Current strength: **1.48-2 lakh personnel**. Motto: *"संरक्षण एवं सुरक्षा"* (Protection and Security). Current DG: Shri Praveer Ranjan, IPS. Annual Budget: ₹16,828.85 crore (2026-27).

**Official Website**: https://cisf.gov.in/

### Posts Offered
- **Constable (Fire)** - 12th with Science
- **Constable (General Duty)** - 10th Pass
- **Head Constable (Ministerial)** - 12th + typing
- **ASI (Steno)** - 12th + stenography
- **Inspector (Executive)** - Graduation
- **Sub-Inspector (SI)** - Graduation
- **Assistant Commandant (AC)** - Graduation (through UPSC CAPF)

### Eligibility
| Post | Education | Age Limit |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| Constable (Fire) | 12th with Science (Physics, Chemistry, Maths) | 18-25 years |
| Constable (GD) | 10th Pass | 18-23 years |
| Head Constable (Ministerial) | 12th Pass + English typing 30 wpm or Hindi 25 wpm | 18-25 years |
| ASI (Steno) | 12th + Steno dictation 80 wpm (10 min), transcription | 18-25 years |
| SI | Bachelor's Degree | 20-25 years |
| AC | Bachelor's Degree | 20-25 years |

### Exam Pattern - Constable (Fire)

**Phase 1: PST/PET & Documentation & Medical**
- Physical Standard Test (PST)
- Physical Efficiency Test (PET)
- Document verification
- Detailed Medical Examination

**Phase 2: Written Exam** (Objective)
- **General Intelligence & Reasoning** - 25 Qs
- **General Knowledge & General Awareness** - 25 Qs
- **Elementary Mathematics** - 25 Qs
- **English/Hindi** - 25 Qs
- **Total: 100 Qs, 100 marks, 2 hours**

**Phase 3: Trade Test (Qualifying)**
- Physical test of firefighting tasks
- Ladder climbing, hose running, rescue operations

### Detailed Syllabus - CISF Constable (Fire)

**Physics (10+2 level)**
- Mechanics: Force, Motion, Work, Energy, Power
- Heat, Light, Sound
- Electricity & Magnetism: Current, Voltage, Resistance
- Modern Physics basics

**Chemistry (10+2 level)**
- Elements, Compounds, Mixtures
- Acids, Bases, Salts
- Periodic Table
- Chemical Reactions
- Combustion, Fire Chemistry

**Mathematics (10th level)**
- Arithmetic, Algebra, Geometry
- Mensuration
- Statistics (basic)

**General Knowledge**
- History, Geography, Polity
- General Science
- Current Affairs

- **Female**: 150 cm (General/OBC)

### Salary Structure

- **SI**: Pay Level-6, ₹35,400-1,12,400

- **Eligibility**: 12th Pass
- **Typing**: English 30 wpm OR Hindi 25 wpm
- **Age**: 18-25 years
- **Exam**: Written (GK, Maths, Reasoning, English/Hindi) + Typing Test

## 3. CRPF (Central Reserve Police Force)

**Overview**: CRPF, India's **largest CAPF** with **247 battalions** and **3.13+ lakh personnel**, was raised on **27 July 1939** as Crown Representative's Police. Renamed CRPF on **28 December 1949** under the CRPF Act, 1949. Motto: *"सेवा और निष्ठा"* (Service and Loyalty). Headquarters: CGO Complex, New Delhi. Current DG: Shri Gyanendra Pratap Singh, IPS. Annual Budget: ₹38,517.93 crore (2026-2027).

**Special Units**: Rapid Action Force (RAF), CoBRA (Commando Battalion for Resolute Action), Special Duty Group, VIP Security.

**Official Website**: https://crpf.gov.in/

## 4. ITBP (Indo-Tibetan Border Police)

**Overview**: ITBP was raised on **24 October 1962** in the wake of the Sino-Indian War. It guards the **3,488 km India-China border** from Karakoram Pass (Ladakh) to Jachep La (Arunachal Pradesh). Personnel are deployed at extreme altitudes (9,000-18,800 ft) where temperatures drop to -45°C. Current DG: Sh. Shatrujeet Kapur, IPS. Specialization: Mountaineering, skiing, disaster response (First Responder in Himalayas).

**Official Website**: https://itbpolice.nic.in/

## 7. NSG (National Security Guard)

**Overview**: NSG is the **counter-terrorism and counter-hijacking force** of India, raised on **16 October 1984** post Operation Blue Star. Often called the "**Black Cats**" due to their black uniforms. Functions under the **Ministry of Home Affairs**.

**Recruitment Note**: NSG has **limited direct recruitment**. Most personnel are **seconded on deputation** from other CAPF/Army for 3-4 years. Special Action Group (SAG) draws from Indian Army Para/Para SF.

### Posts (Mostly Through Deputation)
- **Constable (GD)** - rare
- **Combat Engineers** - through deputation
- **Communication specialists** - through deputation
- **Medical staff** - through deputation
- **ACs and Commandants** - seconded from Army/CRPF/BSF

### Limited Direct Recruitment
- **NSG Combat engineers** (occasional)
- **IT/civilian specialists** (occasional)

### Physical Standards (NSG - Highly Demanding)
- Height: 170+ cm
- 5 km run in 22-25 min
- 1.6 km cross country
- Steeplechase, rope climbing, obstacle course

### Salary
- Pay as per deputation rank + NSG special allowance (₹17,300/month)
- Special Duty Allowance for hazardous operations

### Best For
Highly trained personnel from other forces; not a typical entry point for fresh candidates.

## 8. CAPF AC (UPSC Central Armed Police Forces Assistant Commandant)

**Common exam for AC posts in BSF, CRPF, CISF, ITBP, SSB, Assam Rifles**

- **Conducting Body**: UPSC
- **Frequency**: Once a year
- **Posts**: Assistant Commandant (Group A, Gazetted)

### Eligibility
- **Education**: Bachelor's Degree from recognized university
- **Age**: 20-25 years (relaxation for SC/ST/OBC)
- **Nationality**: Indian citizen
- **Physical**: As per CAPF standards

### Exam Pattern
**Paper 1: General Ability and Intelligence** (Objective - 250 marks, 2 hours)
- General Mental Ability
- General Science
- Current Events
- Indian Polity, Economy
- History, Geography
- Quantitative Aptitude

**Paper 2: General Studies, Essay and Comprehension** (Descriptive - 200 marks, 3 hours)
- Essay writing
- Comprehension passages
- Precis writing
- General Studies

**Physical Standards Test (PST)**
- Men: 165 cm height, 78-83 cm chest
- Women: 157 cm height

**Physical Efficiency Test (PET)**
- Men: 100 m in 16 sec, 800 m in 3 min, Long Jump 3.5 m, High Jump 1.05 m, Shot put 4.5 m
- Women: 100 m in 18 sec, 800 m in 4 min, Long Jump 3 m, High Jump 0.9 m

**Medical Examination**: Detailed

**Interview/Personality Test** (150 marks)

### Total Marks: 600 (250 + 200 + 150)

## 13. Common Eligibility Conditions (All CAPF)

### Age Relaxation
- SC/ST: 5 years
- OBC: 3 years
- Ex-servicemen: 3 years + service period
- Central Government employees: Up to 40 years (for certain posts)
- Widows/divorced women: 5 years

### Nationality
- Indian citizen (mandatory)

### Medical Standards
- **Vision**: 6/6 in one eye, 6/9 in other (correctable to 6/6)
- **Color blindness**: Disqualification for certain posts
- **Height & Weight**: As per standards
- **No major diseases**: Heart, kidney, lungs

### Document Verification
- 10th/12th/Degree certificates
- Domicile certificate
- Caste certificate (if applicable)
- Character certificate
- Photo ID proof (Aadhaar, etc.)

### Important Tips
1. Maintain physical fitness from start (1.6 km/5 km run daily)

3. Solve previous year papers (last 10 years)
4. Read newspaper daily for current affairs
5. Focus on quantitative aptitude basics
6. Join online test series
7. Stay updated with official recruitment notifications

## 14. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**Q1: Can I apply for multiple CAPF simultaneously?**
Yes, you can apply for multiple posts in different CAPF as long as eligibility criteria are met and dates don't clash.

**Q2: Is there any upper age relaxation for OBC?**
Yes, 3 years for OBC (Non-Creamy Layer).

**Q3: What is the probation period?**
Generally 2 years for all CAPF ranks.

**Q4: Can females apply for all posts?**
Yes, female candidates can apply for most posts. For combat roles, the physical standards are relaxed.

**Q5: Is there any negative marking?**
Yes, 0.25 marks deducted for each wrong answer in most CAPF exams.

**Q6: How long is the training period?**
- Constables: 6-9 months
- HC/ASI: 6-12 months
- SI: 1 year
- AC (CAPF): 1.5 years at various academies

**Q7: Can final year students apply?**
Yes, they can appear provisionally, but must produce the certificate at document verification.

**Q8: What is the duty pattern?**
Generally, 7 days duty, 3 days off (varies by location and operational requirements). Border postings have rotation every 2-3 years.

**Q9: Are accommodation and ration provided?**
Yes, free accommodation (barracks for constables), ration, uniform, and medical facilities are provided.

**Q10: Is there a pension after retirement?**
Yes, all CAPF personnel are eligible for pension under CCS (Pension) Rules after 20 years of service (now under NPS for new recruits).
