# SSB Interview & Other Major Defence Examinations

Comprehensive guide to the Services Selection Board (SSB) interview process and major defence examinations in India — covering SSB 5-day procedure, CDS, AFCAT, Agniveer/Agnipath scheme, CAPF AC, Coast Guard exams, preparation books, and official links.

---

## 1. SSB Interview — Comprehensive Guide

### Overview

- The **Services Selection Board (SSB)** is the organisation that conducts interviews for defence aspirants who want to serve in the **Indian Armed Forces** (Indian Army, Indian Navy, Indian Air Force, Indian Coast Guard).
- It is an intensive **five-day evaluation process** that tests intelligence, personality, and officer-like qualities.
- **14 SSB centres** across India — 4 for the Indian Army, 5 for the Indian Navy, 5 for the Indian Air Force.
- **Key locations**: Allahabad, Bhopal, Bangalore, Kolkata, Kapurthala, Varanasi.
- **Goal**: Assess **15 Officer Like Qualities (OLQ)** in candidates through a panel of assessors — Psychologists, Group Testing Officers (GTO), and Interviewing Officers.
- **No right or wrong answers** — selection is based on the concept of **"Manasa, Vacha, Karmana"** (thought, word, action).
- Attempts for SSB are **unlimited**; however, NDA/NA and PABT can be attempted only **once in a lifetime**.
- **Screening ratio**: ~5:1 (only 1 in 5 candidates clears Day 1).
- After recommendation, candidates undergo a **medical examination (4–5 days)** before being placed on a merit list.

### Stage 1: Screening (Day 1)

#### OIR (Officer Intelligence Rating) Test
- **Verbal & Non-Verbal Reasoning**
- ~50 questions each (verbal + non-verbal)
- Duration: ~30–40 minutes
- Tests logical reasoning, pattern recognition, analogies, series, coding-decoding, etc.

#### PPDT (Picture Perception & Description Test)
- A hazy/blurred picture is shown for **30 seconds**.
- Candidates record the number of characters, mood, age, and gender of the **main character**.
- **4 minutes** to write a story (~70 words) — must tell a story, not just describe the picture.
- **Group Discussion** phase: each candidate narrates their story in 1 minute, then the group creates a common story.
- **Result announced same day** — unscreened candidates are sent home.

#### PIQ (Personal Information Questionnaire)
- Screened candidates fill in the **PIQ** — basis for the personal interview.
- Includes: parents' details, education, work experience, hobbies, achievements, etc.

---

### Stage 2: Psychology Tests (Day 2)

#### TAT (Thematic Apperception Test)
- **12 clear pictures + 1 blank slide**.
- Each picture shown for **30 seconds**, **4 minutes** to write a story.
- Last slide is a blank slide — write a story of choice.
- No group discussion; stories reveal personality.

#### WAT (Word Association Test)
- **60 words** shown sequentially.
- Each word displayed for **15 seconds**.
- Candidates write the first thought/sentence that comes to mind.

#### SRT (Situation Reaction Test)
- Booklet of **60 situations**.
- **30 minutes** total time.
- Write **3 responses** to each situation (what you would do, think, feel).

#### SD (Self Description)
- Write your opinion of:
  - Your **parents'** opinion of you
  - Your **teachers'/employers'** opinion of you
  - Your **friends'** opinion of you
  - **Your own** opinion of yourself
  - **Qualities you want to develop**

---

### Stage 2: GTO (Group Testing Officer) Tests (Day 3–4)

#### GD (Group Discussion)
- 2–3 topics per GD.
- 20–30 minutes each.
- Topics: current affairs, social issues, defence-related.

#### GPE (Group Planning Exercise)
- 5–6 problems.
- Each problem has a **map/model, time, resources, and constraints**.
- Group must reach a common solution.

#### PGT (Progressive Group Task)
- **4 obstacles** with increasing difficulty.
- Strict rules: distance limits, noise limits, time limits.

#### HGT (Half Group Task)
- **3 obstacles** — half the group works together.

#### Lecturette
- Candidate chooses 1 of 4 topics.
- **3 minutes** to deliver a speech.

#### IO (Individual Obstacles)
- **10 obstacles** to cross.
- **3 minutes** total.
- Each obstacle has specific rules.

#### CT (Command Task)
- **2 followers** assigned.
- Candidate becomes the **commander** for 1 obstacle.

#### FGT (Final Group Task)
- **1 final obstacle** for the whole group.
- Often the most challenging — tests group dynamics.

#### SNR (Snake Race / Group Obstacle Race)
- Group race with physical obstacles.
- Snake-like rules: whole group stays connected.

---

### Stage 2: Personal Interview (Day 3–4)

- **25–45 minutes** face-to-face with the **Interviewing Officer (IO)**.
- Questions about:
  - Personal life and family background
  - Education and work experience
  - Hobbies, interests, and achievements
  - Current affairs (national/international)
  - Knowledge of **Armed Forces** (organisation, weapons, ranks, recent events)
  - PIQ-based questions (cross-verification)
  - Situational questions

---

### Conference (Day 5)

- All assessors (Psychologist, GTO, IO) discuss every candidate.
- Final assessment meeting with the **Presiding Officer**.
- The assessors have a brief conversation with each candidate — looking for **confidence, honesty, positivity**.
- **Final result announced same day**:
  - **Recommended** → proceed to medical examination
  - **Not Recommended** → may reappear in next attempt

---

### 15 OLQ (Officer Like Qualities) Tested

The SSB assesses candidates on **15 Officer Like Qualities** grouped under **4 factors**:

#### Factor 1: Intelligence
- Reasoning ability
- Ability to absorb and apply
- Knowledge and awareness

#### Factor 2: Social
- Social adaptability
- Cooperation
- Sense of responsibility
- Ability to influence and lead

#### Factor 3: Dynamic
- Initiative
- Determination
- Courage (physical & moral)
- Stamina
- Self-confidence
- Speed of decision

#### Factor 4: Moral
- Honesty
- Integrity
- Loyalty to the nation

## 2. CDS — Combined Defence Services Examination (UPSC)

**Conducting body**: UPSC | **Frequency**: Twice a year | **Mode**: Offline (OMR-based)

### Eligibility
- **IMA, INA, AFA**: Graduation (any stream) | Unmarried | Age: 19–24
- **OTA (Men & Women)**: Graduation | Unmarried (Men), Unmarried/Married (Women) | Age: 19–25

### Exam Pattern

| Subject | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| English | 100 | 100 | 2 hours |
| General Knowledge | 100 | 100 | 2 hours |
| Elementary Mathematics | 100 | 100 | 2 hours |
| **Total** | **300** | **300** | **6 hours** |

> Note: For OTA, only English + GK is required (200 marks).

### Detailed Syllabus

#### English
- Reading Comprehension
- Sentence Correction
- Spotting Errors
- Sentence Rearrangement (Para Jumbles)
- Vocabulary — Synonyms, Antonyms
- Idioms & Phrases
- One Word Substitution
- Cloze Test
- Fill in the blanks
- Active/Passive Voice, Direct/Indirect Speech

#### General Knowledge
- **History**: Indian History (Ancient, Medieval, Modern), World History
- **Geography**: Physical, Indian, World Geography
- **Polity**: Indian Constitution, Political System, Governance
- **Economics**: Indian Economy, Budget, Five Year Plans, Basic Macroeconomics
- **Physics**: Mechanics, Optics, Electricity, Modern Physics
- **Chemistry**: Physical, Organic, Inorganic Chemistry
- **Biology**: Botany, Zoology, Human Physiology
- **Current Affairs**: National & International
- **Defence-related**: Indian Armed Forces, Weapons, Ranks, Recent Operations

#### Elementary Mathematics
- **Algebra**: Sets, Relations, Functions, Quadratic Equations, Sequences & Series, Binomial Theorem, Matrices & Determinants
- **Trigonometry**: Angles, Identities, Inverse Trig, Heights & Distances
- **Geometry**: Lines, Angles, Triangles, Circles, Quadrilaterals
- **Statistics & Probability**: Mean, Median, Mode, Variance, Probability Theorems
- **Mensuration**: Areas, Volumes, Surface Areas
- **Calculus**: Limits, Continuity, Differentiation, Integration (basic)

## 3. AFCAT — Air Force Common Admission Test

**Conducting body**: IAF | **Frequency**: Twice a year | **Mode**: Online (CBT)

### Posts Offered
- **Flying Branch** (Short Service Commission)
- **Ground Duty (Non-Technical)**: Administration, Logistics, Accounts
- **Ground Duty (Technical)**: Aeronautical Engineering, Electronics

### Eligibility
- **Flying Branch**: Graduation (any stream) with Physics & Maths at 10+2, Age 20–24
- **Ground Duty (Non-Tech)**: Graduation, Age 20–26
- **Ground Duty (Tech)**: B.E./B.Tech in relevant discipline, Age 20–26

### Exam Pattern

| Section | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| English | 25 | 75 | 2 hours |
| General Awareness | 25 | 75 | — |
| Numerical Ability | 25 | 75 | — |
| Reasoning & Military Aptitude | 25 | 75 | — |
| **Total** | **100** | **300** | **2 hours** |

> Negative marking: 1 mark deducted for each wrong answer.

### Detailed Syllabus

#### English
- Reading Comprehension
- Cloze Test
- Para Jumbles
- Vocabulary (Synonyms, Antonyms)
- Error Spotting / Sentence Correction
- Idioms & Phrases
- One Word Substitution
- Fill in the Blanks

#### General Awareness
- History (Indian & World)
- Geography (India & World)
- Polity & Constitution
- Economics & Commerce
- General Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
- Defence Knowledge (IAF, Armed Forces)
- Sports & Awards
- Current Affairs (last 6 months)

#### Numerical Ability
- Number System, HCF/LCM
- Simple & Compound Interest
- Profit & Loss, Discount
- Time & Work, Time & Distance
- Mensuration (Area, Volume)
- Data Interpretation (Tables, Charts, Graphs)
- Percentage, Ratio & Proportion, Averages
- Probability

#### Reasoning & Military Aptitude
- Pattern Recognition
- Series (Number, Letter)
- Coding-Decoding
- Syllogism
- Puzzles, Seating Arrangement
- Venn Diagrams
- Visual Reasoning
- Blood Relations, Direction Sense
- Analogy, Classification

### EKT (Engineering Knowledge Test)
- For **Technical Branch** candidates only.
- 50 questions, 60 minutes.
- Topics: Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics, Computer Science (as per specialisation).
- **No negative marking** in EKT.

## 4. Agniveer / Agnipath Scheme

**Launched**: June 2022 | **Service period**: 4 years | **Retention**: Up to 25% to Regular Army

### Overview
- **Agnipath** is a recruitment scheme for enrolling soldiers into the Indian Armed Forces for a **4-year tenure** as **Agniveers**.
- After 4 years, up to **25%** of the batch is retained and enrolled into the **Regular Army**.
- The remaining 75% are released with a **Seva Nidhi** corpus (~₹11.71 lakh as of latest revision) — tax-free, with government's contribution.

### Agniveer (Indian Army)

#### Eligibility
- **Age**: 17.5 to 21 years
- **Education**:
  - **Agniveer (General Duty)**: 10th pass (45% aggregate, 33% in each subject)
  - **Agniveer (Tradesmen)**: 8th pass / 10th pass (as per trade)
  - **Agniveer (Technical)**: 12th pass with Physics, Chemistry, Maths/English (50% aggregate, 40% in each)
  - **Agniveer (Clerk / SKT)**: 12th pass in any stream (60% aggregate, 50% in English)
  - **Havildar Education / JCO RT / Sepoy Pharma**: 12th / Diploma in Pharmacy (as per post)

#### Online CEE (Common Entrance Exam) Pattern

| Subject | Questions | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| General Knowledge | 10 | 20 |
| General Science | 10 | 20 |
| Mathematics | 15 | 30 |
| Logical Reasoning | 5 | 10 |
| **Total** | **40** | **80** (or 100 with bonus) |
| **Duration** | **60 minutes** | — |

> For **Agniveer (Technical)**: Total 50 questions, 100 marks.

#### Physical Fitness Test
- **1.6 km run**: 5 min 30 sec (Group I) / 5 min 45 sec (Group II)
- **Beam (Pull-ups)**: 6–10 (varies by age)
- **9 Feet Ditch** & **Zig-Zag Balance**

### Agniveer (Indian Navy)

#### Matric Recruit (MR)
- **Eligibility**: 10th pass (Maths & Science compulsory)
- **Age**: 17–21
- Exam: Online CEE + Physical Fitness Test (PFT) + Medical

#### Senior Secondary Recruit (SSR)
- **Eligibility**: 12th pass (Physics, Chemistry, Maths/English)
- **Age**: 17–21
- Exam: Online CEE + PFT + Medical

### Agniveer Vayu (Indian Air Force)

#### Intake
- **Science subjects**: 12th with Physics, English, Maths (50% aggregate, 50% in English)
- **Other than Science**: 12th in any stream (50% aggregate, 50% in English)
- **Age**: 17–21

#### Online Exam
- English: 20 questions
- Mathematics: 25 questions
- RAGA (Reasoning & General Awareness): 30 questions
- **Total**: 75 questions, 100 marks, 60 minutes

#### Physical Fitness
- 1.6 km run: 6 min 30 sec
- Push-ups, Sit-ups, Chin-ups

### Agniveer (Indian Coast Guard)
- **Eligibility**: 12th pass | **Age**: 18–22
- Recruitment via Coast Guard recruitment portal.
- Selection: Online exam + Physical + Medical

### Salary & Benefits (as per 7th CPC with Agniveer corpus)
- **Year 1**: ₹30,000/month (₹21,000 in-hand)
- **Year 2**: ₹33,000/month
- **Year 3**: ₹36,500/month
- **Year 4**: ₹40,000/month
- **Seva Nidhi Corpus** (4 years): ~₹11.71 lakh (tax-free, with interest)
- **Life Insurance Cover**: ₹48 lakh (contribution by Govt)
- **Risk & Hardship Allowance**, **Ration Kit & Uniform** provided

## 6. Indian Coast Guard Exams

**Conducting body**: Indian Coast Guard (ICG), Ministry of Defence

### A. Navik (General Duty) — Domestic Branch (DB)
- **Eligibility**: 10th pass (Maths & Science compulsory) | **Age**: 18–22
- **Selection**: Online Exam + Physical Fitness Test + Medical
- **Exam Pattern**:
  - Section A: Mathematics, Science, English, GK, Reasoning — 60 questions, 60 min
  - Section B: Maths & Physics (class 10 level) — 60 questions, 60 min

### B. Navik (General Duty) — Yantrik (Technical)
- **Eligibility**: 12th with Diploma (Mechanical/Electrical/Electronics) or 10th + ITI | **Age**: 18–22
- **Selection**: Online Exam + PFT + Medical

### C. Yantrik (Technical Branch)
- **Eligibility**: 12th pass (PCM) + Diploma/Engineering in relevant trade | **Age**: 18–22
- **Selection**: Online Exam + PFT + Medical

### D. Assistant Commandant (Coast Guard)
- **Eligibility**: Bachelor's degree | **Age**: 21–25
- **Selection**: UPSC Combined Graduate Level (CGL) → Coast Guard Selection Board (similar to SSB) → Medical

### Coast Guard Selection Board (CGSB)
- **Phase 1**: Preliminary Selection Board Exam (PSB) — similar to SSB screening.
- **Phase 2**: Final Selection Board — psychological tests, group tasks, personal interview, assessors' conference.

### Physical Standards
- **1.6 km run**: 7 minutes
- **20 push-ups**, **20 sit-ups**, **10 chin-ups**
- Squat thrust, 5 feet jump, 9 feet ditch climb
