# Indian Army Entry Examinations — Comprehensive Guide

The Indian Army is the land-based branch and largest component of the Indian Armed Forces, with 1,248,000 active personnel and 960,000 reserve troops — making it the largest standing volunteer army in the world. This document covers all major entry schemes for both **Permanent Commission (PC)** and **Short Service Commission (SSC)** into the Indian Army, along with the SSB interview process, the Territorial Army, and the Agniveer/Agnipath scheme.

> **Note**: CDS and NDA entries are UPSC-conducted examinations. Detailed syllabus and exam pattern for these are covered in `02_UPSC_CDS_NDA.md`. They are mentioned here only for completeness of the Indian Army entry landscape.

---

## 1. CDS (Combined Defence Services) Entry

- **Conducting Body**: UPSC
- **Commission Type**: Permanent Commission (for IMA/OTA via Men); SSC (for OTA)
- **Posts**: Indian Military Academy (IMA, Dehradun), Officers Training Academy (OTA, Chennai), Air Force Academy (AFA, Hyderabad), Naval Academy (NA, Ezhimala)
- **Eligibility**: Graduation (any discipline) for IMA/AFA/NA; Graduation for OTA (Men & Women). Age 19–24 (IMA), 19–25 (OTA).
- **Selection**: Written Exam (CDS) → SSB Interview → Medical
- **Training**: IMA – 1½ years; OTA – 49 weeks
- **Detailed syllabus**: See `02_UPSC_CDS_NDA.md`

---

## 2. NDA (National Defence Academy) Entry

- **Conducting Body**: UPSC
- **Commission Type**: Permanent Commission
- **Posts**: Indian Army, Indian Navy, Indian Air Force
- **Eligibility**: 12th (PCM for Navy/Air Force; 12th any stream for Army), 16.5–19.5 years, Unmarried Male
- **Selection**: Written Exam (NDA) → SSB Interview → Medical
- **Training**: 3 years at NDA Khadakwasla + 1 year at IMA/AFA/NA
- **Detailed syllabus**: See `02_UPSC_CDS_NDA.md`

---

## 3. Indian Army Technical Entry Scheme (TES)

- **Conducting Body**: Indian Army (joinindianarmy.nic.in)
- **Commission Type**: Permanent Commission (PC)
- **Posts**: Lieutenant, Indian Army (Technical branches — Corps of Engineers, Corps of Signals, EME)
- **Eligibility**:
  - 10+2 (PCM) with **minimum 60% aggregate** in Physics, Chemistry & Mathematics
  - Age: **16.5 to 19.5 years**
  - **Unmarried Male** Indian citizen
  - Appearing candidates also eligible
- **Selection**: **Direct SSB** (no written examination) → Medical
- **Training**: **4 years** at **Officers Training Academy (OTA), Gaya** (now also affiliated with MCTE)
- **Salary**: Pay Level 10 (7th CPC) — basic pay ₹56,100 + Military Service Pay (MSP) ₹15,500 + allowances
- **Notes**: One of the few entries to get PC directly from 10+2; the longest training period among officer entries (4 years integrated engineering + military training)

## 4. Short Service Commission (SSC) Technical

- **Conducting Body**: Indian Army
- **Commission Type**: Short Service Commission (10+4 years extendable, can opt for PC)
- **Posts**: SSC (Tech) Men (Course 65 onwards) and SSC (Tech) Women (Course 24 onwards) — Corps of Engineers, Corps of Signals, EME, Infantry (Tech Support)
- **Eligibility**:
  - **B.E./B.Tech** in relevant engineering discipline in specified streams
  - Age: **20 to 27 years** (Men), **20 to 25 years** (Women) as on date of commissioning
  - Final year students eligible to apply
  - Unmarried (married candidates of certain age also eligible)
- **Stream-Wise Eligibility** (typical):
  - **Engineering Streams**: Civil, Electrical/EEE, Mechanical, Computer Science, IT, Electronics & Telecom, Electronics & Communication, Instrumentation, Production, Industrial, Automobile, Architecture
- **Selection**:
  - Shortlisting of applications based on percentage cut-off → **Direct SSB (5 days)** → Medical
  - Some courses include a screening written test (shortlisting only — not counted for merit)
- **Training**: **49 weeks** at OTA, Chennai (combined training with SSC Non-Tech) — followed by posting to technical units
- **Detailed Syllabus** (Engineering Discipline-Specific for Shortlisting/SSB Technical):
  - **Engineering Mathematics**: Linear Algebra, Calculus, Differential Equations, Complex Variables, Probability & Statistics, Numerical Methods
  - **Core Subjects** (branch-wise):
    - *Civil*: Structural Analysis, RCC Design, Steel Design, Geotech, Hydraulics, Survey, Environmental
    - *Mechanical*: Thermodynamics, Fluid Mechanics, Machine Design, Heat Transfer, Manufacturing, Industrial Engg
    - *Electrical/EEE*: Networks, Machines, Power Systems, Control Systems, Power Electronics, Measurements
    - *Electronics/ECE/EEE*: Networks, Electronic Devices & Circuits, Digital Electronics, Signals & Systems, Communication, Microprocessors, VLSI
    - *Computer Science/IT*: Data Structures, Algorithms, OS, DBMS, CN, Software Engg, OOP
  - **General Ability**: English, GK, Current Affairs, Reasoning (for SSB Stage 1 screening only)
- **Salary**: Pay Level 10 (7th CPC)

## 5. Combined Defence Services (CDS) + SSC (Non-Technical)

- **Conducting Body**: UPSC (Written) → Indian Army (SSB)
- **Commission Type**: Short Service Commission (SSC) for both Men and Women
- **Posts**: SSC (Non-Technical) — All Arms/Services branches
- **Eligibility**:
  - **Graduation (any discipline)** from a recognized university
  - Age: **19 to 25 years** for Men, **19 to 25 years** for Women
  - For OTA entry (Men & Women)
  - CDS Exam (I) for IMA/INA/AFA, CDS Exam (II) for OTA + entry to Navy/Air Force
- **Selection**: Written (CDS) → **SSB Interview** → Medical
- **Training**: **49 weeks** at **Officers Training Academy (OTA), Chennai**
- **Salary**: Pay Level 10 (7th CPC) on commissioning as Lieutenant
- **Notes**: SSC officers can opt for Permanent Commission subject to eligibility and cadre availability. Women SSC officers are eligible for PC in specific branches per policy.

## 6. Army Cadet College (ACC) Entry

- **Conducting Body**: Indian Army (internal entry)
- **Commission Type**: **Permanent Commission (PC)**
- **Posts**: Lieutenant (on commissioning) — all Combat Arms and selected Services
- **Eligibility**:
  - Serving **Regular Army personnel** (Other Ranks / JCOs)
  - **20 to 27 years**
  - Minimum **2 years of regular service**
  - Senior Secondary School Certificate (10+2) or equivalent
  - Must clear ACC Entrance Exam (written) + SSB
- **Selection**:
  - **Screening Exam** (Written: English, Maths, GK, Current Affairs — internal) → **Direct SSB (5 days)** → Medical
- **Training**: **3 years** at **Army Cadet College (ACC), Dehradun** (Winchester Building, Indian Military Academy complex)
  - 1 year academics (graduation degree from JNU) + 1 year military training + 1 year at IMA with the regular course
- **Award**: **Permanent Commission** on successful completion
- **Notes**: This is a unique path for in-service soldiers to become commissioned officers with PC.

## 7. Special Commissioned Officers Entry (SCO)

- **Conducting Body**: Indian Army (internal entry)
- **Commission Type**: **Permanent Commission (PC)**
- **Posts**: Lieutenant, Indian Army (selected branches)
- **Eligibility**:
  - Serving **JCOs / Other Ranks (OR)** with **10+2** qualification
  - **28 to 34 years**
  - Minimum **5 years of service** (some categories may have different service requirements)
  - Medically fit
  - Must be recommended by Commanding Officer
- **Selection**: **Direct SSB (5 days)** → Medical
- **Training**: **1 year** at **Officers Training Academy (OTA), Chennai**
- **Award**: Permanent Commission
- **Notes**: Entry scheme for senior in-service JCOs/OR to obtain commission; similar to ACC but shorter training.

## 9. NCC (National Cadet Corps) Special Entry Scheme

- **Conducting Body**: Indian Army
- **Commission Type**: **Short Service Commission (SSC)**
- **Posts**: Lieutenant, Indian Army — all Arms/Services (NCC Special entry is open to both Men and Women)
- **Eligibility**:
  - **Graduation (any discipline)** from a recognized university
  - **NCC 'C' Certificate** with **'A' grading** (minimum 'B' grading with some relaxations)
  - Age: **19 to 25 years**
  - For OTA entry (49 weeks training)
  - Unmarried (or specific marital conditions as per notification)
  - Minimum 2 years service in NCC Senior Division/Wing
- **Selection**: **Direct SSB Interview (5 days)** → Medical
  - No written examination
- **Training**: **49 weeks** at **OTA, Chennai**
- **Award**: Short Service Commission
- **Notes**: Reserved 50% vacancies for NCC 'C' certificate holders with 'A' grading. NCC Special entry cadets train with CDS/SSC entries at OTA.

## 10. JAG (Judge Advocate General) Entry

- **Conducting Body**: Indian Army
- **Commission Type**: Short Service Commission (SSC) for 10+4 years; eligible for PC
- **Posts**: **Judge Advocate General Branch (Law Officer)** — Captain (on commissioning) or Lieutenant depending on service profile
- **Eligibility**:
  - **LLB degree** (3-year or 5-year integrated) with **minimum 55% marks**
  - Registered with Bar Council of India
  - Age: **21 to 35 years** (men), 21 to 35 (women) — as per latest notification
  - For Men and Women
  - Practicing advocates preferred
- **Selection**: **Direct SSB Interview (5 days)** → Medical
  - No written examination
- **Training**: **49 weeks** at OTA, Chennai
- **Subjects Tested (during SSB/Interview)**:
  - Constitutional Law, Civil Law, Criminal Law, Military Law, Jurisprudence, International Law
- **Salary**: Pay Level 10 (7th CPC) + NPA (Non-Practicing Allowance, if applicable)
- **Notes**: JAG officers serve as legal advisors to military commands; only LLB holders eligible.

## 11. Army Medical Corps (AMC) Entry

- **Conducting Body**: Indian Army (DGAFMS)
- **Commission Type**: **Permanent Commission (PC)** (since 2023 policy change — earlier SSC)
- **Posts**: **Captain, Army Medical Corps (AMC)** on direct commissioning
- **Eligibility**:
  - **MBBS degree** from a recognized medical college (MCI/NMC approved)
  - Completion of internship
  - Age: **21 to 32 years** (UR); **21 to 37 years** (OBC, SC, ST — as per relaxed norms)
  - Permanent registration with State Medical Council / NMC
- **Selection**: **Direct Interview + Medical Examination** (no SSB)
  - Interview by a board at AMC Centre & College, Lucknow
  - Tests on clinical knowledge, aptitude, motivation
- **Training**: Orientation training at AMC Centre, Lucknow
- **Salary**: Pay Level 10B (7th CPC) — basic pay ₹68,600 + MSP ₹15,500 + NPA (Non-Practicing Allowance) + allowances
- **Notes**: Short service commissioned earlier; now PC offered. Post-PG specialization also granted PC.

## 12. Armed Forces Medical Services Entry (AFMS)

- **Conducting Body**: Directorate General of Armed Forces Medical Services (DGAFMS)
- **Commission Type**: **Permanent Commission (PC)**
- **Posts**: Specialist / Super-Specialist in AFMS — Major/Lt Col rank
- **Eligibility**:
  - **MBBS + Postgraduate degree (MD/MS/DNB)** in relevant specialty
  - Age: up to **35 years** (for PG entry) — varies per specialty
  - Permanent registration with NMC/MCI
- **Selection**: **Direct** — application shortlisting → Interview at designated Command Hospital → Medical
- **Training**: Branch-specific orientation
- **Salary**: Pay Level 10B/11/12 (7th CPC) + NPA
- **Notes**: For PG-qualified specialists. AFMS doctors serve across Army, Navy, and Air Force hospitals.

## 13. SSB (Services Selection Board) Interview — Detailed Process

The SSB is a **5-day selection process** to assess candidates on 15 **Officer Like Qualities (OLQs)** grouped under 4 factors. Conducted at 13 SSB centres across India (Allahabad, Bhopal, Bangalore, Kapurthala, etc.).

### Day 1 — Stage 1: Screening

- **Officer Intelligence Rating (OIR)** Test:
  - Verbal and Non-Verbal Reasoning
  - MCQs — typically 50 questions in ~30 minutes per test
  - Topics: Series, Analogy, Coding, Blood Relations, Syllogism, Venn Diagrams, etc.
- **Picture Perception & Discussion Test (PP&DT)**:
  - A hazy picture shown for 30 seconds
  - Candidate writes a story in 4 minutes
  - Group discussion among ~10–15 candidates for 10–15 minutes
- **Result**: Candidates are either **Screened In** (proceed to Stage 2) or **Screened Out** (sent home)
- **Screening ratio**: Typically 4:1 to 6:1

### Day 2 — Stage 2: Psychology Tests

Four psychological tests assess **Mental & Social** and **Dynamic** OLQs:

1. **Thematic Appreciation Test (TAT)**:
   - 12 pictures shown, each for 30 seconds
   - Write a story on each (5 minutes each for first 11, 4 minutes for last blank slide)
   - Tests imagination, perception, ability to form ideas from incomplete stimuli
2. **Word Association Test (WAT)**:
   - 60 words shown one by one, 15 seconds each
   - Write a sentence using each word
   - Tests speed of thought, association, attitude
3. **Situation Reaction Test (SRT)**:
   - 60 situations, 30 minutes
   - Write practical responses in 3–4 lines each
   - Tests ability to handle real-life situations
4. **Self Description Test (SD)**:
   - Write your parents' opinion, friends' opinion, your own opinion, and what you would like to be
   - Tests self-awareness and personality

### Day 3 & 4 — GTO (Group Testing Officer) Tests

- **Group Discussion (GD)** — 2–3 topics, group of 8–10 candidates
- **Group Planning Exercise (GPE / PFT)** — Map-based problem solving in group; read, discuss, and write individual solution
- **Progressive Group Task (PGT)** — 4 group obstacles of increasing difficulty
- **Half Group Task (HGT)** — 3 obstacles
- **Lecturette** — 3 minutes talk on a chosen topic (1 min prep)
- **Individual Obstacles (IO)** — 10 obstacles to be completed individually within 3 minutes
- **Command Task (CT)** — 2–3 subordinates, 1 obstacle
- **Final Group Task (FGT)** — One final obstacle for the group
- **Snake Race** (optional group obstacle race) at some centres
- These test **Social** and **Dynamic** OLQs

### Day 3 & 4 — Personal Interview

- Conducted by the **Interviewing Officer (IO)** — usually a senior serving officer
- Duration: 30–45 minutes (or more)
- Covers: Family background, education, hobbies, NCC/service, current affairs, motivation, knowledge of armed forces
- Psychometric assessment: confidence, communication, leadership potential

### Day 5 — Conference

- All assessors (Psychologist, GTO, IO) along with the **Presiding Officer (PO)** discuss each candidate
- Final recommendation is made
- Candidates are called one by one and informed of the result
- **Recommended candidates** proceed to **Medical Examination** at the nearest Military Hospital

### Officer Like Qualities (OLQs) — 15 in 4 Factors

1. **Factor I — Planning & Organising (4 OLQs)**:
   - Ability to grasp instructions, plan work, deal with unforeseen problems, decision-making
2. **Factor II — Social Adjustment (4 OLQs)**:
   - Social adaptability, ability to influence, ability to live & work with others, ability to appreciate effort of others
3. **Factor III — Social Effectiveness (4 OLQs)**:
   - Initiative, determination, courage, stamina
4. **Factor IV — Dynamic (3 OLQs)**:
   - Self-confidence, liveliness, mental alertness

### SSB Syllabus Summary for OIR (Reasoning)

- **Verbal**: Series, Analogy, Coding-Decoding, Blood Relations, Syllogism, Venn Diagrams, Direction Sense, Ranking, Statement & Assumptions, Critical Reasoning
- **Non-Verbal**: Mirror/Water Images, Paper Folding/Cutting, Embedded Figures, Figure Series, Cube & Dice, Pattern Completion, Dot Situations

## 14. Territorial Army (TA) Examination

- **Conducting Body**: Indian Army (Directorate General of Territorial Army)
- **Commission Type**: Part-time commissioned officer in the TA
- **Posts**: **TA Officer** (Major, Colonel rank on seniority)
- **Background**:
  - TA was constituted by the **Territorial Army Act, 1948**
  - Headquarters: New Delhi; 5 Group HQs (Pune, Kolkata, Chandigarh, Lucknow, Udhampur)
  - Strength: ~43,000 first-line + 160,000 second-line
  - Motto: "Savdhani Va Shoorta" (Vigilance and Valour)
  - TA Day: **9 October**

### Eligibility

- **Indian citizen**
- Age: **18 to 42 years**
- **Graduation** from a recognized university
- **Gainfully employed** in civilian profession (mandatory)
- Physically and mentally fit
- For Departmental TA Units: Employee of Indian Railways / PSU (IOC, BPCL, HPCL, ONGC)

### Selection Process

- **Stage 1**: Written Examination (Objective MCQ) — 100 marks
- **Stage 2**: SSB Interview (5 days) at designated centres
- **Stage 3**: Medical Examination

### Exam Pattern (Written)

- **Mode**: Offline (OMR-based) / Online
- **Duration**: 2 hours
- **Total Marks**: 100
- **Questions**: 100 objective MCQs
- **Negative Marking**: Yes (typically 0.33 marks deducted per wrong answer)
- **Sections** (typical distribution):
  - **Reasoning & Mental Ability** — 25 Qs
  - **English Language** — 25 Qs
  - **General Knowledge** — 25 Qs
  - **Elementary Mathematics** — 15 Qs
  - **Current Affairs** — 10 Qs

### Detailed Syllabus

**1. Reasoning & Mental Ability**
- Series (Number, Letter)
- Analogy, Classification
- Coding-Decoding, Blood Relations
- Direction Sense, Ranking
- Syllogism, Venn Diagrams
- Statement & Assumptions, Conclusions
- Mirror/Water Images, Paper Folding
- Embedded Figures, Figure Counting, Dice
- Puzzles, Seating Arrangement
- Data Sufficiency, Decision Making

**2. English Language**
- Reading Comprehension
- Sentence Correction, Error Spotting
- Synonyms, Antonyms
- Idioms & Phrases, One-word Substitution
- Cloze Test, Fill in the Blanks
- Active/Passive Voice, Direct/Indirect Speech
- Tenses, Prepositions, Articles
- Vocabulary, Grammar

**3. General Knowledge**
- Indian History (Ancient, Medieval, Modern, Freedom Struggle)
- Indian Polity & Constitution
- Geography (India & World)
- Indian Economy
- General Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology — basic)
- Defence-related GK
- Awards, Books, Capitals, First in India
- Computer Basics
- Sports, Art & Culture

**4. Elementary Mathematics**
- Number System, Simplification
- Percentage, Average, Ratio & Proportion
- Profit & Loss, Simple & Compound Interest
- Time & Work, Speed & Distance
- Algebra (Basic), Geometry, Trigonometry (Basic)
- Mensuration, Data Interpretation (Tables, Bar/Line graphs)
- HCF, LCM, Probability

**5. Current Affairs**
- National & International events (last 6–12 months)
- Government schemes, summits
- Awards, Sports events
- Defence news
- UN, G20, Bilateral relations

### Salary

- **Basic TA Pay** + **rank pay** + TA allowances during embodied service
- Civilian salary from primary employment continues (TA is a part-time commitment)
- Two months of mandatory military training per year
- Entitled to all Indian Army benefits **except** gratuity and pension (computed by full years served)
- **Service Requirement**: 2 months training/year (initial 2–3 months continuous, then annual training)

### TA Ranks (Same as Army)

- Lieutenant → Captain → Major → Lt Colonel → Colonel (TA officers don't generally rise above Colonel)
- Director General (TA): Lt Gen rank, ex-Indian Army officer

### Training

- **Pre-commissioning**: 8 weeks (2 months) at Indian Military Academy or designated training centre
- **Annual training**: 2 months every year
- **Post-commissioning**: Can be called for embodied service during emergency

### Zonal Classification

- **Zone I**: Haryana, HP, J&K, Punjab, Delhi, Chandigarh
- **Zone II**: Bihar, MP, Odisha, UP, Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh, Uttarakhand
- **Zone III**: Assam, Meghalaya, Manipur, Tripura, Nagaland, Sikkim, WB, Mizoram, Arunachal, A&N
- **Zone IV**: AP, Gujarat, Kerala, TN, Rajasthan, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Goa, DNH&DD, Lakshadweep, Puducherry

## 15. Agniveer / Agnipath Scheme — Brief Overview

The **Agnipath Scheme** was introduced in June 2022 to recruit young soldiers into the Indian Army on a **4-year engagement**, with up to **25% retained** for further service of 15+ years (regular cadre). Those not retained exit with a **Seva Nidhi** corpus of ~₹11.71 lakh (tax-free).

### Eligibility

- **Age**: **17.5 to 21 years**
- Education: 8th / 10th / 12th pass (varies by post — General Duty, Technical, Clerk, Tradesman)
- Unmarried Male / Female (for some categories)
- Indian citizen

### Posts Available

- **Agniveer (General Duty)** — 10th pass
- **Agniveer (Technical)** — 12th PCM (50%)
- **Agniveer (Technical, Aviation & Ammunition Examiner)** — 12th
- **Agniveer (Clerk / Store Keeper Technical)** — 12th (any stream)
- **Agniveer (Tradesman)** — 8th / 10th
- **Agniveer (Women — Military Police)** — 10th

### Selection Process

- **Online Common Entrance Exam (CEE)**
- **Physical Fitness Test (PFT)**
- **Physical Measurement Test (PMT)**
- **Medical Examination**

### Exam Pattern — Agniveer Common Entrance Exam (CEE)

- **Mode**: Online (CBT)
- **Duration**: 60 minutes (1 hour)
- **Total Questions**: 50
- **Total Marks**: 100 (2 marks each)
- **Negative Marking**: 0.5 mark per wrong answer
- **Sections**:
  - **General Knowledge** — 15 Qs
  - **General Science** — 15 Qs
  - **Mathematics** — 15 Qs (Class 8/10/12 level)
  - **Logical Reasoning** — 5 Qs
  - **English (for Clerk)** — 25 Qs
- **Languages**: Hindi, English, and 11 regional languages

### Physical Fitness Test (PFT)

- **1.6 km Run** in:
  - 5 min 30 sec — Group I (Pass)
  - 5 min 31 sec to 5 min 45 sec — Group II
- **Beam (Pull-ups)**:
  - 10 pull-ups — Group I
  - 6–9 pull-ups — Group II
- **9 feet Ditch** (long jump)
- **Zig-Zag Balance**

### Benefits During Service

- Salary: ~₹30,000 to ₹40,000/month (starting)
- Risk & Hardship allowances
- Free ration, clothing, accommodation, medical
- **Seva Nidhi** corpus (₹11.71 lakh tax-free on exit at 4 years)
- **Death/Disability Compensation**: Up to ₹1 crore + 50% of Seva Nidhi for family
- 30% of corpus as **tax-free**; balance taxable
- **Skill Certificate** from the Army
- **Class 12 Equivalent Certificate** (if 10th pass, after 4 years)
- **Credit Priority** for CAPF/Police jobs

- **Siachen Allowance**, **High Altitude Allowance**, **Counter-Insurgency Allowance** (as applicable)

- **Pension**: 50% of last drawn basic pay (after 20 years of qualifying service)

- **Free Medical**: AFMS / ECHS
