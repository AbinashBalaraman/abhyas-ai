## 1. MHA (Ministry of Home Affairs) Direct Recruitment

**Overview**: The Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) is the nodal ministry for internal security, border management, and Centre-State coordination. Headed by the Home Minister (currently Shri Amit Shah), with Home Secretary (Govind Mohan, IAS) as bureaucratic head. MHA controls all 7 CAPFs, IB, NIA, NCRB, BPR&D, NSG, Delhi Police, and the IAFS. Annual Budget: ₹2,55,233.53 crore (2026-27). Headquarters: Kartavya Bhavan-03, Janpath, New Delhi.

**Recruitment Portal**: https://mha.gov.in/en/notifications/vacancies (mostly deputation-based; Group B/C posts advertised via SSC, UPSC, CAPF AC)

### Posts Offered
- **Section Officer** (Pay Level 8, ₹47,600-1,51,100) - via SSC CGL LDCE
- **Assistant Section Officer (ASO)** (Pay Level 7) - via SSC CGL
- **Upper Division Clerk (UDC)** (Pay Level 4) - via SSC
- **Lower Division Clerk (LDC)** (Pay Level 2) - via SSC
- **Stenographer Grade-I/II** (Pay Level 6/4) - via SSC Stenographer
- **Assistant / Office Assistant** (Pay Level 6) - via SSC CGL
- **Hindi Translator** - via SSC
- **UDC (Hindi Typing)** - dedicated posts

### Eligibility
| Post | Education | Age Limit |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| Section Officer | Graduation + 3 yrs service (LDCE) | up to 45 |
| ASO | Graduation | 18-30 |
| UDC | Graduation | 18-27 |
| LDC | 12th Pass | 18-27 |
| Steno Gr-II | 12th + Steno skill | 18-27 |
| Steno Gr-I | Graduation + Steno | 18-30 |
| Assistant | Graduation | 18-30 |

Age relaxation: SC/ST - 5 yrs, OBC - 3 yrs, PwD - 10 yrs, Ex-servicemen as per rules.

### Exam Pattern
**Tier-I (CBT - Objective)**: 200 Qs, 200 marks, 60 min
- General Intelligence & Reasoning - 50 Qs
- General Awareness - 50 Qs
- Quantitative Aptitude - 50 Qs
- English Comprehension - 50 Qs
- Negative marking: 0.50 per wrong answer

**Tier-II (Descriptive)**:
- Statistics (for Statistical posts)
- English/Hindi Essay & Letter Writing - 100 marks

**Skill Test** (Qualifying):
- Typing: LDC English 35 wpm / Hindi 30 wpm
- Stenography: 100/120 wpm transcription

### Detailed Syllabus
**General Intelligence & Reasoning**: Analogies, Classification, Series, Coding-Decoding, Blood Relations, Directions, Syllogism, Venn Diagrams, Puzzles, Seating Arrangement, Statement-Conclusion, Mirror/Water Images, Embedded Figures, Dice, Cubes, Counting Figures.

**General Awareness**: Indian History (Freedom Struggle, Constitutional Development), Geography (Indian + World), Polity (Constitution, Parliament, President, PM, SC/HC, Fundamental Rights/Duties), Economy (Five Year Plans, RBI, Budget basics, GST, Banking), General Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology up to 10+2 level), Current Affairs (National/International, Awards, Books, Sports, Capitals, PM/President Visits, Static GK).

**Quantitative Aptitude**: Number System, HCF/LCM, Simplification, Percentage, Average, Ratio & Proportion, Profit-Loss, Simple & Compound Interest, Time & Work, Pipe-Cistern, Time-Speed-Distance, Boat-Stream, Mensuration, Geometry (Triangles, Circles, Quadrilaterals), Trigonometry, Statistics (Mean, Median, Mode, Probability).

**English Comprehension**: Error Spotting, Fill in the blanks, Synonyms/Antonyms, Idioms & Phrases, One-Word Substitution, Spelling Correction, Active-Passive, Direct-Indirect, Reading Comprehension, Cloze Test, Sentence Rearrangement, Tense, Voice, Narration.

**Skill Test Details**:
- LDC: English typing 35 wpm OR Hindi 30 wpm (10 min passage)
- Steno Gr-II: Dictation 80 wpm (10 min), Transcription 50 min English / 65 min Hindi
- Steno Gr-I: 100 wpm (10 min dictation), Transcription 40 min English / 55 min Hindi

## 2. CBI (Central Bureau of Investigation)

**Overview**: India's premier investigating agency, established in 1963 under the Delhi Special Police Establishment (DSPE) Act, 1946. Functions under the Ministry of Personnel, Public Grievances and Pensions. Investigates corruption, economic offences, special crimes. Headquarters: CGO Complex, Lodhi Road, New Delhi. Annual Budget: ₹1,104.98 crore (2026-27). Conviction rate: 69-74% (recent years). Director: Shri Praveen Sood, IPS. Sanctioned strength: 7,274 (vacancies ~883).

**Recruitment Portal**: https://cbi.gov.in/vacancies

### Posts Offered
- **Sub-Inspector (SI)** - direct recruitment through SSC CGL
- **Inspector** - through LDCE/LDE from serving CBI officials
- **LDC / UDC** - ministerial posts
- **Stenographer** - Grade I/II
- **Computer Operator / Programmer** - technical cadre
- **Additional SP / Joint Director** - IPS cadre on deputation
- **SP / DIG / Additional Director** - senior IPS officers on deputation

### CBI SI - Recruitment Path
**Direct Recruitment**: Through SSC CGL (Tier I + II + III)
- Educational Qualification: Any Bachelor's Degree from recognized university
- Age Limit: 20-30 years (relaxation as per SSC norms)
- Physical Standards: As applicable to police forces

**Limited Departmental Competitive Examination (LDCE)**:
- For in-service CBI personnel (Constable to Inspector ranks)
- Internal departmental exam + seniority + ACR

### CBI Inspector
- Through LDCE (Limited Departmental Examination) for in-service CBI staff
- Promotion after 5-8 years of service as SI
- Graduate + departmental exam qualification

### Eligibility
| Post | Education | Age Limit |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| Sub-Inspector | Bachelor's Degree | 20-30 |
| Inspector | Graduation + LDCE | as per service rules |
| LDC | 12th | 18-27 |
| Steno | 12th + Steno | 18-27 |
| Computer Operator | Graduation + typing | 18-27 |
| SP / DIG | IPS Cadre (deputation) | as per IPS |

### Exam Pattern
**SSC CGL Tier-I (Objective)**: 200 Qs, 200 marks, 60 min
- Reasoning: 50 Qs
- English: 50 Qs
- Quant: 50 Qs
- GA: 50 Qs
- Negative marking: 0.50

**Tier-II (Objective + Descriptive)**:
- Quant (Advanced) - 100 Qs, 200 marks
- English - 200 marks
- Statistics - 200 marks
- Finance & Economics - 200 marks
- Descriptive: Essay/Letter/Precis - 200 marks

**Tier-III (Descriptive)**: Essay, Letter, Precis - 100 marks
- Qualifying in nature (qualifying marks 33%)

**PET/Medical** (For SI post): Height, Chest, Endurance test

**Interview**: Not for CBI SI direct recruitment. For senior posts through UPSC (SP cadre) - 100-200 marks.

### Detailed Syllabus
**General Intelligence & Reasoning**: Same as SSC CGL standard - analogies, series, coding, Venn diagrams, syllogism, puzzles, statement-conclusion, dice, cubes, mirror images, paper folding, embedded figures, blood relations, directions, calendar, clock.

**English Language**: Reading comprehension, cloze test, fill in the blanks, spelling, idioms, synonyms/antonyms, one-word substitution, sentence improvement, error spotting, active/passive, direct/indirect, para jumbles, vocabulary.

**Quantitative Aptitude**: Number system, simplification, ratio, percentage, average, SI/CI, profit-loss, time-work, speed-distance, geometry, trigonometry, mensuration, data interpretation (tables, bar graphs, pie charts, line graphs), algebra, statistics, probability.

**General Awareness**: Static GK (History, Geography, Polity, Economics, Science) + Current Affairs (last 6-12 months). Special focus on:
- CBI history, functions, jurisdiction
- Indian Penal Code, CrPC, BNSS
- Prevention of Corruption Act
- DSPE Act
- Interpol procedures
- Cyber crime basics

**Finance & Economics** (For CBI Inspector/LDCE technical):
- Indian Economy basics, Five Year Plans, Banking
- Budget concepts, GST, Direct/Indirect taxes
- RBI functions, monetary policy
- Stock market, financial markets
- Accounting principles, Auditing basics

**Statistics** (For CBI SI through SSC CGL Statistician post):
- Collection, classification, tabulation
- Measures of central tendency & dispersion
- Correlation & regression
- Index numbers, time series
- Probability theory, sampling

### Salary & Pay
- **Sub-Inspector**: Pay Level 6 (₹35,400-1,12,400) + DA + Special pay
- **Inspector**: Pay Level 7 (₹44,900-1,42,400)
- **DSP/Addl. SP**: Pay Level 8/9
- **SP**: Pay Level 10/11
- **DIG**: Pay Level 12/13
- **Director**: Pay Level 17 (₹2,25,000)

Plus: Dearness Allowance, House Rent Allowance, Special Investigative Allowance, Risk Allowance, Travel Allowance, Medical benefits.

## 3. NIA (National Investigation Agency)

**Overview**: India's counter-terrorism law enforcement agency, established on 31 December 2008 under the NIA Act 2008, post the 26/11 Mumbai attacks. Investigates offenses related to terrorism, atomic energy, explosives, counterfeit currency, narcotics with terrorism links. DG: Shri Rakesh Aggarwal, IPS. Operates under MHA. 24 branch/zonal offices across India (Delhi HQ, Mumbai, Hyderabad, Lucknow, Guwahati, Kochi, Kolkata, etc.).

**Recruitment Portal**: https://nia.gov.in/recruitment (mostly deputation-based)

### Posts Offered
- **Sub-Inspector (SI)** - direct recruitment (rare)
- **Inspector** - LDCE/Deputation
- **Assistant** - ministerial
- **LDC / UDC** - clerical
- **Stenographer** - ministerial
- **Constable** - GD/armed
- **Havildar / Inspector (Armed)** - through CAPF recruitment

**Tier-II (Descriptive)** (for some posts):

**Interview/Personality Test**: 100 marks (for Inspector/SI direct)

### Detailed Syllabus
**General Intelligence & Reasoning**: All SSC-level topics - analogies, series, classification, coding-decoding, blood relations, direction, syllogism, statement-conclusion, Venn diagram, mirror/water image, dice/cube, embedded figures, paper cutting/folding, mathematical operations, ranking, seating arrangement.

**English Language**: Comprehension, vocabulary, grammar, sentence correction, idioms/phrases, synonyms/antonyms, one-word substitution, active/passive, direct/indirect, tenses, articles, prepositions, conjunctions.

**Quantitative Aptitude**: Number system, simplification, ratio, percentage, average, profit/loss, SI/CI, time-work, speed-distance, mensuration, geometry, trigonometry, data interpretation, algebra, statistics.

**General Awareness**: 
- NIA Act 2008, UAPA 1967, IPC, CrPC, BNSS

## 4. NCB (Narcotics Control Bureau) - Brief

**Overview**: Apex drug law enforcement agency established in 1986 under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act, 1985. Operates under MHA. Conducts raids, investigations, intelligence gathering on narcotics trafficking. Recent vacancies for 03 Deputy Director General (DDG) on deputation basis (2026).

**Posts**: DDG, Zonal Director, Intelligence Officer, Superintendent, SI, Inspector, Constable, LDC, Steno - mostly deputation.

**Eligibility**: Graduation for officer cadre (IPS/state police/central armed forces on deputation), 10th-12th for subordinate posts.

**Exam**: Through SSC CGL/SSC, UPSC for senior posts.

**Salary**: Pay Level 6 to 13 depending on rank.

## 5. CIF (Counter Intelligence Force) - Brief

**Overview**: Internal security and counter-espionage unit, primarily operates in J&K. Functions under MHA. Works with IB and J&K Police to detect and neutralize terrorist/separatist networks. Recruitment is largely through state police (JKP) and deputation from CAPFs.

**Posts**: Inspector, SI, ASI, HC, Constable - mostly drawn from J&K Police.

**Eligibility**: Indian citizen, J&K domicile, physical fitness, 10th-12th-G raduation.

**Exam**: Through J&K Police recruitment, CAPF AC exam for officer rank, departmental exams.

**Salary**: Pay Level 3 to 11.

## 9. NSG (National Security Guard) - Mostly Deputation

**Overview**: India's primary counter-terrorism and counter-hijacking force, established 1984 post Operation Blue Star. Known as "Black Cats". Operates under MHA. Personnel drawn on deputation from Army, CRPF, BSF, CISF, ITBP, SSB, Assam Rifles. Direct recruitment very limited.

**Posts**: 
- **Special Action Group (SAG)** - Army personnel
- **Special Ranger Group (SRG)** - CAPF personnel
- **Combat roles** - deputation
- **Support staff** - DEO, Steno, Cook, etc.

**Eligibility**: For combat roles - military/para-military service, fitness test. For support - 10th/12th pass.

**Recruitment**: Not open to direct civilians for combat roles. Limited support staff recruitment through SSC.

**Salary**: Pay Level 3 to 17 depending on deputation rank + Special NSG allowance, Risk allowance.

## 10. SPG (Special Protection Group) - Rare

**Overview**: Elite security force for the Prime Minister and immediate family members. Established 1988 post Rajiv Gandhi assassination. Functions under Cabinet Secretariat (NOT MHA, but coordinated). 3000+ personnel, drawn from CRPF, BSF, CISF, ITBP, SSB on deputation.

**Posts**: 
- **Assistant Commandant**
- **Inspector / Sub-Inspector**
- **Combat Support Staff**
- **Drivers, Steno, Cook**

**Eligibility**: Personnel on deputation from CAPFs/AR with excellent service record, physical fitness, no disciplinary action.

**Recruitment**: No direct civilian recruitment. Limited support staff positions rarely.

**Salary**: As per deputation rank + SPG Special allowance, Risk allowance, accommodation.

## 12. Intelligence Bureau (IB)

## 13. RAW (Research and Analysis Wing) - Limited

**Overview**: India's external intelligence agency, established 1968 after Sino-Indian War (1962) and India-Pakistan War (1965). Reports directly to PM through NSA. Secret agency - very little public information on recruitment. Primarily recruits through:
- UPSC Civil Services (Group A) - 50% of executive cadre
- Lateral entry from military, scientific, technical fields
- Limited direct recruitment through Campus Placement

**Posts**:
- **Grade I - Director (Secretary level)**
- **Grade II - Joint Secretary**
- **Grade III - Director**
- **Grade IV - Under Secretary / Senior Research Officer** (Executive Cadre)
- **Grade V - Research Officer / Assistant Director**
- **Support staff - Research Assistants, Technical Officers, Linguists**

**Recruitment Path**:
1. **Civil Services Examination (UPSC)**: For Grade IV Executive Cadre - IFS, IPS, IRS officers can be deputed
2. **Campus Placement**: Premier institutes (IIT, IIM, JNU, Delhi University, St. Stephen's, IIT-Bombay, BHU, JNU School of International Studies, JNU School of Language) - recruitment notices very discreet
3. **Lateral Entry**: From defense services, scientific organizations (DRDO, ISRO, BARC), CAPFs, IFS
4. **Technical Cadre**: Engineers, Scientists, Linguists, Cyber experts

**Eligibility**:
- **Grade IV (Executive)**: Any graduate, 21-30 years, Indian citizen
- **Grade V (Research)**: Post-graduate (relevant field), 21-30 years
- **Technical**: B.Tech / M.Tech in relevant field

**Syllabus**: No public syllabus. Probable areas:
- International Relations, Geopolitics
- Foreign Languages (Arabic, Chinese, Russian, French, Farsi, Pashto, Dari, Urdu)
- Defense Studies, Strategic Studies
- Cyber Security, Technical Surveillance
- Economic Intelligence, Trade Analysis
- Area Studies (country-specific)
- History, Political Science
- Current International Affairs

**Salary**: Pay Level 7 to 17 (top-secret allowance) - significantly higher than civil services equivalents. Special Intelligence Allowance + Risk Allowance + Foreign Allowance.
