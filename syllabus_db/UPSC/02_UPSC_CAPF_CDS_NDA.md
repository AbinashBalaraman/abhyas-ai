# UPSC CAPF, CDS & NDA Examinations — Comprehensive Study Guide

> **Sources**: UPSC official site (upsc.gov.in), Wikipedia (CAPF, NDA India, CDS Exam, NDA & NA Exam), Adda247 CDS Syllabus 2025.

## 1. UPSC CAPF (Central Armed Police Forces) — Assistant Commandant

The **Central Armed Police Forces (CAPF)** comprise seven armed police units under the **Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA)**, Government of India: **Assam Rifles, BSF, CISF, CRPF, ITBP, NSG, and SSB**. The UPSC conducts the **Central Armed Police Forces (ACs) Examination** annually to recruit **Assistant Commandants** (Group A, Gazetted) for BSF, CISF, CRPF, ITBP, and SSB.

### Posts
- **Assistant Commandant (Group A, Gazetted)** in:
  - **BSF** — Border Security Force (Indo-Pak & Indo-Bangladesh borders)
  - **CISF** — Central Industrial Security Force (industrial units, airports)
  - **CRPF** — Central Reserve Police Force (largest, 247 battalions; internal security, anti-Naxal)
  - **ITBP** — Indo-Tibetan Border Police (Indo-China border, 3,488 km)
  - **SSB** — Sashastra Seema Bal (Indo-Nepal & Indo-Bhutan borders)

### Eligibility
- **Nationality**: Indian citizen.
- **Education**: Bachelor's degree from a recognized university.
- **Age (as on cutoff date)**:
  - UR: 20–25 years
  - OBC: 20–28 years (3-year relaxation)
  - SC/ST: 20–30 years (5-year relaxation)
- **Gender**: Both Male & Female eligible.
- **Marital Status**: Unmarried.
- **Physical Standards**: Must meet prescribed height/chest/weight.

### Exam Pattern (3 Stages)

| Stage | Paper | Type | Marks | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Stage I — Written** | Paper-I: General Ability & Intelligence | Objective (MCQ, negative marking: 1/3) | **250** | 2 hours |
| | Paper-II: General Studies, Essay & Comprehension | Descriptive (English/Hindi) | **200** | 3 hours |
| **Stage II — Physical** | PST (Physical Standard Test) + PET (Physical Efficiency Test) | Qualifying | — | — |
| **Stage III — Medical & Interview** | Medical Examination + Interview/Personality Test | Interview | **150** | — |

**Final Merit** = Paper-I (250) + Paper-II (200) + Interview (150) = **600 marks**.

### Detailed Syllabus — Paper I (General Ability & Intelligence)
- **General Mental Ability** — Logical reasoning, analogies, syllogism, series, coding-decoding, direction, blood relations, Venn diagrams, puzzles, data interpretation.
- **General Science** — Physics, Chemistry, Biology (10th/12th level: mechanics, light, electricity, elements, acids/bases, cell, nutrition, diseases).
- **Current Affairs** — National, International, Sports, Awards (national/international), Books & Authors, Summits, Government schemes.
- **Indian Polity & Economy** — Constitution, Preamble, Fundamental Rights/Duties, Parliament, Five Year Plans, economic reforms, banking, budget.
- **History** — Indian History (ancient, medieval, modern) + World History (WWI, WWII, revolutions).
- **Geography** — Indian Geography (physical, climate, rivers, soil) + World Geography (continents, atmosphere, oceanography).

### Detailed Syllabus — Paper II (Descriptive)
- Essay (200–250 words) on national/international issues.
- Modern Indian History & Freedom Struggle.
- Indian Polity & Governance, Public Policy, Rights Issues.
- Indian Economy, Planning, Rural Development, Poverty.
- Geography of India & World.
- Indian National Movement, Freedom Struggle.
- General Science, Technology, Environment.
- Current Affairs of national & international importance.
- Comprehension passages & précis writing.

### Physical Standards (PST)

| Standard | Male | Female |
|---|---|---|
| **Height** | 165 cm (157.5 cm for ST) | 157 cm (152.5 cm for ST) |
| **Chest** | 81 cm (unexpanded) – 86 cm (expanded) | Not applicable |
| **Weight** | Proportional to height | Proportional to height |

### Physical Efficiency Test (PET) — Qualifying

| Event | Male | Female |
|---|---|---|
| 100 m sprint | 16 sec | 18 sec |
| 800 m run | 3 min 45 sec | 4 min 45 sec |
| Long Jump | 3.5 m (3 chances) | 3.0 m (3 chances) |
| High Jump | 1.2 m (3 chances) | 0.9 m (3 chances) |
| Shot Put (7.26 kg) | 4.5 m | — |

### Salary
- **Pay Level 10** (7th CPC): ₹56,100 – ₹1,77,500 (basic pay).
- **Starting in-hand**: ~₹80,000–₹90,000/month (with DA, HRA, allowances).
- **Perks**: Free accommodation, medical, uniform allowance, ration, leave travel, pension.

## 2. UPSC CDS (Combined Defence Services) Examination — I & II

The **Combined Defence Services Examination (CDS)** is conducted by UPSC **twice a year** (CDS-I in April, CDS-II in September) for recruitment of officer cadets into the **Indian Military Academy (IMA), Indian Naval Academy (INA), Air Force Academy (AFA)**, and **Officers Training Academy (OTA)**.

### Posts & Academies
- **IMA, Dehradun** — Indian Army (Permanent Commission, 4-year course).
- **INA, Ezhimala (Kerala)** — Indian Navy (Permanent Commission, 4-year B.Tech).
- **AFA, Dundigal (Hyderabad)** — Indian Air Force (Permanent Commission, 4-year B.Tech).
- **OTA, Chennai** — Indian Army (Short Service Commission, 49 weeks; both male & female).

### Eligibility

| Academy | Gender | Marital Status | Age (on joining) | Education |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **IMA** | Male | Unmarried | 19–24 years | Bachelor's degree (any discipline) |
| **INA** | Male | Unmarried | 19–24 years | B.E./B.Tech (or in final year) |
| **AFA** | Male | Unmarried | 20–24 years | Graduation with Physics & Maths at 10+2; or B.E./B.Tech |
| **OTA (Men)** | Male | Unmarried | 19–25 years | Bachelor's degree (any discipline) |
| **OTA (Women)** | Female | Unmarried, issueless widows/divorcees allowed | 19–25 years | Bachelor's degree (any discipline) |

### Exam Pattern

**For IMA / INA / AFA** (3 papers, total 300 marks):

| Paper | Subject | Marks | Questions | Duration | Negative Marking |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Paper I | English | 100 | 120 | 2 hours | 1/3 |
| Paper II | General Knowledge | 100 | 120 | 2 hours | 1/3 |
| Paper III | Elementary Mathematics | 100 | 100 | 2 hours | 1/3 |

**For OTA** (2 papers, total 200 marks):

| Paper | Subject | Marks | Questions | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Paper I | English | 100 | 120 | 2 hours |
| Paper II | General Knowledge | 100 | 120 | 2 hours |

### Detailed Syllabus

**English**:
- Reading Comprehension (passages)
- Vocabulary: Synonyms, Antonyms, Idioms & Phrases
- Grammar: Spotting Errors, Sentence Correction/Improvement
- Cloze Test, Fill in the blanks
- Para Jumbles, Sentence Rearrangement
- Active/Passive Voice, Direct/Indirect Speech
- Ordering of Words/Sentences

**General Knowledge**:
- **Current Affairs** — National & International, Awards, Books, Sports, Summits
- **History** — Ancient, Medieval, Modern Indian History; Indian National Movement, Growth of Nationalism
- **Geography** — Physical, Social, Economic; Indian & World Geography
- **Indian Polity & Governance** — Constitution, Political System, Panchayati Raj, Public Policy, Rights Issues
- **Economics** — Five Year Plans, Budget, Poverty, Sustainable Development, Social Sector Initiatives
- **Science & Technology** — Space, Biotechnology, Computer, Defence tech developments
- **Environment** — Ecology, Biodiversity, Climate Change, Pollution
- **Defence & Security** — National Security, Internal/External Threats, Role of Armed Forces

**Elementary Mathematics**:
- **Number System** — Natural, Integers, Rationals, Reals; HCF, LCM, Square roots, Decimals, Unitary method, Time & Distance, Time & Work, Percentages, Simple & Compound Interest, Profit & Loss, Ratio & Proportion.
- **Elementary Number Theory** — Divisibility, Prime/Composite, Factorization, Euclidean algorithm, Logarithms.
- **Algebra** — Polynomials, Remainder/Factor theorems, Quadratic equations, Simultaneous linear equations, Inequations, Set language, Laws of indices.
- **Trigonometry** — sin/cos/tan values (0°, 30°, 45°, 60°, 90°), Identities, Heights & Distances.
- **Geometry** — Lines, Angles, Triangles (congruency, similarity), Parallelograms, Circles (tangents, chords), Polygons.
- **Mensuration** — Areas (squares, rectangles, triangles, circles, parallelograms), Surface area & volume of cuboids, cones, cylinders, spheres.
- **Statistics** — Tabulation, Frequency polygons, Bar/Pie charts, Histograms, Measures of central tendency.

### Selection Process
1. **Written Examination** (qualifying cutoff).
2. **SSB Interview** — 5 days: Stage-I (OIR + PP&DT) → Stage-II (Psychology Tests, GTO Tasks, Personal Interview, Conference).
3. **Medical Examination** by Armed Forces Medical Board.

### Salary
- **Pay Level 10** (7th CPC): ₹56,100 – ₹1,77,500 (basic).
- **Starting in-hand**: ~₹75,000–₹85,000/month (with allowances).
- **Perks**: Free accommodation, ration, uniform, medical, canteen (CSD), LTC, pension, post-retirement benefits.

## 3. UPSC NDA & NA (National Defence Academy & Naval Academy) Examination — I & II

The **NDA/NA Exam**, conducted by UPSC **twice a year** (NDA-I in April, NDA-II in September), is the **primary gateway** for admission to the **National Defence Academy (NDA), Khadakwasla (Pune)** and **Indian Naval Academy (INA), Ezhimala**, for a career as a commissioned officer in the **Indian Army, Navy, or Air Force** right after Class 12.

### Posts & Wings
- **NDA (Army)** — 208 vacancies per exam (incl. 10 female seats).
- **NDA (Navy)** — 42 vacancies per exam (incl. 5 female).
- **NDA (Air Force)** — 120 vacancies (Flying 92, Ground Duties Tech 18, Non-Tech 10; incl. 6 female).
- **INA (10+2 Cadet Entry Scheme)** — 36 vacancies (incl. 4 female).
- **Total per exam**: ~406; **Annual intake**: ~812 officer cadets.

### Eligibility
- **Nationality**: Indian citizen (subject to specific conditions for Tibetan/PIO).
- **Gender**: Both Male & Female eligible (since Aug 2021 Supreme Court ruling).
- **Marital Status**: Unmarried.
- **Age**: **16.5 to 19.5 years** (no relaxation for any category).
- **Education**:
  - For **Army**: 10+2 (any stream).
  - For **Navy & Air Force**: 10+2 with **Physics & Mathematics**.

### Exam Pattern (5 hours total)

| Paper | Subject | Marks | Questions | Duration | Negative Marking |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Paper I | **Mathematics** | **300** | 120 | 2.5 hours | 1/3 |
| Paper II | **General Ability Test (GAT)** | **600** | 150 | 2.5 hours | 1/3 |

**GAT Breakdown**:
- **Part A — English**: 200 marks.
- **Part B — General Knowledge**: 400 marks (Physics, Chemistry, General Science, Geography, History, Current Affairs).

### Detailed Syllabus

**Paper I — Mathematics (300 marks)**:
- **Algebra**: Sets, relations, functions; Complex numbers; Quadratic equations; Sequences & Series; Permutations & Combinations; Binomial Theorem; Logarithms; Partial fractions.
- **Matrices & Determinants**: Types, operations, adjoint, inverse, Cramer's Rule.
- **Trigonometry**: Angles, identities, inverse trig functions, heights & distances.
- **Analytical Geometry 2D**: Straight lines, circles, conic sections (parabola, ellipse, hyperbola).
- **Analytical Geometry 3D**: Points, lines, planes in space, distance, section formula.
- **Differential Calculus**: Limits, continuity, differentiation, derivatives, applications (max/min, tangents, normals).
- **Integral Calculus**: Integration (by parts, substitution), definite integrals, area under curves.
- **Differential Equations**: Formation, order/degree, solutions of first-order & first-degree.
- **Vector Algebra**: Vectors in 2D/3D, scalar & vector products, scalar triple product.
- **Statistics & Probability**: Mean, median, mode, standard deviation, probability theorems, conditional probability, Bayes' theorem.

**Paper II — GAT**:

**Part A — English (200 marks)**:
- Grammar: Spotting Errors, Sentence Correction, Active/Passive, Direct/Indirect, Tenses.
- Vocabulary: Synonyms, Antonyms, Idioms, Phrases, One-word Substitution.
- Comprehension: Reading passages, Précis.
- Cohesion: Para Jumbles, Sentence Rearrangement, Cloze Test, Fill in the blanks.

**Part B — General Knowledge (400 marks)**:
- **Physics (10th/12th level)**: Mechanics (motion, force, gravitation), Properties of matter, Heat, Light (reflection, refraction, lenses), Sound, Electricity (current, circuits, magnetism), Modern Physics.
- **Chemistry**: Physical (states of matter, atomic structure, chemical bonding), Inorganic (elements, acids/bases, metals/non-metals), Organic (hydrocarbons, polymers, daily chemistry).
- **General Science**: Biology (cell, nutrition, health, diseases, ecology), Everyday Science, Discoveries.
- **History (India)**: Ancient (Indus Valley, Vedic, Maurya, Gupta), Medieval (Delhi Sultanate, Mughals), Modern (British rule, Freedom Struggle, post-independence).
- **History (World)**: French Revolution, Industrial Revolution, World Wars, UN, Cold War.
- **Geography**: Physical (geomorphology, atmosphere, oceans), Indian Geography (rivers, climate, soil, agriculture, minerals, industries), World Geography.
- **Current Affairs**: National, International, Awards, Books, Defence, Sports, Science & Tech.

### Selection Process
1. **Written Examination** (qualifying cutoff).
2. **SSB Interview** — 5 days, two stages (Stage I: OIR + PP&DT; Stage II: Psychology, GTO Tasks, Personal Interview, Conference).
3. **Medical Examination** (AFMCB / AFMRC).
4. **Air Force candidates** also undergo **CPSS (Computerised Pilot Selection System)** for flying branch.

### Training Path
- **NDA Khadakwasla, Pune** — **3 years** of tri-service academic & military training (JNU/University of Delhi degree: BA/BSc/BTech depending on stream).
  - Army cadets → **IMA Dehradun** (1 year).
  - Naval cadets → **INA Ezhimala** (1 year, B.Tech).
  - Air Force cadets → **AFA Dundigal/Hyderabad** (1 year, B.Tech).

### Salary
- **Cadet at NDA**: ₹56,100 (stipend) + free kit, food, accommodation.
- **On Commission (Lieutenant / Flying Officer / Sub-Lieutenant)**: Pay Level 10 — ₹56,100 – ₹1,77,500 basic.
- **Starting in-hand**: ~₹75,000–₹85,000/month (DA, kit allowance, special forces allowance if applicable).
- **Allowances**: HRA, ration allowance, uniform allowance, flying allowance (IAF), sea service allowance (Navy), high-altitude allowance, Siachen allowance, free medical (AFMC), CSD canteen, free railway warrant, subsidized housing.

## CONCISE SUMMARY

This file comprehensively covers three major UPSC examinations for Armed Forces and Paramilitary careers:

**1. UPSC CAPF (Assistant Commandant)** — Recruits Group A officers for BSF, CISF, CRPF, ITBP, SSB. Eligibility: Bachelor's degree, age 20–25 (UR) with reservations. Selection involves Paper-I (Objective, 250 marks), Paper-II (Descriptive, 200 marks), Physical Efficiency Test (PET: 100m/800m/Long Jump/High Jump), Medical Exam, and Interview (150 marks). Total merit = 600 marks. Pay Level 10 (₹56,100+ basic). Syllabus covers GK, General Science, Polity, Economy, History, Geography, Mental Ability, Current Affairs.

**2. UPSC CDS (Combined Defence Services)** — Gateway to IMA, INA, AFA, OTA. IMA/INA/AFA require 3 papers (English, GK, Elementary Mathematics — 100 marks each, 2 hrs each, negative marking 1/3). OTA has 2 papers (English + GK). Eligibility: Graduate, age 19–24 (IMA/INA/AFA) or 19–25 (OTA); OTA allows women. Selection: Written → 5-day SSB Interview → Medical. Pay Level 10+. Syllabus includes basic Math (Algebra, Trigonometry, Geometry, Mensuration, Statistics), English (Grammar, Vocab, Comprehension), and GK (History, Geography, Polity, Science, Defence).

**3. UPSC NDA & NA Exam** — Entry after Class 12 (16.5–19.5 years) to NDA Khadakwasla (3 years) + IMA/INA/AFA (1 year). Two papers: Mathematics (300 marks) and GAT (600 marks = English 200 + GK 400). Mathematics covers Algebra, Matrices, Trigonometry, 2D/3D Geometry, Calculus, Vectors, Statistics, Probability. Selection: Written → 5-day SSB → Medical → CPSS (IAF flying). Female candidates allowed since 2021. Pay Level 10+ on commission; in-hand ₹75,000–85,000+.
