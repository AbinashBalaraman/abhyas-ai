# UPSC Civil Services Examination (CSE) / IAS / IPS / IFS

## 1. Overview

- **Full Name:** Civil Services Examination (CSE), commonly known as the UPSC IAS Exam
- **Conducting Body:** Union Public Service Commission (UPSC)
- **Type:** Standardised national-level examination (paper-based)
- **Frequency:** Once a year (annual)
- **Established:** 1855 (as far back as the Imperial Civil Service); reconstituted under the Constitution on 26 January 1950
- **Purpose:** Recruitment to higher civil services of the Government of India — All India Services and Central Civil Services (Group A & select Group B)
- **Recognition:** Considered one of the toughest examinations in the world
- **Scale (2022):** ~11.35 lakh registrations; ~5.74 lakh appeared for Prelims; ~1,255 vacancies
- **Qualification rate:** 0.078% (2023)
- **Headquarters of UPSC:** Dholpur House, Shahjahan Road, New Delhi
- **Constitutional basis:** Part XIV (Articles 315–323) of the Constitution of India
- **Total Marks (Final Merit):** 2,025
- **Fee:** ₹100 (Female / SC / ST / PwBD candidates exempted)

## 3. Eligibility

### Nationality
- **IAS / IPS / IFS:** Must be a citizen of India
- **All other services:** Citizen of India OR citizen of Nepal / Bhutan OR Tibetan refugee (settled before 1 Jan 1962) OR person of Indian origin from Pakistan, Myanmar, Sri Lanka, Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Zambia, Malawi, Zaire, Ethiopia, Vietnam

### Educational Qualification
- Bachelor's degree from a recognised Central / State / Deemed university, or
- Degree through correspondence / distance / open university, or
- Foreign degree recognised by Association of Indian Universities (AIU), or
- Final-year appearing candidates also eligible (proof required at Mains)
- MBBS candidates (internship pending), ICAI / ICSI / ICMAI passed candidates also eligible

## 4. Exam Pattern (3 Stages)

### Stage 1: Preliminary Examination (Objective / MCQ)
- **Paper I – General Studies (GS):** 200 marks (counted for merit)
- **Paper II – CSAT (Civil Services Aptitude Test):** 200 marks (qualifying — minimum 33% required)
- Duration: 2 hours each; both papers objective-type; Paper II is qualifying only since 2015

### Stage 2: Mains Examination (Written / Descriptive)
9 papers — 2 qualifying + 7 counted (each 3 hours duration):

| Paper | Subject | Marks |
|-------|---------|-------|
| Paper A | Indian Language (qualifying, from 8th Schedule) | 300 |
| Paper B | English (qualifying) | 300 |
| Paper I | Essay | 250 |
| Paper II | GS Paper I (Heritage, History, Geography, Society) | 250 |
| Paper III | GS Paper II (Governance, Polity, Constitution, IR) | 250 |
| Paper IV | GS Paper III (Tech, Economy, Environment, Security, DM) | 250 |
| Paper V | GS Paper IV (Ethics, Integrity, Aptitude) | 250 |
| Paper VI | Optional Subject Paper 1 | 250 |
| Paper VII | Optional Subject Paper 2 | 250 |
| **Sub-Total (Written)** | | **1,750** |

> Note: Paper A (Indian Language) is not compulsory for candidates from Arunachal Pradesh, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim.

### Stage 3: Personality Test (Interview)
- **Marks:** 275
- Tests academic knowledge, awareness, intellectual qualities, social traits, leadership, integrity, balance of judgment
- Held at UPSC Bhawan, New Delhi
- Duration: 20–40 minutes

### Final Merit
**Mains (1,750) + Interview (275) = 2,025 marks**

## 5. Detailed Syllabus – Prelims Paper I (General Studies)

1. **History:** Indian National Movement, Indian History (ancient / medieval / modern), World History
2. **Geography:** Physical, Indian, World, Economic Geography
3. **Indian Polity & Governance:** Constitution, Political System, Panchayati Raj, Public Policy, Rights Issues
4. **Economic & Social Development:** Planning, Poverty, Inclusion, Demographics, Social Sector Initiatives
5. **Environment & Ecology:** Biodiversity, Climate Change, Environmental Issues
6. **General Science:** Physics, Chemistry, Biology (up to Class X level, with some advanced topics)
7. **Current Affairs:** National, International, Science & Tech, Economic developments
8. **Art & Culture**

## 6. Detailed Syllabus – CSAT (Prelims Paper II)

- Comprehension
- Interpersonal skills including communication skills
- Logical reasoning and analytical ability
- Decision-making and problem-solving
- General mental ability
- Basic numeracy (Class X level)
- Data interpretation (Class X level)
- English language comprehension skills (Class X level)

> Paper II is qualifying; 33% required; not counted for merit.

## 7. Detailed Syllabus – Mains General Studies Papers

### Paper I – Indian Heritage & Culture, History & Geography of the World & Society
- Indian Heritage & Culture (art forms, literature, architecture)
- Modern Indian History (mid-18th century onwards), Freedom Struggle
- Post-independence India
- History of the World (events from 18th century)
- Indian Society – Diversity, Women, Population, Urbanization, Globalization, Social Empowerment
- Geography – Physical, Resources, Industries, Geoinformatics

### Paper II – Governance, Constitution, Polity, Social Justice & International Relations
- Indian Constitution – evolution, features, amendments
- Federalism, Separation of Powers
- Parliament & State Legislatures
- Constitutional & Non-Constitutional bodies
- Governance, Transparency, Accountability, E-governance
- Social Justice, Welfare schemes
- International Relations – India's foreign policy, bilateral & multilateral institutions

### Paper III – Technology, Economic Development, Environment, Security & Disaster Management
- Indian Economy – Planning, Growth, Development, Agriculture, Industry, Infrastructure, Services
- Science & Technology – developments and applications, indigenisation
- Environment – Biodiversity, Climate Change, Conservation
- Disaster Management
- Internal Security – challenges, extremism, cyber security, border management

### Paper IV – Ethics, Integrity & Aptitude
- Ethics & Human Interface
- Attitude, Aptitude, Emotional Intelligence
- Moral Thinkers (Indian & Western)
- Ethics in Public Service
- Probity in Governance
- Case Studies

## 9. Essay Topics – Diverse Range

- Philosophical (e.g., "The best brains of the nation lie in the civil services")
- Social (e.g., "Women's empowerment: rhetoric vs reality")
- Political (e.g., "The right to dissent is the foundation of democracy")
- Economic (e.g., "Inclusive growth: challenges and opportunities")
- International (e.g., "India's role in a multipolar world")
- Environment (e.g., "Climate change: who pays, who benefits?")
- Technology (e.g., "AI and the future of work")
- Abstract / literary / biographical / cultural themes

## 10. Personality Test (Interview) – Tested Areas

- Academic knowledge and awareness
- Intellectual qualities and mental alertness
- Social traits, leadership, initiative
- Moral integrity and balance of judgment
- Communication skills and personality
- Suitability for public service under stress
- Knowledge of current national / international events
- Awareness of India's diversity, social issues, governance challenges

## 15. Quick Facts

- **Established (as Public Service Commission):** 1 October 1926
- **Constitutional Status:** 26 January 1950
- **Current Chairman:** Dr. Ajay Kumar (IAS)
- **Members:** 9–11 (including Chairman)
- **Term of Members:** 6 years or till 65 years (whichever earlier)
- **Languages of Exam:** English, Hindi, and 22 Scheduled languages
- **Fee:** ₹100 (Female / SC / ST / PwBD exempted)
- **Attempts at a glance:** 6 (UR/EWS), 9 (OBC), Unlimited (SC/ST within age)
- **Vacancies (typical):** ~1,000–1,200 per year
- **Candidates appearing (typical):** ~5–6 lakh in Prelims
- **Selection rate:** <0.1%
- **Training:** Lal Bahadur Shastri National Academy of Administration (LBSNAA), Mussoorie
- **Salary (starting):** Pay Level 10 (₹56,100 – ₹1,77,500) + DA + perks
- **Tagline:** "सत्यमेव जयते" (Satyameva Jayate)
- **Article in Constitution:** Article 315 (Part XIV)
