## 1. UPSC EPFO (Enforcement Officer / Accounts Officer)

### 1.1 Overview
- **Conducting Body:** Union Public Service Commission (UPSC)
- **Department:** Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO), under the **Ministry of Labour and Employment**
- **Posts:** **Enforcement Officer (EO) / Accounts Officer (AO)** – Group B, Non-Gazetted (Pay Level 8)
- **Recruitment Mode:** Direct Recruitment through UPSC combined Recruitment Test (RT)

### 1.2 Eligibility Criteria
| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Nationality | Indian Citizen |
| Educational Qualification | Bachelor's degree from a recognised university |
| Age Limit | 18 to 30 years (relaxation for reserved categories) |
| Number of Attempts | No limit (subject to upper age cap) |
| Application Fee | ₹25 (SC/ST/PwBD/Female exempted) |

### 1.3 Exam Pattern (Recruitment Test - RT)

**Note:** EPFO recruitment test is a **single-stage** process – there is **no interview** in the current pattern.

#### Part A: Objective (MCQ)
- **Duration:** 2 Hours
- **Total Questions:** 100
- **Total Marks:** 100 (1 mark per question)
- **Negative Marking:** 1/3rd mark deduction for wrong answers
- **Medium:** Bilingual (English & Hindi)

| Topic | Questions |
|-------|-----------|
| General English | 10 |
| Indian Freedom Struggle | 10 |
| Current Affairs & General Awareness | 10 |
| Indian Polity & Constitution | 10 |
| Indian Economy | 10 |
| General Accounting Principles | 10 |
| Industrial Relations & Labour Laws | 10 |
| General Science | 10 |
| Quantitative Aptitude & Reasoning | 10 |
| Computer Awareness | 10 |

#### Part B: Descriptive
- **Duration:** 2 Hours
- **Total Marks:** 100
- **Medium:** English / Hindi

| Component | Marks |
|-----------|-------|
| Essay Writing (250–300 words) | 50 |
| Letter / Precis Writing | 50 |

### 1.4 Detailed Syllabus

#### A. Industrial Relations & Labour Laws
- Industrial Disputes Act, 1947
- Trade Unions Act, 1926
- Factories Act, 1948
- Minimum Wages Act, 1948
- Payment of Wages Act, 1936
- Employees' State Insurance (ESI) Act, 1948
- Employees' Provident Fund & Miscellaneous Provisions (EPF) Act, 1952
- Payment of Bonus Act, 1965
- Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946
- Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923
- Maternity Benefit Act, 1961
- Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972
- Child Labour (Prohibition & Regulation) Act
- Industrial Disputes: Strikes, Lockouts, Layoff, Retrenchment
- Collective Bargaining & Workers' Participation in Management

#### B. General Accounting Principles
- Double Entry System of Bookkeeping
- Journal, Ledger, Cash Book, Trial Balance
- Final Accounts (Trading, P&L, Balance Sheet)
- Depreciation Accounting (Straight Line, WDV)
- Bank Reconciliation Statement
- Fund Flow & Cash Flow Statements
- Working Capital Management
- Ratio Analysis (Liquidity, Solvency, Profitability)
- Inventory Valuation Methods
- Rectification of Errors
- Bank Audit Essentials

#### C. General Science (10th/12th Level)
- **Physics:** Motion, Force, Energy, Light, Sound, Electricity, Magnetism
- **Chemistry:** Elements, Compounds, Acids/Bases, Metals/Non-metals, Polymers
- **Biology:** Cell, Human Body Systems, Nutrition, Diseases, Ecology

#### D. Indian Polity & Constitution
- Preamble, Fundamental Rights & Duties, DPSP
- Parliamentary System, Federal Structure
- Constitutional Bodies (Election Commission, CAG, UPSC)
- Fundamental Rights & Constitutional Amendments

#### E. Indian Economy
- Basic Economic Concepts (GDP, Inflation, Monetary Policy)
- Planning in India, Five Year Plans
- Banking, RBI, Fiscal Policy
- Tax Structure (Direct & Indirect)
- LPG Reforms, MUDRA, Start-Up India

#### F. Quantitative Aptitude & Reasoning
- Number System, Simplification, Percentage, Ratio
- Profit & Loss, SI/CI, Time & Work
- Series, Coding-Decoding, Syllogism
- Blood Relations, Direction, Puzzles

### 1.5 Salary Structure
- **Pay Level:** 8 (7th CPC)
- **Basic Pay:** ₹47,600 (starting)
- **Gross In-Hand Salary:** ₹60,000 – ₹75,000 (approx., with DA, HRA, perks)
- **Career Progression:** EO/AO → APFC (L-10) → RPFC (L-11/12) → Addl. CPFC (L-13A/14) → CPFC (L-15)

## 2. UPSC IES / ISS (Indian Economic Service / Indian Statistical Service)

### 2.1 Overview
- **Conducting Body:** UPSC
- **Cadre Controlling Authority:** Department of Economic Affairs, **Ministry of Finance**
- **Posts:**
  - **IES** – Indian Economic Service (Group A)
  - **ISS** – Indian Statistical Service (Group A)
- **Cadre Strength:** IES – 518 (approx.); ISS – small elite cadre
- **Training Institute:** Institute of Economic Growth (IEG), Delhi

### 2.2 Eligibility Criteria
| Parameter | IES | ISS |
|-----------|-----|-----|
| Educational Qualification | Master's in Economics / Applied Economics / Business Economics / Econometrics | Master's in Statistics / Mathematical Statistics / Applied Statistics |
| Age Limit | 21–30 years | 21–30 years |
| Attempts | No limit (until age cap) | No limit |

### 2.3 Exam Pattern (Combined IES/ISS Exam)

The exam has **two stages** – Written (no interview).

#### IES – Indian Economic Service Papers
| Paper | Subject | Marks |
|-------|---------|-------|
| I | General English | 100 |
| II | General Studies | 100 |
| III | Economics Paper I (Theory) | 200 |
| IV | Economics Paper II (Indian Economics) | 200 |
| V | Economics Paper III (Statistics, Econometrics) | 200 |
| **Total** | | **800** |

#### ISS – Indian Statistical Service Papers
| Paper | Subject | Marks |
|-------|---------|-------|
| I | General English | 100 |
| II | General Studies | 100 |
| III | Statistics Paper I (Probability, Distribution) | 200 |
| IV | Statistics Paper II (Sampling, Linear Inference) | 200 |
| V | Statistics Paper III (Economics, Operations Research) | 200 |
| **Total** | | **800** |

### 2.4 Detailed Syllabus – IES Economics

#### Paper I: Economic Theory (Micro & Macro)
- **Microeconomics:** Consumer Theory (Utility, Indifference Curve, Demand), Production & Cost, Market Structures (Perfect Competition, Monopoly, Oligopoly), Welfare Economics, General Equilibrium
- **Macroeconomics:** National Income Accounting, Classical & Keynesian Models, Money & Banking, IS-LM, Inflation, Unemployment, Trade (Heckscher-Ohlin, Terms of Trade), Exchange Rate, Balance of Payments

#### Paper II: Indian Economy
- Planning in India (Five Year Plans, NITI Aayog)
- Agriculture, Industry, Services Sectors
- Foreign Trade & FDI
- Money, Banking, Capital Markets
- Public Finance, Fiscal Federalism
- Poverty, Inequality, Demographic Issues
- LPG Reforms 1991 and Beyond
- GST, Demonetisation, Insolvency Code

#### Paper III: Statistics & Econometrics
- Probability Theory, Distribution Theory
- Hypothesis Testing, Estimation
- Simple & Multiple Regression, OLS, GLS
- Time Series, Index Numbers
- Multicollinearity, Heteroscedasticity, Autocorrelation
- Simultaneous Equation Models
- Input-Output Analysis, Linear Programming

### 2.5 Detailed Syllabus – ISS Statistics

#### Paper I: Probability & Distribution
- Classical & Axiomatic Probability
- Random Variables, Expectation, Moments
- Binomial, Poisson, Normal, Exponential
- Joint & Marginal Distributions
- Law of Large Numbers, Central Limit Theorem
- Markov Chains

#### Paper II: Statistical Inference
- Point & Interval Estimation
- Tests of Hypothesis (Z, t, Chi-Square, F)
- ANOVA, Non-Parametric Tests
- Maximum Likelihood Estimation
- Likelihood Ratio Test
- Multivariate Normal Distribution

#### Paper III: Sampling, Design & Economics/OR
- Simple, Stratified, Cluster, Systematic Sampling
- Ratio & Regression Estimators
- Design of Experiments (CRD, RBD, LSD)
- Time Series Analysis
- Index Numbers (Laspeyres, Paasche, Fisher)
- Linear Programming, Game Theory, Queuing Theory
- Economic Statistics (National Income, Price Indices)

### 2.6 Salary Structure
- **Pay Level:** 10 (starting, 7th CPC)
- **Basic Pay:** ₹56,100
- **Gross In-Hand Salary:** ₹85,000 – ₹1,00,000 (approx.)
- **Career Progression:** Research Officer → Joint Director → Deputy Economic Adviser → Economic Adviser → Chief Economic Adviser (L-17 / Apex)

## 3. UPSC IFoS (Indian Forest Service)

### 3.1 Overview
- **Conducting Body:** UPSC
- **Cadre Controlling Authority:** **Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEF&CC)**
- **Status:** One of the **three All India Services** (along with IAS and IPS)
- **Training Institute:** Indira Gandhi National Forest Academy (IGNFA), Dehradun
- **Service Chief:** Director General of Forests (DG of Forests)
- **Cadre Strength:** ~3,131 (2,182 Direct Recruits + 949 Promotional)

### 3.2 Eligibility Criteria
| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Educational Qualification | Bachelor's degree with **at least one** of: Animal Husbandry & Veterinary Science, Botany, Chemistry, Geology, Mathematics, Physics, Zoology, Agriculture, Forestry, Engineering |
| Age Limit | 21–32 years |
| Physical Standards | **Mandatory** (height, chest, fitness tests) |
| Attempts | No limit (subject to age cap) |

### 3.3 Exam Pattern (3 Stages)

#### Stage I: Preliminary Examination (CSE Common)
The IFoS Preliminary is held **along with the Civil Services Preliminary Examination**.

| Paper | Subject | Marks |
|-------|---------|-------|
| Paper I | General Studies (GS) | 200 |
| Paper II | CSAT (Qualifying – 33%) | 200 |

#### Stage II: Main Examination (6 Papers)

| Paper | Subject | Marks |
|-------|---------|-------|
| A | General English (Qualifying – 25%) | 300 |
| B | General Knowledge (Qualifying – 25%) | 300 |
| I | Optional Subject – Paper I | 200 |
| II | Optional Subject – Paper II | 200 |
| III | Optional Subject – Paper III | 200 |
| IV | Optional Subject – Paper IV | 200 |
| **Total (Counted)** | | **800** |

#### Stage III: Personality Test / Interview
- **Marks:** 300
- **Final Merit:** Mains (800) + Interview (300) = 1100

### 3.4 List of Optional Subjects (14 Total)

| # | Optional Subject | # | Optional Subject |
|---|------------------|---|------------------|
| 1 | Agriculture | 8 | Geology |
| 2 | Agricultural Engineering | 9 | Mathematics |
| 3 | Animal Husbandry & Veterinary Science | 10 | Mechanical Engineering |
| 4 | Botany | 11 | Physics |
| 5 | Chemistry | 12 | Statistics |
| 6 | Chemical Engineering | 13 | Zoology |
| 7 | Civil Engineering | 14 | Forestry |

### 3.5 Detailed Syllabus – Major Optional Subjects

#### A. Forestry (Most Popular Choice)
- **Paper I:** Silviculture, Silvicultural Systems, Tree Improvement, Watershed Management, Wildlife Biology, Protected Area Management
- **Paper II:** Forest Mensuration, Remote Sensing & GIS, Forest Economics, Forest Policy & Law, Forest Protection, Tribals & Forest

#### B. Agriculture
- **Paper I:** Agronomy, Soil Science, Plant Physiology, Genetics & Plant Breeding
- **Paper II:** Horticulture, Plant Pathology, Entomology, Agricultural Economics, Extension

#### C. Botany
- **Paper I:** Microbiology, Phycology, Mycology, Bryophytes, Pteridophytes, Gymnosperms, Plant Anatomy, Cell Biology
- **Paper II:** Plant Physiology, Ecology, Economic Botany, Genetics, Evolution, Biodiversity

#### D. Zoology
- **Paper I:** Non-Chordata, Chordata, Cell Biology, Genetics, Evolution
- **Paper II:** Physiology, Biochemistry, Ecology, Applied Zoology, Biostatistics

#### E. Chemistry
- **Paper I:** Inorganic Chemistry (Structure, Bonding, s/p/d/f block), Coordination Chemistry
- **Paper II:** Organic Chemistry, Spectroscopy, Physical Chemistry

#### F. Geology
- **Paper I:** General Geology, Structural Geology, Geomorphology, Palaeontology, Stratigraphy
- **Paper II:** Mineralogy, Petrology, Economic Geology, Hydrogeology, Engineering Geology

#### G. Civil Engineering
- **Paper I:** Structural Analysis, RCC, Steel Structures, Soil Mechanics, Hydraulics
- **Paper II:** Environmental Engineering, Transportation, Survey, Construction Management, Irrigation

#### H. Mathematics
- **Paper I:** Linear Algebra, Calculus, Real Analysis, Complex Analysis, ODE, PDE
- **Paper II:** Modern Algebra, Numerical Analysis, Mechanics, Statistics, Probability

## 4. UPSC CMS (Combined Medical Services Examination)

### 4.1 Overview
- **Conducting Body:** UPSC
- **Purpose:** Recruitment of **Medical Officers (Group A)** in Central Government Organisations
- **Started:** 1977
- **Examination Mode:** Computer Based Test (CBT) – since 2014

### 4.2 Posts / Services Recruited

#### Category I
- **Medical Officer Grade (Level 10)** in the General Duty Medical Officer (GDMO) sub-cadre of the **Central Health Service (CHS)**

#### Category II
- **Assistant Divisional Medical Officer (ADMO)** in the **Indian Railway Health Service (IRHS)**
- **General Duty Medical Officer** in the **New Delhi Municipal Council (NDMC)**
- **General Duty Medical Officer Grade II** in the **Municipal Corporation of Delhi (MCD)**
- (Recent addition) **Insurance Medical Officer (IMO) Grade II** in **ESIC**

### 4.3 Eligibility Criteria
| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Nationality | Indian / Nepal / Bhutan / Tibetan refugee / PIO |
| Educational Qualification | **MBBS** (passed both written and practical parts of final exam) |
| Age Limit | 18 to **32 years** (CHS post: 35 years) |
| Application Fee | ₹200 (Female/SC/ST exempted) |

### 4.4 Exam Pattern (2 Papers + Interview)

| Stage | Subject | Questions | Duration | Marks |
|-------|---------|-----------|----------|-------|
| Paper I | General Medicine (96) + Paediatrics (24) | 120 | 2 hours | 250 |
| Paper II | Surgery (40) + Gynae & Obs (40) + PSM (40) | 120 | 2 hours | 250 |
| Stage II | Personality Test (Interview) | – | ~30 min | 100 |
| **Total** | | | | **600** |

- **Negative Marking:** 1/3rd deduction for wrong answers
- **Both papers** attempted on the **same day**

### 4.5 Detailed Syllabus

#### Paper I: General Medicine & Paediatrics (250 Marks)

**A. General Medicine (96 Qs)**
- Cardiology: Ischemic Heart Disease, Heart Failure, Arrhythmias, Hypertension
- Respiratory: Pneumonia, Asthma, COPD, TB, Pleural Diseases
- Gastrointestinal: Peptic Ulcer, Hepatitis, Cirrhosis, IBS, IBD
- Hematology: Anemia, Leukemia, Lymphoma, Bleeding Disorders
- Neurology: Stroke, Epilepsy, Parkinson's, Migraine, Meningitis
- Endocrinology: Diabetes, Thyroid Disorders, Adrenal Disorders
- Infectious Diseases: Malaria, Typhoid, Dengue, HIV, Rabies
- Rheumatology, Nephrology, Dermatology
- Nutritional & Metabolic Disorders
- Critical Care Medicine, Poisoning

**B. Paediatrics (24 Qs)**
- Common Childhood Diseases (Measles, Mumps, Chickenpox)
- Growth & Development, Nutrition, Immunization
- Neonatal Care, Birth Asphyxia, Prematurity
- Congenital Heart Diseases
- Genetic Disorders, Inborn Errors of Metabolism
- Pediatric Emergencies
- WHO Integrated Management of Neonatal & Childhood Illness (IMNCI)

#### Paper II: Surgery, Gynae & Obs, PSM (250 Marks)

**A. Surgery (40 Qs) – includes ENT, Ophthalmology, Traumatology, Orthopaedics**
- General Surgery: Hernia, Appendicitis, Bowel Obstruction, Perforation
- Surgical Oncology, Laparoscopic Surgery
- Orthopaedics: Fractures, Joint Replacement, Bone Tumors
- ENT: Otitis Media, Sinusitis, Tonsillitis
- Ophthalmology: Cataract, Glaucoma, Retinal Detachment
- Traumatology: Head Injury, Burns, Poly-trauma

**B. Gynaecology & Obstetrics (40 Qs)**
- Pregnancy: Normal & High-Risk
- Labor: Stages, Monitoring, Complications
- Puerperium, Lactation
- Eclampsia, Antepartum Hemorrhage
- Family Planning Methods (Barrier, Hormonal, IUD, Sterilisation)
- Gynaecological Disorders, Infertility
- Menstrual Disorders, Reproductive Tract Infections

**C. Preventive & Social Medicine (PSM) (40 Qs)**
- Epidemiology: Concepts, Methods, Measures
- National Health Programs: NHM, RNTCP, NVBDCP, NACP, Mission Indradhanush
- Demography, Population Dynamics
- Nutrition, Immunization
- Communicable & Non-Communicable Diseases
- Environmental Health, Sanitation
- Occupational Health, Disaster Management
- Health Planning & Management

### 4.6 Salary Structure
- **Pay Level:** 10 (for GDMO), 9 (for some posts)
- **Basic Pay:** ₹56,100 (Level 10)
- **Gross In-Hand Salary:** ₹85,000 – ₹1,10,000 (approx., with NPA, DA, HRA)
- **NPA (Non-Practicing Allowance):** 20% of Basic Pay
- **Career Progression:** MO → Specialist → Senior Specialist → Chief Medical Officer → Director General (Health Services)

### 5.7 Top Tips for Preparation
1. **Know the Syllabus thoroughly** – UPSC exams test depth, not breadth.
2. **Previous Year Papers** are gold – solve the last 10 years for pattern mastery.
3. **Standard Reference Books** over multiple sources – depth > coverage.
4. **Daily Current Affairs** (15–20 mins) is non-negotiable.
5. **Mock Tests** under timed conditions for speed and accuracy.
6. **NCERT Books (Class 6–12)** form the base for all UPSC exams.
7. **Time Management:** 6–9 months dedicated preparation is recommended.
8. **For IFoS Physical Standards** – start walking 25 km/wk well in advance.
9. **For CMS** – keep revising MBBS subjects; focus on differential diagnosis.
10. **For EPFO** – Labour Laws need conceptual clarity (Bare Acts > commentaries).
