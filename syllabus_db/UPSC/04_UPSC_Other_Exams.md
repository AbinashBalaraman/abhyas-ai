## 1. UPSC IES (Engineering Services Examination)

### Overview
The **Engineering Services Examination (ESE)**, informally known as **Indian Engineering Services (IES)**, is conducted annually by UPSC to recruit Group A engineering officers in four streams: Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, and Electronics & Telecommunication. Started in 1923; ~3.5 lakh candidates appear yearly for ~500–700 vacancies.

### Posts (Group A Engineering Services)

**Civil Engineering (CE)** — e.g., Indian Railway Service of Engineers, Central Engineering Service (CPWD), Central Engineering Service (Roads), AEE in Border Road Engineering Service, Indian Defence Service of Engineers (MES), Indian Ordnance Factory Services (Civil), Central Water Engineering Service, Indian Skill Development Service, Survey of India Service.

**Mechanical Engineering (ME)** — e.g., Indian Railway Service of Mechanical Engineering, AEE in GSI Engineering Service, Indian Defence Service of Engineers, Indian Ordnance Factory Services (Mech), Indian Naval Armament/Material Management Service (Mech), Central Power Engineering Service (Mech), Central Electrical and Mechanical Engineering Service (CPWD), Defence Aeronautical Quality Assurance Services, AEE (E&M) Border Roads.

**Electrical Engineering (EE)** — e.g., Indian Railway Service of Electrical Engineers, Central Electrical & Mechanical Engineering Service (CPWD), Indian Defence Service of Engineers, Indian Ordnance Factory Services (Elec), Indian Naval Material Management Service (Elec), Central Power Engineering Service, Indian Naval Armament Service (Elec), Defence Aeronautical Quality Assurance Service/SSO-II.

**Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering (E&T)** — e.g., Indian Railway Service of Signal Engineers, Indian Radio Regulatory Service, Indian Telecommunication Service, Indian Ordnance Factory Services (Electronics), Indian Naval Armament/Material Management Service (E&T), Junior Telecom Officer (Group B), Central Power Engineering Service (E&T), Defence Aeronautical Quality Assurance Service/SSO-II (E&T), Indian Skill Development Service.

### Eligibility
- **Nationality**: Citizen of India (or Nepal/Bhutan; PIO from specified countries with intent of permanent settlement).
- **Educational Qualification**: B.E./B.Tech from a recognized university (or equivalent). M.Sc. with Wireless Communications, Electronics, Radio Physics, or Radio Engineering accepted for certain posts/services.
- **Age**: 21–30 years (as on 1 January of exam year).
  - OBC (Non-Creamy Layer): +3 years
  - SC/ST: +5 years
  - Defence Services Personnel disabled in operations: +4 years
  - Ex-servicemen: +5 years
  - J&K domiciles (1 Jan 1980 – 31 Dec 1989): +5 years
  - Central Govt. Engineering Department employees: +5 years
- **Attempts**: Unlimited until upper age limit.
- **Fee**: ₹200 (Female/SC/ST/PwBD candidates exempted).

### Exam Pattern (3 Stages — Total 1300 Marks)

| Stage | Type | Paper | Duration | Marks |
|-------|------|-------|----------|-------|
| **I: Prelims (Objective)** | Screening | Paper I: General Studies & Engineering Aptitude | 2 hours | 200 |
| | | Paper II: Engineering Discipline (CE/ME/EE/E&T) | 3 hours | 300 |
| **II: Mains (Conventional/Descriptive)** | Merit | Paper I: Engineering Discipline | 3 hours | 300 |
| | | Paper II: Engineering Discipline | 3 hours | 300 |
| **III: Personality Test** | Interview | — | — | 200 |

Candidates qualifying Stage I appear for Stage II; qualifiers of Stage II appear for Stage III. **Stage I is qualifying only** (marks not added to final merit); Stage II (600) + Stage III (200) determine final ranking.

### Detailed Syllabus — Paper I (GS & Engineering Aptitude)

1. **Current Issues**: National/international events of economic, social, industrial, environmental importance; Government policies; Indian Constitution basics.
2. **Engineering Aptitude**: Logical reasoning, analytical ability, problem-solving, design-oriented thinking, numerical analysis.
3. **Engineering Mathematics** (common to all streams):
   - Linear Algebra: Matrices, determinants, eigenvalues, eigenvectors, rank, systems of linear equations.
   - Calculus: Limits, continuity, derivatives, partial derivatives, maxima/minima, indeterminate forms, Taylor/Maclaurin series, definite integrals, applications (area, volume), multiple integrals.
   - Differential Equations: First-order ODEs (variable separable, homogeneous, linear, exact), higher-order linear ODEs with constant coefficients, complementary function, particular integral (PI by undetermined coefficients/variation of parameters), Euler-Cauchy, partial differential equations (Laplace, wave, heat equations), Fourier series.
   - Complex Variables: Analytic functions, Cauchy-Riemann equations, Cauchy's theorem, Cauchy's integral formula, Taylor/Laurent series, residues, complex integration.
   - Probability & Statistics: Conditional probability, Bayes' theorem, random variables (discrete/continuous), binomial, Poisson, normal distributions; correlation, regression, sampling, hypothesis testing, central limit theorem.
   - Numerical Methods: Solutions of algebraic/transcendental equations (Newton-Raphson, bisection), interpolation (Newton, Lagrange), numerical integration (Trapezoidal, Simpson's), numerical solutions of ODEs (Euler, Runge-Kutta).

### Detailed Syllabus — Civil Engineering (CE)

- **Building Materials**: Stone, bricks, cement, lime, timber, mortars, concrete (properties, mix design, durability), ferrous/non-ferrous metals, paints, varnishes, plastics.
- **Solid Mechanics**: Stress, strain, shear force and bending moment diagrams, bending stress, shear stress, deflection of beams, torsion, combined stresses, principal stresses, columns, theories of failure, Mohr's circle.
- **Structural Analysis**: Analysis of statically determinate/indeterminate structures (trusses, beams, frames), arches, cables, influence line diagrams, matrix methods, energy methods, plastic analysis.
- **Design of Steel & RC Structures**: Limit state design, working stress method, beams, slabs, columns, footings, retaining walls, water tanks, design of steel tension/compression members, beams, roof trusses, plate girders.
- **Construction Practice, Planning & Management**: Construction equipment, project planning (CPM, PERT), cost estimation, contracts, safety, quality control.
- **Fluid Mechanics & Hydraulics**: Fluid properties, fluid statics, kinematics, dynamics, Bernoulli's equation, laminar/turbulent flow, flow measurement, boundary layer, drag/lift, open channel flow, hydraulic jump, pipe flow networks.
- **Hydrology & Water Resources**: Hydrologic cycle, precipitation, evaporation, infiltration, runoff estimation, flood routing, reservoir planning, water logging, drainage, irrigation canals, headworks, cross-drainage works.
- **Water Supply & Waste Water Engineering: Quality standards, treatment (sedimentation, filtration, disinfection), distribution networks, sewerage, sewage treatment (primary/secondary/tertiary), sludge disposal.
- **Irrigation Engineering**: Water requirements of crops, duty, delta, consumptive use, irrigation methods, design of irrigation channels, lined/unlined canals, dams (gravity, earth, arch), spillways, energy dissipators.
- **Soil Mechanics & Foundation Engineering**: Index properties, classification, permeability, seepage, effective stress, compaction, consolidation, shear strength, earth pressure theories, bearing capacity, shallow/deep foundations, slope stability.
- **Surveying & Transportation Engineering**: Chain, compass, theodolite, levelling, traversing, triangulation, curves, photogrammetry, GIS basics; highway planning, geometric design, traffic engineering, pavement design, railway engineering, airport planning, tunneling.
- **Environmental Engineering**: Air pollution (sources, effects, control), water pollution, noise pollution, solid waste management, environmental impact assessment, sustainable development.

### Detailed Syllabus — Mechanical Engineering (ME)

- **Engineering Mechanics**: Statics, dynamics, kinematics of particles/rigid bodies, work-energy principle, impulse-momentum, virtual work, friction, central force motion.
- **Strength of Materials**: Stress, strain, bending moment/shear force diagrams, deflection, torsion, columns, springs, thin/thick cylinders, combined loading.
- **Theory of Machines & Vibrations**: Kinematic chains, cams, gears, gear trains, governors, flywheels, balancing, vibrations (free/forced/damped), critical speed, vibration isolation.
- **Design**: Design of machine elements (shafts, keys, couplings, belts, chains, springs, bearings, gears, brakes, clutches, IC engine components, pressure vessels, welding, riveting.
- **Fluid Mechanics & Turbomachinery**: Fluid properties, fluid statics, kinematics, dynamics, boundary layer, pipe flow, dimensional analysis, similitude, hydraulic turbines, centrifugal/reciprocating pumps, compressors.
- **Heat Transfer**: Conduction (1D, 2D, fin), convection (free, forced), radiation, heat exchangers, boiling, condensation, mass transfer.
- **Thermodynamics**: Laws of thermodynamics, properties of pure substances, ideal/gas mixtures, entropy, availability, psychrometrics, VCRS, VARS.
- **Power Engineering & IC Engines**: Steam tables, steam generators, steam turbines, nozzles, condensers, power plants (thermal, hydro, nuclear, renewable); SI/CI engines, fuel systems, combustion, emissions, performance, supercharging.
- **Refrigeration & Air Conditioning**: Vapour compression/absorption systems, refrigerants, psychrometrics, cooling load calculation, duct design, cold storage.
- **Production Engineering**: Casting, forming, welding, machining (turning, drilling, milling, grinding), tool materials, cutting tool geometry, metrology, non-destructive testing, computer-integrated manufacturing (CIM), CNC, additive manufacturing.
- **Industrial Engineering & Operations Research**: Work-study, time/motion study, ergonomics, inventory control (EOQ, ABC, JIT), production planning, forecasting, linear programming, transportation/assignment problems, queuing theory, PERT/CPM, simulation.

### Detailed Syllabus — Electrical Engineering (EE)

- **Engineering Mathematics** (same as Paper I).
- **Electrical Materials**: Conductors, semiconductors, insulators, magnetic materials, dielectric materials, superconductors, photovoltaic materials.
- **Electric Circuits**: Network elements, KCL/KVL, mesh/nodal analysis, network theorems (Thevenin, Norton, Superposition, Maximum Power Transfer), transient response, AC circuits (RLC, resonance, three-phase), coupled circuits, two-port networks, Laplace transform.
- **Fields**: Electrostatics, magnetostatics, Maxwell's equations, electromagnetic waves, waveguides, transmission lines.
- **Electrical Machines**: DC machines (generator, motor, characteristics, starting, speed control, applications); transformers (equivalent circuit, regulation, efficiency, three-phase, autotransformer, special transformers); induction motors (three-phase, single-phase, starting, speed control); synchronous machines (generator, motor, salient pole, parallel operation); fractional HP machines.
- **Power Systems**: Generation (thermal, hydro, nuclear, renewable), transmission (line parameters, performance, sag, corona, insulators), distribution, load flow studies, fault analysis, stability, power system protection (relays, circuit breakers, fuses, surge protection), switchgear, power quality, economics, tariff, restructuring, smart grid.
- **Control Systems**: Open/closed loop, transfer function, block diagram reduction, signal flow graph, time response, stability (Routh-Hurwitz, Nyquist, Bode), root locus, PID controllers, state space analysis, digital control.
- **Electrical Measurements & Instrumentation**: Units, standards, error analysis, measuring instruments (PMMC, MI, electrodynamic, electrostatic, rectifier, induction type), bridges (Wheatstone, Kelvin, Schering, Anderson), potentiometer, CT/PT, oscilloscope, digital instruments, transducers, signal conditioning, data acquisition.
- **Power Electronics**: Power semiconductor devices (SCR, TRIAC, GTO, MOSFET, IGBT), characteristics, firing circuits, commutation; converters (AC-DC, DC-DC, DC-AC, AC-AC), inverters, choppers, cycloconverters, PWM, applications (SMPS, UPS, drives, HVDC).
- **Analog & Digital Electronics**: Diodes, BJT, FET, amplifiers (small signal analysis, frequency response, feedback), oscillators, op-amps (characteristics, applications, active filters), timers; number systems, Boolean algebra, logic gates, combinational/sequential circuits, flip-flops, counters, registers, multiplexers, demultiplexers, encoders, decoders, A/D & D/A converters, memory, microprocessors (8085/8086), microcontrollers.
- **Communication Systems**: AM, FM, PM, sampling, quantization, PCM, delta modulation, ASK, PSK, FSK, QAM, Shannon's theorem, noise analysis, optical fiber communication, satellite communication basics.

### Detailed Syllabus — Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering (E&T)

- **Engineering Mathematics** (same as Paper I).
- **Networks, Signals & Systems**: Network analysis, two-port networks, network theorems; continuous/discrete-time signals, LTI systems, Fourier series, Fourier transform, Laplace transform, Z-transform, sampling theorem, aliasing.
- **Control Systems**: Same as EE syllabus (transfer function, stability, time/frequency response, controllers, state space).
- **Analog & Digital Circuits**: Diodes, BJT, FET, amplifiers, oscillators, op-amps, filters, voltage regulators, 555 timer, PLL; Boolean algebra, logic families (TTL, CMOS), combinational/sequential logic, A/D, D/A, memory, microprocessors, microcontrollers.
- **Communication**: AM, FM, PM, noise, sampling, PCM, DM, DPCM, ADM, digital modulation (ASK, FSK, PSK, QAM, MSK), information theory, error control coding (linear block, convolutional), spread spectrum, multiplexing (TDM, FDM, WDM).
- **Electromagnetic Theory**: Maxwell's equations, plane waves in different media, reflection/refraction, polarization, transmission lines, waveguides, antennas (basic parameters, types), wave propagation (ground, sky, space).
- **Microwave Engineering**: Microwave sources (klystron, magnetron, TWT, Gunn diode), waveguides, microwave components (directional coupler, magic tee, isolator, circulator), microwave measurements.
- **Microprocessors & Computer Engineering**: 8085/8086 architecture, instruction set, assembly language, interrupts, memory & I/O interfacing, peripheral ICs (8255, 8253, 8251, 8259, 8279); computer organization, data structures, algorithms, operating systems, databases, programming (C/C++).
- **VLSI & Embedded Systems**: VLSI design flow, CMOS logic, stick diagrams, layout, FPGA, ASIC design; embedded systems architecture, RTOS basics.
- **RF & Microwave, Digital Communication, Optical Fiber, Satellite, Mobile, Networking**: RF systems, microwave links, cellular concepts (GSM, CDMA, 4G/5G), optical fiber (sources, detectors, losses, dispersion, WDM), satellite orbits, link budget, multiple access (FDMA, TDMA, CDMA).

### Personality Test (Interview)
- 200 marks; assesses suitability for a career in public service.
- Tests: mental alertness, critical powers of assimilation, clear & logical exposition, judgment, variety & depth of interests, social cohesion & leadership, intellectual & moral integrity.
- Notes on current affairs, awareness of socio-economic issues, technical knowledge in chosen engineering discipline.

### Salary & Career Progression
- **Pay Level 10** (₹56,100 – ₹1,77,500) [7th CPC].
- Group A service with fast-track promotions to Chief Engineer, General Manager, Member (Technical), etc.
- Approx. in-hand starting salary: ₹85,000–₹1,00,000 (with DA, HRA, perks).

### Best Books for IES
- **Engineering Mathematics**: B.S. Grewal, M.K. Venkataraman (Higher Engineering Mathematics).
- **General Studies & Engineering Aptitude**: Standard General Knowledge Compendium, Civil/Mechanical/Electrical Engineering Aptitude (by Made Easy).
- **CE/ME/EE/E&T**: Made Easy Publications, IES Master, GATE Academy study material; standard textbooks (R.C. Hibbeler, B.C. Punmia, S.K. Mondal, P.S. Bimbhra, etc.).
- **Previous Years' Papers**: Made Easy/IES Master compilations.
- **Current Affairs**: The Hindu, PIB, Indian Economy (Ramesh Singh/Mishra & Puri).

## 2. UPSC Geo-Scientist (Combined Geo-Scientist and Geologist Examination)

### Overview
The **Combined Geo-Scientist and Geologist Examination** recruits **Group A officers** to the Geological Survey of India (GSI), Central Ground Water Board (CGWB), and other scientific organizations. Conducted in three streams: Geology, Geophysics, and Chemistry.

### Posts (Group A)
- **Geologist, Group A** — Geological Survey of India (Ministry of Mines).
- **Geophysicist, Group A** — GSI.
- **Chemist, Group A** — GSI.
- **Scientist 'B' (Hydrogeology)** — Central Ground Water Board (Ministry of Jal Shakti).
- **Scientist 'B' (Geophysics)** — CGWB.
- **Scientist 'B' (Chemical)** — CGWB.

### Eligibility
- **Nationality**: Indian citizen.
- **Educational Qualification**:
  - For **Geologist** / **Scientist 'B' (Hydrogeology)**: Master's degree in Geology / Geological Science / Applied Geology / Geo-Exploration / Engineering Geology / Marine Geology / Earth Science & Resource Management / Hydrogeology (or equivalent).
  - For **Geophysicist** / **Scientist 'B' (Geophysics)**: M.Sc. in Physics / Applied Physics / Geophysics / Exploration Geophysics / Applied Geophysics / Marine Geophysics (or equivalent); or M.Sc. (Tech) in Applied Geophysics.
  - For **Chemist** / **Scientist 'B' (Chemical)**: M.Sc. in Chemistry / Applied Chemistry / Analytical Chemistry (or equivalent).
- **Minimum Marks**: 50% for UR/EWS; 45% for SC/ST/OBC/PwBD.
- **Age**: 21–32 years (as on 1 January of exam year).
  - OBC: +3 years; SC/ST: +5 years; PwBD: +10 years (UR), +13 (OBC), +15 (SC/ST).
- **Attempts**: No limit (within age).
- **Fee**: ₹200 (exempted for Female/SC/ST/PwBD).

### Exam Pattern (3 Stages)

| Stage | Type | Papers | Duration | Marks |
|-------|------|--------|----------|-------|
| **I: Prelims (Objective)** | Screening | Stream-specific Papers I, II, III | 2 hours each | 100 each (Total 300) |
| **II: Mains (Descriptive)** | Merit | Stream-specific Papers I, II, III | 3 hours each | 200 each (Total 600) |
| **III: Personality Test** | Interview | — | — | 200 |

Note: From 2024, Mains has 3 descriptive papers (was 2 earlier for some streams). Prelims is qualifying; Mains (600) + Interview (200) = **800** final merit marks. Candidates choose one stream (Geology/Geophysics/Chemistry).

### Detailed Syllabus — Geology Stream

**Paper I (Geology – General):**
- **Physical Geology**: Geomorphology, weathering, soil, mass wasting, geological work of rivers, glaciers, wind, oceans; volcanism, earthquakes, plate tectonics, continental drift, seafloor spreading, isostasy.
- **Structural Geology**: Stress, strain, deformation, folds, faults, joints, foliation, lineation, unconformities, structural analysis, stereographic projection, shear zones.
- **Mineralogy & Crystallography**: Crystal systems, symmetry, Miller indices; physical, chemical, optical properties of rock-forming minerals; silicate structures; mineral groups (olivine, pyroxene, amphibole, mica, feldspar, garnet, quartz, oxides, carbonates, sulphides).
- **Petrology**: Igneous, sedimentary, metamorphic rocks; classification, texture, structure, genesis; phase diagrams; magmatic differentiation; sedimentary environments, facies, diagenesis; metamorphic grade, facies, P-T-t paths.
- **Stratigraphy**: Principles of stratigraphy, code of stratigraphic nomenclature; geological time scale; Indian stratigraphy (Archaean, Cuddapah, Vindhyan, Gondwana, Triassic-Jurassic, Cretaceous, Deccan Traps, Tertiary, Quaternary); significant boundary stratotypes.
- **Paleontology**: Origin of life, fossils, modes of preservation; systematic paleontology (invertebrates, vertebrates, microfossils, palynology, paleobotany); evolutionary trends; applications in stratigraphy & paleoecology.

**Paper II (Geology – Applied):**
- **Economic Geology**: Ore genesis (magmatic, hydrothermal, sedimentary, residual, supergene); ore minerals of Cu, Pb, Zn, Au, Fe, Mn, Cr, Al, Sn, W, U, Th; coal, petroleum, radioactive minerals; Indian mineral deposits.
- **Hydrogeology**: Occurrence of groundwater, aquifers (confined, unconfined, leaky, perched), aquifer properties (porosity, permeability, transmissivity, storage coefficient); Darcy's law; groundwater flow (Dupuit's equation, well hydraulics – Theis, Jacob, Chow); water wells (construction, development, yield tests); groundwater exploration (geological, geophysical methods); quality of groundwater (physical, chemical, biological, standards, contamination, pollution); groundwater management, recharge, saline water intrusion; Indian hydrogeological conditions.
- **Engineering Geology**: Geotechnical properties of rocks & soils; geological considerations in construction (dams, tunnels, highways, bridges, buildings); landslides, slope stability; earthquake-resistant design; geological site investigations.
- **Geochemistry & Isotope Geology**: Major, trace, REE geochemistry; isotope geochemistry (radiogenic, stable); geochronology (K-Ar, Rb-Sr, Sm-Nd, U-Pb, fission track, C-14); applications.

**Paper III (Geology – Advanced):**
- **Mineral Exploration**: Stages of mineral exploration; geological, geophysical, geochemical, geobotanical methods; remote sensing; sampling; reserve estimation; geostatistics; case studies of Indian mineral deposits.
- **Mining & Ore Dressing**: Methods of mining (opencast, underground); drilling, blasting, ventilation, support; ore dressing (comminution, sizing, concentration methods – gravity, magnetic, electrostatic, froth flotation); environmental aspects of mining.
- **Remote Sensing & GIS**: Principles of remote sensing (EM spectrum, sensors, platforms); visual interpretation, digital image processing; GIS (data models, spatial analysis); GPS; applications in geology.
- **Environmental Geology**: Natural hazards (earthquakes, landslides, floods, cyclones, tsunamis, volcanic eruptions); environmental impact assessment; waste disposal; pollution (air, water, soil); climate change; disaster management.

### Detailed Syllabus — Geophysics Stream

**Paper I (Geophysics – General):**
- **Solid Earth Geophysics**: Earth's structure, composition, gravity field (gravimeters, gravity anomalies, isostasy, isostatic anomalies), seismology (elastic wave theory, body & surface waves, earthquake seismology, magnitude, intensity, seismographs, focal mechanism), geomagnetism (Earth's magnetic field, paleomagnetism, magnetometers), geochronology, radioactivity, heat flow, geodynamics.

**Paper II (Geophysics – Methods):**
- **Gravity & Magnetic Methods**: Gravity prospecting (instruments, corrections, anomalies over simple shapes, interpretation); magnetic prospecting (instruments, corrections, anomalies, interpretation).
- **Seismic Methods**: Seismic refraction & reflection (theory, instruments, surveys, processing, interpretation, CDP stacking, migration).
- **Electrical & Electromagnetic Methods**: Resistivity (sounding, profiling, instruments, interpretation), SP, IP, EM methods (frequency domain, time domain, MT, CSAMT), GPR.
- **Well Logging & Borehole Geophysics**: Open hole logs (SP, GR, resistivity, density, sonic, neutron), correlation, dipmeter; production logging.
- **Radiometric Methods**: Radioactive decay, radiometric prospecting, gamma-ray spectrometry.
- **Geophysical Signal Processing & Inversion**: Fourier transform, filtering, deconvolution, migration; inverse theory.

**Paper III (Geophysics – Applied):**
- **Mineral Exploration Geophysics**: Integrated geophysical surveys for minerals (base metals, ferrous, non-ferrous, noble metals, industrial minerals, coal), case studies.
- **Hydrogeophysics & Engineering Geophysics**: Geophysical methods for groundwater (resistivity, EM, NMR), site investigations for civil engineering projects (dams, tunnels, buildings).
- **Geophysical Data Interpretation & Case Studies**: Indian case studies (GSI, ONGC, CGWB, AMD).
- **Remote Sensing & GIS**: Same as Geology syllabus; with focus on geophysical applications.

### Detailed Syllabus — Chemistry Stream

**Paper I (Chemistry – Inorganic):**
- **Inorganic Chemistry**: Periodic table, periodicity; chemical bonding (ionic, covalent, coordinate, hydrogen, metallic); acids, bases; non-aqueous solvents; structure, properties, reactions of s-, p-, d-, f-block elements; coordination compounds (Werner's theory, IUPAC nomenclature, isomerism, bonding theories – VBT, CFT, MOT); organometallics, bioinorganic chemistry; nuclear chemistry (radioactivity, nuclear reactions, fission, fusion).

**Paper II (Chemistry – Organic):**
- **Organic Chemistry**: Bonding, structure (atomic, molecular orbitals); reaction mechanisms (SN1, SN2, E1, E2, addition, elimination, substitution, free radical, carbene, nitrene); stereochemistry (isomerism, chirality, R/S, E/Z, conformations); carbohydrates, amino acids, proteins, nucleic acids, lipids, vitamins, hormones, alkaloids, terpenes, flavonoids; reagents (organometallic, oxidizing, reducing); photochemistry, pericyclic reactions; spectroscopy (UV-Vis, IR, NMR, Mass).

**Paper III (Chemistry – Physical & Analytical):**
- **Physical Chemistry**: Thermodynamics (laws, enthalpy, entropy, free energy, chemical equilibrium, phase equilibrium, solutions, colligative properties); kinetics (rate laws, order, molecularity, mechanisms, enzyme kinetics, fast reactions); electrochemistry (ionic equilibrium, conductance, EMF, fuel cells, corrosion); surface chemistry, colloids, adsorption; quantum chemistry; solid state; chemical bonding.
- **Analytical Chemistry**: Classical methods (gravimetric, volumetric – acid-base, redox, complexometric, precipitation); instrumental methods (potentiometry, conductometry, polarography, amperometry, coulometry, spectroscopy – AAS, ICP-AES, ICP-MS, XRF, XRD, NMR, IR, UV-Vis, chromatography – GC, HPLC, TLC, mass spectrometry); environmental, geochemical, water & soil analysis.

### Personality Test (Interview)
- 200 marks; assesses scientific aptitude, reasoning ability, awareness of recent developments in earth sciences / chemistry, suitability for Group A scientific service.

### Salary & Career
- **Pay Level 10** (₹56,100 – ₹1,77,500).
- Promotions: Director, Chief Geologist, Dy. Director General, Director General (GSI).
- Field allowance, HRA, transport allowance apply.

### Best Books for Geo-Scientist
- **Geology**: Engineering Geology by D.V. Reddy; Structural Geology by M.P. Billings; Petrology by Tyrell / Blatt & Tracy; Stratigraphy of India by M.S. Krishnan; Economic Geology by Umeshwar Prasad; Hydrogeology by K. Todd & L. Mays; IGNOU Geology materials.
- **Geophysics**: Applied Geophysics by Telford, Geldart & Sheriff; Gravity & Magnetic Methods by Dobrin & Savit; Seismic Methods by Yilmaz; Electromagnetic Methods by Kaufman & Keller; Mining Geophysics by Parasnis.
- **Chemistry**: Inorganic Chemistry by J.D. Lee / Shriver & Atkins; Organic Chemistry by Morrison & Boyd / Clayden; Physical Chemistry by P. Atkins; Analytical Chemistry by Skoog / Christian.

## 3. UPSC SO (Section Officer) LDCE

### Overview
Section Officer (Grade 'B') in Central Secretariat Service (CSS) is recruited through a **Limited Departmental Competitive Examination (LDCE)** conducted by UPSC. Open only to existing Central Government employees (UDC/Assistants with prescribed service).

### Posts
- **Section Officer (Grade 'B')** in Central Secretariat Service (CSS) and other attached/subordinate offices of various Ministries/Departments.
- Ranks: Section Officer / Assistant Director (in Directorates).

### Eligibility (LDCE for SO Grade 'B')
- **Who can apply**: Lower Division Clerks (LDCs) / Assistants / equivalent grade employees of CSS and other Central Govt. offices.
- **Service requirement**: Minimum 5 years of regular service in the feeder grade (rules vary per cadre).
- **Age limit**: Generally up to 50 years (relaxation as per Government orders for SC/ST).
- **Educational Qualification**: No separate minimum, but graduation preferred.

### Exam Pattern
- **Online/Computer Based Test (CBT)**: Objective MCQs
- **Descriptive Paper**: Essay/Letter/Report writing (rare for LDCE).
- **No Interview** in most cases (selection purely on written exam).

### Detailed Syllabus (Objective CBT)
- **English Language**: Comprehension, vocabulary, grammar, sentence correction, idioms, one-word substitution, synonyms/antonyms.
- **Reasoning & General Intelligence**: Analogies, coding-decoding, series, syllogism, blood relations, direction, Venn diagrams, statement & assumption, data sufficiency.
- **Quantitative Aptitude**: Number system, simplification, percentage, average, ratio, profit-loss, simple/compound interest, time-work, speed-distance, geometry, data interpretation (bar, pie, table, line).
- **General Awareness**: Indian Polity, Constitution, History, Geography, Economics, Science, Current Affairs, Government schemes, Awards, Books, Sports.
- **Computer Knowledge**: Basics of MS Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint), internet, email, operating systems, networking fundamentals.

### Salary & Career
- **Pay Level 7** (₹44,900 – ₹1,42,400).
- Promotions: Under Secretary → Deputy Secretary → Director → Joint Secretary → Additional Secretary (CSS hierarchy).
- Pay Level 8 (Section Officer NFSG) → Pay Level 10 (Assistant Secretary) → Pay Level 11 (Under Secretary) → Pay Level 12 (Deputy Secretary) → Pay Level 13 (Director) → Pay Level 14 (Joint Secretary) → Pay Level 15 (Additional Secretary).

### Best Books
- Standard General Knowledge Compendium; R.S. Aggarwal (Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning); Wren & Martin (English); Standard Exam Guide/ Disha Section Officer guides.

## 4. UPSC ASO (Assistant Section Officer) in CSS

### Overview
**Assistant Section Officer (ASO)** is the entry-level (probationer) Group B (Non-Gazetted) post in CSS, recruited through a **Limited Departmental Competitive Examination (LDCE)** for existing UDCs/Assistants. **No direct open recruitment** by UPSC for ASO; only departmental candidates.

### Posts
- **ASO** in Central Secretariat Service, attached/subordinate offices of various Ministries, Cabinet Secretariat.

### Eligibility
- **Who can apply**: LDCs / UDCs / Assistants / equivalent grade with prescribed service (3–5 years, depending on cadre).
- **Age limit**: Up to 50 years (relaxation for SC/ST).
- **Educational Qualification**: Graduation or equivalent (per service rules).

### Exam Pattern (LDCE)
- **Stage 1: Objective Test (CBT)** – MCQs
- **Stage 2: Descriptive Test** – Essay, Letter, Précis, Comprehension
- **No Interview** in most LDCEs (purely on written merit).

### Detailed Syllabus
- **English Language & Comprehension**: Reading comprehension, grammar, sentence improvement, fill in the blanks, error spotting, active-passive, direct-indirect, idioms, vocabulary.
- **Reasoning**: Verbal & non-verbal reasoning, analogies, coding, series, statement & conclusion, syllogism, data sufficiency.
- **General Awareness**: Indian Polity (Constitution, Parliament, ministries), History (Ancient, Medieval, Modern), Geography (India & World), Economics (basic concepts, Five Year Plans), Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Computer basics), Current Affairs, Books & Authors, Awards, Sports.
- **Quantitative Aptitude**: Number system, simplification, percentage, average, ratio, profit-loss, SI/CI, time-work, speed-distance, geometry, data interpretation.
- **Descriptive Test (Stage 2)**: Essay writing (200–250 words), Letter writing (formal/informal), Précis writing, Comprehension passage.

### Salary & Career
- **Pay Level 7** (₹44,900 – ₹1,42,400) [Entry Grade of CSS].
- Career path: ASO → SO → Under Secretary → Deputy Secretary → Director → Joint Secretary → Additional Secretary.
- DA, HRA, Medical, LTC, GPF, pension benefits.

### Best Books
- Standard General Knowledge Compendium; R.S. Aggarwal (Quant, Reasoning); Wren & Martin (English); Standard Exam GuideASO/LDCE guides; Pratiyogita Darpan; newspapers (The Hindu, Indian Express).

## 5. UPSC Stenographer Grade 'A' & 'B'

### Overview
UPSC recruits **Stenographers** to various Ministries/Departments through an open Computer Based Examination (CBE) followed by a **Shorthand Skill Test**.

### Posts
- **Stenographer Grade 'A'** — Group 'B' Gazetted (Pay Level 6 / 7).
- **Stenographer Grade 'B'** — Group 'C' (Pay Level 4 / 5).
- Departments: Ministries of Railways, Defence, External Affairs, Home Affairs, Finance, etc.

### Eligibility
- **Educational Qualification**: 12th Pass (or equivalent) from a recognized Board/University.
- **Skill**: English/Hindi Shorthand at prescribed speed (Grade 'B' 80 wpm; Grade 'A' 100 wpm).
- **Age**: 18–27 years (Grade 'B'); 18–30 (Grade 'A'). OBC +3, SC/ST +5, PwBD +10.

### Exam Pattern
- **Stage 1: Computer Based Examination (Objective)**: 200 questions, 200 marks, 2 hours.
- **Stage 2: Shorthand Skill Test (Qualifying)**:
  - **Grade 'A'**: 100 wpm English/Hindi shorthand dictation for 10 minutes, transcription 40 min (Eng) / 55 min (Hindi) on computer.
  - **Grade 'B'**: 80 wpm shorthand, transcription 50 min (Eng) / 65 min (Hindi).

### Detailed Syllabus (Objective CBE)
- **English Language & Comprehension**: Vocabulary, grammar, sentence correction, comprehension, idioms, synonyms/antonyms, active-passive, direct-indirect.
- **General Awareness**: Polity, History, Geography, Economics, Science, Current Affairs, Books, Awards, Sports.
- **Reasoning & General Intelligence**: Analogies, coding-decoding, series, syllogism, blood relations, Venn diagrams, statement & conclusion, data sufficiency.
- **Note**: No Quantitative Aptitude; clerical aptitude is tested (spelling, sentence order, etc.) in some years.

### Salary
- **Grade 'B'**: Pay Level 4 (₹25,500 – ₹81,100).
- **Grade 'A'**: Pay Level 6 (₹35,400 – ₹1,12,400) or Pay Level 7 (₹44,900 – ₹1,42,400) for senior positions.
- DA, HRA, transport allowance, leave benefits.

### Best Books
- Standard General Knowledge Compendium; Wren & Martin (English); Standard Previous Years Solved Papers's / Standard Exam GuideStenographer guides; shorthand practice (Pitman / shorthand books).

## 6. UPSC IES (Indian Economic Service) and ISS (Indian Statistical Service)

### 6.1 Indian Economic Service (IES)
- **Post**: Group A cadre under Ministry of Finance (Department of Economic Affairs); cadre posts in 55 Union Ministries/Departments.
- **Role**: Economic analysis & policy advice; cadre strength ~518.
- **Eligibility**: Master's degree in Economics / Applied Economics / Business Economics / Econometrics / Mathematical Economics / Commerce (with Economics).
- **Age**: 21–30 years.
- **Exam Pattern**: Single combined exam (IES + ISS) – Written examination + Interview.
  - General Economics – I (200 marks)
  - General Economics – II (200 marks)
  - Indian Economics – I (200 marks)
  - Indian Economics – II (200 marks)
  - **Total 800 marks written + 200 interview = 1000**
- **Topics**: Microeconomics, Macroeconomics, Indian Economy, Public Finance, Trade, Development, Statistics, Mathematics for Economists, International Trade, Monetary Economics, Planning.
- **Pay Level 10** (₹56,100 – ₹1,77,500) initial; senior posts higher.

### 6.2 Indian Statistical Service (ISS)
- **Post**: Group A under Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation (MoSPI); cadre posts in Central Statistical Organisation (CSO), NSSO, NITI Aayog, etc.
- **Eligibility**: Master's degree in Statistics / Mathematical Statistics / Applied Statistics / Operations Research (with Statistics papers).
- **Age**: 21–30 years.
- **Exam Pattern**: Same as IES (same exam conducted together with IES).
  - **Statistics – I** (200 marks): Probability, Mathematical Statistics, Distribution Theory, Estimation, Hypothesis Testing, Sampling.
  - **Statistics – II** (200 marks): Applied Statistics, Time Series, Index Numbers, Demography, National Income, Multivariate Analysis.
  - **Statistics – III** (200 marks): Statistical Methods, Economics, Operations Research, Computer Applications, Indian Statistical System.
  - **Statistics – IV** (200 marks): Operations Research, Computer Science, Numerical Analysis, Linear Models, Design of Experiments.
  - **Total 800 written + 200 interview = 1000**.
- **Pay Level 10** initial.

### Best Books for IES/ISS
- IES: Indian Economy by Ramesh Singh / S.K. Mishra; Public Finance by Musgrave & Musgrave; Macroeconomics by Dornbusch & Fischer; International Economics by Salvatore.
- ISS: Mathematical Statistics by P. Mukhopadhyay; Linear Algebra by Strang; Time Series by Box & Jenkins; Sampling by Cochran; Indian Statistical System publications.

## 7. UPSC Other Central Services (Brief)

UPSC recruits to numerous other central services through various exams / LDCEs. Key ones include:

### 7.1 Indian Statistical Service (ISS) – see Section 6.2

### 7.2 Indian Economic Service (IES – Economics) – see Section 6.1

### 7.3 Central Secretariat Service (CSS)
- **Recruitment**: Since 2003, no direct recruitment through CSE. **Section Officer (Grade 'B')** recruited through LDCE by UPSC.
- **Posts**: Section Officer → Under Secretary → Deputy Secretary → Director → Joint Secretary → Additional Secretary.
- **Pay Levels**: 7 to 15.
- **Roles**: Policy formulation, noting, drafting, parliamentary work, administrative continuity in Ministries.

### 7.4 Central Industrial Security Force (CISF) – Assistant Commandants
- Conducted by UPSC as a separate exam (limited recruitment).

### 7.5 Central Police Forces (CAPF) – Assistant Commandants
- Common exam, but Assistant Commandant posts in BSF, CRPF, CISF, ITBP, SSB, NSG.
- Pay Level 10 (₹56,100 – ₹1,77,500).

### 7.6 Combined Defence Services (CDS)
- For entry to Indian Military Academy (IMA), Air Force Academy (AFA), Naval Academy (NA), Officers Training Academy (OTA).
- **Eligibility**: Graduation (B.A./B.Sc./B.Com./B.E./B.Tech).
- **Exam**: English, GK, Mathematics (for IMA/INA/AFA); English, GK (OTA).
- **Age**: 19–25 (varies by academy).

### 7.7 National Defence Academy (NDA) & Naval Academy (NA)
- For entry to Army, Navy, Air Force wings of NDA + Indian Naval Academy.
- **Eligibility**: 12th Pass (with Physics, Maths for Navy/Air Force).
- **Exam**: Mathematics + GAT (English, GK, Science, Geography, Current Affairs).
- **Age**: 16.5–19.5 years.

### 7.8 Combined Medical Services (CMS)
- For **Assistant Divisional Medical Officer** in Railways, Municipal Corporations, etc.
- **Eligibility**: MBBS, age 18–32.
- **Exam**: Computer-based (Paper I: General Medicine & Paediatrics; Paper II: Surgery, G&O, Preventive & Social Medicine).
- **Pay Level 10** (₹56,100 – ₹1,77,500).

### 7.9 Indian Forest Service (IFoS) Exam
- Conducted along with CSE Prelims & Mains (separate Optional papers + separate Interview).
- **Eligibility**: Bachelor's degree with at least one of – Botany, Chemistry, Geology, Mathematics, Physics, Statistics, Zoology, Agriculture, Animal Husbandry & Veterinary Science, Computer Science, Economics, Engineering, Forestry.
- **Age**: 21–32 (relaxations as per CSE).
- **Pay Level 10** initial, with field allowances.

### 7.10 Indian Civil Accounts Service (ICAS), IDAS, ICLS, IRAS, ITS, etc.
- All Group A central services. Recruitment mostly through **CSE** (toppers who don't get IAS/IFS/IPS get central services).
- ICAS: MOF, IDAS: Ministry of Defence, ICLS: Corporate Law Ministry, IRAS: Railways, ITS: Income Tax, etc.
- Pay Level 10 initial; fast-track to senior administrative positions.

### 7.11 Other UPSC Advertised Posts
- **Scientist 'B' (Geology, Geophysics, Chemistry)**: Same as Combined Geo-Scientist.
- **Drugs Inspector, Assistant Director (various), Specialist, Legal Adviser, Scientific Officer, etc.** – advertised regularly on upsc.gov.in under "Recruitment" section. Each has its own eligibility, exam, and syllabus (advertisement-based).

### 7.12 CBI (DSP) LDCE
- Limited Departmental Competitive Exam for Deputy Superintendent of Police in CBI.
- For Inspectors of CBI; restricted to CBI personnel.

### 7.13 LDCEs (Limited Departmental Competitive Examinations)
- **Section Officer/Steno (Grade B-Grade I) LDCE** – For CSS personnel.
- **SO/ASO LDCE** – For UDC/Assistants.
- **Assistant Director Grade I/II (IEDS) LDCE** – For various cadres.
- Conducted by UPSC for internal promotions.

## Summary

This document covers **8 major UPSC examinations** in detail:

1. **IES (Engineering Services)** — Premier engineering exam with 3-stage process (Prelims, Mains, Interview), 4 streams (CE/ME/EE/E&T), Group A services in Railways, CPWD, Defence, Power, Telecom, etc. Pay Level 10.
2. **Geo-Scientist** — 3-stage exam for Geologist/Geophysicist/Chemist in GSI & CGWB. Master's degree required. Pay Level 10.
3. **SO (LDCE)** — Section Officer Grade 'B' in CSS, departmental LDCE. Pay Level 7.
4. **ASO (LDCE)** — Entry-level CSS post, departmental LDCE. Pay Level 7.
5. **Stenographer (A & B)** — Open UPSC recruitment for 12th-pass with shorthand skill. Pay Level 4-6/7.
6. **IES (Indian Economic Service) & ISS (Indian Statistical Service)** — Group A scientific services, combined written + interview, Master's degree in relevant subject. Pay Level 10.
7. **Other Central Services** — CAPF AC, CDS, NDA, CMS, IFoS, CBI LDCE, CSS, ICAS, IDAS, etc. (briefly covered).
8. **Best Books & Official Links** — Comprehensive list of study materials and authoritative sources.

**Key takeaways**: UPSC offers 12+ major exams beyond CSE, covering engineering, science, defence, medical, economics, statistics, and administrative services. Most Group A posts start at Pay Level 10 (₹56,100–₹1,77,500). All exams use either 1- or 3-stage patterns with objective/descriptive/interview components. Eligibility ranges from 12th pass (Steno) to Master's degree (Geo-Scientist, IES-ISS). The official upsc.gov.in is the single source for notifications, syllabus, and applications.
