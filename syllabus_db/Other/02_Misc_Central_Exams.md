## CATEGORY A: Indian Government Departments (Direct Recruitment)

### 1. Income Tax Inspector (CBDT) — *Covered in Other file*
**Brief**: Recruited through SSC CGL. Income Tax Inspector in Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT) under Dept of Revenue, Ministry of Finance. Pay Level 7 (₹44,900–1,42,400). Exam: SSC CGL Tier-I + Tier-II + Tier-III (Descriptive).

### 2. CBIC Inspector (Customs/Excise) — *Covered*
**Brief**: Recruited through SSC CGL. Inspector of Customs/Excise/Preventive Officer in Central Board of Indirect Taxes & Customs (CBIC). Pay Level 7. Exam: SSC CGL.

### 3. EPFO SSA/Stenographer/UDC — *Covered*
**Brief**: Employees' Provident Fund Organisation. Posts: Social Security Assistant (SSA), Stenographer Grade-II, Upper Division Clerk (UDC). Recruitment through SSC. Pay Level 4–6.

### 4. ESIC Various Posts — *Covered*
**Brief**: Employees' State Insurance Corporation. Posts: UDC, Stenographer, MTS. Recruitment through SSC. Pay Level 1–4.

### 5. DSSSB Various Posts — *Covered*
**Brief**: Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board. Posts: TGT, PGT, PRT, LDC, UDC, etc. Pay Level 2–8. Exam: One-Tier CBT (200 marks).

### 6. DRDO Scientist 'B' / Technical Officer

**Conducting Body**: Recruitment & Assessment Centre (RAC), DRDO

**Posts**:
- Scientist 'B' (DRDS Cadre) — Pay Level 10 (₹56,100–1,77,500) + DA, HRA, perks
- Technical Officer 'B' — Pay Level 10
- Junior Research Fellow (JRF) — ₹37,000/month + HRA (initial 2 years, extendable)
- Research Associate (RA) — ₹54,000/month + HRA

**Eligibility**:
- **Scientist 'B'**: B.E./B.Tech (1st division, 65% aggregate or CGPA 6.5/10) in relevant discipline OR M.Sc. (65%) in relevant subjects OR M.E./M.Tech. Age: not exceeding 28 years (UR), relaxations for reserved.
- **JRF**: M.Sc./B.E./B.Tech/M.Tech (1st division) with NET/GATE qualification.

**Exam Pattern (Scientist 'B')**:
- **Tier-I**: General Aptitude Test (Objective) — 100 Questions, 100 Marks (1 hour)
  - Reasoning, English Language, General Awareness, Quantitative Aptitude
- **Tier-II**: Subject-Specific Technical — 100 Questions, 100 Marks (1 hour) — 60% weightage
- **Interview**: 50 Marks (qualifying)
- **Final Selection**: Tier-II (60%) + Interview (40%) = 100% weightage

**Detailed Syllabus (Subject-Specific)**:

**Mechanical Engineering**:
- Engineering Mechanics, Strength of Materials
- Thermodynamics, Heat Transfer
- Fluid Mechanics, Fluid Machinery
- Theory of Machines, Machine Design
- Manufacturing Processes: Casting, Forming, Welding, Machining
- Engineering Materials, Metallurgy
- IC Engines, Refrigeration & Air Conditioning
- Power Plant Engineering
- Industrial Engineering, Operations Research
- CAD/CAM, Robotics basics

**Electrical Engineering**:
- Networks: Network theorems, AC/DC circuits, Transient analysis, Two-port networks
- Machines: DC Machines, Transformers, Induction Motors, Synchronous Machines
- Power Systems: Generation, Transmission, Distribution, Switchgear, Protection
- Control Systems: Time/frequency response, Stability, Bode plot, Nyquist, PID
- Power Electronics: SCR, MOSFET, IGBT, Rectifiers, Inverters, Choppers, AC-DC converters
- Electrical & Electronic Measurements, Instrumentation
- Analog & Digital Electronics
- Signals & Systems, Electromagnetic Theory

**Electronics & Telecommunication**:
- Networks, Electronic Devices & Circuits (BJT, FET, Op-amps)
- Analog & Digital Communication, Signal Processing
- Digital Electronics, Microprocessors & Microcontrollers
- VLSI Design basics
- Electromagnetic Theory, Antennas, Waveguides
- Microwave Engineering, Radar
- Control Systems, Computer Architecture
- Digital Signal Processing, Information Theory & Coding
- Optical Fiber Communication, Satellite Communication

**Computer Science & Engineering**:
- Data Structures, Algorithms, Analysis of Algorithms
- Operating Systems: Process, Memory, File management, Deadlock, Scheduling
- Database Management Systems: ER Model, Normalization, SQL, Transactions
- Computer Networks: OSI, TCP/IP, Routing, Network Security
- Software Engineering: SDLC, Testing, Project Management, UML
- Object-Oriented Programming (OOP): C++, Java, Polymorphism, Inheritance
- Theory of Computation: Automata, Turing machines, Decidability
- Compiler Design: Lexical analysis, Parsing, Code generation
- Web Technologies: HTML, CSS, JavaScript basics
- Discrete Mathematics, Graph Theory
- Programming in C, Data structures implementation
- Artificial Intelligence basics, Machine Learning basics
- Cyber Security, Cryptography basics

**Civil Engineering**:
- Structural Engineering: Structural Analysis, RCC, Steel Design
- Geotechnical Engineering: Soil Mechanics, Foundation Engineering
- Water Resources: Fluid Mechanics, Hydrology, Irrigation
- Environmental Engineering: Water Supply, Sewage Treatment, Pollution Control
- Transportation: Highway Engineering, Traffic Engineering
- Surveying, Remote Sensing, GIS
- Construction Management, Estimation
- Concrete Technology

**Salary**: Scientist 'B' — Basic ₹56,100 + DA (~50% of basic) + HRA + Perks. Gross in-hand approximately ₹85,000–95,000/month. JRF — ₹37,000–42,000/month.

**Official Link**: https://rac.drdo.gov.in/ | https://www.drdo.gov.in/drdo/careers

## CATEGORY B: Railways, Transport, Highways

### 15. Indian Railway Traffic Service (IRTS) — *Through UPSC CSE*
**Brief**: IRTS officers manage commercial operations of Indian Railways — freight, passenger traffic, ticketing, catering, parcel, marketing. Recruited through UPSC Civil Services Examination. Pay Level 10 at entry. 2-year training at Indian Railways Institute of Transport Management (IRITM), Lucknow.

### 16. Indian Railway Personnel Service (IRPS) — *Through UPSC*
**Brief**: IRPS officers handle HR, industrial relations, welfare, training, recruitment of railway staff. Recruited through UPSC CSE. Training at National Academy of Indian Railways (NAIR), Vadodara.

### 17. IRMS (Indian Railway Medical Service) — *Through UPSC CMS/Combined Medical Services*
**Brief**: IRMS doctors serve railway hospitals. Recruitment through UPSC CMS. Pay Level 10 (Jr. scale). They handle medical care for railway employees & families.

### 50. IBBI (Insolvency Professional) Exam

**Conducting Body**: Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (IBBI) — under MCA

**Purpose**: Qualifying exam for registration as Insolvency Professional (IP) under IBC Code 2016.

**Eligibility**:
- Graduate from recognized university
- Member of ICSI, ICAI, ICMAI, Bar Council, or
- Passed Limited Insolvency Examination (LIE) — conducted by IBBI

**Exam Pattern (LIE — Limited Insolvency Examination)**:
- **Online MCQ + Descriptive (Viva)**
- **Three papers**:
  - **Paper I**: Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 (50%)
  - **Paper II**: Related Laws (Companies Act, LLP Act, IBC rules, etc.) (30%)
  - **Paper III**: Valuation, Finance, and Insolvency Process (20%)
- **Negative marking**: 25% per wrong answer (0.25 for 1 mark)
- **Passing**: 60% aggregate
- **Frequency**: Twice a year (June/December)
- **Time limit**: 1 year after clearing 1st paper to complete all 3

**Detailed Syllabus**:

**Paper I: Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 (100 Marks)**:
- **Preamble, Applicability, Definitions**
- **Insolvency Resolution Process**:
  - Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP)
  - Initiation by Corporate Debtor, Financial Creditor, Operational Creditor
  - Interim Resolution Professional (IRP), Resolution Professional (RP)
  - Committee of Creditors (CoC)
  - Information Memorandum, Expression of Interest
  - Resolution Plan, Section 12A withdrawal
  - Time limits (330 days, 90 days CIRP)
- **Liquidation Process**:
  - Initiation, Liquidator appointment
  - Liquidation Estate, Priority of Distribution (Section 53)
  - Avoidance Transactions (Section 43, 45, 49, 50)
  - Preferential, Undervalued, Fraudulent transactions
- **Fast Track Insolvency**
- **Voluntary Liquidation**
- **Pre-packaged Insolvency Resolution Process (PIRP)**
- **Personal Guarantor Insolvency**
- **Individual Insolvency**
- **Adjudicating Authority**: NCLT, NCLAT
- **Information Utilities (IUs)**
- **Insolvency Professional Agencies (IPAs)**
- **Cross Border Insolvency**
- **Recent Amendments, Rules, Regulations**

**Paper II: Related Laws and Rules/Regulations under IBBI (60 Marks)**:
- **Companies Act 2013** (key sections):
  - Incorporation, MOA/AOA, Prospectus
  - Directors, Board, Audit, AGM/EGM
  - Compromises, Arrangements, Amalgamations
  - Oppression & Mismanagement
  - Winding Up (Tribunal)
- **Partnership Act 1932, LLP Act 2008**
- **Indian Contract Act 1872** (key sections)
- **Sale of Goods Act 1930** (relevant)
- **Transfer of Property Act 1882** (relevant)
- **Indian Stamp Act 1899** (relevant)
- **Registration Act 1908**
- **Indian Evidence Act 1872** (relevant)
- **SARFAESI Act 2002**
- **RDDBFI Act 1993** (Recovery of Debts)
- **FEMA, 1999** (relevant)
- **SEBI Act, SCRA, Depositories Act** (relevant)
- **Negotiable Instruments Act 1881** (relevant)
- **Code of Civil Procedure, 1908** (jurisdiction, appeals)
- **Income Tax Act 1961** (key sections, TDS on liquidation)
- **CGST Act 2017, Customs Act 1962** (relevant)
- **Specific IBC Rules/Regulations**: CIRP Regulations, Liquidation Process Regulations, IP Regulations, IPA Regulations, IU Regulations, Pre-pack Regulations, Personal Guarantor Regulations, Valuation Regulations, Avoidance Regulations

**Paper III: Valuation, Finance, and Insolvency Process (40 Marks)**:
- **Valuation**:
  - Purpose of Valuation, Standards (ICAI, IBBI Registered Valuers)
  - Methods: Income Approach (DCF), Market Approach (Comparables), Cost Approach (Net Asset)
  - Valuation of Businesses, Tangible/Intangible Assets
  - Goodwill, Brands, Patents
- **Finance basics**:
  - Time Value of Money, NPV, IRR
  - Capital Budgeting, Cost of Capital
  - Working Capital Management
  - Financial Statement Analysis
  - Risk & Return
- **Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process in practice**:
  - CoC meetings, voting
  - Section 12A settlement
  - Resolution Plans: feasibility, viability
  - Information Memorandum preparation
- **Liquidation Mechanics**:
  - Asset identification, sale (e-auction)
  - Distribution waterfall
  - Avoidance transactions
- **Practical case studies**

**Fees**: ₹5,000/- per paper (approximately)
**Salary (After IP)**: ₹10–50 LPA. Top IPs: ₹1 Cr+ LPA. Engagement as IRP/RP/Liquidator by NCLT.

**Official Link**: https://ibbi.gov.in/examination | https://ibbi.gov.in/
