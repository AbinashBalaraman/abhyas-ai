# Nursing, Paramedical & Medical/Allied Health Science Examinations in India

> Comprehensive study guide covering central and state-level nursing, paramedical, and PG medical entrance examinations in India. Compiled from INC, AIIMS, JIPMER, PGIMER, NIMHANS, NBE, ESIC, RRB, and state commission sources.

---

## 1. AIIMS Nursing Officer Recruitment Common Eligibility Test (NORCET)

**Conducting Body:** AIIMS, New Delhi (aiimsexams.ac.in)

### Posts
- **Nursing Officer (Staff Nurse Grade II)** — Group B, Non-Gazetted
- Recruited across all AIIMS (New Delhi, Bhopal, Bhubaneswar, Jodhpur, Patna, Raipur, Rishikesh, Mangalagiri, Nagpur, Bathinda, Bibinagar, Deoghar, Gorakhpur, Guwahati, Kalyani, Raebareli, Rajkot, Bilaspur, etc.)

### Eligibility
- **Option A:** B.Sc (Hons) Nursing / B.Sc Nursing from an INC-recognized institution + registration with State/INC Nursing Council.
- **Option B:** Diploma in General Nursing and Midwifery (GNM) from an INC-recognized institution + **2 years of post-qualification clinical experience** in a minimum 50-bedded hospital + valid registration.
- **Age Limit:** 18–30 years (relaxation: SC/ST 5 yrs, OBC 3 yrs, PwD 10–15 yrs, Govt servants as per rules).
- **Citizenship:** Indian citizen.

### Exam Pattern (NORCET) – Latest
- **Mode:** Online Computer-Based Test (CBT), bilingual (English & Hindi)
- **Duration:** 3 hours (180 minutes)
- **Total Questions:** 150 MCQs
- **Total Marks:** 150
- **Marking:** +1 for correct, -1/3 for wrong, 0 for un-attempted
- **Subject Distribution:**
  - **Nursing Subject (Core):** 130 questions, 130 marks
  - **General English:** 10 questions, 10 marks
  - **General Knowledge / Aptitude / Reasoning:** 10 questions, 10 marks
- **Syllabus Weightage (Nursing):** Pre-clinical, Medical-Surgical, OBG, Paediatric, Community Health, Mental Health, and Trends in Nursing.

### Detailed Syllabus – Nursing (Core)

#### 1. Anatomy & Physiology
- Skeletal, muscular, cardiovascular, respiratory, digestive, nervous, endocrine, excretory, reproductive systems
- Blood & lymph, special senses, cell biology, tissues

#### 2. Nutrition
- Macronutrients, micronutrients, balanced diet, RDA, deficiency diseases
- Therapeutic diets, nutrition in pregnancy/lactation, pediatric nutrition, community nutrition
- National nutrition programmes (ICDS, Mid-day Meal, Vitamin A, Iron Folic Acid)

#### 3. Biochemistry
- Carbohydrates, proteins, lipids, enzymes, vitamins, minerals
- Acid-base balance, nucleic acids, hormones, organ function tests

#### 4. Nursing Foundations
- Principles of nursing, nursing process, admission/discharge
- Vital signs, hygiene, comfort measures, safety, immobility
- Health assessment, body mechanics, infection control
- Documentation, communication, patient education, bioethics
- First aid, emergency nursing, wound care, specimen collection

#### 5. Psychology
- Cognitive, affective, behavioral processes, learning, motivation, emotion
- Personality theories (Freud, Erikson, Piaget, Maslow)
- Mental health & hygiene, defense mechanisms, counseling

#### 6. Microbiology
- Classification, morphology, sterilization, disinfection
- Immunity, antigen-antibody, hypersensitivity, vaccines
- Pathogens: bacteria, viruses, fungi, parasites; lab diagnosis

#### 7. Sociology
- Society, culture, family, social stratification
- Social change, social control, urbanization, social problems
- Marriage, religion, social welfare, social institutions

#### 8. Medical-Surgical Nursing (Highest Weightage)
- General: perioperative care, fluid/electrolyte, shock, burns, pain, oncology
- Cardiovascular: CAD, MI, heart failure, hypertension, ECG
- Respiratory: TB, pneumonia, asthma, COPD, ARDS
- GIT: peptic ulcer, hepatitis, cirrhosis, appendicitis
- Renal: ARF, CRF, dialysis, nephrotic syndrome
- Endocrine: DM, thyroid, adrenal disorders
- Neuro: stroke, epilepsy, head injury, meningitis
- Musculoskeletal: fractures, arthritis, osteoporosis
- Eye, ENT, integumentary, reproductive

#### 9. Community Health Nursing (PHN)
- Public health concepts, epidemiology, demography
- Family planning, MCH, immunization, communicable & non-communicable diseases
- National health programmes (RNTCP, NVBDCP, NACP, NLEP, IDSP, NHM)
- Health education, environmental sanitation, occupational health

#### 10. Child Health Nursing (Pediatric)
- Growth & development, IMNCI, Kangaroo Mother Care
- Pediatric disorders: respiratory, GI, cardiac, renal, hematological
- Neonatal nursing, prematurity, congenital anomalies
- Pediatric emergencies, immunization schedule, breast-feeding

#### 11. Mental Health Nursing (Psychiatric)
- Mental status examination, therapeutic communication
- Schizophrenia, mood disorders, anxiety, OCD, substance abuse
- Personality disorders, eating disorders, child & adolescent psychiatry
- Therapies: CBT, ECT, psychotherapy, psychopharmacology

#### 12. OBG Nursing (Midwifery & Gynecological)
- Reproductive system, embryology, fetal development
- Normal pregnancy, ANC, labor, PNC
- High-risk pregnancy, complications
- Newborn care, breast-feeding
- Gynecological disorders, infertility, family planning methods
- MTP, safe motherhood, RCH

#### 13. Nursing Research & Statistics
- Research process, design, sampling, data collection
- Analysis, interpretation, reporting, ethics
- Statistics: mean, median, mode, SD, correlation, hypothesis testing

#### 14. Professional Trends in Nursing
- Nursing as a profession, code of ethics
- Professional organizations (INC, TNAI, ICN)
- Continuing education, in-service education
- Nursing regulation, registration, licensure
- Scope of practice, autonomy, accountability

#### 15. Administration & Ward Management
- Principles of management (planning, organizing, staffing, directing, controlling)
- Ward management, material management, budgeting
- Performance appraisal, supervision, leadership styles
- Quality assurance, accreditation (NABH)
- Health economics, health policy

### Salary & Perks
- **Pay Level 7** of 7th CPC: ₹44,900 – ₹1,42,400 + NPA (for certain posts) + DA + HRA
- Gross with allowances: approx ₹60,000–80,000/month initially
- LTC, medical, pension (NPS), and other central government benefits

## 2. AIIMS CRE (Common Recruitment Examination)

**Conducting Body:** AIIMS, New Delhi
**Purpose:** Single-window recruitment for non-faculty posts across all AIIMS institutes.

### Posts Covered
- **Group A:** Assistant Nursing Superintendent, Administrative Officer, Accounts Officer, Store Officer, Librarian, Medical Social Service Officer
- **Group B:** Nursing Officer, Assistant Accounts Officer, Office Superintendent, Stenographer, CSSD Officer
- **Group C:** Hospital Attendant, Sanitary Attendant, Lab Attendant, OT Assistant, Pharmacist, Technician (Lab/Radiology/Ophthalmic/Anaesthesia), Tailor, etc.

### Common Syllabus
- **General Intelligence & Reasoning:** Analogies, coding-decoding, number series, syllogism, blood relations, direction sense, Venn diagrams
- **General Awareness / GK:** Indian polity, history, geography, science, current affairs
- **Quantitative Aptitude:** Number system, simplification, percentage, ratio, profit-loss, time-work, averages
- **English Language:** Comprehension, grammar, vocabulary, sentence correction
- **Subject-Specific:** Based on the post applied (e.g., for Nursing Officer – nursing syllabus; for Pharmacist – pharmacy; for Technician – technical)

## 3. AIIMS Junior Resident (Non-Academic)

**Conducting Body:** AIIMS, New Delhi
**Salary:** Pay Level 10 (₹56,100 – ₹1,77,500) + NPA

### Eligibility
- **MBBS** degree (including completion of internship) from an MCI/NMC recognized university
- **Age:** 18–30 years (relaxation as per GoI norms)
- **Registration:** Valid registration with Delhi Medical Council (DMC) or NMC/MCI
- Tenure: 6 months (extendable up to 1 year)

### Selection Process
1. **Online CBT** (if the number of applicants is large): MCQs covering MBBS subjects (Pre, Para, Clinical)
2. **Interview / Departmental Assessment** (5–10 times the number of vacancies)
3. **Document Verification**

### Exam Pattern (CBT, if held)
- 100 MCQs, 100 marks, 90 minutes
- Negative marking: -1/3
- Subjects: Anatomy, Physiology, Biochemistry, Pathology, Microbiology, Pharmacology, FMT, Community Medicine, Medicine, Surgery, OBG, Pediatrics, ENT, Ophthalmology

## 5. JIPMER Nursing & Paramedical Recruitment

**Conducting Body:** JIPMER, Puducherry (jipmer.edu.in)
**Salary:** Pay Level 5–7 (₹29,200 – ₹1,42,400)

### Posts (Group B & C)
- Nursing Officer
- Pharmacist
- Lab Technician (MLT)
- X-Ray Technician / Radiographic Technician
- Junior Engineer (Civil / Electrical / Mechanical)
- Steno Grade II / III
- Lower Division Clerk (LDC)
- Operation Theatre Assistant
- Cardiac Technician, EEG/EMG Technician
- Ophthalmic Technician, Dental Mechanic
- Junior Perfusion Technician
- Transplant Coordinator, Medical Social Worker
- Dietician, Speech Therapist, Physiotherapist
- Anesthesia Technician, ICU Technician

### Eligibility
- **Nursing Officer:** B.Sc (Hons) Nursing / B.Sc Nursing / GNM with 2 yrs experience (INC recognized)
- **Pharmacist:** D.Pharm / B.Pharm + State Pharmacy Council registration
- **Lab Technician:** 10+2 (Science) + DMLT / B.Sc (MLT)
- **X-Ray Tech:** 10+2 (Science) + Diploma/B.Sc in Radiography
- **Junior Engineer:** Diploma / B.E / B.Tech in relevant branch
- **Steno:** Any degree + shorthand (80/100 wpm) + typing

### Exam Pattern
- **Mode:** Online CBT
- **Total:** 100 MCQs, 100 marks, 90 minutes
- **Sections:**
  - **Subject-related:** 70–80 questions
  - **General English:** 10 questions
  - **General Knowledge / Current Affairs:** 10 questions
  - **Reasoning / Aptitude:** 10 questions
- **Marking:** +1 correct, -0.25 (1/4) wrong
- **Normalization:** Multi-shift normalization used
- **Minimum Cutoff:** UR 50%, OBC 45%, SC/ST 40%, PwD 45%

### Detailed Syllabus (Sample: Nursing Officer)
- Anatomy, Physiology, Nutrition, Biochemistry
- Nursing Foundations, Psychology, Microbiology, Sociology
- Medical-Surgical Nursing (incl. ICU, Emergency, OT)
- Community Health Nursing, Family Welfare
- Pediatric Nursing, OBG / Midwifery
- Mental Health / Psychiatric Nursing
- Professional Trends, Administration, Education & Research
- English (grammar, comprehension, vocabulary)
- GK (Indian polity, history, geography, science, current affairs)
- Reasoning (analogies, coding, series, syllogism)

### Syllabus (Other Posts – Subject-wise)
- **Pharmacist:** Pharmaceutics, Pharmaceutical Chemistry, Pharmacognosy, Pharmacology, Toxicology, Community Pharmacy, Hospital Pharmacy, Clinical Pharmacy, Drug Laws
- **Lab Technician:** Clinical Pathology, Hematology, Biochemistry, Microbiology, Histopathology, Blood Banking, Lab Management, Quality Control
- **Radiography Tech:** Physics of X-ray/radiation, radiographic techniques, dark room, contrast studies, radiation safety, CT/MRI basics
- **JE (Civil):** Engineering Mechanics, Building Materials, RCC, Steel Design, Surveying, Soil Mechanics, Water Supply, Sanitation, Irrigation, Construction Management
- **Steno:** Shorthand, Typing, English Grammar, Office Procedures, General Awareness

## 13. NEET SS (Super Speciality) – Brief

**Conducting Body:** National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (nbe.edu.in)
**Eligibility:** MD/MS/DNB recognized qualification in relevant broad specialty
**Pattern:** 150 MCQs (60% super-specialty + 40% feeder broad specialty)
**Marking:** +4, -1
**Duration:** 2.5 hours
**Coverage:** DM (Cardiology, Neurology, Nephrology, Endocrinology, Medical Oncology, Medical Gastroenterology, Pulmonology, Hematology, etc.) and MCh (Surgical Oncology, Neurosurgery, Cardiothoracic, Urology, Pediatric Surgery, Plastic Surgery, etc.)
**For INIs (AIIMS, JIPMER, PGIMER, NIMHANS, SCTIMST):** Admission through INI-SS (separate exam by AIIMS, Delhi)

## 14. NEET PG (Postgraduate)

**Conducting Body:** National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS) (nbe.edu.in)
**Purpose:** Admission to MD, MS, PG Diploma, DNB, DrNB, NBEMS Diploma courses in Medical Educational Institutions of Central/State Govts, AFMS, ESI Institutions
**NOT** for AIIMS, PGIMER, JIPMER, NIMHANS, SCTIMST (covered by INI-CET)

### Eligibility
- **MBBS degree / provisional pass certificate** from an Indian Medical Council Act-recognized institution
- **Registration** with Indian / State Medical Council
- **Completed / completing internship** by the cut-off date
- **Foreign Medical Graduates:** Must clear FMGE (Foreign Medical Graduate Examination) + registration + internship
- **No upper age limit** (per NMC 2022)

### Exam Pattern (Latest)
- **Mode:** Online CBT
- **Duration:** 3.5 hours (210 minutes)
- **Total Questions:** 200 MCQs (single best response)
- **Total Marks:** 800
- **Marking:** +4 correct, -1 wrong, 0 unattempted
- **Language:** English only
- **Time Structure (2024 onwards):** 5 sections of 40 Qs each, 42 min per section (auto-submit)
- **Score Range:** -200 to +800
- **Validity:** 1 year (current session only)
- **Application Fee:** ₹3,500 (Gen/OBC), ₹2,500 (SC/ST/PwD)

### Detailed Syllabus (19 Subjects)
- **Pre-Clinical:** Anatomy, Physiology, Biochemistry
- **Para-Clinical:** Pathology, Pharmacology, Microbiology, Forensic Medicine & Toxicology, Preventive & Social Medicine (Community Medicine)
- **Clinical:** General Medicine, General Surgery, Obstetrics & Gynaecology, Paediatrics, Otorhinolaryngology, Ophthalmology, Dermatology, Anesthesiology, Radiology, Radiotherapy, Psychiatry, Orthopaedics

### Subject-wise Topics (Key Areas)

#### Anatomy
- General anatomy, gross anatomy of all systems, embryology, histology, neuroanatomy, genetics

#### Physiology
- General, systemic (cardiovascular, respiratory, renal, GI, endocrine, neuro, reproductive), applied physiology

#### Biochemistry
- Chemistry of biomolecules, enzymes, vitamins, minerals, metabolism (Carbs, Proteins, Lipids, Nucleic acids), molecular biology, organ function tests

#### Pathology
- General pathology (cell injury, inflammation, neoplasia), systemic pathology, hematology, clinical pathology

#### Pharmacology
- General pharmacology, ANS, CNS, CVS, Endocrine, Chemotherapy, Immunomodulators, Toxicology

#### Microbiology
- General, immunology, bacteriology, virology, mycology, parasitology, applied clinical microbiology

#### Forensic Medicine
- Legal procedures, identification, autopsy, injuries, toxicology, sexual offences, forensic psychiatry

#### Preventive & Social Medicine
- Epidemiology, biostatistics, nutrition, environmental health, occupational health, MCH, family planning, communicable/non-communicable diseases, health programs

#### General Medicine
- Cardiology, neurology, nephrology, endocrinology, gastroenterology, pulmonology, hematology, infectious diseases, dermatology (basics), psychiatry (basics)

#### General Surgery
- General surgical principles, GI surgery, urology, neurosurgery basics, orthopaedics basics, anaesthesia basics, trauma

#### OBG
- Obstetrics (normal & high-risk pregnancy, labor, PPH, eclampsia), Gynaecology (menstrual disorders, infertility, oncology), family planning

#### Pediatrics
- Neonatology, growth & development, immunization, nutrition, pediatric diseases, pediatric emergencies

#### ENT, Ophthalmology
- Basic principles, common diseases, emergencies, surgical procedures

### Counselling
- **50% All India Quota (AIQ):** Conducted by MCC (mcc.nic.in) – 15% SC, 7.5% ST, 27% OBC, 5% PwD, 10% EWS
- **50% State Quota:** Respective state counselling authorities
- **Deemed/Central Universities:** MCC
- **DNB seats:** MCC

## 15. INI-CET (Institute of National Importance Combined Entrance Test)

**Conducting Body:** AIIMS, New Delhi (aiimsexams.ac.in)
**Purpose:** PG admission (MD, MS, DM, MCh, MDS, MD Hospital Administration) at INIs:
- AIIMS (all)
- JIPMER
- PGIMER Chandigarh
- NIMHANS Bengaluru
- SCTIMST Trivandrum

### Eligibility
- **MBBS / BDS** from recognized university
- **Completion of internship** by the cut-off
- **Registration** with State/Indian Medical/Dental Council
- For DM/MCh: MD/MS in relevant feeder specialty

### Exam Pattern
- **Mode:** Online CBT
- **Duration:** 3 hours (180 minutes)
- **Total Questions:** 200 MCQs
- **Marking:** +1 correct, -1/3 wrong
- **Sections:**
  - **Basic Clinical Sciences (Anatomy, Physiology, Biochemistry, Pathology, Microbiology, Pharmacology):** ~50 Qs
  - **Para-Clinical / Community Medicine / FMT:** ~50 Qs
  - **Clinical Subjects (Medicine, Surgery, OBG, Pediatrics, ENT, Ophth, Dermatology, Psychiatry, Anesthesia, Radiology, Ortho):** ~100 Qs
- **Held:** Twice a year (January & July sessions)
- **Counselling:** Online by AIIMS — separate 100% seats of INIs

### Syllabus
- Same as MBBS curriculum (all 19 subjects as in NEET PG) with greater depth, especially in clinical and applied aspects
- Emphasis on recent advances, AIIMS-style reasoning and application-based MCQs
