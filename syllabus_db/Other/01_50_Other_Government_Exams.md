# 50+ Other Central Government Examinations — Comprehensive Reference

> This file consolidates lesser-covered but highly important Central Government recruitment examinations under categories of Tax/Finance, Audit/Accounts, CAPF/Police, Intelligence/Security, Scientific bodies, Delhi-specific boards, and miscellaneous organisations. Information compiled from UPSC, SSC, MHA, official CAPF websites, and the Central Armed Police Forces (Wikipedia) reference. Conducting bodies, eligibility, exam patterns, syllabus, and pay levels reflect 2025–26 cycles.

## CATEGORY A: TAX / FINANCE / CUSTOMS

### 1. Income Tax Inspector (CBDT)
- **Conducting Body:** SSC (CGL)
- **Posts:** Income Tax Inspector (CBDT), Inspector of Income Tax
- **Eligibility:** Bachelor's degree (any stream); Age 18–30
- **Exam Pattern:** SSC CGL Tier-I (CBT) → Tier-II (CBT) → Document Verification
- **Key Syllabus:** Quantitative Aptitude, English, Reasoning, General Awareness; Tier-II: Maths, English, Finance & Accounts (Income Tax Act, GST basics), Economics
- **Salary/Pay Level:** Pay Level-7 (₹44,900–₹1,42,400)
- **Official Link:** https://incometaxindia.gov.in/, https://www.ssc.nic.in/

### 2. CBIC Inspector (Central Excise / Customs / Service Tax)
- **Conducting Body:** SSC (CGL)
- **Posts:** Inspector (CGST/Central Excise), Inspector (Customs Preventive)
- **Eligibility:** Bachelor's degree; Age 18–30
- **Exam Pattern:** SSC CGL Tier-I + Tier-II + DV
- **Key Syllabus:** Tier-II: Maths, English, Finance & Accounts, Economics & Governance (Indirect Taxes, Customs Act, GST)
- **Salary/Pay Level:** Pay Level-7 (₹44,900–₹1,42,400)
- **Official Link:** https://www.cbic.gov.in/, https://www.ssc.nic.in/

### 3. GST Inspector (CBIC)
- **Conducting Body:** SSC (CGL)
- **Posts:** Inspector of GST
- **Eligibility:** Bachelor's degree; Age 18–30
- **Exam Pattern:** CGL Tier-I + Tier-II (Finance/Accounts + Economics) + DV
- **Key Syllabus:** CGST Act, IGST Act, GST Compensation Cess, Customs Tariff Act, Finance & Accounts
- **Salary/Pay Level:** Pay Level-7 (₹44,900–₹1,42,400)
- **Official Link:** https://www.cbic.gov.in/

### 4. Tax Assistant (Income Tax / Central Excise)
- **Conducting Body:** SSC (CHSL)
- **Posts:** Tax Assistant (CBDT & CBIC)
- **Eligibility:** 12th Pass; Age 18–27; Typing speed 30 wpm English / 25 wpm Hindi
- **Exam Pattern:** SSC CHSL Tier-I (CBT) → Tier-II (Descriptive) → Skill Test
- **Key Syllabus:** English, Maths, Reasoning, GA; Descriptive: Essay/Letter; Skill Test on computer
- **Salary/Pay Level:** Pay Level-4 (₹25,500–₹81,100)
- **Official Link:** https://www.ssc.nic.in/

### 5. Preventive Officer (CBIC)
- **Conducting Body:** SSC (CGL)
- **Posts:** Preventive Officer in Customs
- **Eligibility:** Bachelor's degree; Age 18–30
- **Exam Pattern:** SSC CGL Tier-I + Tier-II
- **Key Syllabus:** Customs Act 1962, Indian Customs Tariff, FERA/FEMA basics, Maths, English, Finance
- **Salary/Pay Level:** Pay Level-7 (₹44,900–₹1,42,400)
- **Official Link:** https://www.cbic.gov.in/

### 6. Examiner (CBIC)
- **Conducting Body:** SSC (CGL)
- **Posts:** Examiner of Customs
- **Eligibility:** Bachelor's degree; Age 18–30
- **Exam Pattern:** SSC CGL Tier-I + Tier-II
- **Key Syllabus:** Customs examination procedures, tariff classification, valuation, Tier-II common syllabus
- **Salary/Pay Level:** Pay Level-6 (₹35,400–₹1,12,400)
- **Official Link:** https://www.cbic.gov.in/

### 7. Appraiser (CBIC)
- **Conducting Body:** SSC (CGL)
- **Posts:** Appraiser (Customs/Central Excise)
- **Eligibility:** Bachelor's degree; Age 18–30
- **Exam Pattern:** SSC CGL Tier-I + Tier-II + DV
- **Key Syllabus:** Customs valuation, Anti-dumping, GST laws, International trade; Tier-II common
- **Salary/Pay Level:** Pay Level-7 (₹44,900–₹1,42,400)
- **Official Link:** https://www.cbic.gov.in/

### 8. Assistant Central Intelligence Officer (ACIO) – Intelligence Bureau
- **Conducting Body:** MHA / IB
- **Posts:** ACIO Grade-II/Executive
- **Eligibility:** Graduate + Basic Computer Skills; Age 18–27
- **Exam Pattern:** Tier-I (CBT – 100 MCQ) → Tier-II (Descriptive – Essay, English Comprehension, Précis) → Interview
- **Key Syllabus:** GA, Maths, Reasoning, English; Tier-II: Current Affairs, General Studies, English writing
- **Salary/Pay Level:** Pay Level-7 (₹44,900–₹1,42,400) + Special Security Allowance
- **Official Link:** https://www.mha.gov.in/, https://www.ssc.nic.in/

### 9. Inspector of Narcotics (NCB)
- **Conducting Body:** UPSC
- **Posts:** Inspector of Narcotics
- **Eligibility:** Degree in Chemistry/Pharmacy/Medicine; Age 18–30
- **Exam Pattern:** UPSC Recruitment Test (CBT) + Interview
- **Key Syllabus:** General Studies, Chemistry (Organic, Inorganic, Physical), Narcotic Drugs & Psychotropic Substances Act 1985
