## 1. Why Mixed Practice Matters

In real exams (SSC CGL, IBPS PO, RRB NTPC), questions don't come labelled by topic. A candidate must identify the underlying concept within 30-60 seconds and apply the correct formula or technique. Mixed practice builds:
- **Pattern recognition** — quickly identifying the topic
- **Formula recall** — under time pressure
- **Cross-topic synthesis** — many real problems use 2-3 concepts together
- **Error avoidance** — common mistakes per topic

---

## 2. Strategy for Solving Mixed Problems

### 2.1 The 5-Step Method

1. **Read the problem twice** — identify what's given and what's asked.
2. **Identify the topic** — is it Number System, Algebra, Geometry, etc.?
3. **Recall the relevant formula** — mentally list 2-3 candidate formulas.
4. **Set up the equation** — define variables and write the equation.
5. **Solve and verify** — solve algebraically, then verify the answer makes sense.

### 2.2 Time Management

For a 25-question quant paper in 60 minutes:
- Easy questions (direct formula): 30-45 seconds each
- Moderate questions: 60-90 seconds each
- Hard questions: 90-120 seconds each

Practice under timed conditions.

### 2.3 Common Traps

- **Per cent vs percentage point**
- **Profit% on cost price vs profit% on selling price**
- **Rate of interest per annum vs per month**
- **Simple vs compound interest**
- **Mean vs median vs mode**
- **Permutation vs combination** (order matters vs not)

## 3. Topic-Wise Quick Formula Recap

### 3.1 Number System
- HCF × LCM = product of two numbers
- Divisibility by 11: alternating sum of digits
- Surds: $\sqrt{a} \times \sqrt{b} = \sqrt{ab}$

### 3.2 Percentage
- Successive %: $a + b + \frac{ab}{100}$ for two-step
- "A is x% more than B" → $A = B(1 + x/100)$
- Election problems: account for non-voters and invalid votes

### 3.3 Profit/Loss/Discount
- Profit% = $\frac{SP - CP}{CP} \times 100$
- SP = CP × (1 + Profit%/100)
- Two successive discounts of a% and b% = $a + b - \frac{ab}{100}$ single discount

### 3.4 SI/CI
- SI = $\frac{PRT}{100}$
- CI = $P(1 + R/100)^T - P$
- CI - SI for 2 years = $P(R/100)^2$

### 3.5 Ratio/Proportion
- Ratio $a:b:c = ak:bk:ck$
- Mixture: weighted average

### 3.6 Time/Work
- Work rate additive: $\frac{1}{A} + \frac{1}{B} +...$
- If A can do work in $x$ days, A's one-day work = $1/x$

### 3.7 TSD
- Speed = Distance/Time
- Average speed = Total distance / Total time
- Relative speed for trains: $A + B$ (opposite), $|A - B|$ (same direction)

### 3.8 Algebra
- $(a+b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2$
- $(a-b)^2 = a^2 - 2ab + b^2$
- $a^2 - b^2 = (a+b)(a-b)$

### 3.9 Geometry
- Triangle area = $\frac{1}{2}bh$
- Circle area = $\pi r^2$; circumference = $2\pi r$
- Pythagoras: $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$ (right triangle)

### 3.10 Mensuration
- Volume of cuboid = $lwh$
- Volume of cylinder = $\pi r^2 h$
- Volume of cone = $\frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h$
- Volume of sphere = $\frac{4}{3}\pi r^3$

### 3.11 Trigonometry
- $\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1$
- $\sin 2\theta = 2\sin\theta\cos\theta$
- $\tan(A+B) = \frac{\tan A + \tan B}{1 - \tan A \tan B}$

### 3.12 Statistics
- Mean = $\frac{\sum f_i x_i}{\sum f_i}$
- Median: middle value of sorted data
- Mode: most frequent value
- Variance = $E(X^2) - [E(X)]^2$

### 3.13 Probability
- $P(E) = \frac{\text{favorable}}{\text{total}}$
- $P(A \cup B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A \cap B)$
- Bayes: $P(A|B) = \frac{P(B|A)P(A)}{P(B)}$

## 4. Practice Questions (20 PYQs — Mixed)

**Q1.** If a sum of money triples itself in 12 years at simple interest, find the rate.
(a) 10% (b) 12% (c) 16.67% (d) 25%
**Answer**: (c) 16.67%. $P + SI = 3P \Rightarrow SI = 2P$. $R = \frac{2P \times 100}{P \times 12} = 16.67\%$

**Q2.** A man buys a watch for ₹800 and sells for ₹1000. Find profit%.
(a) 20% (b) 25% (c) 30% (d) 18%
**Answer**: (b) 25%. $\frac{200}{800} \times 100 = 25\%$

**Q3.** A train 240 m long passes a pole in 24 seconds. Find the speed.
(a) 10 m/s (b) 20 m/s (c) 36 km/h (d) 40 km/h
**Answer**: (c) 36 km/h. Speed = $\frac{240}{24} = 10$ m/s = $\frac{10 \times 18}{5} = 36$ km/h

**Q4.** Two pipes fill a tank in 12 and 15 hours. A leak empties in 20 hours. If all open, time to fill?
(a) 8 hours (b) 10 hours (c) 12 hours (d) 7.5 hours
**Answer**: (b) 10 hours. Net rate = $\frac{1}{12} + \frac{1}{15} - \frac{1}{20} = \frac{5+4-3}{60} = \frac{6}{60} = \frac{1}{10}$

**Q5.** The ratio of ages of A and B is 5:7. After 6 years, it will be 3:4. Find present age of A.
(a) 30 (b) 35 (c) 25 (d) 28
**Answer**: (a) 30. $\frac{5x+6}{7x+6} = \frac{3}{4} \Rightarrow 20x+24 = 21x+18 \Rightarrow x = 6$. A = 5×6 = 30

**Q6.** Find the mean of 12, 15, 18, 21, 24.
(a) 16 (b) 18 (c) 20 (d) 22
**Answer**: (b) 18. $\frac{12+15+18+21+24}{5} = \frac{90}{5} = 18$

**Q7.** A die is rolled twice. Probability of getting sum 9?
(a) 1/9 (b) 1/12 (c) 4/36 (d) 1/8
**Answer**: (c) 4/36. Pairs: (3,6), (4,5), (5,4), (6,3) = 4 outcomes. $\frac{4}{36} = \frac{1}{9}$. So (a) 1/9. **Answer**: (a) 1/9

**Q8.** The compound interest on ₹1000 for 2 years at 10% per annum is:
(a) ₹200 (b) ₹210 (c) ₹100 (d) ₹220
**Answer**: (b) ₹210. $1000(1.1)^2 - 1000 = 1210 - 1000 = 210$

**Q9.** If $\sin\theta = 4/5$ (acute angle), find $\cos 2\theta$.
(a) 7/25 (b) -7/25 (c) 24/25 (d) -24/25
**Answer**: (a) 7/25. $\cos\theta = 3/5$. $\cos 2\theta = 1 - 2(16/25) = 1 - 32/25 = -7/25$. Hmm. Recompute: $\cos 2\theta = 2\cos^2\theta - 1 = 2(9/25) - 1 = 18/25 - 25/25 = -7/25$. So **-7/25**, option (b).

**Q10.** A rectangle has length 12 cm and width 5 cm. Find the length of the diagonal.
(a) 13 cm (b) 15 cm (c) 17 cm (d) 14 cm
**Answer**: (a) 13 cm. $\sqrt{144 + 25} = 13$

**Q11.** The HCF of 24, 36, 60 is:
(a) 6 (b) 12 (c) 24 (d) 4
**Answer**: (b) 12. $\text{HCF}(24,36) = 12$. $\text{HCF}(12, 60) = 12$

**Q12.** If $x + y = 10$ and $xy = 21$, find $x^2 + y^2$.
(a) 58 (b) 100 (c) 79 (d) 100
**Answer**: (a) 58. $(x+y)^2 - 2xy = 100 - 42 = 58$

**Q13.** 30% of a number is 90. Find the number.
(a) 200 (b) 250 (c) 300 (d) 350
**Answer**: (c) 300. $0.3x = 90 \Rightarrow x = 300$

**Q14.** The volume of a sphere with radius 7 cm is:
(a) $\frac{4}{3}\pi \times 343$ (b) $\frac{4}{3}\pi \times 49$ (c) $4\pi \times 49$ (d) None
**Answer**: (a) $\frac{4}{3}\pi(7)^3 = \frac{4}{3}\pi \times 343$

**Q15.** A sum of ₹10000 invested at 8% SI for 3 years yields interest:
(a) ₹2400 (b) ₹2000 (c) ₹3000 (d) ₹2500
**Answer**: (a) ₹2400. $SI = \frac{10000 \times 8 \times 3}{100} = 2400$

**Q16.** A boat takes 4 hours to go 30 km downstream and 6 hours to return. Find the speed of the boat in still water.
(a) 6.25 km/h (b) 7.5 km/h (c) 5 km/h (d) 8 km/h
**Answer**: (a) 6.25 km/h. Downstream speed = 30/4 = 7.5. Upstream speed = 30/6 = 5. Boat speed = (7.5+5)/2 = 6.25 km/h

**Q17.** If $\log_{10} 2 = 0.3010$, find $\log_{10} 8$.
(a) 0.6020 (b) 0.9030 (c) 0.3010 (d) 0.7526
**Answer**: (b) 0.9030. $\log 8 = 3 \log 2 = 3 \times 0.3010 = 0.9030$

**Q18.** The angle of elevation of the sun when the shadow of a pole is $\sqrt{3}$ times its height is:
(a) 30° (b) 45° (c) 60° (d) 75°
**Answer**: (a) 30°. $\tan\theta = h/(\sqrt{3}h) = 1/\sqrt{3} \Rightarrow \theta = 30°$

**Q19.** A bag has 5 red, 4 blue balls. Two balls drawn at random. Probability both are red?
(a) 1/3 (b) 5/18 (c) 5/9 (d) 1/9
**Answer**: (b) 5/18. $\frac{{}^5C_2}{{}^9C_2} = \frac{10}{36} = \frac{5}{18}$

**Q20.** A man can complete a work in 10 days, another in 12 days. If they work together, time to complete?
(a) 5 days (b) 6 days (c) 60/11 days (d) 11 days
**Answer**: (c) 60/11 days. $\frac{1}{10} + \frac{1}{12} = \frac{6+5}{60} = \frac{11}{60}$. Time = 60/11 days

## 6. Common Mistake Patterns

### 6.1 Number System
- Confusing LCM with HCF
- Wrong application of divisibility rule of 7

### 6.2 Percentage
- Mixing % change with % point change
- Forgetting the +ab/100 term in successive %

### 6.3 SI/CI
- Using SI formula for CI problem
- Confusing annual rate with monthly rate

### 6.4 Geometry
- Wrongly assuming angles without proof
- Forgetting to convert units (cm² to m²)

### 6.5 Trigonometry
- Sign errors in different quadrants
- Wrong domain of inverse functions
