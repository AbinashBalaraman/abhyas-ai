# Chapter 7: Insurance & Pension

> **Chapter Overview:** Insurance and Pension form an **important area of banking awareness** in IBPS PO/Clerk/SO, Insurance exams (LIC, NIACL, UIIC, NICL), SSC CGL, and RRB Officer Scale-I/II. Typical question load: 3-5 questions per exam. This chapter covers IRDAI, types of insurance, LIC and general insurers, PFRDA, NPS, APY, and UPS.

## 1. IRDAI — Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India

### 1.1 Establishment & Composition

- **Established:** 1999
- **Constituted by:** IRDA Act, 1999
- **Headquarters:** Sy No. 115/1, Financial District, Nanakramguda, **Hyderabad – 500032, Telangana**
- **Moved from Delhi to Hyderabad:** 2001
- **Composition:** 10 members — Chairperson, 5 full-time + 4 part-time members
- **Current IRDAI Chairperson:** T. L. Alamelu (as of June 2026) [⚠️ VERIFY — IRDAI chairs change frequently; confirm at irdai.gov.in]
- **Current PFRDA Chairperson:** **Ajay Seth, IAS** (appointed August 2025)
- **FDI Limit in Insurance:** **74%** (as per 2021 Union Budget)

### 1.2 Functions (Section 14 of IRDA Act 1999)

- Issue/renew/modify/withdraw registrations
- Protect policyholder interests
- Specify qualifications/code of conduct for intermediaries, agents
- Regulate rates and terms
- Inspect insurers and intermediaries
- Adjudicate disputes
- Specify investments, solvency margin, etc.

## 2. Types of Insurance

### 2.1 Life Insurance

- **LIC (Life Insurance Corporation of India):** Established 1956; Nationalised 19 January 1956
- LIC absorbed 154 Indian + 16 non-Indian insurers + 75 provident societies
- LIC had monopoly until late 1990s
- Currently **24 life insurance companies** operating

Major private players: HDFC Life, ICICI Prudential, SBI Life, Max Life, Bajaj Allianz, Tata AIA

### 2.2 General Insurance (Non-Life)

General insurance covers:
- **Property insurance** (fire, theft, flood)
- **Motor insurance** (third-party, comprehensive)
- **Health insurance**
- **Travel insurance**
- **Marine insurance**

**4 PSU General Insurance Companies:**
1. National Insurance Company
2. New India Assurance Company
3. Oriental Insurance Company
4. United India Insurance Company

- **GIC Re (General Insurance Corporation of India):** National Re-insurer; incorporated 1971, effective 1 January 1973
- 28 general insurance companies (including ECGC and AIC)
- New India Assurance, Oriental Insurance, National Insurance: Nationalised 1972
- New reform 2024: Three of four PSU general insurers (Oriental, National, United India) to be merged into New India Assurance [⚠️ VERIFY status of merger as of June 2026]

### 2.3 Health Insurance

**PMJAY (Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana) — Ayushman Bharat:**
- Launched: 23 September 2018
- **Cover:** ₹5 lakh per family per year
- **Beneficiaries:** 50+ crore (bottom 40% of population as per SECC database)
- **PMJAY Vay Vandana Card:** For senior citizens 70+ years (announced Sep 2024)

Other health schemes:
- **RSBY (Rashtriya Swasthya Bima Yojana):** Predecessor; launched 2008
- **ECHS** (Ex-Servicemen Contributory Health Scheme)
- **CGHS** (Central Government Health Scheme)

IRDAI removed age limit (65 years) for purchasing health insurance from 1 April 2024

- Density: ~$92 per capita [⚠️ VERIFY]

## 3. Important Insurance Terms

- **Premium:** Amount paid by policyholder to insurer
- **Sum Assured:** Amount paid out on claim
- **Beneficiary:** Person receiving the claim amount
- **Nominee:** Person designated to receive policy proceeds
- **Rider:** Additional benefit attached to a base policy
- **Maturity:** Date when policy term ends
- **Surrender Value:** Amount received on early termination
- **Free-look Period:** 15 days to review and cancel
- **Proposer:** Person who buys the policy
- **Endowment Policy:** Combines insurance + savings
- **ULIP (Unit Linked Insurance Plan):** Insurance + investment in market
- **Term Insurance:** Pure insurance; no maturity benefit
- **Whole Life Insurance:** Covers entire life
- **Annuity:** Regular payment for a defined period

## 4. PFRDA — Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority

### 4.1 Establishment

- **Interim PFRDA:** 23 August 2003
- **PFRDA Act enacted:** 19 September 2013
- **Act enforced:** 1 February 2014
- **Replaces:** 1999 "OASIS" project (Old Age Social & Income Security)
- **Headquarters:** New Delhi

### 4.2 Functions

- Regulate NPS, APY, and other pension schemes
- Register and regulate pension funds
- Protect subscriber interests
- Promote development of pension sector

## 5. NPS — National Pension System

### 5.1 Key Facts

- **Formally started:** 1 January 2004
- **Notified:** 22 December 2003
- **Type:** Defined-contribution pension scheme
- **Open to all citizens:** Since **1 May 2009**
- **Predecessor:** Old Pension Scheme (OPS) — defined benefit

### 5.2 Subscriber Statistics

- As of 30 April 2023: **6,35,43,628 subscribers** (~6.35 crore)
- AUM (April 2023): ₹8,98,954 crore (US$94 billion)
- As of late 2025/early 2026:
  - NPS Total Subscribers: **~7.7 crore** (government + private sector combined)
  - NPS AUM: **₹16,77,514 crore** (~₹16.78 lakh crore)
- **NPS Lite / lite subscribers:** ~2.23 crore (smaller subset; this was the source of the original confusion in the QA audit)
- **APY Subscribers:** ~7.6 crore active (separate scheme) [⚠️ VERIFY — numbers change monthly; use PFRDA's official dashboard for exam-prep]​

### 5.3 NPS Tiers

- **Tier I:** Basic pension account; locked till 60
- **Tier II:** Voluntary savings; withdraw anytime
- **NPS Vatsalya:** For minors (launched 2024)

### 5.4 Investment Options

- **Active Choice:** Subscriber chooses asset class
  - Equity (E): Up to 75% (max 50% in some cases)
  - Corporate Debt (C)
  - Government Securities (G)
  - Alternative Investment Funds (A)
- **Auto Choice (Life Cycle Fund):** Default; based on age

## 6. APY — Atal Pension Yojana

### 6.1 Key Facts

- **Original launch (as Swavalamban):** 2010-11
- **Relaunched as APY:** **9 May 2015** by PM Modi in Kolkata
- **Named after:** Former PM Atal Bihari Vajpayee
- **Target:** Unorganized sector
- **Eligibility:** Age 18-40 years
- **Pension range:** ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 per month (at age 60)
- **Government co-contribution:** 50% of total contribution or ₹1,000 per annum, whichever is lower, for 5 years (for subscribers enrolled 1 June 2015 - 31 March 2016)

### 6.2 APY Statistics

- Subscribers (as of 2023): 5.20+ crore
- Subscribers (April 2026): 7.64 crore active
- Crossed 9 crore gross enrolments: 22 April 2026 milestone

## 7. UPS — Unified Pension Scheme

- **Announced:** 24 August 2024 by PM Modi
- **For:** Central Government employees
- **Combines features of Old Pension Scheme (defined benefit) and NPS**
- **Effective date:** 1 April 2025 [⚠️ VERIFY]
- **Guarantee:** 50% of average basic pay of last 12 months as pension
- **Assured minimum pension:** ₹10,000 per month (for those with min 10 years service)
- **Inflation indexation (Dearness Relief)** like OPS

---

## 8. Old Pension Scheme (OPS) vs NPS vs UPS

| Feature | OPS | NPS | UPS |
|---------|-----|-----|-----|
| Type | Defined benefit | Defined contribution | Hybrid |
| Contribution | None from employee | 10% (employee) + 14% (employer) | 10% + 14% |
| Pension amount | 50% of last basic pay + DA | Variable (market-linked) | 50% of avg basic pay |
| Inflation | DA applicable | Not guaranteed | DR applicable |
| Risk | Govt bears | Subscriber bears | Govt bears |
