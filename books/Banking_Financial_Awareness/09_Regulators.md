# Chapter 9: Indian Financial Regulators

> **Chapter Overview:** This chapter consolidates **all major Indian financial regulators** — RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, PFRDA, IFSCA, and other financial regulatory bodies. These questions appear in 2-3 questions per banking/SSC exam. Topics include regulator mandates, structure, key officials, and recent updates.

## 1. Overview of Indian Financial Regulators

India has a **multi-regulator financial system** with separate regulators for banking, securities, insurance, and pensions. This structure was developed over decades to provide specialized oversight for each sector.

| Regulator | Function | HQ | Established |
|-----------|----------|-----|------------|
| **RBI** | Monetary authority, banking regulation, currency | Mumbai | 1935 |
| **SEBI** | Securities market, mutual funds | Mumbai | 1988/1992 |
| **IRDAI** | Insurance | Hyderabad | 1999 |
| **PFRDA** | Pension | New Delhi | 2003 (interim) / 2014 (statutory) |
| **IFSCA** | International Financial Services Centre | GIFT City, Gandhinagar | 2020 |
| **FMC** (merged with SEBI 2015) | Commodity derivatives (now SEBI) | Mumbai | 1953 (as Forward Markets Commission) |

## 2. Reserve Bank of India (RBI)

### 2.1 Mandate

- **Monetary authority** — formulates and implements monetary policy
- **Issuer of currency** — issues and manages currency notes
- **Banker to the government** — manages government accounts, handles public debt
- **Banker's bank** — lender of last resort
- **Regulator of banking system** — supervises banks, UCBs, NBFCs
- **Manager of foreign exchange** — manages forex reserves
- **Regulator/supervisor of payment and settlement systems**

### 2.2 Key Officials (as of June 2026)

- **Governor:** Sanjay Malhotra, IAS
- **Deputy Governors:** Swaminathan Janakiraman, Poonam Gupta, Shirish Chandra Murmu, Rohit Jain

### 2.3 Structure

- 1 Governor + 4 Deputy Governors
- 21-member Central Board of Directors
- 4 regional boards (Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, New Delhi)
- 27 zonal offices (commonly cited)

## 3. Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)

### 3.1 Establishment

- **Non-statutory body:** Constituted 12 April 1988 (Government resolution)
- **Statutory body:** SEBI Act 1992; provisions came into force **30 January 1992**
- **Headquarters:** Mumbai

### 3.2 Mandate

- Protect interests of investors in securities
- Promote development of securities market
- Regulate stock exchanges, brokers, sub-brokers, merchant bankers, mutual funds, etc.

### 3.3 Key Functions

- Registration and regulation of stock brokers, sub-brokers
- Regulation of stock exchanges
- Regulation of merchant bankers, mutual funds
- Insider trading regulations (PIT Regulations, 2015)
- Takeover Code
- Prohibition of Fraudulent and Unfair Trade Practices

### 3.4 Major SEBI Regulations

- **SEBI (ICDR) Regulations, 2018** — Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements
- **SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015** — Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements
- **SEBI (PIT) Regulations, 2015** — Prohibition of Insider Trading
- **SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011** — Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers

## 4. Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI)

### 4.1 Establishment

- **Established:** 1999
- **Constituted by:** IRDA Act, 1999
- **Headquarters:** Hyderabad, Telangana
- **Current Chairperson:** Ajay Seth, IAS (as of July 2025)

### 4.2 Mandate

- Issue/renew/modify/withdraw registrations of insurers and intermediaries
- Protect policyholder interests
- Specify qualifications/code of conduct for intermediaries, agents
- Regulate rates and terms of insurance
- Inspect insurers and intermediaries
- Adjudicate disputes
- Specify investments, solvency margin, etc.

### 4.3 Key Functions

- **Registration of insurers** (life and general)
- **Solvency margin regulation** (minimum 1.5 for life, 1.5 for general)
- **Product approval** (file and use)
- **Investment regulations** (pattern of investment)
- **Third-party administrator (TPA)** regulations

## 5. Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA)

### 5.1 Establishment

- **Interim PFRDA:** 23 August 2003
- **PFRDA Act enacted:** 19 September 2013
- **Act enforced:** 1 February 2014
- **Headquarters:** New Delhi

### 5.2 Mandate

- Regulate NPS, APY, and other pension schemes
- Register and regulate pension funds (PFM, POP, CRA)
- Protect subscriber interests
- Promote development of pension sector

### 5.3 Pension Fund Managers (PFMs)

- SBI Pension Fund
- UTI Retirement Solutions
- LIC Pension Fund
- ICICI Prudential Pension Fund
- Kotak Mahindra Pension Fund
- HDFC Pension Fund
- Aditya Birla Sun Life Pension Fund
- TATA Pension Fund

## 6. International Financial Services Centres Authority (IFSCA)

### 6.1 Establishment

- **Established:** 27 April 2020
- **Headquarters:** GIFT City, Gandhinagar, Gujarat
- **Under:** International Financial Services Centres Authority Act, 2019

### 6.2 Mandate

- Single unified regulator for IFSC (International Financial Services Centre) at GIFT City
- Regulates financial products, services, and institutions in IFSC
- Replaces multiple regulators (RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, PFRDA) in IFSC

### 6.3 Functions

- Regulate banking, capital markets, insurance, pension in IFSC
- Promote financial services exports
- Set up unified regulator for IFSC

## 7. Other Important Regulatory/Advisory Bodies

### 7.1 FSDC — Financial Stability and Development Council

- **Chairperson:** Union Finance Minister
- **Members:** Heads of all financial regulators (RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, PFRDA, IFSCA) + senior officials
- **Purpose:** Coordinate financial sector regulation, macroprudential oversight

### 7.2 FSDC Sub-Committee

- Chaired by: RBI Governor
- Members: SEBI, IRDAI, PFRDA heads
- Focus: Financial stability, inter-regulatory coordination

### 7.3 FMC — Forward Markets Commission (Merged with SEBI 2015)

- Originally constituted under Forward Contracts (Regulation) Act, 1952
- Regulated commodity derivatives
- Merged with SEBI on 28 September 2015

### 7.4 CCI — Competition Commission of India

- Established: 2003
- Body: Statutory
- Purpose: Anti-trust, competition regulation
- HQ: New Delhi

### 7.5 IBBI — Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India

- Established: 2016 (under IBC 2016)
- HQ: New Delhi
- Regulates Insolvency Professionals, Information Utilities, ARCs
- Writes rules for CIRP, liquidation

### 7.6 NABARD — National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development

- Established: 12 July 1982
- HQ: Mumbai
- Apex institution for agriculture and rural development finance

### 7.7 SIDBI — Small Industries Development Bank of India

- Established: 2 April 1990
- HQ: Lucknow
- Principal financial institution for MSME promotion, financing, development

### 7.8 NHB — National Housing Bank

- Established: 1988
- HQ: Delhi
- Regulator for housing finance companies
- Now under RBI (merged)

### 7.9 FIMMDA — Fixed Income Money Market and Derivatives Association of India

- Industry body for bond, money, derivatives markets
- Publishes FBIL benchmarks

### 7.10 FBIL — Financial Benchmarks India Pvt Ltd

- Established: 2015
- Manages MIBOR, MIFOR, benchmark rates

### 7.11 IBEF — India Brand Equity Foundation

- Trust established by CII
- Promotes Indian brands globally

## 8. National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI)

- **Established:** 2008
- **HQ:** Mumbai
- **Operates:** UPI, IMPS, AePS, RuPay, NACH, CTS
- Promoted by: 10 banks (10% each)
