# Chapter 6: Loans, Priority Sector Lending & Non-Performing Assets (NPA)

> **Chapter Overview:** This chapter covers the critical banking topics of **Loans, Priority Sector Lending (PSL), and Non-Performing Assets (NPA)** — the most-tested areas in IBPS PO/Clerk Mains, SSC CGL, and RRB Scale-II Officer exams. Typical question load: 2-4 questions per banking exam paper. Topics include types of loans, PSL categories and targets, NPA classification, SMA norms, provisioning, and recovery mechanisms (SARFAESI, IBC).

## 1. Types of Loans

### 1.1 Retail Loans

| Loan Type | Security | Tenure | Interest Rate | Tax Benefit |
|-----------|----------|--------|---------------|-------------|
| **Personal Loan** | Unsecured | 1-5 years | 10-24% p.a. | None |
| **Home Loan** | Property | up to 30 years | 8.5-11% p.a. | Sec 80C (principal), Sec 24(b) (interest up to ₹2 lakh) |
| **Car Loan** | Vehicle | up to 7-8 years | 8-12% p.a. | None |
| **Education Loan** | Co-obligation | up to 15 years | 8-15% p.a. | Sec 80E (interest, no upper limit) |
| **Gold Loan** | Gold ornaments | 6 months-3 years | 7-14% p.a. | None |
| **Loan Against Property (LAP)** | Property | 5-15 years | 9-13% p.a. | None |

### 1.2 Business Loans

- **Working Capital Loan** — for day-to-day operations
- **Term Loan** — fixed amount for fixed tenure
- **Cash Credit** — flexible limit; draw and repay
- **Overdraft** — withdraw beyond balance up to limit
- **Demand Loan** — repayable on demand
- **Bill Discounting** — discounting of bills receivable

### 1.3 PMMY — Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana

- **Launched:** 8 April 2015 by PM Narendra Modi
- **Administrator:** MUDRA Ltd (Micro Units Development & Refinance Agency)
- **Three categories:**

| Category | Loan Amount |
|---|---|
| **Shishu** | Up to ₹50,000 |
| **Kishore** | ₹50,001 to ₹5 lakh |
| **Tarun** | ₹5,00,001 to ₹10 lakh |

Extended to ₹20 lakh under Tarun Plus in some categories

### 1.4 KCC — Kisan Credit Card

- **Launched:** 1998 (based on R. V. Gupta Committee recommendations)
- Provides short-term credit to farmers
- Credit limit: Based on land holding, cropping pattern
- **Interest:** 7% (with 3% subsidy for timely repayment → effective 4%)

## 2. Priority Sector Lending (PSL) — RBI Norms

### 2.1 Total PSL Target

- **Total Priority Sector target: 40% of ANBC** (Adjusted Net Bank Credit) or CEOB (Credit Equivalent of Off-Balance Sheet Exposure), whichever higher
- **Achievement by all scheduled commercial banks:** 40% of ANBC mandatory
- **Foreign banks with 20+ branches:** 40% of ANBC
- **Foreign banks with <20 branches:** 32% (currently transitioning to 40%)

### 2.2 PSL Categories

| Category | Target | Details |
|----------|--------|---------|
| **Agriculture** | 18% of ANBC | Farm credit, agri infrastructure, ancillary activities |
| **Micro Enterprises (MSME)** | 7.5% of ANBC | Small enterprises |
| **Export Credit** | 2% of ANBC | (up to 32% of ANBC inclusive) |
| **Education** | Part of Social Infrastructure | |
| **Housing** | Loans up to ₹35 lakh (metros), ₹25 lakh (others) | |
| **Social Infrastructure** | Renewable Energy, Health, etc. | |

### 2.3 Weaker Sections Priority

- **10% of ANBC** to be lent to Weaker Sections
- **Includes:** SC/ST, Women, Minorities, Disabled persons
- **Plus:** Small & Marginal Farmers, Artisans

### 2.4 MSME Lending Rule

All PSU Banks must lend **minimum 7.5% of total credit to MSMEs** (as per RBI norms)

## 3. Non-Performing Assets (NPA) — Definition and Classification

### 3.1 Definition

An asset (loan/advance) becomes NPA when:
- **Interest and/or installment of principal remains overdue for more than 90 days**
- For Agriculture loans: Overdue for 2 cropping seasons (short duration) or 1 cropping season (long duration)

### 3.2 Categories of NPAs

| Category | Definition |
|----------|------------|
| **Standard Asset** | Performing; not NPA |
| **Sub-Standard Asset** | NPA for up to 12 months |
| **Doubtful Asset** | NPA for more than 12 months |
| **Doubtful Category 1 (D1)** | Up to 1 year |
| **Doubtful Category 2 (D2)** | 1-3 years |
| **Doubtful Category 3 (D3)** | More than 3 years |
| **Loss Asset** | Uncollectible; identified by RBI/auditor |

### 3.3 Standard Asset Classification with SMA Norms (Effective 2022)

- **SMA-0 (Special Mention Account - 0):** Principal or interest overdue **1-30 days**
- **SMA-1:** Overdue **31-60 days**
- **SMA-2:** Overdue **61-90 days**
- **NPA:** Overdue beyond **90 days**

### 3.4 Provisioning Norms (RBI)

| Category | Provision Required |
|---|---|
| Standard Assets (sub-standard secured) | 0.25%–1% |
| Sub-Standard | 15% (secured) / 25% (unsecured) |
| Doubtful D1 | 25% |
| Doubtful D2 | 40% |
| Doubtful D3 | 100% |
| Loss Assets | 100% |

## 4. Recovery Mechanisms

### 4.1 SARFAESI Act, 2002

**Securitisation and Reconstruction of Financial Assets and Enforcement of Security Interest Act, 2002**

Allows banks/financial institutions to recover NPAs by:
1. **Securitisation**
2. **Asset reconstruction** (via ARCs - Asset Reconstruction Companies)
3. **Enforcement of security interest** without court intervention

### 4.2 IBC 2016 — Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code

- **Enacted:** 2016; came into force: **1 December 2016**
- **Key institutions:**
  - **NCLT** (National Company Law Tribunal) — Adjudicating authority
  - **NCLAT** (National Company Law Appellate Tribunal) — Appellate authority
  - **IBBI** (Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India) — Regulator
- **CIRP (Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process):**
  1. Default (₹1 crore threshold)
  2. Application to NCLT by financial creditor, operational creditor, or corporate debtor
  3. **Interim Resolution Professional (IRP)** appointed by NCLT
  4. Committee of Creditors (CoC) constituted
  5. **Resolution Plan** within 180 days (90-day extension possible, max 330 days)
  6. If no resolution → **Liquidation**

### 4.3 Other Recovery Channels

- **DRT (Debt Recovery Tribunal)** — under RDDBFI Act, 1993
- **Lok Adalats** — alternative dispute resolution
- **One-time settlements (OTS)** — negotiated settlement

## 6. CRR and SLR

### 6.1 CRR (Cash Reserve Ratio)

- % of Net Demand & Time Liabilities (NDTL) banks must maintain with RBI as **cash**
- Currently ~4-4.5% (varies with policy)
- No interest paid on CRR balances (until 2007; partial interest from 2007)
- Maintained daily

### 6.2 SLR (Statutory Liquidity Ratio)

- % of demand & time liabilities to be maintained in:
  - Government securities
  - Cash
  - Gold
- Currently ~18%
- Maintained daily on a fortnightly average basis

## 7. Practice Questions (20 PYQs)

**Q1.** The Priority Sector Lending target is ____ of ANBC.
(a) 30% (b) 40% (c) 50% (d) 60%
**Answer**: (b) 40%

**Q2.** An asset becomes NPA when overdue for more than:
(a) 30 days (b) 60 days (c) 90 days (d) 180 days
**Answer**: (c) 90 days

**Q3.** SMA-1 category applies when overdue for:
(a) 1-30 days (b) 31-60 days (c) 61-90 days (d) 91-180 days
**Answer**: (b) 31-60 days

**Q4.** Provisioning for Doubtful D2 assets is:
(a) 25% (b) 40% (c) 75% (d) 100%
**Answer**: (b) 40%

**Q5.** SARFAESI Act was passed in:
(a) 1999 (b) 2000 (c) 2002 (d) 2004
**Answer**: (c) 2002

**Q6.** IBC was enacted in:
(a) 2014 (b) 2016 (c) 2018 (d) 2020
**Answer**: (b) 2016

**Q7.** NCLT stands for:
(a) National Company Law Tribunal (b) National Civil Law Tribunal (c) National Company Legal Tribunal (d) New Company Law Tribunal
**Answer**: (a) National Company Law Tribunal

(a) 2014 (b) 2015 (c) 2016 (d) 2018

**Q18.** IBC came into force on:

**Q19.** Maximum time for CIRP under IBC is:

**Q20.** MSME lending target is:
