# Chapter 10: Government Schemes & Financial Inclusion

> **Chapter Overview:** Government schemes and financial inclusion programs form a **major component of SSC CGL, RRB, and Banking exams** — typically 3-5 questions per paper. This chapter covers the key financial inclusion and welfare schemes: PMJDY, PMMY, PMSBY, PMJJBY, APY, SSY, Stand-Up India, PM SVANidhi, ECLGS, and Aatmanirbhar Bharat.
>
> **Primary sources:** Banking_Awareness_web_extracted (lines 894-971), Wikipedia schemes, official portals.

## 1. PMJDY — Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana

### 1.1 Key Facts

- **Launched:** 28 August 2014 by PM Narendra Modi (announced on Independence Day, 15 August 2014)
- **Slogan:** "Mera Khata, Bhagya Vidhata" (My account, fortune maker)
- **Run by:** Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance
- **JAM Trinity:** Jan Dhan + Aadhaar + Mobile

### 1.2 Records

- **15 million bank accounts opened on inauguration day** (Guinness World Record, 20 January 2015)
- "The most bank accounts opened in one week as part of financial inclusion campaign: 18,096,130" (August 23-29, 2014) — Guinness

### 1.3 Statistics

- **As of August 2023:** 50.09 crore accounts; ₹2,03,505 crore deposits; avg ₹4,063 per account
- **As per World Bank Spring 2025:** PMJDY expanded financial inclusion by facilitating opening of over **550 million bank accounts**; supported DBT saving ₹3.48 lakh crore (USD 4.19 billion) in leakages as of March 2023

### 1.4 Features

- Zero balance account (Basic Savings Bank Deposit Account - BSBDA)
- RuPay debit card
- **Accident insurance:** Up to **₹2 lakh** (originally ₹1 lakh, increased)
- **Life insurance cover:** **₹30,000** for accounts opened between 15 Aug 2014 – 31 Jan 2015
- **Overdraft facility:** Up to **₹10,000** (for one account per household, preferably female)

## 2. PMMY — Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana

### 2.1 Key Facts

- **Launched:** 8 April 2015 by PM Narendra Modi
- **Administrator:** MUDRA Ltd (Micro Units Development & Refinance Agency)
- **Target:** Non-corporate, non-farm small/micro enterprises

### 2.2 Three Categories

| Category | Loan Amount |
|---|---|
| **Shishu** | Up to ₹50,000 |
| **Kishore** | ₹50,001 to ₹5 lakh |
| **Tarun** | ₹5,00,001 to ₹10 lakh |

Extended to ₹20 lakh under Tarun Plus in some categories [⚠️ VERIFY latest]

### 2.3 Features

- Collateral-free loans
- Available from banks, NBFCs, MFIs
- Refinance via MUDRA Ltd

## 3. PMSBY — Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana

### 3.1 Key Facts

- **Launched:** 9 May 2015
- **Premium:** **₹20 per year** (revised to ₹20 from 1 June 2022 from ₹12 originally)
- **Coverage:**
  - **Accidental death and full disability: ₹2 lakh**
  - **Partial disability: ₹1 lakh**
- **Available to:** Savings bank account holders aged 18-70 years
- **Premium auto-debited from bank account**

---

## 4. PMJJBY — Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana

### 4.1 Key Facts

- **Launched:** 9 May 2015
- **Premium:** **₹436 per year** (revised; original ₹330) [⚠️ VERIFY latest]
- **Coverage:** **₹2 lakh** in case of death (from any cause)
- **Available to:** Savings bank account holders aged 18-50 years (life cover up to 55 years)
- **Renewable annually**

---

## 5. APY — Atal Pension Yojana

### 5.1 Key Facts (Already covered in Chapter 7)

- **Relaunched as APY:** 9 May 2015 by PM Modi in Kolkata
- **Target:** Unorganized sector
- **Eligibility:** Age 18-40 years
- **Pension range:** ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 per month (at age 60)
- **Subscribers (April 2026):** 7.64 crore active

---

## 6. Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)

### 6.1 Key Facts

- **Launched:** 22 January 2015 (Beti Bachao, Beti Padhao)
- **Part of:** Beti Bachao, Beti Padhao
- **For:** Girl child below 10 years
- **Account opened by:** Parent/guardian
- **Tenure:** 21 years from opening
- **Current interest rate:** 8.2% per annum (FY25-26) [⚠️ VERIFY]
- **Maximum deposit:** ₹1.5 lakh per year
- **Tax benefits:** Sec 80C exemption on deposits; interest tax-free

---

## 7. Stand-Up India

### 7.1 Key Facts

- **Launched:** 5 April 2016
- **For:** SC/ST and Women entrepreneurs
- **Loan range:** ₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore
- **For:** Greenfield enterprises (first-time venture)
- **Target:** At least one SC/ST and one Woman per bank branch

---

## 8. PM SVANidhi — PM Street Vendor's AtmaNirbhar Nidhi

### 8.1 Key Facts

- **Launched:** 1 June 2020
- **For:** Street vendors (urban)
- **Loan amounts:** ₹10,000 → ₹20,000 → ₹50,000 (3 tranches)
- **Interest subsidy:** 7% on timely repayment
- **No collateral**

---

## 9. ECLGS — Emergency Credit Line Guarantee Scheme

### 9.1 Key Facts

- **Launched:** 24 March 2020 (during COVID-19)
- **By:** NCGTC (National Credit Guarantee Trustee Company)
- **For:** MSMEs, MUDRA borrowers, businesses
- **100% credit guarantee** to banks
- **Original amount:** ₹3 lakh crore (subsequently expanded to ₹4.5 lakh crore) [⚠️ VERIFY]
- **Loan:** Up to 20% of outstanding credit, max ₹5 crore

---

## 10. Aatmanirbhar Bharat (Self-Reliant India)

### 10.1 Key Facts

- **Announced:** 12 May 2020 by PM Modi
- **₹20 lakh crore economic package**
- **5 pillars:** Economy, Infrastructure, System, Demography, Demand
- **5 phases** announced
- **Multiple schemes within:** ECLGS, PM SVANidhi, Garib Kalyan Rojgar Abhiyaan, etc.

## 11. Other Important Government Schemes

### 11.1 PM-KISAN

- **Direct income support:** ₹6,000/year to farmers
- In 3 installments of ₹2,000 each

### 11.2 PMSYM — Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maandhan

- **Pension scheme** for unorganized workers
- Minimum assured pension: ₹3,000/month after age 60

### 11.3 PM Matru Vandana Yojana

- **Maternity benefit:** ₹5,000
- For pregnant and lactating women

### 11.4 PM Ujjwala Yojana

- **Free LPG connection** to BPL families

### 11.5 Saubhagya Scheme

- Free electricity connections to all households

### 11.6 Ayushman Bharat

- Already covered in Chapter 7

### 11.7 KCC — Kisan Credit Card

- Already covered in Chapter 6

### 11.8 CGTMSE — Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro & Small Enterprises

- Provides credit guarantee to collateral-free loans for MSEs
- Set up by GoI and SIDBI

### 11.9 MUDRA Ltd

- Refinance institution for MFIs and NBFCs

### 11.10 PM Vishwakarma Yojana

- For traditional artisans and craftspeople
- Launched: 2023
- Components: Recognition, skill training, toolkit incentive, credit support, marketing support
