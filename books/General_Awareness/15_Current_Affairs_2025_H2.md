## 10. Practice Questions (20 PYQs from H2 2025)

**Q1.** Which country hosted the BRICS Summit 2025?
(a) Russia (b) Brazil (c) China (d) India
**Answer**: (b) Brazil (Rio de Janeiro, July 2025)

**Q2.** Operation Sindoor (May 2025) was India's response to:
(a) Surgical strike (b) Pahalgam terror attack (c) Surgical Strike 2 (d) Uri attack
**Answer**: (b) Pahalgam terror attack (Apr 22, 2025; 26 tourists killed in J&K)

**Q3.** SSLV-D3 was launched in which month of 2025?
(a) Jul (b) Aug (c) Sep (d) Oct
**Answer**: (b) August 2025; EOS-08 satellite

**Q4.** RBI's Repo Rate as of Sep 2025:
(a) 6.0% (b) 6.25% (c) 6.5% (d) 6.75%
**Answer**: (c) 6.5% (unchanged since Feb 2023)

**Q5.** The PM Vishwakarma Yojana was launched in:
(a) Aug 2023 (b) Sep 2023 (c) Jan 2024 (d) Apr 2024
**Answer**: (b) September 17, 2023

**Q6.** The Chess Olympiad 2025 was held in:
(a) Budapest (b) Baku (c) None (online) (d) New York
**Answer**: (a) Budapest, Hungary (Sep 2025)

**Q7.** India was elected to UNSC non-permanent seat for:
(a) 2024-25 (b) 2026-27 (c) 2025-26 (d) 2027-28
**Answer**: (b) 2026-27 (elected June 2025)

**Q8.** G20 Summit 2025 was held in:
(a) India (b) South Africa (c) Brazil (d) USA
**Answer**: (b) Johannesburg, South Africa (Nov 22-23, 2025)

**Q9.** The 1st nuclear submarine commissioned in India was:
(a) INS General Studies(b) INS Arighat (c) INS Vikrant (d) INS Kalvari
**Answer**: (a) INS General Studies(commissioned 2016)

**Q10.** UPI international expansion (H2 2025) includes all except:
(a) UAE (b) Singapore (c) Japan (d) France
**Answer**: (c) Japan (not yet operational; planned)

**Q11.** The 18th installment of PM-KISAN was released in:
(a) Aug 2025 (b) Sep 2025 (c) Oct 2025 (d) Nov 2025
**Answer**: (c) October 2025

**Q12.** DPDP Rules 2025 were notified in:
(a) Apr 2025 (b) May 2025 (c) Jun 2025 (d) Jul 2025
**Answer**: (b) May 2025 (Digital Personal Data Protection Rules notified under DPDP Act 2023)

**Q13.** LIGO India is being constructed in:
(a) Pune, Maharashtra (b) Hingoli, Maharashtra (c) Bengaluru, Karnataka (d) Udhagamandalam, TN
**Answer**: (b) Hingoli, Maharashtra (construction began Oct 2025)

**Q14.** The Asia Cup 2025 T20 was won by:
(a) India (b) Sri Lanka (c) Pakistan (d) Bangladesh
**Answer**: (a) India (won Sep 2025)

**Q15.** India's 5G coverage by Aug 2025 reached:
(a) 90% districts (b) 95% districts (c) 99% districts (d) 100% districts
**Answer**: (c) ~99% of districts covered

**Q16.** The Digital Personal Data Protection Act was passed in:
(a) 2022 (b) 2023 (c) 2024 (d) 2025
**Answer**: (b) 2023 (DPDP Act 2023; Rules notified May 2025)

**Q17.** PM Awas Yojana (Urban 2.0) interest subsidy is for loans up to:
(a) ₹10 lakh (b) ₹15 lakh (c) ₹20 lakh (d) ₹25 lakh
**Answer**: (a) ₹10 lakh (₹2.5L subsidy for MIG-I, ₹2.5L for MIG-II)

**Q18.** Indian Chess Olympiad 2025 — India "B" team medal:
(a) Gold (b) Silver (c) Bronze (d) No medal
**Answer**: (a) Gold (women's team)

**Q19.** "PMJDY" completed 11 years on:
(a) Aug 28, 2025 (b) Aug 15, 2025 (c) Jan 26, 2025 (d) Sep 5, 2025
**Answer**: (a) August 28, 2025

**Q20.** The Semiconductor Mission's fab in Dholera is being built by:
(a) Tata-Powerchip (b) Reliance (c) Vedanta-Foxconn (d) Adani
**Answer**: (a) Tata-Powerchip (Gujarat)
