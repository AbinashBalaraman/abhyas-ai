# Chapter 5: Indian Polity & Constitution

> **Chapter Overview:** This chapter covers the Indian Constitution, its structure, key articles, fundamental rights, directive principles, governance structures, and constitutional bodies. The Polity section carries 5–8 questions in SSC CGL Tier-I, 4–6 in RRB NTPC, 3–5 in IBPS PO, and is core to UPSC Prelims.
>
> **Primary sources:** Standard General Knowledge2026 GK (Indian Polity & Constitution section, p. 201-249), General Studies 2023 (Indian Polity, p. 51-64), supplemented by standard reference on the Constitution of India.

## 1. Comprehensive Content

### 1.1 Evolution & Making of the Constitution

**Background:** British era governance acts — Regulating Act 1773, Pitt's India Act 1784, Charter Acts 1793/1813/1833/1853, Government of India Act 1858, Indian Councils Act 1861/1892/1909, Government of India Act 1919, Government of India Act 1935 (most important — basis of Indian Constitution).

**Constituent Assembly:**
- Formed: 1946 (Cabinet Mission Plan)
- Total members: 389 (292 from British Provinces + 93 from Princely States + 4 from Chief Commissioners' Provinces)
- After partition (1947): Reduced to 299 members
- **First meeting:** 9 December 1946 (Temporary Chairman: Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha; later permanent)
- **Permanent Chairman (President):** **Dr. Rajendra Prasad** (elected 11 Dec 1946)
- **Vice-President:** H.C. Mukherjee
- **Constitutional Adviser:** **B.N. Rau** (B.N. Rau prepared the initial draft)
- **Drafting Committee Chairman:** **Dr. B.R. Ambedkar** (with 7 members: N. Gopalaswami Ayyangar, Alladi Krishnaswami Ayyar, K.M. Munshi, Syed Mohammad Saadulla, B.L. Mitter, D.P. Khaitan — replaced by N. Madhava Rau after Khaitan's death)
- **Objective Resolution** moved by **Jawaharlal Nehru** (13 Dec 1946); adopted 22 Jan 1947.
- **Enactment:** 26 November 1949 (adopted by CA)
- **Commencement:** **26 January 1950** (chosen to commemorate Purna Swaraj declaration of 1930)
- **Total Sessions:** 11; **Total days of sittings:** 165; **Total time:** 2 years 11 months 18 days
- **Original Constitution:** 395 Articles + 8 Schedules + 22 Parts
- **Present (as of 2026):** 470+ Articles, 12 Schedules, 25 Parts

### 1.2 Preamble

- **Solemnly resolved** to constitute India into a **SOVEREIGN SOCIALIST SECULAR DEMOCRATIC REPUBLIC** and to secure to all its citizens:
  - **JUSTICE** (social, economic, political)
  - **LIBERTY** (of thought, expression, belief, faith, worship)
  - **EQUALITY** (of status and opportunity)
  - **FRATERNITY** (assuring dignity of individual and unity of nation)
- **Amended** by 42nd Amendment (1976): added "Socialist", "Secular", "Integrity".
- Preamble is part of the Constitution (Berubari Union case, 1960 overruled by Kesavananda Bharati 1973).

### 1.5 Fundamental Rights (Part III, Art. 12–35)

Originally 7; now 6 (Right to Property removed by 44th Amendment, 1978 — made legal right under Art. 300A):
1. **Right to Equality (Art. 14–18)**
2. **Right to Freedom (Art. 19–22)**
3. **Right against Exploitation (Art. 23–24)**
4. **Right to Freedom of Religion (Art. 25–28)**
5. **Cultural and Educational Rights (Art. 29–30)**
6. **Right to Constitutional Remedies (Art. 32)**
- Suspended during National Emergency (Art. 358 & 359 — 44th Amendment limits this to 2 cases, not all).

### 1.6 Directive Principles of State Policy (Part IV, Art. 36–51)

- **DPSP** (Ireland-inspired) — non-justiciable; fundamental in governance.
- Important: Art. 38 (welfare state), Art. 39 (equal distribution of wealth), Art. 40 (Panchayati Raj), Art. 41 (Right to work), Art. 44 (Uniform Civil Code), Art. 45 (free education for children; now Art. 21A), Art. 48 (cow slaughter prohibition, organisation of agriculture), Art. 48A (environment — added 42nd Amendment), Art. 51 (international peace).

### 1.7 Fundamental Duties (Part IVA, Art. 51A) — 42nd Amendment 1976

10 duties: abide by Constitution, cherish noble ideals, uphold sovereignty, defend country, render national service, common brotherhood, value and preserve heritage, protect natural environment, develop scientific temper, safeguard public property, strive for excellence.

### 1.8 Schedules of the Constitution

1. **1st:** List of States and UTs with territories.
2. **2nd:** Salaries, allowances, privileges of President, Governors, Speaker, Judges, CAG.
3. **3rd:** Forms of Oaths and Affirmations.
4. **4th:** Allocation of seats in Rajya Sabha to States and UTs.
5. **5th:** Administration and control of Scheduled Areas and Scheduled Tribes; provisions for administration of tribal areas (Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram).
6. **6th:** Administration of Tribal Areas in Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram.
7. **7th:** Division of powers between Union and States (Three Lists — Union, State, Concurrent).
8. **8th:** Languages (originally 14; now 22 by 92nd Amendment 2003).
9. **9th:** Acts protected from judicial review (certain land reform laws).
10. **10th:** Anti-defection law (52nd Amendment, 1985).
11. **11th:** Panchayat Raj (73rd Amendment, 1992).
12. **12th:** Municipalities (74th Amendment, 1992).

### 1.9 Three Lists (Seventh Schedule)

- **Union List (~97 subjects):** Defence, foreign affairs, atomic energy, banking, currency, customs, post, telegraph, railways, interstate trade, war & peace, national highways, census.
- **State List (~66 subjects):** Police, public order, state courts, prisons, local government, public health, agriculture, land, water supply, fisheries, markets, gambling.
- **Concurrent List (~47 subjects):** Education, forests, criminal law (IPC), marriage & divorce, electricity, newspapers, books, printing press, drugs, population control, weights & measures.

### 1.11 Structure of Government

**Parliament (Union Legislature):** President + Lok Sabha (Lower House) + Rajya Sabha (Upper House).
- **Lok Sabha:** 543 elected + 2 nominated (Anglo-Indian, abolished but restored through Art. 333 temporarily). Maximum strength 552. Speaker (Art. 93) — elected by LS, can vote only on tied issue. Current strength: 543. Term: 5 years.
- **Rajya Sabha:** 250 members (12 nominated by President + 238 elected by State Legislative Assemblies). Vice-President is ex-officio Chairman; Deputy Chairman elected by RS. Special powers: Art. 249 (national importance), Art. 312 (All-India Services), Art. 352 (Emergency), creation of All-India Services.
- **Money Bill:** Introduced only in Lok Sabha; can be introduced by Minister only; RS can recommend (14 days) but LS has final say.
- **Joint Sitting:** President can summon (Art. 108); presided by Speaker; deadlock only on ordinary bills.

**President (Art. 52–62):** Head of State, indirectly elected (electoral college = elected LS + RS + State Legislative Assemblies; elected MLAs have vote value based on population). Term 5 years. Electoral quota system (proportional representation with single transferable vote). Impeachment by Parliament (Art. 61). Powers: executive, legislative (assent, veto, pocket veto), judicial (pardon — Art. 72), financial (Money Bill), emergency (Art. 352, 356, 360), ordinance (Art. 123).
- **Veto:** Absolute, Suspensive, Pocket (no time limit), Qualified.
- **First President:** Dr. Rajendra Prasad (longest serving — 12 years).
- **First Woman President:** Pratibha Patil (2007-2012).

**Vice-President:** Ex-officio Chairman of Rajya Sabha. Term 5 years. Acts as acting President in absence.

**Prime Minister (Art. 75):** Leader of majority party in LS; appointed by President. Council of Ministers (Art. 74) headed by PM aids President.
- First PM: Jawaharlal Nehru (longest serving).
- **First non-Congress PM:** Morarji Desai (1977).

**Supreme Court (Art. 124–147):** Apex court; 34 judges including CJI; appointed by President. Original jurisdiction (Art. 32), appellate (Art. 133–136), advisory (Art. 143). Removal by Parliament (impeachment) — never used.
- **First CJI:** H.J. Kania.

**High Courts:** Art. 214. Each state/UT has HC. 25 HCs in India. Jurisdiction: original, appellate, writs (Art. 226).

### 1.12 State Government

**Governor (Art. 153–167):** Appointed by President (not elected). Term 5 years (pleasure of President). Can pardon (Art. 161) but not death sentence. Acts as agent of Centre.
- **CM:** Head of state government; leader of majority party in Vidhan Sabha.
- **Vidhan Sabha:** State Legislative Assembly; Vidhan Parishad (Legislative Council) — only in 6 states (Bihar, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, UP — as of 2026).
- **Speaker:** Presides over Assembly.

### 1.14 Emergency Provisions (Part XVIII)

**Three Types of Emergency:**

| Emergency | Article | Ground | Effect | Parliamentary Approval |
|-----------|---------|--------|--------|----------------------|
| National Emergency | 352 | War, external aggression, armed rebellion | Centre gets extra powers; Fundamental Rights restricted (Art. 358 & 359); LS elections extended | Special majority |
| President's Rule (State Emergency) | 356 | Failure of constitutional machinery in state | State government dismissed; Governor takes over | Simple majority |
| Financial Emergency | 360 | Financial stability threatened | Centre can direct states on financial matters | Simple majority |

**National Emergency declared 3 times in India:**
1. 1962 — Sino-Indian War
2. 1971 — Indo-Pakistan War (Bangladesh Liberation)
3. 1975-77 — Internal disturbance (Indira Gandhi; lifted by Morarji Desai)

**Effects of National Emergency:**
- Fundamental Rights under Art. 19 automatically suspended (Art. 358).
- Other FRs can be suspended via presidential order (Art. 359).
- Centre can issue directions to states on any matter.
- Life of Lok Sabha can be extended by 1 year at a time (max 6 months after emergency ends).

### 1.15 Constitutional Doctrines (Important Legal Concepts)

| Doctrine | Meaning | Source |
|----------|---------|--------|
| **Basic Structure** | Parliament cannot amend the basic structure of Constitution | Kesavananda Bharati v. State of Kerala (1973) |
| **Doctrine of Eclipse** | Pre-constitutional laws inconsistent with FRs are "eclipsed" but not void | Bhikaji v. State of MP (1955) |
| **Doctrine of Severability** | If part of a law is unconstitutional, only that part is void | RMDC v. Union of India (1957) |
| **Doctrine of Waiver** | A person can waive fundamental rights (debatable) | — |
| **Doctrine of Prospective Overruling** | Court ruling applies prospectively | Golaknath v. State of Punjab (1967) |
| **Doctrine of Implied Powers** | Constitution has implied powers beyond expressed | — |
| **Doctrine of Pleasure** | President/Governor serves at pleasure (Art. 310) | — |
| **Doctrine of Pith and Substance** | Determine true nature of legislation for List conflict | — |

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Quorum** | 1/10 of total members in LS/RS for proceedings |
| **Casting Vote** | Speaker's vote on tied issue |
| **Money Bill** | Bill dealing with taxes, government expenditure (Art. 110) |

| **Resolution** | Formal expression of opinion by House |

| **Supplementary Grant** | Additional budget during fiscal year |

**State Legislature subjects:** State List, Concurrent List (residuary powers go to Union in India, not states).

### 1.13 Local Self-Government

**Panchayati Raj (73rd Amendment 1992):** 3-tier system — Gram Panchayat, Panchayat Samiti, Zila Parishad. Elections by State Election Commission. Reservation for SC/ST (proportionate) + 1/3 seats for women. Article 40 (DPSP); 11th Schedule (29 subjects).

**Municipalities (74th Amendment 1992):** Nagar Panchayat → Municipal Council → Municipal Corporation. 12th Schedule (18 subjects).

### 1.14 Constitutional Bodies

- **Election Commission (Art. 324):** Chief Election Commissioner + 2 Election Commissioners. Conduct elections.
- **Finance Commission (Art. 280):** Every 5 years; recommends tax devolution. 16th FC (chairman: Arvind Panagariya).
- **UPSC / SPSC (Art. 315):** Recruitment to civil services. 9th Schedule of Constitution includes IAS, IPS, IFS etc.
- **CAG (Art. 148):** Audits Union and State accounts. Reports to Parliament.
- **Attorney General (Art. 76)** / **Advocate General (Art. 165)**
- **Special Officer for Linguistic Minorities (Art. 350B)**
- **National Commissions:** SC, ST, OBC, Women, Minorities, Human Rights, Backward Classes.
- **NITI Aayog:** Replaced Planning Commission (Jan 1, 2015). Chairman: PM. CEO: appointed by PM.

### 1.15 National Symbols

- **National Flag:** Tricolor (saffron, white, green — horizontal); navy-blue Ashoka Chakra (24 spokes) in centre. Adopted 22 July 1947.
- **National Emblem:** Lion Capital of Ashoka (Sarnath) — adopted 26 Jan 1950. "Satyameva Jayate" (Truth Alone Triumphs).
- **National Anthem:** "Jana Gana Mana" by Rabindranath Tagore (adopted 24 Jan 1950).
- **National Song:** "Vande Mataram" by Bankim Chandra Chatterji.
- **National Calendar:** Saka Era (Chaitra 1 = 22 March).
- **National Animal:** Bengal Tiger.
- **National Bird:** Indian Peacock.
- **National Flower:** Lotus.
- **National Tree:** Banyan.
- **National Fruit:** Mango.
- **National River:** Ganga.
- **National Game:** Hockey (de facto).
- **National Heritage Animal:** Indian Elephant.
