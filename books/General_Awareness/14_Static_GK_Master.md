## 12. Practice Questions (20 PYQs)

**Q1.** Who is the "Father of the Indian Constitution"?
(a) Jawaharlal Nehru (b) B.R. Ambedkar (c) Mahatma Gandhi (d) Sardar Patel
**Answer**: (b) B.R. Ambedkar

**Q2.** The 42nd Constitutional Amendment is also known as:
(a) Right to Property (b) Mini Constitution (c) Emergency Amendment (d) Anti-defection
**Answer**: (b) Mini Constitution (added words Socialist, Secular, Integrity to Preamble)

**Q3.** The Right to Education (Article 21A) was added by which amendment?
(a) 84th (b) 86th (c) 87th (d) 92nd
**Answer**: (b) 86th Amendment, 2002

**Q4.** The "Make in India" initiative was launched in:
(a) 2012 (b) 2014 (c) 2015 (d) 2016
**Answer**: (b) 25 September 2014

**Q5.** The Indian Army's "Operation Vijay" refers to:
(a) 1947-48 Kashmir (b) 1961 Goa (c) 1962 NEFA (d) 1971 Bangladesh
**Answer**: (b) 1961 (Goa); also used for 1999 Kargil

**Q6.** The Reserve Bank of India was established on:
(a) 1 Jan 1935 (b) 1 April 1935 (c) 1 April 1934 (d) 1 Jan 1949
**Answer**: (b) 1 April 1935 under RBI Act 1934; nationalized 1949

**Q7.** GST (Goods and Services Tax) was rolled out in India on:
(a) 1 July 2017 (b) 1 April 2017 (c) 1 Jan 2017 (d) 1 April 2018
**Answer**: (a) 1 July 2017

**Q8.** India's rank in UN Human Development Index 2023-24 (approx):
(a) 130-135 (b) 100-110 (c) 150-160 (d) 80-90
**Answer**: (a) ~134 (improving from 132 in 2022) [⚠️ VERIFY current rank]

**Q9.** The "Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana" was launched in:
(a) Aug 2014 (b) Jan 2015 (c) Aug 2015 (d) Jan 2016
**Answer**: (a) 28 August 2014

**Q10.** Which country won the most FIFA World Cups?
(a) Germany (b) Argentina (c) Brazil (d) Italy
**Answer**: (c) Brazil (5: 1958, 1962, 1970, 1994, 2002); Argentina won 2022

**Q11.** The "Bharat Ratna" award was instituted in:
(a) 1950 (b) 1954 (c) 1955 (d) 1969
**Answer**: (b) 2 January 1954

**Q12.** The headquarters of UNESCO is in:
(a) New York (b) Geneva (c) Paris (d) Vienna
**Answer**: (c) Paris

**Q13.** Which state has the highest literacy rate as per Census 2011?
(a) Goa (b) Kerala (c) Maharashtra (d) Tamil Nadu
**Answer**: (b) Kerala (93.91%)

**Q14.** The "Bharat Ratna" award carries a medal in the shape of:
(a) Sun (b) Lotus (c) Ashoka Chakra (d) Peepal leaf
**Answer**: (d) Peepal leaf

**Q15.** Which classical dance originated in Kerala?
(a) Bharatanatyam (b) Kuchipudi (c) Mohiniyattam (d) Odissi
**Answer**: (c) Mohiniyattam; also Kathakali from Kerala

**Q16.** The Indian National Congress was founded in:
(a) 1875 (b) 1885 (c) 1905 (d) 1920
**Answer**: (b) 28 December 1885 by A.O. Hume

**Q17.** The "Right to Constitutional Remedies" is under Article:
(a) 19 (b) 21 (c) 32 (d) 226
**Answer**: (c) Article 32 (Dr. Ambedkar called it "heart and soul of the Constitution")

**Q18.** Which is the longest river in India?
(a) Godavari (b) Yamuna (c) Ganga (d) Brahmaputra
**Answer**: (c) Ganga (2,525 km); within India

**Q19.** The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) was founded in:
(a) 1962 (b) 1969 (c) 1972 (d) 1980
**Answer**: (b) 15 August 1969; HQ Bengaluru; formed under DOS

**Q20.** The "Chipko Movement" (1973) was associated with:
(a) Forest conservation (b) Anti-liquor (c) Tribal rights (d) Land reform
**Answer**: (a) Forest conservation; led by Sunderlal Bahuguna in Uttarakhand
