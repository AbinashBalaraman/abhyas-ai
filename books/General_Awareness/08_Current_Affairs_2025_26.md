## 7. Science & Technology / ISRO Achievements

### 7.1 ISRO Missions 2025-2026

- **19 May 2025**: **EOS-09 satellite** failed shortly after launch from Satish Dhawan Space Centre.
- **30 July 2025**: **NASA-ISRO Synthetic Aperture Radar (NISAR)** launched — first dual-band radar imaging satellite, joint NASA-ISRO project.
- **2 November 2025**: ISRO launched its **heaviest communications satellite CMS-03** (4,410 kg / 9,720 lb) from Satish Dhawan Space Centre.
- **24 December 2025**: ISRO launched heaviest payload — **US-built AST SpaceMobile communications satellite (6,100 kg / 13,400 lb)**.
- **SpaDeX Mission (early 2025)**: ISRO tested space docking capabilities — technology for Chandrayaan-4, Gaganyaan, Bharatiya Antariksh Station.

### 7.2 Gaganyaan Programme (India's First Human Spaceflight)

- Approved by Union Cabinet **28 December 2018**; announced by PM Modi 15 August 2018
- **Budget:** ₹20,193 crore (US$2.1 billion) — including additional ₹11,170 crore approved 18 September 2024
- **Maiden crewed launch:** Targeted for 2027-2028
- **Crew capacity:** 3 people; **Orbit:** 400 km Low Earth Orbit; **Mission duration:** Up to 7 days
- **Test Vehicle (TV-D1) Mission 1:** 21 October 2023 - successful high-altitude abort test
- **Test Vehicle (TV-D2):** Scheduled Q4 2026
- **Rakesh Sharma** (3 April 1984): First Indian in space (Soviet Soyuz T-11)
- **Wing Commander Shubhanshu Shukla** (25 June 2025): Second Indian in space, as part of Axiom Mission 4 to ISS

### 7.3 Other Science/Tech

- **January 2025**: First HMPV Virus case in India detected in Bengaluru.
- **21 January 2026**: Nipah virus outbreak reported in West Bengal (at least 2 cases in Barasat).
