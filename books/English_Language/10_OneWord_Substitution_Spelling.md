# Chapter 10: One Word Substitutions & Spelling Rules

> **Chapter Overview:** This chapter covers **One-Word Substitutions (OWS)** and **Spelling Rules**. **OWS** tests the ability to express a phrase/description using a single word. **Spelling** tests correct word formation. Together, they contribute **3-5 questions per SSC CGL Tier-I**, **2-3 in Banking**, and **2-3 in RRB NTPC**. OWS is heavily tested in SSC (Previous Years Solved PapersCh.8 declares ~1100 PYQs).
>
> **Comprehensive exam-aligned study material covering standard syllabi for SSC, RRB, Banking, and State PSC examinations.

## 1. Conceptual Foundation

### 1.1 What is One-Word Substitution?

**One-Word Substitution (OWS)** is a single word that replaces a **phrase or description**. It tests vocabulary, word knowledge, and the ability to express complex ideas concisely.

### 1.2 What are Spelling Rules?

**Spelling** tests the **correct letter sequence** of words. SSC/Banking exams test common misspellings and confusable pairs.

### 1.3 Why These Topics Matter

- **OWS** builds vocabulary — useful for Cloze Test, RC, and Vocabulary.
- **Spelling** tests attention to detail.
- **Combined** they contribute 4-6 questions per Tier-I.

## 3. Spelling Rules

### 3.1 Common Misspelling Patterns

**i before e rule (most cases):**
- Believe, receive, ceiling, deceive, conceive.
- Exceptions: weird, foreign, neighbor, seize, leisure.

[NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS — standard spelling rule]

**Silent letters:**
- B: doubt, debt, subtle, doubt.
- C: science, scene, scissors, ascend.
- G: sign, design, foreign, reign.
- H: hour, honest, honor, exhibit.
- K: knife, knee, know, knock, knowledge.
- L: calm, half, talk, walk, yolk.
- P: psychology, pneumatic, psalm, pneumonia, pterodactyl.
- T: castle, listen, often, whistle, Christmas.
- U: build, guard, guide, guest, guitar.
- W: write, wrong, wrap, wren, two, who.

[NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

### 3.2 Doubling the Final Consonant

- For words ending in **single consonant + single vowel + single consonant** (CVC pattern), **double the consonant** when adding a suffix starting with a vowel:
- Run + ing → Running (double n).
- Big + er → Bigger (double g).
- Stop + ed → Stopped (double p).
- Get + ing → Getting (double t).

**Exceptions:**
- Words with more than one syllable where the stress is NOT on the last syllable: visit → visiting (no double).
- Words ending in 'w', 'x', 'y': show → showing, fix → fixed, play → playing.

[NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS — standard rule]

### 3.3 Dropping the Final 'e'

When adding a suffix starting with a vowel, **drop the final 'e'**:
- Make + ing → Making.
- Hope + ed → Hoped.
- Live + er → Liver (person who lives).
- BUT: Words ending in 'ce', 'ge', 'se' KEEP the 'e' to keep soft sound: notice + able → noticeable (keep e).

[NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

### 3.4 Changing 'y' to 'i'

When a word ends in **consonant + y** and we add a suffix not starting with 'i':
- Happy + ness → Happiness (y → i).
- Easy + er → Easier (y → i).
- Beauty + ful → Beautiful (y → i).

**Exceptions:** Adding -ing: study + ing → studying (y unchanged).

[NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

## 4. Speed-Solving Shortcuts

### 4.1 The "Category Recognition" Trick

Recognize the OWS category (person, place, action, time, etc.) to narrow down the answer.

[NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

### 4.2 The "Suffix Recognition" Trick

OWS often use specific suffixes:
- **-phile** (lover): bibliophile, zoophile.
- **-phobia** (fear): acrophobia, claustrophobia.
- **-cide** (killing): suicide, fratricide.
- **-logy** (study): zoology, biology.
- **-graphy** (writing): biography, geography.
- **-cracy** (government): democracy, monarchy.
- **-ism** (belief): atheism, optimism.

[NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

### 4.3 The "Common Root" Trick

Many OWS share common roots:
- **bio** = life: biology, biography, biodegradable.
- **geo** = earth: geology, geography, geometry.
- **astro** = star: astrology, astronomy, astronaut.
- **aqua** = water: aquarium, aquatic, aqueduct.
- **auto** = self: autobiography, automobile, automatic.

[NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

### 4.4 The "i before e" Trick

Memorize: "I before E, except after C, or when sounded as 'ay' as in 'neighbor' and 'weigh'."

[NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

### 4.5 The "Silent Letter" Trick

Memorize common silent letter words: doubt (b), knife (k), hour (h), etc.

[NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

### 4.6 The "Homophone" Trick

For confusable pairs, memorize the **meaning difference** (accept/except, affect/effect, etc.).

[NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

### 4.7 The "Practice" Trick

Spelling improves with practice. **Read** 15 minutes daily; **write** new words 3 times each.

[NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

## 5. Question Types

### 5.1 Type 1 — Choose Correct OWS

**Example:** A person who studies the stars is called:
(a) Geologist (b) Astronomer (c) Botanist (d) Zoologist
**Solution:** Stars → Astronomy → Astronomer. **Answer: (b).** *Time: 15 sec.* [§2]

### 5.2 Type 2 — Identify Category

**Example:** "Bibliophile" is a:
(a) Person (b) Place (c) Action (d) Quality
**Solution:** Biblio + phile = book lover = Person. **Answer: (a).** *Time: 15 sec.*

### 5.3 Type 3 — Match Description to OWS

**Example:** A speech delivered without preparation:
(a) Eulogy (b) Panegyric (c) Extempore (d) Soliloquy
**Solution:** "Without preparation" → Extempore. **Answer: (c).** *Time: 15 sec.*

### 5.4 Type 4 — Spelling Identification

**Example:** Find the correctly spelled word:
(a) Recieve (b) Receive (c) Reciece (d) Receeve
**Solution:** "I before E except after C" → Receive. **Answer: (b).** *Time: 10 sec.*

### 5.5 Type 5 — Spelling Correction

**Example:** Find the misspelled word:
(a) Definitely (b) Seperate (c) Successful (d) Necessary
**Solution:** Seperate → Separate. **Answer: (b).** *Time: 10 sec.*

### 5.6 Type 6 — Confusable Pair

**Example:** Choose correct: "The ___ of the medicine was immediate."
(a) affect (b) effect
**Solution:** "Effect" (noun = result). **Answer: (b).** *Time: 10 sec.*

### 5.7 Type 7 — Suffix Recognition

**Example:** "Hydrophobia" means:
(a) Love of water (b) Fear of water (c) Study of water (d) Living in water
**Solution:** "Phobia" = fear. **Answer: (b).** *Time: 15 sec.*

### 8.3 Spelling Rules

| Rule | Example |
|------|---------|
| i before e (most) | Believe, Receive |
| Silent letters | Doubt, Knife, Hour |
| Double final consonant (CVC) | Running, Bigger |
| Drop e + vowel suffix | Making, Hoped |
| y → i (consonant + y) | Happiness |

### 8.4 Top 5 Shortcuts

1. **Suffix recognition** for OWS.
2. **Common roots** (bio, geo, astro).
3. **i before e** rule (with exceptions).
4. **Silent letter list** memorization.
5. **Confusable pairs** by meaning.

4. **Misspelling "separate"**.
