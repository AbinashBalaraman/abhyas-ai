# Chapter 8: Direct-Indirect Narration & Active-Passive Voice

> **Chapter Overview:** This chapter covers two **interconnected transformation topics**: **Direct-Indirect Narration (Speech)** and **Active-Passive Voice**. Both require understanding of **Tense** (see Chapter 7) and **Sentence Structure**. **Narration** tests the ability to convert reported speech to direct speech and vice versa. **Voice** tests the ability to convert between active and passive constructions. Together, they contribute **5-8 questions per SSC CGL Tier-I**, **10-15 in Tier-II**, **5-8 in IBPS PO**, and **3-5 in RRB NTPC**.

## 1. Conceptual Foundation

### 1.1 What is Narration?

**Narration** is the act of reporting someone's words. There are two forms:
- **Direct Speech**: Exact words of the speaker, in quotes. "I am happy," he said.
- **Indirect Speech**: Reported words, no quotes. He said that he was happy.

### 1.2 What is Voice?

**Voice** indicates the relationship between the subject and the verb:
- **Active Voice**: Subject performs the action. "He wrote the letter."
- **Passive Voice**: Subject receives the action. "The letter was written by him."

### 1.3 Why These Topics Matter

- **Tense backshift** tests both Narration AND Tense mastery.
- **Voice transformation** tests Sentence Structure.
- **High frequency** in SSC CGL Tier-II (often 5-7 Qs combined).
- **Foundation for Cloze Test** (Chapter 5) and Reading Comprehension (Chapter 4).

## 2. Direct-Indirect Narration — Rules

### 2.1 The Basic Conversion

**Direct:** He said, "I am happy."
**Indirect:** He said that he was happy.

**Changes:**
- **Quotes removed**.
- **Conjunction "that"** added (optional in indirect).
- **Pronoun shifted**: "I" → "he" (1st person → 3rd person).
- **Tense backshifted**: "am" → "was" (see §2.3).

### 2.2 Reporting Verb Changes

| Direct Reporting Verb | Indirect Reporting Verb |
|----------------------|-------------------------|
| say | said |
| says | said |
| said | said |
| tell | told |
| tell (object) | told (object) |

- "He **said to** me, '...'" → "He **told** me that..."
- "He **said**, '...'" → "He **said** that..."

[NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS — standard rules]

### 2.3 Tense Backshift (Sequence of Tenses)

When the reporting verb is in **past tense** (said, told), the reported verb shifts back one tense:

| Direct | Indirect |
|--------|----------|
| Present Simple (V1/V1+s) | Past Simple (V2) |
| Present Continuous (is/am/are + V1+ing) | Past Continuous (was/were + V1+ing) |
| Present Perfect (has/have + V3) | Past Perfect (had + V3) |
| Past Simple (V2) | Past Perfect (had + V3) |
| Past Continuous (was/were + V1+ing) | Past Perfect Continuous (had + been + V1+ing) |
| Past Perfect (had + V3) | Past Perfect (no change) |
| Future (will + V1) | would + V1 |

### 2.4 Modal Backshift

| Direct Modal | Indirect Modal |
|--------------|----------------|
| will | would |
| can | could |
| may | might |
| shall | should |
| must | had to / must (depending) |
| would, could, might, should, ought to, used to | NO CHANGE (already past) |

### 2.5 Other Word Changes

| Direct | Indirect |
|--------|----------|
| this | that |
| these | those |
| here | there |
| now | then |
| today | that day |
| tomorrow | the next day / the following day |
| yesterday | the previous day / the day before |
| ago | before |
| thus | so |
| hence | therefore |
| "Sir" | "respectfully" (often omitted) |

[NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS — standard changes]

### 2.6 Question Conversion

**Direct:** He said, "Are you coming?"
**Indirect:** He asked if/whether I was coming.

**Changes:**
- Reporting verb: "said" → "asked" / "enquired" / "wondered".
- Question mark removed.
- **"if" or "whether"** replaces the question structure.
- Reverse the order: "Are you coming?" → "if I was coming".
- Tense backshift.

[NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

### 2.7 Exclamation/Command Conversion

**Exclamations:**
- **Direct:** He said, "What a beautiful flower!"
- **Indirect:** He exclaimed that it was a very beautiful flower. / He exclaimed with joy that...

**Commands:**
- **Direct:** He said, "Go there."
- **Indirect:** He ordered/commanded/requested to go there. (Use "to" + V1)

**Requests:**
- **Direct:** He said, "Please help me."
- **Indirect:** He requested to help him. / He requested that I help him.

[NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

### 2.8 Examples

**Example 1:** He said, "I am reading a book."
**Indirect:** He said that he was reading a book.

**Example 2:** She said, "I will come tomorrow."
**Indirect:** She said that she would come the next day.

**Example 3:** He said to me, "Are you happy?"
**Indirect:** He asked me if/whether I was happy.

**Example 4:** The teacher said, "Open your books."
**Indirect:** The teacher ordered/asked to open the books.

## 3. Active-Passive Voice — Rules

### 3.1 When to Use Passive

Use **Passive Voice** when:
1. The **action is more important** than the doer.
2. The **doer is unknown** or unimportant.
3. The **doer is obvious** from context.
4. **Scientific/descriptive** writing: "Water is composed of hydrogen and oxygen."

[NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS — standard usage rules]

### 3.2 The Basic Conversion

**Active:** S + V + O.
**Passive:** O + be + V3 + by + S.

- **Active:** He wrote the letter.
- **Passive:** The letter was written by him.

### 3.3 Tense-Based Voice Conversion

| Tense | Active | Passive |
|-------|--------|---------|
| Present Simple | He **writes** a letter. | A letter **is written** by him. |
| Past Simple | He **wrote** a letter. | A letter **was written** by him. |
| Future Simple | He **will write** a letter. | A letter **will be written** by him. |
| Present Continuous | He **is writing** a letter. | A letter **is being written** by him. |
| Past Continuous | He **was writing** a letter. | A letter **was being written** by him. |
| Present Perfect | He **has written** a letter. | A letter **has been written** by him. |
| Past Perfect | He **had written** a letter. | A letter **had been written** by him. |
| Future Perfect | He **will have written** a letter. | A letter **will have been written** by him. |
| Modal | He **can write** a letter. | A letter **can be written** by him. |

[NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS — standard transformation table]

### 3.4 Special Cases

**Intransitive Verbs (no object):** No passive possible.
- "He sleeps." (No object → no passive)

**Imperative (commands):** Passive uses "be + V3".
- "Open the door." → "The door should be opened." / "Let the door be opened."

**Interrogative (questions):** Question form + passive.
- "Did he write the letter?" → "Was the letter written by him?"

[NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS — standard rules]

### 3.5 Voice with Modals

| Active | Passive |
|--------|---------|
| He **can** do it. | It **can be** done by him. |
| She **must** submit. | It **must be** submitted by her. |
| They **should** complete. | It **should be** completed by them. |

[NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS — important for Modal+Passive]

### 3.6 Voice with Two Objects

When there are two objects (direct + indirect), passive can use either:
- **Active:** He gave me a book.
- **Passive (1):** I was given a book by him. (Indirect object becomes subject)
- **Passive (2):** A book was given to me by him. (Direct object becomes subject)

[NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

### 3.7 Examples

**Example 1:** Active: The cat killed the mouse.
**Passive:** The mouse was killed by the cat.

**Example 2:** Active: She is writing a letter.
**Passive:** A letter is being written by her.

**Example 3:** Active: He has completed the work.
**Passive:** The work has been completed by him.

**Example 4:** Active: They will build a new house.
**Passive:** A new house will be built by them.

## 4. Speed-Solving Shortcuts

### 4.1 The "Tense Backshift" Trick

For narration, **shift tense back by one step** when reporting verb is past:
- Present → Past
- Past → Past Perfect
- Will → Would
- Can → Could

[NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

### 4.2 The "Person Shift" Trick

For narration, **change pronouns** based on perspective:
- 1st person (I, me, my) → 3rd person (he, him, his) [subject of reporting verb]
- 2nd person (you, your) → depends on object of reporting verb
- 3rd person (he, she, they) → NO CHANGE

[NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

### 4.3 The "Time/Place Shift" Trick

For narration, change time/place words:
- today → that day
- tomorrow → the next day
- yesterday → the day before
- here → there
- this → that

[NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

### 4.4 The "Reporting Verb" Trick

Match the reporting verb to the sentence type:
- Statement → said/told
- Question → asked/enquired
- Command → ordered/commanded
- Request → requested/asked
- Exclamation → exclaimed

[NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

### 4.5 The "Object Becomes Subject" Trick for Voice

In passive, the **object** of the active sentence becomes the **subject** of the passive sentence.
- "He wrote a letter." → "A letter was written by him."

[NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

### 4.6 The "Be + V3" Trick for Voice

The passive uses the appropriate form of "**be**" (is/was/will be/has been/etc.) + **V3** (past participle).

[NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

### 4.7 The "Modal + be + V3" Trick for Voice with Modals

Modal + be + V3 in passive:
- can be done
- must be submitted
- should be completed

[NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

## 5. Classification of Question Types

### 5.1 Type 1 — Direct to Indirect (Statement)

**Example:** He said, "I am happy." → He said that he **was** happy.
**Solution:** Tense backshift. *Time: 30 sec.* [See §2]

### 5.2 Type 2 — Direct to Indirect (Question)

**Example:** He said, "Are you coming?" → He asked if/whether I **was** coming.
**Solution:** "Ask" + "if/whether" + backshift. *Time: 30 sec.* [See §2.6]

### 5.3 Type 3 — Direct to Indirect (Command)

**Example:** He said, "Sit down." → He ordered/asked me to sit down.
**Solution:** Reporting verb change + "to" + V1. *Time: 30 sec.* [See §2.7]

### 5.4 Type 4 — Active to Passive (Simple Tense)

**Example:** He wrote a letter. → A letter **was written** by him.
**Solution:** Object → subject; "was" + V3. *Time: 30 sec.* [See §3]

### 5.5 Type 5 — Active to Passive (Modal)

**Example:** He can do it. → It **can be done** by him.
**Solution:** Modal + be + V3. *Time: 30 sec.* [See §3.5]

### 5.6 Type 6 — Passive to Active

**Example:** A letter was written by him. → He **wrote** a letter.
**Solution:** "By him" → subject; "was written" → "wrote". *Time: 30 sec.* [See §3]

### 5.7 Type 7 — Exclamation Conversion

**Example:** He said, "What a beautiful flower!" → He exclaimed that it **was** a very beautiful flower.
**Solution:** Reporting verb change + tense backshift + "very". *Time: 30 sec.* [See §2.7]

### 5.8 Type 8 — Voice with Two Objects

**Example:** He gave me a book. → I was given a book by him. / A book was given to me by him.
**Solution:** Either object can be the subject in passive. *Time: 30 sec.* [See §3.6]

## 6. Previous Year Questions Analysis (5 PYQs)

### PYQ 1: SSC CGL 2024 — Narration
**Q:** He said, "I am going home." Indirect:
(a) He said that he is going home (b) He said that he was going home (c) He said that he went home (d) He said that he goes home
**Solution:** Tense backshift: "am" → "was". **Answer: (b).** *Time: 30 sec.* [Previous Years Solved PapersCh.4 pattern]

### PYQ 2: IBPS PO 2023 — Voice
**Q:** Active: "The teacher praised the student." Passive:
(a) The student was praised by the teacher (b) The student is praised by the teacher (c) The student has been praised (d) The teacher has praised
**Solution:** "Praised" (V2) → "was praised". **Answer: (a).** *Time: 30 sec.* [Standard]

### PYQ 3: SSC CGL 2023 Mains — Narration with Question
**Q:** He said, "Will you help me?" Indirect:
(a) He asked if I will help him (b) He asked if I would help him (c) He asked whether I would help him (d) Both b and c
**Solution:** "Will" → "would" (backshift). "If" or "whether". **Answer: (d).** *Time: 30 sec.* [Standard]

### PYQ 4: RRB NTPC 2024 — Voice with Modal
**Q:** Active: "He can solve the problem." Passive:
(a) The problem can be solved by him (b) The problem can solved by him (c) The problem is solved by him (d) The problem can be solve by him
**Solution:** "can + be + V3". **Answer: (a).** *Time: 30 sec.* [Standard]

### PYQ 5: SSC CGL 2023 — Narration with Command
**Q:** He said to me, "Bring me a glass of water." Indirect:
(a) He ordered me to bring him a glass of water (b) He asked me to bring him a glass of water (c) He told me to bring him a glass of water (d) All are possible
**Solution:** "Said to" + command → "ordered/asked/told me to". **Answer: (d).** *Time: 30 sec.* [Standard]

## 8. Chapter Summary & Revision Card

### 8.1 Compact Rule Table — Narration

| Rule | Change |
|------|--------|
| Reporting verb | said → said; said to → told; said "?" → asked |
| Tense | Backshift (1 step) |
| Pronouns | 1st → 3rd; 2nd depends; 3rd no change |
| Time/Place | today→that day, tomorrow→next day, here→there |
| Question | "?" + "if/whether" + backshift |
| Command | "to" + V1 (after ordered/asked) |
| Exclamation | "exclaimed" + "very" + backshift |

### 8.2 Compact Rule Table — Voice

| Tense | Active → Passive |
|-------|------------------|
| Present Simple | writes → is written |
| Past Simple | wrote → was written |
| Future Simple | will write → will be written |
| Present Continuous | is writing → is being written |
| Past Continuous | was writing → was being written |
| Present Perfect | has written → has been written |
| Past Perfect | had written → had been written |
| Modal | can write → can be written |

### 8.3 Top 5 Shortcuts

1. **Tense backshift** — Present → Past, Past → Past Perfect, Will → Would.
2. **Pronoun shift** — I/me → he/him (in third-person reports).
3. **Time/place shift** — today → that day, etc.
4. **Object → Subject in Passive** — direct object becomes the new subject.
5. **Modal + be + V3** — for passive with modals.

### 8.4 5 Common Mistakes

1. **No tense backshift** when reporting verb is past.
2. **Pronoun unchanged** in narration (must change 1st person).
3. **Time word unchanged** (today should become that day).
4. **Voice with intransitive verb** — impossible.
5. **Wrong V3 form** in passive.

### 8.5 "If You See ___, Use ___" Decision Rules

| If You See... | Use... |
|---------------|--------|
| "He said, '...'" | Indirect: said that + backshift |
| "He said to me, '...'" | Indirect: told me that + backshift |
| "He said, '...?'" | Indirect: asked if/whether + backshift |
| "He said, '...!'" | Indirect: exclaimed + backshift + very |
| "He said, 'Go...'" | Indirect: ordered/asked to + V1 |
| "Active sentence" | Convert object to subject + be + V3 |
| "Modal in passive" | Modal + be + V3 |
