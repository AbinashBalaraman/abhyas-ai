## 5. Spelling Mastery (Common Errors)

|---|---|---|
| Accommodate | Accomodate | 2 C's, 2 M's (ac-comm-odate) |
| Achieve | Acheive | ie before ve (ach-ieve) |
| Acknowledge | Acknowlege | "edge" before "know" |
| Acquaintance | Acquaintence | quain + tance |
| Advantage | Addvantage | dd + vantage |
| Beginning | Begging | "inn" twice (begin-ning) |
| Believe | Beleive | ie before ve |
| Calendar | Calander | "ar" after "end" |
| Category | Catagory | "egory" not "agory" |
| Committee | Comittee | 2 M's, 2 T's, 2 E's |
| Conscious | Concious | "sci" not "sci" |
| Convenient | Convinient | "ve" + "ni" |
| Definitely | Definately | "ite" not "ately" |
| Disappear | Disapear | 2 P's, 1 R |
| Embarrass | Embarass | 2 R's, 2 S's |
| Familiar | Familiarize | 1 L after "i" |
| February | Febuary | "r" after "b" |
| Guarantee | Guarentee | 2 E's in middle |
| Harassment | Harrassment | 1 R, 2 S's |
| Independent | Independant | "ent" not "ant" |
| Millennium | Millenium | 2 N's, 2 L's |
| Mischievous | Mischievious | NO "i" after "v" |

## 6. Practice Questions (20 PYQs)

**Q1.** Synonym of **ACUMEN**:
(a) Ignorance (b) Keen insight (c) Stupidity (d) Laziness
**Answer**: (b) Keen insight

**Q2.** Antonym of **BENEVOLENT**:
(a) Kind (b) Generous (c) Malevolent (d) Friendly
**Answer**: (c) Malevolent

**Q3.** "Bite the bullet" means:
(a) Run away (b) Face an unpleasant situation (c) Chew food (d) Fight
**Answer**: (b) Face an unpleasant situation

**Q4.** One who walks in sleep:
(a) Somniloquist (b) Somnambulist (c) Insomniac (d) Somnifer
**Answer**: (b) Somnambulist

**Q5.** Correct spelling:
(a) Accomodate (b) Accommodate (c) Acommodate (d) Acomodate
**Answer**: (b) Accommodate

**Q6.** Idiom "a blessing in disguise":
(a) Visible good (b) Hidden good (c) Clear bad (d) Hidden evil
**Answer**: (b) Something that initially seems bad but turns out good

**Q7.** Synonym of **MITIGATE**:
(a) Worsen (b) Lessen (c) Ignore (d) Increase
**Answer**: (b) Lessen

**Q8.** One who compiles a dictionary:
(a) Bibliophile (b) Lexicographer (c) Numismatist (d) Philatelist
**Answer**: (b) Lexicographer

**Q9.** "See eye to eye":
(a) Disagree (b) Agree (c) Stare (d) Look
**Answer**: (b) Agree

**Q10.** Correct spelling:
(a) Recieve (b) Receive (c) Reciece (d) Receeve
**Answer**: (b) Receive (i before e, except after c)

**Q11.** Antonym of **EPHEMERAL**:
(a) Short-lived (b) Brief (c) Eternal (d) Temporary
**Answer**: (c) Eternal

**Q12.** "Spill the beans":
(a) Cook mess (b) Reveal secret (c) Make mistake (d) Waste food
**Answer**: (b) Reveal secret

**Q13.** One who is afraid of heights:
(a) Agoraphobic (b) Acrophobic (c) Hydrophobic (d) Claustrophobic
**Answer**: (b) Acrophobic

**Q14.** Synonym of **TACITURN**:
(a) Talkative (b) Garrulous (c) Reserved (d) Loud
**Answer**: (c) Reserved

**Q15.** Correct spelling:
(a) Definately (b) Definitely (c) Definitly (d) Definately
**Answer**: (b) Definitely

**Q16.** "Raining cats and dogs":
(a) Light rain (b) No rain (c) Heavy rain (d) Sunshine
**Answer**: (c) Heavy rain

**Q17.** One word for "government by the wealthy":
(a) Democracy (b) Plutocracy (c) Aristocracy (d) Monarchy
**Answer**: (b) Plutocracy

**Q18.** Synonym of **CANDID**:
(a) Deceitful (b) Frank (c) Hidden (d) Secretive
**Answer**: (b) Frank

**Q19.** Antonym of **MAGNANIMOUS**:
(a) Generous (b) Noble (c) Petty (d) Kind
**Answer**: (c) Petty

**Q20.** "Burn the midnight oil":
(a) Light lamps (b) Sleep early (c) Work late (d) Take rest
**Answer**: (c) Work late
