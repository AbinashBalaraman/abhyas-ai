# Practice Set: Inequalities & Input-Output — SBI PO Prelims (Set 4)

**Pattern:** 10 Questions | 45 seconds each | Total 7.5 minutes
**Difficulty:** Easy-Medium (SBI PO Prelims Standard)

---

### Q1. Statements: A ≤ B < C. Conclusion: A < C?
(a) Yes (b) No (c) Cannot be determined (d) A = C

**Answer:** (a) Yes
**Solution:** A ≤ B < C → A < C ✓.
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

---

### Q2. Statements: P ≥ Q > R < S. Conclusion: P > S?
(a) Yes (b) No (c) Cannot be determined (d) P ≤ S

**Answer:** (c) Cannot be determined
**Solution:** P ≥ Q > R < S. Different segments.
**Time Target:** 30 sec |

---

### Q3. Statements: X = Y > Z = W < V. Conclusion: Y > V?
(a) Yes (b) No (c) Cannot be determined (d) Y = V

**Answer:** (c) Cannot be determined
**Solution:** X = Y > Z = W < V. Y and V in different segments.
**Time Target:** 35 sec |

---

### Q4. Coded: A & B = A = B; A $ B = A > B; A @ B = A < B. A & B $ C @ D means?
(a) A = B > C < D (b) A = B < C > D (c) A > B = C < D (d) A = B > C > D

**Answer:** (a) A = B > C < D
**Solution:** & = =, $ = >, @ = <. A = B > C < D.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

### Q5. Input-Output: 
Input: 73 19 56 84 32 47
Step I: 84 73 19 56 32 47
Step II: 84 73 56 19 32 47
Step III: 84 73 56 47 19 32
Step IV = ?
(a) 84 73 56 47 32 19 (b) 84 73 56 47 19 32 (c) 84 73 56 32 19 47 (d) 84 73 47 56 19 32

**Answer:** (a) 84 73 56 47 32 19
**Solution:** Descending order. 32 at position 5, 19 at position 6.
**Time Target:** 50 sec |

---

### Q6. Statements: A < B, B ≤ C, C > D, D = E. Conclusion: A < E?
(a) Yes (b) No (c) Cannot be determined (d) A = E

**Answer:** (c) Cannot be determined
**Solution:** A < B ≤ C > D = E. Different segments.
**Time Target:** 35 sec |

---

### Q7. Statements: P > Q = R, R ≥ S, S < T. Conclusion: P > T?
(a) Yes (b) No (c) Cannot be determined (d) P = T

**Answer:** (c) Cannot be determined
**Solution:** P > Q = R ≥ S < T. P and T in different segments.
**Time Target:** 30 sec |

---

### Q8. Coded: A # B = A ≤ B; A @ B = A > B. A # B @ C means?
(a) A ≤ B > C (b) A ≤ B < C (c) A ≥ B > C (d) A < B > C

**Answer:** (a) A ≤ B > C
**Solution:** # = ≤, @ = >. A ≤ B > C.
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

### Q9. Input-Output: 
Input: 65 28 91 14 73 49
Step I: 91 65 28 14 73 49

Step III: 91 73 65 49 28 14

(a) 91 73 65 49 28 14 (b) 91 73 65 49 14 28 (c) 91 73 49 65 28 14 (d) 91 65 73 49 28 14

**Answer:** (a) 91 73 65 49 28 14

**Solution:** A > B ≥ C = D > E → A > E ✓.
