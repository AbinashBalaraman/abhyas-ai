### Q1. Statements: A > B > C < D. Conclusion: A > D?
(a) Yes (b) No (c) Cannot be determined (d) A ≤ D

**Answer:** (c) Cannot be determined
**Solution:** A > B > C < D. A and D in different segments.
**Time Target:** 30 sec |

### Q2. Statements: P < Q = R > S. Conclusion: P < S?
(a) Yes (b) No (c) Cannot be determined (d) P = S

**Answer:** (c) Cannot be determined
**Solution:** P < Q = R > S. P and S in different segments.
**Time Target:** 30 sec |

### Q3. Statements: X ≥ Y = Z ≤ W. Conclusion: X ≥ W?
(a) Yes (b) No (c) Cannot be determined (d) X < W

**Answer:** (a) Yes
**Solution:** X ≥ Y = Z ≤ W → X ≥ W ✓.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

### Q4. Coded: A % B = A ≤ B; A & B = A > B. A % B & C means?
(a) A ≤ B > C (b) A ≤ B < C (c) A ≥ B > C (d) A < B > C

**Answer:** (a) A ≤ B > C
**Solution:** % = ≤, & = >. A ≤ B > C.
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

### Q5. Input-Output: 
Input: 41 25 78 13 56
Step I: 78 41 25 13 56
Step II: 78 56 41 25 13
Step III = ?
(a) 78 56 41 25 13 (b) 78 56 41 13 25 (c) 78 41 56 25 13 (d) 78 56 25 41 13

**Answer:** (a) 78 56 41 25 13
**Solution:** Largest first (78), then 2nd largest (56), then 41, then 25, then 13. Wait — Step II has 78 56 41 25 13. So Step III is the same? Or different logic.
**Time Target:** 35 sec |

### Q6. Statements: A > B, B ≥ C, C = D, D < E. Conclusion: A > E?
(a) Yes (b) No (c) Cannot be determined (d) A < E

**Answer:** (c) Cannot be determined
**Solution:** A > B ≥ C = D < E. A and E in different segments.

### Q7. Statements: P = Q, Q < R, R ≤ S, S > T. Conclusion: P < T?
(a) Yes (b) No (c) Cannot be determined (d) P = T

**Answer:** (c) Cannot be determined
**Solution:** P = Q < R ≤ S > T. Different segments.
**Time Target:** 30 sec |

### Q8. Coded: M # N = M < N; M @ N = M ≥ N. M # N @ P means?
(a) M < N ≥ P (b) M < N ≤ P (c) M > N ≥ P (d) M < N < P

**Answer:** (a) M < N ≥ P
**Solution:** # = <, @ = ≥. M < N ≥ P.
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

### Q9. Input-Output: 
Input: 18 92 47 31 65 24
Step I: 92 18 47 31 65 24
Step II: 92 65 18 47 31 24
Step III: 92 65 47 18 31 24
Step IV = ?
(a) 92 65 47 31 18 24 (b) 92 65 47 18 31 24 (c) 92 65 31 18 47 24 (d) 92 65 47 18 24 31

**Answer:** (a) 92 65 47 31 18 24
**Solution:** Descending order. After 92, 65, 47, next is 31 at position 4, then 24, then 18.
**Time Target:** 50 sec |
