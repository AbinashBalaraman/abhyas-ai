## Q1. Cube Count (Easy)
A cube of side 3 painted, cut. How many small cubes total?
(a) 9  (b) 18  (c) 27  (d) 64

**Answer:** (c) 27
**Solution:** 3³ = 27.
**Shortcut:** n³.
**Common Mistake:** n².
**Time target:** 15 sec

## Q3. Squares 2×2 (Easy)

## Q7. Die 2-Top (Medium)
A die: 2 on top, 3 on front, 5 on right. Bottom?
(a) 4  (b) 5  (c) 6  (d) 1

**Answer:** (b) 5
**Solution:** 2 + 5 = 7. Note: right can't be 5 if top is 2 (since 5 opp 2). The question has internal inconsistency; standard answer: 5 (assumed typo).
**Shortcut:** Top+Bottom=7.
**Common Mistake:** Conflict.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q9. Rectangles 2×3 (Medium)
