## Passage 1

The Indian monsoon is critical to the country's agriculture. About 70% of annual rainfall occurs during the monsoon season (June-September). A weak monsoon can lead to drought and crop failures, while an excess can cause flooding. Farmers across India closely watch the monsoon's progress, as their livelihoods depend on timely and adequate rainfall. The Indian Meteorological Department (IMD) issues forecasts each year, but the monsoon's complex patterns make accurate prediction challenging. Climate change is adding further uncertainty, with some scientists suggesting more erratic rainfall patterns in the future.

### Q1.
What percentage of India's annual rainfall occurs during the monsoon?
- (a) 50% (b) 60% (c) 70% (d) 80%

**Answer:** (c) 70%
**Solution:** "About 70% of annual rainfall occurs during the monsoon season" — directly stated.
**Strategy:** Factual/Detail question — find the EXACT line in the passage.
**Time Target:** 30 sec

---

### Q2.
What is the main idea of the passage?
- (a) Indian agriculture depends on the monsoon.
- (b) The monsoon is unpredictable.
- (c) Floods damage Indian crops.
- (d) Farmers are worried about climate change.

**Answer:** (a) Indian agriculture depends on the monsoon.
**Solution:** The passage discusses the monsoon's role in agriculture, with sub-points (drought, floods, forecasts, climate change). All sub-points support the central theme: agriculture's dependence on the monsoon.
**Strategy:** Main idea question — look at intro + conclusion + topic sentences.
**Time Target:** 60 sec

---

### Q3.
The word "erratic" in the passage most nearly means:
- (a) Predictable (b) Irregular (c) Heavy (d) Steady

**Answer:** (b) Irregular
**Solution:** "Erratic" = irregular, unpredictable. Context: "more erratic rainfall patterns" (climate change making rainfall less predictable).
**Strategy:** Vocabulary in context — find the word, read surrounding sentence.
**Time Target:** 30 sec

---

### Q4.
According to the passage, the IMD:
- (a) Always predicts the monsoon accurately.
- (b) Issues forecasts but prediction is challenging.
- (c) Was recently established.
- (d) Focuses only on rainfall.

**Answer:** (b) Issues forecasts but prediction is challenging.
**Solution:** "The Indian Meteorological Department (IMD) issues forecasts each year, but the monsoon's complex patterns make accurate prediction challenging" — both issuing forecasts and the challenge are mentioned.
**Strategy:** Factual question — locate the relevant sentence.
**Time Target:** 45 sec

### Q5.
It can be inferred that climate change will:
- (a) Improve agricultural yields.
- (b) Make the monsoon more predictable.
- (c) Add to existing challenges in monsoon prediction.
- (d) Have no impact on the monsoon.

**Answer:** (c) Add to existing challenges in monsoon prediction.
**Solution:** "Climate change is adding further uncertainty" — the word "further" implies adding to existing difficulty (already complex patterns).
**Strategy:** Inference question — extend the passage's logic.

## Passage 2

The concept of Universal Basic Income (UBI) has gained traction globally. UBI proposes that every adult citizen receive a fixed sum from the government, regardless of income or employment status. Proponents argue it can reduce poverty and provide a safety net in the face of automation-driven job losses. Critics, however, point to the enormous cost and the potential reduction in incentive to work. Pilot programs in countries like Finland and Kenya have shown mixed results, with some reporting improved well-being but others noting limited economic impact. The debate continues, with no clear consensus on whether UBI is a sustainable solution to modern economic challenges.

### Q6.
What is UBI?
- (a) Income for unemployed people only.
- (b) Fixed income for all adults.
- (c) Income based on need.
- (d) A loan from the government.

**Answer:** (b) Fixed income for all adults.
**Solution:** "UBI proposes that every adult citizen receive a fixed sum from the government, regardless of income or employment status."
**Strategy:** Factual question — locate the definition.
**Time Target:** 30 sec

**Answer:** (b) Reduce poverty and provide a safety net.
**Solution:** "Proponents argue it can reduce poverty and provide a safety net in the face of automation-driven job losses."

## Passage 3

The discovery of penicillin by Alexander Fleming in 1928 revolutionized medicine. Before antibiotics, even minor infections could be fatal. The widespread use of antibiotics has saved countless lives but has also led to the rise of antibiotic-resistant bacteria, often called "superbugs." This resistance develops through natural selection: bacteria that survive antibiotic exposure reproduce, passing on resistance genes. The medical community now faces a crisis where common infections may once again become deadly. Addressing this requires both new antibiotic development and more responsible prescribing practices to slow the emergence of resistance.

### Q11.
What is the central theme of the passage?
- (a) The discovery of penicillin.
- (b) The double-edged nature of antibiotics: life-saving but resistance-creating.
- (c) The danger of superbugs.
- (d) The importance of natural selection.

**Answer:** (b) The double-edged nature of antibiotics: life-saving but resistance-creating.
**Solution:** The passage begins with penicillin's benefits, then discusses the resistance problem. Both sides are covered.
**Strategy:** Main idea — capture the dual nature.
**Time Target:** 60 sec

---

### Q12.
The word "revolutionized" most nearly means:
- (a) Destroyed (b) Transformed (c) Continued (d) Questioned

**Answer:** (b) Transformed
**Solution:** "Revolutionized" = completely changed. Context: "Before antibiotics, even minor infections could be fatal" → after: lives saved.
**Strategy:** Vocabulary in context.
**Time Target:** 30 sec

**Solution:** "This resistance develops through natural selection: bacteria that survive antibiotic exposure reproduce, passing on resistance genes."

The author would most likely support:

- (b) Continued research into antibiotic resistance.
