## Q1. Mirror T (Easy)
Mirror of "T":
(a) T  (b) ⊥  (c) Different  (d) Cannot say

**Answer:** (a) T
**Solution:** T is symmetric in vertical mirror.
**Shortcut:** Symmetric.
**Common Mistake:** ⊥.
**Time target:** 5 sec

---

## Q2. Mirror V (Easy)
Mirror of "V":
(a) V  (b) ^  (c) Different  (d) Cannot say

**Answer:** (a) V
**Solution:** V is symmetric.
**Shortcut:** Symmetric.
**Common Mistake:** ^.
**Time target:** 5 sec

## Q3. Water 6 (Easy)
Water image of "6":
(a) 9  (b) 6  (c) Different  (d) 0

**Answer:** (a) 9
**Solution:** 6 upside down = 9.
**Shortcut:** Visualize.
**Common Mistake:** 6.
**Time target:** 5 sec

## Q4. Classification Numbers (Easy)
Odd one: 8, 27, 64, 100, 125
(a) 8  (b) 27  (c) 64  (d) 100

**Answer:** (d) 100
**Solution:** 100 not a perfect cube.
**Shortcut:** Check cubes.
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q5. Series Letters (Medium)
Series: A, C, F, J, ?
(a) N  (b) O  (c) P  (d) Q

**Answer:** (b) O
**Solution:** Differences: 2, 3, 4, 5. J+5 = O.
**Shortcut:** Increasing differences.
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q6. Series Numbers (Medium)
Series: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?
(a) 36  (b) 40  (c) 42  (d) 44

**Answer:** (c) 42
**Solution:** n² + n pattern. 6² + 6 = 42.
**Shortcut:** n(n+1).
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q7. Mirror Arrow (Medium)
Mirror of arrow → (right):
(a) →  (b) ←  (c) ↑  (d) ↓

**Answer:** (b) ←
**Solution:** Left-right flip → right becomes left.
**Shortcut:** Mirror.
**Common Mistake:** Vertical flip.
**Time target:** 5 sec

---

## Q8. Dice Standard (Medium)
Standard die, 6 on top. Bottom?
(a) 1  (b) 2  (c) 3  (d) 5

**Answer:** (a) 1
**Solution:** 1-6 opposite.
**Shortcut:** 1-6 pair.
**Time target:** 5 sec

## Q9. Painted 4×4 1-face (Medium)
4×4×4 cube painted, cut. Cubes with 1 face painted?
(a) 16  (b) 24  (c) 32  (d) 48

**Answer:** (b) 24
**Solution:** 6(4-2)² = 24.
**Shortcut:** 6(n-2)².
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q10. Triangles Star (Hard)
Triangles in 5-pointed star:

## Q11. Die Cube (Hard)
Die: 1 on top, 2 on front, 3 on right. Left?
(a) 4  (b) 5  (c) 6  (d) Cannot say

**Answer:** (a) 4
**Solution:** Left = opposite of 3 = 4.
**Shortcut:** 3-4 pair.
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q12. Rectangles 2×3 (Hard)
Rectangles in 2×3 grid:
(a) 12  (b) 15  (c) 18  (d) 24

**Answer:** (c) 18
**Solution:** C(3,2)×C(4,2) = 3×6 = 18.
**Shortcut:** C(m+1,2)×C(n+1,2).
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 60 sec
