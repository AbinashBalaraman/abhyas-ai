## Q1. Distance Formula (Easy)
Find the distance between (3, 4) and (0, 0).
(a) 3  (b) 4  (c) 5  (d) 7

**Answer:** (c) 5
**Solution:** $\sqrt{(3-0)^2 + (4-0)^2} = \sqrt{9+16} = \sqrt{25} = 5$
**Shortcut:** $\sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$ from origin.
**Common Mistake:** Just $x + y = 7$.
**Time target:** 20 sec

## Q2. Midpoint (Easy)
Find the midpoint of the line segment joining (2, 4) and (6, 8).
(a) (4, 6)  (b) (4, 4)  (c) (6, 6)  (d) (4, 8)

**Answer:** (a) (4, 6)
**Solution:** Midpoint = $(\frac{2+6}{2}, \frac{4+8}{2}) = (4, 6)$
**Shortcut:** Average of coordinates.
**Common Mistake:** Adding instead of averaging.
**Time target:** 20 sec

## Q3. Slope (Easy)
Find the slope of the line through (1, 2) and (4, 8).
(a) 1  (b) 2  (c) 3  (d) 4

**Answer:** (b) 2
**Solution:** $\frac{8-2}{4-1} = 6/3 = 2$
**Shortcut:** $\Delta y / \Delta x$.
**Common Mistake:** Reversing the order.
**Time target:** 20 sec

## Q4. Point-Slope Form (Easy — Trap)
Equation of the line with slope 2 passing through (3, 5)?
(a) $y = 2x - 1$  (b) $y = 2x + 1$  (c) $y = 2x - 5$  (d) $y = 2x + 5$

**Answer:** (a) $y = 2x - 1$
**Solution:** $5 = 2(3) + c \Rightarrow c = -1$. $y = 2x - 1$.
**Shortcut:** Substitute (3, 5) into $y = mx + c$.
**Common Mistake:** Using $c = 5$.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q5. Intercept Form (Medium)
Find the equation of the line with x-intercept 3 and y-intercept 4.
(a) $\frac{x}{3} + \frac{y}{4} = 1$  (b) $4x + 3y = 12$  (c) Both a and b  (d) None

**Answer:** (c) Both a and b
**Solution:** Both forms are equivalent.
**Shortcut:** $\frac{x}{a} + \frac{y}{b} = 1$.
**Common Mistake:** Picking only one form.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q6. Circle Equation (Medium)
Find the equation of the circle with center (2, 3) and radius 5.
(a) $x^2 + y^2 - 4x - 6y - 12 = 0$  (b) $x^2 + y^2 + 4x + 6y + 12 = 0$  (c) $x^2 + y^2 - 4x - 6y + 12 = 0$  (d) None

**Answer:** (a) $x^2 + y^2 - 4x - 6y - 12 = 0$
**Solution:** $(x-2)^2 + (y-3)^2 = 25 \Rightarrow x^2 - 4x + 4 + y^2 - 6y + 9 = 25 \Rightarrow x^2 + y^2 - 4x - 6y - 12 = 0$
**Shortcut:** Expand $(x-h)^2 + (y-k)^2 = r^2$.
**Common Mistake:** Sign errors in expansion.
**Time target:** 90 sec

## Q7. Center and Radius (Medium)
Find the center and radius of $x^2 + y^2 + 4x - 6y + 9 = 0$.
(a) (-2, 3), 2  (b) (2, -3), 2  (c) (-2, 3), 4  (d) None

**Answer:** (a) (-2, 3), 2
**Solution:** $g = 2, f = -3, c = 9$. Center = $(-g, -f) = (-2, 3)$. Radius = $\sqrt{4 + 9 - 9} = 2$.
**Shortcut:** $x^2 + y^2 + 2gx + 2fy + c = 0$ → center $(-g, -f)$, radius $\sqrt{g^2 + f^2 - c}$.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting to negate $g$ and $f$.
**Time target:** 90 sec

## Q8. Triangle Area (Medium)
Find the area of triangle with vertices (0, 0), (4, 0), (0, 3).
(a) 6  (b) 7  (c) 12  (d) 8

**Answer:** (a) 6
**Solution:** Right triangle. Area = $\frac{1}{2} \times 4 \times 3 = 6$.
**Shortcut:** $\frac{1}{2} \times$ base $\times$ height.
**Common Mistake:** Computing the determinant incorrectly.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q9. Centroid (Medium)
Find the centroid of triangle with vertices (2, 4), (6, 8), (10, 12).
(a) (6, 8)  (b) (4, 6)  (c) (8, 10)  (d) (5, 7)

**Answer:** (a) (6, 8)
**Solution:** $(\frac{2+6+10}{3}, \frac{4+8+12}{3}) = (6, 8)$
**Shortcut:** Average all vertices.
**Common Mistake:** Just averaging two.
**Time target:** 45 sec

## Q10. Perpendicular Lines (Medium)
Find the angle between lines $y = x$ and $y = -x$.
(a) 30°  (b) 45°  (c) 90°  (d) 60°

**Answer:** (c) 90°
**Solution:** Slopes 1 and -1, product = -1 → perpendicular.
**Shortcut:** $m_1 m_2 = -1$ → perpendicular.
**Common Mistake:** Confusing parallel/perp rules.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q11. Point-Line Distance (Hard)
Distance from point (1, 2) to line $3x + 4y + 5 = 0$.
(a) 3  (b) 4  (c) 16/5  (d) 5

**Answer:** (c) 16/5
**Solution:** $\frac{|3(1) + 4(2) + 5|}{\sqrt{9 + 16}} = \frac{16}{5}$
**Shortcut:** $d = \frac{|ax_0 + by_0 + c|}{\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}}$.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting absolute value.
**Time target:** 90 sec

## Q12. Section Formula (Hard)
Find the point dividing (2, 4) and (6, 8) in ratio 1:1 internally.
(a) (4, 6)  (b) (3, 5)  (c) (5, 7)  (d) (4, 8)

**Answer:** (a) (4, 6)
**Solution:** 1:1 ratio = midpoint = (4, 6).
**Shortcut:** Equal ratio = midpoint.
**Common Mistake:** Using section formula.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q13. Perpendicular Slope (Hard)
Find the slope of a line perpendicular to the line through (1, 1) and (2, 3).
(a) -1/2  (b) 2  (c) 1/2  (d) -2

**Answer:** (a) -1/2
**Solution:** Slope of given = (3-1)/(2-1) = 2. Perpendicular = -1/2.
**Shortcut:** Negative reciprocal.
**Common Mistake:** Using 2 (same slope).
**Time target:** 45 sec

---

## Q14. Collinear (Hard)
The points (1, 1), (2, 2), (3, k) are collinear. Find k.
(a) 2  (b) 3  (c) 4  (d) 5

**Answer:** (b) 3
**Solution:** Slope (1,1)-(2,2) = 1. Slope (2,2)-(3,k) = (k-2)/1 = 1 → k = 3.
**Shortcut:** Equal slopes.
**Common Mistake:** Using (1,1)-(3,k) slope.
**Time target:** 60 sec

---

## Q15. Parallel Line (Medium)
Find the equation of the line parallel to $y = 2x + 3$ passing through (1, 5).
(a) $y = 2x + 3$  (b) $y = 2x + 5$  (c) $y = 2x - 3$  (d) $y = 2x - 5$

**Answer:** (a) $y = 2x + 3$
**Solution:** Parallel = same slope = 2. $5 = 2(1) + c \Rightarrow c = 3$. So $y = 2x + 3$.
**Shortcut:** Same slope, find c.
**Common Mistake:** Confusing parallel with perpendicular.
**Time target:** 60 sec
