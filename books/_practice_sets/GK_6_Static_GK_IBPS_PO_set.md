### Q1. Param Vir Chakra is India's highest:
(a) Civilian award (b) Wartime military decoration (c) Peacetime military decoration (d) Sports award

**Answer: (b) Wartime military decoration**

**Solution:** The Param Vir Chakra is India's highest wartime military decoration. It is awarded for the most conspicuous bravery, self-sacrifice, or extreme devotion to duty in the presence of the enemy. The medal is bronze and the recipients get a replica plus a monthly pension of ₹10,000. The first recipient was Major Somnath Sharma (1947-48, Kashmir). 21 Param Vir Chakras have been awarded. The Ashoka Chakra is the highest peacetime military decoration. The Bharat Ratna is the highest civilian award.

### Q2. The "Father of Hockey" in India is:
(a) Dhyan Chand (b) Roop Singh (c) Balbir Singh Sr (d) Leslie Claudius

**Answer: (a) Dhyan Chand**

**Solution:** Major Dhyan Chand (1905-1979) is known as the "Father of Indian Hockey" or "Hockey Wizard". His birthday (29 August) is celebrated as National Sports Day in India. India won 8 Olympic gold medals in hockey (1928-1980) during this era. The Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award (renamed from Khel Ratna in 2021) is India's highest sporting honor. Other hockey legends: Roop Singh, Balbir Singh Sr, Leslie Claudius. The National Sports Awards include Arjuna Award, Dronacharya Award (for coaches), and Dhyan Chand Award (lifetime contribution).

### Q3. The Indian National Song "Vande Mataram" was composed by:
(a) Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay (b) Rabindranath Tagore (c) Mahatma Gandhi (d) Sarojini Naidu

**Answer: (a) Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay**

**Solution:** "Vande Mataram" was composed by Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay in the 1870s in his novel "Anandamath". It was adopted as the National Song of India on 24 January 1950. It is written in Sanskritized Bengali. Rabindranath Tagore composed "Jana Gana Mana" (the National Anthem). Mahatma Gandhi is not the composer of any national song. Sarojini Naidu is called the "Nightingale of India" and was a freedom fighter. The "Bharat Mata" painting was created by Abanindranath Tagore.

### Q4. The Magsaysay Award is known as:
(a) Nobel of the East (b) Asia's Nobel Prize (c) Oscar of Asia (d) Booker of Asia

**Answer: (b) Asia's Nobel Prize**

**Solution:** The Ramon Magsaysay Award is known as "Asia's Nobel Prize" and is given annually for outstanding contributions by Asian individuals. It was established in 1957. Indian recipients include Mother Teresa, Vinoba Bhave, Jayaprakash Narayan, Standard Reference Bedi, Arvind Kejriwal, and others. The award is named after Ramon Magsaysay, the former President of the Philippines. The "Nobel of the East" is not a standard term; the "Nobel of Literature" in Asia is sometimes used for the award. The "Nobel Prize of Asia" is the Magsaysay Award.

### Q5. The "Nightingale of India" is:
(a) M.S. Subbulakshmi (b) Lata Mangeshkar (c) Asha Bhosle (d) S. Janaki

**Answer: (b) Lata Mangeshkar**

Subbulakshmi was a Carnatic vocalist and Bharat Ratna recipient (1998). Asha Bhosle (1933-2026) was her sister and also a legendary playback singer (died 12 April 2026). S. Janaki is a renowned South Indian playback singer. Nightingale of India is also used for Sarojini Naidu (poet and freedom fighter).

### Q6. The ICC (International Cricket Council) was founded in:
(a) 1907 (b) 1909 (c) 1910 (d) 1920

**Answer: (b) 1909**

**Solution:** The ICC was founded on 15 June 1909 as the Imperial Cricket Conference. It was renamed the International Cricket Conference in 1965 and then the International Cricket Council in 1989. The ICC headquarters is in Dubai, UAE (since 2005; previously in Lord's, London). The current ICC Chairman is Jay Shah (since 1 December 2024). India is a founder member and BCCI (Board of Control for Cricket in India) was founded in 1928. India has 108 members (as of 2024).

### Q7. The Indian Premier League (IPL) was started in:
(a) 2006 (b) 2007 (c) 2008 (d) 2010

**Answer: (c) 2008**

**Solution:** The Indian Premier League (IPL) was started on 18 April 2008. It is a city-based franchise T20 league. The most successful teams are Chennai Super Kings (CSK) and Mumbai Indians (MI), each with 5 titles. The highest individual score is Chris Gayle (175* for RCB vs PWI, 2013). The highest team total is RCB (263/5 in 2013). The most runs scorer is Virat Kohli. The 2025 IPL was won by Royal Challengers Bengaluru (RCB) - their first title. Virat Kohli retired from Test cricket in May 2025.

### Q8. The Indian woman to first win an Olympic medal was:
(a) Mary Kom (b) Saina Nehwal (c) Karnam Malleswari (d) PV Sindhu

**Answer: (c) Karnam Melleswari**

**Solution:** Karnam Malleswari was the first Indian woman to win an Olympic medal — a bronze in weightlifting (69 kg category) at the 2000 Sydney Olympics. PV Sindhu won silver in 2016 Rio (badminton) and bronze in 2020 Tokyo (badminton). Mary Kom is a 6-time World Champion boxer (no Olympic medal). Saina Nehwal won bronze at 2012 London Olympics (badminton). The 2024 Paris Olympics saw several Indian women including Manu Bhaker (shooting) win medals. India won 7 medals at 2024 Paris Olympics.

### Q9. The "Hockey Wizards" of India won how many Olympic gold medals in field hockey?
(a) 5 (b) 6 (c) 8 (d) 10

**Answer: (c) 8**

**Solution:** India won 8 Olympic gold medals in field hockey (1928, 1932, 1936, 1948, 1952, 1956, 1964, 1980) - a record that lasted for decades. India was unbeaten in Olympic hockey for 30+ years. The Indian team was called the "Hockey Wizards" due to their dominance. The first Olympic gold in any sport for India was this hockey gold in 1928 Amsterdam. India has since struggled in Olympic hockey but won the 1975 Hockey World Cup and various Asian Games medals.

### Q10. The first Indian in space was:
(a) Kalpana Chawla (b) Rakesh Sharma (c) Sunita Williams (d) Shubhanshu Shukla

**Answer: (b) Rakesh Sharma**

**Solution:** Wing Commander Rakesh Sharma (born 1949) was the first Indian in space. He flew aboard the Soviet Soyuz T-11 on 3 April 1984. He spent 7 days in space and famously said "Saare Jahan Se Accha" when asked how India looked from space. Kalpana Chawla (1962-2003) was an Indian-American NASA astronaut who died in the Space Shuttle Columbia disaster. Sunita Williams (born 1965) is an Indian-American NASA astronaut. Shubhanshu Shukla (25 June 2025) became the second Indian in space, on Axiom Mission 4 to ISS.
