## Q10. Clock 5:30 (Medium)
Angle at 5:30?
(a) 0°  (b) 15°  (c) 30°  (d) 45°

**Answer:** (b) 15°
**Solution:** 30×5 - 5.5×30 = 150 - 165 = -15. 15°.
**Shortcut:** |30H-5.5M|.
**Common Mistake):** Other.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q14. Calendar Hard (Hard)
If 5 May 2023 was Friday, 5 May 2024?
(a) Friday  (b) Saturday  (c) Sunday  (d) Monday

**Answer:** (c) Sunday
**Solution:** 2024 is leap. 366 mod 7 = 2. Friday + 2 = Sunday.
**Shortcut:** +2 for leap.
**Common Mistake):** +1.
**Time target:** 60 sec
