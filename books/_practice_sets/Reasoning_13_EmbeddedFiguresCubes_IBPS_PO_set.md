## Q1. Cube Total (Easy)
A cube of side 4 painted, cut. Total small cubes?
(a) 16  (b) 32  (c) 64  (d) 256

**Answer:** (c) 64
**Solution:** 4³ = 64.
**Shortcut:** n³.
**Common Mistake:** n².
**Time target:** 15 sec

## Q4. Squares 3×3 (Easy)
Squares in 3×3 grid:
(a) 9  (b) 14  (c) 16  (d) 25

**Answer:** (b) 14
**Solution:** 3×4×7/6 = 14.
**Shortcut:** n(n+1)(2n+1)/6.
**Common Mistake:** n².
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q6. Painted 4×4 1-face (Medium)
4×4×4 cube painted, cut. Cubes with 1 face painted?
(a) 8  (b) 16  (c) 24  (d) 32

**Answer:** (c) 24
**Solution:** 6(4-2)² = 6×4 = 24.
**Shortcut:** 6(n-2)².
**Common Mistake):** Other.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q8. Rectangles 3×3 (Medium)
Rectangles in 3×3 grid:
(a) 9  (b) 18  (c) 27  (d) 36

**Answer:** (d) 36

## Q10. Triangles Sub (Hard)

## Q11. Painted 8×8 (Hard)

## Q12. Die Hard (Hard)
