# Practice Set: Analogy & Classification — SSC CGL Tier-I (Set 1)

**Source:** Reasoning Standard Reference Ch.1, Standard Reference Reasoning SSC Sec.1, Standard Reference Reasoning SSC Ch.4
**Pattern:** 15 Questions | 60 seconds each | Total 15 minutes
**Difficulty:** Easy-Medium (SSC CGL Tier-I Standard)

---

## Instructions
- Each question carries equal marks.
- Read the relationship carefully — direction matters.
- Most questions are factual recall or simple pattern completion.

---

### Q1. Japan : Yen :: China : ?
(a) Dollar (b) Yuan (c) Won (d) Peso

**Answer:** (b) Yuan
**Solution:** Country : Currency relationship. China uses Yuan (Renminbi).
**Shortcut:** Direct GK recall.
**Common Mistake:** Confusing Won (South Korea) with Yuan (China).
**Time Target:** 15 sec |

---

### Q2. Cat : Kitten :: Dog : ?
(a) Puppy (b) Cub (c) Calf (d) Fawn

**Answer:** (a) Puppy
**Solution:** Animal : Young-one. Cat→Kitten, Dog→Puppy.
**Common Mistake:** Picking "Cub" (lion/tiger young-one).
**Time Target:** 15 sec |

---

### Q3. Thermometer : Temperature :: Ammeter : ?
(a) Current (b) Voltage (c) Resistance (d) Pressure

**Answer:** (a) Current
**Solution:** Instrument : Measurement. Thermometer measures temperature; Ammeter measures current.
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

---

### Q4. Find odd: Tiger, Lion, Leopard, Wolf
(a) Tiger (b) Lion (c) Leopard (d) Wolf

**Answer:** (d) Wolf
**Solution:** Tiger, Lion, Leopard = big cats (Felidae). Wolf = canine (Canidae).
**Common Mistake:** Picking Lion as odd (assuming tiger is unique).
**Time Target:** 15 sec |

---

### Q5. Find next: 1, 4, 9, 16, ?
(a) 20 (b) 25 (c) 30 (d) 36

**Answer:** (b) 25
**Solution:** $n^2$: 1², 2², 3², 4² → 5² = 25.
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

---

### Q6. Fox : Cunning :: Rabbit : ?
(a) Courageous (b) Dangerous (c) Timid (d) Ferocious

**Answer:** (c) Timid
**Solution:** Animal behaviour trait. Fox = cunning, Rabbit = timid.
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

---

### Q7. Find odd: Copper, Iron, Zinc, Wood
(a) Copper (b) Iron (c) Zinc (d) Wood

**Answer:** (d) Wood
**Solution:** Copper, Iron, Zinc = metals. Wood is not.
**Time Target:** 10 sec |

---

### Q8. Find next: 2, 5, 8, 11, ?
(a) 13 (b) 14 (c) 15 (d) 16

**Answer:** (b) 14
**Solution:** Arithmetic series, +3 each term. 11+3 = 14.
**Time Target:** 15 sec |

---

### Q9. AZBY : CXDW :: EVFU : ?
(a) GHTI (b) GTHS (c) HTSI (d) GRTI

**Answer:** (a) GHTI
**Solution:** Opposite pairs (A-Z, B-Y, C-X, D-W, E-V, F-U, G-T, H-S). Next is GHTI.
**Shortcut:** Opposite letters sum to 27.
**Time Target:** 30 sec |

---

### Q10. Find odd: Sparrow, Crow, Eagle, Bat
(a) Sparrow (b) Crow (c) Eagle (d) Bat

**Answer:** (d) Bat
**Solution:** Sparrow, Crow, Eagle = birds (Aves). Bat = mammal (Mammalia).
**Common Mistake:** Bat can fly, students pick it as bird.
**Time Target:** 15 sec |

---

### Q11. Train : Passenger :: Aeroplane : ?
(a) Pilot (b) Aeronaut (c) Driver (d) Traveller

**Answer:** (b) Aeronaut
**Solution:** Mode of travel : traveller. Passenger travels by train; aeronaut travels by aeroplane.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

---

### Q12. Find odd: 4, 9, 16, 23
(a) 4 (b) 9 (c) 16 (d) 23

**Answer:** (d) 23
**Solution:** 4=2², 9=3², 16=4² (perfect squares). 23 is not.
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

---

### Q13. Find next: 1, 4, 11, 22, 37, ?
(a) 52 (b) 54 (c) 56 (d) 58

**Answer:** (c) 56
**Solution:** First differences: 3, 7, 11, 15. Second differences: 4, 4, 4 (constant). Next first diff = 19. 37+19 = 56.
**Shortcut:** Difference-of-difference → quadratic.
**Time Target:** 60 sec |

---

### Q14. Player : Team :: Minister : ?
(a) Council (b) Cabinet (c) Parliament (d) State

**Answer:** (b) Cabinet
**Solution:** Member : Group. Player in team; Minister in cabinet (council of ministers).
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

---

### Q15. Smile : Lips :: Wink : ?
(a) Eyes (b) Eyebrow (c) Nose (d) Mouth

**Answer:** (a) Eyes
**Solution:** Action : Body part. Smile uses lips; Wink uses eyes.
**Time Target:** 15 sec |
