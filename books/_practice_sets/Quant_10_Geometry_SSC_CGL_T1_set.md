## Q1. Sum of angles (Easy)
Sum of interior angles of a pentagon:
(a) 360°  (b) 540°  (c) 720°  (d) 900°

**Answer:** (b) 540°
**Solution:** (5−2)·180° = 540°. ✓
**Shortcut:** (n−2)·180°.
**Common Mistake:** Using (n+2)·180.
**Time target:** 20 sec

---

## Q2. Right triangle hypotenuse (Easy)
Right triangle legs 5, 12. Hypotenuse:
(a) 11  (b) 13  (c) 15  (d) 17

**Answer:** (b) 13
**Solution:** 5²+12² = 25+144 = 169 = 13². ✓
**Shortcut:** Pythagorean triple (5,12,13).
**Common Mistake:** Adding instead of squaring.
**Time target:** 20 sec

## Q3. Equilateral triangle area (Easy)
Equilateral triangle side 6. Area:
(a) 9√3  (b) 12√3  (c) 15√3  (d) 18√3

**Answer:** (a) 9√3
**Solution:** (√3/4)·36 = 9√3. ✓
**Shortcut:** Direct formula.
**Common Mistake:** Missing √3.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q4. Chord radius (Easy — Trap)
Chord of length 8 is at distance 3 from center. Radius:
(a) 4  (b) 5  (c) 6  (d) 7

**Answer:** (b) 5
**Solution:** r² = 3² + 4² = 25 → r = 5. ✓
**Shortcut:** Perpendicular from center bisects chord.
**Common Mistake:** Using 8 instead of 4.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q5. Inradius (Medium)
Triangle area 30, semiperimeter 10. Inradius:
(a) 2  (b) 3  (c) 4  (d) 5

**Answer:** (b) 3
**Solution:** r = Δ/s = 30/10 = 3. ✓
**Shortcut:** Direct formula.
**Common Mistake:** Multiplying instead of dividing.
**Time target:** 15 sec

## Q6. Triangle medians area (Medium)
Medians 6, 8, 10. Original triangle area:
(a) 32  (b) 36  (c) 40  (d) 48

**Answer:** (a) 32
**Solution:** Medians form right triangle (6²+8² = 100 = 10²). Area of medians triangle = ½·6·8 = 24. Original = (4/3)·24 = 32. ✓
**Shortcut:** Triangle with medians, then × 4/3.
**Common Mistake:** Multiplying by 3/4.
**Time target:** 90 sec

## Q7. Similar triangles (Medium)
Two similar triangles have sides in ratio 3:5. Area ratio:
(a) 9:25  (b) 3:5  (c) 6:10  (d) 27:125

**Answer:** (a) 9:25
**Solution:** Area ratio = (side ratio)². ✓
**Shortcut:** Square the side ratio.
**Common Mistake:** Using the side ratio.
**Time target:** 20 sec

---

## Q8. Rhombus area (Medium)
Rhombus diagonals 16, 12. Area:
(a) 96  (b) 48  (c) 192  (d) 64

**Answer:** (a) 96
**Solution:** (½)·16·12 = 96. ✓
**Shortcut:** (½)·d1·d2.
**Common Mistake:** Multiplying d1·d2 directly.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q9. Cyclic quadrilateral (Medium)
Cyclic quadrilateral opposite angles 70°, 110°. They are:
(a) Complementary  (b) Supplementary  (c) Equal  (d) Cannot say

**Answer:** (b) Supplementary
**Solution:** Opposite angles of cyclic quad sum to 180°. ✓
**Shortcut:** Property of cyclic quadrilateral.
**Common Mistake:** Confusing with parallelogram.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q10. Tangent from external (Medium)
From external point, tangent 12, secant PA=8. Find PB:
(a) 14  (b) 16  (c) 18  (d) 20

**Answer:** (c) 18
**Solution:** PT² = PA·PB → 144 = 8·PB → PB = 18. ✓
**Shortcut:** Power of a point.
**Common Mistake:** Setting up the formula.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q11. Inscribed square in circle (Medium)
Square inscribed in circle of radius 7. Side of square:
(a) 7  (b) 7√2  (c) 14  (d) 7√3

**Answer:** (b) 7√2
**Solution:** Diagonal = 14. Side = 14/√2 = 7√2. ✓
**Shortcut:** Diagonal = diameter.
**Common Mistake:** Setting side = radius.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q12. Parallelogram area (Medium)
Parallelogram sides 8, 12 with angle 30°. Area:
(a) 24  (b) 48  (c) 24√3  (d) 96

**Answer:** (b) 48
**Solution:** 8·12·sin 30° = 96·0.5 = 48. ✓
**Shortcut:** ab sin θ.
**Common Mistake:** Using sin 60°.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q13. Median to hypotenuse (Hard)
Median to hypotenuse of right triangle is 5. Hypotenuse:
(a) 5  (b) 8  (c) 10  (d) 12

**Answer:** (c) 10
**Solution:** Median = hypotenuse/2 → hypotenuse = 10. ✓
**Shortcut:** Right triangle property.
**Common Mistake:** Multiplying instead.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q14. Tangent + secant (Hard)
From point 13 cm from center, tangent 12 cm. Radius:
(a) 4  (b) 5  (c) 6  (d) 7

**Answer:** (b) 5
**Solution:** r² + 12² = 13² → r² = 25 → r = 5. ✓
**Shortcut:** Pythagoras.
**Common Mistake:** Setting up wrong triangle.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q15. Hexagon area (Hard — Trap)
Regular hexagon side 4. Area:
(a) 12√3  (b) 24√3  (c) 36√3  (d) 48√3

**Answer:** (b) 24√3
**Solution:** (3√3/2)·16 = 24√3. ✓
**Shortcut:** Hexagon area formula.
**Common Mistake:** Using 6·(√3/4)·a².
**Time target:** 60 sec
