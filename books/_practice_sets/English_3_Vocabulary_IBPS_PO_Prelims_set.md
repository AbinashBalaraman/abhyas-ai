### Q1.
"A place where bees are kept" is called:
- (a) Abattoir (b) Aviary (c) Apiary (d) Aquarium

**Answer:** (c) Apiary
**Solution:** Apiary = bee yard. Latin "apis" = bee. Abattoir = slaughterhouse. Aviary = bird place. Aquarium = fish place.
**Grammar Rule:** OWS — Place category.
**Common Mistake:** Confusing similar "place" words.
**Time Target:** 20 sec

---

### Q2.
"OBSTINATE" is most opposite to:
- (a) Stubborn (b) Flexible (c) Determined (d) Resolute

**Answer:** (b) Flexible
**Solution:** Obstinate = stubborn, unyielding. Opposite = flexible/cooperative.
**Grammar Rule:** Antonym — meaning opposite.
**Common Mistake:** Choosing "determined" (synonym-like).
**Time Target:** 20 sec

---

### Q3.
"EPHEMERAL" is most similar to:
- (a) Permanent (b) Short-lived (c) Ancient (d) Eternal

**Answer:** (b) Short-lived
**Solution:** Ephemeral = lasting a very short time. Permanent/eternal are antonyms.
**Grammar Rule:** Synonym — meaning match.
**Common Mistake:** Choosing antonym "permanent."
**Time Target:** 20 sec

---

### Q4.
"A study of ancient writing and inscriptions" is called:
- (a) Paleography (b) Paleontology (c) Philology (d) Philosophy

**Answer:** (a) Paleography
**Solution:** Paleography = study of ancient handwriting. Paleontology = study of fossils. Philology = study of language. Philosophy = love of wisdom.
**Grammar Rule:** OWS — Field of study.
**Common Mistake:** Confusing "paleo-" roots.
**Time Target:** 25 sec

---

### Q5.
Choose the correctly spelt word:
- (a) Definately (b) Definitely (c) Definately (d) Definitly

**Answer:** (b) Definitely
**Solution:** "Definitely" — single 'a', NOT "definately" (which is a common misspelling).
**Grammar Rule:** Spelling — root "definite" + -ly.
**Common Mistake:** Adding extra 'a' before 'te' (definately).
**Time Target:** 20 sec

---

### Q6.
"One who is all-powerful" is called:
- (a) Omnipotent (b) Omniscient (c) Omnipresent (d) Plenary

**Answer:** (a) Omnipotent
**Solution:** Omnipotent = all-powerful. Omniscient = all-knowing. Omnipresent = everywhere.
**Grammar Rule:** OWS — Person category.
**Common Mistake:** Confusing "potent/scient/present."
**Time Target:** 20 sec

---

### Q7.
"BONA FIDE" means:
- (a) In good faith (b) Bad faith (c) Forced (d) Optional

**Answer:** (a) In good faith
**Solution:** Bona fide (Latin) = genuine, in good faith. Antonym = mala fide.
**Grammar Rule:** Foreign word — Latin origin.
**Common Mistake:** Confusing with "mala fide."
**Time Target:** 20 sec

---

### Q8.
"A person who knows many languages" is called:
- (a) Polyglot (b) Bilingual (c) Linguist (d) Monolingual

**Answer:** (a) Polyglot
**Solution:** Polyglot = knows multiple languages. Bilingual = two languages. Linguist = studies language. Monolingual = one language.
**Grammar Rule:** OWS — Person category.
**Common Mistake:** Confusing polyglot with bilingual.
**Time Target:** 20 sec

---

### Q9.
"To bell the cat" means:
- (a) To delay unnecessarily (b) To take a difficult task upon oneself (c) To be the leader (d) To be noisy

**Answer:** (b) To take a difficult task upon oneself
**Solution:** "Bell the cat" = undertake a risky task that benefits the group.
**Grammar Rule:** Idiom — figurative meaning.
**Common Mistake:** Taking literally.
**Time Target:** 25 sec

---

### Q10.
"ABOLISH" is most opposite in meaning to:
- (a) Establish (b) End (c) Continue (d) Stop

**Answer:** (a) Establish
**Solution:** Abolish = formally end. Antonym = establish/begin.
**Grammar Rule:** Antonym — meaning opposite.
**Common Mistake:** Choosing "end" (which is similar in meaning).
**Time Target:** 20 sec

## IBPS PO Prelims Strategy

1. **OWS by category:** Group by type (Person/Place/Action).
2. **Root-based decoding:** "poly-" (many), "omni-" (all), "auto-" (self).
3. **Antonym vs. Synonym traps:** "Establish" (opposite of abolish) vs. "end" (similar to abolish).
4. **Spelling: pronunciation:** Pronounce mentally to identify double letters.
