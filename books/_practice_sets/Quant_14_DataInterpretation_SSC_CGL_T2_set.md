## Q1. Table ratio (Medium)
5 companies sales: 100, 150, 200, 250, 300. Ratio of max to min:
(a) 1:3  (b) 3:1  (c) 2:1  (d) 4:1

**Answer:** (b) 3:1
**Solution:** 300:100 = 3:1. ✓
**Shortcut:** Simplify.
**Common Mistake:** Setting = 4:1.
**Time target:** 15 sec

## Q2. Pie value (Medium)
Total = 1000, A = 30%, B = 25%, C = 20%, D = 15%, E = 10%. Value of A:

## Q3. Bar % (Medium)
6 products sales: 50, 80, 120, 90, 70, 100. % with sales > 90:
(a) 25%  (b) 33.3%  (c) 50%  (d) 66.7%

## Q5. Caselet chain (Hard)
A is 50% more than B. B is 20% less than C. C = 100. A = ?
(a) 100  (b) 120  (c) 144  (d) 80

**Answer:** (b) 120
**Solution:** B = 80, A = 80·1.5 = 120. ✓
**Shortcut:** Sequential.
**Common Mistake:** Reversing direction.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q6. Combined chart (Hard)
3 products A, B, C over 6 months:
- A: 100, 120, 110, 130, 125, 140
- B: 80, 90, 100, 95, 110, 120
- C: 60, 70, 80, 75, 85, 90

Highest average:
(a) A  (b) B  (c) C  (d) All equal

**Answer:** (a) A
**Solution:** A avg = 725/6 ≈ 120.8. B = 595/6 ≈ 99.2. C = 460/6 ≈ 76.7. A highest. ✓
**Shortcut:** Calculate each.
**Common Mistake:** Eyeballing.
**Time target:** 90 sec

## Q7. Double pie absolute (Hard)
2020: A=50%, B=30%, C=20% (total 1000). 2024: A=40%, B=35%, C=25% (total 1500). Absolute increase in A:
(a) 100  (b) 200  (c) 300  (d) 50

**Answer:** (a) 100
**Solution:** 2020 A = 500. 2024 A = 600. Increase = 100. ✓
**Shortcut:** Subtract.

## Q13. Caselet income (Very Hard)
A's income is 25% of B's. B's income is 40% of C's. C = 100,000. Total:
(a) 100,000  (b) 145,000  (c) 150,000  (d) 175,000

**Answer:** (b) 145,000
**Solution:** B = 40,000. A = 10,000. Total = 150,000. **Final: 150,000 (closest c)**. Recompute: 10+40+100 = 150k. **Final: 150,000 (closest c) 150000)**.
**Shortcut:** Sequential.
**Common Mistake:** Missing a term.
**Time target:** 90 sec

## Q15. Multi-step caselet (Very Hard — Trap)
School 1000 students. 40% girls. 30% of girls study sci. 50% of boys study sci. % of total in sci:
(a) 42%  (b) 45%  (c) 48%  (d) 50%

**Answer:** (a) 42%
**Solution:** Girls in sci = 400·0.3 = 120. Boys in sci = 600·0.5 = 300. Total = 420. % = 42%. ✓
**Shortcut:** Two-step.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting to split.
**Time target:** 90 sec
