### Q1. The 2025 ICC Champions Trophy was won by:
(a) Australia (b) India (c) New Zealand (d) England

**Answer: (b) India**

**Solution:** India won the 2025 ICC Champions Trophy on 9 March 2025 in Dubai — a record third Champions Trophy title (after 2002 shared with Sri Lanka and 2013). India also won the 2025 Women's Cricket World Cup on 2 November 2025 — the team's first-ever Women's CWC trophy. India won the 2026 T20 World Cup (3rd title). Recent Indian cricket captains: Rohit Sharma (white-ball, T20); the 2025 Champions Trophy was led by Rohit Sharma.

### Q2. The current RBI Governor (as of June 2026) is:
(a) Shaktikanta Das (b) Sanjay Malhotra (c) Raghuram Rajan (d) Urjit Patel

**Answer: (b) Sanjay Malhotra**

**Solution:** Sanjay Malhotra (IAS) is the current RBI Governor (since 11 December 2024). Previous recent Governors: Shaktikanta Das (2018-2024), Urjit Patel (2016-2018), Raghuram Rajan (2013-2016), D. Subbarao (2008-2013). RBI was established on 1 April 1935 under the RBI Act, 1934. The first Governor was Sir Osborne Smith; first Indian Governor was C.D. Deshmukh. The MPC (Monetary Policy Committee) is chaired by the RBI Governor. The inflation target is 4% CPI.

### Q3. The 2024 Paris Olympics saw India win how many medals?
(a) 3 (b) 5 (c) 7 (d) 10

**Answer: (c) 7**

**Solution:** India won 7 medals at the 2024 Paris Olympics (1 silver, 5 bronze + 1 mixed). The biggest highlight was Neeraj Chopra winning silver in Javelin (his 2nd Olympic medal after gold in 2020 Tokyo). Manu Bhaker won 2 bronze medals in shooting — a historic achievement for an Indian woman shooter. The 2020 Tokyo Olympics saw India win 7 medals (1 gold - Neeraj Chopra javelin; 2 silver - PV Sindhu badminton, Mirabai Chanu weightlifting, Ravi Kumar Dahiya wrestling; 3 bronze). India also won 29 medals at 2024 Paris Paralympics (7 gold).

### Q4. The current Chairperson of IRDAI is:
(a) T.L. Ramakrishna (b) Ajay Seth (c) Subhash Chandra Garg (d) Debasish Panda

**Answer: (b) Ajay Seth**

**Solution:** Ajay Seth (IAS) is the current Chairperson of IRDAI (as of July 2025). IRDAI was established in 1999 under the IRDA Act, 1999. Its headquarters is in Hyderabad (moved from Delhi in 2001). It regulates life insurance (24 companies) and general insurance (28 companies including ECGC and AIC) in India. The FDI limit in the insurance sector is 74% (raised in 2021 Budget). Recent changes: IRDAI removed age limit (65 years) for health insurance from 1 April 2024. LIC was nationalized on 19 January 1956.

### Q5. The "Bharat Ratna" was awarded to Zakir Hussain in:
(a) 2022 (b) 2023 (c) 2024 (d) 2025

### Q6. D. Gukesh became the World Chess Champion in:
(a) 2023 (b) December 2024 (c) 2025 (d) 2026

**Answer: (b) December 2024**

**Solution:** D. Gukesh became the World Chess Champion in December 2024 at age 18, defeating China's Ding Liren. He is the first Indian to win the Classical World Chess Championship since Viswanathan Anand (5-time champion). He is also the youngest World Chess Champion ever. The 2024 World Championship was held in Singapore. He was awarded the Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award in 2024. Divya Deshmukh (India) won the Women's Chess World Cup 2025.

### Q7. The "Kumbh Mela 2025" was held at:
(a) Haridwar (b) Prayagraj (c) Ujjain (d) Nashik

**Answer: (b) Prayagraj**

**Solution:** The Maha Kumbh Mela 2025 was held at Prayagraj (Allahabad) from 13 January to 26 February 2025. It was the largest religious gathering in the world, with over 400 crore visits. A tragic crowd crush on 29 January 2025 killed 30 people. The Kumbh Mela is held every 3 years rotationally at four locations: Prayagraj, Haridwar, Ujjain, and Nashik. It is the world's largest peaceful gathering of pilgrims. Hindus consider bathing at the Sangam (confluence of Ganga, Yamuna, and mythical Saraswati) during Kumbh to be highly auspicious.

**Time Target:** 30s | **Chapter Reference:** GK/07_Art_Culture_World_Geography.md, p.316, p.678 | **VERIFY:** No

### Q8. "Maratha Military Landscapes of India" was added to UNESCO WHS in:
(a) 2021 (b) 2023 (c) 2024 (d) 2025

**Answer: (d) 2025**

**Solution:** "Maratha Military Landscapes of India" (Maharashtra) was added to the UNESCO World Heritage List in 2025. With this, India has 43+ UNESCO WHS (as of 2025-26). Other recent inscriptions: Dholavira (Gujarat, 2021), Ramappa Temple (Telangana, 2021), Sacred Ensembles of the Hoysala (Karnataka, 2023). The first Indian UNESCO WHS were inscribed in 1983: Agra Fort, Ajanta Caves, Ellora Caves, and Taj Mahal. India has 18 biosphere reserves (Cold Desert in Ladakh designated in 2025).
