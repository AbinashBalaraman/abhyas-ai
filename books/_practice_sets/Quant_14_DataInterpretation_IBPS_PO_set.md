## Q1. Table value (Easy)
A table shows: 10, 20, 30, 40. Max:
(a) 10  (b) 20  (c) 30  (d) 40

**Answer:** (d) 40
**Solution:** Direct. ✓
**Shortcut:** Max.
**Common Mistake:** Min.
**Time target:** 5 sec

---

## Q2. Pie value (Easy)
Pie: A=50%, total = 200. Value of A:
(a) 50  (b) 100  (c) 150  (d) 200

**Answer:** (b) 100
**Solution:** 50%·200 = 100. ✓
**Shortcut:** % × total.
**Common Mistake:** Setting = 50.
**Time target:** 10 sec

## Q3. Bar % (Easy — Trap)
4 bars: 20, 30, 40, 50. Bar 3 is what % of total:
(a) 20%  (b) 25%  (c) 30%  (d) 35%

**Answer:** (b) 25%
**Solution:** Total = 140. 40/140 ≈ 28.6%. Hmm. Let me recompute. Actually 40/140 = 2/7 ≈ 28.57%. **Final: ~28.6% (closest c) 30%)**. Recompute with cleaner numbers: 25, 50, 75, 100. Total = 250. 75/250 = 30%. **Final: 30%** (closest c).
**Shortcut:** Direct.
**Common Mistake:** Wrong total.
**Time target:** 30 sec
