## Q17. Series Hard (Hard)
2, 12, 36, 80, 150, ?
(a) 210  (b) 252  (c) 270  (d) 300

**Answer:** (b) 252
**Solution:** n(n+1)(n+2)? 2×3×4=24. No. Try n²(n+1). 2×3=6. No. Try n³+? 8+4=12. 27+9=36. 64+16=80. 125+25=150. So n³+n². 6³+36 = 216+36=252.
**Shortcut:** Pattern.
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 90 sec
