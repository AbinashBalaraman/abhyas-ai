### Q1.
**Spot the error:** "The list of items **are** on the table."
- (a) The list (b) of items (c) are (d) on the table

**Answer:** (c) are
**Solution:** "The list" is the subject (singular) → "is." "Of items" is a prepositional phrase (modifier).
**Grammar Rule:** Subject of sentence = "list" (singular), not "items."
**Common Mistake:** Treating prepositional phrase as subject.
**Time Target:** 20 sec

---

### Q2.
**Spot the error:** "Everyone **are** ready for the meeting."
- (a) Everyone (b) are (c) ready (d) for the meeting

**Answer:** (b) are
**Solution:** "Everyone" is an indefinite pronoun → singular verb "is." Correct: "Everyone **is** ready."
**Grammar Rule:** Indefinite pronouns (everyone, somebody, each, anyone) → singular verb.
**Common Mistake:** Using plural verb with -one/-body pronouns.
**Time Target:** 15 sec

---

### Q3.
**Spot the error:** "Mathematics **are** my favorite subject."
- (a) Mathematics (b) are (c) my favorite (d) subject

**Answer:** (b) are
**Solution:** "Mathematics" is treated as singular in standard English (ends in -s but refers to a single field). Correct: "Mathematics **is** my favorite subject."
**Grammar Rule:** Subjects ending in -s but referring to a single field (mathematics, physics, news, economics) → singular verb.
**Common Mistake:** Plural verb for -ics words.
**Time Target:** 20 sec

---

### Q4.
**Spot the error:** "The team **are** playing well today."
- (a) The team (b) are (c) playing well (d) today

**Answer:** (b) are
**Solution:** "Team" is a collective noun → singular verb (British English). Correct: "The team **is** playing well."
**Grammar Rule:** Collective nouns (team, family, jury) → singular verb.
**Common Mistake:** Using plural verb with collective nouns.
**Time Target:** 20 sec

---

### Q5.
**Spot the error:** "**Many a** students were absent."
- (a) Many a (b) students (c) were (d) absent

**Answer:** (b) students
**Solution:** "Many a" + singular noun + singular verb. Correct: "**Many a student was** absent."
**Grammar Rule:** "Many a / More than one" + singular noun + singular verb.
**Common Mistake:** Treating "many a" as plural.
**Time Target:** 20 sec

---

### Q6.
**Spot the error:** "He **is** taller than me."
- (a) He (b) is (c) taller than (d) me

**Answer:** (d) me
**Solution:** Formal grammar requires "taller than I" (subject case after "than"). However, "than me" is widely accepted in informal English. In exams, (d) is often the answer in formal style. Note: Both forms appear in tests; check for the most formal option.
**Grammar Rule:** "Than" + subject case in formal grammar.
**Common Mistake:** Always defaulting to "I" (correct formally) without considering context.
**Time Target:** 30 sec

---

### Q7.
**Sentence Improvement:** "He **gave to the students** books."
- (a) gave the students books (b) gave books to the students (c) gave to students books (d) No improvement

**Answer:** (b) gave books to the students
**Solution:** "Give" typically takes indirect + direct object ("gave someone something"). "Gave to someone something" is awkward. The natural form is "gave books to the students" or "gave the students books."
**Grammar Rule:** Verb + indirect object + direct object OR Verb + direct object + to + indirect object.
**Common Mistake:** Putting "to" before indirect object when it comes first.
**Time Target:** 30 sec

---

### Q8.
**Spot the error:** "A number of students **is** volunteering for the event."
- (a) A number (b) of students (c) is volunteering (d) for the event

**Answer:** (c) is volunteering
**Solution:** "A number of" + plural noun → plural verb "are." Correct: "A number of students **are** volunteering."
**Grammar Rule:** "A number of" → plural; "The number of" → singular.
**Common Mistake:** Using singular with "a number of."
**Time Target:** 25 sec

---

### Q9.
**Spot the error:** "**The number of** applications **are** increasing."
- (a) The number (b) of applications (c) are (d) increasing

**Answer:** (c) are
**Solution:** "The number of" → singular verb "is." Correct: "The number of applications **is** increasing."
**Grammar Rule:** "The number of" + plural noun → singular verb.
**Common Mistake:** Using plural verb with "the number of."
**Time Target:** 25 sec

---

### Q10.
**Spot the error:** "**Each** of the boys **are** intelligent."
- (a) Each (b) of the boys (c) are (d) intelligent

**Answer:** (c) are
**Solution:** "Each of" + plural noun → singular verb "is." Correct: "Each of the boys **is** intelligent."
**Grammar Rule:** "Each of / Either of / Neither of" + plural noun → singular verb.
**Common Mistake:** Plural verb after "each of."
**Time Target:** 20 sec

## IBPS PO Prelims Strategy

1. **Find the head noun:** Ignore prepositional phrases; the subject is the noun before "of/in/on."
2. **Indefinite pronouns:** -one/-body/-thing → singular verb.
3. **"A/The number of":** Memorize the difference (plural vs. singular).
4. **Collective nouns:** Team, family, jury → singular verb (British standard).
