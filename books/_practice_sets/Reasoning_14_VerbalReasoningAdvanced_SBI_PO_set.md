## Q5. Clock 12:30 (Medium)
Angle at 12:30?
(a) 165°  (b) 180°  (c) 195°  (d) 360°

**Answer:** (a) 165°
**Solution:** 30×0 - 5.5×30 = -165. 165°.
**Shortcut:** |30H-5.5M|.
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q9. Clock 6:30 (Medium)

**Answer:** (b) 15°
**Solution:** 30×6 - 5.5×30 = 180 - 165 = 15°.

## Q10. Calendar 1 Jan 2025 (Medium)
1 Jan 2025 was:
(a) Monday  (b) Tuesday  (c) Wednesday  (d) Sunday

**Answer:** (c) Wednesday
**Solution:** 1 Jan 2024 = Monday. 2024 is leap, so +2 = Wednesday.
**Shortcut:** +2 for leap.
**Common Mistake:** +1.
**Time target:** 45 sec
