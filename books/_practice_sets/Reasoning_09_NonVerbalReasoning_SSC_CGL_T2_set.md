## Q1. Mirror "P" (Easy)
Mirror of "P":
(a) P  (b) q  (c) b  (d) d

**Answer:** (b) q
**Solution:** Mirror of P is q.
**Shortcut:** Visualize.
**Common Mistake:** Saying P.
**Time target:** 5 sec

## Q2. Water "b" (Easy)
Water image of "b":
(a) b  (b) p  (c) q  (d) d

**Answer:** (b) p
**Solution:** Top-bottom flip.
**Shortcut:** Water = vertical flip.
**Common Mistake:** Confusing with mirror.

## Q7. Painted Cube 4×4 (Medium)
4×4×4 cube painted, cut. Cubes with 2 faces painted?
(a) 8  (b) 12  (c) 16  (d) 24

**Answer:** (d) 24
**Solution:** $12(4-2) = 24$.
**Shortcut:** $12(n-2)$.
**Common Mistake):** 12 (corners).
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q8. Squares 3×3 (Medium)
Squares in 3×3 grid:
(a) 9  (b) 14  (c) 16  (d) 25

**Answer:** (b) 14
**Solution:** $3 \times 4 \times 7/6 = 14$.
**Shortcut:** $n(n+1)(2n+1)/6$.
**Common Mistake):** $n^2$.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q9. Rectangles 4×4 (Medium)
Rectangles in 4×4 grid:
(a) 64  (b) 100  (c) 120  (d) 144

**Answer:** (b) 100
**Solution:** $\binom{5}{2}^2 = 10^2 = 100$.
**Shortcut:** $C(m+1, 2)^2$.
**Common Mistake):** Just 16.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q10. Painted 5×5 1-face (Hard)
5×5×5 cube painted. Cubes with 1 face painted?
(a) 24  (b) 48  (c) 54  (d) 96

**Answer:** (c) 54
**Solution:** $6(5-2)^2 = 6 \times 9 = 54$.
**Shortcut:** $6(n-2)^2$.
**Common Mistake):** 48.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q11. Die Two Positions (Hard)
Die Pos 1: 1,2,3. Pos 2: 1,3,5. Opposite of 2 in Pos 1?
(a) 4  (b) 5  (c) 6  (d) 1

**Answer:** (c) 6
**Solution:** 2 adjacent to 1, 3 in Pos 1. In Pos 2, 5 adj to 1, 3 → 5 adj to 2 → 2 opp 6.
**Shortcut:** Three-rule for dice.
**Common Mistake):** Other.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q12. Triangles 6-Star (Hard)
Triangles in 6-pointed star (Star of David):

**Answer:** (b) 24
**Solution:** Star of David has 2 large + 6 small + others ≈ 24.
**Shortcut:** Approximation.
**Common Mistake):** Less.

## Q13. Cubes Painted 7×7 (Hard)
7×7×7 cube painted. Total painted (at least 1 face)?
(a) 218  (b) 296  (c) 343  (d) 125

**Answer:** (a) 218
**Solution:** Total = 7³ = 343. Interior = 5³ = 125. Painted = 218.
**Shortcut:** Total − interior.
**Common Mistake):** Just 343.
**Time target:** 60 sec

---

## Q14. Paper Folding Hard (Hard)
Paper folded 3 times, 2 holes cut. Unfolded:
(a) 4  (b) 6  (c) 8  (d) 16

**Answer:** (d) 16
**Solution:** $2 \times 2^3 = 16$.
**Shortcut:** Holes × 2^folds.
**Common Mistake):** Other.
**Time target:** 30 sec
