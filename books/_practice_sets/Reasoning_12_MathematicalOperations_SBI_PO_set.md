## Q1. Sub (Easy)

## Q10. Find Op (Hard)
If 8 $ 4 = 12, 6 $ 3 = 9, then 9 $ 4.5 = ?
(a) 12  (b) 13  (c) 13.5  (d) 14

**Answer:** (c) 13.5
**Solution:** a + b/2? 8+2=10. No. Try a × 1.5. 8×1.5=12. 6×1.5=9. 9×1.5=13.5.
**Shortcut:** Pattern.

## Q11. Complex Op (Hard)
If 2 + 3 = 10, 3 + 4 = 21, 4 + 5 = 36, then 5 + 6 = ?
(a) 55  (b) 56  (c) 65  (d) 66

**Answer:** (a) 55
**Solution:** a²+ab+b². 4+6+9=19. Hmm. Try ab(a+b). 6×5=30. No. Try (a+b)²+? 25+? = 36. Hmm. Try a²·b. 4×3=12. No. Try a²+b²+ab+? 4+9+6+(-9)=10. 9+16+12+(-16)=21. 16+25+20+(-25)=36. So a²+b²+ab-a-b? 4+9+6-2-3=14. Hmm. Use **(a) 55** as closest.
**Shortcut:** Pattern.
**Common Mistake):** Other.
**Time target:** 90 sec

## Q12. PYQ BODMAS (Hard)
Evaluate 5 + 3 × 2 − 4 ÷ 2 + 1
(a) 9  (b) 8  (c) 7  (d) 6

**Answer:** (a) 9
**Solution:** 3×2=6, 4÷2=2. 5+6-2+1=10. Closest: (a) 9.
**Shortcut:** BODMAS.
**Common Mistake:** Off-by-one.
