### Q1. He said, "I was reading when the bell rang." — Indirect:
(a) He said that he was reading when the bell rang
(b) He said that he had been reading when the bell rang
(c) He said that he is reading when the bell rang
(d) He said that he had read when the bell rang

**Answer:** (b) He said that he had been reading when the bell rang

**Solution:** **Past Continuous** (was reading) → **Past Perfect Continuous** (had been reading). When the main clause action is in past, the secondary action shifts to Past Perfect Continuous. [Source: §2.3 Tense Backshift Table, §3.5]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "was reading" — not applying backshift.

**Time Target:** 30 sec

---

### Q2. Active: "He gave me a book." — Passive (using "I" as subject):
(a) A book was given to me by him
(b) I was given a book by him
(c) Both (a) and (b) are correct
(d) I was given a book from him

**Answer:** (c) Both (a) and (b) are correct

**Solution:** With **two objects** (direct: book; indirect: me), either object can become the subject in passive. [Source: §3.6 Voice with Two Objects]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing only (a) or only (b) — both are grammatically valid.

**Time Target:** 30 sec

---

### Q3. He said, "Will you help me?" — Indirect:
(a) He asked if I will help him
(b) He asked if I would help him
(c) He asked whether I would help him
(d) Both (b) and (c)

**Answer:** (d) Both (b) and (c)

**Solution:** **Interrogative with future**:
- Reporting verb: "asked" (for questions)
- Use "**if**" OR "**whether**" (both are correct)
- "Will" → "**would**" (modal backshift)
- "You/me" → "I/him" (pronoun shift)

[Source: §2.4 Modal Backshift, §2.6]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "will" — failing to backshift modal.

**Time Target:** 25 sec

---

### Q4. Active: "The cat killed the mouse." — Passive:
(a) The cat was killed by the mouse
(b) The mouse is killed by the cat
(c) The mouse was killed by the cat
(d) The mouse was being killed by the cat

**Answer:** (c) The mouse was killed by the cat

**Solution:** **Past Simple** (killed) → **Past Simple Passive** (was + V3). "The mouse" (object) becomes subject. [Source: §3.3]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "cat was killed" — confusing subject and object.

**Time Target:** 15 sec

---

### Q5. He said, "I could help you if I were there." — Indirect:
(a) He said that he could help me if he was there
(b) He said that he could help me if he were there
(c) He said that he can help me if he were there
(d) He said that he could helped me if he were there

**Answer:** (b) He said that he could help me if he were there

**Solution:** **Modals in past (could, might, would, should, ought to, used to) do NOT backshift** — they remain unchanged. Subjunctive "were" stays as "were". [Source: §2.4 — "would, could, might, should, ought to, used to" → NO CHANGE]

**Common Mistake:** Changing "could" to "can" (option c) — already-past modals don't backshift.

**Time Target:** 30 sec

---

### Q6. Active: "She must submit the report." — Passive:
(a) The report must submit by her
(b) The report must be submitted by her
(c) The report is submitted by her
(d) The report must be submitted by she

**Answer:** (b) The report must be submitted by her

**Solution:** **Modal + passive** = "**must + be + V3**". [Source: §3.5 Voice with Modals]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "must submit" (active form) or (c) "is submitted" (loses modal).

**Time Target:** 15 sec

---

### Q7. He said to me, "Where do you live?" — Indirect:
(a) He asked me where I live
(b) He asked me where I lived
(c) He asked me where did I live
(d) He asked me that where I lived

**Answer:** (b) He asked me where I lived

**Solution:** **Interrogative (wh-question) conversion**:
- Reporting verb: "asked"
- "Where do you live?" → "**where I lived**" (no "if/whether" for wh-questions)
- Reverse the question structure
- Tense backshift: "do live" → "lived"

[Source: §2.6 Question Conversion]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (c) "did I live" — keeping the question structure (should be statement form).

**Time Target:** 25 sec

### Q8. Active: "They have built a new bridge." — Passive:
(a) A new bridge is built by them
(b) A new bridge was built by them
(c) A new bridge has been built by them
(d) A new bridge is being built by them

**Answer:** (c) A new bridge has been built by them

**Solution:** **Present Perfect** (have built) → **Present Perfect Passive** (have been + V3). [Source: §3.3]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (b) "was built" (Past Simple Passive — wrong aspect).

**Time Target:** 15 sec

### Q9. She said, "I have been waiting for two hours." — Indirect:
(a) She said that she has been waiting for two hours
(b) She said that she had been waiting for two hours
(c) She said that she had been waiting for two hours
(d) She said that she was waiting for two hours

**Answer:** (c) She said that she had been waiting for two hours

**Solution:** **Present Perfect Continuous** (have been waiting) → **Past Perfect Continuous** (had been waiting). Tense backshift. [Source: §2.3 Tense Backshift Table]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "has been waiting" — not applying backshift.

**Time Target:** 20 sec

### Q10. Active: "We should respect our elders." — Passive:
(a) Our elders should be respected by us
(b) Our elders should respect by us
(c) Our elders should respected by us
(d) Our elders are respected by us

**Answer:** (a) Our elders should be respected by us

**Solution:** **Modal (should) + passive** = "**should + be + V3**". [Source: §3.5]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (c) "should respected" — missing "be".

**Time Target:** 15 sec

### Q11. He said, "I may go there tomorrow." — Indirect:
(a) He said that he may go there the next day
(b) He said that he might go there the next day
(c) He said that he might go there tomorrow
(d) He said that he goes there the next day

**Answer:** (b) He said that he might go there the next day

**Solution:** **Three changes:**
1. "**may**" → "**might**" (modal backshift)
2. "**I**" → "**he**" (pronoun shift)
3. "**tomorrow**" → "**the next day**" (time shift)

[Source: §2.4 Modal Backshift, §2.5]

**Common Mistake:** Keeping "may" unchanged (option a) — past reporting verb requires modal backshift.

**Time Target:** 25 sec

---

### Q12. Active: "Someone stole my wallet." — Passive:
(a) My wallet is stolen by someone
(b) My wallet was stolen by someone
(c) My wallet has been stolen by someone
(d) My wallet was being stolen by someone

**Answer:** (b) My wallet was stolen by someone

**Solution:** **Past Simple** (stole) → **Past Simple Passive** (was + V3). [Source: §3.3]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (c) "has been stolen" (Present Perfect — wrong tense).

**Time Target:** 15 sec

---

### Q13. He said, "I shall return tomorrow." — Indirect:
(a) He said that he will return the next day
(b) He said that he shall return the next day
(c) He said that he should return the next day
(d) He said that he would return the next day

**Answer:** (d) He said that he would return the next day

**Solution:** "**Shall**" (with I/we for future) → "**would**" in indirect speech. "Tomorrow" → "the next day". [Source: §2.4 Modal Backshift, §2.5]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "will" — failing to backshift.

**Time Target:** 25 sec

---

### Q14. Active: "Open the door." — Passive:
(a) The door is opened
(b) The door should be opened / Let the door be opened
(c) The door is being opened
(d) The door was opened

**Answer:** (b) The door should be opened / Let the door be opened

**Solution:** **Imperative** (command) → **Passive**: "**Let + object + be + V3**" or "**Object + should be + V3**". [Source: §3.4 Special Cases — Imperative]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "is opened" — wrong for imperative.

**Time Target:** 25 sec

---

### Q15. He said to her, "Are you feeling well?" — Indirect:
(a) He asked her if she is feeling well
(b) He asked her if she was feeling well
(c) He asked her whether she was feeling well
(d) Both (b) and (c)

**Answer:** (d) Both (b) and (c)

**Solution:** **Interrogative conversion**:
- "said to" + question → "**asked**"
- "if" OR "**whether**" (both valid)
- "Are you" → "**she was**" (backshift + pronoun)

[Source: §2.6]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing only (b) — "whether" is also correct.

**Time Target:** 25 sec
