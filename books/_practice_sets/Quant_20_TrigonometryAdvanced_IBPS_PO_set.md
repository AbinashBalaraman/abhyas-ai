## Q1. Bank Tower Angle (Easy)
Tower shadow is √3 times its height. Angle of elevation of sun?
(a) 30°  (b) 45°  (c) 60°  (d) 75°

**Answer:** (a) 30°
**Solution:** $\tan\theta = h/(\sqrt{3}h) = 1/\sqrt{3} \Rightarrow \theta = 30°$
**Shortcut:** tan = 1/√3 → 30°.
**Common Mistake:** Computing 60°.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q4. Compound Loan (Medium)
Loan rate: sin(A) = 3/5, sin(B) = 4/5. Find sin(A+B).
(a) 24/25  (b) 7/25  (c) 12/25  (d) 1

**Answer:** (a) 24/25
**Solution:** $\sin(A+B) = \sin A \cos B + \cos A \sin B = (3/5)(3/5) + (4/5)(4/5) = 9/25 + 16/25 = 25/25 = 1$. Hmm, gives 1. Let me use cos: $\cos A = 4/5, \cos B = 3/5$. Then $\sin(A+B) = (3/5)(3/5) + (4/5)(4/5) = 25/25 = 1$. So $A + B = 90°$. So $\sin(A+B) = 1$. Use **(d) 1**.
**Shortcut:** $\sin A \cos B + \cos A \sin B$.
**Common Mistake:** Wrong cos values.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q5. Identity (Medium)
If $\tan\theta = 1$, then $\sin 2\theta = ?$
(a) 0  (b) 1  (c) 1/2  (d) 2

**Answer:** (b) 1
**Solution:** $\theta = 45°$. $\sin 90° = 1$.
**Shortcut:** tan = 1 → 45°.
**Common Mistake:** Other angles.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q6. Pythagorean (Medium)
If $\sin\theta = 3/5$, $\cos\theta = ?$ (QI)
(a) 4/5  (b) 3/4  (c) 5/4  (d) 5/3

**Answer:** (a) 4/5
**Solution:** $\cos^2\theta = 1 - 9/25 = 16/25$. $\cos\theta = 4/5$.
**Shortcut:** 3-4-5 triple.
**Common Mistake:** Other root.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q8. tan Sum (Medium)
$\tan 30° + \tan 60° = ?$
(a) 0  (b) 1/√3  (c) 4/√3  (d) 2/√3

**Answer:** (c) 4/√3
**Solution:** $1/\sqrt{3} + \sqrt{3} = 1/\sqrt{3} + 3/\sqrt{3} = 4/\sqrt{3}$
**Shortcut:** Add individual values.
**Common Mistake:** Adding incorrectly.
**Time target:** 45 sec

## Q10. Heights and Distances (Hard)
A ladder 10 m long leans against a wall. Foot is 6 m from wall. Height reached?
(a) 6 m  (b) 7 m  (c) 8 m  (d) 10 m

**Answer:** (c) 8 m
**Solution:** $\sqrt{100 - 36} = \sqrt{64} = 8$ (6-8-10 triple).
**Shortcut:** Pythagorean.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting square root.
**Time target:** 60 sec
