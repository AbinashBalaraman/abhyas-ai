# Practice Set: Analogy & Classification — SBI PO Prelims (Set 4)

**Source:** Reasoning Standard Reference Ch.1, Standard Reference Reasoning 8th, Standard Reference Reasoning SSC Sec.1
**Pattern:** 10 Questions | 45 seconds each | Total 7.5 minutes
**Difficulty:** Easy-Medium (SBI PO Prelims Standard)

---

### Q1. Pen : Writer :: Scalpel : ?
(a) Butcher (b) Surgeon (c) Doctor (d) Nurse

**Answer:** (b) Surgeon
**Solution:** Tool : Primary User. Pen is primarily used by writer; Scalpel by surgeon.
**Time Target:** 15 sec |

---

### Q2. Find odd: 2, 4, 8, 16, 24
(a) 2 (b) 4 (c) 8 (d) 24

**Answer:** (d) 24
**Solution:** 2, 4, 8, 16 are powers of 2 (2¹, 2², 2³, 2⁴). 24 is not.
**Time Target:** 15 sec |

---

### Q3. Author : Book :: Composer : ?
(a) Music (b) Song (c) Singer (d) Instrument

**Answer:** (b) Song
**Solution:** Worker : Product. Author produces book; Composer produces song.
**Time Target:** 15 sec |

---

### Q4. Find next: 1, 3, 6, 10, 15, ?
(a) 18 (b) 20 (c) 21 (d) 22

**Answer:** (c) 21
**Solution:** Triangular numbers. Differences: 2, 3, 4, 5, 6. 15+6 = 21.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

---

### Q5. Cow : Milk :: Hen : ?
(a) Egg (b) Chicken (c) Feather (d) Cluck

**Answer:** (a) Egg
**Solution:** Animal : Product. Cow produces milk; Hen produces egg.
**Time Target:** 15 sec |

---

### Q6. Find odd: Rose, Lily, Sunflower, Lotus
(a) Rose (b) Lily (c) Sunflower (d) Lotus

**Answer:** (c) Sunflower
**Solution:** Rose, Lily, Lotus grow in water/wet conditions; Sunflower grows on land. Actually, all are flowers. **Better answer:** (c) Sunflower is the only non-water flower.
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

---

### Q7. 7 : 50 :: 5 : ?
(a) 26 (b) 30 (c) 36 (d) 40

**Answer:** (a) 26
**Solution:** $n^2 + 1$: 7²+1=50; 5²+1=26.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

---

### Q8. Find odd: 121, 144, 169, 200
(a) 121 (b) 144 (c) 169 (d) 200

**Answer:** (d) 200
**Solution:** 121=11², 144=12², 169=13² (perfect squares). 200 is not.
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

---

### Q9. Branch : Tree :: Leaf : ?
(a) Branch (b) Plant (c) Flower (d) Root

**Answer:** (b) Plant
**Solution:** Part : Whole. Branch is part of tree; Leaf is part of plant.
**Time Target:** 15 sec |

---

### Q10. Find next: 2, 9, 28, 65, ?
(a) 100 (b) 120 (c) 126 (d) 144

**Answer:** (c) 126
**Solution:** $n^3 + 1$: 1³+1=2, 2³+1=9, 3³+1=28, 4³+1=65, 5³+1=126.
**Time Target:** 35 sec |
