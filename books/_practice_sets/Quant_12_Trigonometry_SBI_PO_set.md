## Q4. sin²45° (Medium)
(a) 0  (b) 1/2  (c) 1  (d) √2

**Answer:** (b) 1/2
**Solution:** sin 45° = 1/√2, sin² = 1/2. ✓
**Shortcut:** Direct.
**Common Mistake:** Setting = 1.

## Q5. sec 0° (Medium)

## Q8. From 30 m, angle 60° (Medium)
(a) 30  (b) 30/√3  (c) 30√3  (d) 60

**Answer:** (c) 30√3
**Solution:** h = 30·tan 60° = 30√3. ✓
**Shortcut:** d·tan θ.
**Common Mistake:** Setting = 30/√3.

## Q9. (1+cos θ)(1−cos θ) (Hard)
(a) 0  (b) 1  (c) sin²θ  (d) cos²θ

**Answer:** (c) sin²θ
**Solution:** 1 − cos²θ = sin²θ. ✓
**Shortcut:** Difference of squares.
**Common Mistake:** Setting = 1.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q10. Conditional + sin 2θ (Hard — Trap)
sinθ = 3/5, sin 2θ (acute):
(a) 24/25  (b) 6/25  (c) 7/25  (d) 18/25

**Answer:** (a) 24/25
**Solution:** cos = 4/5. sin 2θ = 2·3/5·4/5 = 24/25. ✓
**Shortcut:** 2 sin cos.
**Common Mistake:** Setting = 6/25.
**Time target:** 30 sec
