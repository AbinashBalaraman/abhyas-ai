## Q1. Between (Easy)
A is 5th from left. B is 8th from left. How many between?
(a) 2  (b) 3  (c) 4  (d) 5

**Answer:** (a) 2
**Solution:** 8 - 5 - 1 = 2.
**Shortcut:** Higher - lower - 1.
**Common Mistake:** Just difference.
**Time target:** 15 sec

---

## Q2. Total in Row (Easy)
A is 7th from left and 10th from right. Total?
(a) 15  (b) 16  (c) 17  (d) 18

**Answer:** (b) 16
**Solution:** 7 + 10 - 1 = 16.
**Shortcut:** Left + Right - 1.
**Common Mistake:** +1.
**Time target:** 15 sec

---

## Q3. Right Turn (Easy)
Facing N, turn right. Now facing?
(a) N  (b) E  (c) S  (d) W

**Answer:** (b) E
**Solution:** N→E (right turn).
**Shortcut:** N→E→S→W.
**Common Mistake:** Left turn.
**Time target:** 5 sec

---

## Q4. Distance (Easy)
A walks 3 km N, 4 km E. Direct distance?
(a) 5 km  (b) 6 km  (c) 7 km  (d) 12 km

**Answer:** (a) 5 km
**Solution:** √(9+16) = 5 (3-4-5).
**Shortcut:** Pythagorean.
**Common Mistake:** 7.
**Time target:** 20 sec

---

## Q5. Shadow (Easy)
Sun in East. Where is shadow?
(a) East  (b) West  (c) N  (d) S

**Answer:** (b) West
**Solution:** Shadow opposite to sun.
**Shortcut:** Opposite.
**Common Mistake:** Same direction.
**Time target:** 5 sec

## Q6. Top+Bottom (Medium)
A is 15th from top, B is 8th from bottom. 3 between. Total?
(a) 23  (b) 25  (c) 26  (d) 28

**Answer:** (c) 26
**Solution:** 15 + 8 + 3 = 26.
**Shortcut:** Top + Bottom + Between.
**Common Mistake):** Top + Bottom only.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q7. Multi-Turns (Medium)
Facing N, turn left, then right, then left. Now facing?
(a) N  (b) S  (c) E  (d) W

**Answer:** (d) W
**Solution:** N→W→N→W.
**Shortcut:** Track each turn.
**Common Mistake):** Other.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q8. Position Chain (Medium)
A is 4 km N of B. B is 3 km E of C. Where is A from C?
(a) NE  (b) NW  (c) SE  (d) SW

**Answer:** (a) NE
**Solution:** C → 3E → B → 4N → A. So A is NE of C.
**Shortcut:** Chain movements.
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q9. 5-12 Distance (Medium)
A is 5 km N, then 12 km E. Direct distance?
(a) 13 km  (b) 15 km  (c) 17 km  (d) 20 km

**Answer:** (a) 13 km
**Solution:** √(25+144) = 13 (5-12-13).
**Shortcut:** Pythagorean triple.
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 20 sec

## Q10. Total (Medium)
A is 10th from left, 15th from right. Total?
(a) 23  (b) 24  (c) 25  (d) 26

**Answer:** (b) 24
**Solution:** 10 + 15 - 1 = 24.
**Shortcut:** Left + Right - 1.
**Common Mistake:** 25.
**Time target:** 15 sec

---

## Q11. Between (Medium)
6 people in row. A is 3rd from left. B is 5th from right. How many between?
(a) 0  (b) 1  (c) 2  (d) Cannot determine

**Answer:** (a) 0
**Solution:** A is 3rd from left, so 4th from right (in 6-person row). B is 5th from right. Between: 5-4-1 = 0.
**Shortcut:** Convert to same direction.
**Common Mistake):** Other.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q12. 8-15 Distance (Medium)
A walks 8 km N, then 6 km W. How far from start?
(a) 10 km  (b) 12 km  (c) 14 km  (d) 20 km

**Answer:** (a) 10 km
**Solution:** √(64+36) = 10 (6-8-10).
**Shortcut:** 6-8-10.
**Common Mistake:** 12.
**Time target:** 20 sec

## Q13. Shadow NW (Medium)
Shadow points NW at noon. Where is sun?
(a) NW  (b) NE  (c) SE  (d) SW

**Answer:** (c) SE
**Solution:** Opposite.
**Shortcut:** Opposite.
**Common Mistake:** Same.

## Q14. Multi-Top (Hard)
A ranks 12th from top, 18th from bottom. B ranks 9th from top. Between A and B?
(a) 0  (b) 1  (c) 2  (d) 3

**Answer:** (c) 2
**Solution:** A is 12th, B is 9th. Between: 12-9-1 = 2.
**Shortcut:** Direct count.
**Common Mistake):** 1.
**Time target:** 45 sec

---

## Q15. Cancel Movements (Hard)
A walks 5 km N, 3 km E, 5 km S, 3 km W. Where from start?
(a) At start  (b) 2 km N  (c) 2 km E  (d) 2 km S

**Answer:** (a) At start
**Solution:** All cancel.
**Shortcut:** N+S=0, E+W=0.
**Common Mistake):** Other.
**Time target:** 30 sec
