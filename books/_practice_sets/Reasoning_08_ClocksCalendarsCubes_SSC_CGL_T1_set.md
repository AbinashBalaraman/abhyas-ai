## Q1. Clock Angle — 3:40 (Easy)
What is the angle between the hands of a clock at 3:40?
(a) 110°  (b) 120°  (c) 130°  (d) 140°

**Answer:** (c) 130°
**Solution:** Minute = 6×40 = 240°. Hour = 0.5×(60×3+40) = 110°. Diff = 240-110 = 130°.
**Shortcut:** Minute angle − Hour angle.
**Common Mistake:** Not adjusting hour for minutes.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q2. Clock Angle — 4:25 (Easy)
At 4:25, the angle between the hands is:
(a) 12.5°  (b) 15°  (c) 17.5°  (d) 20°

**Answer:** (c) 17.5°
**Solution:** Minute = 6×25 = 150°. Hour = 0.5×265 = 132.5°. Diff = 17.5°.
**Shortcut:** Direct.
**Common Mistake:** Using 4:00.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q3. Mirror Time (Easy)
What is the mirror image of 4:30?
(a) 7:30  (b) 8:30  (c) 6:30  (d) 5:30

## Q5. Dice — Top/Bottom (Easy)
A standard die shows 5 on top. What's on the bottom?
(a) 2  (b) 3  (c) 1  (d) 4

**Answer:** (a) 2
**Solution:** 5 + 2 = 7 (opposite faces).
**Shortcut:** Memorize: 1-6, 2-5, 3-4.

## Q6. Painted Cube 5×5 (Easy)
A 5×5×5 cube painted, then cut. How many 3-face painted?
(a) 8  (b) 12  (c) 27  (d) 64

**Answer:** (a) 8
**Solution:** 3-faced = corners = 8 (always).
**Shortcut:** Corners are always 8.
**Common Mistake:** Computing 12(n-2) (that's 2-faced).
**Time target:** 20 sec

## Q7. Clock Coincide (Medium)
When do the hands coincide between 5 and 6?
(a) 5:27 3/11  (b) 5:30  (c) 5:25  (d) 5:30 5/11

**Answer:** (a) 5:27 3/11
**Solution:** $\frac{60H}{11} = \frac{60 \times 5}{11} = 300/11 = 27\frac{3}{11}$ minutes.
**Shortcut:** 60H/11.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting the fraction.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q11. Painted Cube 6×6 (Medium)
6×6×6 cube painted, cut. Cubes with 1 face painted?
(a) 96  (b) 108  (c) 144  (d) 216

**Answer:** (a) 96
**Solution:** $6(6-2)^2 = 6 \times 16 = 96$.
**Shortcut:** $6(n-2)^2$.
**Common Mistake):** Forgetting square.

## Q13. Clock Gain (Hard)
A clock gains 5 min per hour. Set correctly at 12 noon. Time shown at actual 12 midnight?
(a) 1:00 am  (b) 12:50 am  (c) 1:05 am  (d) 12:55 am

**Answer:** (a) 1:00 am
**Solution:** 12 hours = 720 min. Clock shows 720 + 12×5 = 780 min = 13 hours = 1:00 am.
**Shortcut:** Actual time + gain.
**Common Mistake):** Forgetting gain.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q14. Painted Cube 8×8 (Hard)
8×8×8 cube painted, cut. Cubes with 1 face painted?
(a) 96  (b) 192  (c) 216  (d) 384

**Answer:** (c) 216
**Solution:** $6(8-2)^2 = 6 \times 36 = 216$.
**Shortcut:** $6(n-2)^2$.
**Common Mistake:** Using n².
**Time target:** 45 sec

## Q15. Calendar — 29 Feb 2000 (Hard)
Find the day of the week on 29 Feb 2000 (leap year).
(a) Monday  (b) Tuesday  (c) Wednesday  (d) Sunday

**Answer:** (b) Tuesday
**Solution:** 1 Jan 2000 = Saturday. Days to 29 Feb = 31+28 = 59. 59 mod 7 = 3. Saturday + 3 = Tuesday.
**Shortcut:** Odd day calculation.
**Common Mistake:** Wrong start.
**Time target:** 90 sec
