## Q1. Venn — Mammals (Easy)
Venn for "Mammals, Dogs, Cats":
(a) Two disjoint inside one larger  (b) Three disjoint  (c) Three overlapping  (d) All inside each other

**Answer:** (a) Two disjoint inside one larger
**Solution:** Dogs and Cats inside Mammals, but Dogs and Cats disjoint.
**Shortcut:** Real-world thinking.
**Common Mistake:** Other.

## Q9. Clock Coincide (Medium)
Hands coincide between 1 and 2?
(a) 1:05 5/11  (b) 1:10 10/11  (c) 1:00  (d) 1:15

**Answer:** (b) 1:10 10/11
**Solution:** 60×1/11 = 60/11 = 5 5/11. Wait, let me recompute: M = 60H/11 = 60/11 = 5 5/11. Hmm. For H=1, 60/11 = 5.45. So 1:05 5/11. Hmm. Let me use: M = 60H/11 = 60/11 = 5.45. So 1:05 5/11. Use **(a) 1:05 5/11**.
**Shortcut:** 60H/11.
**Common Mistake:** Calculation.
**Time target:** 60 sec
