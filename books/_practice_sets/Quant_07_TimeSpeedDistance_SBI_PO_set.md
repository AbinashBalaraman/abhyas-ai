## Q1. Basic (Easy)
450 km in 5 hrs. Speed:
(a) 80 km/h  (b) 90 km/h  (c) 100 km/h  (d) 70 km/h

**Answer:** (b) 90 km/h
**Solution:** S = 450/5 = 90. ✓
**Shortcut:** Direct.
**Common Mistake:** Wrong unit.
**Time target:** 15 sec

---

## Q2. Train (Easy)
Train 300 m at 60 km/h. Time to pass a pole:
(a) 15 sec  (b) 18 sec  (c) 20 sec  (d) 12 sec

**Answer:** (b) 18 sec
**Solution:** 60 km/h = 16.67 m/s. T = 300/16.67 = 18 sec. ✓
**Shortcut:** Convert and divide.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting conversion.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q3. Boat (Easy)
Boat 10 km/h, stream 2 km/h. Downstream speed:
(a) 8 km/h  (b) 12 km/h  (c) 5 km/h  (d) 10 km/h

**Answer:** (b) 12 km/h
**Solution:** v_d = 10 + 2 = 12. ✓
**Shortcut:** v_b + v_s.
**Common Mistake:** Subtracting.
**Time target:** 15 sec

---

## Q4. Average speed (Medium)
Equal time at 20 and 40 km/h. Average:
(a) 25 km/h  (b) 30 km/h  (c) 33.33 km/h  (d) 28 km/h

**Answer:** (b) 30 km/h
**Solution:** Equal time ⟹ arithmetic = (20+40)/2 = 30. ✓
**Shortcut:** Arithmetic mean for equal time.
**Common Mistake:** Harmonic (26.67).
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q5. Opposite (Medium)
A at 30 km/h, B at 20 km/h opposite, 200 km. Meet:
(a) 2 hrs  (b) 4 hrs  (c) 6 hrs  (d) 3 hrs

**Answer:** (b) 4 hrs
**Solution:** v_rel = 50. T = 200/50 = 4 hrs. ✓
**Shortcut:** Sum for opposite.
**Common Mistake:** Subtracting.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q6. Race (Medium)
A beats B by 25 m in 500 m race. A:B speed:
(a) 20:19  (b) 19:20  (c) 21:19  (d) 500:475

**Answer:** (a) 20:19
**Solution:** A:B = 500:475 = 20:19. ✓
**Shortcut:** D:(D−d).
**Common Mistake:** Reversing.
**Time target:** 15 sec

---

## Q7. Late departure (Medium)
A leaves 8 AM at 30 km/h. B leaves 8:30 AM at 40 km/h. B catches A at:
(a) 9:30 AM  (b) 10:00 AM  (c) 10:30 AM  (d) 11:00 AM

**Answer:** (c) 10:30 AM
**Solution:** A's head start by 8:30 = 15 km. v_rel = 10. T = 1.5 hr. B catches at 8:30 + 1.5 = 10:00 AM. **Answer: (b) 10:00 AM**.
**Final Answer: (b) 10:00 AM** (1.5 hrs after B starts)
**Shortcut:** Head start / v_rel.
**Common Mistake:** Time difference.
**Time target:** 60 sec

---

## Q8. Train overtake (Hard)
Train 100 m at 50 km/h. Man runs at 10 km/h same direction. Time for train to pass man:
(a) 9 sec  (b) 10 sec  (c) 12 sec  (d) 8 sec

**Answer:** (a) 9 sec
**Solution:** v_rel = 40 km/h = 11.11 m/s. T = 100/11.11 = 9 sec. ✓
**Shortcut:** Length / v_rel.
**Common Mistake:** Static.
**Time target:** 45 sec

---

## Q9. Boat round trip (Hard)
Boat 16 km/h, stream 4 km/h. Round trip 60 km each way. Time:
(a) 8 hrs  (b) 10 hrs  (c) 12 hrs  (d) 9 hrs

**Answer:** (b) 10 hrs
**Solution:** v_u = 12, v_d = 20. T = 60/12 + 60/20 = 5 + 3 = 8 hrs. **Answer: (a) 8 hrs**.
**Final Answer: (a) 8 hrs** (5+3)
**Shortcut:** Sum upstream and downstream times.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting different speeds.
**Time target:** 75 sec

## Q10. Multi-leg (Hard — Trap)
2 km in 1st min, 4 km in 2nd, 6 km in 3rd. Average speed per minute:
(a) 3 km/min  (b) 4 km/min  (c) 5 km/min  (d) 2 km/min

**Answer:** (b) 4 km/min
**Solution:** Total = 12 km. Total time = 3 min. Avg = 4 km/min. ✓
**Shortcut:** Total / total time.
**Common Mistake:** Arithmetic mean (4 — same here).
**Time target:** 30 sec
