# SSC CGL Tier 1 — Logical Deductions (Statement & Assumption, Conclusion, Course of Action, Cause & Effect)

> **Exam:** SSC CGL Tier 1 | **Time:** 15 min (60 sec/Q) | **Total:** 15 Qs
> **Difficulty:** 5 Easy, 7 Medium, 3 Hard | **Trap Qs:** 2
> **Source basis:** Ch. 10 Logical Deductions

## Q1. Assumption (Easy)
Statement: "Please use the staircase instead of the lift." Assumption:
(a) Lift is broken  (b) People use lifts  (c) Staircase is safer  (d) Lift is expensive

**Answer:** (b) People use lifts
**Solution:** Without people using lifts, no notice needed.
**Shortcut:** Necessity test.
**Common Mistake:** Staircase safer.
**Time target:** 20 sec

---

## Q2. Conclusion (Easy)
Statement: "Sales have increased by 20% this year." Conclusion:
(a) Company is profitable  (b) More products sold  (c) Quality improved  (d) Customers happier

**Answer:** (b) More products sold
**Solution:** Direct conclusion.
**Shortcut:** New info test.
**Common Mistake:** Profitable.
**Time target:** 15 sec

---

## Q3. Course of Action (Easy)
Statement: "Heavy traffic on the road." Action:
(a) Build a flyover  (b) Ban all vehicles  (c) Ignore  (d) Increase toll

**Answer:** (a) Build a flyover
**Solution:** Practical.
**Shortcut:** Practicality test.
**Common Mistake:** Ban all.
**Time target:** 20 sec

---

## Q4. Cause-Effect (Easy)
Cause: "Heavy rain." Effect:
(a) Drought  (b) Floods  (c) Heat wave  (d) Sunshine

**Answer:** (b) Floods
**Solution:** Heavy rain → floods.
**Shortcut:** Direct link.
**Common Mistake:** Drought.
**Time target:** 10 sec

---

## Q5. Assertion-Reason (Easy)
A: "Plants need water." R: "Water is essential for life."
(a) Both true, R explains A  (b) Both true, R doesn't explain  (c) A true, R false  (d) A false, R true

**Answer:** (b) Both true, R doesn't explain
**Solution:** R is too general.
**Shortcut:** Check if R specifically explains A.
**Common Mistake:** Marking (a).
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q6. Assumption (Medium)
Statement: "Save water, save life." Assumption:
(a) Water abundant  (b) Water scarce  (c) Water expensive  (d) Water free

**Answer:** (b) Water scarce
**Solution:** Implicit.
**Shortcut:** Necessity.
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 20 sec

## Q7. Conclusion (Medium)
Statement: "The bridge collapsed yesterday." Conclusion: (I) People injured. (II) Bridge was old.
(a) Only I  (b) Only II  (c) Both  (d) Neither

**Answer:** (d) Neither
**Solution:** Neither is definite.
**Shortcut:** Definite conclusion only.
**Common Mistake:** Marking Both.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q9. Cause (Medium)
Statement 1: "The shop was closed." Statement 2: "It was a national holiday."
(a) 1 cause, 2 effect  (b) 2 cause, 1 effect  (c) Independent  (d) Cannot say

**Answer:** (b) 2 cause, 1 effect
**Solution:** Holiday → shop closed.
**Shortcut:** Time order.
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 25 sec

## Q10. Assertion-Reason (Medium)
A: "India won the cricket match." R: "Indian team played well."
(a) Both true, R explains  (b) Both true, R doesn't explain  (c) A true, R false  (d) A false, R true

**Solution:** Winning implies playing well.
**Shortcut:** Check explanation.
**Common Mistake):** Other.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q11. Assumption (Medium)

## Q12. Conclusion (Medium)
Statement: "The train is running late." Conclusion:
(a) Passengers waiting  (b) Train will come  (c) Delay significant  (d) Train cancelled

## Q13. Conclusion Both (Hard)
Statement: "Despite heavy rain, the match continued." Conclusion: (I) Match important. (II) Ground had good drainage.
(a) Only I  (b) Only II  (c) Both  (d) Neither

**Answer:** (d) Neither
**Solution:** Neither is definite.
**Shortcut:** Definite only.

## Q14. Cause-Effect Multi (Hard)
Statement 1: "Petrol price increased." Statement 2: "Govt reduced fuel subsidies." Statement 3: "Crude oil prices increased globally." Most likely cause?
(a) 1  (b) 2  (c) 3  (d) Both 2 and 3

**Answer:** (d) Both 2 and 3
**Solution:** Both 2 and 3 affect price.
**Shortcut:** Direct causes.
**Common Mistake:** Only 2.
**Time target:** 60 sec

---

## Q15. Assertion-Reason (Hard)
A: "Smoking causes cancer." R: "Tobacco contains carcinogens."
(a) Both true, R explains  (b) Both true, R doesn't explain  (c) A true, R false  (d) A false, R true

**Answer:** (a) Both true, R explains
**Solution:** R is the mechanism.
**Shortcut:** Specific explanation.
**Common Mistake):** Other.
**Time target:** 30 sec
