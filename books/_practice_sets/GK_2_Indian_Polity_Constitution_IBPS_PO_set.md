### Q1. The Indian Constitution is the longest in the world. The original Constitution had how many Articles?
(a) 395 (b) 400 (c) 450 (d) 470

**Answer: (a) 395**

**Solution:** The original Indian Constitution (1950) had 395 Articles, 8 Schedules, and 22 Parts. Over the years through amendments, it has grown to 470+ Articles, 12 Schedules, and 25 Parts. The Constitution is the longest written constitution in the world. The 12 Schedules include: 1st (States/UTs), 2nd (Salaries), 3rd (Oaths), 4th (Rajya Sabha seats), 5th/6th (Tribal Areas), 7th (Three Lists), 8th (Languages), 9th (Land laws), 10th (Anti-defection), 11th (Panchayats), 12th (Municipalities).

### Q2. The "Socialist" and "Secular" words were added to the Preamble by which Amendment?
(a) 1st (b) 42nd (c) 44th (d) 86th

**Answer: (b) 42nd**

**Solution:** The 42nd Amendment (1976) added three words to the Preamble: "Socialist", "Secular", and "Integrity". Originally, the Preamble declared India as a "Sovereign Democratic Republic". After 1976, it became "Sovereign Socialist Secular Democratic Republic". The 42nd Amendment is called the "Mini-Constitution" because of its sweeping changes. Many of its provisions were reversed by the 44th Amendment (1978) during the Janata Party government.

### Q3. The Indian Constitution has how many Fundamental Rights currently?
(a) 6 (b) 7 (c) 8 (d) 5

**Answer: (a) 6**

**Solution:** Originally 7 Fundamental Rights were there. The Right to Property was removed as a Fundamental Right by the 44th Amendment (1978) and made a legal right under Article 300A. The remaining 6 Fundamental Rights are: (1) Right to Equality (Art. 14-18), (2) Right to Freedom (Art. 19-22), (3) Right against Exploitation (Art. 23-24), (4) Right to Freedom of Religion (Art. 25-28), (5) Cultural and Educational Rights (Art. 29-30), (6) Right to Constitutional Remedies (Art. 32).

**Time Target:** 30s | **Chapter Reference:** GK/02_Indian_Polity_Constitution.md, p.20-21 | **VERIFY:** No

### Q4. Fundamental Duties were added to the Constitution by the:
(a) 42nd Amendment (b) 44th Amendment (c) 52nd Amendment (d) 86th Amendment

**Answer: (a) 42nd Amendment**

**Solution:** Fundamental Duties (Article 51A, 10 duties) were added by the 42nd Amendment (1976) based on the recommendations of the Swaran Singh Committee. They are applicable only to citizens (not foreigners). Originally inspired by the USSR Constitution. The duties include: abide by the Constitution, cherish noble ideals, uphold sovereignty, defend the country, render national service, common brotherhood, preserve heritage, protect environment, develop scientific temper, safeguard public property, strive for excellence.

### Q5. The Rajya Sabha can have a maximum strength of:
(a) 245 (b) 250 (c) 252 (d) 260

**Answer: (b) 250**

**Solution:** The maximum strength of the Rajya Sabha is 250 members, of which 238 are elected by State Legislative Assemblies (using single transferable vote) and 12 are nominated by the President (from literature, science, art, social service). The Rajya Sabha is a permanent body (not subject to dissolution); 1/3 of its members retire every 2 years. The current strength is 245. The Lok Sabha's maximum strength is 552 (currently 543 elected + 2 Anglo-Indian nominated, though nomination was abolished by 104th Amendment).

**Time Target:** 30s | **Chapter Reference:** GK/02_Indian_Polity_Constitution.md, p.32 | **VERIFY:** No

### Q6. Which Schedule of the Constitution contains the list of recognized languages?
(a) 6th (b) 7th (c) 8th (d) 9th

**Answer: (c) 8th**

**Solution:** The 8th Schedule of the Constitution contains the list of 22 recognized languages. Originally 14 languages were included; the 92nd Amendment (2003) added Bodo, Dogri, Maithili, and Santhali. The 22 languages are: Assamese, Bengali, Bodo, Dogri, Gujarati, Hindi, Kannada, Kashmiri, Konkani, Maithili, Malayalam, Manipuri, Marathi, Nepali, Odia, Punjabi, Sanskrit, Santhali, Sindhi, Tamil, Telugu, Urdu. Hindi and English are the official languages of the Union (Article 343).

### Q7. The Election Commission of India is established under which Article?
(a) Article 280 (b) Article 312 (c) Article 324 (d) Article 338

**Answer: (c) Article 324**

**Solution:** The Election Commission is established under Article 324. It consists of a Chief Election Commissioner and 2 Election Commissioners (since 1993). They are appointed by the President. The Commission conducts elections to Parliament, State Legislatures, and the offices of President and Vice-President. Article 280 deals with the Finance Commission. Article 312 deals with All-India Services. Article 338 deals with the National Commission for SCs.

**Time Target:** 30s | **Chapter Reference:** GK/02_Indian_Polity_Constitution.md, p.18, p.36 | **VERIFY:** No

### Q8. The President's Rule in a state is imposed under which Article?
(a) Article 352 (b) Article 356 (c) Article 360 (d) Article 365

**Answer: (b) Article 356**

**Solution:** Article 356 deals with President's Rule in a state (state emergency) when the constitutional machinery fails. It cannot be imposed for more than 3 years (with conditions). Article 352 is National Emergency (war/external aggression/armed rebellion). Article 360 is Financial Emergency (never declared). Article 365 deals with the failure of state to comply with Union directions. President's Rule has been imposed many times, most notably in Manipur and various other states.

**Time Target:** 30s | **Chapter Reference:** GK/02_Indian_Polity_Constitution.md, p.22 | **VERIFY:** No

### Q9. The Supreme Court of India was established under which Article?
(a) Article 124 (b) Article 214 (c) Article 226 (d) Article 32

**Answer: (a) Article 124**

**Solution:** The Supreme Court of India is established under Article 124. It has 34 judges including the Chief Justice of India (CJI). The CJI and other judges are appointed by the President. The original jurisdiction is under Article 32, appellate jurisdiction under Articles 133-136, and advisory jurisdiction under Article 143. Article 214 deals with the establishment of High Courts. Article 226 provides writ jurisdiction to the High Courts. The first CJI was H.J. Kania (1950-51).

**Time Target:** 30s | **Chapter Reference:** GK/02_Indian_Polity_Constitution.md, p.35 | **VERIFY:** No

### Q10. The first President of India was:
(a) Dr. S. Radhakrishnan (b) Dr. Rajendra Prasad (c) C. Rajagopalachari (d) Jawaharlal Nehru

**Answer: (b) Dr. Rajendra Prasad**

**Solution:** Dr. Rajendra Prasad was the first President of India (1950-1962) and the longest-serving President (12 years). He was also the President of the Constituent Assembly. The first Vice-President was Dr. S. Radhakrishnan (1952-1962). The first PM was Jawaharlal Nehru (1947-1964), the longest-serving PM. The first Home Minister was Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel. The first woman President was Pratibha Patil (2007-2012).

**Time Target:** 30s | **Chapter Reference:** GK/02_Indian_Polity_Constitution.md, p.34, p.36 | **VERIFY:** No
