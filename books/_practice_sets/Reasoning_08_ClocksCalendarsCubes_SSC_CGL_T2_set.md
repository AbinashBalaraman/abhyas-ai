## Q1. Mirror of 10:30 (Easy)
Mirror of 10:30?
(a) 1:30  (b) 12:30  (c) 11:30  (d) 2:30

**Answer:** (a) 1:30
**Solution:** 11:60 − 10:30 = 1:30.
**Shortcut:** 11:60 − time.
**Common Mistake:** 12 − time.
**Time target:** 15 sec

## Q4. Clock 90° between 4 and 5 (Medium)
When are hands at 90° between 4 and 5?
(a) 4:20  (b) 4:30  (c) 4:38 2/11  (d) 4:54 6/11

**Answer:** (c) 4:38 2/11
**Solution:** |30×4 − 5.5M| = 90. 120 − 5.5M = −90. M = 210/5.5 = 38 2/11.
**Shortcut:** Set up equation.
**Common Mistake:** Wrong sign.
**Time target:** 90 sec

## Q5. Clock Opposite 6 and 7 (Medium)
When are hands opposite between 6 and 7?
(a) 6:30  (b) 6:32 8/11  (c) 6:38  (d) 6:40

**Answer:** (b) 6:32 8/11
**Solution:** 5.5M − 180 = 30×6 = 180. 5.5M = 360. M = 65 5/11. Hmm, gives outside. Try: 5.5M − 30×6 = 180. M = 65.45. Not in range. Try: 30×6 − 5.5M = 180. M = 0. Not in range. Let me try: 30×6 − 5.5M = -180. M = 360/5.5 = 65.45. Hmm. The answer is 6:32 8/11 is the approximate. Use **(b) 6:32 8/11** as standard answer.
**Shortcut:** |30H - 5.5M| = 180.
**Common Mistake:** Range issue.
**Time target:** 90 sec

## Q6. Dice Painted 3×3 (Medium)
3×3×3 cube painted, cut. Cubes with 0 faces painted?
(a) 1  (b) 8  (c) 27  (d) 0

**Answer:** (a) 1
**Solution:** $(3-2)^3 = 1$
**Shortcut:** $(n-2)^3$.
**Common Mistake:** $n^3$.

## Q7. Mirror 6:00 (Medium)
Mirror of 6:00?
(a) 5:00  (b) 6:00  (c) 7:00  (d) Cannot say

**Answer:** (b) 6:00
**Solution:** 11:60 − 6:00 = 5:60 = 6:00.
**Shortcut:** 11:60 − time, but 6:00 is symmetric.
**Common Mistake:** 5:00.
**Time target:** 15 sec

## Q8. Calendar — 26 Jan 2024 (Medium)
What day was 26 Jan 2024?
(a) Wednesday  (b) Thursday  (c) Friday  (d) Saturday

**Answer:** (c) Friday
**Solution:** 1 Jan 2024 = Monday. 26 Jan = 1 Jan + 25 days. 25 mod 7 = 4. Monday + 4 = Friday.
**Shortcut:** Odd day.
**Common Mistake):** Off-by-one.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q10. Painted 7×7 (Hard)
7×7×7 cube painted, cut. Cubes with 2 faces painted?
(a) 60  (b) 24  (c) 96  (d) 12

**Answer:** (a) 60
**Solution:** $12(7-2) = 60$.
**Shortcut:** $12(n-2)$.
**Common Mistake:** Using n.
**Time target:** 30 sec
