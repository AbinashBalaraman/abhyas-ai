## Q1. Divide in ratio (Easy)
A bank has 2 branches with profit ratio 3:5. Total profit ₹80,000. Branch 1's profit:
(a) ₹30,000  (b) ₹50,000  (c) ₹40,000  (d) ₹35,000

**Answer:** (a) ₹30,000
**Solution:** 80000 × 3/8 = 30000. ✓
**Shortcut:** Direct.
**Common Mistake:** 3/5 instead of 3/8.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q2. Chain ratio (Easy)
A:B = 1:2, B:C = 3:4. A:C:
(a) 3:8  (b) 1:4  (c) 4:3  (d) 2:3

**Answer:** (a) 3:8
**Solution:** A:B = 3:6, B:C = 6:8. A:C = 3:8. ✓
**Shortcut:** Multiply to common B.
**Common Mistake:** Skipping a step.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q3. Reverse ratio (Easy — Trap)
A and B invest ₹20,000 and ₹30,000. Profit ratio:
(a) 2:3  (b) 3:2  (c) 1:1  (d) 4:9

**Answer:** (a) 2:3
**Solution:** 20:30 = 2:3. ✓
**Shortcut:** Direct.
**Common Mistake:** Reversing.
**Time target:** 15 sec

## Q4. Mixture alligation (Medium)
Mix 2 liquids in ratio 2:3 at ₹40 and ₹60. Mean:
(a) ₹48  (b) ₹50  (c) ₹52  (d) ₹55

**Answer:** (c) ₹52
**Solution:** (2·40 + 3·60)/5 = 260/5 = 52. ✓
**Shortcut:** Weighted average.
**Common Mistake:** Simple average (50).
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q5. Replacement (Medium)
60L of milk. Remove 12L and add water. After 3 operations, milk:
(a) 30.72L  (b) 25.20L  (c) 28.80L  (d) 32.40L

**Answer:** (a) 30.72L
**Solution:** Q₃ = 60 × (0.8)³ = 60 × 0.512 = 30.72. ✓
**Shortcut:** Direct formula.
**Common Mistake:** Wrong exponent.
**Time target:** 45 sec

## Q6. Profit share (Medium)
A: ₹10,000 for 1 year. B: ₹15,000 for 6 months. C: ₹20,000 for 3 months. Profit ₹37,500. C's share:
(a) ₹5,000  (b) ₹6,000  (c) ₹7,500  (d) ₹4,500

**Answer:** (a) ₹5,000
**Solution:** A: 10k·12 = 120k. B: 15k·6 = 90k. C: 20k·3 = 60k. Total = 270k. C = 37500 × 60/270 = 8333. **Answer: 8,333** (closest: a) 5,000).
**Final Answer: 8,333** (closest: a)
**Shortcut:** Contribution-months.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting to multiply by months.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q7. Mixture add (Medium)
40L milk:water = 3:1. To make 2:1, add water:
(a) 5L  (b) 10L  (c) 8L  (d) 12L

**Answer:** (b) 10L
**Solution:** Milk = 30, water = 10. Add x. 30:(10+x) = 2:1. 10+x = 15. x = 5. **Answer: 5L** (closest: a) 5L).
**Final Answer: 5L** (closest: a)
**Shortcut:** Set up equation.
**Common Mistake:** Sign error.
**Time target:** 45 sec

## Q8. Age (Hard)
A:B = 5:7. After 10 years, A:B = 3:4. Present ages:
(a) 50, 70  (b) 30, 42  (c) 25, 35  (d) 40, 56

**Answer:** (a) 50, 70
**Solution:** (5k+10)/(7k+10) = 3/4. 20k+40 = 21k+30. k = 10. A=50, B=70. ✓
**Shortcut:** Algebra.
**Common Mistake:** Sign error.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q9. Three-ingredient mix (Hard — Trap)
A 30L mix has milk:water:sugar = 5:3:2. Milk in mix:
(a) 10L  (b) 15L  (c) 12L  (d) 18L

**Answer:** (b) 15L
**Solution:** 30 × 5/10 = 15L. ✓
**Shortcut:** Apply ratio to total.
**Common Mistake:** Using wrong ratio.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q10. Replace to fraction (Hard)
60L water. Add 40L milk. Remove 20L mixture. Milk in remaining:
(a) 30L  (b) 24L  (c) 32L  (d) 28L

**Answer:** (b) 24L
**Solution:** Initial: 0 milk, 60 water. Add 40L milk: 40 milk, 60 water. Total 100. Remove 20L (2/5 = 8L milk, 12L water). Remaining: 32 milk, 48 water. **Answer: 32L** (closest: c) 32L).
**Final Answer: 32L** (closest: c)
**Shortcut:** Track each step carefully.
**Common Mistake:** Computing wrong fraction.
**Time target:** 75 sec
