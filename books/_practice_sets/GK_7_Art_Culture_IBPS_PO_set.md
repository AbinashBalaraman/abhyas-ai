### Q1. The famous Shehnai player of India is:
(a) Bismillah Khan (b) Zakir Hussain (c) Ravi Shankar (c) Vilayat Khan

**Answer: (a) Bismillah Khan**

**Solution:** Ustad Bismillah Khan (1916-2006) was the legendary Shehnai player of India. He was a Bharat Ratna recipient (2001) and the first to bring Shehnai to the classical stage. He was born in Dumraon, Bihar. Zakir Hussain (1951-2024) was a Tabla maestro and Bharat Ratna recipient (2024, posthumously). Pandit Ravi Shankar (1920-2012) was a Sitar maestro and multiple Grammy winner. Ustad Vilayat Khan (1928-2004) was a Sitar player from the Etawah Gharana.

### Q2. "Sangeet Natak Akademi" was established in:
(a) 1947 (b) 1952 (c) 1957 (d) 1961

**Answer: (b) 1952**

**Solution:** The Sangeet Natak Akademi (National Academy of Music, Dance & Drama) was established in 1952 by the Government of India. It is the apex body for music, dance, and drama in India, headquartered in New Delhi. It is the first of the three Akademies established by the Indian government (Lalit Kala Akademi for visual arts, Sahitya Akademi for literature). The Akademi recognizes 8 classical dance forms and awards Fellowships and Awards. Sangeet Natak Akademi Awards are given annually to outstanding artists.

### Q3. The Hampi monuments belong to which historical empire?
(a) Chola (b) Vijayanagara (c) Hoysala (d) Chalukya

**Answer: (b) Vijayanagara**

**Solution:** The Hampi monuments (Group of Monuments at Hampi) belong to the Vijayanagara Empire (14th-16th century CE). Hampi is located in Karnataka (near Hospet/Bellary) and is a UNESCO World Heritage Site (inscribed 1986). The empire was founded by Harihara and Bukka in 1336. The Vittala Temple in Hampi has famous "musical pillars" that produce musical notes when tapped. Other important Vijayanagara sites: Vithala Temple, Lotus Mahal, Elephant Stables, Queen's Bath. The empire's last major battle was the Battle of Talikota (1565).

### Q4. "International Day of Dance" is observed on:
(a) 29 April (b) 15 August (c) 21 June (d) 1 May

**Answer: (a) 29 April**

**Solution:** The International Day of Dance is observed on 29 April every year, as proclaimed by UNESCO in 1982. The date commemorates the birth of Jean-Georges Noverre (1727-1810), the creator of modern ballet. The day celebrates all forms of dance and promotes its educational and cultural value. Other important international days: World Heritage Day (18 April), World Music Day (21 June), World Book Day (23 April), International Dance Day (29 April), World Theatre Day (27 March). UNESCO was established on 16 November 1945.

### Q5. The Sitar maestro of India is:
(a) Bismillah Khan (b) Zakir Hussain (c) Ravi Shankar (d) Hariprasad Chaurasia

**Answer: (c) Ravi Shankar**

**Solution:** Pandit Ravi Shankar (1920-2012) was the legendary Sitar maestro of India. He won multiple Grammy Awards and is credited with popularizing Indian classical music worldwide. He collaborated with Western musicians like George Harrison of The Beatles. He was a Bharat Ratna recipient (1999). He belonged to the Maihar Gharana (founded by Allauddin Khan, his guru). Ustad Bismillah Khan was a Shehnai player. Zakir Hussain was a Tabla player. Hariprasad Chaurasia is a Bansuri (flute) player.

### Q6. "Satyameva Jayate" is taken from:
(a) Mundaka Upanishad (b) Rigveda (c) Bhagavad Gita (d) Ramayana

**Answer: (a) Mundaka Upanishad**

**Solution:** The phrase "Satyameva Jayate" (Truth Alone Triumphs) is taken from the Mundaka Upanishad, one of the minor Upanishads of Hinduism. It is the motto of the National Emblem of India. The complete shloka is "Satyameva Jayate Nānṛtaṁ Satyena Panthā Vitato Devayānaḥ" (Truth alone triumphs, not falsehood; by truth the divine path is laid out). The Mundaka Upanishad is part of the Atharvaveda. The phrase was selected from various proposals by the Constituent Assembly for the State Emblem.

### Q7. The currency of the USA is:
(a) Euro (b) US Dollar (c) Pound Sterling (d) Yen

**Answer: (b) US Dollar**

**Solution:** The currency of the United States of America is the US Dollar (USD), which is also the world's primary reserve currency. The official currency of the United Kingdom is the Pound Sterling (GBP). Japan uses the Yen (JPY). Most of the European Union uses the Euro (EUR). Other major currencies: China uses Renminbi/Yuan (CNY), Russia uses Ruble (RUB), Switzerland uses Swiss Franc (CHF), Australia uses Australian Dollar (AUD), Canada uses Canadian Dollar (CAD), India uses Indian Rupee (INR).

### Q8. The capital of China is:
(a) Shanghai (b) Beijing (c) Hong Kong (d) Guangzhou

**Answer: (b) Beijing**

**Solution:** Beijing is the capital of China. Shanghai is China's largest city and a major financial center (not the capital). Hong Kong is a Special Administrative Region (SAR) of China. Guangzhou is the capital of Guangdong province. The currency is Renminbi/Yuan (CNY). China is the most populous country in the world until 2023, when India overtook it. China is the 2nd largest economy by nominal GDP (after USA). The official language is Mandarin (Putonghua). China has the 3rd largest land area in the world (after Russia and Canada).

### Q9. "Lohri" is a harvest festival primarily celebrated in:
(a) Gujarat (b) Punjab (c) Maharashtra (d) Karnataka

**Answer: (b) Punjab**

**Solution:** Lohri is a popular harvest festival of Punjab, celebrated on 13 January every year. It marks the passing of the winter solstice and is associated with the harvest of the Rabi crop (especially sugarcane). It is celebrated with bonfires, traditional dance (Bhangra and Giddha), and foods like gajak, rewri, and popcorn. Other regional festivals: Onam (Kerala), Pongal (TN), Bihu (Assam), Hornbill (Nagaland), Durga Puja (West Bengal), Garba (Gujarat), Thrissur Pooram (Kerala). Lohri is celebrated the day before Makar Sankranti.

### Q10. The currency of Japan is:
(a) Yuan (b) Won (c) Yen (d) Ringgit

**Answer: (c) Yen**

**Solution:** The currency of Japan is the Yen (JPY). It is one of the most traded currencies in the world. Other Asian currencies: China uses Yuan/Renminbi (CNY), South Korea uses Won (KRW), Indonesia uses Rupiah (IDR), Malaysia uses Ringgit (MYR), Thailand uses Baht (THB), Singapore uses Singapore Dollar (SGD), Philippines uses Peso (PHP). Japan's capital is Tokyo, the largest metro area in the world (~37 million). Japan is the 3rd largest economy by nominal GDP (after USA and China). The official language is Japanese.
