## Q1. Assumption (Easy)
"Please don't park your vehicle in front of the gate." Assumption:
(I) People park here.  (II) Gate is functional.
(a) Only I  (b) Only II  (c) Both  (d) Neither

**Answer:** (a) Only I
**Solution:** Only assumption I is necessary.
**Shortcut:** Necessity test.
**Common Mistake:** Both.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q2. Conclusion (Easy)
"The government has banned plastic bags in the city." Conclusion: (I) People will use cloth bags. (II) Plastic pollution will reduce.
(a) Only I  (b) Only II  (c) Both  (d) Neither

**Answer:** (c) Both
**Solution:** Both follow.
**Shortcut:** Direct consequences.
**Common Mistake:** Neither.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q3. Action (Easy)
"The local lake is heavily polluted." Action:
(I) Ban industrial discharge.  (II) Ban all fishing.
(a) Only I  (b) Only II  (c) Both  (d) Neither

**Answer:** (a) Only I
**Solution:** Only I is practical.
**Shortcut:** Practicality.
**Common Mistake:** Both.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q4. Cause (Medium)
Statement 1: "Cricket match cancelled." Statement 2: "It rained heavily."
(a) 1 cause, 2 effect  (b) 2 cause, 1 effect  (c) Independent  (d) Cannot say

**Answer:** (b) 2 cause, 1 effect
**Solution:** Rain → match cancelled.
**Shortcut:** Time order.
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q5. Assertion (Medium)
A: "City needs more hospitals." R: "Population growing rapidly."
(a) Both true, R explains  (b) Both true, R doesn't explain  (c) A true, R false  (d) A false, R true

**Answer:** (a) Both true, R explains
**Solution:** Growing population → need more hospitals.
**Shortcut:** Specific explanation.
**Common Mistake):** Other.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q6. Assumption (Medium)
"The company is laying off employees due to losses." Assumption:
(a) Company profitable  (b) Many employees  (c) Layoffs reduce costs  (d) Employees skilled

**Answer:** (c) Layoffs reduce costs
**Solution:** Cost-cutting measure.
**Shortcut:** Necessity.
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q7. Conclusion (Medium)
"Education is key to success." Conclusion:
(a) All educated are successful  (b) Uneducated are unsuccessful  (c) Education helps in success  (d) Success requires education

**Answer:** (c) Education helps in success
**Solution:** Most reasonable.
**Shortcut:** Direct inference.
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q8. Cause (Medium)
Statement 1: "New policy reduced accidents by 30%." Statement 2: "Government increased traffic fines."
(a) 1 cause, 2 effect  (b) 2 cause, 1 effect  (c) Independent  (d) Cannot say

**Answer:** (b) 2 cause, 1 effect

**Shortcut:** Causal link.

## Q9. Assertion Democracy (Hard)

## Q10. Course of Action (Hard)
"Crime in city has increased 50% in last year." Action:
(I) Increase police force.  (II) Implement curfews.
(a) Only I  (b) Only II  (c) Both  (d) Neither

**Answer:** (a) Only I
**Solution:** I is practical. II is extreme.
**Shortcut:** Practicality.
**Common Mistake:** Both.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q11. Statement Conclusion (Hard)
"Government has launched a new education scheme." Conclusion:
(a) All students will benefit  (b) Education quality will improve  (c) Teachers will be appointed  (d) Some benefit expected

**Answer:** (d) Some benefit expected
**Solution:** Definite but not certain.
**Shortcut:** Definite only.
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q12. Cause and Effect (Hard)
Statement 1: "Sales dropped 20%." Statement 2: "Company raised prices 30%."
(a) 1 cause, 2 effect  (b) 2 cause, 1 effect  (c) Independent  (d) Cannot say

**Answer:** (b) 2 cause, 1 effect
**Solution:** Higher prices → lower sales.
**Shortcut:** Causal link.
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 45 sec

---

## Q13. Multiple Statement (Hard)
Statements: (1) All doctors are graduates. (2) Some graduates are smart. Conclusion: (I) Some doctors are smart. (II) Some smart are graduates.
(a) Only I  (b) Only II  (c) Both  (d) Neither

**Answer:** (b) Only II
**Solution:** II follows from (2). I is not certain.
**Shortcut:** Syllogistic.
**Common Mistake:** Both.
**Time target:** 60 sec

---

## Q14. Action Multi (Hard)
"Flood affected 10,000 families." Actions: (I) Relief camps. (II) Long-term rehabilitation. (III) Stop rainfall.
(a) I and II  (b) II and III  (c) All  (d) I only

**Answer:** (a) I and II
**Solution:** I and II are practical. III is impossible.
**Shortcut:** Practicality.
**Common Mistake:** All.
**Time target:** 60 sec
