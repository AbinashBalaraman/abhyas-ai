## Q7. Tower height (Medium)
From 10 m, angle 45°. Tower height:
(a) 5  (b) 10  (c) 15  (d) 20

**Answer:** (b) 10
**Solution:** h = 10·tan 45° = 10. ✓
**Shortcut:** d·tan 45° = d.
**Common Mistake:** Setting = 5.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q8. cos²θ − sin²θ (Medium)
(a) sin 2θ  (b) cos 2θ  (c) 1  (d) tan 2θ

**Answer:** (b) cos 2θ
**Solution:** cos 2θ = cos²θ − sin²θ. ✓
**Shortcut:** Identity.
**Common Mistake:** Setting = sin 2θ.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q9. From 10 m, angle 30° (Hard)
(a) 10/√3  (b) 10√3  (c) 10  (d) 5√3

**Answer:** (a) 10/√3
**Solution:** h = 10·tan 30° = 10/√3. ✓
**Shortcut:** d·tan 30°.
**Common Mistake:** Setting = 10√3.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q10. Conditional (Hard — Trap)
sinθ = 5/13, cosθ (acute):
(a) 5/12  (b) 12/13  (c) 13/12  (d) 8/13

**Answer:** (b) 12/13
**Solution:** cos² = 1 − 25/169 = 144/169 → cos = 12/13. ✓
**Shortcut:** Identity.
**Common Mistake:** Setting = 5/12.
**Time target:** 30 sec
