## Q1. Sum from table (Easy)
A=100, B=200, C=300. Total:
(a) 500  (b) 600  (c) 700  (d) 800

**Answer:** (b) 600
**Solution:** 100+200+300 = 600. ✓
**Shortcut:** Direct.
**Common Mistake:** Wrong sum.
**Time target:** 10 sec

---

## Q2. Pie slice percentage (Easy)
Pie slice 90° in full 360° circle. Percentage:
(a) 20%  (b) 25%  (c) 30%  (d) 50%

**Answer:** (b) 25%
**Solution:** 90/360 = 1/4 = 25%. ✓
**Shortcut:** Angle/360.
**Common Mistake:** Setting = 20%.
**Time target:** 10 sec

---

## Q3. Bar max (Easy)
Bar chart: 10, 20, 30, 40, 50. Max:
(a) 10  (b) 30  (c) 50  (d) 40

**Answer:** (c) 50
**Solution:** Highest value. ✓
**Shortcut:** Direct.
**Common Mistake:** Setting = 40.

## Q4. Line graph % change (Easy — Trap)
Sales: 100 → 120. % change:
(a) 10%  (b) 20%  (c) 25%  (d) 30%

**Answer:** (b) 20%
**Solution:** (120−100)/100 = 20%. ✓
**Shortcut:** (New−Old)/Old.
**Common Mistake:** Setting = 25%.

## Q6. Table average (Medium)
Sales: 100, 120, 130, 140, 150. Average:
(a) 120  (b) 125  (c) 128  (d) 130

**Answer:** (c) 128
**Solution:** (100+120+130+140+150)/5 = 640/5 = 128. ✓
**Shortcut:** Sum/n.
**Common Mistake:** Setting = 130.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q8. Bar percentage (Medium)
A=80, B=100, C=120, D=140. B is what % of total:
(a) 20%  (b) 22.5%  (c) 25%  (d) 30%

**Answer:** (b) 22.5%
**Solution:** Total = 440. B% = 100/440 ≈ 22.7%. ✓
**Shortcut:** X/Total × 100.
**Common Mistake:** Setting = 25%.
**Time target:** 45 sec

## Q9. Line % increase (Medium)

**Answer:** (c) 160%

**Shortcut:** (New−Old)/Old.

## Q10. Caselet multi-step (Medium)
600 students, 60% boys, 40% of girls study science. Girls in science:
(a) 96  (b) 100  (c) 120  (d) 144

**Answer:** (a) 96
**Solution:** Girls = 240. Girls science = 240·0.4 = 96. ✓
**Shortcut:** Sequential.
**Common Mistake:** Applying 40% to total.
**Time target:** 45 sec

## Q14. CAGR (Hard)
Sales 100, 110, 121, 133.1, 146.4. CAGR (5 yrs):
(a) 8%  (b) 10%  (c) 12%  (d) 15%

**Answer:** (b) 10%
**Solution:** (146.4/100)^(1/4) − 1 ≈ 10%. ✓
**Shortcut:** CAGR formula.
**Common Mistake:** Linear growth.
**Time target:** 90 sec

## Q15. Caselet complex (Hard — Trap)
P = 50% of Q, Q = 25% of R, S = 2R. R = 100. Total of P+Q+R+S:
(a) 400  (b) 450  (c) 500  (d) 337.5

**Answer:** (d) 337.5
**Solution:** Q = 25, P = 12.5, S = 200. Total = 12.5+25+100+200 = 337.5. ✓
**Shortcut:** Sequential.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting a term.
**Time target:** 90 sec
