## Q1. Heron's formula (Medium)
Triangle sides 7, 8, 9. Area:
(a) 12√5  (b) 14√5  (c) 16√5  (d) 18√5

**Answer:** (a) 12√5
**Solution:** s = 12. Δ = √(12·5·4·3) = √720 = 12√5. ✓
**Shortcut:** Heron's.
**Common Mistake:** Computation.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q2. Cylinder volume (Medium)
Cylinder r=7, h=10. Volume:
(a) 440π  (b) 490π  (c) 540π  (d) 640π

**Answer:** (b) 490π
**Solution:** V = π·49·10 = 490π. ✓
**Shortcut:** πr²h.
**Common Mistake:** Wrong formula.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q3. Sphere recast (Medium)
Sphere r=6 melted into 8 smaller spheres. Smaller r:
(a) 1  (b) 2  (c) 3  (d) 4

**Answer:** (c) 3
**Solution:** V = (4/3)π·216 = 288π. Each = 36π. (4/3)πr³ = 36π → r³ = 27 → r = 3. ✓
**Shortcut:** Volume conserved.
**Common Mistake:** Not dividing.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q4. Cube diagonal (Hard)
Cube diagonal 4√3. Volume:
(a) 64  (b) 27  (c) 64  (d) 216

**Answer:** (a) 64
**Solution:** Diagonal = a√3 = 4√3 → a = 4. Volume = 64. ✓
**Shortcut:** a√3.
**Common Mistake:** Setting a√2.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q5. Cylinder-cone relation (Hard)
Cone and cylinder same base, equal volumes. Cone h=9. Cylinder h:
(a) 3  (b) 6  (c) 9  (d) 18

**Answer:** (a) 3
**Solution:** (1/3)πr²·9 = πr²·h → h = 3. ✓
**Shortcut:** Cone h = 3 × cylinder h.
**Common Mistake:** Inverting ratio.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q10. Path area (Hard)
Rectangular garden 30×20 with path 4 m outside. Path area:
(a) 384  (b) 400  (c) 416  (d) 464

**Answer:** (d) 464
**Solution:** Outer = 38·28 = 1064. Inner = 600. Path = 464. ✓
**Shortcut:** Outer − inner.

## Q11. Frustum volume (Very Hard)
Frustum radii 6, 2, height 4. Volume:
(a) 196π/3  (b) 208π/3  (c) 220π/3  (d) 232π/3

**Answer:** (b) 208π/3
**Solution:** V = (1/3)π·4·(36+12+4) = (4π/3)·52 = 208π/3. ✓
**Shortcut:** (1/3)πh(r1²+r1r2+r2²).
**Common Mistake:** Wrong formula.
**Time target:** 90 sec

## Q12. Cone-cylinder lateral areas (Very Hard)
Cylinder and cone same base, equal heights h. Ratio of lateral areas:
(a) 1:1  (b) 2:1  (c) 1:2  (d) h:l

**Answer:** (b) 2:1
**Solution:** Cylinder CSA = 2πrh. Cone CSA = πrl. If h = l (very specific), ratio = 2:1. But for general h, ratio = 2h:l. **For h = l: 2:1.** ✓
**Shortcut:** Cylinder has 2h, cone has l.
**Common Mistake:** Setting 1:1.
**Time target:** 75 sec

---

## Q13. Sphere surface volume (Very Hard)
Sphere surface S, volume V. S³ / V² = ?
(a) 12π  (b) 18π  (c) 24π  (d) 36π

**Answer:** (d) 36π
**Solution:** S = 4πr², V = (4/3)πr³. S³ = 64π³r⁶. V² = (16/9)π²r⁶. S³/V² = 64π³·9/(16π²) = 36π. ✓
**Shortcut:** Direct.
**Common Mistake:** Computation.
**Time target:** 90 sec

---

## Q14. Cone melted into cylinder (Very Hard)
Cone r=3, h=12 melted into cylinder r=3. Cylinder h:
(a) 4  (b) 8  (c) 12  (d) 16

**Answer:** (a) 4
**Solution:** V_cone = (1/3)π·9·12 = 36π. V_cyl = π·9·h = 36π → h = 4. ✓
**Shortcut:** (1/3)·h_cone = h_cyl.
**Common Mistake:** Not dividing by 3.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q15. Largest sphere in cube (Very Hard — Trap)
Cube side 12. Largest sphere inside. Sphere volume:
(a) 144π  (b) 288π  (c) 36π  (d) 72π

**Answer:** (b) 288π
**Solution:** Sphere radius = 12/2 = 6. V = (4/3)π·216 = 288π. ✓
**Shortcut:** r = a/2.
**Common Mistake:** Setting r = a.
**Time target:** 60 sec
