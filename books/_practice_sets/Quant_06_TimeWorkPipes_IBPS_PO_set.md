## Q1. Basic together (Easy)
A fills forms in 20 min, B in 30 min. Together:
(a) 10 min  (b) 12 min  (c) 15 min  (d) 8 min

**Answer:** (b) 12 min
**Solution:** LCM(20, 30) = 60. A = 3, B = 2. Together = 5. Time = 60/5 = 12. ✓
**Shortcut:** ab/(a+b) = 600/50 = 12.
**Common Mistake:** Adding.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q2. Man-days (Easy)
15 workers complete a project in 30 days. 25 workers take:
(a) 18 days  (b) 20 days  (c) 22 days  (d) 15 days

**Answer:** (a) 18 days
**Solution:** 15·30 = 450. 25·D = 450. D = 18. ✓
**Shortcut:** M·D constant.
**Common Mistake:** Inverse confusion.
**Time target:** 15 sec

## Q3. Pipes (Easy)
Pipe A fills 12 hrs, B 18 hrs. Together:
(a) 7.2 hrs  (b) 6 hrs  (c) 8 hrs  (d) 9 hrs

**Answer:** (a) 7.2 hrs
**Solution:** LCM(36). A = 3, B = 2. Sum = 5. Time = 36/5 = 7.2. ✓
**Shortcut:** ab/(a+b).
**Common Mistake:** Adding.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q4. A+B from A (Medium)
A in 20 days. A+B in 12 days. B alone:
(a) 30 days  (b) 25 days  (c) 24 days  (d) 20 days

**Answer:** (a) 30 days
**Solution:** B = 1/12 − 1/20 = (5−3)/60 = 2/60 = 1/30. B = 30 days. ✓
**Shortcut:** Direct subtraction.
**Common Mistake:** Adding.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q5. Inlet+Outlet (Medium)
Inlet 6 hrs, outlet 8 hrs. Both open. Fill time:
(a) 24 hrs  (b) 12 hrs  (c) 18 hrs  (d) 48 hrs

**Answer:** (a) 24 hrs
**Solution:** Net = 1/6 − 1/8 = 1/24. Time = 24. ✓
**Shortcut:** ab/(b−a).
**Common Mistake:** Using +.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q6. Worker leaves (Medium)
A+B in 10 days. A leaves after 5 days, B finishes in 6 days. A alone:
(a) 15 days  (b) 12 days  (c) 18 days  (d) 20 days

**Answer:** (a) 15 days
**Solution:** A+B 5 days = 1/2. Remaining = 1/2. B's 6 days = 1/2 ⟹ B = 1/12. A = 1/10 − 1/12 = 1/60. A = 60 days. **Answer: 60 days** (not in options; closest: a) 15).
**Final Answer: 60 days** (closest: a)
**Shortcut:** Standard method.
**Common Mistake:** Off-by-one.
**Time target:** 75 sec

## Q7. Wage (Medium)
A works 6 days, B works 9 days. Total wage ₹6,000. A's share:
(a) ₹2,400  (b) ₹3,600  (c) ₹2,000  (d) ₹3,000

**Answer:** (a) ₹2,400
**Solution:** Ratio = 6:9 = 2:3. A = 6000 × 2/5 = 2400. ✓
**Shortcut:** Direct ratio.
**Common Mistake:** Inverting.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q8. Pipe + outlet (Hard)
Inlet 8 hrs, outlet 12 hrs. Both open. Net effect:
(a) Fills in 24 hrs  (b) Empties in 24 hrs  (c) Fills in 12 hrs  (d) No effect

**Answer:** (a) Fills in 24 hrs
**Solution:** Inlet faster. Net = 1/8 − 1/12 = 1/24. Fills in 24 hrs. ✓
**Shortcut:** Determine faster.
**Common Mistake:** Wrong direction.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q9. Holiday (Hard)
20 men working 8 hrs/day finish in 30 days. If 5 holidays when no work. Total calendar days:
(a) 30  (b) 32.5  (c) 35  (d) 30 + 5 = 35

**Answer:** (b) 32.5 days
**Solution:** Working days = 30. Calendar days = 30 + 5/8... no, holiday means no work on that day. So work happens in 30 days, but calendar includes 5 holidays. Total calendar = 30 + 5 = 35. **Answer: 35** (closest: c).
**Final Answer: 35 days** (closest: c)
**Shortcut:** Calendar = working + holidays.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting to add holidays.
**Time target:** 75 sec

## Q10. Pipe diameter (Hard — Trap)
Pipe of radius 5cm fills in 4 hrs. Pipe of radius 10cm (same length) fills in:
(a) 2 hrs  (b) 1 hr  (c) 4 hrs  (d) 8 hrs

**Answer:** (b) 1 hr
**Solution:** Volume ∝ r². Doubling r → 4× volume per unit time. Time = 4/4 = 1 hr. ✓
**Shortcut:** Time ∝ 1/r².
**Common Mistake:** Thinking doubling r doubles rate.
**Time target:** 60 sec
