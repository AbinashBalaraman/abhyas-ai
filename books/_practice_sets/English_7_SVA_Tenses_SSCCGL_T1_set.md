# English Chapter 7: Subject-Verb Agreement & Tenses
## Practice Set — SSC CGL Tier-I (15 Questions, 60 sec each)

> **Source:** `books/English_Language/07_SubjectVerbAgreement_Tenses.md`
> **Time Target:** 15 minutes total | **Difficulty:** Foundation to Intermediate
> **Coverage:** SVA rules (each/every, either/or, collective nouns), Tense selection (time signals)

---

### Q1. "He ___ to school daily."
(a) go
(b) goes
(c) going
(d) gone

**Answer:** (b) goes

**Solution:** Singular third-person subject (he) + present simple → add **-s** to verb. "He goes" (not "go"). [Source: §2.2 Singular Verb Formation]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "go" — using base form instead of adding -s for 3rd person singular.

**Time Target:** 5 sec

---

### Q2. "They ___ in the park now."
(a) is
(b) was
(c) are playing
(d) has played

**Answer:** (c) are playing

**Solution:** "They" is plural + "now" (present continuous time signal) → "are playing". [Source: §3.4 Present Continuous, §3.8 Time Signal Table]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "is" (singular for plural subject) or (d) "has played" (present perfect, not continuous).

**Time Target:** 10 sec

---

### Q3. "Neither of the candidates ___ the form."
(a) have
(b) has
(c) are having
(d) were

**Answer:** (b) has

**Solution:** "**Neither**" is a singular indefinite pronoun → takes a **singular verb**. "Neither...has" (not "have"). [Source: §2.4 Rule 4 — Each, Every, Either, Neither as subject]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "have" — assuming plural because of "candidates" (plural noun). The subject is "Neither", not "candidates".

**Time Target:** 10 sec

---

### Q4. "I ___ a book since morning."
(a) am reading
(b) have been reading
(c) read
(d) was reading

**Answer:** (b) have been reading

**Solution:** "Since morning" with continuing action → **Present Perfect Continuous** (have been + V1+ing). "Since + point in time" + ongoing action. [Source: §3.4 Present Perfect Continuous, §3.8]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "am reading" (present continuous — doesn't emphasize duration from past).

**Time Target:** 15 sec

---

### Q5. "Each of the students ___ a book."
(a) have
(b) has
(c) are having
(d) were having

**Answer:** (b) has

**Solution:** "**Each**" is singular → takes singular verb "has". [Source: §2.4 Rule 4 — "Each" is singular]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "have" — confusing "Each" with "All".

**Time Target:** 5 sec

---

### Q6. "He ___ before she arrived."
(a) left
(b) had left
(c) has left
(d) was leaving

**Answer:** (b) had left

**Solution:** "Before she arrived" (past reference) → action completed before another past action = **Past Perfect** (had + V3). [Source: §3.5 Past Perfect — "past of the past", §3.8]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "left" (Past Simple) — doesn't show the earlier-than-past relationship.

**Time Target:** 15 sec

---

### Q7. "Mathematics ___ a difficult subject."
(a) are
(b) is
(c) were
(d) have been

**Answer:** (b) is

**Solution:** "**Mathematics**" (along with Physics, Economics, News) takes a **singular verb** despite ending in -s. [Source: §2.4 Rule 7 — News, Mathematics, Physics, Economics, Athletics → singular]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "are" — thinking plural because of -s ending.

**Time Target:** 5 sec

---

### Q8. "I ___ to the market yesterday."
(a) go
(b) went
(c) have gone
(d) had gone

**Answer:** (b) went

**Solution:** "Yesterday" is a past time signal → **Past Simple** (V2). [Source: §3.5 Past Simple, §3.8 Time Signal Table]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (c) "have gone" (Present Perfect) — but "yesterday" is a definite past time, not an unspecified past.

**Time Target:** 10 sec

---

### Q9. "A number of students ___ absent."
(a) is
(b) was
(c) are
(d) has been

**Answer:** (c) are

**Solution:** "**A number of** + plural noun" → **plural verb**. [Source: §2.4 Rule 8 — "A number of" is plural, "The number of" is singular]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "is" — confusing "A number of" (plural) with "The number of" (singular).

**Time Target:** 10 sec

---

### Q10. "He said that he ___ happy."
(a) is
(b) was
(c) has been
(d) will be

**Answer:** (b) was

**Solution:** In reported speech, when the reporting verb is past ("said"), the reported verb shifts back: present → past. "**Was**" (Past Simple) replaces "is". [Source: §3.7 Sequence of Tenses, Chapter 8 §2.3 Tense Backshift]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "is" — failing to apply tense backshift in narration.

**Time Target:** 15 sec

---

### Q11. "The team ___ playing well this season."
(a) are
(b) is
(c) were
(d) have

**Answer:** (b) is

**Solution:** "**Team**" is a **collective noun** → singular verb. The team acts as a unit. [Source: §2.4 Rule 6 — Collective nouns: singular]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "are" — treating collective noun as plural because of multiple members.

**Time Target:** 10 sec

---

### Q12. "I ___ here for 5 years now."
(a) live
(b) lived
(c) have lived
(d) am living

**Answer:** (c) have lived

**Solution:** "For 5 years" with action continuing to present → **Present Perfect** (has/have + V3). [Source: §3.4 Present Perfect — action begun in past continuing to present, §3.8]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (b) "lived" (Past Simple — implies the action is finished, not continuing).

**Time Target:** 10 sec

---

### Q13. "Many a student ___ failed in the exam."
(a) have
(b) has
(c) are
(d) were

**Answer:** (b) has

**Solution:** "**Many a**" is followed by a **singular noun** and takes a **singular verb**. "Many a student" = "Many students" (formal) but treated as singular. [Source: §2.4 Rule 9 — "Many a / Many an" is singular]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "have" — thinking plural because of "many".

**Time Target:** 10 sec

---

### Q14. "By next year, I ___ here for 10 years."
(a) will work
(b) will have worked
(c) will be working
(d) worked

**Answer:** (b) will have worked

**Solution:** "By next year" (future reference point) + "for 10 years" (duration) → **Future Perfect** (will have + V3). [Source: §3.6 Future Perfect, §3.8]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "will work" (Future Simple — doesn't emphasize completion by a specific future time).

**Time Target:** 30 sec

---

### Q15. "Either he or they ___ coming."
(a) is
(b) was
(c) are
(d) has

**Answer:** (c) are

**Solution:** With "**Either...or / Neither...nor**", the verb agrees with the **nearest subject**. "They" is plural → "are". [Source: §2.4 Rule 3 — Closest subject wins]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "is" — taking the verb of the first subject ("he") instead of the nearest.

**Time Target:** 15 sec
