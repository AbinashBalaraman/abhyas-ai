### Q1. The IMF was founded in:
(a) 1944 (b) 1945 (c) 1950 (d) 1951

**Answer: (a) 1944**

**Solution:** The IMF (International Monetary Fund) was founded on 22 July 1944 at the Bretton Woods Conference. The IMF headquarters is in Washington, D.C., USA. The IMF has 191 members. The current Managing Director is Kristalina Georgieva (Bulgaria). The IMF's main purposes: promote international monetary cooperation, facilitate international trade, foster economic growth, provide temporary financial assistance for BoP issues, and prevent financial crises. The IMF's quota system determines voting power and SDR allocations. India is a top 10 quota holder with ~2.75% quota.

### Q4. The SDR basket consists of how many currencies?
(a) 3 (b) 4 (c) 5 (d) 6

**Answer: (c) 5**

**Solution:** The SDR (Special Drawing Rights) basket consists of 5 currencies: USD (US Dollar), EUR (Euro), RMB (Chinese Yuan), JPY (Japanese Yen), and GBP (British Pound Sterling). SDR is an international reserve asset created by the IMF in 1969. The weights (2022 review) are: USD 43.38%, EUR 29.31%, RMB 12.28%, JPY 7.59%, GBP 7.44%. The composition is reviewed every 5 years (last review 2022). SDR can be exchanged for freely usable currencies. SDR is part of India's forex reserves (about $10 billion).

### Q5. The World Bank was founded at:
(a) Bretton Woods (b) Paris (c) Geneva (d) London

**Answer: (a) Bretton Woods**

**Solution:** The World Bank was founded at the Bretton Woods Conference in 1944 (along with the IMF). The World Bank Group has 5 institutions: IBRD (International Bank for Reconstruction and Development, 1944), IDA (International Development Association, 1960), IFC (International Finance Corporation, 1956), MIGA (Multilateral Investment Guarantee Agency, 1988), and ICSID (International Centre for Settlement of Investment Disputes, 1966). The current World Bank President is Ajay Banga (since June 2023) — an Indian-American. The World Bank HQ is in Washington, D.C. IDA is the "World Bank's window for the poorest".

### Q6. IDA stands for:
(a) International Development Association (b) International Debt Agency (c) International Development Aid (d) Investment Development Authority

**Answer: (a) International Development Association**

**Solution:** IDA (International Development Association) is the part of the World Bank Group that provides concessional loans and grants to the poorest countries. IDA was established in 1960. IDA is the "World Bank's window for the poorest" — providing zero or low-interest loans (called "credits") to the world's poorest countries. IDA borrowers are mostly in Africa. IDA is replenished every 3 years by donor countries. Other World Bank institutions: IBRD (middle-income lending), IFC (private sector), MIGA (political risk insurance), ICSID (investment dispute settlement). The 5 institutions together form the World Bank Group.

### Q7. The ADB was established in:
(a) 1960 (b) 1964 (c) 1966 (d) 1970

**Answer: (c) 1966**

**Solution:** The ADB (Asian Development Bank) was established on 19 December 1966. Its headquarters is in 6 ADB Avenue, Mandaluyong, Metro Manila, Philippines. ADB has 69 members. The current President is Masato Kanda (since 24 February 2025). Traditionally, the ADB President is Japanese. India is a founding member of ADB. India's share in ADB is 6.317% (as of 31 December 2020). ADB aims to reduce poverty in Asia-Pacific through inclusive economic growth, environmentally sustainable growth, and regional integration. ADB provides loans, technical assistance, and equity investments.

### Q10. The NDB headquarters is in:
(a) Beijing (b) New Delhi (c) Moscow (d) Shanghai

**Answer: (d) Shanghai**

**Solution:** The NDB (New Development Bank / BRICS Bank) headquarters is in Shanghai, China. The NDB treaty was signed in July 2014 (Fortaleza, Brazil) and came into force in July 2015. The current President is Dilma Rousseff (since 24 March 2023). The first NDB President was K. V. Kamath (India). The initial authorized capital was US$100 billion. NDB has since expanded to include Bangladesh, UAE, Uruguay, Egypt, Algeria, Colombia, Ethiopia, and Uzbekistan.

### Q12. FATF was established in:
(a) 1985 (b) 1989 (c) 1995 (d) 2000

**Answer: (b) 1989**

**Solution:** FATF (Financial Action Task Force) was established in 1989 at the G7 Paris Summit. FATF sets international standards (40 Recommendations) to prevent money laundering, terrorist financing, and proliferation financing. FATF has 40 jurisdictions (39 countries + 2 regional organizations: EU and GCC). FATF is headquartered in Paris, France. India is a member of FATF. FATF publishes "Grey List" (jurisdictions under increased monitoring) and "Black List" (high-risk jurisdictions). The current FATF President is Elisa de Anda Madrazo (Mexico, 2024-2026).

### Q14. The BIS was established in:
(a) 1920 (b) 1930 (c) 1940 (d) 1950

**Answer: (b) 1930**

**Solution:** The BIS (Bank for International Settlements) was established on 17 May 1930. Its headquarters is in Basel, Switzerland. BIS is the world's oldest international financial organisation. BIS is the "bank for central banks" — providing banking services to central banks and international financial institutions. BIS hosts the BCBS (Basel Committee on Banking Supervision), which issues the Basel Accords. BIS fosters international monetary and financial cooperation. The current BIS General Manager is Agustín Carstens (since 2017). India is a member of BIS (as a small share holder since 2019).
