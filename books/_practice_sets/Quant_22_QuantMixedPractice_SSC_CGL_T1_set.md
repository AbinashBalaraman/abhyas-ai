## Q1. SI — Triple (Easy)
If a sum of money triples itself in 12 years at simple interest, find the rate.
(a) 10%  (b) 12%  (c) 16.67%  (d) 25%

**Answer:** (c) 16.67%
**Solution:** $P + SI = 3P \Rightarrow SI = 2P$. $R = \frac{2P \times 100}{P \times 12} = 16.67\%$
**Shortcut:** Triples = 2P interest. Rate = $2 \times 100 / T$.
**Common Mistake:** Computing 1/12 = 8.33%.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q2. Profit (Easy)
A man buys a watch for ₹800 and sells for ₹1000. Find profit%.
(a) 20%  (b) 25%  (c) 30%  (d) 18%

**Answer:** (b) 25%
**Solution:** $\frac{200}{800} \times 100 = 25\%$
**Shortcut:** (SP-CP)/CP × 100.
**Common Mistake):** Using SP as base.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q3. Train (Easy)
A train 240 m long passes a pole in 24 seconds. Speed?
(a) 10 m/s  (b) 20 m/s  (c) 36 km/h  (d) 40 km/h

**Answer:** (c) 36 km/h
**Solution:** Speed = $\frac{240}{24} = 10$ m/s = $\frac{10 \times 18}{5} = 36$ km/h
**Shortcut:** Length/time for pole, then ×18/5.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting to convert.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q4. Pipes (Easy)
Two pipes fill a tank in 12 and 15 hours. Leak empties in 20 hours. If all open, time to fill?
(a) 8 hours  (b) 10 hours  (c) 12 hours  (d) 7.5 hours

**Answer:** (b) 10 hours
**Solution:** Net rate = $\frac{1}{12} + \frac{1}{15} - \frac{1}{20} = \frac{5+4-3}{60} = \frac{6}{60} = \frac{1}{10}$
**Shortcut:** LCM for combined work.
**Common Mistake:** Adding instead of subtracting leak.
**Time target:** 90 sec

## Q5. Ages (Medium)
Ratio of ages of A and B is 5:7. After 6 years, it will be 3:4. Find present age of A.
(a) 30  (b) 35  (c) 25  (d) 28

**Answer:** (a) 30
**Solution:** $\frac{5x+6}{7x+6} = \frac{3}{4} \Rightarrow 20x+24 = 21x+18 \Rightarrow x = 6$. A = 5×6 = 30.
**Shortcut:** Set up equation with x.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting +6.
**Time target:** 90 sec

## Q6. Mean (Medium)
Find the mean of 12, 15, 18, 21, 24.
(a) 16  (b) 18  (c) 20  (d) 22

**Answer:** (b) 18
**Solution:** $\frac{12+15+18+21+24}{5} = \frac{90}{5} = 18$
**Shortcut:** Sum/count.
**Common Mistake:** Median instead.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q7. Probability Dice (Medium)
A die is rolled twice. Probability of getting sum 9?
(a) 1/9  (b) 1/12  (c) 4/36  (d) 1/8

**Answer:** (c) 4/36
**Solution:** Pairs: (3,6), (4,5), (5,4), (6,3) = 4. $\frac{4}{36} = \frac{1}{9}$. So **(a) 1/9**.
**Shortcut:** 4 pairs.
**Common Mistake:** Missing some pairs.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q8. CI (Medium)
The compound interest on ₹1000 for 2 years at 10% per annum:
(a) ₹200  (b) ₹210  (c) ₹100  (d) ₹220

**Answer:** (b) ₹210
**Solution:** $1000(1.1)^2 - 1000 = 1210 - 1000 = 210$
**Shortcut:** $P(1+r/100)^T - P$.
**Common Mistake:** Using SI formula.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q9. Trigonometry (Medium)
If $\sin\theta = 4/5$ (acute), find $\cos 2\theta$.
(a) 7/25  (b) -7/25  (c) 24/25  (d) -24/25

**Answer:** (b) -7/25
**Solution:** $\cos 2\theta = 1 - 2\sin^2\theta = 1 - 2(16/25) = 1 - 32/25 = -7/25$
**Shortcut:** $\cos 2\theta = 1 - 2\sin^2\theta$.
**Common Mistake:** Sign error.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q10. Geometry (Medium)
A rectangle has length 12 cm and width 5 cm. Diagonal?
(a) 13 cm  (b) 15 cm  (c) 17 cm  (d) 14 cm

**Answer:** (a) 13 cm
**Solution:** $\sqrt{144 + 25} = 13$ (5-12-13).
**Shortcut:** Pythagorean.
**Common Mistake:** Other triples.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q11. HCF (Medium)
HCF of 24, 36, 60:
(a) 6  (b) 12  (c) 24  (d) 4

**Answer:** (b) 12
**Solution:** $\text{HCF}(24,36) = 12$. $\text{HCF}(12, 60) = 12$.
**Shortcut:** Stepwise HCF.
**Common Mistake:** Returning 6.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q12. Algebra (Medium)
If $x + y = 10$ and $xy = 21$, find $x^2 + y^2$.
(a) 58  (b) 100  (c) 79  (d) 100

**Answer:** (a) 58
**Solution:** $(x+y)^2 - 2xy = 100 - 42 = 58$
**Shortcut:** Identity.
**Common Mistake:** Just $(x+y)^2$.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q13. Percentage (Medium)
30% of a number is 90. Find the number.
(a) 200  (b) 250  (c) 300  (d) 350

**Answer:** (c) 300

**Shortcut:** Divide by %.
**Common Mistake:** Multiplying.

## Q14. Sphere Volume (Hard)
Volume of sphere with radius 7 cm:
(a) $\frac{4}{3}\pi \times 343$  (b) $\frac{4}{3}\pi \times 49$  (c) $4\pi \times 49$  (d) None

## Q15. SI (Hard)
₹10000 at 8% SI for 3 years yields:
(a) ₹2400  (b) ₹2000  (c) ₹3000  (d) ₹2500

**Answer:** (a) ₹2400
**Solution:** $SI = \frac{10000 \times 8 \times 3}{100} = 2400$
**Shortcut:** $\frac{PRT}{100}$.
**Common Mistake:** Using CI.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q16. Boats (Hard)
Boat takes 4 hrs downstream (30 km) and 6 hrs return. Still water speed?
(a) 6.25 km/h  (b) 7.5 km/h  (c) 5 km/h  (d) 8 km/h

**Answer:** (a) 6.25 km/h
**Solution:** Down = 30/4 = 7.5. Up = 30/6 = 5. Still = (7.5+5)/2 = 6.25.
**Shortcut:** Average of down and up.
**Common Mistake:** Difference.
**Time target:** 90 sec

## Q17. Logarithm (Hard)
If $\log_{10} 2 = 0.3010$, find $\log_{10} 8$.
(a) 0.6020  (b) 0.9030  (c) 0.3010  (d) 0.7526

**Answer:** (b) 0.9030
**Solution:** $\log 8 = 3 \log 2 = 3 \times 0.3010 = 0.9030$
**Shortcut:** $\log a^n = n \log a$.
**Common Mistake):** Using $0.3010$ directly.
**Time target:** 45 sec

## Q18. Height-Distance (Hard)
Pole shadow is $\sqrt{3}$ times its height. Sun's elevation?
(a) 30°  (b) 45°  (c) 60°  (d) 75°

**Answer:** (a) 30°
**Solution:** $\tan\theta = h/(\sqrt{3}h) = 1/\sqrt{3} \Rightarrow \theta = 30°$
**Shortcut:** tan = 1/√3 → 30°.
**Common Mistake:** Computing 60°.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q19. Probability Bag (Hard)
Bag: 5 red, 4 blue. Two balls drawn. P(both red)?
(a) 1/3  (b) 5/18  (c) 5/9  (d) 1/9

**Answer:** (b) 5/18
**Solution:** $\frac{{}^5C_2}{{}^9C_2} = \frac{10}{36} = 5/18$
**Shortcut:** Combinations.
**Common Mistake:** Permutations.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q20. Time-Work (Hard)
A can do in 10 days, B in 12 days. Together?
(a) 5 days  (b) 6 days  (c) 60/11 days  (d) 11 days

**Answer:** (c) 60/11 days
**Solution:** $\frac{1}{10} + \frac{1}{12} = \frac{6+5}{60} = \frac{11}{60}$. Time = 60/11 days.
**Shortcut:** $\frac{1}{A} + \frac{1}{B}$.
**Common Mistake:** Just averaging.
**Time target:** 90 sec
