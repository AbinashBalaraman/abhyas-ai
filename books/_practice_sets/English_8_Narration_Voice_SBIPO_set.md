# English Chapter 8: Direct-Indirect Narration & Active-Passive Voice

> **Source:** `books/English_Language/08_Narration_Voice_ActivePassive.md`

### Q1. He said, "I am reading." — Indirect:
(a) He said that he is reading
(b) He said that he was reading
(c) He said that he has been reading
(d) He said that he reads

**Answer:** (b) He said that he was reading

**Solution:** "**am reading**" (Present Continuous) → "**was reading**" (Past Continuous). [Source: §2.3]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "is reading" — not applying backshift.

**Time Target:** 5 sec

---

### Q2. Active: "The dog bit the man." — Passive:
(a) The man was bitten by the dog
(b) The man is bitten by the dog
(c) The dog was bitten by the man
(d) The man was biting the dog

**Answer:** (a) The man was bitten by the dog

**Solution:** **Past Simple** (bit) → **Past Simple Passive** (was + V3). "The man" (object) becomes subject. [Source: §3.3]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (c) "dog was bitten" — confusing subject and object.

**Time Target:** 5 sec

---

### Q3. He said, "I am going home." — Indirect:
(a) He said that he is going home
(b) He said that he was going home
(c) He said that he goes home
(d) He said that he has gone home

**Answer:** (b) He said that he was going home

**Solution:** Present Continuous "am going" → Past Continuous "was going". [Source: §2.3]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "is going" — not backshifting.

**Time Target:** 5 sec

---

### Q4. Active: "She writes a letter." — Passive:
(a) A letter is written by her
(b) A letter was written by her
(c) A letter has been written by her
(d) A letter is being written by her

**Answer:** (a) A letter is written by her

**Solution:** **Present Simple** (writes) → **Present Simple Passive** (is + V3). [Source: §3.3]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (b) "was written" (Past — wrong tense).

**Time Target:** 5 sec

---

### Q5. He said, "I have been waiting." — Indirect:
(a) He said that he has been waiting
(b) He said that he had been waiting
(c) He said that he was waiting
(d) He said that he waited

**Answer:** (b) He said that he had been waiting

**Solution:** **Present Perfect Continuous** → **Past Perfect Continuous** (had been + V1+ing). [Source: §2.3]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "has been waiting" — not applying backshift.

**Time Target:** 5 sec

---

### Q6. Active: "We must complete the task." — Passive:
(a) The task must complete by us
(b) The task must be completed by us
(c) The task is completed by us
(d) The task must be complete by us

**Answer:** (b) The task must be completed by us

**Solution:** **Modal (must) + passive** = "**must + be + V3**". [Source: §3.5]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "must complete" (active form) or missing "be".

**Time Target:** 5 sec

---

### Q7. He said, "I will be there tomorrow." — Indirect:
(a) He said that he will be there tomorrow
(b) He said that he would be there the next day
(c) He said that he would be there tomorrow
(d) He said that he is there the next day

**Answer:** (b) He said that he would be there the next day

**Solution:** **Three changes:**
1. "**will**" → "**would**"
2. "**I**" → "**he**"
3. "**tomorrow**" → "**the next day**"

[Source: §2.3, §2.4, §2.5]

**Common Mistake:** Keeping "tomorrow" unchanged (option c).

**Time Target:** 5 sec

---

### Q8. Active: "He teaches us English." — Passive:
(a) We are taught English by him
(b) English is taught to us by him
(c) Both (a) and (b)
(d) We teach English by him

**Answer:** (c) Both (a) and (b)

**Solution:** **Two objects** (direct: English; indirect: us) → either object can become subject in passive. [Source: §3.6 Voice with Two Objects]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing only one — both are valid passives.

**Time Target:** 5 sec

---

### Q9. He said, "I could help you." — Indirect:
(a) He said that he could help me
(b) He said that he can help me
(c) He said that he would help me
(d) He said that he helps me

**Answer:** (a) He said that he could help me

**Solution:** **"Could"** (already past modal) does NOT backshift — it remains "could". Pronoun: "I" → "he", "you" → "me". [Source: §2.4 — already-past modals don't change]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (b) "can" — wrongly backshifting "could".

**Time Target:** 5 sec

---

### Q10. Active: "Someone has stolen my bag." — Passive:
(a) My bag has been stolen by someone
(b) My bag is stolen by someone
(c) My bag was stolen by someone
(d) My bag is being stolen by someone

**Answer:** (a) My bag has been stolen by someone

**Solution:** **Present Perfect** (has stolen) → **Present Perfect Passive** (has been + V3). [Source: §3.3]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (b) "is stolen" (wrong aspect).

**Time Target:** 5 sec
