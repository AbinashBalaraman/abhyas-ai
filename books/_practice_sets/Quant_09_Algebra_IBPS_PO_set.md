## Q1. Linear (Easy)
Solve 3x − 7 = 8:
(a) 3  (b) 4  (c) 5  (d) 6

**Answer:** (c) 5
**Solution:** 3x = 15 → x = 5. ✓
**Shortcut:** Direct.
**Common Mistake:** Arithmetic.
**Time target:** 15 sec

---

## Q2. Roots (Easy)
Roots of x² − 7x + 12 = 0:
(a) 3, 4  (b) 2, 6  (c) 1, 12  (d) −3, −4

**Answer:** (a) 3, 4
**Solution:** (x−3)(x−4) = 0. ✓
**Shortcut:** Factorize.
**Common Mistake:** Sign.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q3. Identity (Easy — Trap)
If a + b = 5, ab = 6, then a² + b² = ?
(a) 11  (b) 12  (c) 13  (d) 14

**Answer:** (c) 13
**Solution:** 25 − 12 = 13. ✓
**Shortcut:** (a+b)² − 2ab.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting the 2ab.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q4. Surd (Medium)
√50 + √18 = ?
(a) 7√2  (b) 8√2  (c) 6√2  (d) 5√2

**Answer:** (b) 8√2
**Solution:** 5√2 + 3√2 = 8√2. ✓
**Shortcut:** Simplify first.
**Common Mistake:** Wrong coefficient.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q5. Discriminant (Medium)
D of x² − 4x + 4 = 0:
(a) 0  (b) 1  (c) 2  (d) 4

**Answer:** (a) 0
**Solution:** 16 − 16 = 0. ✓
**Shortcut:** b²−4ac.
**Common Mistake:** Wrong sign.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q6. Sum of roots (Medium)
Sum of roots of 2x² + 5x − 3 = 0:
(a) −5/2  (b) 5/2  (c) −3/2  (d) 3/2

**Answer:** (a) −5/2
**Solution:** −b/a = −5/2. ✓
**Shortcut:** Vieta.
**Common Mistake:** Sign.
**Time target:** 15 sec

## Q7. Quadratic with min (Medium)
Min of x² + 4x + 1:
(a) −3  (b) 1  (c) −1  (d) 3

**Answer:** (a) −3
**Solution:** (x+2)² − 4 + 1 = (x+2)² − 3. Min = −3. ✓
**Shortcut:** Complete the square.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting the constant.
**Time target:** 45 sec

---

## Q8. Linear 2 var (Medium)
Solve x + y = 7, x − y = 3. Find x:
(a) 3  (b) 4  (c) 5  (d) 6

**Answer:** (c) 5
**Solution:** Add: 2x = 10 → x = 5. ✓
**Shortcut:** Add equations.
**Common Mistake:** Subtracting.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q9. Word problem (Hard)
A is twice B. Sum of ages is 36. A's age:
(a) 12  (b) 18  (c) 24  (d) 30

**Answer:** (c) 24
**Solution:** 2B + B = 36 → B = 12, A = 24. ✓
**Shortcut:** Substitute.
**Common Mistake:** Wrong ratio.
**Time target:** 45 sec

---

## Q10. a³ + b³ (Hard — Trap)
If a + b = 4, ab = 3, find a³ + b³:
(a) 28  (b) 36  (c) 64  (d) 48

**Answer:** (a) 28
**Solution:** 64 − 3·3·4 = 64 − 36 = 28. ✓
**Shortcut:** (a+b)³ − 3ab(a+b).
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting the 3.
**Time target:** 60 sec
