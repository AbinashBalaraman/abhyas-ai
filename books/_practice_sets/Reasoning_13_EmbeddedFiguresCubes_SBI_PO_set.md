## Q1. Cube Total 5 (Easy)
A cube of side 5 painted, cut. Total?
(a) 25  (b) 100  (c) 125  (d) 625

**Answer:** (c) 125
**Solution:** 5³ = 125.
**Shortcut:** n³.
**Common Mistake:** n².
**Time target:** 15 sec

## Q8. Rectangles 2×3 (Medium)
Rectangles in 2×3 grid:
(a) 12  (b) 15  (c) 18  (d) 24

**Answer:** (c) 18
**Solution:** C(3,2)×C(4,2) = 3×6 = 18.
**Shortcut:** C(m+1,2)×C(n+1,2).
**Common Mistake):** Other.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q9. Squares 4×4 (Medium)

## Q11. Rectangles 4×4 (Hard)
Rectangles in 4×4 grid:
(a) 64  (b) 100  (c) 120  (d) 144

**Answer:** (b) 100
**Solution:** C(5,2)² = 10² = 100.
**Shortcut:** C(m+1,2)².

## Q12. Painted 7×7 (Hard)
7×7×7 cube painted. At least 1 face painted?
(a) 125  (b) 218  (c) 296  (d) 343

**Answer:** (b) 218
**Solution:** 343 - 125 = 218.
**Shortcut:** Total - interior.
**Common Mistake):** Just 343.
**Time target:** 60 sec
