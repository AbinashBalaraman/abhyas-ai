# IBPS PO Prelims — Geometry (Triangles, Circles, Quadrilaterals)

## Q1. Triangle type (Easy)
Triangle angles 60°, 70°, 50°. It is:
(a) Acute  (b) Right  (c) Obtuse  (d) Cannot determine

**Answer:** (a) Acute
**Solution:** All angles < 90°. ✓
**Shortcut:** All < 90° = acute.
**Common Mistake:** Confusing types.
**Time target:** 15 sec

---

## Q2. Right triangle (Easy)
Right triangle legs 6, 8. Hypotenuse:
(a) 9  (b) 10  (c) 12  (d) 14

**Answer:** (b) 10
**Solution:** 36+64 = 100 = 10². ✓
**Shortcut:** 6-8-10 triple.
**Common Mistake:** Wrong sum.
**Time target:** 20 sec

---

## Q3. Equilateral area (Easy — Trap)
Equilateral triangle side 8. Area:
(a) 12√3  (b) 14√3  (c) 16√3  (d) 18√3

**Answer:** (c) 16√3
**Solution:** (√3/4)·64 = 16√3. ✓
**Shortcut:** Direct.
**Common Mistake:** Computation.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q4. Inradius (Medium)
Triangle area 24, semiperimeter 8. Inradius:
(a) 2  (b) 3  (c) 4  (d) 6

**Answer:** (b) 3
**Solution:** r = Δ/s = 24/8 = 3. ✓
**Shortcut:** Direct.
**Common Mistake:** Multiplying.
**Time target:** 15 sec

---

## Q5. Square inscribed in circle (Medium)
Circle radius 5, square inscribed. Square side:
(a) 5  (b) 5√2  (c) 10  (d) 5√3

**Answer:** (b) 5√2
**Solution:** Diagonal = 10, side = 10/√2 = 5√2. ✓
**Shortcut:** Diagonal = 2r.
**Common Mistake:** Setting side = r.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q6. Rhombus side (Medium)
Rhombus diagonals 8, 6. Side:
(a) 5  (b) 6  (c) 7  (d) 10

**Answer:** (a) 5
**Solution:** side² = (4²+3²) = 25 → side = 5. ✓
**Shortcut:** (d1/2)² + (d2/2)² = a².
**Common Mistake:** Using full diagonals.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q7. Tangent secant (Medium)
Tangent 6, secant nearer part 4. Find farther part:
(a) 5  (b) 8  (c) 9  (d) 10

**Answer:** (a) 5
**Solution:** 6² = 4·(4+x) → 36 = 16+4x → x = 5. ✓
**Shortcut:** Power of a point.
**Common Mistake:** Setting up wrong.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q8. Triangle medians (Hard)
Medians 3, 4, 5. Original area:
(a) 8  (b) 12  (c) 16  (d) 20

**Answer:** (a) 8
**Solution:** Medians form right triangle (3²+4²=25=5²). Area of medians triangle = ½·3·4 = 6. Original = (4/3)·6 = 8. ✓
**Shortcut:** (4/3) × median triangle area.
**Common Mistake:** Multiplying by 3/4.
**Time target:** 90 sec

---

## Q9. Cyclic quadrilateral (Hard)
Cyclic quad angles are 70°, 110°, 80°, ?. Last angle:
(a) 100°  (b) 90°  (c) 110°  (d) 80°

**Answer:** (a) 100°
**Solution:** Sum = 360°. 70+110+80+? = 360 → ? = 100. ✓ Also opposite to 80° (which pairs with 100). ✓
**Shortcut:** Sum 360 + opposite 180.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting sum 360.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q10. Two circles tangent (Hard — Trap)
Two circles radii 3, 2 internally tangent. Distance between centers:
(a) 1  (b) 5  (c) 6  (d) 12

**Answer:** (a) 1
**Solution:** Internal tangency: d = |r1−r2| = 1. ✓
**Shortcut:** |r1−r2| for internal.
**Common Mistake:** Adding radii.
**Time target:** 30 sec
