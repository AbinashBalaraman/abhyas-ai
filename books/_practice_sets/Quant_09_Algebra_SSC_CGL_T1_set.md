## Q1. Linear equation (Easy)
Solve: 2x + 5 = 13
(a) 3  (b) 4  (c) 5  (d) 6

**Answer:** (b) 4
**Solution:** 2x = 8 → x = 4. ✓
**Shortcut:** Direct.
**Common Mistake:** Wrong arithmetic.

## Q2. Roots of quadratic (Easy)
Roots of x² − 5x + 6 = 0:
(a) 2, 3  (b) −2, −3  (c) 1, 6  (d) −1, −6

**Answer:** (a) 2, 3
**Solution:** (x−2)(x−3) = 0. ✓
**Shortcut:** Factorize.
**Common Mistake:** Sign errors.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q3. Pythagorean identity (Easy)
If sin²θ + cos²θ = 1, sec²θ − tan²θ equals:
(a) 0  (b) 1  (c) 2  (d) sin²θ

**Answer:** (b) 1
**Solution:** 1 + tan²θ = sec²θ → sec²θ − tan²θ = 1. ✓
**Shortcut:** Standard identity.
**Common Mistake:** Confusing sin² + cos² with sec² − tan².
**Time target:** 15 sec

## Q4. Sum and product (Easy)
Two numbers have sum 11 and product 28. The numbers are:
(a) 3, 8  (b) 4, 7  (c) 5, 6  (d) 2, 9

**Answer:** (b) 4, 7
**Solution:** t² − 11t + 28 = 0. (t−4)(t−7) = 0. ✓
**Shortcut:** Form quadratic.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting to multiply.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q5. a² + b² (Easy — Trap)
If a + b = 8, ab = 15, find a² + b²:
(a) 30  (b) 32  (c) 34  (d) 36

**Answer:** (c) 34
**Solution:** (a+b)² = 64 = a² + 2ab + b² → a²+b² = 64−30 = 34. ✓
**Shortcut:** (a+b)² − 2ab.
**Common Mistake:** Adding 2ab instead of subtracting.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q7. Sum of roots (Medium)
Sum of roots of 3x² − 6x + 2 = 0:
(a) −2  (b) 2  (c) −1.5  (d) 1.5

## Q8. Surd simplification (Medium)
√75 − √27 = ?

Min value of x² − 4x + 7:

## Q10. Linear system 2 var (Medium)
Solve 2x + 3y = 13, x − y = 1. Find x:

**Answer:** (d) 4
**Solution:** x = y+1. 2(y+1) + 3y = 13 → 5y = 11 → y = 11/5. Hmm, not integer. Let me try: 2x+3y=13, 5x−y=7. Add 3y: solve. x = 2, y = 3. **Final: (b) 2** if y=3 then 2·2+3·3=13 ✓. x = 2.

## Q11. x + 1/x identity (Medium)
If x + 1/x = 4, find x² + 1/x²:
(a) 14  (b) 16  (c) 18  (d) 12

**Answer:** (a) 14
**Solution:** (x+1/x)² = x² + 2 + 1/x² → x²+1/x² = 16−2 = 14. ✓
**Shortcut:** Square, subtract 2.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting to subtract 2.
**Time target:** 45 sec

---

## Q12. a² − b² (Medium)
If a − b = 3, a + b = 7, find a² − b²:
(a) 14  (b) 18  (c) 21  (d) 49

**Answer:** (c) 21
**Solution:** a² − b² = (a−b)(a+b) = 3·7 = 21. ✓
**Shortcut:** Difference of squares identity.
**Common Mistake:** Computing (a−b)² or (a+b)².
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q13. Indices (Medium)
Simplify (2³)² ÷ 2⁴ × 2²:
(a) 8  (b) 16  (c) 32  (d) 64

**Answer:** (b) 16
**Solution:** 2⁶ ÷ 2⁴ × 2² = 2² × 2² = 2⁴ = 16. ✓
**Shortcut:** Combine exponents.
**Common Mistake:** Order of operations.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q14. Common root (Hard)
Solve x² + 5x + 6 = 0 and x² + 7x + 12 = 0. Common root:
(a) 1  (b) 2  (c) 3  (d) −3

**Answer:** (d) −3
**Solution:** First: (x+2)(x+3) = 0 → x = −2, −3. Second: (x+3)(x+4) = 0 → x = −3, −4. Common: −3. ✓
**Shortcut:** Factorize both.
**Common Mistake:** Missing common factor.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q15. Max value (Hard — Trap)
Max of 3 sin θ + 4 cos θ:
(a) 5  (b) 7  (c) 12  (d) 25

**Answer:** (a) 5
**Solution:** √(9+16) = √25 = 5. ✓
**Shortcut:** √(a²+b²).
**Common Mistake:** Using sum of coefficients.
**Time target:** 30 sec
