### Q1.
"One who writes dictionaries" is called:
- (a) Lexicographer (b) Linguist (c) Philologist (d) Calligrapher

**Answer:** (a) Lexicographer
**Solution:** Lexicographer = dictionary writer. Linguist = language scholar. Philologist = historical language scholar. Calligrapher = beautiful handwriting specialist.
**Grammar Rule:** OWS — Person category.
**Common Mistake:** Confusing lexicographer with linguist.
**Time Target:** 25 sec

---

### Q2.
"CANDID" is most similar in meaning to:
- (a) Secretive (b) Frank (c) Cunning (d) Polite

**Answer:** (b) Frank
**Solution:** Candid = honest, frank, outspoken. Secretive is the antonym.
**Grammar Rule:** Synonym — meaning match.
**Common Mistake:** Choosing "polite" (related but not synonymous).
**Time Target:** 20 sec

---

### Q3.
"ALTRUISM" is most similar in meaning to:
- (a) Selfishness (b) Selflessness (c) Greed (d) Egoism

**Answer:** (b) Selflessness
**Solution:** Altruism = selfless concern for others. Selfishness/egoism are antonyms.
**Grammar Rule:** Synonym — Latin root "alter" (other).
**Common Mistake:** Choosing selfishness (antonym).
**Time Target:** 20 sec

---

### Q4.
"A person who is against change" is called:
- (a) Radical (b) Conservative (c) Liberal (d) Progressive

**Answer:** (b) Conservative
**Solution:** Conservative = opposed to change. Radical/liberal/progressive all favor change.
**Grammar Rule:** OWS — Person category.
**Common Mistake:** Confusing "conservative" with "preservative."
**Time Target:** 25 sec

---

### Q5.
Choose the correctly spelt word:
- (a) Recieve (b) Receive (c) Receeve (d) Receeve

**Answer:** (b) Receive
**Solution:** "Receive" = ei after c (i before e rule: except after c).
**Grammar Rule:** Spelling — i before e (except after c).
**Common Mistake:** Using "ie" after c (recieve is wrong).
**Time Target:** 20 sec

---

### Q6.
"LUCID" is most similar in meaning to:
- (a) Confusing (b) Clear (c) Dark (d) Muddled

**Answer:** (b) Clear
**Solution:** Lucid = clear, easily understood. Confusing/muddled are antonyms.
**Grammar Rule:** Synonym — Latin "lux" (light).
**Common Mistake:** Choosing "confusing" (antonym).
**Time Target:** 20 sec

---

### Q7.
"To leave no stone unturned" means:
- (a) To be careless (b) To make every possible effort (c) To be lazy (d) To give up

**Answer:** (b) To make every possible effort
**Solution:** "Leave no stone unturned" = try every possible means.
**Grammar Rule:** Idiom — figurative meaning.
**Common Mistake:** Taking literally (turning stones).
**Time Target:** 25 sec

---

### Q8.
"PRAGMATIC" is most similar in meaning to:
- (a) Idealistic (b) Practical (c) Theoretical (d) Impractical

**Answer:** (b) Practical
**Solution:** Pragmatic = dealing with things sensibly and realistically. Idealistic is the opposite.
**Grammar Rule:** Synonym — Greek "pragma" (deed).
**Common Mistake:** Choosing "idealistic" (antonym).
**Time Target:** 25 sec

---

### Q9.
Choose the correctly spelt word:
- (a) Embarass (b) Embarrass (c) Embarras (d) Embarras

**Answer:** (b) Embarrass
**Solution:** "Embarrass" = double 'r' + double 's'.
**Grammar Rule:** Spelling — root doubled before suffix.
**Common Mistake:** Single 'r' or 's'.
**Time Target:** 20 sec

---

### Q10.
"A person who walks in sleep" is called:
- (a) Somnambulist (b) Somniloquist (c) Insomniac (d) Narcoleptic

**Answer:** (a) Somnambulist
**Solution:** Somnambulist = sleepwalker. Insomniac = can't sleep. Somniloquist = sleep-talker.
**Grammar Rule:** OWS — Person category; "somn-" (sleep) + "ambul" (walk).
**Common Mistake:** Confusing sleep-related OWS.
**Time Target:** 25 sec

## SBI PO Prelims Strategy

1. **Banking vocabulary:** Focus on finance/economics OWS (e.g., "fiscal," "monetary").
2. **Latin/Greek roots:** "Alter" (other), "lux" (light), "somn" (sleep) help decode.
3. **Idiom meaning:** Decode literal image, then apply figurative meaning.
4. **Spelling: i before e:** "Receive" (ei after c); "Believe" (ie elsewhere).
