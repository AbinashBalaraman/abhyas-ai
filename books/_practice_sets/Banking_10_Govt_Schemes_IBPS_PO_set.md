### Q1. PM SVANidhi is for:
(a) Farmers (b) Street vendors (c) Women (d) Students

**Answer: (b) Street vendors**

**Solution:** PM SVANidhi (PM Street Vendor's AtmaNirbhar Nidhi) is for street vendors (urban). It was launched on 1 June 2020 (during COVID-19). The loan amounts are in 3 tranches: ₹10,000 → ₹20,000 → ₹50,000. There is an interest subsidy of 7% on timely repayment. No collateral is required. The scheme aims to provide working capital to street vendors who were hit by the COVID-19 pandemic and lockdown. PM SVANidhi is part of the Aatmanirbhar Bharat Abhiyan. The scheme is implemented through banks and MFIs. Cashback of ₹100 per month for digital transactions.

### Q2. ECLGS was launched in:
(a) 2018 (b) 2019 (c) 2020 (d) 2021

**Answer: (c) 2020**

**Solution:** ECLGS (Emergency Credit Line Guarantee Scheme) was launched on 24 March 2020 (during COVID-19). The scheme is by NCGTC (National Credit Guarantee Trustee Company). ECLGS is for MSMEs, MUDRA borrowers, and businesses. The scheme provides 100% credit guarantee to banks. The original amount was ₹3 lakh crore (subsequently expanded to ₹4.5 lakh crore). The loan is up to 20% of outstanding credit, with a maximum of ₹5 crore. ECLGS was part of the Aatmanirbhar Bharat package (₹20 lakh crore economic package announced 12 May 2020). ECLGS provided liquidity support during the COVID-19 pandemic.

**Time Target:** 30s | **Chapter Reference:** Banking/10_Govt_Schemes.md, p.155-165 | **VERIFY:** No

### Q3. The Aatmanirbhar Bharat package was announced on:
(a) 1 January 2020 (b) 12 May 2020 (c) 1 June 2020 (d) 15 August 2020

**Answer: (b) 12 May 2020**

**Solution:** The Aatmanirbhar Bharat (Self-Reliant India) package was announced on 12 May 2020 by PM Modi. The package is worth ₹20 lakh crore (about 10% of GDP). The 5 pillars: Economy, Infrastructure, System, Demography, Demand. The package was announced in 5 phases. Multiple schemes within the package: ECLGS, PM SVANidhi, Garib Kalyan Rojgar Abhiyaan, etc. The package aimed to make India self-reliant after the COVID-19 pandemic. It focused on land, labour, liquidity, and laws. The Aatmanirbhar Bharat package is a major economic reform.

**Time Target:** 30s | **Chapter Reference:** Banking/10_Govt_Schemes.md, p.170-180 | **VERIFY:** No

### Q4. PM-KISAN provides direct income support of:
(a) ₹2,000/year (b) ₹4,000/year (c) ₹6,000/year (d) ₹10,000/year

**Answer: (c) ₹6,000/year**

**Solution:** PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi) provides direct income support of ₹6,000 per year to farmers. The amount is paid in 3 installments of ₹2,000 each. The scheme was launched on 24 February 2019 (interim budget 2019-20). The scheme aims to provide financial support to small and marginal farmers. The amount is directly transferred to the bank accounts of farmers via DBT. PM-KISAN is part of the government's effort to double farmers' income. The scheme covers all land-holding farmer families.

**Time Target:** 30s | **Chapter Reference:** Banking/10_Govt_Schemes.md, p.186-192 | **VERIFY:** No

### Q5. The PMJDY life insurance cover is:
(a) ₹10,000 (b) ₹30,000 (c) ₹1 lakh (d) ₹2 lakh

**Answer: (b) ₹30,000**

**Solution:** The PMJDY life insurance cover is ₹30,000 (for accounts opened between 15 August 2014 - 31 January 2015). PMJDY was launched on 28 August 2014. Other features: zero-balance account (BSBDA - Basic Savings Bank Deposit Account), RuPay debit card, accident insurance up to ₹2 lakh, overdraft up to ₹10,000. The life insurance of ₹30,000 was a special introductory offer for early accounts. After 31 January 2015, new accounts may not have the same life insurance cover. PMJDY has been hugely successful in financial inclusion.

**Time Target:** 30s | **Chapter Reference:** Banking/10_Govt_Schemes.md, p.30-32 | **VERIFY:** No

### Q6. PMSBY provides partial disability cover of:
(a) ₹50,000 (b) ₹1 lakh (c) ₹2 lakh (d) ₹5 lakh

**Answer: (b) ₹1 lakh**

**Solution:** PMSBY (Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana) provides partial disability cover of ₹1 lakh. Full disability and accidental death cover is ₹2 lakh. The premium is ₹20 per year (revised from ₹12 originally on 1 June 2022). Available to savings bank account holders aged 18-70 years. The premium is auto-debited from the bank account. PMSBY was launched on 9 May 2015 by PM Modi. PMSBY is one of the 3 social security schemes launched on 9 May 2015 (along with PMJJBY and APY). PMSBY provides affordable accident insurance to millions.

**Time Target:** 30s | **Chapter Reference:** Banking/10_Govt_Schemes.md, p.69-78 | **VERIFY:** No

### Q7. The PMJDY accident insurance cover is:
(a) ₹1 lakh (b) ₹2 lakh (c) ₹5 lakh (d) ₹10 lakh

**Answer: (b) ₹2 lakh**

**Solution:** The PMJDY (Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana) accident insurance cover is up to ₹2 lakh. PMJDY was launched on 28 August 2014. The account features: zero-balance (BSBDA - Basic Savings Bank Deposit Account), RuPay debit card, accident insurance up to ₹2 lakh, life insurance ₹30,000 (for accounts opened between 15 Aug 2014 - 31 Jan 2015), and overdraft up to ₹10,000. The accident insurance is for the RuPay card holder. PMJDY has brought millions of unbanked Indians into the formal banking system.

**Time Target:** 30s | **Chapter Reference:** Banking/10_Govt_Schemes.md, p.28-30 | **VERIFY:** No

### Q8. APY is named after which former PM?
(a) Jawaharlal Nehru (b) Indira Gandhi (c) Atal Bihari Vajpayee (d) Rajiv Gandhi

**Answer: (c) Atal Bihari Vajpayee**

**Solution:** APY (Atal Pension Yojana) is named after former Prime Minister Atal Bihari Vajpayee. APY was relaunched (after the original Swavalamban Scheme of 2010-11) on 9 May 2015 by PM Modi in Kolkata. The scheme is targeted at the unorganized sector. The eligibility is 18-40 years. The pension range is ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 per month at age 60. APY has 7.64 crore active subscribers as of April 2026. The scheme is regulated by PFRDA. APY provides guaranteed pension by the Government of India.

**Time Target:** 30s | **Chapter Reference:** Banking/10_Govt_Schemes.md, p.97-110 | **VERIFY:** No

PMSBY was launched on 9 May 2015 by PM Modi. The scheme is renewable annually. PMSBY is one of the 3 social security schemes launched on 9 May 2015 (along with PMJJBY and APY).

### Q10. SSY's current interest rate is approximately:
(a) 6% (b) 7% (c) 8% (d) 9%

**Answer: (c) 8%**

**Solution:** SSY (Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana) has a current interest rate of approximately 8% per annum (8.2% for FY25-26; subject to quarterly revisions by the Government). SSY was launched on 22 January 2015 by PM Modi as part of the "Beti Bachao, Beti Padhao" campaign. SSY is for the girl child below 10 years. The maximum deposit is ₹1.5 lakh per year. SSY provides tax benefits under Section 80C on deposits; interest is tax-free. The account matures when the girl turns 21. SSY is one of the highest interest rate small savings schemes.
