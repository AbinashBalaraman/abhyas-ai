# Chapter 1 — Grammar (Tenses, Voice, Narration) | IBPS PO Prelims

### Q1.
**Spot the error:** "He **is owning** a car."
- (a) He (b) is owning (c) a (d) car

**Answer:** (b) is owning
**Solution:** "Own" is a stative verb → no continuous form. Correct: "He **owns** a car."
**Grammar Rule:** Stative verbs (own, know, like, want, belong) do not admit progressive (-ing) form.
**Common Mistake:** Using "is -ing" with stative verbs.
**Time Target:** 15 sec

---

### Q2.
**Spot the error:** "The temple **is standing** in the heart of the city."
- (a) The temple (b) is standing (c) in the heart of the city (d) No error

**Answer:** (b) is standing
**Solution:** "Stand" (in the sense of "be located") is stative. Correct: "The temple **stands** in the heart of the city."
**Grammar Rule:** Stative verbs (stand, sit, lie, etc., in static sense) reject continuous.
**Common Mistake:** Treating "stand" as dynamic.
**Time Target:** 15 sec

---

### Q3.
**Convert to Passive:** "We **are hearing** the bell."
- (a) The bell is being heard by us. (b) The bell is heard by us. (c) The bell was heard by us. (d) The bell has been heard by us.

**Answer:** (b) The bell is heard by us.
**Solution:** "Hear" is stative → no continuous. The active "We hear the bell" → passive "The bell is heard by us."
**Grammar Rule:** Stative "hear" rejects continuous; passive uses simple present "is + V3."
**Common Mistake:** Writing "is being heard" (continuous passive) for stative verb.
**Time Target:** 20 sec

---

### Q4.
**Convert to Passive:** "He wrote a letter."
- (a) A letter was written by him. (b) A letter is written by him. (c) A letter has been written by him. (d) A letter was being written by him.

**Answer:** (a) A letter was written by him.
**Solution:** Simple Past → "was/were + V3." Singular subject "letter" → "was written."
**Grammar Rule:** Voice Rule I — Simple Past → was/were + V3.
**Common Mistake:** Using "is" (present) instead of "was" (past).
**Time Target:** 20 sec

---

### Q5.
**Convert to Indirect Speech:** Ram said, "I am happy."
- (a) Ram said that he is happy. (b) Ram said that he was happy. (c) Ram says that he is happy. (d) Ram said that I was happy.

**Answer:** (b) Ram said that he was happy.
**Solution:** Back-shift Present Continuous "am" → Past Continuous "was." Pronoun "I" → "he."
**Grammar Rule:** Narration back-shift: Present Continuous → Past Continuous.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting to back-shift tense.
**Time Target:** 25 sec

---

### Q6.
**Spot the error:** "She **has went** to the market."
- (a) She (b) has went (c) to the market (d) No error

**Answer:** (b) has went
**Solution:** "Went" is V2 (Simple Past). With "has" (auxiliary), V3 "gone" is required. Correct: "She has **gone** to the market."
**Grammar Rule:** Present Perfect = has/have + V3 (past participle).
**Common Mistake:** Confusing V2 ("went") with V3 ("gone") after "has/have."
**Time Target:** 20 sec

---

### Q7.
**Convert to Passive:** "The children were flying kites."
- (a) Kites were being flown by the children. (b) Kites were flown by the children. (c) Kites are flown by the children. (d) Kites are being flown by the children.

**Answer:** (a) Kites were being flown by the children.
**Solution:** Past Continuous → "was/were + being + V3." Plural subject "children" → "were being."
**Grammar Rule:** Voice Rule IV — Continuous → is/was being + V3.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting "being" (writing "were flown" which is Simple Past passive).
**Time Target:** 25 sec

---

### Q8.
**Convert to Indirect Speech:** He said, "The earth revolves around the sun."
- (a) He said that the earth revolved around the sun. (b) He said that the earth revolves around the sun. (c) He says the earth revolves around the sun. (d) He said that the earth will revolve around the sun.

**Answer:** (b) He said that the earth revolves around the sun.
**Solution:** Universal/scientific truth → NO back-shift.
**Grammar Rule:** Universal truths are not back-shifted in indirect speech.
**Common Mistake:** Back-shifting scientific facts.
**Time Target:** 25 sec

---

### Q9.
**Convert to Passive:** "They are building a new bridge."
- (a) A new bridge is built by them. (b) A new bridge is being built by them. (c) A new bridge was being built by them. (d) A new bridge has been built by them.

**Answer:** (b) A new bridge is being built by them.
**Solution:** Present Continuous → "is/am/are + being + V3." Singular subject "bridge" → "is being built."
**Grammar Rule:** Voice Rule IV — Continuous tenses → is/am/are + being + V3.
**Common Mistake:** Dropping "being" (writing "is built" which is Simple Present).
**Time Target:** 25 sec

---

### Q10.
**Spot the error:** "He **does not know** to swim."
- (a) He (b) does not know (c) to swim (d) No error

**Answer:** (c) to swim
**Solution:** "Know" is followed by "how + to-infinitive." Correct: "He does not know **how to** swim."
**Grammar Rule:** "Know" + "how to" + base verb.
**Common Mistake:** Writing "know to" without "how."
**Time Target:** 20 sec

## IBPS PO Prelims Strategy

1. **Speed first:** 30 sec/Q — read fast, identify rule, choose.
2. **Stative verb check:** First scan for "is/are + V-ing" with stative verbs.
3. **Verb form check:** V2 vs V3 confusion after "has/have/had" is a top trap.
4. **Skip hard Qs:** Mark and return if exceeds 30 sec.
