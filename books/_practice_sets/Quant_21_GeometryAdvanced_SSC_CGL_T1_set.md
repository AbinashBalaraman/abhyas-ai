## Q1. Hexagon Diagonal (Easy)
In a regular hexagon, find the ratio of the longest diagonal to the side.
(a) √3  (b) 2  (c) √3  (d) 1

**Answer:** (b) 2
**Solution:** In a regular hexagon, longest diagonal = 2 × side.
**Shortcut:** Memorize: hexagon longest diagonal = 2 × side.
**Common Mistake:** Using √3 (that's short diagonal ratio).
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q2. Octagon Angles (Easy)
The sum of interior angles of an octagon is:
(a) 900°  (b) 1080°  (c) 720°  (d) 1260°

**Answer:** (b) 1080°
**Solution:** $(8-2) \times 180° = 1080°$
**Shortcut:** $(n-2) \times 180°$.
**Common Mistake:** Using $(n-1) \times 180$.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q3. Decagon Diagonals (Easy — Trap)
How many diagonals does a decagon have?
(a) 25  (b) 35  (c) 45  (d) 55

**Answer:** (b) 35
**Solution:** $\frac{n(n-3)}{2} = \frac{10 \times 7}{2} = 35$
**Shortcut:** $\frac{n(n-3)}{2}$.
**Common Mistake:** Using $\frac{n(n-1)}{2}$.
**Time target:** 45 sec

## Q4. Tangent Length (Medium)
A tangent to a circle of radius 6 cm from an external point is 8 cm long. Find the distance from the point to the center.
(a) 10 cm  (b) 12 cm  (c) 14 cm  (d) 8 cm

**Answer:** (a) 10 cm
**Solution:** $\sqrt{8^2 + 6^2} = \sqrt{100} = 10$ (Pythagorean with 6-8-10).
**Shortcut:** Tangent ⊥ radius.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting to use Pythagorean.
**Time target:** 90 sec

## Q5. Power of Point (Medium)
In a circle, two chords AB and CD intersect at P inside. If PA = 4, PB = 6, PC = 3, find PD.
(a) 6  (b) 8  (c) 9  (d) 10

**Answer:** (b) 8
**Solution:** $PA \cdot PB = PC \cdot PD \Rightarrow 24 = 3 \cdot PD \Rightarrow PD = 8$
**Shortcut:** Power of a point: $PA \cdot PB = PC \cdot PD$.
**Common Mistake:** Using $4 + 6 = 10$.
**Time target:** 90 sec

## Q6. Cyclic Quadrilateral (Medium — Trap)
A cyclic quadrilateral has angles in AP. Find the largest angle.
(a) 90°  (b) 105°  (c) 120°  (d) 100°

**Answer:** (c) 120°
**Solution:** Angles in AP: a-3d, a-d, a+d, a+3d. Sum = 4a = 360, so a = 90. Cyclic: opposite angles sum to 180. (a-3d) + (a+d) = 180 → 2a - 2d = 180 → a - d = 90. With a = 90, d = 0. All 90° — that's a rectangle. For non-trivial, the angles are 60°, 75°, 105°, 120° (d=15, sum 360, opposite sum 180). Largest = 120°.
**Shortcut:** Use AP property and cyclic constraint.
**Common Mistake:** Missing the cyclic constraint.
**Time target:** 150 sec

## Q7. Chord Distance (Medium)
A chord of length 8 cm is at a distance of 3 cm from the center. Find the radius.
(a) 5 cm  (b) √73 cm  (c) √7 cm  (d) 4 cm

**Answer:** (a) 5 cm
**Solution:** $\sqrt{4^2 + 3^2} = 5$ (3-4-5).
**Shortcut:** Perpendicular from center bisects chord.
**Common Mistake:** Wrong Pythagorean.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q8. Inscribed Angle (Medium)
The angle subtended by a chord at the center is 60°. The angle subtended at any point on the major arc is:
(a) 30°  (b) 60°  (c) 90°  (d) 120°

**Answer:** (a) 30°
**Solution:** Inscribed angle = half of central angle.
**Shortcut:** Inscribed = half central.
**Common Mistake:** Returning same.
**Time target:** 45 sec

## Q9. Pentagon Angle (Medium)
In a regular pentagon, each interior angle is:
(a) 108°  (b) 120°  (c) 90°  (d) 100°

**Answer:** (a) 108°
**Solution:** $(5-2) \times 180/5 = 108°$
**Shortcut:** $(n-2) \times 180/n$.
**Common Mistake:** Using 100.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q10. Inscribed Circle (Hard)
A circle is inscribed in a square of side 8 cm. Find the radius of the circle.
(a) 2 cm  (b) 4 cm  (c) 8 cm  (d) 16 cm

**Answer:** (b) 4 cm
**Solution:** Radius = side/2 = 4.
**Shortcut:** Inscribed circle diameter = side.
**Common Mistake:** Using side.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q11. Touching Circles (Hard)
Two circles of radii 5 cm and 12 cm touch externally. Find the distance between their centers.
(a) 7 cm  (b) 17 cm  (c) 8.5 cm  (d) 60 cm

**Answer:** (b) 17 cm
**Solution:** $5 + 12 = 17$ (externally tangent).
**Shortcut:** Sum of radii for external.
**Common Mistake:** Difference for internal.
**Time target:** 45 sec

## Q12. Inscribed Square (Hard)
A square is inscribed in a circle of radius R. Find the side of the square.
(a) R  (b) R√2  (c) R/√2  (d) 2R

**Answer:** (b) R√2
**Solution:** Diagonal of square = 2R = side × √2 → side = R√2.
**Shortcut:** Diagonal = 2R (diameter).
**Common Mistake:** Using R/√2.
**Time target:** 90 sec

## Q13. Median = Altitude (Hard)
In a triangle, if a median is also an altitude, the triangle is:
(a) Right  (b) Acute  (c) Isosceles  (d) Obtuse

**Answer:** (c) Isosceles
**Solution:** Median = altitude implies two equal sides from the median vertex.
**Shortcut:** Recognize the property.
**Common Mistake:** Saying right.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q14. Inradius (Hard)
The inradius of a triangle with area 30 and semi-perimeter 10 is:
(a) 2  (b) 3  (c) 5  (d) 6

**Answer:** (b) 3
**Solution:** $r = A/s = 30/10 = 3$
**Shortcut:** $r = \text{Area}/\text{Semi-perimeter}$.
**Common Mistake:** Using full perimeter.
**Time target:** 45 sec

---

## Q15. Isosceles Area (Hard)
An isosceles triangle has equal sides 10 cm and base 12 cm. Find the area.
(a) 48 cm²  (b) 60 cm²  (c) 24 cm²  (d) 96 cm²

**Answer:** (a) 48 cm²
**Solution:** Height = $\sqrt{100 - 36} = 8$. Area = $\frac{1}{2} \times 12 \times 8 = 48$
**Shortcut:** $\sqrt{10^2 - 6^2}$ for height.
**Common Mistake):** Forgetting to halve the base.
**Time target:** 120 sec
