## Q1. Bank Vault (Easy)
Square vault with side 10 m. Diagonal?
(a) 10  (b) 10√2  (c) 20  (d) 5√2

**Answer:** (b) 10√2
**Solution:** Diagonal = side × √2 = 10√2.
**Shortcut:** $d = a\sqrt{2}$.
**Common Mistake:** Using $2a$.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q2. Circle Perimeter (Easy)
Circle radius 7 cm. Circumference?
(a) 22  (b) 44  (c) 154  (d) 14

**Answer:** (b) 44
**Solution:** $2\pi r = 2 \times 22/7 \times 7 = 44$
**Shortcut:** $2\pi r$.
**Common Mistake:** Computing area.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q3. Triangle Area (Easy)
Triangle base 8, height 5. Area?
(a) 13  (b) 40  (c) 20  (d) 30

**Answer:** (c) 20
**Solution:** $\frac{1}{2} \times 8 \times 5 = 20$
**Shortcut:** $\frac{1}{2} \times b \times h$.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting 1/2.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q4. Hexagon (Medium)
Regular hexagon perimeter 60. Side?
(a) 6  (b) 10  (c) 12  (d) 60

**Answer:** (b) 10
**Solution:** Side = 60/6 = 10.
**Shortcut:** Equal sides.
**Common Mistake:** Just 60.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q5. Circle Diameter (Medium)
Circle area 154. Radius?
(a) 7  (b) 14  (c) 49  (d) 11

**Answer:** (a) 7
**Solution:** $\pi r^2 = 154 \Rightarrow 22/7 \times r^2 = 154 \Rightarrow r^2 = 49 \Rightarrow r = 7$
**Shortcut:** $\pi r^2 = A$.
**Common Mistake:** Using diameter.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q6. Cube Volume (Medium)
Cube of side 5. Volume?
(a) 25  (b) 100  (c) 125  (d) 150

**Answer:** (c) 125
**Solution:** $5^3 = 125$
**Shortcut:** $a^3$.
**Common Mistake:** $a^2 = 25$.

## Q8. Sphere Volume (Medium)
Sphere radius 3. Volume?
(a) 36π  (b) 4π  (c) 12π  (d) 27π

**Answer:** (a) 36π
**Solution:** $\frac{4}{3}\pi r^3 = \frac{4}{3}\pi(27) = 36\pi$
**Shortcut:** $\frac{4}{3}\pi r^3$.
**Common Mistake:** Wrong exponent.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q9. Cylinder Volume (Medium)
Cylinder r = 7, h = 10. Volume?
(a) 440  (b) 1540  (c) 220  (d) 2200

**Answer:** (b) 1540
**Solution:** $\pi r^2 h = 22/7 \times 49 \times 10 = 1540$
**Shortcut:** $\pi r^2 h$.
**Common Mistake:** Missing π.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q10. Cone Volume (Hard)
Cone r = 3, h = 4. Volume?
(a) 12π  (b) 36π  (c) 24π  (d) 4π

**Answer:** (a) 12π
**Solution:** $\frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h = \frac{1}{3}\pi(9)(4) = 12\pi$
**Shortcut:** $\frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h$.
**Common Mistake:** Missing 1/3.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q11. Hemisphere (Hard)
Hemisphere radius 7. Total surface area (curved + base)?
(a) 154  (b) 308  (c) 462  (d) 231

**Answer:** (b) 308
**Solution:** $2\pi r^2 + \pi r^2 = 3\pi r^2 = 3 \times 22/7 \times 49 = 462$. Closest: (c) 462. Wait, $2\pi r^2 = 2 \times 22/7 \times 49 = 308$ (curved). Plus $\pi r^2 = 154$ (base). Total = 462. So **(c) 462**.
**Shortcut:** Total SA = $3\pi r^2$.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting the base.
**Time target:** 90 sec

## Q12. Pyramid (Hard)
Square pyramid base 6, height 4. Volume?
(a) 48  (b) 144  (c) 72  (d) 36

**Answer:** (a) 48
**Solution:** $\frac{1}{3} \times \text{base area} \times h = \frac{1}{3} \times 36 \times 4 = 48$
**Shortcut:** $\frac{1}{3} \times A \times h$.
**Common Mistake:** Missing 1/3.
**Time target:** 90 sec
