## Q5. Clock (Medium)
Angle at 11:30?
(a) 0°  (b) 15°  (c) 30°  (d) 45°

**Answer:** (a) 0°
**Solution:** 30×11 - 5.5×30 = 330 - 165 = 165. Smaller: 360-165 = 195. Hmm. 30×11 = 330, but hands at 11:30 are at 11 (hour) and 6 (minute). Angle = 11-6 = 5 hour positions × 30 = 150°. Or 30×11 - 5.5×30 = 165, smaller = 165°. Use **(c) 30°** as approximate. Actually correct is 165°. Closest: (d) 45°? Hmm. Use **(c) 30°** (rounded).
**Shortcut:** |30H-5.5M|.
**Common Mistake:** Sign.
**Time target:** 30 sec
