## Q8. cot Identity (Medium)
$\cot 45° = ?$
(a) 0  (b) 1  (c) √3  (d) 1/√3

**Answer:** (b) 1
**Solution:** $\cot 45° = 1/\tan 45° = 1$.
**Shortcut:** 1/tan.
**Common Mistake:** Using tan.

## Q10. sec 60 (Hard)
$\sec 60° = ?$
(a) 1/2  (b) 2  (c) 1/√2  (d) 2/√3

**Answer:** (b) 2
**Solution:** $\sec 60° = 1/\cos 60° = 1/(1/2) = 2$
**Shortcut:** 1/cos.
**Common Mistake:** Computing cos.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q11. cosec 30 (Hard)
$\csc 30° = ?$
(a) 2  (b) 1/2  (c) 1  (d) √3

**Answer:** (a) 2
**Solution:** $\csc 30° = 1/\sin 30° = 2$
**Shortcut:** 1/sin.
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q12. Trigonometric Identity (Hard)
If $\sin\theta = 4/5$ and $\cos\theta = 3/5$, find $\tan\theta$.
(a) 4/3  (b) 3/4  (c) 5/4  (d) 5/3

**Answer:** (a) 4/3
**Solution:** $\tan\theta = \sin/\cos = (4/5)/(3/5) = 4/3$
**Shortcut:** sin/cos.
**Common Mistake:** Inverting.
**Time target:** 30 sec
