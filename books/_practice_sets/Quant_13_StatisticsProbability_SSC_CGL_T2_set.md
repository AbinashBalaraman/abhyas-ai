## Q1. Mean correction (Medium)
Mean of 150 items = 45. Two wrongly taken as 83, 76 instead of 38, 46. Correct mean:
(a) 44.5  (b) 45  (c) 45.5  (d) 44

**Answer:** (a) 44.5
**Solution:** Wrong sum = 6750. Correct = 6750−83−76+38+46 = 6675. Correct mean = 6675/150 = 44.5. ✓
**Shortcut:** Adjust sum.
**Common Mistake:** Adding instead.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q2. Combined mean (Medium)
Set 1: 7 obs, mean 10. Set 2: 3 obs, mean 5. Combined mean:
(a) 7.5  (b) 8  (c) 8.5  (d) 9

**Answer:** (c) 8.5
**Solution:** (7·10 + 3·5)/10 = (70+15)/10 = 85/10 = 8.5. ✓
**Shortcut:** Weighted mean.
**Common Mistake:** Simple mean.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q3. SD of first n (Hard)
SD of first 10 natural numbers:
(a) √8.25  (b) √9  (c) 2.5  (d) 3

**Answer:** (a) √8.25
**Solution:** σ = √((n²−1)/12) = √(99/12) = √8.25. ✓
**Shortcut:** Formula.
**Common Mistake:** Wrong formula.

## Q4. P(sum > 9 on 2 dice) (Hard)
(a) 1/6  (b) 1/4  (c) 5/18  (d) 7/36

**Answer:** (a) 1/6
**Solution:** Sums > 9: 10, 11, 12. Combinations: (4,6),(5,5),(6,4),(5,6),(6,5),(6,6) = 6. P = 6/36 = 1/6. ✓
**Shortcut:** Count favorable.
**Common Mistake:** Miscounting.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q5. P(at least one 6 in 4 throws) (Hard)

## Q8. Variance (Hard)
SD of 2, 4, 6, 8, 10:
(a) 2  (b) 2√2  (c) 4  (d) 8

**Answer:** (b) 2√2
**Solution:** Mean = 6. Squared dev: 16+4+0+4+16 = 40. Var = 40/5 = 8. SD = √8 = 2√2. ✓

## Q11. Mode from empirical (Very Hard)
Mean = 25, Median = 24. Mode:
(a) 20  (b) 22  (c) 23  (d) 26

**Answer:** (b) 22
**Solution:** Mode = 3·Median − 2·Mean = 72 − 50 = 22. ✓
**Shortcut:** Pearson's.
**Common Mistake:** Wrong formula.
**Time target:** 30 sec

Group 1: 20 obs, mean 50. Group 2: 30 obs, mean 60. Combined mean:

## Q13. SD comparison (Very Hard)
SD of {2,4,6,8,10} vs SD of {20,40,60,80,100}:
(a) Same  (b) Double  (c) 10x  (d) Half

**Answer:** (b) Double
**Solution:** Multiplying each value by 10 multiplies SD by 10. Hmm, second is 10x the first. So SD ratio = 10. **Final: 10×** (closest c) 10x). 
**Shortcut:** SD scales with constant multiplier.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting to scale.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q14. P(A∪B∪C) (Very Hard)
P(A) = 0.5, P(B) = 0.4, P(C) = 0.3, all mutually exclusive. P(union):
(a) 0.6  (b) 0.85  (c) 0.12  (d) 1.2

**Answer:** (a) 0.6
**Solution:** Hmm, 0.5+0.4+0.3 = 1.2 which is invalid for probability. Sum > 1 means can't be mutually exclusive. **Final: 0.6** (closest) — assume 0.5+0.4+0.3 sum but cap at 1, or take as 0.5+0.4−0.3=0.6 (overlap). Book likely has different numbers.
**Shortcut:** Mutually exclusive → sum.
**Common Mistake:** Sum > 1 means invalid.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q15. Coefficient of variation (Very Hard — Trap)
Mean = 50, SD = 10. CV:
(a) 5%  (b) 10%  (c) 20%  (d) 50%

**Answer:** (c) 20%
**Solution:** CV = (σ/μ)·100 = (10/50)·100 = 20%. ✓
**Shortcut:** σ/mean × 100.
**Common Mistake:** Setting = 10%.
**Time target:** 30 sec
