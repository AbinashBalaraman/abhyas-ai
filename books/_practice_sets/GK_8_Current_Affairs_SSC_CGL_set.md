### Q4. Which Indian writer won the 2025 International Booker Prize?

**Answer: (b) Banu Mushtaq**

Jhumpa Lahiri is a Pulitzer Prize winner. Shehan Karunatilaka (Sri Lankan) won the 2022 Booker for "The Seven Moons of Maali Almeida".

### Q5. Operation Sindoor was conducted by India in:

**Solution:** Operation Sindoor (7-10 May 2025) was conducted by the Indian Army and Air Force in response to the 2025 Pahalgam attack (22 April 2025) where 26 tourists were killed by terrorists in J&K. This was a major India-Pakistan military standoff. On 28 July 2025, Operation Mahadev killed 3 LeT militants including the Pahalgam attack mastermind in Srinagar.

### Q7. Who became the second Indian in outer space in 2025?
(a) Rakesh Sharma (b) Shubhanshu Shukla (c) Kalpana Chawla (d) Sunita Williams

**Answer: (b) Shubhanshu Shukla**

**Solution:** Wing Commander Shubhanshu Shukla became the second Indian in space on 25 June 2025, as a group captain of Axiom Mission 4 (launched from Kennedy Space Center, USA). The first Indian in space was Rakesh Sharma (3 April 1984, Soviet Soyuz T-11). Kalpana Chawla (1962-2003) was an Indian-American NASA astronaut who died in the 2003 Columbia disaster. Sunita Williams is an Indian-American NASA astronaut (born 1965, currently in space on Boeing Starliner test flight).

### Q11. India's heaviest communications satellite launched in 2025 is:
(a) CMS-01 (b) CMS-02 (c) CMS-03 (d) GSAT-30

**Answer: (c) CMS-03**

**Solution:** ISRO launched CMS-03 (4,410 kg / 9,720 lb) on 2 November 2025, India's heaviest communications satellite. On 24 December 2025, ISRO launched an even heavier payload — the US-built AST SpaceMobile communications satellite (6,100 kg / 13,400 lb) — the heaviest payload ever launched by ISRO. EOS-09 satellite failed shortly after launch on 19 May 2025. NASA-ISRO's NISAR satellite (joint) was launched on 30 July 2025. The SpaDeX mission tested space docking capabilities for Chandrayaan-4 and Gaganyaan.
