## Q1. SI (Easy)
₹5000 at 10% SI for 2 years:
(a) ₹500  (b) ₹1000  (c) ₹1500  (d) ₹2000

**Answer:** (b) ₹1000
**Solution:** $SI = \frac{5000 \times 10 \times 2}{100} = 1000$
**Shortcut:** $\frac{PRT}{100}$.
**Common Mistake:** Using CI formula.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q2. Profit-Loss (Easy)
Item at ₹120 sold for ₹150. Profit%?
(a) 20%  (b) 25%  (c) 30%  (d) 15%

**Answer:** (b) 25%
**Solution:** $\frac{30}{120} \times 100 = 25\%$
**Shortcut:** (SP-CP)/CP × 100.
**Common Mistake:** Using SP.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q3. Average (Easy)
Average of 10, 20, 30, 40, 50:
(a) 25  (b) 30  (c) 35  (d) 20

**Answer:** (b) 30
**Solution:** $\frac{10+20+30+40+50}{5} = 30$
**Shortcut:** Sum/count.
**Common Mistake:** Median.
**Time target:** 20 sec

## Q4. Train Speed (Medium)
Train 200m passes platform 100m in 15s. Speed?
(a) 20 km/h  (b) 24 km/h  (c) 72 km/h  (d) 60 km/h

**Answer:** (c) 72 km/h
**Solution:** Total = 300m. Speed = 300/15 = 20 m/s = 72 km/h.
**Shortcut:** Convert m/s to km/h by ×18/5.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting platform.
**Time target:** 60 sec

---

## Q5. Time-Work (Medium)
Pipe A fills in 6 hours, B in 8 hours. Together?
(a) 7 hours  (b) 24/7 hours  (c) 4 hours  (d) 14 hours

**Answer:** (b) 24/7 hours
**Solution:** $\frac{1}{6} + \frac{1}{8} = \frac{4+3}{24} = \frac{7}{24}$. Time = 24/7.
**Shortcut:** LCM.
**Common Mistake:** Adding times.
**Time target:** 60 sec

---

## Q6. LCM (Medium)
LCM of 12, 15, 20:
(a) 30  (b) 60  (c) 120  (d) 180

**Answer:** (b) 60
**Solution:** $12 = 2^2 \cdot 3, 15 = 3 \cdot 5, 20 = 2^2 \cdot 5$. LCM = $2^2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 = 60$.
**Shortcut:** Max powers.
**Common Mistake:** Using HCF.
**Time target:** 60 sec

---

## Q7. Ratio (Medium)
A:B = 2:3, B:C = 4:5. A:B:C?
(a) 8:12:15  (b) 2:3:5  (c) 4:6:5  (d) 2:4:5

**Answer:** (a) 8:12:15
**Solution:** A:B = 2:3 = 8:12. B:C = 4:5 = 12:15. A:B:C = 8:12:15.
**Shortcut:** Make B common.
**Common Mistake:** Direct multiplication.
**Time target:** 90 sec

## Q8. Mensuration (Medium)
Volume of cube side 6:
(a) 36  (b) 72  (c) 216  (d) 180

**Answer:** (c) 216
**Solution:** $6^3 = 216$
**Shortcut:** $a^3$.
**Common Mistake):** $a^2$.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q9. Compound (Medium)
₹1000 at 10% CI for 2 years:
(a) ₹200  (b) ₹210  (c) ₹220  (d) ₹100

**Answer:** (b) ₹210
**Solution:** $1000(1.1)^2 - 1000 = 1210 - 1000 = 210$.
**Shortcut:** $P(1+r)^T - P$.
**Common Mistake:** Using SI.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q10. Geometry (Medium)
Triangle sides 3, 4, 5. Type?
(a) Right  (b) Equilateral  (c) Acute  (d) Obtuse

**Answer:** (a) Right
**Solution:** $3^2 + 4^2 = 5^2$ → right.
**Shortcut:** Pythagorean test.
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q11. Probability (Hard)
Bag: 4 red, 6 black. 2 drawn. P(both red)?
(a) 2/15  (b) 1/5  (c) 4/25  (d) 1/3

**Answer:** (a) 2/15
**Solution:** $\frac{{}^4C_2}{{}^{10}C_2} = \frac{6}{45} = 2/15$
**Shortcut:** Combinations.
**Common Mistake):** With replacement.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q14. Permutation (Hard)
Arrangements of "EARTH"?
