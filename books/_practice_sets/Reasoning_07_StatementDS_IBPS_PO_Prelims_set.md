# Practice Set: Statement, Conclusion, Assumption, Data Sufficiency — IBPS PO Prelims (Set 3)

**Source:** Standard Reference Reasoning SSC Ch.10, Standard Reference Reasoning 8th Ch.7 & Ch.8
**Pattern:** 10 Questions | 45 seconds each | Total 7.5 minutes
**Difficulty:** Easy-Medium (IBPS PO Prelims Standard)

---

### Q1. Statement: "The company is offering a discount on all products this weekend." Assumption:
(a) Customers are attracted by discounts
(b) Company has surplus stock
(c) Weekend is busy (d) Discount means loss

**Answer:** (a) Customers are attracted by discounts
**Solution:** Offering discount assumes it works.
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

---

### Q2. Statement: "All doctors are busy during the pandemic." Conclusion: I. Doctors work hard. II. Patients face delays.
(a) Only I follows (b) Only II follows (c) Both follow (d) Neither follows

**Answer:** (c) Both follow
**Solution:** "Busy" implies working hard and patients face delays.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

---

### Q3. Data Sufficiency: What is X?
I. X + 5 = 12
II. X is a positive integer.
(a) I alone is sufficient (b) II alone is sufficient (c) Both together are sufficient (d) Cannot be determined

**Answer:** (a) I alone is sufficient
**Solution:** I gives X = 7. Sufficient.
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

---

### Q4. Course of Action: "Floods have severely affected many villages in the state."
(a) Relocate villages permanently (b) Government should send relief teams and rescue operations
(c) Stop rainfall (d) Build houses in cities

**Answer:** (b) Government should send relief teams and rescue operations
**Solution:** Practical, addresses immediate problem.
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

---

### Q5. Statement: "Time and tide wait for none." Conclusion: I. We should respect time. II. We cannot control nature.
(a) Only I follows (b) Only II follows (c) Both follow (d) Neither follows

**Answer:** (c) Both follow
**Solution:** The proverb suggests respecting time and acknowledging natural forces.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

---

### Q6. Statement: "The hospital has introduced online appointment booking for patients." Assumption:
(a) Patients are comfortable with online booking
(b) Hospital has internet
(c) Doctors prefer online (d) Patients are tech-savvy

**Answer:** (a) Patients are comfortable with online booking
**Solution:** The initiative assumes patients can use it.
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

---

### Q7. Data Sufficiency: Who is the tallest among A, B, C?
I. A is taller than B. B is shorter than C.
II. A is shorter than C.
(a) I alone is sufficient (b) II alone is sufficient (c) Both together are sufficient (d) Cannot be determined

**Answer:** (a) I alone is sufficient
**Solution:** I: A > B, C > B. But A vs C not determined. **Answer: (d) Cannot be determined.**
**Time Target:** 35 sec |

---

### Q8. Statement: "The train arrived 30 minutes late due to fog." Conclusion: I. Fog delayed the train. II. Trains run on time usually.
(a) Only I follows (b) Only II follows (c) Both follow (d) Neither follows

**Answer:** (a) Only I follows
**Solution:** Direct: fog caused delay. "Usually on time" is not stated.
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

---

### Q9. Course of Action: "There is a severe water shortage in the city."
(a) Ban bathing (b) Implement water rationing and conservation measures
(c) Pray for rain (d) Increase water prices

**Answer:** (b) Implement water rationing and conservation measures
**Solution:** Practical.
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

---

### Q10. Data Sufficiency: What is the cost of one pen?
I. 5 pens cost Rs. 100.
II. 3 pens cost Rs. 60.
(a) I alone is sufficient (b) II alone is sufficient (c) Both together are sufficient (d) Cannot be determined

**Answer:** (a) I alone is sufficient (Rs. 20)
**Solution:** I gives 100/5 = 20. Sufficient.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |
