### Q1. Statements:
S1: All cats are dogs.
S2: Some dogs are birds.
Conclusions:
I. Some cats are birds.
II. Some birds are dogs.
(a) Only I (b) Only II (c) Both (d) Neither

**Answer:** (b) Only II
**Solution:** Cats inside Dogs. Dogs ∩ Birds ≠ ∅. Reversible: some birds are dogs ✓. I: birds part of dogs may not be cats.
**Time Target:** 30 sec |

S1: All boys are students.

Conclusions:
I. All boys are learners.

### Q3. Statements:
S1: No metal is a gas.
S2: All solids are metals.
Conclusions:
I. No solid is a gas.
II. Some gases are not solids.
(a) Only I (b) Only II (c) Both (d) Neither

**Answer:** (c) Both
**Solution:** Metals separate from gases. Solids inside metals. So solids also separate from gases. I ✓. II: "All gases are not solid" → "Some gases are not solids" ✓.

### Q4. Statements:
S1: Some A are B.
S2: All B are C.
Conclusions:
I. Some A are C.
II. All A are C.
(a) Only I (b) Only II (c) Both (d) Neither

**Answer:** (a) Only I
**Solution:** A ∩ B ≠ ∅. B inside C. So A ∩ C ≠ ∅. I ✓. II: not all A are C (A may have parts outside B).
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

### Q5. Statements:
S1: All P are Q.
S2: All Q are R.
S3: No R is S.
Conclusions:
I. No P is S.
II. Some S are R.
(a) Only I (b) Only II (c) Both (d) Either/Or

**Answer:** (a) Only I
**Solution:** P → Q → R. R separate from S. P also separate from S. I ✓. II: "No R is S" → "All S are not R" → "Some S are not R" (not "Some S are R"). II doesn't follow.
**Time Target:** 40 sec |

### Q6. Statements:
S1: Some A are B.
S2: No B is C.
Conclusions:
I. Some A are not C.
II. Some C are not A.
(a) Only I (b) Only II (c) Both (d) Neither

**Answer:** (a) Only I
**Solution:** A ∩ B ≠ ∅. B separate from C. The A ∩ B is not C. So some A are not C ✓. II: doesn't follow (no relation between A and C outside of B).
