## Q1. Action (Easy)
"Branch is overcrowded." Action:
(a) Open new branch  (b) Increase staff  (c) Reduce hours  (d) Charge fees

**Answer:** (b) Increase staff
**Solution:** Practical.
**Shortcut:** Address problem.
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 20 sec

---

## Q2. Assumption (Easy)
"Use SBI credit card for cashback." Assumption:
(a) Cards accepted  (b) Cashback exists  (c) Limited offer  (d) Free card

**Answer:** (b) Cashback exists
**Solution:** Without cashback, no incentive.
**Shortcut:** Necessity.
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q4. Assertion (Medium)
A: "Customers prefer net banking." R: "It's faster than visiting branch."
(a) Both true, R explains  (b) Both true, R doesn't explain  (c) A true, R false  (d) A false, R true

**Solution:** R explains preference.
**Shortcut:** Check explanation.
**Common Mistake):** Other.

## Q10. Conclusion Syllogism (Hard)
"Some loans are personal. All personal have interest." Conclusion:
(a) All loans have interest  (b) Some loans have interest  (c) No loan has interest  (d) All personal are loans

**Answer:** (b) Some loans have interest
**Solution:** Some loans are personal. All personal have interest. So some loans have interest.
**Shortcut:** Syllogism.
**Common Mistake:** All.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q11. Multi-Statement (Hard)
Statements: (1) Some customers are senior citizens. (2) All senior citizens get discount. (3) Discount on FD. Conclusion: Some FD holders are senior citizens.
(a) Yes  (b) No  (c) Cannot say  (d) Always

**Answer:** (c) Cannot say
**Solution:** Not enough info.
**Shortcut:** Definite only.
**Common Mistake:** Yes.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q12. Action (Hard)
