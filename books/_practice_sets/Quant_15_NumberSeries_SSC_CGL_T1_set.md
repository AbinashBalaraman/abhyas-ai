## Q1. AP (Easy)
2, 4, 6, 8, ?
(a) 9  (b) 10  (c) 11  (d) 12

**Answer:** (b) 10
**Solution:** d=2. Next = 8+2 = 10. ✓
**Shortcut:** AP.
**Common Mistake:** d=3.
**Time target:** 10 sec

## Q2. GP (Easy)
3, 9, 27, 81, ?
(a) 162  (b) 243  (c) 324  (d) 405

**Answer:** (b) 243
**Solution:** r=3. Next = 81·3 = 243. ✓
**Shortcut:** GP.
**Common Mistake:** r=4.
**Time target:** 10 sec

## Q11. Interleaved (Medium)
3, 2, 6, 4, 9, 6, 12, 8, ?
(a) 13  (b) 14  (c) 15  (d) 16

**Answer:** (c) 15
