## Q1. Mean (Easy)
Mean of 4, 6, 8, 10:
(a) 6  (b) 7  (c) 8  (d) 9

**Answer:** (b) 7
**Solution:** (4+6+8+10)/4 = 28/4 = 7. ✓
**Shortcut:** Direct.
**Common Mistake:** Wrong sum.
**Time target:** 15 sec

---

## Q2. Mode (Easy)
Mode of 1, 2, 2, 3, 4:
(a) 1  (b) 2  (c) 3  (d) 4

**Answer:** (b) 2
**Solution:** 2 appears twice. ✓
**Shortcut:** Most frequent.
**Common Mistake:** First value.
**Time target:** 10 sec

---

## Q3. P(coin) (Easy — Trap)
P(head) + P(tail):
(a) 0  (b) 1  (c) 1/2  (d) 2

**Answer:** (b) 1
**Solution:** 1/2 + 1/2 = 1. ✓
**Shortcut:** Complement.
**Common Mistake:** Setting = 2.
**Time target:** 10 sec

---

## Q4. P(red ball) (Medium)
Bag: 5 red, 3 blue. P(red):
(a) 3/8  (b) 5/8  (c) 1/2  (d) 1/4

**Answer:** (b) 5/8
**Solution:** 5/8. ✓
**Shortcut:** Favorable/total.
**Common Mistake:** Setting = 3/8.
**Time target:** 15 sec

## Q5. P(A∩B) independent (Medium)
P(A) = 0.5, P(B) = 0.4, independent. P(A∩B):
(a) 0.20  (b) 0.10  (c) 0.90  (d) 0.45

**Answer:** (a) 0.20
**Solution:** 0.5·0.4 = 0.2. ✓
**Shortcut:** Multiply.
**Common Mistake:** Setting = 0.9.
**Time target:** 20 sec

## Q6. Median (Medium)
Median of 1, 3, 5, 7, 9:
(a) 3  (b) 5  (c) 7  (d) 9

**Answer:** (b) 5
**Solution:** Middle = 5. ✓
**Shortcut:** Middle.
**Common Mistake:** Setting = 3.
**Time target:** 15 sec

## Q7. P(spade or heart) (Medium)
(a) 1/2  (b) 1/4  (c) 2/3  (d) 1/3

**Answer:** (a) 1/2
**Solution:** 26 + 26 = 52 out of 52. Wait, that's the whole deck. Spade + heart = 26 cards out of 52 = 1/2. ✓
**Shortcut:** Two suits = 1/2.
**Common Mistake:** Setting = 1/4.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q8. Mean of 1-10 (Medium)
(a) 4  (b) 5  (c) 5.5  (d) 6

**Answer:** (c) 5.5
**Solution:** (1+...+10)/10 = 55/10 = 5.5. ✓
**Shortcut:** (n+1)/2.
**Common Mistake:** Setting = 5.
**Time target:** 15 sec

---

## Q9. P(A∪B) (Hard)
P(A) = 0.3, P(B) = 0.4, mutually exclusive. P(A∪B):
(a) 0.7  (b) 0.5  (c) 0.12  (d) 0.1

**Answer:** (a) 0.7
**Solution:** 0.3 + 0.4 = 0.7 (no intersection). ✓
**Shortcut:** Sum for mutually exclusive.
**Common Mistake:** Subtracting.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q10. SD simple (Hard — Trap)
SD of 1, 2, 3, 4, 5:
(a) √2  (b) √3  (c) 2  (d) 1

**Answer:** (a) √2
**Solution:** Mean = 3. Squared dev: 4+1+0+1+4 = 10. Var = 2. SD = √2. ✓
**Shortcut:** Direct.
**Common Mistake:** Setting = √3.
**Time target:** 75 sec
