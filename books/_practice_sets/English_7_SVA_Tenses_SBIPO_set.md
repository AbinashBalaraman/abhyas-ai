# English Chapter 7: Subject-Verb Agreement & Tenses
## Practice Set — SBI PO Prelims (10 Questions, 30 sec each)

### Q1. "The rich ___ not always happy."
(a) is
(b) was
(c) are
(d) has been

**Answer:** (c) are

**Solution:** "**The + adjective**" referring to a **class of people** (the rich, the poor, the blind) → **plural verb**. [Source: §2.4 Rule 18 — "The + adjective" (class of people) → plural]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "is" (treating "rich" as a singular quality rather than a class).

**Time Target:** 5 sec

---

### Q2. "I ___ this book since 2020."
(a) read
(b) am reading
(c) have been reading
(d) had read

**Answer:** (c) have been reading

**Solution:** "**Since 2020**" + action continuing to present → **Present Perfect Continuous**. [Source: §3.4 Present Perfect Continuous, §3.8]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "read" (Present Simple — doesn't show duration from past).

**Time Target:** 5 sec

---

### Q3. "Each boy and each girl ___ a book."
(a) have
(b) has
(c) are having
(d) were

**Answer:** (b) has

**Solution:** "**Each**" before each subject → singular verb. "Each X and each Y" treats each as separate singular. [Source: §2.4 Rule 4 — "Each" is singular]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "have" (treating as compound subject).

**Time Target:** 5 sec

---

### Q4. "She ___ to Paris last year."
(a) goes
(b) has gone
(c) went
(d) had gone

**Answer:** (c) went

**Solution:** "Last year" → **Past Simple**. [Source: §3.5, §3.8]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (b) "has gone" (Present Perfect — wrong with "last year").

**Time Target:** 3 sec

---

### Q5. "Bread and butter ___ his daily breakfast."
(a) are
(b) is
(c) were
(d) have been

**Answer:** (b) is

**Solution:** "**Bread and butter**" as a **single item** (one type of breakfast) → **singular verb**. When two nouns refer to a single concept, use singular. [Source: §2.4 — Single concept with "and"]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "are" (treating as two separate items).

**Time Target:** 5 sec

---

### Q6. "By next week, I ___ this book."
(a) will finish
(b) will have finished
(c) finish
(d) am finishing

**Answer:** (b) will have finished

**Solution:** "**By next week**" (future reference) + completion → **Future Perfect** (will have + V3). [Source: §3.6 Future Perfect, §3.8]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "will finish" (Future Simple — wrong aspect).

**Time Target:** 5 sec

---

### Q7. "The jury ___ given its verdict."
(a) have
(b) has
(c) are giving
(d) were giving

**Answer:** (b) has

**Solution:** "**Jury**" is a collective noun → singular verb. [Source: §2.4 Rule 6 — Collective nouns: singular]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "have" (treating as plural).

**Time Target:** 5 sec

---

### Q8. "While I ___ dinner, the doorbell rang."
(a) had
(b) was having
(c) am having
(d) have

**Answer:** (b) was having

**Solution:** "**While**" + past action in progress when another action occurred → **Past Continuous** for the longer action. [Source: §3.5 Past Continuous, §3.8]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "had" (Past Perfect — wrong; the action was ongoing, not completed earlier).

**Time Target:** 5 sec

---

### Q9. "Ramu, as well as his friends, ___ here."
(a) are
(b) is
(c) were
(d) have been

**Answer:** (b) is

**Solution:** "**As well as**" → verb agrees with the **first subject** ("Ramu" is singular). [Source: §2.4 Rule 2 — "with/as well as/together with" → first subject]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "are" (treating as compound subject like "and").

**Time Target:** 5 sec

---

### Q10. "He ___ for two hours when she called."
(a) slept
(b) has slept
(c) had been sleeping
(d) was sleeping

**Answer:** (c) had been sleeping

**Solution:** "**For two hours**" (duration) + "when she called" (interrupted past action) → **Past Perfect Continuous** (had been + V1+ing). [Source: §3.5 Past Perfect Continuous, §3.8]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (d) "was sleeping" (Past Continuous — doesn't show the past-of-past duration).

**Time Target:** 5 sec
