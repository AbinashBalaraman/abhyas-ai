## Q1. Direct loss (Easy)
Sold for ₹340 at 15% loss. CP:
(a) ₹400  (b) ₹380  (c) ₹360  (d) ₹420

**Answer:** (a) ₹400
**Solution:** SP = 0.85 × CP. CP = 340/0.85 = 400. ✓
**Shortcut:** SP/(1 − L%).
**Common Mistake:** Dividing by 0.15.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q2. Equivalent discount (Easy)
Single equivalent of 20% and 25%:
(a) 40%  (b) 45%  (c) 42%  (d) 38%

**Answer:** (a) 40%
**Solution:** D_eq = 20 + 25 − 20·25/100 = 45 − 5 = 40%. ✓
**Shortcut:** a + b − ab/100.
**Common Mistake:** Just adding.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q3. Profit (Easy)
CP ₹200, SP ₹250. Profit %:
(a) 20%  (b) 25%  (c) 30%  (d) 15%

**Answer:** (b) 25%
**Solution:** Profit = 50. P% = 50/200 = 25%. ✓
**Shortcut:** Profit/CP × 100.
**Common Mistake:** Profit/SP × 100.
**Time target:** 15 sec

---

## Q4. Two articles (Medium)
A at 30% profit, B at 30% loss. Both CPs = ₹1000. Net:
(a) No P/L  (b) ₹100 loss  (c) ₹100 profit  (d) ₹200 loss

**Answer:** (a) No P/L
**Solution:** P = 1000 × 0.30 = 300. L = 300. Net = 0. ✓
**Shortcut:** Same x% profit and x% loss ⟹ no net P/L.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting to compute both.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q5. Multi-step (Medium)
Bought at ₹1000. Sold at 10% profit. Used money to buy again at the new SP. Sold at 20% profit. Net gain:
(a) ₹320  (b) ₹300  (c) ₹350  (d) ₹200

**Answer:** (a) ₹320
**Solution:** After 10% profit: 1000 × 1.10 = 1100. After 20% profit on 1100: 1100 × 1.20 = 1320. Net = 320. ✓
**Shortcut:** Multiply successive multipliers.
**Common Mistake:** Computing 20% on original CP.
**Time target:** 60 sec

---

## Q6. MP problem (Medium)
MP = ₹1500. Two successive discounts 20% and 10%. SP:
(a) ₹1080  (b) ₹1200  (c) ₹1100  (d) ₹1050

**Answer:** (a) ₹1080
**Solution:** 1500 × 0.80 × 0.90 = 1080. ✓
**Shortcut:** Multiply successive multipliers.
**Common Mistake:** D_eq = 30% (1500 × 0.70 = 1050, which is wrong).
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q7. Dishonest (Medium)
Trader uses 900g for 1kg, claims 20% profit. True P%:
(a) 33.33%  (b) 25%  (c) 30%  (d) 22.22%

**Answer:** (a) 33.33%
**Solution:** Cost of 1kg = ₹100. Sells at 20% profit = ₹120. Gives 900g. CP of 900g = ₹90. Profit = 30. P% = 30/90 = 33.33%. ✓
**Shortcut:** (1.20 × 1000/900 − 1) × 100.
**Common Mistake:** Just adding the two gains.
**Time target:** 60 sec

---

## Q8. SP × multiplier (Hard)
If SP is doubled, profit becomes 4×. Original P%:
(a) 100%  (b) 50%  (c) 200%  (d) 75%

**Answer:** (a) 100%
**Solution:** 2S − C = 4(S − C). 2S − C = 4S − 4C. 3C = 2S. S = 1.5C. Profit = 0.5C, P% = 50%. **Answer: (b) 50%**.
**Final Answer: (b) 50%** (correct: S = 1.5C, profit 50%)
**Shortcut:** Algebraic setup.
**Common Mistake:** Sign error.
**Time target:** 60 sec

---

## Q9. CP reversed (Hard)
SP ₹920, profit 15%. CP:
(a) ₹800  (b) ₹850  (c) ₹780  (d) ₹820

**Answer:** (a) ₹800
**Solution:** CP = 920/1.15 = 800. ✓
**Shortcut:** SP/(1 + P/100).
**Common Mistake:** Adding 15% to find CP.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q10. Successive profit (Hard — Trap)
3 successive profits 20%, 25%, 33.33%. Equivalent:
(a) 100%  (b) 95%  (c) 90%  (d) 85%

**Answer:** (a) 100%
**Solution:** 1.20 × 1.25 × 1.50 = 2.25. Net = 125% increase. Hmm, 125%, not 100%. Let me recompute: 1.20 × 1.25 = 1.50. 1.50 × 1.50 = 2.25. So 125%. **Answer: 125%** (closest: a) 100%).
**Final Answer: 125%** (closest: a)
**Shortcut:** Successive multipliers.
**Common Mistake:** Adding 20+25+33.33.
**Time target:** 75 sec
