## Q1. Distance from Bank (Easy)
ATM at (2, 3) and customer at (5, 7). Distance?
(a) 4  (b) 5  (c) 6  (d) 7

**Answer:** (b) 5
**Solution:** $\sqrt{9 + 16} = 5$
**Shortcut:** 3-4-5 triple.
**Common Mistake:** Just $3 + 4 = 7$.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q2. Midpoint Branches (Easy)
Two branches at (10, 20) and (30, 40). Midpoint?
(a) (20, 30)  (b) (15, 25)  (c) (40, 60)  (d) (20, 20)

**Answer:** (a) (20, 30)
**Solution:** $(\frac{10+30}{2}, \frac{20+40}{2}) = (20, 30)$
**Shortcut:** Average.
**Common Mistake:** Just adding.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q3. Distance Origin (Easy)
Distance from (0,0) to (5, 12)?
(a) 13  (b) 17  (c) 12  (d) 7

**Answer:** (a) 13
**Solution:** $\sqrt{25 + 144} = 13$ (5-12-13 triple).
**Shortcut:** Memorize Pythagorean triples.
**Common Mistake:** Just 17.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q4. Circle through Origin (Medium)
Circle $x^2 + y^2 - 4x - 6y = 0$ has:
(a) Center (2, 3)  (b) Center (-2, -3)  (c) Center (4, 6)  (d) Center (0, 0)

**Answer:** (a) Center (2, 3)
**Solution:** $g = -2, f = -3$. Center = (-g, -f) = (2, 3).
**Shortcut:** $(-g, -f)$ for center.
**Common Mistake:** Wrong sign.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q5. Bank Branch Distance (Medium)
Branch A at (1, 2) and B at (4, 6). Distance?
(a) 3  (b) 4  (c) 5  (d) 25

**Answer:** (c) 5
**Solution:** $\sqrt{9 + 16} = 5$
**Shortcut:** 3-4-5 triple.
**Common Mistake:** Computing $\sqrt{9} + \sqrt{16} = 7$.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q6. Triangle Area (Medium)
Triangle vertices (0,0), (6,0), (0,8). Area?
(a) 24  (b) 48  (c) 14  (d) 28

**Answer:** (a) 24
**Solution:** $\frac{1}{2} \times 6 \times 8 = 24$
**Shortcut:** Right triangle area.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting 1/2.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q7. Centroid (Medium)
Centroid of triangle (0,0), (3,0), (0,3)?
(a) (1, 1)  (b) (3, 3)  (c) (1, 1.5)  (d) (1.5, 1.5)

**Answer:** (a) (1, 1)
**Solution:** $(\frac{0+3+0}{3}, \frac{0+0+3}{3}) = (1, 1)$
**Shortcut:** Average.
**Common Mistake:** Adding instead of averaging.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q8. Slope — Sales (Medium)
Sales at (1, 100) and (5, 300). Slope of trend?
(a) 50  (b) 75  (c) 100  (d) 200

**Answer:** (a) 50
**Solution:** $\frac{300-100}{5-1} = 200/4 = 50$
**Shortcut:** $\Delta y / \Delta x$.
**Common Mistake:** Using 300/5 = 60.
**Time target:** 45 sec

---

## Q9. Equation of Line (Medium)
Line through (0, 3) and (4, 0)?
(a) $3x + 4y = 12$  (b) $4x + 3y = 12$  (c) Both  (d) None

**Answer:** (a) $3x + 4y = 12$
**Solution:** X-int = 4, Y-int = 3. $\frac{x}{4} + \frac{y}{3} = 1$. $3x + 4y = 12$.
**Shortcut:** Intercept form.
**Common Mistake:** Mixing up intercepts.
**Time target:** 60 sec

---

## Q10. Circle — Tangent (Hard)
Circle $x^2 + y^2 = 25$. Distance from (0, 0) to (5, 0)?
(a) 0  (b) 5  (c) 25  (d) On the circle

**Answer:** (d) On the circle
**Solution:** $5^2 + 0^2 = 25$ → on circle.
**Shortcut:** Check if sum equals $r^2$.
**Common Mistake:** Just computing distance.
**Time target:** 45 sec

---

## Q11. Inradius (Hard)
Triangle with area 30, semi-perimeter 10. Inradius?
(a) 2  (b) 3  (c) 5  (d) 6

**Answer:** (b) 3
**Solution:** $r = A/s = 30/10 = 3$.
**Shortcut:** $r = \text{Area}/\text{Semi-perimeter}$.
**Common Mistake:** Using perimeter.
**Time target:** 45 sec

---

## Q12. Line Equation (Hard)
Equation of y-axis?
(a) y = 0  (b) x = 0  (c) y = x  (d) y = -x

**Answer:** (b) x = 0
**Solution:** Y-axis: x = 0 for all y.
**Shortcut:** Memorize axes.
**Common Mistake:** Confusing with x-axis.
**Time target:** 15 sec
