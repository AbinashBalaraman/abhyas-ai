## Q1. Photosynthesis equation (Easy)
The photosynthesis equation is:
(a) 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ (b) 6CO₂ + 6O₂ → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6H₂O (c) C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O (d) C₆H₁₂O₆ → 2C₂H₅OH + 2CO₂

**Answer:** (a) 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂
**Solution:** Photosynthesis: 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ (in presence of sunlight and chlorophyll). Option (c) is respiration; (d) is fermentation.
**Shortcut:** Photosynthesis: CO₂ + H₂O → Glucose + O₂. Respiration: Glucose + O₂ → CO₂ + H₂O + Energy.
**Common Mistake:** Picking (c) (that's respiration, opposite).
**Time target:** 15 sec

## Q2. Site of photosynthesis (Easy)
Photosynthesis occurs in:
(a) Mitochondria (b) Chloroplasts (c) Nucleus (d) Ribosomes

**Answer:** (b) Chloroplasts
**Solution:** Chloroplasts contain chlorophyll (green pigment) which absorbs light energy. Photosynthesis occurs in the chloroplasts of green plant cells, mainly in leaves. Mitochondria are for cellular respiration.
**Shortcut:** Chloroplast = photosynthesis (green). Mitochondria = respiration (energy).
**Common Mistake:** Choosing mitochondria (that's respiration).
**Time target:** 15 sec

## Q3. Salivary amylase (Easy)
Salivary amylase digests:
(a) Proteins (b) Fats (c) Starch (d) Vitamins

**Answer:** (c) Starch
**Solution:** Salivary amylase (ptyalin) in saliva breaks down starch (a complex carbohydrate) into maltose (a simpler sugar) in the mouth. Pepsin digests proteins (stomach); lipase digests fats (small intestine).
**Shortcut:** Saliva = amylase = starch → sugar.
**Common Mistake:** Confusing with pepsin (which is for proteins).
**Time target:** 15 sec

## Q4. Functional unit of kidney (Easy)
The functional unit of the human kidney is:
(a) Neuron (b) Nephron (c) Alveolus (d) Villus

**Answer:** (b) Nephron
**Solution:** Each kidney contains about 1 million nephrons (the structural and functional unit). Each nephron has a glomerulus (filter) and tubule (reabsorption). Filters blood → forms urine.
**Shortcut:** Kidney = nephron. Neuron = nerve. Alveolus = lung. Villus = intestine.
**Common Mistake:** Confusing nephron (kidney) with neuron (nerve cell).
**Time target:** 15 sec

## Q5. Aerobic vs anaerobic respiration (Medium)
Aerobic respiration produces _____ ATP per glucose molecule, anaerobic produces _____:
(a) 38, 2 (b) 2, 38 (c) 18, 4 (d) 100, 10

**Answer:** (a) 38, 2
**Solution:** Aerobic respiration (with O₂) → complete breakdown → 38 ATP/glucose. Anaerobic respiration (without O₂, e.g., in muscles or yeast) → incomplete → 2 ATP/glucose.
**Shortcut:** Aerobic = more ATP (38). Anaerobic = less ATP (2).
**Common Mistake:** Reversing (anaerobic doesn't produce more).
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q6. Site of complete digestion (Medium)
The site of complete digestion of food in humans is:
(a) Mouth (b) Stomach (c) Small intestine (d) Large intestine

**Answer:** (c) Small intestine
**Solution:** Small intestine receives bile (liver, emulsifies fats), pancreatic juice (trypsin, lipase, amylase), and intestinal enzymes. Completes digestion of carbohydrates, proteins, fats. Villi absorb nutrients.
**Shortcut:** Small intestine = complete digestion + absorption.
**Common Mistake:** Saying stomach (only partial protein digestion here).
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q7. Anaerobic respiration in yeast (Medium)
In yeast, anaerobic respiration produces:
(a) Lactic acid + CO₂ (b) Ethanol + CO₂ (c) Ethanol + H₂O (d) Acetic acid + CO₂

**Answer:** (b) Ethanol + CO₂
**Solution:** Yeast fermentation: C₆H₁₂O₆ → 2C₂H₅OH (ethanol) + 2CO₂ + Energy. Used in baking (CO₂ makes dough rise) and brewing (alcohol). In muscles, anaerobic respiration produces lactic acid.
**Shortcut:** Yeast → ethanol + CO₂. Muscles → lactic acid.
**Common Mistake:** Saying lactic acid (that's in muscles, not yeast).
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q8. Alveoli function (Medium — Trap)
Alveoli in the lungs are specialised for:
(a) Filtering air (b) Gas exchange (c) Producing mucus (d) Warming air

**Answer:** (b) Gas exchange
**Solution:** Alveoli are tiny balloon-like air sacs at the end of bronchioles. They have thin walls (one cell thick) surrounded by capillaries → efficient diffusion of O₂ (into blood) and CO₂ (out of blood). Total surface area ~80 m².
**Shortcut:** Alveoli = gas exchange (O₂ ↔ CO₂).
**Common Mistake:** Saying filter/mucus/warm (those happen in nasal cavity).
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q9. Haemoglobin function (Medium)
Haemoglobin in red blood cells:
(a) Fights infection (b) Helps in clotting (c) Transports oxygen (d) Carries nutrients

**Answer:** (c) Transports oxygen
**Solution:** Haemoglobin (Hb) in RBCs binds O₂ in lungs (forms oxyhaemoglobin, bright red) and releases O₂ in tissues. Each Hb can bind 4 O₂ molecules. WBCs fight infection; platelets help clotting; plasma carries nutrients.
**Shortcut:** Hb = O₂ transport. RBC = red (haem). WBC = white (infection fighter).
**Common Mistake:** Saying clotting (that's platelets).
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q10. Double circulation (Medium)
Double circulation in mammals means:
(a) Blood passes through the heart twice in one complete cycle (b) Blood flows through two circuits (pulmonary + systemic) (c) Both (a) and (b) (d) Heart has two chambers

**Answer:** (c) Both (a) and (b)
**Solution:** Double circulation = blood passes through the heart twice per cycle, going through two circuits: (1) pulmonary (heart → lungs → heart) and (2) systemic (heart → body → heart). Mammals have 4-chambered heart.
**Shortcut:** Double circulation = heart twice per cycle, 2 loops.
**Common Mistake:** Saying 2 chambers (mammals have 4).
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q11. Xylem function (Medium)
Xylem in plants transports:
(a) Food (b) Water and minerals (c) Hormones (d) Gases

**Answer:** (b) Water and minerals
**Solution:** Xylem carries water and minerals from roots → leaves (and other parts). Unidirectional. Phloem transports food (sucrose) from leaves → all parts (bidirectional — translocation).
**Shortcut:** XyLEM = water (Lifts water). PhLOEM = food (LOwer direction also).
**Common Mistake:** Saying food (that's phloem).
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q12. ATP energy (Medium — Trap)
The energy released by hydrolysis of one mole of ATP is approximately:
(a) 7.3 kcal (b) 30.5 kJ (c) 100 kJ (d) Both (a) and (b)

**Answer:** (d) Both (a) and (b)
**Solution:** ATP → ADP + Pi releases ~30.5 kJ/mol ≈ 7.3 kcal/mol. Both units are correct (just different scales: 1 kcal = 4.184 kJ).
**Shortcut:** ATP hydrolysis ≈ 30.5 kJ/mol ≈ 7.3 kcal/mol.
**Common Mistake:** Picking only one option (both are valid).
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q13. Blood clotting element (Hard)
Blood clotting requires:
(a) Vitamin K and Calcium (b) Vitamin C and Iron (c) Vitamin D and Phosphorus (d) Vitamin A and Zinc

**Answer:** (a) Vitamin K and Calcium
**Solution:** Vitamin K is essential for synthesis of clotting factors (prothrombin and others) in the liver. Calcium ions (Ca²⁺) are required cofactors for many clotting enzymes. Vitamin C deficiency → scurvy (not clotting). Vitamin D → Ca absorption.
**Shortcut:** Clotting = Vit K + Ca²⁺.
**Common Mistake:** Saying Vitamin C (that prevents scurvy, not clotting).
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q14. Oxygen transport in blood (Hard)
Most of the oxygen transported in human blood is:
(a) Dissolved in plasma (b) Bound to haemoglobin (c) As bicarbonate (d) Dissolved in cytoplasm

**Answer:** (b) Bound to haemoglobin
**Solution:** ~98% of O₂ is transported bound to haemoglobin (as oxyhaemoglobin). Only ~3% is dissolved in plasma. CO₂ is mostly transported as bicarbonate (HCO₃⁻) in plasma (~70%).
**Shortcut:** O₂ → Hb (98%). CO₂ → bicarbonate (70%).
**Common Mistake:** Saying dissolved (only ~3% is dissolved).
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q15. Excretory product in humans (Hard)
The main excretory product in humans is:
(a) Ammonia (b) Urea (c) Uric acid (d) Creatinine

**Answer:** (b) Urea
**Solution:** Humans excrete urea (formed in liver from deamination of amino acids). Fish excrete ammonia (highly toxic, needs much water). Birds and reptiles excrete uric acid (less toxic, less water). Creatinine is a minor waste product.
**Shortcut:** Mammals → urea. Fish → ammonia. Birds/reptiles → uric acid.
**Common Mistake:** Saying ammonia (that's for fish).
**Time target:** 45 sec
