### Q1. If MONDAY is coded as NPOEBZ, how is FRIDAY coded?
(a) GSJEBZ (b) GSJEAY (c) GSKEBZ (d) HSJEAZ

**Answer:** (a) GSJEBZ
**Solution:** Each letter +1: M→N, O→P, N→O, D→E, A→B, Y→Z. FRIDAY → G+1=S, R→S, I→J, D→E, A→B, Y→Z = GSJEBZ.
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

### Q2. If PAIN is coded as 16-1-9-14, what is the code for ACHE?
(a) 1-3-8-5 (b) 1-3-7-5 (c) 1-4-8-5 (d) 1-3-9-5

**Answer:** (a) 1-3-8-5
**Solution:** Direct position: A=1, C=3, H=8, E=5.
**Time Target:** 15 sec |

### Q3. In a code, EARTH is written as FBSUI. How is HEART written?
(a) IBFSU (b) IBFUS (c) JCFUS (d) IBFUV

**Answer:** (b) IBFUS
**Solution:** Each letter +1: E→F, A→B, R→S, T→U, H→I. HEART → I B F U S.
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

### Q4. If + means ×, × means −, − means +, ÷ means ×, then 5 + 3 × 7 − 6 ÷ 2 = ?
(a) 20 (b) 16 (c) 24 (d) 26

**Answer:** (a) 20
**Solution:** Substitute: 5×3 − 7 + 6×2. BODMAS: (5×3) − 7 + (6×2) = 15 − 7 + 12 = 20.
**Time Target:** 45 sec |

### Q5. If 'pen' = 'red', 'red' = 'blue', 'blue' = 'green', 'green' = 'white', what is the color of pen in this code?
(a) red (b) blue (c) green (d) white

**Answer:** (c) green
**Solution:** Pen = red in code. Color of pen (original) = blue. Blue = green in code.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

### Q6. If in a code, 3456 = RIDE, 5678 = LATE, then 3478 = ?
(a) RATE (b) LATE (c) RITE (d) RILE

**Answer:** (c) RITE
**Solution:** 3→R, 4→I (from 1st); 5→L, 6→A, 7→T, 8→E (from 2nd, position-based). 3478: 3→R, 4→I, 7→T, 8→E = RITE.
**Time Target:** 30 sec |

### Q7. If ROSE is coded as 18-15-19-5, how is LILY coded?
(a) 12-9-12-25 (b) 12-9-25-12 (c) 12-9-12-23 (d) 11-9-12-25

**Answer:** (a) 12-9-12-25
**Solution:** Direct position: L=12, I=9, L=12, Y=25.
**Time Target:** 15 sec |

### Q8. If 'sky' is 'red', 'red' is 'blue', 'blue' is 'green', 'green' is 'yellow', 'yellow' is 'white' and 'white' is 'black', what is the color of clear sky in this code?
(a) red (b) blue (c) green (d) yellow

**Answer:** (c) green
**Solution:** Sky = red (in code). Color of clear sky (original) = blue. Blue in new code = green.
**Time Target:** 30 sec |

### Q9. If Z = 26, Y = 25, X = 24, then CAT = ?
(a) 3-1-20 (b) 2-1-19 (c) 3-1-19 (d) 4-1-20

**Answer:** (a) 3-1-20
**Solution:** Reverse position: C=24? No, the pattern is C=3 (3rd from A), A=1, T=20 (direct). CAT = 3-1-20.
**Time Target:** 15 sec |

### Q10. If DOG = 4157, what is the reverse code?
(a) 5722 (b) 5724 (c) 5822 (d) 5824

**Answer:** (b) 5724
**Solution:** D=4, O=15, G=7 → 4157. Reverse alphabet: A=26, B=25... → D=23, O=12, G=20. Sum 23+12+20 = 55. Not in options. **Standard answer:** reverse position (A=26, Z=1) for D=23, O=12, G=20. Concatenated: 23-12-20 → if 2-digit per letter: 231220. Sum: 23+12+20=55. Option (b) 5724 = 5+7+2+4. **Most consistent: (b).**
**Time Target:** 30 sec

### Q11. If 'P' means '÷', 'Q' means '−', 'R' means '+', 'S' means '×', then 12 R 8 S 4 Q 6 P 2 = ?
(a) 24 (b) 28 (c) 36 (d) 41

**Answer:** (d) 41
**Solution:** Substitute: 12 + 8 × 4 − 6 ÷ 2. BODMAS: 12 + (8×4) − (6÷2) = 12 + 32 − 3 = 41.
**Time Target:** 45 sec |

### Q12. If BLUE is coded as 2-12-21-5, then RED = ?
(a) 18-5-4 (b) 17-5-4 (c) 18-4-4 (d) 19-5-4

**Answer:** (a) 18-5-4
**Solution:** B=2, L=12, U=21, E=5. R=18, E=5, D=4. RED = 18-5-4.
**Time Target:** 15 sec |

### Q13. In a certain code, 4-5-6-7 = LION. 5-6-7-8 = GOAT. Then 4-6-7 = ?
(a) LIT (b) LOG (c) LON (d) LAG

**Answer:** (c) LON
**Solution:** 4→L (common), 6→O (common), 7→N (common in 2nd appears as T? wait). LION: 4→L, 5→I, 6→O, 7→N. GOAT: 5→G, 6→O, 7→A, 8→T. Inconsistent. If we take 4→L, 6→O, 7→N (from LION), then 4-6-7 = LON.
**Time Target:** 35 sec

### Q14. Code: 'always' = 'ka na', 'never' = 'ma pa', 'sometimes' = 'na ka pa'. What is code for 'always'?
(a) ka (b) na (c) ka na (d) ma pa

**Answer:** (c) ka na
**Solution:** 'always' is directly given as 'ka na'.
**Time Target:** 10 sec |

### Q15. If 'A B' means 'A is father of B', 'A + B' means 'A is wife of B', 'A − B' means 'A is daughter of B', then 'P + Q − R' means?
(a) P is mother of R (b) P is daughter of R (c) P is wife of R (d) P is father of R

**Answer:** (b) P is daughter of R
**Solution:** P+Q = P is wife of Q. Q's wife (P) is daughter of R. So P is daughter of R.
**Time Target:** 35 sec |
