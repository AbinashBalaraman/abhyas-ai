## Q1. Analogy (Easy)
4 : 67 :: 5 : ?
(a) 124  (b) 126  (c) 128  (d) 130

**Answer:** (c) 128
**Solution:** 4³+3=67. 5³+3=128.
**Shortcut:** a³+3.
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q20. Coding Complex (Hard)
If A+B = 4, A+B+C = 6, A+B+C+D = 8, A+B+C+D+E = 10. Find E.
(a) 0  (b) 1  (c) 2  (d) 4

**Answer:** (c) 2
**Solution:** Each letter adds 2: A=2, B=2, C=2, D=2, E=2. So E=2.
**Shortcut:** Pattern.
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 60 sec
