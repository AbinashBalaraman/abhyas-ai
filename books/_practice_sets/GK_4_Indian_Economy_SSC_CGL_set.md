### Q1. The Planning Commission of India was established on:
(a) 15 August 1947 (b) 26 January 1950 (c) 15 March 1950 (d) 1 January 2015

**Answer: (c) 15 March 1950**

**Solution:** The Planning Commission was established on 15 March 1950 by an executive resolution of the Union Cabinet, based on the recommendations of the Advisory Planning Board (1946) under PM Nehru. It was dissolved on 1 January 2015 and replaced by NITI Aayog. The first Five Year Plan (1951-56) was based on the Harrod-Domar Model and focused on agriculture. The first Deputy Chairman was Gulzarilal Nanda. The 12th Five Year Plan (2012-17) was the last.

### Q2. NITI Aayog replaced the Planning Commission on:
(a) 15 March 1950 (b) 1 April 2015 (c) 1 January 2015 (d) 15 August 2014

**Answer: (c) 1 January 2015**

**Solution:** NITI Aayog (National Institution for Transforming India) replaced the Planning Commission on 1 January 2015. NITI Aayog is chaired by the PM and serves as a policy think tank. Unlike the Planning Commission, NITI Aayog is advisory only (no fund allocation). The first Vice-Chairperson was Arvind Panagariya. The current VC is Suman Bery. NITI Aayog prepares Vision Documents (15-year), Strategy (7-year), and Action Agenda (3-year).

### Q3. The LPG reforms of 1991 refer to:
(a) Liberalization, Privatization, Globalization (b) Local, Public, Government (c) Land, Public, Goods (d) Liberal, Progressive, Green

**Answer: (a) Liberalization, Privatization, Globalization**

**Solution:** The 1991 reforms (LPG) refer to Liberalization, Privatization, and Globalization. They were triggered by the 1990-91 Balance of Payments crisis when forex reserves dropped to 2 weeks of imports. P.V. Narasimha Rao was the PM and Dr. Manmohan Singh was the Finance Minister. Key reforms: industrial licensing abolished (except 18 industries), MRTP Act diluted, FERA replaced by FEMA (1999), SEBI strengthened, rupee devaluation (July 1991), and Narasimham Committee (1991, 1998).

### Q4. GST was implemented in India on:
(a) 1 April 2017 (b) 1 July 2017 (c) 1 January 2017 (d) 1 December 2017

**Answer: (b) 1 July 2017**

**Solution:** GST (Goods and Services Tax) was implemented on 1 July 2017 under the 101st Constitutional Amendment Act, 2016. It replaced multiple central and state taxes (Excise Duty, Service Tax, VAT, etc.). GST is a destination-based, multi-stage tax. The GST Council is chaired by the Union Finance Minister with members from all states. GST has 5 main slabs: 0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, and 28%. Components: CGST, SGST, IGST, and UTGST.

**Time Target:** 30s | **Chapter Reference:** GK/04_Indian_Economy.md, p.262, p.598 | **VERIFY:** No

### Q5. The GST Council is chaired by:
(a) Prime Minister (b) RBI Governor (c) Union Finance Minister (d) Finance Minister of the largest state

**Answer: (c) Union Finance Minister**

**Solution:** The GST Council is chaired by the Union Finance Minister under Article 279A of the Constitution. Members include the Union Minister of State for Finance and the Finance Ministers of all States and UTs (with legislature). Decisions are taken by 3/4 majority of weighted votes (Central: 1/3; States: 2/3). Quorum requires 1/2 of total members. The 56th GST Council meeting in December 2024 rationalized GST rates.

**Time Target:** 30s | **Chapter Reference:** GK/04_Indian_Economy.md, p.273-279 | **VERIFY:** No

### Q6. The Indian Rupee (₹) symbol was adopted on:
(a) 26 January 1950 (b) 15 August 1947 (c) 15 July 2010 (d) 1 April 1935

**Answer: (c) 15 July 2010**

**Solution:** The Indian Rupee symbol (₹) was adopted on 15 July 2010 by the Indian government. The symbol was designed by Udaya Kumar Dharmalingam and selected from a competition. RBI is the currency issuing authority for notes; Government of India issues coins. The RBI was established on 1 April 1935 and nationalized on 1 January 1949. RBI's first Governor was Sir Osborne Smith; first Indian Governor was C.D. Deshmukh.

### Q7. RBI was established on:
(a) 1 April 1934 (b) 1 April 1935 (c) 15 August 1947 (d) 1 January 1949

**Answer: (b) 1 April 1935**

**Solution:** The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) was established on 1 April 1935 under the RBI Act, 1934. The recommendation was based on the Hilton Young Commission (1926). RBI was nationalized on 1 January 1949. The first Governor was Sir Osborne Smith (British); the first Indian Governor was C.D. Deshmukh (1943-1949). The headquarters was originally in Kolkata, moved to Mumbai in 1937. The current RBI Governor is Sanjay Malhotra (since 11 December 2024).

### Q9. The 101st Constitutional Amendment is related to:
(a) Goods and Services Tax (b) Right to Education (c) Cooperative Societies (d) Banking

**Answer: (a) Goods and Services Tax**

**Solution:** The 101st Constitutional Amendment Act, 2016 enabled GST (Goods and Services Tax). It added Article 279A for the GST Council. The 86th Amendment (2002) made the Right to Education (6-14 years) a Fundamental Right under Article 21A. The 97th Amendment (2011) was about Cooperative Societies. The 103rd Amendment (2019) provided 10% EWS reservation. The 104th Amendment (2020) extended SC/ST reservation in legislatures. The 106th Amendment (2023) provided Women's Reservation.

### Q10. "Fiscal Deficit" means:
(a) Excess of revenue expenditure over revenue receipts (b) Total expenditure minus total receipts excluding borrowings (c) Difference between exports and imports (d) Difference between income and expenditure

**Answer: (b) Total expenditure minus total receipts excluding borrowings**

**Solution:** Fiscal Deficit = Total Expenditure - Total Receipts (excluding borrowings). The Fiscal Deficit target for FY 2025-26 is 4.4% of GDP. The Revenue Deficit is the difference between revenue expenditure and revenue receipts. Primary Deficit = Fiscal Deficit - Interest Payments. Current Account Deficit (CAD) is the difference between exports and imports of goods, services, and transfers. India's CAD is around 1% of GDP (FY25).

**Time Target:** 45s | **Chapter Reference:** GK/04_Indian_Economy.md, p.67-68 | **VERIFY:** No

### Q11. NABARD was established in:
(a) 1975 (b) 1982 (c) 1990 (d) 1999

**Answer: (b) 1982**

**Solution:** NABARD (National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development) was established on 12 July 1982. It is the apex institution for agriculture and rural development finance, headquartered in Mumbai. It was established based on the recommendations of the Sivaraman Committee. SIDBI (Small Industries Development Bank of India) was established on 2 April 1990 with HQ in Lucknow. EXIM Bank was established on 7 January 1982 with HQ in Mumbai. NHB (National Housing Bank) was established in 1988.

### Q12. SEBI (Securities and Exchange Board of India) was established as a statutory body in:
(a) 1988 (b) 1990 (c) 1992 (d) 1995

**Answer: (c) 1992**

**Solution:** SEBI was constituted as a non-statutory body on 12 April 1988 and became a statutory body under the SEBI Act 1992. Its headquarters is in Mumbai. SEBI regulates stock exchanges, brokers, mutual funds, and protects investor interests. The BSE (Bombay Stock Exchange, 1875) is Asia's oldest; its index is Sensex (30 stocks). NSE (National Stock Exchange) was established in 1992 and started trading in 1994; its index is Nifty 50 (50 stocks).

### Q13. The IBC (Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code) came into force on:
(a) 1 December 2015 (b) 1 December 2016 (c) 1 April 2017 (d) 1 January 2018

**Answer: (b) 1 December 2016**

**Solution:** The Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), 2016 came into force on 1 December 2016. It created the IBBI (Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India) as the regulator and the NCLT (National Company Law Tribunal) as the adjudicating authority. The CIRP (Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process) has a maximum period of 180 + 90 = 270 days, extendable up to 330 days. If no resolution plan is approved, the company goes into liquidation. Major cases resolved under IBC: Bhushan Steel (Tata Steel), Videocon, Jet Airways (liquidation).

**Time Target:** 30s | **Chapter Reference:** GK/04_Indian_Economy.md, p.564, p.636 | **VERIFY:** No

### Q14. India's rank by nominal GDP (as of 2024-25) is:
(a) 3rd (b) 5th (c) 7th (d) 10th

**Answer: (b) 5th**

**Solution:** India is the 5th largest economy by nominal GDP (after USA, China, Japan, Germany) and 3rd by Purchasing Power Parity (PPP). India overtook the UK as the 5th largest economy in 2022. India's nominal GDP (FY24-25) is approximately ₹301 lakh crore (US$3.6 trillion). The current RBI Governor is Sanjay Malhotra. The Fiscal Deficit target for FY 2025-26 is 4.4% of GDP. India's per capita income (FY24-25) is approximately ₹1.99 lakh.

### Q15. PMJDY (Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana) was launched on:
(a) 15 August 2014 (b) 28 August 2014 (c) 1 January 2015 (d) 26 January 2015

**Answer: (b) 28 August 2014**

**Solution:** PMJDY was launched on 28 August 2014 by PM Narendra Modi (announced on Independence Day, 15 August 2014). The slogan is "Mera Khata, Bhagya Vidhata" (My account, fortune maker). It provides zero-balance bank accounts (Basic Savings Bank Deposit Account - BSBDA) with RuPay debit card. Features: Accident insurance up to ₹2 lakh, life insurance ₹30,000 (for accounts opened between 15 Aug 2014 - 31 Jan 2015), overdraft up to ₹10,000. Over 50 crore accounts have been opened.
