## Q1. Conclusion (Easy)
"The bank has increased interest rates on deposits." Conclusion:
(a) More deposits  (b) Customers will benefit  (c) Bank profitable  (d) Less loans

**Answer:** (a) More deposits
**Solution:** Direct.
**Shortcut:** Direct consequence.
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 20 sec

---

## Q2. Action (Easy)
"ATMs frequently run out of cash." Action:
(a) Increase cash  (b) Close ATMs  (c) Reduce ATMs  (d) Increase fees

**Answer:** (a) Increase cash
**Solution:** Practical.
**Shortcut:** Address problem.
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 20 sec

---

## Q3. Cause (Easy)
"Bank queues long." Cause?
(a) Fewer staff  (b) More customers  (c) System slow  (d) All above possible

**Answer:** (d) All above possible
**Solution:** Multiple causes possible.
**Shortcut:** Direct causes.
**Common Mistake):** Only one.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q4. Assumption (Medium)
"Apply for the loan before 31st." Assumption:
(a) Loans available  (b) Limited time  (c) Apply online  (d) Approval certain

**Answer:** (b) Limited time
**Solution:** Without limit, no deadline.
**Shortcut:** Necessity.
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q5. Conclusion (Medium)
"Bank has introduced mobile banking." Conclusion:
(a) All customers use mobile  (b) Transactions will increase  (c) Branches close  (d) Fees reduced

**Answer:** (b) Transactions will increase
**Solution:** Direct.
**Shortcut:** Direct.
**Common Mistake:** All use.

## Q6. Assertion (Medium)
A: "All banks need deposits." R: "Banks lend from deposits."
(a) Both true, R explains  (b) Both true, R doesn't explain  (c) A true, R false  (d) A false, R true

**Answer:** (a) Both true, R explains
**Solution:** R explains why.
**Shortcut:** Check explanation.
**Common Mistake):** Other.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q7. Action Multi (Medium)
"Loan defaults increasing." Actions: (I) Stricter credit checks. (II) Lower interest.
(a) Only I  (b) Only II  (c) Both  (d) Neither

**Answer:** (a) Only I
**Solution:** I addresses problem.
**Shortcut:** Practicality.
**Common Mistake:** Both.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q8. Cause (Medium)
Statement 1: "ATM cash stolen." Statement 2: "Security guard absent."
(a) 1 cause, 2 effect  (b) 2 cause, 1 effect  (c) Independent  (d) Cannot say

**Answer:** (b) 2 cause, 1 effect
**Solution:** No security → theft.
**Shortcut:** Causal link.
**Common Mistake:** Independent.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q9. Conclusion Syllogism (Medium)
"All customers are account holders. Some account holders are premium." Conclusion:
(I) Some customers are premium.  (II) All premium are customers.
(a) Only I  (b) Only II  (c) Both  (d) Neither

**Answer:** (d) Neither
**Solution:** Neither is certain.
**Shortcut:** Definite.
**Common Mistake:** Both.
**Time target:** 45 sec

---

## Q10. Action Practical (Hard)
"Bank's NPAs increased 40%." Best action?
(a) Hide the data  (b) Recover loans  (c) Stop lending  (d) Increase interest

**Answer:** (b) Recover loans
**Solution:** Addresses problem directly.
**Shortcut:** Practical.
**Common Mistake:** Stop lending.
**Time target:** 45 sec

---

## Q11. Multi Statement (Hard)
Statements: (1) All employees are paid. (2) Some employees are managers. (3) No manager is owner. Conclusion: Some paid are not owners.
(a) Yes  (b) No  (c) Cannot say  (d) Always

**Answer:** (a) Yes
**Solution:** All employees are paid. Some are managers. No manager is owner. So some paid (managers) are not owners. ✓
**Shortcut:** Syllogism.
**Common Mistake:** Cannot say.
**Time target:** 60 sec

---

## Q12. Action (Hard)
"Customer complaints about service." Best action?
(I) Hire more staff.  (II) Improve training.  (III) Reduce branches.
(a) I and II  (b) II and III  (c) All  (d) I only

**Answer:** (a) I and II
**Solution:** I and II address problem. III is extreme.
**Shortcut:** Practicality.
**Common Mistake:** All.
**Time target:** 45 sec
