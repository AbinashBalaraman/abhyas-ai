# Practice Set: Inequalities & Input-Output — SSC CGL Tier-I (Set 1)

**Source:** Standard Reference Reasoning SSC Ch.3 SRE-149-192, Standard Reference Reasoning 8th Ch.26 & Ch.29
**Pattern:** 15 Questions | 60 seconds each | Total 15 minutes
**Difficulty:** Easy-Medium (SSC CGL Tier-I Standard)

---

### Q1. Statements: A > B ≥ C < D = E. Conclusion: A ? E
(a) A > E (b) A < E (c) A ≥ E (d) Cannot be determined

**Answer:** (d) Cannot be determined
**Solution:** A > B ≥ C < D = E. A and E are in different segments. No definite relation.
**Time Target:** 30 sec |

---

### Q2. Statements: P > Q, Q ≥ R, R > S. Conclusion: P > S?
(a) Yes (b) No (c) Cannot be determined (d) P ≤ S

**Answer:** (a) Yes
**Solution:** P > Q ≥ R > S → P > S ✓.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

---

### Q3. Statements: A ≤ B, B = C, C < D. Conclusion: A < D?
(a) Yes (b) No (c) Cannot be determined (d) A ≥ D

**Answer:** (a) Yes
**Solution:** A ≤ B = C < D → A < D ✓.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

---

### Q4. Statements: X ≥ Y > Z < W. Conclusion: X > W?
(a) Yes (b) No (c) Cannot be determined (d) X ≤ W

**Answer:** (c) Cannot be determined
**Solution:** X ≥ Y > Z < W. Z < W (Z is less than W), but X ≥ Y > Z so X > Z. X and W in different directions from Z. Cannot determine X vs W.
**Time Target:** 35 sec |

---

### Q5. Statements: P = Q, Q ≥ R, R > S. Conclusion: P > S?
(a) Yes (b) No (c) Cannot be determined (d) P ≤ S

**Answer:** (a) Yes
**Solution:** P = Q ≥ R > S → P ≥ R > S → P > S ✓.
**Time Target:** 30 sec |

---

### Q6. Statements: A > B, B ≥ C, C < D, D = E. Conclusion: A > E?
(a) Yes (b) No (c) Cannot be determined (d) A < E

**Answer:** (c) Cannot be determined
**Solution:** A > B ≥ C < D = E. C and E connected by <, but A and C are connected by >. A and E in different segments.
**Time Target:** 35 sec |

---

### Q7. Coded: A @ B means A ≥ B; A # B means A < B. A @ B # C is?
(a) A ≥ B < C (b) A ≥ B > C (c) A < B < C (d) A > B > C

**Answer:** (a) A ≥ B < C
**Solution:** @ = ≥, # = <. So A ≥ B < C.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

---

### Q8. Coded: P $ Q means P = Q; P % Q means P > Q. P $ Q % R means?
(a) P = Q > R (b) P = Q < R (c) P > Q = R (d) P < Q = R

**Answer:** (a) P = Q > R
**Solution:** $ = =, % = >. So P = Q > R.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

### Q9. Input: 47 56 32 19 88 73 25 64. Step I: 88 47 56 32 19 73 25 64. Step II: 88 73 47 56 32 19 25 64. Step III = ?
(a) 88 73 64 47 56 32 19 25 (b) 88 73 64 47 56 32 25 19 (c) 88 64 73 47 56 32 19 25 (d) 88 73 64 47 56 19 32 25

**Answer:** (a) 88 73 64 47 56 32 19 25
**Solution:** Logic: Each step takes the largest remaining and places it at the leftmost available. After 88, 73, the next largest is 64. So Step III: 88 73 64 ... + remaining in original order.
**Time Target:** 50 sec |

### Q10. Input-Output: 
Input: 23 56 18 42 91 67
Step I: 91 23 56 18 42 67
Step II: 91 67 23 56 18 42
Step III = ?
(a) 91 67 56 23 18 42 (b) 91 67 23 56 18 42 (c) 91 56 67 23 18 42 (d) 91 67 42 23 56 18

**Answer:** (a) 91 67 56 23 18 42
**Solution:** Pattern: largest to left, then second largest to position 2, then third largest to position 3, etc.
**Time Target:** 50 sec |

---

### Q11. Statements: A ≥ B, B > C, C ≥ D. Conclusion I: A > D. Conclusion II: A ≥ C.
(a) Only I (b) Only II (c) Both (d) Neither

**Answer:** (c) Both
**Solution:** A ≥ B > C ≥ D → A > D ✓. A ≥ B > C → A > C, hence A ≥ C ✓.
**Time Target:** 35 sec |

---

### Q12. Statements: P < Q = R > S < T. Conclusion: Q > T?
(a) Yes (b) No (c) Cannot be determined (d) Q ≤ T

**Answer:** (c) Cannot be determined
**Solution:** Q = R > S < T. R and S in one segment, T separately. Q and T in different segments.
**Time Target:** 35 sec |

---

### Q13. Coded: A & B = A > B; A * B = A ≤ B. A & B * C means?
(a) A > B ≤ C (b) A > B < C (c) A ≤ B < C (d) A > B > C

**Answer:** (a) A > B ≤ C
**Solution:** & = >, * = ≤. So A > B ≤ C.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

---

### Q14. Input-Output: 
Input: cat dog bat ant
Step I: ant cat dog bat
Step II: ant bat cat dog
Step III = ?
(a) ant bat cat dog (b) ant bat dog cat (c) bat ant cat dog (d) ant cat bat dog

**Answer:** (a) ant bat cat dog
**Solution:** Words sorted alphabetically.
**Time Target:** 35 sec |

---

### Q15. Statements: A ≤ B, B = C, C < D, D ≥ E. Conclusion: A ≤ E?
(a) Yes (b) No (c) Cannot be determined (d) A > E

**Answer:** (c) Cannot be determined
**Solution:** A ≤ B = C < D ≥ E. A and E in different segments (different direction chain).
**Time Target:** 40 sec |
