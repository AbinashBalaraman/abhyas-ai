### Q1. Statements:
S1: All pens are books.
S2: All books are bags.
S3: No bag is a box.
Conclusions:
I. Some pens are not boxes.
II. At least some bags are pens.
(a) Only I follows (b) Only II follows (c) Both follow (d) Neither follows

**Answer:** (c) Both follow
**Solution:** Venn: Pens → Books → Bags. Bags separate from Box. I: Pens inside Bags, Boxes outside Bags → Some pens are not boxes ✓. II: Pens are inside Bags, so "some bags are pens" ✓.
**Time Target:** 45 sec |

### Q2. Statements:
S1: All cats are animals.
S2: Some animals are wild.
Conclusions:
I. Some cats are wild.
II. Some wild are animals.
(a) Only I (b) Only II (c) Both (d) Neither

**Answer:** (b) Only II
**Solution:** I doesn't follow (wild part of animals may not include cats). II: "Some animals are wild" is reversible → "Some wild are animals" ✓.

### Q5. Statements:
S1: Some students are boys.
S2: All boys are tall.
Conclusions:
I. Some students are tall.
II. Some tall are students.
(a) Only I (b) Only II (c) Both (d) Neither

**Answer:** (c) Both
**Solution:** Students ∩ Boys ≠ ∅. Boys inside Tall. So Students ∩ Tall ≠ ∅ (some students are tall) ✓. Reversible: some tall are students ✓.

### Q12. Statements:

### Q15. Statements:
S1: Some P are Q.
S2: No Q is R.
Conclusions:
I. Some P are not R.
II. Some Q are P.
(a) Only I (b) Only II (c) Both (d) Either/Or

**Answer:** (b) Only II
**Solution:** I: P includes Q (which is not R) but P also has parts outside Q which may be R. Doesn't follow definitively. II: "Some P are Q" → "Some Q are P" ✓.
