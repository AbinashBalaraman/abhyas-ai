# Practice Set: Statement, Conclusion, Assumption, Data Sufficiency — SSC CGL Tier-II (Mains) (Set 2)

**Source:** Standard Reference Reasoning SSC Ch.10, Standard Reference Reasoning 8th Ch.7 & Ch.8
**Pattern:** 15 Questions | 60 seconds each | Total 15 minutes
**Difficulty:** Hard (SSC CGL Tier-II Mains Standard)

---

### Q1. Statement: "The company has decided to increase the price of its products to improve profit margins." Assumption:
(a) Customers will continue to buy
(b) Product quality is high
(c) Competitors will also raise prices
(d) Costs are rising

**Answer:** (a) Customers will continue to buy
**Solution:** The price increase assumes customers will not switch to competitors.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

---

### Q2. Statement: "Regular exercise and balanced diet are essential for a healthy life." Conclusion: I. People who don't exercise are unhealthy. II. Exercise alone can ensure health.
(a) Only I follows (b) Only II follows (c) Both follow (d) Neither follows

**Answer:** (d) Neither follows
**Solution:** "Essential" doesn't mean "only" or that absence = unhealthy.
**Time Target:** 30 sec |

---

### Q3. Data Sufficiency: What is the value of X + Y?
I. X = 10
II. Y = 20
(a) I alone is sufficient (b) II alone is sufficient (c) Both together are sufficient (d) Cannot be determined

**Answer:** (c) Both together are sufficient
**Solution:** Together: X + Y = 30.
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

---

### Q4. Statement: "The new policy aims to reduce unemployment by providing skill training." Assumption:
(a) Skill training reduces unemployment
(b) People want jobs
(c) Government is responsible for jobs
(d) Training is expensive

**Answer:** (a) Skill training reduces unemployment
**Solution:** The policy's logic assumes training → jobs.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

### Q5. Course of Action: "There has been a significant increase in cyber crimes in the country."
(a) Ban the internet (b) Strengthen cyber laws and train police
(c) Arrest all hackers (d) Make computers illegal

**Answer:** (b) Strengthen cyber laws and train police
**Solution:** Practical action.
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

---

### Q6. Statement: "All successful people wake up early." Conclusion: I. Waking up early leads to success. II. People who don't wake up early are unsuccessful.
(a) Only I follows (b) Only II follows (c) Both follow (d) Neither follows

**Answer:** (a) Only I follows
**Solution:** "All successful wake up early" implies waking up early is associated with success. Reverse doesn't follow (correlation ≠ causation).
**Time Target:** 30 sec |

---

### Q7. Data Sufficiency: Is P > Q?
I. P = 2Q
II. Q = 5
(a) I alone is sufficient (b) II alone is sufficient (c) Both together are sufficient (d) Either alone is sufficient

**Answer:** (a) I alone is sufficient (with positive numbers)
**Solution:** P = 2Q means P > Q (if Q > 0). I alone sufficient.
**Time Target:** 30 sec |

---

### Q8. Statement: "The factory has been closed due to environmental violations." Assumption:
(a) Environmental violations are serious
(b) Factory was at fault
(c) Pollution is harmful
(d) Other factories violate too

**Answer:** (b) Factory was at fault
**Solution:** "Closed due to violations" assumes the factory caused them.
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

### Q9. Statement: "Education is the most powerful weapon to change the world." Conclusion: I. Educated people can change the world. II. Without education, world cannot change.
(a) Only I follows (b) Only II follows (c) Both follow (d) Neither follows

**Answer:** (a) Only I follows

### Q10. Data Sufficiency: What is the total number of students in a class?
I. Boys are 60% of the class.
II. There are 24 boys.
(a) I alone is sufficient (b) II alone is sufficient (c) Both together are sufficient (d) Cannot be determined

**Answer:** (c) Both together are sufficient
**Solution:** Together: 24 boys = 60%, total = 40.
**Time Target:** 30 sec |

### Q12. Statement: "Honesty is the best policy." Conclusion: I. Dishonest people fail. II. Honest people are successful.

### Q13. Statement: "The company has appointed a new CEO with extensive experience in the industry." Assumption:
(a) Industry experience is important for the role
(b) New CEO will be successful
(c) Old CEO was unsuccessful
(d) CEO salary is high

**Answer:** (a) Industry experience is important for the role
**Solution:** "Extensive experience" mentioned as positive assumes it matters.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

### Q14. Data Sufficiency: How many days will A and B together take to complete a work?
I. A alone can do it in 10 days.
II. B alone can do it in 20 days.
(a) I alone is sufficient (b) II alone is sufficient (c) Both together are sufficient (d) Cannot be determined

**Answer:** (c) Both together are sufficient
**Solution:** Together: A+B rate = 1/10 + 1/20 = 3/20. Time = 20/3 days.
**Time Target:** 35 sec |

---

### Q15. Statement: "The government should invest more in rural development to reduce urban migration." Conclusion: I. Urban migration is a problem. II. Rural development reduces urban migration.
(a) Only I follows (b) Only II follows (c) Both follow (d) Neither follows

**Answer:** (c) Both follow
**Solution:** The statement assumes urban migration is a problem and rural development addresses it.
**Time Target:** 30 sec |
