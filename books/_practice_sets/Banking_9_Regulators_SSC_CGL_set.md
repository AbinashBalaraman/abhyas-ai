### Q1. The headquarters of RBI is in:
(a) New Delhi (b) Kolkata (c) Mumbai (d) Chennai

**Answer: (c) Mumbai**

**Solution:** The headquarters of the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) is in Mumbai, Maharashtra. Originally, RBI's headquarters was in Kolkata, and it was shifted to Mumbai in 1937. RBI is the central bank of India, established on 1 April 1935. RBI has 4 regional boards (Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, New Delhi) and 27 zonal offices. The current RBI Governor is Sanjay Malhotra (since 11 December 2024). RBI performs multiple functions: monetary authority, issuer of currency, banker to the government, banker's bank, regulator of banking, manager of foreign exchange, and regulator of payment systems.

### Q2. SEBI was constituted in:
(a) 1985 (b) 1988 (c) 1992 (d) 1995

**Answer: (b) 1988**

**Solution:** SEBI (Securities and Exchange Board of India) was constituted as a non-statutory body on 12 April 1988 (Government resolution). It became a statutory body under the SEBI Act 1992 (provisions came into force on 30 January 1992). SEBI's headquarters is in Mumbai. SEBI regulates the securities market, stock exchanges, brokers, mutual funds, and other intermediaries. SEBI's preamble is "to protect the interests of investors in securities and to promote the development of, and to regulate the securities market". The current SEBI Chairman is Tuhin Kanta Pandey (verify).

### Q3. SEBI's statutory form came into force on:
(a) 12 April 1988 (b) 30 January 1992 (c) 1 April 1992 (d) 1 January 1995

**Answer: (b) 30 January 1992**

**Solution:** SEBI's statutory form came into force on 30 January 1992 under the SEBI Act 1992. SEBI was first constituted as a non-statutory body on 12 April 1988 (Government resolution). The SEBI Act 1992 made it a statutory body. SEBI is headquartered in Mumbai. SEBI regulates the securities market, including stock exchanges (BSE, NSE), brokers, sub-brokers, mutual funds, and other intermediaries. SEBI's major regulations: ICDR (2018), LODR (2015), PIT (2015), SAST (2011). SEBI is sometimes called the "watchdog of the Indian securities market".

### Q4. IRDAI headquarters is in:
(a) New Delhi (b) Mumbai (c) Hyderabad (d) Chennai

### Q5. IRDAI was established in:
(a) 1995 (b) 1999 (c) 2000 (d) 2005

**Answer: (b) 1999**

**Solution:** IRDAI (Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India) was established in 1999 under the IRDA Act, 1999. Its headquarters is in Hyderabad (moved from Delhi in 2001). IRDAI has 10 members (Chairperson, 5 full-time, 4 part-time). The current Chairperson is Ajay Seth, IAS (as of July 2025). IRDAI regulates the insurance industry, including registration of insurers, solvency margin regulation, product approval, investment regulations, and TPA regulations. The FDI limit was raised to 74% in 2021. IRDAI removed the age limit (65 years) for health insurance from 1 April 2024.

### Q6. PFRDA headquarters is in:

### Q8. IFSCA was established in:
(a) 2015 (b) 2018 (c) 2020 (d) 2022

**Answer: (c) 2020**

**Solution:** IFSCA (International Financial Services Centres Authority) was established on 27 April 2020 under the International Financial Services Centres Authority Act, 2019. IFSCA is headquartered in GIFT City, Gandhinagar, Gujarat. IFSCA is a single unified regulator for IFSC (International Financial Services Centre) at GIFT City, replacing multiple regulators (RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, PFRDA) in IFSC. IFSCA regulates financial products, services, and institutions in IFSC, including banking, capital markets, insurance, and pension. The current IFSCA Chairperson is K. Rajaraman (verify). IFSCA aims to promote financial services exports from India.

### Q10. FSDC is chaired by:
(a) RBI Governor (b) PM of India (c) Union Finance Minister (d) SEBI Chairman

**Answer: (c) Union Finance Minister**

**Solution:** FSDC (Financial Stability and Development Council) is chaired by the Union Finance Minister. It is an apex-level body for coordinating financial sector regulation and macroprudential oversight. FSDC's members include heads of all financial regulators (RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, PFRDA, IFSCA) and senior officials. The FSDC Sub-Committee is chaired by the RBI Governor and includes SEBI, IRDAI, PFRDA heads. FSDC was set up in 2010 in the wake of the 2008 global financial crisis. FSDC is an executive body, not a statutory one (no act of Parliament). It focuses on financial stability, inter-regulatory coordination, and financial sector development.

### Q11. NPCI is headquartered in:
(a) New Delhi (b) Mumbai (c) Bengaluru (d) Hyderabad

**Answer: (b) Mumbai**

**Solution:** NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India) is headquartered in Mumbai. NPCI was established in 2008 under Section 8 of the Companies Act. NPCI is the umbrella organization for retail payments in India. NPCI operates UPI, IMPS, NACH, AePS, RuPay, CTS, e-RUPI, and Bharat Bill Payment System (BBPS). NPCI is promoted by 10 banks (each holding 10% stake). NPCI is regulated by RBI under the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007. NPCI plays a key role in India's digital payment revolution. UPI handles ~640 million transactions daily (as of August 2025).

### Q14. IBBI is the regulator for:
(a) Insurance (b) Banking (c) Insolvency and Bankruptcy (d) Securities

**Answer: (c) Insolvency and Bankruptcy**

**Solution:** IBBI (Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India) is the regulator for insolvency and bankruptcy in India. IBBI was established in 2016 under the IBC (Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code), 2016. IBBI is headquartered in New Delhi. IBBI regulates Insolvency Professionals (IPs), Information Utilities (IUs), and Asset Reconstruction Companies (ARCs). IBBI writes rules for the Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP) and liquidation. IBBI has 10 members (Chairperson + 3 whole-time + 4 part-time). The NCLT is the adjudicating authority and NCLAT is the appellate authority. IBBI has played a key role in resolving corporate insolvency cases.

### Q15. NABARD was established in:
(a) 1975 (b) 1980 (c) 1982 (d) 1990

**Answer: (c) 1982**

**Solution:** NABARD (National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development) was established on 12 July 1982. Its headquarters is in Mumbai. NABARD is the apex institution for agriculture and rural development finance. NABARD was established based on the recommendations of the Sivaraman Committee. NABARD supervises State Cooperative Banks (StCBs) and Central Cooperative Banks (CCBs) under Section 35(6) of the BR Act. NABARD provides refinance to banks for agricultural and rural lending. NABARD is also the nodal agency for implementing many government schemes in rural areas.
