## Q1. Mirror H (Easy)
Mirror of "H":
(a) H  (b) I  (c) Different  (d) Cannot say

**Answer:** (a) H
**Solution:** H is symmetric in vertical mirror.
**Shortcut:** Symmetric letters: A, H, I, M, O, T, U, V, W, X, Y.
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 5 sec

---

## Q2. Mirror M (Easy)
Mirror of "M":
(a) M  (b) W  (c) Different  (d) Cannot say

**Answer:** (a) M
**Solution:** M is symmetric in vertical mirror.
**Shortcut:** Symmetric.
**Common Mistake:** W.
**Time target:** 5 sec

## Q3. Water d (Easy)
Water of "d":
(a) d  (b) b  (c) q  (d) p

**Answer:** (c) q
**Solution:** Top-bottom flip.
**Shortcut:** Water = vertical flip.
**Common Mistake:** b.
**Time target:** 5 sec

## Q4. Classification Shapes (Easy)
Odd one: square, triangle, circle, pentagon
(a) square  (b) triangle  (c) circle  (d) pentagon

**Answer:** (c) circle
**Solution:** Circle has no sides; others have sides.
**Shortcut:** Sides count.
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 15 sec

## Q5. Series Squares (Medium)
Series: 1, 4, 9, 16, ?
(a) 20  (b) 25  (c) 30  (d) 36

**Answer:** (b) 25
**Solution:** n-squared pattern. 5-squared = 25.
**Shortcut:** n-squared.
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 15 sec

## Q6. Painted 3×3 3-face (Medium)
3×3×3 cube painted, cut. Cubes with 3 faces painted?
(a) 1  (b) 4  (c) 6  (d) 8

**Answer:** (d) 8
**Solution:** Corners = 8.
**Shortcut:** Always 8.
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 15 sec

## Q7. Triangles 4-Triangle (Medium)
Triangles in a triangle divided into 4 smaller:
(a) 4  (b) 6  (c) 8  (d) 13

**Answer:** (c) 8
**Solution:** 4 small + 1 large + 3 inverted = 8.
**Shortcut:** Count.
**Common Mistake:** Missing inverted.
**Time target:** 60 sec

---

## Q8. Mirror CHN (Medium)
Mirror of "CHN":
(a) NHO  (b) HNC  (c) NHC  (d) ONH

**Answer:** (c) NHC
**Solution:** Each letter mirrored, reversed in order.
**Shortcut:** Reverse order with each letter flipped.
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q9. Squares 5×5 (Medium)
Squares in 5×5 grid:
(a) 30  (b) 45  (c) 55  (d) 65

**Answer:** (c) 55
**Solution:** n(n+1)(2n+1)/6 = 5×6×11/6 = 55.
**Shortcut:** n(n+1)(2n+1)/6.
**Common Mistake:** Less.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q10. Die Back (Hard)
Die: front=2, right=3, top=1. Back?
(a) 5  (b) 6  (c) 4  (d) 1

**Answer:** (a) 5
**Solution:** Back = opposite of 2 = 5.
**Shortcut:** Sum to 7.
**Common Mistake:** Bottom (6).
**Time target:** 45 sec

---

## Q11. Series Hard (Hard)
Series: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?
(a) 36  (b) 40  (c) 42  (d) 44

**Answer:** (c) 42
**Solution:** n-squared + n pattern. 6-squared + 6 = 42.
**Shortcut:** n(n+1).
**Common Mistake:** Other pattern.
**Time target:** 60 sec

---

## Q12. Painted 6×6 zero (Hard)
6×6×6 cube painted, cut. Cubes with 0 faces painted?
(a) 64  (b) 100  (c) 125  (d) 216

**Answer:** (a) 64
**Solution:** (6-2)³ = 64.
**Shortcut:** (n-2)³.
**Common Mistake:** n³.
**Time target:** 20 sec
