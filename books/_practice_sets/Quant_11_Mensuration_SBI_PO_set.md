# SBI PO Prelims — Mensuration (2D & 3D)

> **Exam:** SBI PO Prelims | **Time:** 8 min (45 sec/Q) | **Total:** 10 Qs
> **Difficulty:** 3 Easy, 5 Medium, 2 Hard | **Trap Qs:** 1

## Q1. Square perimeter (Easy)
Square side 7. Perimeter:
(a) 14  (b) 28  (c) 49  (d) 7

**Answer:** (b) 28
**Solution:** 4·7 = 28. ✓
**Shortcut:** 4a.
**Common Mistake:** Using area.
**Time target:** 15 sec

---

## Q2. Triangle area (Easy)
Triangle base 8, height 5. Area:
(a) 40  (b) 20  (c) 13  (d) 30

**Answer:** (b) 20
**Solution:** ½·8·5 = 20. ✓
**Shortcut:** ½·b·h.
**Common Mistake:** b·h.
**Time target:** 15 sec

---

## Q3. Cube volume (Easy — Trap)
Cube side 6. Volume:
(a) 36  (b) 216  (c) 18  (d) 12

**Answer:** (b) 216
**Solution:** 6³ = 216. ✓
**Shortcut:** a³.
**Common Mistake:** a².
**Time target:** 15 sec

---

## Q4. Cylinder TSA (Medium)
Cylinder r=7, h=2. TSA:
(a) 44π  (b) 28π  (c) 56π  (d) 126π

**Answer:** (d) 126π
**Solution:** TSA = 2πr(r+h) = 2π·7·9 = 126π. ✓
**Shortcut:** 2πr(r+h).
**Common Mistake:** CSA only.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q5. Cone volume (Medium)
Cone r=6, h=7. Volume:
(a) 84π  (b) 252π  (c) 42π  (d) 126π

**Answer:** (a) 84π
**Solution:** (1/3)π·36·7 = 84π. ✓
**Shortcut:** (1/3)πr²h.
**Common Mistake:** Using cylinder.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q6. Circle area (Medium)
Circle r=14. Area:
(a) 196  (b) 616  (c) 154  (d) 88

**Answer:** (b) 616
**Solution:** A = (22/7)·196 = 616. ✓
**Shortcut:** πr² with π=22/7.
**Common Mistake:** Using π=3.14.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q7. Sphere volume (Medium)
Sphere r=3. Volume:
(a) 36π  (b) 27π  (c) 4π  (d) 12π

**Answer:** (a) 36π
**Solution:** V = (4/3)π·27 = 36π. ✓
**Shortcut:** (4/3)πr³.
**Common Mistake:** Using 4πr².
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q8. Cuboid TSA (Medium)
Cuboid 4×3×2. TSA:
(a) 24  (b) 52  (c) 12  (d) 26

**Answer:** (b) 52
**Solution:** 2(lb+bh+hl) = 2(12+6+8) = 52. ✓
**Shortcut:** 2(ab+bc+ca).
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting to multiply by 2.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q9. Path inside (Hard)
Path 2 m wide inside 30×20 m garden. Remaining garden area:
(a) 416  (b) 484  (c) 384  (d) 600

**Answer:** (a) 416
**Solution:** Inner dimensions = 26×16 = 416. ✓
**Shortcut:** (L−2w)(B−2w).
**Common Mistake:** Subtracting 2w only once.
**Time target:** 60 sec

---

## Q10. Cone-cylinder melt (Hard — Trap)
Cone r=3, h=12 melted into cylinder r=3. Cylinder h:
(a) 4  (b) 8  (c) 12  (d) 36

**Answer:** (a) 4
**Solution:** V_cone = (1/3)π·9·12 = 36π. V_cyl = π·9·h → h = 4. ✓
**Shortcut:** h_cyl = (1/3)·h_cone.
**Common Mistake:** Setting h_cyl = 3·h_cone.
**Time target:** 45 sec
