### Q1. He said, "I am happy." — Indirect:
(a) He said that he is happy
(b) He said that he was happy
(c) He said that he has been happy
(d) He said that he is being happy

**Answer:** (b) He said that he was happy

**Solution:** "Said" is past → **tense backshift**: "am" → "was". Pronoun shift: "I" → "he". [Source: §2.1, §2.3]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "is happy" — not applying backshift.

**Time Target:** 5 sec

---

### Q2. Active: "He wrote a letter." — Passive:
(a) A letter was written by him
(b) A letter is written by him
(c) A letter has been written by him
(d) A letter was being written by him

**Answer:** (a) A letter was written by him

**Solution:** **Past Simple** (wrote) → **Past Simple Passive** (was + V3). [Source: §3.3]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (b) "is written" (Present Passive — wrong tense).

**Time Target:** 5 sec

---

### Q3. He said, "I will come." — Indirect:
(a) He said that he will come
(b) He said that he would come
(c) He said that he will be coming
(d) He said that he came

**Answer:** (b) He said that he would come

**Solution:** "**Will**" → "**would**" (modal backshift). [Source: §2.4]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "will" — not backshifting.

**Time Target:** 5 sec

### Q4. Active: "She bought a dress." — Passive:
(a) A dress is bought by her
(b) A dress was bought by her
(c) A dress has been bought by her
(d) A dress was being bought by her

**Answer:** (b) A dress was bought by her

**Solution:** **Past Simple** (bought) → **Past Simple Passive** (was + V3). [Source: §3.3]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "is bought" (wrong tense).

**Time Target:** 5 sec

---

### Q5. He said, "I have done it." — Indirect:
(a) He said that he has done it
(b) He said that he had done it
(c) He said that he did it
(d) He said that he does it

**Answer:** (b) He said that he had done it

**Solution:** "**Have done**" (Present Perfect) → "**had done**" (Past Perfect). [Source: §2.3]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "has done" — not applying backshift.

**Time Target:** 5 sec

---

### Q6. Active: "They are painting the wall." — Passive:
(a) The wall is painted by them
(b) The wall is being painted by them
(c) The wall was being painted by them
(d) The wall has been painted by them

**Answer:** (b) The wall is being painted by them

**Solution:** **Present Continuous** (are painting) → **Present Continuous Passive** (is being + V3). [Source: §3.3]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "is painted" (loses continuous).

**Time Target:** 5 sec

---

### Q7. He said to me, "Please help me." — Indirect:
(a) He ordered me to help him
(b) He requested me to help him
(c) He said to help me
(d) He told me to help him

**Answer:** (b) He requested me to help him

**Solution:** "**Please**" indicates request → use "**requested/asked**". [Source: §2.7]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "ordered" — "ordered" is for commands, not polite requests.

**Time Target:** 5 sec

---

### Q8. Active: "He can do it." — Passive:
(a) It can be done by him
(b) It can do by him
(c) It is done by him
(d) It can be do by him

**Answer:** (a) It can be done by him

**Solution:** **Modal + passive** = "**can + be + V3**". [Source: §3.5]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (b) "can do" — missing "be" between modal and V3.

**Time Target:** 5 sec

---

### Q9. She said, "I went to school." — Indirect:
(a) She said that she went to school
(b) She said that she had gone to school
(c) She said that she has gone to school
(d) She said that she goes to school

**Answer:** (b) She said that she had gone to school

**Solution:** **Past Simple** (went) → **Past Perfect** (had gone). [Source: §2.3]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "went" — not applying backshift.

**Time Target:** 5 sec

---

### Q10. Active: "They will complete the work." — Passive:
(a) The work will be completed by them
(b) The work is completed by them
(c) The work will complete by them
(d) The work will have completed by them

**Answer:** (a) The work will be completed by them

**Solution:** **Future Simple** (will complete) → **Future Simple Passive** (will be + V3). [Source: §3.3]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (b) "is completed" (wrong tense).

**Time Target:** 5 sec
