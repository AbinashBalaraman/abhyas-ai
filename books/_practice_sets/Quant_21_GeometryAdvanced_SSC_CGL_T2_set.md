## Q1. Pentagon Diagonals (Easy)
Number of diagonals in pentagon:
(a) 5  (b) 7  (c) 9  (d) 10

**Answer:** (a) 5
**Solution:** $\frac{5 \times 2}{2} = 5$
**Shortcut:** $\frac{n(n-3)}{2}$.
**Common Mistake:** Using ${}^5C_2 = 10$.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q2. Hexagon Interior Angle (Easy)
Each interior angle of regular hexagon:
(a) 90°  (b) 120°  (c) 108°  (d) 135°

**Answer:** (b) 120°
**Solution:** $(6-2) \times 180/6 = 120°$
**Shortcut:** $(n-2) \times 180/n$.
**Common Mistake:** Using 108.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q3. Circle Area (Easy)
Area of circle with radius 7 cm:
(a) 22  (b) 44  (c) 154  (d) 196

**Answer:** (c) 154
**Solution:** $\pi r^2 = 22/7 \times 49 = 154$
**Shortcut:** $\pi r^2$.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting π.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q4. Concentric Circles (Medium)
Two concentric circles with radii 3 and 5. Area of ring?
(a) 8π  (b) 16π  (c) 25π  (d) 50π

**Answer:** (b) 16π
**Solution:** $\pi(25 - 9) = 16\pi$
**Shortcut:** Difference of areas.
**Common Mistake:** Difference of radii.
**Time target:** 45 sec

## Q5. Triangle — Apollonius (Medium — Trap)
In a triangle, median to BC has length 5. AB = 4, AC = 6. Find BC.
(a) 2√10  (b) 2√7  (c) √58  (d) None

**Answer:** (d) None (theoretical: 2)
**Solution:** $m_a^2 = \frac{2b^2 + 2c^2 - a^2}{4}$. $25 = (2 \times 16 + 2 \times 36 - a^2)/4 = (104 - a^2)/4$. $a^2 = 4$. $a = 2$. None match exactly. Closest: (a) 2√10. Use **(a) 2√10** as the closest.
**Shortcut:** Apollonius's theorem.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting the formula.
**Time target:** 150 sec

## Q6. Tangent Length (Medium)
From external point, two tangents of length 5 cm. Distance to center is 13. Radius?
(a) 12  (b) 5  (c) 13  (d) 8

**Answer:** (a) 12
**Solution:** $\sqrt{13^2 - 5^2} = \sqrt{144} = 12$
**Shortcut:** 5-12-13 triple.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting to subtract.
**Time target:** 90 sec

## Q7. Inscribed Hexagon (Medium)
Regular hexagon inscribed in circle of radius R. Side length?
(a) R  (b) R/2  (c) R√3  (d) R√2

**Answer:** (a) R
**Solution:** Side of inscribed regular hexagon = R (radius).
**Shortcut:** Memorize: hexagon side = R.
**Common Mistake:** Using R√3.
**Time target:** 45 sec

## Q8. Cyclic Quadrilateral Sides (Hard)
In cyclic quadrilateral, opposite angles:
(a) Sum to 90°  (b) Sum to 180°  (c) Are equal  (d) Sum to 360°

**Answer:** (b) Sum to 180°
**Solution:** Property of cyclic quadrilateral.
**Shortcut:** Opposite angles supplementary.
**Common Mistake:** Returning 360.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q9. Equilateral Triangle (Hard)
Circumradius of equilateral triangle with side 6:
(a) 2√3  (b) 3√3  (c) 6√3  (d) 6

**Answer:** (a) 2√3
**Solution:** $R = a/\sqrt{3} = 6/\sqrt{3} = 2\sqrt{3}$
**Shortcut:** $R = a/\sqrt{3}$.
**Common Mistake:** Using $a/2$.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q10. Sector Area (Hard)
Sector with central angle 90° and radius 14:
(a) 154  (b) 308  (c) 77  (d) 196

**Answer:** (a) 154
**Solution:** $\frac{90}{360} \times \pi r^2 = \frac{1}{4} \times 22/7 \times 196 = 154$
**Shortcut:** $\frac{\theta}{360} \times \pi r^2$.
**Common Mistake:** Using 360.
**Time target:** 90 sec

## Q11. Locus (Hard)
Locus of point equidistant from two fixed points:
(a) Line joining them  (b) Perpendicular bisector  (c) Circle  (d) Ellipse

**Answer:** (b) Perpendicular bisector
**Solution:** Standard locus.
**Shortcut:** Perpendicular bisector.
**Common Mistake:** Returning circle.

## Q12. 15° Polygon (Hard)
How many sides does a regular polygon have if each exterior angle is 24°?
(a) 10  (b) 12  (c) 15  (d) 20

**Answer:** (c) 15
**Solution:** $360°/24° = 15$
**Shortcut:** $n = 360°/\text{exterior angle}$.
**Common Mistake:** Using 360.
**Time target:** 45 sec

## Q13. Tangent from External (Hard)
From external point, two tangents to a circle. Angle between them is 60°. Angle subtended at center by chord of contact:
(a) 60°  (b) 120°  (c) 90°  (d) 180°

**Answer:** (b) 120°
**Solution:** Angle between tangents = 180° - (angle at center). So 60° = 180° - θ → θ = 120°.
**Shortcut:** 180° - angle between tangents.
**Common Mistake:** Returning same.
**Time target:** 90 sec

---

## Q14. Incenter Distance (Hard)
In equilateral triangle, incenter is at:
(a) Centroid  (b) Circumcenter  (c) Same as all centers  (d) Vertex

**Answer:** (c) Same as all centers
**Solution:** In equilateral, all 4 centers coincide.
**Shortcut:** Special property of equilateral.
**Common Mistake:** Returning centroid only.
**Time target:** 30 sec
