## Q1. Series — Triangle Pattern (Easy)
What is the next figure in the series: △, ▲, ▽, ▼, ?
(a) △  (b) ◯  (c) ▢  (d) ▭

**Answer:** (a) △
**Solution:** Pattern: up, up-filled, down, down-filled. Next up.
**Shortcut:** Identify the rule.
**Common Mistake:** Random choice.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q2. Mirror Image "A" (Easy)
Mirror image of "A":
(a) A  (b) ∀  (c) Different  (d) Both a and c

**Answer:** (d) Both a and c
**Solution:** A is symmetric in vertical mirror.
**Shortcut:** Symmetric letters: A, H, I, M, O, T, U, V, W, X, Y.
**Common Mistake:** Marking different.
**Time target:** 5 sec

---

## Q3. Water Image "T" (Easy)
Water image of "T":
(a) ⊥  (b) T  (c) ⊢  (d) Different

**Answer:** (a) ⊥
**Solution:** T top-bottom flipped = ⊥.
**Shortcut:** Vertical flip.
**Common Mistake:** Mirror instead of water.
**Time target:** 5 sec

## Q4. Classification (Easy)
Odd one out: ◯, ◯, ◯, ▢
(a) 1st  (b) 2nd  (c) 3rd  (d) 4th

**Answer:** (d) 4th
**Solution:** Square is different from circles.
**Shortcut:** Identify common property.
**Common Mistake:** Random.
**Time target:** 10 sec

## Q5. Cube Vertices (Easy)
How many vertices does a cube have?
(a) 4  (b) 6  (c) 8  (d) 12

**Answer:** (c) 8
**Solution:** 8 vertices.
**Shortcut:** Cube has 6 faces, 8 vertices, 12 edges.
**Common Mistake:** Confusing with faces.
**Time target:** 5 sec

## Q6. Series — Gradual Fill (Medium)
Series: ○, ◐, ◑, ●, ?
(a) ○  (b) ◐  (c) ◑  (d) Different

**Answer:** (d) Different (fully filled)
**Solution:** Pattern of increasing fill. Next is fully filled.
**Shortcut:** Identify the rule.
**Common Mistake:** Cycling.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q7. Analogy — Triangle (Medium)
Analogy: △ : ◁ :: ▽ : ?
(a) ▷  (b) ◁  (c) △  (d) ◀

**Answer:** (a) ▷
**Solution:** Rotated/inverted.
**Shortcut:** Match transformation.
**Common Mistake:** Random.
**Time target:** 20 sec

## Q8. Mirror Arrow (Medium)
Mirror of arrow ↖ (up-left):
(a) ↖  (b) ↗  (c) ↙  (d) ↘

**Answer:** (b) ↗
**Solution:** Mirror reverses left-right. Up-left → up-right.
**Shortcut:** Left-right flip.
**Common Mistake):** Top-bottom flip.
**Time target:** 15 sec

---

## Q9. Triangles in subdivided triangle (Medium)
How many triangles in a triangle divided into 4 smaller ones (by 3 medians)?
(a) 6  (b) 7  (c) 8  (d) 13

**Answer:** (c) 8
**Solution:** 4 small + 1 large + 3 inverted = 8.
**Shortcut:** Count systematically.
**Common Mistake:** Missing inverted.
**Time target:** 60 sec

---

## Q10. Paper Folding (Medium)
A paper folded once and 2 circles cut. Unfolded:
(a) 2  (b) 4  (c) 6  (d) 8

**Answer:** (b) 4
**Solution:** $2 \times 2^1 = 4$.
**Shortcut:** Holes × 2^folds.
**Common Mistake:** Other exponent.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q11. Squares 4×4 (Medium)
Squares in a 4×4 grid:
(a) 16  (b) 30  (c) 36  (d) 50

**Answer:** (b) 30
**Solution:** $4 \times 5 \times 9/6 = 30$.
**Shortcut:** $n(n+1)(2n+1)/6$.
**Common Mistake:** Just $n^2$.
**Time target:** 60 sec

---

## Q12. Mirror "CDE" (Medium)
Mirror of "CDE":
(a) EDC  (b) CDE  (c) DEC  (d) EDĆ

**Answer:** (a) EDC
**Solution:** Each letter mirrored, reversed.
**Shortcut:** Reverse order with each letter flipped.
**Common Mistake):** Just reverse.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q13. Die bottom (Hard)
A die: 1 on top, 2 on front, 3 on right. Bottom?
(a) 4  (b) 5  (c) 6  (d) Cannot determine

**Answer:** (c) 6
**Solution:** 1 + 6 = 7. Top 1 → bottom 6.
**Shortcut:** Sum to 7.
**Common Mistake):** Other pair.
**Time target:** 15 sec

---

## Q14. Rectangles 3×3 (Hard)
Rectangles in a 3×3 grid:
(a) 9  (b) 18  (c) 27  (d) 36

**Answer:** (d) 36
**Solution:** $\binom{4}{2}^2 = 6^2 = 36$.
**Shortcut:** $C(m+1, 2) \times C(n+1, 2)$.
**Common Mistake):** Just 9.
**Time target:** 60 sec

---

## Q15. Paper Fold 3 times (Hard)
Paper folded 3 times, 1 hole cut. Unfolded:
(a) 1  (b) 2  (c) 4  (d) 8

**Answer:** (d) 8
**Solution:** $1 \times 2^3 = 8$.
**Shortcut:** Holes × 2^folds.
**Common Mistake):** Other exponent.
**Time target:** 30 sec
