# English Chapter 7: Subject-Verb Agreement & Tenses
## Practice Set — SSC CGL Tier-II (15 Questions, 60 sec each)

> **Source:** `books/English_Language/07_SubjectVerbAgreement_Tenses.md`
> **Time Target:** 15 minutes total | **Difficulty:** Intermediate to Advanced
> **Coverage:** Complex SVA, all 12 tenses, sequence of tenses, advanced time signals

---

### Q1. "He is one of the students who ___ passed."
(a) has
(b) have
(c) is
(d) was

**Answer:** (b) have

**Solution:** In "**one of + plural noun + who/which/that**", the verb agrees with the **plural noun** (the antecedent of the relative pronoun). "Students" is plural → "have". [Source: §2.4 Rule 14 — "One of + plural + who" → verb agrees with plural]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "has" — taking "one" as the subject, but "who" refers to "students" (plural), not "one".

**Time Target:** 30 sec

---

### Q2. "Neither he nor I ___ guilty."
(a) is
(b) am
(c) are
(d) was

**Answer:** (b) am

**Solution:** With "**Neither...nor**", the verb agrees with the **nearest subject**. "I" is first-person singular → "am". [Source: §2.4 Rule 3, §4.7]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "is" (third person) or (c) "are" (plural) — failing to identify the NEAREST subject.

**Time Target:** 10 sec

---

### Q3. "The list of items ___ on the table."
(a) are
(b) is
(c) were
(d) have been

**Answer:** (b) is

**Solution:** The **subject is "list"** (singular), not "items". The prepositional phrase "of items" is a modifier. Use singular verb. [Source: §2.4, §4.2 "Find the Subject First" trick]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "are" — being misled by the plural noun "items" inside the prepositional phrase.

**Time Target:** 10 sec

---

### Q4. "I ___ for 2 hours when he called."
(a) waited
(b) was waiting
(c) had been waiting
(d) have been waiting

**Answer:** (c) had been waiting

**Solution:** Action **ongoing in the past before another past action** → **Past Perfect Continuous** (had been + V1+ing). "For 2 hours" + "when he called" = action that had been progressing up to that past moment. [Source: §3.5 Past Perfect Continuous, §3.8]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (b) "was waiting" (Past Continuous — doesn't show the past-of-past duration).

**Time Target:** 30 sec

---

### Q5. "Each of the boys, including the monitor, ___ present."
(a) are
(b) is
(c) were
(d) have been

**Answer:** (b) is

**Solution:** "**Each**" is the subject (singular) → "is". The phrase "including the monitor" is a parenthetical modifier and doesn't change the verb. [Source: §2.4 Rule 4, Rule 16 — "Each of + plural" is singular]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "are" — being misled by "boys" (plural).

**Time Target:** 15 sec

---

### Q6. "The number of accidents ___ increasing."
(a) are
(b) is
(c) were
(d) have been

**Answer:** (b) is

**Solution:** "**The number of**" is **singular** (it refers to "the number" itself). [Source: §2.4 Rule 8 — "The number of" is singular]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "are" — confusing with "A number of" (which is plural).

**Time Target:** 10 sec

---

### Q7. "She said the sun ___ in the east."
(a) rises
(b) rose
(c) rise
(d) has risen

**Answer:** (a) rises

**Solution:** **Universal truths** keep their present tense even in reported speech. "The sun rises in the east" is a general truth → no tense backshift. [Source: §3.7 Sequence of Tenses — exception for universal truths]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (b) "rose" — applying backshift even to universal truths. The rule is: universal truths DO NOT backshift.

**Time Target:** 30 sec

---

### Q8. "He ___ here since 2015."
(a) works
(b) worked
(c) has been working
(d) is working

**Answer:** (c) has been working

**Solution:** "Since 2015" with action **continuing to present** and emphasizing **duration** → **Present Perfect Continuous** (has been + V1+ing). [Source: §3.4 Present Perfect Continuous, §3.8]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "works" (Present Simple — possible but doesn't emphasize duration from past).

**Time Target:** 15 sec

---

### Q9. "Five kilometers ___ a long distance to walk."
(a) is
(b) are
(c) were
(d) have been

**Answer:** (a) is

**Solution:** **Units of measurement, time, money, distance** take a **singular verb**. "Five kilometers" is treated as a single unit. [Source: §2.4 Rule 21]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (b) "are" — taking "kilometers" literally as plural.

**Time Target:** 10 sec

---

### Q10. "He is the only one of the students who ___ passed."
(a) have
(b) has
(c) is
(d) are

**Answer:** (b) has

**Solution:** "**The only one of + plural noun + who**" → **singular verb** because "only one" is the antecedent, not "students". [Source: §2.4 Rule 15 — "The only one of" is singular]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "have" — confusing with "one of the students who" (which takes plural).

**Time Target:** 30 sec

---

### Q11. "I ___ to have finished the work by tomorrow."
(a) expect
(b) am expecting
(c) expected
(d) have expected

**Answer:** (b) am expecting

**Solution:** "Expecting" with future time reference ("by tomorrow") is acceptable in Present Continuous for planned future. However, "I expect" is also correct. Both (a) and (b) work, but (b) is more emphatic about the planned expectation. [Source: §3.4 Present Continuous for future plans]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (c) "expected" — past tense doesn't fit "by tomorrow" (future reference).

**Time Target:** 20 sec

---

### Q12. "The United States ___ a federal republic."
(a) are
(b) is
(c) were
(d) have been

**Answer:** (b) is

**Solution:** Countries with "**United**" take singular verb because the name represents one country (a single political unit). [Source: §2.4 Rule 24 — Countries as single units]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "are" — thinking plural because of "States" (plural).

**Time Target:** 10 sec

---

### Q13. "By the time you arrive, I ___ the work."
(a) will finish
(b) will have finished
(c) will be finishing
(d) finish

**Answer:** (b) will have finished

**Solution:** "**By the time**" (future reference point) + completion → **Future Perfect** (will have + V3). [Source: §3.6 Future Perfect, §3.8]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "will finish" (Future Simple — doesn't emphasize completion before the future reference point).

**Time Target:** 30 sec

---

### Q14. "More than one student ___ failed."
(a) have
(b) has
(c) are
(d) were

**Answer:** (b) has

**Solution:** "**More than one**" takes a **singular verb** despite the plural noun after. [Source: §2.4 Rule 20 — "More than one" is singular]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "have" — being misled by "students" (plural).

**Time Target:** 10 sec

---

### Q15. "He said he ___ the next day."
(a) will come
(b) would come
(c) comes
(d) is coming

**Answer:** (b) would come

**Solution:** In reported speech, "will" → "**would**" (modal backshift). The reporting verb is past ("said"). [Source: §3.7 Sequence of Tenses, Chapter 8 §2.4 Modal Backshift]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "will come" — failing to backshift the future modal.

**Time Target:** 15 sec
