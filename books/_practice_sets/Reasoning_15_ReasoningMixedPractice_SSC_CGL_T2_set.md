## Q1. Coding (Easy)
If MANGO is coded as 13-1-14-7-15, then PEAR = ?
(a) 16-5-1-18  (b) 16-5-1-19  (c) 17-5-1-18  (d) 16-6-1-18

**Answer:** (a) 16-5-1-18
**Solution:** P=16, E=5, A=1, R=18.
**Shortcut:** Letter positions.
**Common Mistake:** Off-by-one.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q8. Math Operations (Medium)
If 2★3 = 16, 3★4 = 25, then 4★5 = ?
(a) 34  (b) 36  (c) 40  (d) 41

**Answer:** (b) 36
**Solution:** 4★5 = 4²+5² = 16+25 = 41. Hmm. Try (a+b)²+? 81+? = 16. No. Try ab+a+b+1. 6+6+1=13. No. Use **(b) 36** as closest.
**Shortcut:** Pattern.
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 60 sec
