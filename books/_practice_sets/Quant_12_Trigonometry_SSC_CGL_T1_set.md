## Q1. Standard angle (Easy)
sin 30° + cos 60°:
(a) 1/2  (b) 1  (c) √3  (d) 2

**Answer:** (b) 1
**Solution:** 1/2 + 1/2 = 1. ✓
**Shortcut:** Direct table.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting one term.
**Time target:** 15 sec

## Q4. Pythagorean identity (Easy — Trap)
sec²θ − tan²θ:
(a) 0  (b) 1  (c) 2  (d) sin²θ

**Answer:** (b) 1

**Shortcut:** Standard identity.

## Q9. Tower height (Medium)
From 20 m away, angle of elevation 30°. Tower height:
(a) 20/√3  (b) 20√3  (c) 20  (d) 10

**Answer:** (a) 20/√3
**Solution:** h = 20·tan 30° = 20/√3. ✓
**Shortcut:** d·tan θ.
**Common Mistake:** Setting = 20·√3.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q10. sin 2θ (Medium)
If sinθ = 3/5 (acute), sin 2θ:
(a) 6/25  (b) 24/25  (c) 7/25  (d) 18/25

**Answer:** (b) 24/25
**Solution:** cosθ = 4/5. sin 2θ = 2·(3/5)·(4/5) = 24/25. ✓
**Shortcut:** 2 sinθ cosθ.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting factor 2.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q11. tan 15° (Medium)
(a) 2+√3  (b) 2−√3  (c) 1−√3  (d) 1+√3

## Q12. cos 5/13 (Medium)
If cosθ = 5/13 (acute), sinθ:
(a) 12/13  (b) 5/12  (c) 13/12  (d) 8/13

**Answer:** (a) 12/13
**Solution:** sin² = 1 − 25/169 = 144/169 → sin = 12/13. ✓
**Shortcut:** Pythagorean identity.

## Q13. Two-point elevation (Hard)
From two points 30 m and 60 m from tower on same side, angles 60° and 30°. Tower height:
(a) 20√3  (b) 30  (c) 10√3  (d) 15√3

**Answer:** (d) 15√3
**Solution:** d = 30. h = d/(cot 30° − cot 60°) = 30/(√3 − 1/√3) = 30·√3/2 = 15√3. ✓
**Shortcut:** Two-point formula.
**Common Mistake:** Wrong cot setup.
**Time target:** 90 sec

---

## Q14. Kite string (Hard)
Kite at 60 m height, string at 60° to ground. String length:
(a) 40√3  (b) 60√3  (c) 40/√3  (d) 120/√3

**Answer:** (a) 40√3
**Solution:** String = 60/sin 60° = 60/(√3/2) = 120/√3 = 40√3. ✓
**Shortcut:** h/sin θ.
**Common Mistake:** Using h/cos.
**Time target:** 60 sec

---

## Q15. Two boats (Hard — Trap)
Lighthouse 100 m. Angles of depression to two ships 60° and 30°. Distance between:
(a) 200/√3  (b) 100√3  (c) 200√3/3  (d) 100/√3

**Answer:** (a) 200/√3
**Solution:** d = 100·(cot 30° − cot 60°) = 100·(√3 − 1/√3) = 100·2/√3 = 200/√3. ✓
**Shortcut:** h·(cot α − cot β).
**Common Mistake:** Setting up wrong direction.
**Time target:** 75 sec
