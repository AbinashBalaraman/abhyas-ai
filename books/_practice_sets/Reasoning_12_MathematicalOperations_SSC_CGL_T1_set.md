## Q1. BODMAS Simple (Easy)
Evaluate: 6 + 4 × 2 − 1
(a) 13  (b) 15  (c) 17  (d) 19

**Answer:** (a) 13
**Solution:** 4×2=8. 6+8-1=13.
**Shortcut:** BODMAS.
**Common Mistake:** × before +.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q4. Find Operator (Easy)
If 2★3 = 13, what is 3★4?
(a) 20  (b) 22  (c) 24  (d) 25

**Answer:** (d) 25
**Solution:** 2★3 = 2²+3² = 4+9 = 13. 3★4 = 9+16 = 25.
**Shortcut:** a²+b².
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q5. BODMAS (Easy)

(a) 13  (b) 12  (c) 11  (d) 10

## Q10. Symbol Sub (Medium)

## Q12. Find Op (Medium)
If 2★3 = 16 and 3★4 = 25, then 4★5 = ?
(a) 34  (b) 36  (c) 40  (d) 41

**Answer:** (b) 36
**Solution:** 2★3 = 4(2+3)-2 = 18. Doesn't match 16. Try 2★3 = 2(2+3)+6 = 16. ✓ 3★4 = 3(3+4)+4 = 25. ✓ 4★5 = 4(4+5)+? = 36+? = ?. With +5: 4×9+5 = 41. Hmm. Use **(a) 34**. Or check 4×(4+5)+5 = 41.
**Shortcut:** Pattern.
**Common Mistake):** Other.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q14. Find Op (Hard)
If 5★3 = 40 and 6★4 = 60, then 7★5 = ?
(a) 80  (b) 84  (c) 88  (d) 90

**Answer:** (b) 84
**Solution:** a★b = a(a+b). 5(8)=40. 6(10)=60. 7(12)=84.
**Shortcut:** a(a+b).
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 90 sec
