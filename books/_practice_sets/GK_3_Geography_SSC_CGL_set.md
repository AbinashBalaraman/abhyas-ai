### Q1. The highest peak in the world is:
(a) K2 (b) Kangchenjunga (c) Mount Everest (d) Lhotse

**Answer: (c) Mount Everest**

**Solution:** Mount Everest (8,848.86 m as per 2020 China-Nepal joint survey) is the highest peak in the world, located on the Nepal-Tibet border. It was named after Sir George Everest. Tenzing Norgay (Nepal) and Edmund Hillary (New Zealand) first summited it in 1953. Other notable peaks: K2 (8,611 m, Pakistan/China - second highest, "Killer Mountain"), Kangchenjunga (8,586 m, India/Nepal - third highest).

### Q2. The Tropic of Cancer does NOT pass through which Indian state?
(a) Gujarat (b) Rajasthan (c) Odisha (d) Tripura

**Answer: (c) Odisha**

**Solution:** The Tropic of Cancer (23.5°N) passes through 8 Indian states: Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Tripura, and Mizoram. It does NOT pass through Odisha. The Tropic of Cancer passes through the middle of India, dividing it into tropical and subtropical zones. The Tropic of Capricorn (23.5°S) passes through 10 countries including Brazil, Australia, South Africa.

### Q3. The longest river in the world is:
(a) Amazon (b) Nile (c) Yangtze (d) Mississippi

**Answer: (b) Nile**

**Solution:** The Nile (6,650 km) in Africa is the longest river in the world, originating from Lake Victoria and flowing into the Mediterranean Sea. The Amazon (6,400 km) in South America is the largest river by volume. The Yangtze (6,300 km) in China is the longest river in Asia. In India, the Ganga (2,525 km) is the longest river, originating from the Gangotri Glacier. The Brahmaputra (Tsangpo in Tibet) is 2,900 km if measured from its origin.

### Q4. The largest desert in the world (hot desert) is:
(a) Arabian (b) Gobi (c) Sahara (d) Kalahari

**Answer: (c) Sahara**

**Solution:** The Sahara (9.2 million km²) in North Africa is the largest hot desert in the world. The largest cold desert is the Antarctic (14.2 million km²). The Gobi (1.3 million km²) is a cold desert in Mongolia/China. The Arabian (2.3 million km²) is in the Arabian Peninsula. The Thar (Great Indian Desert) is about 200,000 km², mostly in Rajasthan. The Atacama (Chile) is the driest desert.

### Q5. The "Father of the Green Revolution" in India is:
(a) Norman Borlaug (b) M.S. Swaminathan (c) C. Subramaniam (d) Verghese Kurien

**Answer: (b) M.S. Swaminathan**

**Solution:** M.S. Swaminathan is called the "Father of the Green Revolution in India" for his role in introducing high-yielding varieties of wheat and rice. Norman Borlaug is the "Father of the Green Revolution" globally. Verghese Kurien is the "Father of the White Revolution" (milk - Operation Flood). The Green Revolution (1966-70s) made India self-sufficient in food grains. Sam Pitroda is associated with the Yellow Revolution (oilseeds).

### Q6. India's first biosphere reserve is:
(a) Sundarbans (b) Nilgiri (c) Nanda Devi (d) Gulf of Mannar

**Answer: (b) Nilgiri**

**Solution:** The Nilgiri Biosphere Reserve (Tamil Nadu/Kerala/Karnataka) was the first to be designated in 1986. Other major biosphere reserves: Sundarbans (largest mangrove), Gulf of Mannar (TN), Pachmarhi (MP), Nanda Devi (Uttarakhand), Manas (Assam). India has 18 biosphere reserves as of 2025-26, including the newly designated Cold Desert (Ladakh) in 2025. There are 13 UNESCO Biosphere Reserves in India as of 2025.

### Q7. Project Tiger was launched in:
(a) 1971 (b) 1973 (c) 1975 (d) 1980

**Answer: (b) 1973**

**Solution:** Project Tiger was launched on 1 April 1973 by PM Indira Gandhi. India now has 56+ tiger reserves (as of 2024). The first national park of India was Jim Corbett (1936 as Hailey NP; renamed 1957). Major tiger reserves: Jim Corbett (Uttarakhand), Ranthambore (Rajasthan), Kanha (MP - inspiration for Kipling's Jungle Book), Bandhavgarh (MP - highest density), Sundarbans (WB - Royal Bengal Tiger), Kaziranga (Assam - one-horned rhinoceros).

### Q8. Indian Standard Time (IST) is based on:
(a) 80°E longitude (b) 82.5°E longitude (c) 85°E longitude (d) 90°E longitude

**Answer: (b) 82.5°E longitude**

**Solution:** Indian Standard Time (IST) is based on 82.5°E longitude, which passes through Mirzapur (UP). IST is UTC+5:30 (5 hours 30 minutes ahead of GMT/UTC). 1° longitude = 4 minutes time difference. The Prime Meridian (0°) passes through Greenwich (UK). The International Date Line (~180° longitude) — crossing it changes the date by 1 day. India has only one time zone (IST) despite spanning about 30° of longitude.

**Time Target:** 30s | **Chapter Reference:** GK/03_Geography.md, p.103-104, p.527 | **VERIFY:** No

### Q9. The largest state (by area) in India is:
(a) Madhya Pradesh (b) Maharashtra (c) Rajasthan (d) Uttar Pradesh

**Answer: (c) Rajasthan**

**Solution:** Rajasthan is the largest state in India by area (342,239 km²). The smallest state is Goa (3,702 km²). The most populous state is Uttar Pradesh (~230 million). The most populous UT is Delhi. India's largest UT (by area) is Ladakh (after 2019 reorganization). India has 28 states and 8 UTs (as of 2024-25). The Tropic of Cancer passes through 8 states. The largest country in the world is Russia; the most populous is India (since April 2023, overtaking China).

### Q10. The Sundarbans is the world's largest:
(a) Desert (b) Delta (c) Lake (d) Mangrove forest

**Answer: (b) Delta**

**Solution:** The Sundarbans (Ganges-Brahmaputra delta) is the world's largest delta and the largest tidal halophytic mangrove forest. It is shared between India and Bangladesh, and is home to the Royal Bengal Tiger. The Sundarbans is a UNESCO World Heritage Site (1987). It is located in West Bengal (India) and Khulna Division (Bangladesh). The delta covers about 10,000 km². The Sundari tree gives the delta its name. Bhitarkanika (Odisha) is another major mangrove in India.

**Time Target:** 45s | **Chapter Reference:** GK/03_Geography.md, p.251, p.529, p.556 | **VERIFY:** No

### Q11. The longest river in India is:
(a) Godavari (b) Brahmaputra (c) Ganga (d) Narmada

**Answer: (c) Ganga**

**Solution:** The Ganga (2,525 km) is the longest river in India, originating from the Gangotri Glacier. It forms at Devprayag where Bhagirathi meets Alaknanda. The Brahmaputra (2,900 km total) is longer if measured from its source in Tibet, but only ~916 km flows through India. The Godavari (1,465 km) is the longest peninsular river and is called "Dakshin Ganga". The Krishna (1,400 km) is the second-longest peninsular river. The Narmada (1,312 km) is the "Lifeline of Madhya Pradesh" and flows in a rift valley.

### Q12. Kaziranga National Park is famous for:
(a) Asiatic Lion (b) One-horned Rhinoceros (c) Royal Bengal Tiger (d) Snow Leopard

**Answer: (b) One-horned Rhinoceros**

**Solution:** Kaziranga National Park (Assam) is famous for the Indian one-horned rhinoceros. It is a UNESCO World Heritage Site (1985). The Asiatic Lion is found in Gir National Park (Gujarat). The Royal Bengal Tiger is found in many reserves including Sundarbans, Jim Corbett, Ranthambore. The Snow Leopard is found in the Himalayas (Ladakh, Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand). Project Rhino in India is similar to Project Tiger.

### Q13. The deepest ocean trench is:
(a) Tonga Trench (b) Mariana Trench (c) Java Trench (d) Puerto Rico Trench

**Answer: (b) Mariana Trench**

**Solution:** The Mariana Trench in the Pacific Ocean is the deepest point in the world (Challenger Deep, ~11,034 m or 36,070 feet). The Tonga Trench is the second deepest. The Java Trench is in the Indian Ocean. The Puerto Rico Trench is in the Atlantic Ocean. The Pacific Ocean is the largest and deepest ocean, covering ~46% of the global ocean area. The Mariana Trench was first explored by the Trieste bathyscaphe in 1960 (Walsh and Piccard).

**Time Target:** 30s | **Chapter Reference:** GK/03_Geography.md, p.92, p.539 | **VERIFY:** No

### Q14. World's highest waterfall is:
(a) Niagara Falls (b) Angel Falls (c) Victoria Falls (d) Tugela Falls

**Answer: (b) Angel Falls**

**Solution:** Angel Falls (979 m, Venezuela) is the world's highest waterfall, located in Canaima National Park. It is named after Jimmie Angel, a US aviator. Niagara Falls (USA/Canada) is the largest by volume. Victoria Falls (Zambia/Zimbabwe) is one of the largest by combined width and height. In India, Kunchikal Falls (Karnataka, 455 m) is the highest. Dudhsagar Falls (310 m) is in Goa. Jog Falls (253 m) is in Karnataka.

### Q15. The "Dakshin Ganga" is the name of which Indian river?
(a) Krishna (b) Kaveri (c) Godavari (d) Narmada

**Answer: (c) Godavari**

**Solution:** The Godavari (1,465 km) is called "Dakshin Ganga" (Ganga of the South) because of its length and significance. It originates from Trimbakeshwar (Nashik, Maharashtra) and is the longest peninsular river. The Kaveri (~800 km) is called the "Ganga of the South" by some. The Krishna (1,400 km) is the second-longest peninsular river. The Narmada (1,312 km) is the "Lifeline of Madhya Pradesh" and flows west into the Arabian Sea in a rift valley.
