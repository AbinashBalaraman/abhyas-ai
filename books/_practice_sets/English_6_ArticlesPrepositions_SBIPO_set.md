### Q1. "I need ___ one-rupee coin."
(a) a
(b) an
(c) the
(d) no article

**Answer:** (a) a

**Solution:** "**One**-rupee" is pronounced with the **/w/ sound** ("wun"). Use "a" before consonant sounds. [Source: §2.4 — "a one-rupee note"]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (b) "an" because "one" starts with "o" (vowel letter). The sound is "w" (consonant).

**Time Target:** 3 sec

---

### Q2. "He is married ___ her for 10 years."
(a) with
(b) to
(c) of
(d) from

**Answer:** (b) to

**Solution:** "**Married to**" is the correct collocation. [Source: §3.6 — verb + preposition patterns]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "with" — "married with" is non-standard; standard is "married to".

**Time Target:** 3 sec

---

### Q3. "I will go ___ Monday."
(a) in
(b) on
(c) at
(d) by

**Answer:** (b) on

**Solution:** Days of the week use "**on**" (on Monday, on Friday). [Source: §3.3 — In/On/At (Time)]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "in" — "in" is for months/years/seasons.

**Time Target:** 3 sec

---

### Q4. "___ Mount Everest is the highest peak."
(a) A
(b) An
(c) The
(d) No article

**Answer:** (d) No article

**Solution:** **Mountains as proper nouns** typically take **no article** — "Mount Everest" (not "the Mount Everest"). However, mountain ranges take "the" (the Himalayas, the Alps). [Source: §2.7 — Proper nouns; §2.6 — Geographical features for ranges]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (c) "the" — confusing single mountains with mountain ranges.

**Time Target:** 5 sec

---

### Q5. "You ___ not smoke here."
(a) can
(b) may
(c) must
(d) shall

**Answer:** (c) must

**Solution:** "**Must not**" expresses strong prohibition. "Cannot/may not" express weaker prohibition. "Must" is for strict rules. [Source: §5.4 — Must: Strong obligation]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "can" (ability/possibility), (b) "may" (permission).

**Time Target:** 5 sec

---

### Q6. "He is ___ expert in finance."
(a) a
(b) an
(c) the
(d) no article

**Answer:** (b) an

**Solution:** "Expert" starts with a **vowel sound /ɛ/**. Use "an" before vowel sounds. [Source: §2.3 Sound Rule]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "a" because of "e" being a vowel letter — but the test is the sound, and "expert" starts with a vowel sound.

**Time Target:** 3 sec

---

### Q7. "She died ___ cancer."
(a) with
(b) of
(c) from
(d) for

**Answer:** (b) of

**Solution:** "**Die of**" is used for diseases/medical causes. [Source: §3.6 — Verb + Preposition]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (c) "from" — "die from" is for external causes (wounds, accidents); "die of" is for diseases.

**Time Target:** 5 sec

---

### Q8. "We discussed ___ the matter."
(a) about
(b) on
(c) (no preposition)
(d) of

**Answer:** (c) (no preposition)

**Solution:** "**Discuss**" is a **transitive verb** that takes a direct object. It does NOT take a preposition. "Discuss the matter" (NOT "discuss about the matter"). [Source: §3.5 — Common Preposition Errors]

**Common Mistake:** Adding "about" — "discuss about" is a very common Indian English error.

**Time Target:** 5 sec

---

### Q9. "I am fond ___ coffee."
(a) at
(b) in
(c) of
(d) with

**Answer:** (c) of

**Solution:** "Fond **of**" is the correct collocation. [Source: §3.6 — "fond of"]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "at" (incorrect), (b) "in" (incorrect).

**Time Target:** 3 sec

---

### Q10. "___ weather is pleasant today."
(a) A
(b) An
(c) The
(d) No article

**Answer:** (c) The

**Solution:** "Weather" here is **specific** (today's weather, identified by context). "The" is used for specific reference. [Source: §2.6 — Specific noun already identified]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (d) "no article" — uncountable nouns in general take no article, but specific instances use "the".

**Time Target:** 5 sec
