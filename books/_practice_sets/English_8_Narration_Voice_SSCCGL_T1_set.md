### Q1. He said, "I am tired." — Indirect form:
(a) He said that he is tired
(b) He said that he was tired
(c) He said that he has been tired
(d) He said that he be tired

**Answer:** (b) He said that he was tired

**Solution:** Reporting verb "said" is past → tense backshift: **Present Simple "am" → Past Simple "was"**. Pronoun "I" → "he" (1st person to 3rd person of reporter). [Source: §2.1 Basic Conversion, §2.3 Tense Backshift]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "is tired" — failing to apply tense backshift.

**Time Target:** 15 sec

---

### Q2. She said, "I will call you tomorrow." — Indirect:
(a) She said that she will call me tomorrow
(b) She said that she would call me the next day
(c) She said that she would call me tomorrow
(d) She said that she called me the next day

**Answer:** (b) She said that she would call me the next day

**Solution:** **Three changes needed:**
1. **Tense**: "will" → "would" (modal backshift)
2. **Pronoun**: "I/you" → "she/me" (1st/2nd person shift)
3. **Time**: "tomorrow" → "the next day" (time word shift)

[Source: §2.3, §2.4 Modal Backshift, §2.5 Other Word Changes]

**Common Mistake:** Keeping "tomorrow" unchanged (option c) — time words must shift in indirect speech.

**Time Target:** 20 sec

### Q3. Active: "He wrote a letter." — Passive:
(a) A letter is written by him
(b) A letter was written by him
(c) A letter has been written by him
(d) A letter was being written by him

**Answer:** (b) A letter was written by him

**Solution:** **Past Simple** (wrote) → **Past Simple Passive** (was + V3). Object "a letter" becomes subject. [Source: §3.3 Tense-Based Voice Conversion]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "is written" (Present Passive — wrong tense).

**Time Target:** 15 sec

---

### Q4. Active: "She is reading a book." — Passive:
(a) A book is being read by her
(b) A book is read by her
(c) A book was being read by her
(d) A book has been read by her

**Answer:** (a) A book is being read by her

**Solution:** **Present Continuous** (is reading) → **Present Continuous Passive** (is being + V3). [Source: §3.3]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (b) "is read" (loses the continuous aspect).

**Time Target:** 15 sec

### Q5. He said, "I have finished the work." — Indirect:
(a) He said that he has finished the work
(b) He said that he had finished the work
(c) He said that he finished the work
(d) He said that he was finishing the work

**Answer:** (b) He said that he had finished the work

**Solution:** **Present Perfect** (have finished) → **Past Perfect** (had finished). Tense backshift. [Source: §2.3 Tense Backshift Table]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "has finished" — failing to backshift Present Perfect.

**Time Target:** 15 sec

### Q6. He said to me, "Are you coming?" — Indirect:
(a) He asked me if I am coming
(b) He asked me if I was coming
(c) He told me if I was coming
(d) He said to me if I was coming

**Answer:** (b) He asked me if I was coming

**Solution:** **Interrogative conversion rules:**
- Reporting verb "said to" → "**asked**" (for questions)
- Question mark removed; use "**if/whether**"
- Reverse order: "Are you coming?" → "if I was coming"
- Tense backshift: "are" → "was"

[Source: §2.6 Question Conversion]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (c) "told" — "told" is for statements, not questions; use "asked/enquired".

**Time Target:** 20 sec

### Q7. Active: "The teacher praised the student." — Passive:
(a) The student is praised by the teacher
(b) The student was praised by the teacher
(c) The student has been praised by the teacher
(d) The student was being praised by the teacher

**Answer:** (b) The student was praised by the teacher

**Solution:** **Past Simple** (praised) → **Past Simple Passive** (was + V3). [Source: §3.3]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "is praised" (Present Passive — wrong tense).

**Time Target:** 15 sec

### Q8. He said, "I went to the market yesterday." — Indirect:
(a) He said that he went to the market yesterday
(b) He said that he had gone to the market the day before
(c) He said that he has gone to the market the day before
(d) He said that he had gone to the market yesterday

**Answer:** (b) He said that he had gone to the market the day before

**Solution:** **Three changes:**
1. "went" (Past Simple) → "**had gone**" (Past Perfect)
2. "yesterday" → "**the day before**"
3. "I" → "he"

[Source: §2.3, §2.5]

**Common Mistake:** Keeping "yesterday" unchanged (option d) — time words shift in indirect speech.

**Time Target:** 30 sec

### Q9. Active: "They are building a new house." — Passive:
(a) A new house is built by them
(b) A new house is being built by them
(c) A new house was being built by them
(d) A new house has been built by them

**Answer:** (b) A new house is being built by them

**Solution:** **Present Continuous** (are building) → **Present Continuous Passive** (is being + V3). "A new house" (object) becomes subject. [Source: §3.3]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "is built" (loses continuous aspect).

**Time Target:** 15 sec

### Q10. He said, "Bring me a glass of water." — Indirect:
(a) He ordered me to bring him a glass of water
(b) He said to bring me a glass of water
(c) He ordered me for bringing him a glass of water
(d) He ordered that I bring him a glass of water

**Answer:** (a) He ordered me to bring him a glass of water

**Solution:** **Imperative conversion:**
- Reporting verb "said" → "**ordered**" (for commands)
- "Bring" → "**to bring**" (use "to" + V1 after ordered/asked/requested)
- Pronoun: "me" → "him" (object of reported verb)

[Source: §2.7 Commands Conversion]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (c) "ordered for" — "ordered" doesn't take "for" here; use "to + V1".

**Time Target:** 20 sec

---

### Q11. Active: "He can solve this puzzle." — Passive:
(a) This puzzle can solved by him
(b) This puzzle can be solved by him
(c) This puzzle is solved by him
(d) This puzzle can be solving by him

**Answer:** (b) This puzzle can be solved by him

**Solution:** **Modal + passive** = "**can/may/must/should + be + V3**". The modal stays before "be". [Source: §3.5 Voice with Modals]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "can solved" — missing "be" between modal and V3.

**Time Target:** 15 sec

---

### Q12. She said, "What a beautiful scene!" — Indirect:
(a) She exclaimed that what a beautiful scene
(b) She exclaimed that it was a very beautiful scene
(c) She said that it is a very beautiful scene
(d) She exclaimed a beautiful scene

**Answer:** (b) She exclaimed that it was a very beautiful scene

**Solution:** **Exclamation conversion rules:**
- Reporting verb "said" → "**exclaimed**"
- "What a...!" → "**it was a very**..." (use "very" + backshift)
- Exclamation mark removed

[Source: §2.7 Exclamation Conversion]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (c) "said" — "exclaimed" is the correct verb for exclamations.

**Time Target:** 25 sec

### Q13. Active: "The teacher has taught the students." — Passive:
(a) The students have been taught by the teacher
(b) The students are taught by the teacher
(c) The students were taught by the teacher
(d) The students are being taught by the teacher

**Answer:** (a) The students have been taught by the teacher

**Solution:** **Present Perfect** (has taught) → **Present Perfect Passive** (have been + V3). [Source: §3.3]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (b) "are taught" (loses the perfect aspect).

**Time Target:** 15 sec

### Q14. He said to me, "Please help me." — Indirect:
(a) He ordered me to help him
(b) He requested me to help him
(c) He told me to help him
(d) Both (b) and (c) are correct

**Answer:** (d) Both (b) and (c) are correct

**Solution:** "**Please**" indicates a **request**. Reporting verb options: "**requested**" (formal) or "**asked/told**" (less formal). All are acceptable. [Source: §2.7 Requests Conversion]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing only (a) "ordered" — "ordered" is for commands, not polite requests.

**Time Target:** 25 sec

### Q15. Active: "They will complete the project." — Passive:
(a) The project is completed by them
(b) The project will complete by them
(c) The project will be completed by them
(d) The project will have been completed by them

**Answer:** (c) The project will be completed by them

**Solution:** **Future Simple** (will complete) → **Future Simple Passive** (will be + V3). [Source: §3.3]

**Common Mistake:** Choosing (a) "is completed" (Present Passive — wrong tense).

**Time Target:** 15 sec
