## Q1. Mirror 5:20 (Easy)
Mirror of 5:20?
(a) 6:40  (b) 7:40  (c) 4:40  (d) 8:40

**Answer:** (a) 6:40
**Solution:** 11:60 − 5:20 = 6:40.
**Shortcut:** 11:60.
**Common Mistake:** Borrow.
**Time target:** 15 sec

## Q5. Clock 9:00 (Medium)
Angle at 9:00?
(a) 90°  (b) 180°  (c) 270°  (d) 0°

**Answer:** (a) 90°
**Solution:** 30×9 = 270 or 90° (smaller).
**Shortcut:** 30H gives the angle.
**Common Mistake:** Using 270 directly.
**Time target:** 15 sec

## Q7. Painted 3×3 2-face (Medium)

## Q8. Calendar Leap Rule (Medium)
Which is NOT a leap year?
(a) 2000  (b) 2004  (c) 1900  (d) 2024

**Answer:** (c) 1900
**Solution:** Div by 100 not 400 → not leap.
**Shortcut:** Century years not leap unless div by 400.
**Common Mistake):** All div by 4 are leap.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q9. Calendar 1 Jan 2025 (Medium)
1 Jan 2025 is:
(a) Monday  (b) Tuesday  (c) Wednesday  (d) Sunday

**Answer:** (c) Wednesday
**Solution:** 1 Jan 2024 = Monday. 1 Jan 2025 = Monday + 366 mod 7 = Monday + 2 = Wednesday.
**Shortcut:** +2 for leap year.
**Common Mistake):** +1.
**Time target:** 45 sec

---

## Q10. Clock 4:20 (Medium)
Angle at 4:20?
(a) 0°  (b) 10°  (c) 20°  (d) 30°

**Answer:** (b) 10°
**Solution:** 30×4 − 5.5×20 = 120 − 110 = 10°.
**Shortcut:** |30H - 5.5M|.
**Common Mistake):** Other.
**Time target:** 30 sec
