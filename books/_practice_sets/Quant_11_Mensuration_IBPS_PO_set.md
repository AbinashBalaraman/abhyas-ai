# IBPS PO Prelims — Mensuration (2D & 3D)

> **Exam:** IBPS PO Prelims | **Time:** 8 min (45 sec/Q) | **Total:** 10 Qs
> **Difficulty:** 3 Easy, 5 Medium, 2 Hard | **Trap Qs:** 1
> **Source basis:** Ch. 11 — banking context mensuration

---

## Q1. Square (Easy)
Square side 9. Area:
(a) 18  (b) 36  (c) 81  (d) 90

**Answer:** (c) 81
**Solution:** 9² = 81. ✓
**Shortcut:** a².
**Common Mistake:** 2a.
**Time target:** 15 sec

---

## Q2. Cube surface (Easy)
Cube side 4. TSA:
(a) 16  (b) 64  (c) 96  (d) 24

**Answer:** (c) 96
**Solution:** 6a² = 6·16 = 96. ✓
**Shortcut:** 6a².
**Common Mistake:** Using 4a² (LSA).
**Time target:** 15 sec

---

## Q3. Triangle (Easy — Trap)
Right triangle legs 5, 12. Area:
(a) 30  (b) 60  (c) 17  (d) 34

**Answer:** (a) 30
**Solution:** ½·5·12 = 30. ✓
**Shortcut:** ½·b·h.
**Common Mistake:** 5·12.
**Time target:** 15 sec

---

## Q4. Cylinder volume (Medium)
Cylinder r=7, h=5. Volume:
(a) 245π  (b) 220π  (c) 175π  (d) 70π

**Answer:** (a) 245π
**Solution:** V = π·49·5 = 245π. ✓
**Shortcut:** πr²h.
**Common Mistake:** CSA formula.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q5. Cone volume (Medium)
Cone r=3, h=4. Volume:
(a) 12π  (b) 36π  (c) 24π  (d) 48π

**Answer:** (a) 12π
**Solution:** V = (1/3)π·9·4 = 12π. ✓
**Shortcut:** (1/3)πr²h.
**Common Mistake:** Using cylinder formula.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q6. Sphere TSA (Medium)
Sphere r=10. TSA:
(a) 100π  (b) 400π  (c) 1000π/3  (d) 200π

**Answer:** (b) 400π
**Solution:** 4πr² = 4π·100 = 400π. ✓
**Shortcut:** 4πr².
**Common Mistake:** Using volume.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q7. Cuboid volume (Medium)
Cuboid 8×5×3. Volume:
(a) 120  (b) 16  (c) 240  (d) 60

**Answer:** (a) 120
**Solution:** 8·5·3 = 120. ✓
**Shortcut:** lbh.
**Common Mistake:** Wrong multiplication.
**Time target:** 15 sec

---

## Q8. Hemisphere (Medium)
Hemisphere r=6. CSA:
(a) 36π  (b) 72π  (c) 108π  (d) 144π

**Answer:** (b) 72π
**Solution:** CSA = 2πr² = 2π·36 = 72π. ✓
**Shortcut:** 2πr².
**Common Mistake:** Using 3πr² (TSA).
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q9. Sector area (Hard)
Sector radius 7, angle 90°. Area:
(a) 38.5  (b) 49  (c) 77  (d) 154

**Answer:** (a) 38.5
**Solution:** A = (90/360)·π·49 = (¼)·(22/7)·49 = 38.5. ✓
**Shortcut:** (θ/360)·πr².
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting fraction.
**Time target:** 45 sec

---

## Q10. Cone slant height (Hard — Trap)
Cone r=5, h=12. Slant height:
(a) 7  (b) 13  (c) 17  (d) √119

**Answer:** (b) 13
**Solution:** l = √(25+144) = √169 = 13. ✓
**Shortcut:** √(r²+h²).
**Common Mistake:** Adding r+h.
**Time target:** 30 sec
