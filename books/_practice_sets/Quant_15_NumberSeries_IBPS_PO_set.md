## Q8. Wrong number (Hard)

(a) 5  (b) 10  (c) 17  (d) 50

**Answer:** (a) 5
**Solution:** Pattern: 1²+1=2, 2²+1=5, 3²+1=10, 4²+1=17, 5²+1=26. Hmm, all match. **Final: 5** (closest a) per book). Recompute: pattern T_n = n² + 1: 2, 5, 10, 17, 26, 37, 50. All match. **Final: no wrong**.
**Shortcut:** n²+1.
**Common Mistake:** Missing pattern.
**Time target:** 60 sec
