### Q1. UPI was launched on:
(a) 22 November 2010 (b) 11 April 2016 (c) 30 December 2016 (d) 1 December 2022

**Answer: (b) 11 April 2016**

**Solution:** UPI (Unified Payments Interface) was launched on 11 April 2016 by NPCI. It was inaugurated by Dr. Raghuram G. Rajan (then RBI Governor) in Mumbai with 21 member banks. UPI operates on IMPS infrastructure. UPI enables real-time inter-bank transactions through mobile phones. UPI is regulated by RBI. Per transaction limit: ₹1 lakh (general), ₹2 lakh (select merchants), ₹5 lakh (capital markets). As of August 2025, UPI handles ~640 million transactions daily, about 50% of world's digital transactions by volume.

### Q2. UPI is operated by:
(a) RBI (b) SEBI (c) NPCI (d) SIDBI

**Answer: (c) NPCI**

**Solution:** UPI is operated by NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India). NPCI was established in 2008 under Section 8 of the Companies Act. NPCI is the umbrella organization for retail payments in India and operates UPI, IMPS, NACH, AePS, RuPay, CTS, and e-RUPI. NPCI is headquartered in Mumbai. It is promoted by 10 banks (each holding 10% stake). NPCI plays a key role in India's digital payment revolution. NPCI is regulated by RBI under the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007.

### Q3. IMPS was launched in:
(a) 2004 (b) 2008 (c) 2010 (d) 2016

**Answer: (c) 2010**

**Solution:** IMPS (Immediate Payment Service) was launched on 22 November 2010. It is a real-time, 24x7, inter-bank fund transfer service operated by NPCI. IMPS uses mobile number + MMID or account number + IFSC for transactions. The transaction limit is up to ₹5 lakh per transaction (with additional factor authentication). IMPS is available through mobile banking, internet banking, and SMS. IMPS is the underlying infrastructure for UPI. UPI essentially extends IMPS by adding virtual payment addresses (VPAs).

### Q4. The minimum amount for RTGS is:
(a) ₹50,000 (b) ₹1 lakh (c) ₹2 lakh (d) ₹5 lakh

**Answer: (c) ₹2 lakh**

**Solution:** The minimum amount for RTGS (Real Time Gross Settlement) is ₹2 lakh. There is no maximum amount. RTGS was launched in 2004 and is operated by RBI (not NPCI). It is real-time, gross settlement (one transaction at a time, not batched). RTGS charges were made NIL since July 2019. RTGS is available 24x7 since 2019 (originally 9 AM to 4:30 PM on working days). It is used for large-value transactions like property purchase, large business payments, and securities settlement. NEFT, in contrast, has no minimum amount and uses batched settlement.

### Q5. CBDC retail pilot started on:
(a) 1 November 2022 (b) 1 December 2022 (c) 1 April 2023 (d) 1 January 2023

**Answer: (b) 1 December 2022**

**Solution:** The CBDC (Central Bank Digital Currency) retail pilot (e₹-R) started on 1 December 2022. The wholesale pilot (e₹-W) started on 1 November 2022. CBDC is the digital form of fiat currency issued by RBI (called "Digital Rupee" or "e₹"). CBDC is 100% backed by government securities, has no interest (like cash), and is legal tender under Section 26 of the RBI Act. India follows a two-tier model: RBI issues to banks, banks distribute to public. CBDC can be transacted via UPI QR codes.

### Q6. RuPay was launched in:
(a) 2008 (b) 2010 (c) 2012 (d) 2014

**Answer: (c) 2012**

**Solution:** RuPay is India's domestic card payment network launched in 2012 by NPCI. It was created to reduce dependence on Visa and Mastercard. RuPay cards are used in PMJDY accounts, ATMs, POS, and e-commerce. RuPay has three variants: RuPay Classic, RuPay Platinum, and RuPay Select. RuPay is accepted internationally through partnerships (Discover, JCB, etc.). It has lower transaction fees than international networks. India has 5 card networks: Visa, Mastercard, RuPay (NPCI), American Express (Amex), and Diners Club.

### Q7. The BHIM app was launched by:
(a) RBI (b) NPCI (c) SBI (d) Government of India

**Answer: (b) NPCI**

**Solution:** BHIM (Bharat Interface for Money) was launched on 30 December 2016 by PM Modi, and developed by NPCI. It is based on UPI. BHIM allows users to send/receive money, scan QR codes, and check balance. It supports multiple bank accounts. The app is available in multiple languages. BHIM 2.0 was launched in 2018 with additional features. BHIM 3.0 was released in 2021 with new features. NPCI is the developer while the Government of India launched it.

### Q8. e-RUPI was launched on:
(a) 11 April 2016 (b) 30 December 2016 (c) 2 August 2021 (d) 1 December 2022

**Answer: (c) 2 August 2021**

**Solution:** e-RUPI was launched on 2 August 2021. It is an e-Voucher based digital payment system developed by NPCI. e-RUPI uses QR code or SMS string-based vouchers. It is targeted at healthcare, scholarships, and CSR activities. e-RUPI ensures leak-proof delivery of welfare services (no middleman). The user receives a voucher via SMS/QR which can be redeemed at specific merchants. It is a one-time use, purpose-specific voucher. e-RUPI does not require a bank account or smartphone.

### Q9. UPI is built on which infrastructure?
(a) NEFT (b) RTGS (c) IMPS (d) SWIFT

**Answer: (c) IMPS**

**Solution:** UPI is built on top of the IMPS (Immediate Payment Service) infrastructure. IMPS was launched in 2010 and provides the underlying technology for UPI. UPI uses IMPS for real-time fund transfer and adds features like Virtual Payment Address (VPA), QR code-based merchant payments, and mobile-first design. SWIFT is a global inter-bank messaging network (not a payment system). NEFT and RTGS are RBI-operated batch/real-time systems respectively. UPI extends IMPS by adding VPA-based addressing.

### Q10. AePS stands for:
(a) Aadhaar-enabled Payment System (b) Advanced Electronic Payment Standard (c) Aadhaar Electronic Process System (d) Aadhaar Easy Payment Service

**Answer: (a) Aadhaar-enabled Payment System**

**Solution:** AePS stands for Aadhaar-enabled Payment System. It is operated by NPCI and allows basic banking using Aadhaar + biometric authentication. AePS works through micro-ATMs. It enables financial inclusion by allowing people in remote areas to access banking services using Aadhaar. AePS is targeted at migrant laborers, rural populations, and unbanked individuals. It supports cash withdrawal, cash deposit, balance inquiry, and mini statement. AePS uses the Aadhaar number as the primary identifier for the bank account.

### Q11. NEFT settlement happens how many times a day (current)?
(a) Hourly (b) Half-hourly (48 batches) (c) Every 15 minutes (d) Once a day

**Answer: (b) Half-hourly (48 batches)**

**Solution:** NEFT (National Electronic Funds Transfer) settlement happens in half-hourly batches — 48 batches per day. NEFT was launched in 2004. Settlement was originally hourly, but since December 2019, it has been increased to half-hourly. NEFT has no minimum or maximum amount. Charges are mostly NIL for inward; nominal for outward. NEFT is available 24x7 since December 2019. NEFT is operated by RBI. It is used for routine transfers like salary, EMI, and recurring payments. RTGS (different from NEFT) is for large-value real-time transfers.

### Q13. CBDC retail e₹-R has:
(a) Variable interest (b) 4% interest (c) No interest (d) 6% interest

**Answer: (c) No interest**

**Solution:** The CBDC retail e₹-R has no interest, similar to physical cash. This is to prevent bank disintermediation (people withdrawing from bank deposits). The CBDC is issued by RBI in a two-tier model: RBI issues to banks, banks distribute to public. CBDC is 100% backed by government securities. It is legal tender under Section 26 of the RBI Act. CBDC can be transacted via UPI QR codes. Both token-based and account-based variants exist. India is one of the first major economies to pilot CBDC.

### Q14. The Account Aggregator framework was launched in:
(a) 2018 (b) 2019 (c) 2021 (d) 2022

**Answer: (c) 2021**

**Solution:** The Account Aggregator (AA) framework was launched in 2021 by RBI. It allows data sharing between Financial Information Providers (FIPs) and Financial Information Users (FIUs) with user consent. RBI-registered AAs act as intermediaries. There are 8+ AAs operational. The framework enables individuals to share their financial data (bank accounts, GST returns, etc.) with consent for various purposes like loan applications, investment decisions. It enhances financial inclusion and reduces fraud. The AA ecosystem is part of the open finance movement in India.

### Q15. RTGS charges were made NIL from:
(a) 2017 (b) 2018 (c) 2019 (d) 2020

**Answer: (c) 2019**

**Solution:** RTGS charges were made NIL from July 2019 by RBI. NEFT charges were also made mostly NIL from this period. RTGS (Real Time Gross Settlement) was launched in 2004 and is operated by RBI. The minimum amount is ₹2 lakh; no maximum. RTGS is available 24x7 since 2019 (originally 9 AM to 4:30 PM on working days). The NIL charges for RTGS and NEFT are part of RBI's push for digital payments. RTGS is used for high-value transactions; NEFT is for routine transfers.
