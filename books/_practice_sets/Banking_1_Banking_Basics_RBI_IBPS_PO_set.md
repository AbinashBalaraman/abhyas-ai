## Questions

### Q1. The RBI Act was passed in:
(a) 1934 (b) 1935 (c) 1949 (d) 1956

**Answer: (a) 1934**

**Solution:** The RBI Act was passed in 1934. Under this Act, the RBI was established on 1 April 1935. The Act has been amended several times, most notably in 2016 (Sections 45ZA, 45ZB) to provide statutory basis for the Monetary Policy Committee (MPC). The 2016 amendment also brought in the Flexible Inflation Targeting (FIT) framework. The Bank Rate is defined under Section 49 of the RBI Act. The 2016 amendment was part of the Finance Act 2016.

### Q2. Who was the first Indian Governor of RBI?
(a) C.D. Deshmukh (b) Manmohan Singh (c) Raghuram Rajan (d) Urjit Patel

**Answer: (a) C.D. Deshmukh**

**Solution:** C.D. Deshmukh (Chintaman Dwarkanath Deshmukh) was the first Indian Governor of RBI (1943-1949). He was the first Indian to head RBI. Before him, the first Governor was Sir Osborne Smith (1935-1937, British). Other early Indian Governors: K.G. Ambegaonkar, H.V.R. Iengar, P.C. Bhattacharya, L.K. Jha. C.D. Deshmukh later became a Union Minister. The current RBI Governor is Sanjay Malhotra (since 11 December 2024).

### Q3. The tolerance band for the inflation target under FIT is:
(a) ±1% (b) ±2% (c) ±3% (d) ±4%

**Answer: (b) ±2%**

**Solution:** The tolerance band for the inflation target under FIT is ±2%, meaning the acceptable CPI inflation range is 2% to 6% (target 4%). If average inflation falls outside this band for 3 consecutive quarters, RBI must submit a report to the Central Government. The framework was retained for 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2031 (announced 25 March 2026). The operating target is the Weighted Average Call Rate (WACR), which is aligned with the policy repo rate.

**Time Target:** 30s | **Chapter Reference:** Banking/01_Banking_Basics_RBI.md, p.105-110 | **VERIFY:** No

### Q4. The MPC's inflation target was retained for the period April 2026 to March 2031 on:
(a) 25 March 2026 (b) 1 April 2025 (c) 31 March 2024 (d) 1 January 2026

**Answer: (a) 25 March 2026**

**Solution:** The Central Government retained the inflation target of 4% CPI (with ±2% tolerance band) for the period 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2031, announced on 25 March 2026. The framework requires the Government to review the target every 5 years. The FIT framework was introduced in 2016 with the RBI Act amendment. The previous review was in 2021. The 16th Finance Commission (chaired by Arvind Panagariya) deals with tax devolution.

### Q5. The floor of the LAF corridor is:
(a) Repo rate (b) MSF rate (c) SDF rate (d) Bank rate

**Answer: (c) SDF rate**

**Solution:** The SDF (Standing Deposit Facility) rate is the floor of the LAF corridor. The Repo rate is the mid-point (policy rate), and the MSF rate is the ceiling. SDF was introduced in April 2022 to replace the fixed Reverse Repo Rate as the floor. The Bank Rate is aligned with the MSF Rate (Section 49 of RBI Act). SDF is 25 bps below Repo; MSF is 25 bps above Repo. The corridor width is 50 bps.

**Time Target:** 30s | **Chapter Reference:** Banking/01_Banking_Basics_RBI.md, p.124-135 | **VERIFY:** No

### Q6. The ceiling of the LAF corridor is:
(a) Repo rate (b) MSF rate (c) SDF rate (d) Reverse repo rate

**Answer: (b) MSF rate**

**Solution:** The MSF (Marginal Standing Facility) rate is the ceiling of the LAF corridor. It is a penal rate (25 bps above the Repo Rate) for emergency overnight borrowing by banks. Banks can dip into their SLR portfolio up to 2% below the prescribed SLR to borrow under MSF. The Bank Rate is aligned with the MSF Rate under Section 49 of the RBI Act, 1934. The corridor structure: SDF (floor) → Repo (mid) → MSF (ceiling). Corridor width = 50 bps.

**Time Target:** 30s | **Chapter Reference:** Banking/01_Banking_Basics_RBI.md, p.128-135 | **VERIFY:** No

### Q7. The number of regional boards of RBI is:
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 5

**Answer: (c) 4**

**Solution:** RBI has 4 regional boards: Mumbai (West), Kolkata (East), Chennai (South), and New Delhi (North). Each regional board has 5 members. The regional boards are advisory in nature. RBI has 27 zonal offices across India. The Central Board of Directors has 21 members (1 Governor + 4 Deputy Governors + 2 Finance Ministry representatives + 10 government-nominated directors + 4 local board representatives). The headquarters is in Mumbai.

**Time Target:** 30s | **Chapter Reference:** Banking/01_Banking_Basics_RBI.md, p.34 | **VERIFY:** No

### Q8. Broad money in India is denoted as:
(a) M1 (b) M2 (c) M3 (d) M4

**Answer: (c) M3**

**Solution:** Broad money in India is M3. The money supply definitions are: M1 = Currency with public + Demand deposits with banks + Other deposits with RBI. M2 = M1 + Savings deposits with post office. M3 = M1 + Time deposits with banks (= Broad money). M4 = M3 + Total deposits with post office. Reserve money (M0) = Currency in circulation + Bankers' deposits with RBI + Other deposits with RBI. M3 is the most commonly used measure of money supply.

**Time Target:** 30s | **Chapter Reference:** Banking/01_Banking_Basics_RBI.md, p.150-157 | **VERIFY:** No

### Q9. The MPC meeting minutes are published how many days after the meeting?
(a) 7 days (b) 14 days (c) 21 days (d) 30 days

**Answer: (b) 14 days**

**Solution:** The MPC meeting minutes are published 14 days after each meeting, as mandated by the RBI Act. This ensures transparency and accountability of monetary policy decisions. The MPC meets at least 4 times a year (typically 6) — the 2026 meetings are scheduled in April, June, August, October, December, and February 2027. The current external MPC members (notified 1 October 2024) are Dr. Nagesh Kumar, Saugata Bhattacharya, and Prof. Ram Singh.

**Time Target:** 30s | **Chapter Reference:** Banking/01_Banking_Basics_RBI.md, p.85-99 | **VERIFY:** No

### Q10. The Bank Rate is aligned with which rate under the RBI Act, 1934?
(a) Repo Rate (b) MSF Rate (c) SDF Rate (d) Reverse Repo Rate

**Answer: (b) MSF Rate**

**Solution:** The Bank Rate is aligned with the MSF Rate under Section 49 of the RBI Act, 1934. The Bank Rate is the rate at which RBI is prepared to buy or rediscount bills of exchange or other commercial paper. Both Bank Rate and MSF Rate move together (currently 25 bps above the Repo Rate). The Bank Rate is not actively used as a policy tool currently but signals RBI's policy stance. Section 49 of the RBI Act defines the Bank Rate.
