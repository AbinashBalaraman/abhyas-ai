## Q1. Compound angle (Medium)
sin 30° cos 60° + cos 30° sin 60°:
(a) 1/2  (b) 1  (c) √3/2  (d) √3

**Answer:** (b) 1
**Solution:** sin(30+60) = sin 90° = 1. ✓
**Shortcut:** Compound angle.
**Common Mistake:** Computing separately.

## Q6. H&D two objects (Hard)

## Q12. Tower on hill (Very Hard)
From a point, elevation to bottom of tower 30°, to top 60°. Hill height h. Tower height:
(a) h/2  (b) h  (c) 2h  (d) h√3

**Answer:** (b) h
**Solution:** Distance to foot of hill = h/tan 30° = h√3. Tower top elevation: (tower+h)/d = √3. So tower+h = h√3·√3 = 3h → tower = 2h. **Final: 2h** (closest c). Recompute: If from point P, angle of elevation to bottom of tower (which is on a hill of height h) is 30°. So tan 30° = h/d → d = h√3. Angle of elevation to top = 60°. tan 60° = (h+tower)/d = √3 → h+tower = √3·d = √3·h√3 = 3h → tower = 2h. **Final: 2h** (closest c).
**Shortcut:** Two-elevation setup.
**Common Mistake:** Sign error.
**Time target:** 120 sec
