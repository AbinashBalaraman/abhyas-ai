## Q1. Linear (Easy)
Solve 5x + 3 = 18:
(a) 2  (b) 3  (c) 4  (d) 5

**Answer:** (b) 3
**Solution:** 5x = 15 → x = 3. ✓
**Shortcut:** Direct.
**Common Mistake:** Arithmetic.
**Time target:** 15 sec

---

## Q2. Roots (Easy)
Roots of x² − 4 = 0:
(a) ±1  (b) ±2  (c) ±3  (d) ±4

**Answer:** (b) ±2
**Solution:** x² = 4 → x = ±2. ✓
**Shortcut:** Direct square root.
**Common Mistake:** Missing negative.
**Time target:** 15 sec

---

## Q3. Sum/product (Easy)
If α+β = 7, αβ = 12, the quadratic equation with α, β as roots:
(a) x² − 7x + 12  (b) x² + 7x + 12  (c) x² − 7x − 12  (d) x² + 7x − 12

**Answer:** (a) x² − 7x + 12
**Solution:** x² − (sum)x + product = 0. ✓
**Shortcut:** Vieta's reverse.
**Common Mistake:** Sign.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q4. a² − b² (Medium)
If a = 5, b = 3, a² − b²:
(a) 4  (b) 8  (c) 16  (d) 2

**Answer:** (c) 16
**Solution:** (5−3)(5+3) = 2·8 = 16. ✓
**Shortcut:** Difference of squares.
**Common Mistake:** Computing separately.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q5. Surd (Medium)
√(98) simplifies to:
(a) 7√2  (b) 6√2  (c) 7√3  (d) 8√2

**Answer:** (a) 7√2
**Solution:** √98 = √(49·2) = 7√2. ✓
**Shortcut:** Factor.
**Common Mistake:** Not prime factoring.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q6. Discriminant (Medium)
Roots of 3x² − 2x − 1 = 0 are:
(a) Real and equal  (b) Real and distinct  (c) Complex  (d) None

**Answer:** (b) Real and distinct
**Solution:** D = 4 + 12 = 16 > 0. ✓
**Shortcut:** b² − 4ac.
**Common Mistake:** Wrong formula.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q7. Word problem (Medium)
Father's age is 3 times son's. After 6 years, sum will be 60. Son's age:
(a) 10  (b) 12  (c) 14  (d) 16

**Answer:** (b) 12
**Solution:** 3S+6 + S+6 = 60 → 4S = 48 → S = 12. ✓
**Shortcut:** Substitute.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting 6 each.
**Time target:** 45 sec

---

## Q8. Product of roots (Medium)
Product of roots of 4x² − 8x + 3 = 0:
(a) 3/4  (b) 8/4  (c) −3/4  (d) −8/4

**Answer:** (a) 3/4
**Solution:** c/a = 3/4. ✓
**Shortcut:** Vieta.
**Common Mistake:** Wrong sign.
**Time target:** 15 sec

## Q9. Surd equation (Hard)
Solve √(x+3) = 5:
(a) 20  (b) 22  (c) 25  (d) 28

**Answer:** (b) 22
**Solution:** x+3 = 25 → x = 22. ✓
**Shortcut:** Square both sides.
**Common Mistake:** Missing square.
**Time target:** 45 sec

---

## Q10. (a+b)² expand (Hard — Trap)
If a = 2x, b = 3y, (a+b)² = ?
(a) 4x² + 9y²  (b) 4x² + 12xy + 9y²  (c) 4x² + 6xy + 9y²  (d) 4x² + 9y² + xy

**Answer:** (b) 4x² + 12xy + 9y²
**Solution:** (2x+3y)² = 4x² + 2·2x·3y + 9y² = 4x² + 12xy + 9y². ✓
**Shortcut:** Apply identity with a=2x, b=3y.
**Common Mistake:** Missing 2ab term.
**Time target:** 45 sec
