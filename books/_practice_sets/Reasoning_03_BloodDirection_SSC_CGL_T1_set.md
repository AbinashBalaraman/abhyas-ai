# Practice Set: Blood Relations & Direction — SSC CGL Tier-I (Set 1)

**Source:** Reasoning Standard Reference Ch.16 & Ch.9, Standard Reference Reasoning SSC Ch.2 & Ch.5, Standard Reference Reasoning SSC Sec.6 & Sec.7
**Pattern:** 15 Questions | 60 seconds each | Total 15 minutes
**Difficulty:** Easy-Medium (SSC CGL Tier-I Standard)

---

### Q1. A is the son of B. B is the daughter of C. How is A related to C?
(a) Son (b) Grandson (c) Brother (d) Nephew

**Answer:** (b) Grandson
**Solution:** B is C's daughter; A is B's son. So A is C's grandson.
**Time Target:** 15 sec |

---

### Q2. Pointing to a man, Anu says, "He is the husband of my mother's sister." How is the man related to Anu?
(a) Uncle (b) Brother (c) Father (d) Father-in-law

**Answer:** (a) Uncle
**Solution:** Mother's sister = maternal aunt. Her husband = maternal uncle (by marriage).
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

---

### Q3. A walks 3 km North, then 4 km East. What is the distance from start?
(a) 5 km (b) 6 km (c) 7 km (d) 1 km

**Answer:** (a) 5 km
**Solution:** √(3² + 4²) = √25 = 5 km NE.
**Time Target:** 15 sec |

---

### Q4. A man is facing South. He turns 90° clockwise. Which direction does he face now?
(a) East (b) West (c) North (d) South

**Answer:** (b) West
**Solution:** Facing S + 90° clockwise = facing W.
**Time Target:** 10 sec |

---

### Q5. At sunrise, a person faces North. Where is his shadow?
(a) North (b) South (c) East (d) West

**Answer:** (d) West
**Solution:** Sunrise = sun East. Shadow = West. Person faces North. West is behind him.
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

---

### Q6. Pointing to a photograph, A says, "She is the daughter of my grandfather's only daughter." How is the person related to A?
(a) Sister (b) Mother (c) Aunt (d) Cousin

**Answer:** (a) Sister
**Solution:** A's grandfather's only daughter = A's mother. The person is A's mother's daughter = A's sister.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

---

### Q7. A and B are brothers. C is A's father's brother. D is B's mother's husband. How is D related to C?
(a) Same person (b) Brother (c) Father (d) Cousin

**Answer:** (b) Brother
**Solution:** A's father = B's father. A's father's brother = A's uncle (C). B's mother's husband = B's father = A's father. So D = A's father; C = A's father's brother. D and C are brothers.
**Time Target:** 30 sec |

---

### Q8. A walks 10 km South, turns right, walks 6 km, turns right, walks 10 km. Where is A from start?
(a) 6 km West (b) 6 km East (c) 6 km North (d) At start

**Answer:** (a) 6 km West
**Solution:** Start (0,0). S 10: (0,-10). Right=West. W 6: (-6,-10). Right=North. N 10: (-6,0). Final: 6 km West.
**Time Target:** 35 sec |

---

### Q9. A is the father of B. B is the sister of C. C is the mother of D. How is A related to D?
(a) Father (b) Grandfather (c) Uncle (d) Father-in-law

**Answer:** (b) Grandfather
**Solution:** A father of B. B sister of C. So A father of C. C mother of D. A is grandfather of D.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

---

### Q10. At 6 PM, a person is facing West. Where is his shadow?
(a) West (b) East (c) North (d) South

**Answer:** (b) East
**Solution:** 6 PM = sunset. Sun in West. Shadow = East. Person faces West. East is behind him.
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

---

### Q11. A, B, C, D are family. A is son of B. B is daughter of C. D is wife of C. How is A related to D?
(a) Son (b) Grandson (c) Nephew (d) Cannot be determined

**Answer:** (b) Grandson
**Solution:** C is patriarch. C's wife = D. C's daughter = B. B's son = A. A is grandson of C, hence grandson of D.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

---

### Q12. A man walks 5 km South, turns right, walks 3 km, turns right, walks 5 km. In which direction from start?
(a) East (b) West (c) South (d) North

**Answer:** (b) West
**Solution:** Start (0,0). S 5: (0,-5). Right=West. W 3: (-3,-5). Right=North. N 5: (-3,0). Final: 3 km West.
**Time Target:** 30 sec |

---

### Q13. Pointing to a lady, a man said, "She is the daughter of the woman who is the mother of the husband of my mother." How is the lady related to the man?
(a) Sister (b) Daughter (c) Aunt (d) Wife

**Answer:** (c) Aunt
**Solution:** My mother's husband = my father. The mother of my father = my paternal grandmother. The daughter of my paternal grandmother = my father's sister = my aunt.
**Time Target:** 40 sec |

---

### Q14. Ram walks 8 km North, turns right, walks 6 km, turns right, walks 8 km. How far from start?
(a) 6 km East (b) 6 km West (c) 14 km East (d) 0 km

**Answer:** (a) 6 km East
**Solution:** N 8: (0,8). Right=East. E 6: (6,8). Right=South. S 8: (6,0). 6 km East.
**Time Target:** 35 sec |

---

### Q15. Pointing to a man, Sita said, "He is the son of the only brother of my mother's mother." How is Sita related to that man?
(a) Aunt (b) Cousin (c) Sister (d) Niece

**Answer:** (b) Cousin
**Solution:** Mother's mother = maternal grandmother. Her only brother = grandmother's brother. His son = grandmother's brother's son = mother's cousin = my cousin.
**Time Target:** 40 sec |
