### Q1. IRDAI was established in:
(a) 1995 (b) 1999 (c) 2001 (d) 2005

**Answer: (b) 1999**

**Solution:** IRDAI (Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India) was established in 1999 under the IRDA Act, 1999. It is headquartered in Hyderabad (moved from Delhi in 2001). The current Chairperson is Ajay Seth, IAS (as of July 2025). IRDAI regulates the insurance industry in India - both life insurance (24 companies) and general insurance (28 companies). The FDI limit in the insurance sector was raised to 74% in 2021 Budget (from 49% earlier). IRDAI protects policyholders' interests and promotes the development of the insurance sector.

### Q3. LIC was established in:
(a) 1947 (b) 1950 (c) 1956 (d) 1960

**Answer: (c) 1956**

**Solution:** LIC (Life Insurance Corporation of India) was established in 1956. LIC was nationalized on 19 January 1956. LIC absorbed 154 Indian insurers, 16 non-Indian insurers, and 75 provident societies. LIC had a monopoly on life insurance in India until the late 1990s. Currently there are 24 life insurance companies operating in India. Major private players: HDFC Life, ICICI Prudential, SBI Life, Max Life, Bajaj Allianz, Tata AIA. LIC's iconic ad "Zindagi ke saath bhi, zindagi ke baad bhi" is famous. LIC was demutualized and listed on stock exchanges in 2017.

### Q4. LIC was nationalized on:
(a) 1 January 1956 (b) 19 January 1956 (c) 26 January 1956 (d) 1 April 1956

**Answer: (b) 19 January 1956**

**Solution:** LIC (Life Insurance Corporation of India) was nationalized on 19 January 1956 through the Life Insurance Corporation Act, 1956. The LIC was established by the Act of Parliament, absorbing 154 Indian insurers, 16 non-Indian insurers, and 75 provident societies. ### Q5. The FDI limit in the insurance sector in India is:
(a) 26% (b) 49% (c) 74% (d) 100%

**Answer: (c) 74%**

**Solution:** The FDI (Foreign Direct Investment) limit in the insurance sector in India is 74% (raised from 49% in 2021). The increase to 74% was announced in the 2021 Union Budget and was subsequently amended in the Insurance Act, 1938. The foreign investment up to 74% is under the automatic route (no prior approval required). Beyond 74%, foreign investment requires government approval. The limit for insurance intermediaries is 100%. The 74% limit applies to both life and general insurance companies. This was a major reform to attract foreign capital.

### Q6. How many PSU General Insurance Companies are there?
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 5

**Answer: (c) 4**

**Solution:** There are 4 PSU General Insurance Companies in India: National Insurance Company, New India Assurance Company, Oriental Insurance Company, and United India Insurance Company. All four were nationalized in 1972. GIC Re (General Insurance Corporation of India) is the national re-insurer, incorporated in 1971 and effective from 1 January 1973. As of 2024, the Government of India is in the process of merging 3 of the 4 PSU general insurers (Oriental, National, United India) into New India Assurance. After the merger, there will be effectively 1 major PSU general insurer.

### Q7. PMJAY was launched on:
(a) 15 August 2018 (b) 23 September 2018 (c) 26 January 2019 (d) 1 April 2018

**Answer: (b) 23 September 2018**

**Solution:** PMJAY (Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana), also known as Ayushman Bharat, was launched on 23 September 2018. It is the world's largest health insurance scheme. PMJAY provides health cover of ₹5 lakh per family per year. The scheme targets 50+ crore beneficiaries (bottom 40% of population as per SECC database). PMJAY covers secondary and tertiary care hospitalization. The PMJAY Vay Vandana Card was announced in September 2024 for senior citizens 70+ years (separate cover). The scheme is cashless and paperless at the point of service.

### Q9. PFRDA Act was enacted in:
(a) 2003 (b) 2013 (c) 2015 (d) 2020

**Answer: (b) 2013**

**Solution:** PFRDA Act was enacted on 19 September 2013 and enforced on 1 February 2014. The interim PFRDA was set up on 23 August 2003. PFRDA replaced the 1999 "OASIS" (Old Age Social & Income Security) project. PFRDA is headquartered in New Delhi. PFRDA regulates NPS (National Pension System), APY (Atal Pension Yojana), and other pension schemes. The current Chairman is Deepak Mohanty. PFRDA registers and regulates pension funds, POPs (Points of Presence), and CRAs (Central Recordkeeping Agencies).

### Q10. NPS was open to all citizens from:
(a) 1 January 2004 (b) 1 May 2009 (c) 1 April 2010 (d) 1 January 2010

**Answer: (b) 1 May 2009**

**Solution:** NPS (National Pension System) was open to all citizens from 1 May 2009. NPS was originally for government employees (notified on 22 December 2003, started on 1 January 2004). NPS is a defined-contribution pension scheme. NPS is regulated by PFRDA. NPS has Tier I (locked till 60) and Tier II (voluntary savings) accounts. NPS Vatsalya was launched in 2024 for minors. As of June 2026, NPS has 2.23 crore subscribers and AUM of ₹16,77,514 crore. NPS offers Active Choice and Auto Choice (Life Cycle Fund) for asset allocation.

### Q11. APY was relaunched in:
(a) 2010 (b) 2014 (c) 2015 (d) 2016

**Answer: (c) 2015**

**Solution:** APY (Atal Pension Yojana) was relaunched (after the original Swavalamban Scheme of 2010-11) on 9 May 2015 by PM Modi in Kolkata. APY is named after former PM Atal Bihari Vajpayee. APY has 7.64 crore active subscribers as of April 2026.

### Q13. UPS was announced in:
(a) 2023 (b) 2024 (c) 2025 (d) 2026

**Answer: (b) 2024**

**Solution:** UPS (Unified Pension Scheme) was announced on 24 August 2024 by PM Modi. UPS is for Central Government employees. It combines features of the Old Pension Scheme (OPS) (defined benefit) and NPS (defined contribution). UPS provides a guaranteed pension of 50% of average basic pay of last 12 months. The assured minimum pension is ₹10,000 per month (for those with min 10 years service). UPS is effective from 1 April 2025. UPS is a hybrid scheme that provides both fixed benefit and contribution. This was a major reform for central government employees.

### Q14. UPS provides guaranteed pension of:
(a) 25% of avg basic pay (b) 50% of avg basic pay (c) 75% of avg basic pay (d) 100% of avg basic pay

**Answer: (b) 50% of avg basic pay**

**Solution:** UPS (Unified Pension Scheme) provides a guaranteed pension of 50% of the average basic pay of the last 12 months before retirement. This is similar to the Old Pension Scheme (OPS). The assured minimum pension is ₹10,000 per month for those with minimum 10 years of service. UPS is inflation-indexed (Dearness Relief applicable). UPS contribution is 10% (employee) + 14% (employer), same as NPS. The government bears the risk in UPS, unlike NPS where the subscriber bears the market risk. UPS is effective from 1 April 2025.
