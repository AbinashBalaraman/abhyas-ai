## Q1. Table total (Easy)
A=200, B=300, C=500. Total:
(a) 800  (b) 1000  (c) 1200  (d) 1500

**Answer:** (b) 1000
**Solution:** 200+300+500 = 1000. ✓
**Shortcut:** Direct.
**Common Mistake:** Wrong sum.
**Time target:** 10 sec

---

## Q2. Pie slice (Easy)
Total 400. B = 30%. Value:
(a) 80  (b) 100  (c) 120  (d) 140

**Answer:** (c) 120
**Solution:** 30%·400 = 120. ✓
**Shortcut:** Direct.
**Common Mistake:** Setting = 100.
**Time target:** 15 sec

## Q3. Bar max (Easy — Trap)

**Solution:** Direct. ✓

## Q4. Line % (Medium)
100 → 150. % increase:
(a) 25%  (b) 50%  (c) 75%  (d) 100%

**Answer:** (b) 50%
**Solution:** 50/100 = 50%. ✓
**Shortcut:** (New−Old)/Old.
**Common Mistake:** Setting = 25%.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q5. Caselet % (Medium)
Total 500. X = 40% of total. X = ?
(a) 100  (b) 150  (c) 200  (d) 250

**Answer:** (c) 200
**Solution:** 40%·500 = 200. ✓
**Shortcut:** Direct.
**Common Mistake:** Setting = 250.
**Time target:** 20 sec

## Q6. Table ratio (Medium)
A=300, B=200, C=500. Ratio A:C:

**Solution:** 300:500 = 3:5. ✓

## Q7. Pie angle (Medium)
D = 40% of pie. Angle:
(a) 90°  (b) 120°  (c) 144°  (d) 180°

**Answer:** (c) 144°
**Solution:** 40%·360° = 144°. ✓
**Shortcut:** % × 360.
**Common Mistake:** Setting = 120°.

## Q8. Bar average (Hard)
5 bars: 10, 20, 30, 40, 50. Average:
(a) 25  (b) 30  (c) 35  (d) 40

**Answer:** (b) 30
**Solution:** 150/5 = 30. ✓
**Shortcut:** Sum/n.
**Common Mistake:** Setting = 25.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q9. Caselet chain (Hard)
A = 2B. B = 50% of C. C = 100. Total:
(a) 150  (b) 200  (c) 250  (d) 300

**Answer:** (c) 250
**Solution:** B = 50. A = 100. Total = 250. ✓
**Shortcut:** Sequential.
**Common Mistake:** Missing a term.
**Time target:** 60 sec

---

## Q10. Multi-step caselet (Hard — Trap)
School 800 students. 30% girls. 20% of girls study sci. 60% of boys study sci. Total in sci:
(a) 360  (b) 384  (c) 400  (d) 420

**Answer:** (b) 384
**Solution:** Girls sci = 240·0.2 = 48. Boys sci = 560·0.6 = 336. Total = 384. ✓
**Shortcut:** Sequential.
**Common Mistake:** Wrong %s.
**Time target:** 90 sec
