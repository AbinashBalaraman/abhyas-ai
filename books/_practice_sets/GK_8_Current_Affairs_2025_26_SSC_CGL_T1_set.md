### Q1. The 15th Vice President of India (taking oath in September 2025) is:
(a) Jagdeep Dhankhar (b) C. P. Radhakrishnan (c) Droupadi Murmu (d) Om Birla

**Answer: (b) C. P. Radhakrishnan**

**Solution:** C. P. Radhakrishnan was elected as the 15th Vice President of India on 9 September 2025 and took oath on 12 September 2025. He succeeded Jagdeep Dhankhar who resigned on 21 July 2025. The Vice President is also the ex-officio Chairman of the Rajya Sabha. C. P. Radhakrishnan was previously Governor of Jharkhand and Maharashtra.

### Q2. The Chief Justice of India (since 24 November 2025) is:
(a) B. R. Gavai (b) Surya Kant (c) D. Y. Chandrachud (d) U. U. Lalit

**Answer: (b) Surya Kant**

**Solution:** Justice Surya Kant became the Chief Justice of India (CJI) on 24 November 2025, succeeding B. R. Gavai. He is the 53rd CJI. As CJI, he heads the Supreme Court and is the head of the judiciary in India. The CJI is appointed by the President of India and has a tenure until the age of 65.

### Q3. The Governor of the Reserve Bank of India (since 11 December 2024) is:
(a) Shaktikanta Das (b) Sanjay Malhotra (c) Urjit Patel (d) Raghuram Rajan

**Answer: (b) Sanjay Malhotra**

**Solution:** Sanjay Malhotra became the 26th Governor of the Reserve Bank of India on 11 December 2024, succeeding Shaktikanta Das. He is a 1990-batch IAS officer from the Rajasthan cadre. The RBI Governor is appointed for a 3-year term (extendable). He chairs the Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) which sets India's repo rate.

### Q7. Operation Sindoor was conducted by India in May 2025 against targets in:
(a) Bangladesh (b) Myanmar (c) Pakistan (d) China

**Answer: (c) Pakistan**

**Solution:** Operation Sindoor was conducted by the Indian Army and Air Force on 7-10 May 2025 in response to the 22 April 2025 Pahalgam terror attack (in which 26 tourists were killed). It involved surgical strikes on terrorist targets in Pakistan. A ceasefire agreement between India and Pakistan was reached on 10 May 2025.

### Q9. The Waqf (Amendment) Act, 2025 received Presidential assent and came into effect on:
(a) 28 February 2025 (b) 5 April 2025 (c) 22 August 2025 (d) 15 September 2025

**Answer: (b) 5 April 2025**

**Solution:** The Waqf (Amendment) Act, 2025 came into effect on 5 April 2025, replacing the Mussalman Wakf Act, 1923. The Act brought significant changes to the management and registration of Waqf properties. On 15 September 2025, the Supreme Court suspended some parts of the Act, putting them under judicial review.

(a) 5 April 2025 (b) 15 July 2025 (c) 22 August 2025 (d) 17 April 2026
