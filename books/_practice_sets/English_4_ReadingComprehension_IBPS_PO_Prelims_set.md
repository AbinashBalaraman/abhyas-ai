## Passage 1

The Reserve Bank of India's recent monetary policy decision has drawn attention from market analysts. Citing concerns about persistent inflation, the MPC voted to maintain the repo rate at 6.5%, contrary to expectations of a rate cut. The governor emphasized that the decision was aimed at ensuring price stability, even at the cost of slower economic growth. Bond yields rose sharply following the announcement, while equity markets showed mixed reactions, with banking stocks gaining on expectations of stable margins. Economists suggest that the central bank's hawkish stance reflects its commitment to controlling inflation before shifting focus to growth. The next policy review is scheduled for next month, and analysts will be watching for signals of a potential rate cut if inflation shows signs of moderation.

### Q1.
Why did the RBI maintain the repo rate?
- (a) To boost economic growth.
- (b) To control inflation.
- (c) To help banking stocks.
- (d) To support bond markets.

**Answer:** (b) To control inflation.
**Solution:** "Citing concerns about persistent inflation, the MPC voted to maintain the repo rate" + "aimed at ensuring price stability."
**Strategy:** Factual question — find the explicit reason.
**Time Target:** 30 sec

---

### Q2.
How did bond markets react to the announcement?
- (a) Yields fell sharply. (b) Yields rose sharply. (c) No change. (d) Recovered quickly.

**Answer:** (b) Yields rose sharply.
**Solution:** "Bond yields rose sharply following the announcement."
**Strategy:** Factual question.
**Time Target:** 25 sec

---

### Q3.
The governor's decision reflects a preference for:
- (a) Growth over inflation control.
- (b) Inflation control over growth.
- (c) Banking profits over economy.
- (d) Markets over policy.

**Answer:** (b) Inflation control over growth.
**Solution:** "The decision was aimed at ensuring price stability, even at the cost of slower economic growth."
**Strategy:** Inference — note the phrase "even at the cost of."
**Time Target:** 45 sec

---

### Q4.
The word "hawkish" most nearly means:
- (a) Peaceful (b) Aggressive / favoring tight policy (c) Indifferent (d) Generous

**Answer:** (b) Aggressive / favoring tight policy
**Solution:** "Hawkish" in monetary policy = favoring tight policy (high rates to control inflation).
**Strategy:** Vocabulary in context — finance term.
**Time Target:** 25 sec

---

### Q5.
What does the passage suggest about the next policy review?
- (a) A rate cut is certain. (b) A rate cut is possible if inflation moderates. (c) The rate will rise further. (d) The MPC will be dissolved.

**Answer:** (b) A rate cut is possible if inflation moderates.
**Solution:** "analysts will be watching for signals of a potential rate cut if inflation shows signs of moderation."
**Strategy:** Inference — note "if" (conditional).
**Time Target:** 45 sec

## Passage 2

Digital lending has emerged as one of the fastest-growing segments in India's financial sector. The model leverages technology to assess creditworthiness, disburse loans, and collect repayments, often without physical branches. While this has expanded access to credit for underserved populations, it has also raised concerns about predatory practices, data privacy, and regulatory oversight. The Reserve Bank of India has issued new guidelines requiring digital lending platforms to disclose all charges upfront, restrict data collection to necessary information, and appoint grievance officers. Industry experts believe these measures will enhance transparency and protect consumers. However, the rapid pace of innovation means regulators must continually update frameworks to address emerging challenges, including AI-based lending decisions and the use of alternative data for credit scoring.

### Q6.
According to the passage, digital lending:
- (a) Has slowed significantly.
- (b) Has expanded access to credit but raised concerns.
- (c) Is fully regulated.
- (d) Has no issues.

**Answer:** (b) Has expanded access to credit but raised concerns.
**Solution:** "While this has expanded access to credit for underserved populations, it has also raised concerns about predatory practices, data privacy, and regulatory oversight."
**Strategy:** Main idea — capture both sides.
**Time Target:** 30 sec

---

### Q7.
What is one of the new RBI requirements for digital lending?
- (a) Banning all digital platforms. (b) Disclosing all charges upfront. (c) Increasing interest rates. (d) Removing grievance officers.

**Answer:** (b) Disclosing all charges upfront.
**Solution:** "The Reserve Bank of India has issued new guidelines requiring digital lending platforms to disclose all charges upfront."
**Strategy:** Factual question.
**Time Target:** 25 sec

---

### Q8.
The author would most likely agree that:
- (a) Digital lending should be banned.
- (b) Digital lending is beneficial but needs careful regulation.
- (c) Digital lending is perfect.
- (d) Regulation will kill innovation.

**Answer:** (b) Digital lending is beneficial but needs careful regulation.
**Solution:** The passage acknowledges benefits, lists concerns, and supports the new guidelines.
**Strategy:** Author's view — find the balanced stance.
**Time Target:** 45 sec

---

### Q9.
The word "predatory" most nearly means:
- (a) Helpful (b) Exploitative (c) Fair (d) Transparent

**Answer:** (b) Exploitative
**Solution:** "Predatory" practices = exploiting customers (e.g., hidden fees, unfair terms).
**Strategy:** Vocabulary in context.
**Time Target:** 20 sec

---

### Q10.
What is implied about the future of digital lending regulation?
- (a) It will end. (b) It must continually evolve. (c) It will remain static. (d) It will be replaced by self-regulation.

**Answer:** (b) It must continually evolve.
**Solution:** "regulators must continually update frameworks to address emerging challenges."
**Strategy:** Inference — note "continually update."
**Time Target:** 30 sec

## IBPS PO Prelims Strategy

1. **Speed:** 30 sec/Q — read fast, locate answer.
2. **Banking vocabulary:** Familiarize with terms like "repo rate," "hawkish," "yield."
3. **Inference vs. Factual:** Don't confuse — inference is implied, factual is stated.
4. **Tone matters:** Look for words like "experts believe" vs. "must" (mandatory vs. opinion).
