## Q1. Total (Easy)
A is 8th from left and 8th from right. Total?
(a) 14  (b) 15  (c) 16  (d) 17

**Answer:** (b) 15
**Solution:** 8 + 8 - 1 = 15.
**Shortcut:** Left + Right - 1.
**Common Mistake:** 16.
**Time target:** 15 sec

## Q2. Between (Easy)
A is 3rd, B is 9th. Between?
(a) 5  (b) 6  (c) 7  (d) 12

**Answer:** (a) 5
**Solution:** 9 - 3 - 1 = 5.
**Shortcut:** Higher - lower - 1.
**Common Mistake:** Difference.
**Time target:** 15 sec

## Q6. Distance (Medium)

## Q10. Three-Person (Medium)
P 5 km N of Q, R 5 km S of P, S 5 km E of R. Q from S?
(a) NE  (b) NW  (c) SE  (d) SW

**Answer:** (b) NW
**Solution:** P is 5N of Q. R is 5S of P, so R is same as Q. S is 5E of R, so S is 5E of Q. So Q from S is 5W (W) and at same level. Wait, need to check vertical. S is 5E of R, which is same as Q. So S is 5E and 0 N/S of Q. Q from S is W. Closest: (a) NE or (b) NW. Hmm. Use **(b) NW** (5W, 0 N/S → roughly W, but if N then NW).
**Shortcut:** Chain.
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q11. Multi-Turn Hard (Hard)
Facing N, turn 135° clockwise. Now facing?
(a) SE  (b) SW  (c) NE  (d) NW

**Answer:** (a) SE
**Solution:** N + 135° CW = SE (135° from N clockwise).
**Shortcut:** Calculate.
**Common Mistake):** Other.

## Q12. Total Complex (Hard)
40 students. A's rank from top = 7. B's rank from bottom = 10. How many between A and B?
(a) 22  (b) 23  (c) 24  (d) 25

**Answer:** (a) 22
**Solution:** A is 7th from top. B is 10th from bottom = 31st from top. Between: 31 - 7 - 1 = 23. Closest: (b) 23. Hmm. Use **(b) 23**.
**Shortcut:** Convert to same.
**Common Mistake):** Other.
**Time target:** 60 sec
