# Practice Set: Blood Relations & Direction — SSC CGL Tier-II (Mains) (Set 2)

**Source:** Reasoning Standard Reference Ch.16 & Ch.9, Standard Reference Reasoning SSC Ch.2 & Ch.5
**Pattern:** 15 Questions | 60 seconds each | Total 15 minutes
**Difficulty:** Hard (SSC CGL Tier-II Mains Standard)

---

### Q1. A family: A+B married, has 2 children C and D. C+E married, has child F. D+G married. How is F related to G?
(a) Son (b) Grandson (c) Nephew (d) Niece

**Answer:** (c) Nephew
**Solution:** A-B parents, C-D children. C-E married, F is their child. G is married to D. F is G's nephew (F is child of C, who is sibling of D).
**Time Target:** 30 sec |

---

### Q2. A walks 4 km East, then 3 km North, then 4 km West, then 3 km South. Where is A from start?
(a) 4 km East (b) At start (c) 3 km North (d) 4 km West

**Answer:** (b) At start
**Solution:** E 4: (4,0). N 3: (4,3). W 4: (0,3). S 3: (0,0). Back to start.
**Time Target:** 30 sec |

---

### Q3. Pointing to a man, Priya says, "His mother is the only daughter of my mother's mother." How is the man related to Priya?
(a) Brother (b) Son (c) Nephew (d) Cousin

**Answer:** (a) Brother
**Solution:** My mother's mother = my maternal grandmother. Her only daughter = my mother (since my mother is from that family). So his mother = my mother. The man is my brother.
**Time Target:** 35 sec |

---

### Q4. A, B, C, D, E sit in a row. A is to the immediate right of B. C is to the immediate left of D. E is at the leftmost. Who is in the middle?
(a) A (b) B (c) C (d) Cannot be determined

**Answer:** (c) C
**Solution:** E at leftmost (position 1). B-A at positions 2-3 (B at 2, A at 3). C-D at 4-5 (C at 4, D at 5). Middle of 5 = position 3 = A. Wait, **C is at 4.** Re-check: E(1), B(2), A(3), C(4), D(5). Middle = 3 = A. **Answer: (a) A.**
**Time Target:** 30 sec

---

### Q5. Pointing to a photograph of a girl, Ramesh says, "Her mother is the wife of the only son of my grandmother's husband." How is Ramesh related to the girl?
(a) Father (b) Brother (c) Uncle (d) Cannot be determined

**Answer:** (a) Father
**Solution:** My grandmother's husband = my grandfather. The only son of grandfather = my father. The wife of my father = my mother. Her daughter = the girl. The girl is my sister or me. So Ramesh is the girl's father OR brother.
**Time Target:** 45 sec |

---

### Q6. At sunrise, a man stands facing North-East. His shadow is to his:
(a) Right (b) Left (c) Front (d) Behind

**Answer:** (b) Left
**Solution:** Sun in East. Shadow falls West. Man faces NE. West is to his left-back. Most direct: West relative to NE facing is to the LEFT.
**Time Target:** 35 sec |

---

### Q7. A family: P+Q married, sons R and S. R+T married, daughter U. How is U related to P?
(a) Daughter (b) Granddaughter (c) Niece (d) Daughter-in-law

**Answer:** (b) Granddaughter
**Solution:** P is grandfather. R is P's son. U is R's daughter. U is P's granddaughter.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

---

### Q8. A is facing North. He turns 135° clockwise. Which direction?
(a) SE (b) SW (c) NE (d) E

**Answer:** (a) SE
**Solution:** North + 135° clockwise = SE (45°+90° = 135°).
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

---

### Q9. Pointing to a person, A says, "He is the son of the only son of my wife's mother." How is the person related to A?
(a) Son (b) Brother (c) Nephew (d) Father-in-law

**Answer:** (a) Son
**Solution:** My wife's mother = my mother-in-law. Her only son = my wife's brother (or my husband if no brother). His son = my nephew or my husband's son (if only son of mother-in-law is my husband).
**Time Target:** 45 sec |

---

### Q10. A walks 6 km South, then 8 km East. Distance and direction?
(a) 10 km SE (b) 14 km SE (c) 10 km SW (d) 14 km SW

**Answer:** (a) 10 km SE
**Solution:** S 6: (0,-6). E 8: (8,-6). Distance = √(64+36) = √100 = 10. Direction = SE.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

---

### Q11. At sunset, a woman faces South-West. Her shadow is in which direction?
(a) East (b) West (c) NE (d) SW

**Answer:** (a) East
**Solution:** Sunset = sun in West. Shadow = East. Direct.
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

---

### Q12. A and B are brothers. C is A's mother. D is B's father. E is C's husband. How is E related to D?
(a) Same person (b) Father (c) Brother (d) Cousin

**Answer:** (a) Same person
**Solution:** A's mother = C. B's father = D. A and B are brothers, so they share parents. C and D are married (E is C's husband = A's father = B's father = D). So E = D.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

---

### Q13. Ravi walks 5 km towards South, then turns right and walks 3 km, then turns right and walks 5 km. Direction from start?
(a) East (b) West (c) South (d) North

**Answer:** (b) West
**Solution:** S 5: (0,-5). Right=West. W 3: (-3,-5). Right=North. N 5: (-3,0). 3 km West.
**Time Target:** 30 sec |

---

### Q14. Pointing to a woman, Ravi said, "She is the daughter of the only child of my grandmother." How is the woman related to Ravi?
(a) Sister (b) Mother (c) Aunt (d) Wife

**Answer:** (a) Sister
**Solution:** My grandmother's only child = my mother (or my uncle/aunt). Her daughter = my sister (or my cousin).
**Time Target:** 35 sec |

---

### Q15. A clock shows 3 o'clock. The hour hand points East. The minute hand points:
(a) North (b) South (c) West (d) East

**Answer:** (a) North
**Solution:** 3 o'clock = hour at 3 (East). Minute at 12 (top) = North.
**Time Target:** 15 sec |
