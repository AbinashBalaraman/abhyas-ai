### Q1.
**Spot the error:** "I **am knowing** the answer."
- (a) I (b) am knowing (c) the answer (d) No error

**Answer:** (b) am knowing
**Solution:** "Know" is stative → no continuous. Correct: "I **know** the answer."
**Grammar Rule:** Stative verbs (know, believe, want, like) reject -ing.
**Common Mistake:** Using "am knowing" for knowledge states.
**Time Target:** 15 sec

---

### Q2.
**Spot the error:** "This house **is belonging** to me."
- (a) This house (b) is belonging (c) to me (d) No error

**Answer:** (b) is belonging
**Solution:** "Belong" is stative. Correct: "This house **belongs** to me."
**Grammar Rule:** Stative verb "belong" → Simple Present only.
**Common Mistake:** Adding continuous form to "belong."
**Time Target:** 15 sec

---

### Q3.
**Spot the error:** "I **am not hating** him."
- (a) I (b) am not hating (c) him (d) No error

**Answer:** (b) am not hating
**Solution:** "Hate" is stative (feeling). Correct: "I **don't hate** him."
**Grammar Rule:** Stative feelings/emotions reject continuous; use Simple Present + do/does.
**Common Mistake:** Using "am not hating" for emotions.
**Time Target:** 20 sec

---

### Q4.
**Convert to Passive:** "Who taught you French?"
- (a) By whom you were taught French? (b) By whom were you taught French? (c) French was taught you by whom? (d) You were taught French by whom?

**Answer:** (b) By whom were you taught French?
**Solution:** Interrogative passive: invert auxiliary "were" and subject "you." Preposition "by" moves to front with "whom."
**Grammar Rule:** Voice — Interrogative active → Interrogative passive with subject-auxiliary inversion.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting subject-auxiliary inversion in passive questions.
**Time Target:** 25 sec

---

### Q5.
**Convert to Passive:** "She will complete the work tomorrow."
- (a) The work will be completed by her tomorrow. (b) The work will completed by her tomorrow. (c) The work is completed by her tomorrow. (d) The work would be completed by her tomorrow.

**Answer:** (a) The work will be completed by her tomorrow.
**Solution:** Future Simple → "will + be + V3." "Tomorrow" stays as is (it's part of the original sentence, not converted in passive).
**Grammar Rule:** Voice Rule II — Future → will + be + V3.
**Common Mistake:** Dropping "be" (writing "will completed").
**Time Target:** 20 sec

---

### Q6.
**Convert to Indirect Speech:** He said, "I have finished my homework."
- (a) He said that he has finished his homework. (b) He said that he had finished his homework. (c) He said that he finished his homework. (d) He said that he have finished his homework.

**Answer:** (b) He said that he had finished his homework.
**Solution:** Back-shift Present Perfect "have finished" → Past Perfect "had finished." Pronoun "I/my" → "he/his."
**Grammar Rule:** Present Perfect → Past Perfect in indirect speech.
**Common Mistake:** Keeping Present Perfect instead of back-shifting.
**Time Target:** 25 sec

---

### Q7.
**Spot the error:** "He **has went** to school."
- (a) He (b) has went (c) to school (d) No error

**Answer:** (b) has went
**Solution:** V3 of "go" is "gone" (not "went," which is V2). Correct: "He has **gone** to school."
**Grammar Rule:** Present Perfect = has/have + V3.
**Common Mistake:** Mixing V2 "went" with V3 "gone."
**Time Target:** 15 sec

---

### Q8.
**Convert to Passive:** "Did she write the letter?"
- (a) Was the letter written by her? (b) Is the letter written by her? (c) Did the letter written by her? (d) Was the letter wrote by her?

**Answer:** (a) Was the letter written by her?
**Solution:** Interrogative Simple Past passive → "Was/Were + subject + V3 + by + agent?" "Letter" is singular → "was written."
**Grammar Rule:** Voice — Past interrogative → "Was/Were + subject + V3 + by + agent?"
**Common Mistake:** Using "did" (active auxiliary) in passive; using V1 "wrote" instead of V3 "written."
**Time Target:** 25 sec

---

### Q9.
**Convert to Indirect Speech:** He said to me, "Please help me."
- (a) He requested me to help him. (b) He said to me to help him. (c) He requested me that I should help him. (d) He told me to help him.

**Answer:** (a) He requested me to help him.
**Solution:** Imperative with "please" → "requested + obj + to + V1." "Said to" → "requested." Pronoun "me" → "him."
**Grammar Rule:** Imperative with polite word → "requested/asked + object + to + V1."
**Common Mistake:** Using "said + to + V1" (incorrect construction).
**Time Target:** 25 sec

---

### Q10.
**Spot the error:** "She **is not believing** in God."
- (a) She (b) is not believing (c) in God (d) No error

**Answer:** (b) is not believing
**Solution:** "Believe" is stative (mental state). Correct: "She **does not believe** in God."
**Grammar Rule:** Stative verb "believe" → Simple Present with do/does.
**Common Mistake:** Using continuous with mental-state verbs.
**Time Target:** 20 sec

## SBI PO Prelims Strategy

1. **Stative verb identification is critical:** This chapter has many stative verb errors.
2. **V2 vs V3 confusion:** "has went/went" type errors appear frequently.
3. **Interrogative passive:** Watch for subject-auxiliary inversion.
4. **Polite imperatives:** "Please" → "requested/asked" (not "said to").
