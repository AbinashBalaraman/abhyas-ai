# Practice Set: Statement, Conclusion, Assumption, Data Sufficiency — SSC CGL Tier-I (Set 1)

**Source:** Standard Reference Reasoning SSC Ch.10 SRE-463-519, Reasoning Standard Reference Ch.15 pp.363-394, Standard Reference Reasoning 8th Ch.7 & Ch.8
**Pattern:** 15 Questions | 60 seconds each | Total 15 minutes
**Difficulty:** Easy-Medium (SSC CGL Tier-I Standard)

---

### Q1. Statement: "The government has banned the use of plastic bags to protect the environment." Which assumption is implicit?
(a) Plastic bags harm the environment
(b) Plastic bags are useful
(c) Government is powerful (d) People use plastic bags

**Answer:** (a) Plastic bags harm the environment
**Solution:** The ban is justified by the assumption that plastic bags harm the environment.
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

---

### Q2. Statement: "All students should study regularly to pass the exam." Conclusion: I. Regular study leads to passing. II. Irregular study leads to failure.
(a) Only I follows (b) Only II follows (c) Both follow (d) Neither follows

**Answer:** (a) Only I follows
**Solution:** "Should study regularly to pass" implies regular study leads to passing. The reverse (irregular → failure) is too strong.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

---

### Q3. Statement: "Company X has launched a new product that is cheaper than competitors." Assumption:
(a) Customers prefer cheaper products
(b) Company X is a market leader
(c) Product is of good quality (d) Competitors are expensive

**Answer:** (a) Customers prefer cheaper products
**Solution:** The claim about "cheaper" being a benefit assumes customers care about price.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

---

### Q4. Statement: "Smoking is injurious to health." Conclusion: I. Smokers should quit. II. Non-smokers are healthy.
(a) Only I follows (b) Only II follows (c) Both follow (d) Neither follows

**Answer:** (a) Only I follows
**Solution:** "Injurious to health" implies smokers should quit. Non-smokers being healthy doesn't follow (other factors).
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

### Q5. Course of Action: "There has been a sudden increase in dengue cases in the city."
(a) The city should be closed (b) Authorities should conduct awareness campaigns and fogging
(c) People should leave the city (d) Dengue should be banned

**Answer:** (b) Authorities should conduct awareness campaigns and fogging
**Solution:** Practical action addressing the problem.
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

---

### Q6. Data Sufficiency: What is the value of X?
I. X² = 25
II. X > 0
(a) I alone is sufficient (b) II alone is sufficient (c) Both together are sufficient (d) Either alone is sufficient

**Answer:** (a) I alone is sufficient (almost), or (c) with II
**Solution:** I gives X = ±5. II alone gives no info. Together X = 5. **Answer: (c) Both together are sufficient.**
**Time Target:** 30 sec |

### Q7. Statement: "India won the cricket match against Australia." Assumption:
(a) Cricket matches are important
(b) India played well (c) Australia played poorly (d) Cricket is popular

**Answer:** (b) India played well
**Solution:** Winning implies playing well.
**Time Target:** 15 sec |

### Q8. Statement: "He is the best player in the team." Conclusion: I. He plays well. II. Others in the team don't play as well.
(a) Only I follows (b) Only II follows (c) Both follow (d) Neither follows

**Answer:** (c) Both follow
**Solution:** "Best" implies plays well AND others play worse.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

---

### Q9. Course of Action: "Heavy traffic congestion on the main road during peak hours."
(a) Ban all vehicles (b) Build flyovers or implement traffic management
(c) Close the road (d) Ignore the problem

**Answer:** (b) Build flyovers or implement traffic management
**Solution:** Practical, addresses the root cause.
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

---

### Q10. Data Sufficiency: Is A > B?
I. A = 10
II. B = 5
(a) I alone is sufficient (b) II alone is sufficient (c) Both together are sufficient (d) Cannot be determined

**Answer:** (c) Both together are sufficient
**Solution:** I alone: don't know B. II alone: don't know A. Together: 10 > 5 ✓.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

---

### Q11. Statement: "All employees must complete the training within a month." Conclusion: I. Training is important. II. Some employees may not complete.
(a) Only I follows (b) Only II follows (c) Both follow (d) Neither follows

**Answer:** (a) Only I follows
**Solution:** "Must complete" implies training is important. The "may not" is speculative, doesn't follow.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

---

### Q12. Statement: "The price of vegetables has increased due to heavy rains." Assumption:
(a) Heavy rains damage crops
(b) Vegetables are essential
(c) Rains are frequent (d) Prices fluctuate

**Answer:** (a) Heavy rains damage crops
**Solution:** The statement assumes rains damage crops, reducing supply and increasing price.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

---

### Q13. Course of Action: "Many school children are not attending classes regularly."
(a) Schools should be closed (b) Authorities should investigate causes and take corrective action
(c) Children should be punished (d) Teachers should be fired

**Answer:** (b) Authorities should investigate causes and take corrective action
**Solution:** Practical and addresses the problem.
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

---

### Q14. Data Sufficiency: What is the age of Ravi?
I. Ravi is 5 years older than Sita.
II. Sita is 20 years old.
(a) I alone is sufficient (b) II alone is sufficient (c) Both together are sufficient (d) Cannot be determined

**Answer:** (c) Both together are sufficient
**Solution:** Together: Ravi = 25. Either alone doesn't give Ravi's age.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

---

### Q15. Statement: "Private schools are better than government schools." Conclusion: I. Government schools are not good. II. All private schools are good.
(a) Only I follows (b) Only II follows (c) Both follow (d) Neither follows

**Answer:** (a) Only I follows
**Solution:** Comparison implies gov schools are not as good. "All private" is too strong.
**Time Target:** 30 sec |
