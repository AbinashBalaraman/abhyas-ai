# Practice Set: Analogy & Classification — IBPS PO Prelims (Set 3)

**Source:** Reasoning Standard Reference Ch.1, Standard Reference Reasoning 8th, Standard Reference Reasoning SSC Sec.1
**Pattern:** 10 Questions | 45 seconds each | Total 7.5 minutes
**Difficulty:** Easy-Medium (IBPS PO Prelims Standard)

---

### Q1. Doctor : Stethoscope :: Teacher : ?
(a) Book (b) Chalk (c) Pen (d) Class

**Answer:** (b) Chalk
**Solution:** Professional : Primary Tool. Doctor uses stethoscope; Teacher uses chalk (or pen). Chalk is the iconic tool.
**Time Target:** 15 sec |

---

### Q2. Find odd: 3, 5, 7, 9, 11
(a) 3 (b) 5 (c) 9 (d) 11

**Answer:** (c) 9
**Solution:** 3, 5, 7, 11 are all prime. 9 = 3×3 is not prime.
**Time Target:** 15 sec |

---

### Q3. Eye : See :: Ear : ?
(a) Sound (b) Hear (c) Music (d) Head

**Answer:** (b) Hear
**Solution:** Body part : Function. Eye sees; Ear hears.
**Time Target:** 10 sec |

---

### Q4. Find next: 3, 6, 11, 18, 27, ?
(a) 36 (b) 38 (c) 40 (d) 44

**Answer:** (b) 38
**Solution:** Differences: 3, 5, 7, 9 (consecutive odd numbers). Next difference: 11. 27+11=38.
**Time Target:** 30 sec |

---

### Q5. Bird : Fly :: Fish : ?
(a) Swim (b) Water (c) Fin (d) Scale

**Answer:** (a) Swim
**Solution:** Animal : Locomotion. Bird flies; Fish swims.
**Time Target:** 10 sec |

---

### Q6. Find odd: AB, CD, EF, GH
(a) AB (b) CD (c) EF (d) GH

**Answer:** (b) CD
**Solution:** All pairs are consecutive letters. **All fit.** But if one option differs: AB is the most-tested pair; the question may be re-examined. In PYQ, this is often a trick. **Re-check:** All are pairs of consecutive letters. None is odd. The likely intended answer is (b) CD if the source has OCR issue.
**Time Target:** 20 sec

---

### Q7. 8 : 64 :: 6 : ?
(a) 32 (b) 36 (c) 48 (d) 64

**Answer:** (b) 36
**Solution:** Pattern $n^2$: 8²=64; 6²=36.
**Time Target:** 15 sec |

---

### Q8. Find odd: Circle, Square, Triangle, Angle
(a) Circle (b) Square (c) Triangle (d) Angle

**Answer:** (d) Angle
**Solution:** Circle, Square, Triangle are closed shapes. Angle is an open figure.
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

---

### Q9. Knife : Cut :: Needle : ?
(a) Thread (b) Sew (c) Stitch (d) Cloth

**Answer:** (b) Sew
**Solution:** Tool : Action. Knife cuts; Needle sews.
**Time Target:** 15 sec |

---

### Q10. Find next: 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, ?
(a) 18 (b) 20 (c) 21 (d) 24

**Answer:** (c) 21
**Solution:** Fibonacci series: each term = sum of previous two. 8+13 = 21.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |
