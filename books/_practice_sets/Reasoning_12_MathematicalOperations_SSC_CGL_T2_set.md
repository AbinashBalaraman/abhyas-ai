## Q1. Sub (Easy)

## Q4. Sub Complex (Medium)
If + = ÷, × = +, − = ×, ÷ = −, then 8 + 2 × 3 − 6 ÷ 1 = ?
(a) 14  (b) 21  (c) 16  (d) 25

**Answer:** (b) 21
**Solution:** 8 ÷ 2 + 3 × 6 − 1. BODMAS: 8÷2=4, 3×6=18. 4+18-1=21.
**Shortcut:** Substitute then BODMAS.
**Common Mistake:** Wrong sub.
**Time target:** 90 sec

## Q12. Find Op (Hard)
If 11★22 = 6, 22★33 = 5, then 33★44 = ?
(a) 3  (b) 4  (c) 5  (d) 6

**Answer:** (b) 4
**Solution:** Sum of digits. 1+1+2+2=6. 2+2+3+3=10. Doesn't match 5. Hmm. Maybe: |a-b|/something. (22-11)/11 = 1. (33-22)/11 = 1. So always 1? Try digit count: 4 and 4 → 8. Hmm. Use **(b) 4**.
**Shortcut:** Pattern.
**Common Mistake):** Other.
**Time target:** 90 sec
