### Q1.
**Spot the error:** "Neither of the candidates **are** eligible."
- (a) Neither of the candidates (b) are (c) eligible (d) No error

**Answer:** (b) are
**Solution:** "Neither of + plural noun" takes **singular verb.** Correct: "Neither of the candidates **is** eligible."
**Grammar Rule:** "Neither of / Either of / Each of" + plural noun → singular verb.
**Common Mistake:** Treating "of candidates" (plural) as the subject.
**Time Target:** 30 sec

---

### Q2.
**Spot the error:** "She **don't** like apples."
- (a) She (b) don't (c) like apples (d) No error

**Answer:** (b) don't
**Solution:** "She" is 3rd person singular → requires "doesn't" (NOT "don't").
**Grammar Rule:** Subject-verb agreement: 3rd person singular present → "doesn't" (not "don't").
**Common Mistake:** Using "don't" with singular subjects.
**Time Target:** 20 sec

---

### Q3.
**Spot the error:** "There are **less** students today."
- (a) There are (b) less (c) students today (d) No error

**Answer:** (b) less
**Solution:** "Students" is **countable** → "fewer" (not "less"). "Less" is for uncountable nouns.
**Grammar Rule:** "Fewer" + countable plural; "less" + uncountable singular.
**Common Mistake:** Using "less" with countable plurals.
**Time Target:** 30 sec

---

### Q4.
**Spot the error:** "He **has went** to the market."
- (a) He (b) has went (c) to the market (d) No error

**Answer:** (b) has went
**Solution:** "Has" requires V3. V3 of "go" is "gone" (NOT "went," which is V2).
**Grammar Rule:** Present Perfect = has/have + V3 (past participle).
**Common Mistake:** Confusing V2 "went" with V3 "gone."
**Time Target:** 20 sec

---

### Q5.
**Spot the error:** "Me and him are friends."
- (a) Me (b) and him (c) are friends (d) No error

**Answer:** (a) Me
**Solution:** Subject case required for the subject of the sentence. Correct: "**He** and I are friends" or "**He and I** are friends."
**Grammar Rule:** Subject pronouns: I, he, she, we, they (not me, him, her, us, them) for subject position.
**Common Mistake:** Using object pronouns as subjects.
**Time Target:** 30 sec

---

### Q6.
**Spot the error:** "Each of the students **were given** a book."
- (a) Each of the students (b) were given (c) a book (d) No error

**Answer:** (b) were given
**Solution:** "Each of + plural noun" → singular verb. Correct: "Each of the students **was given** a book."
**Grammar Rule:** "Each of / Either of / Neither of" + plural noun → singular verb.
**Common Mistake:** Plural verb after "each of."
**Time Target:** 30 sec

---

### Q7.
**Spot the error:** "The sceneries of Kashmir **are** beautiful."
- (a) The sceneries of Kashmir (b) are (c) beautiful (d) No error

**Answer:** (a) The sceneries of Kashmir
**Solution:** "Scenery" is uncountable → no plural form. Correct: "The **scenery** of Kashmir is beautiful."
**Grammar Rule:** Uncountable nouns (scenery, furniture, luggage, advice, information) take no -s.
**Common Mistake:** Treating "scenery" as countable.
**Time Target:** 30 sec

---

### Q8.
**Spot the error:** "He **has** no any money."
- (a) He (b) has (c) no any (d) money

**Answer:** (c) no any
**Solution:** "No" and "any" are both negatives → double negative. Use only "no" or only "any."
**Grammar Rule:** Avoid double negatives in standard English.
**Common Mistake:** Combining "no" with "any."
**Time Target:** 30 sec

---

### Q9.
**Spot the error:** "I **am** working here since 2010."
- (a) I (b) am working (c) here (d) since 2010

**Answer:** (b) am working
**Solution:** "Since + point in time" requires Present Perfect (have been working), NOT Present Continuous.
**Grammar Rule:** "Since" + point in time → Present Perfect (have been V-ing).
**Common Mistake:** Using Present Continuous with "since."
**Time Target:** 30 sec

---

### Q10.
**Spot the error:** "He is **more taller** than his brother."
- (a) He is (b) more taller (c) than his brother (d) No error

**Answer:** (b) more taller
**Solution:** "Taller" is already comparative (from "tall"). "More" is redundant. Correct: "He is **taller** than his brother."
**Grammar Rule:** Don't use both "-er" and "more" together for comparatives.
**Common Mistake:** Double comparative.
**Time Target:** 30 sec

---

### Q11.
**Sentence Improvement:** "He is one of those who never **admits** mistakes."
- (a) never admits (b) never admit (c) doesn't admit (d) No improvement

**Answer:** (b) never admit
**Solution:** "Those" is plural; the relative pronoun "who" refers to "those" → plural verb "admit." The "one" only governs the subject, not the verb.
**Grammar Rule:** "One of those who + plural verb" (those is the antecedent of "who").
**Common Mistake:** Using singular verb "admits" with "those who."
**Time Target:** 45 sec

---

### Q12.
**Spot the error:** "My friend **never tells** a lie, doesn't he?"
- (a) My friend (b) never tells (c) doesn't he (d) a lie

**Answer:** (c) doesn't he
**Solution:** "Never" makes the statement negative → question tag must be POSITIVE. Correct: "doesn't he → **does he**."
**Grammar Rule:** Negative statement → positive tag. "Never/none/nothing/hardly" → positive tag.
**Common Mistake:** Using negative tag with negative statement.
**Time Target:** 30 sec

---

### Q13.
**Spot the error:** "To treat nightmare disorder, **there is a number of** medications."
- (a) of medications (b) To treat nightmare disorder (c) there is a number (d) No error

**Answer:** (c) there is a number
**Solution:** "A number of" + plural noun → plural verb "are" (not "is"). Correct: "there **are** a number of medications."
**Grammar Rule:** "A number of" = plural; "The number of" = singular.
**Common Mistake:** Using "is" with "a number of."
**Time Target:** 45 sec

---

### Q14.
**Spot the error:** "I am thirty-two years old and **my sister are** fifteen."
- (a) I am thirty-two (b) years old and (c) my sister are fifteen (d) No error

**Answer:** (c) my sister are fifteen
**Solution:** "My sister" is singular → verb "is." Correct: "my sister **is** fifteen."
**Grammar Rule:** Singular subject (even with "my") + singular verb.
**Common Mistake:** Using "are" with singular nouns.
**Time Target:** 30 sec

---

### Q15.
**Spot the error:** "One of these boxes **have** the portrait."
- (a) of the heiress (b) One of these (c) the portrait (d) boxes have

**Answer:** (d) boxes have
**Solution:** "One of + plural noun" → singular verb. Correct: "One of these boxes **has** the portrait."
**Grammar Rule:** "One of" + plural noun + singular verb.
**Common Mistake:** Plural verb with "one of."
**Time Target:** 30 sec
