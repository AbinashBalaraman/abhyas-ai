# Practice Set: Statement, Conclusion, Assumption, Data Sufficiency — SBI PO Prelims (Set 4)

**Source:** Standard Reference Reasoning SSC Ch.10, Standard Reference Reasoning 8th Ch.7 & Ch.8
**Pattern:** 10 Questions | 45 seconds each | Total 7.5 minutes
**Difficulty:** Easy-Medium (SBI PO Prelims Standard)

---

### Q1. Statement: "The bank has reduced interest rates on home loans." Assumption:
(a) People will take more home loans
(b) Bank wants to lend more
(c) Interest rates were high (d) Housing is expensive

**Answer:** (a) People will take more home loans
**Solution:** The reduction assumes it will boost demand.
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

---

### Q2. Statement: "The government has launched a scheme to provide free education to all girls." Conclusion: I. Girls' education is important. II. All girls will be educated.
(a) Only I follows (b) Only II follows (c) Both follow (d) Neither follows

**Answer:** (a) Only I follows
**Solution:** The scheme implies girls' education matters. "All will be educated" is too strong.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

---

### Q3. Data Sufficiency: What is the profit percentage?
I. Cost price = Rs. 100.
II. Selling price = Rs. 120.
(a) I alone is sufficient (b) II alone is sufficient (c) Both together are sufficient (d) Cannot be determined

**Answer:** (c) Both together are sufficient
**Solution:** Together: Profit = 20%, 20%.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

---

### Q4. Course of Action: "Air pollution in Delhi has reached dangerous levels."
(a) Ban all vehicles (b) Implement odd-even scheme and promote public transport
(c) Move Delhi to another state (d) Plant trees in summer

**Answer:** (b) Implement odd-even scheme and promote public transport
**Solution:** Practical, addresses vehicular pollution.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

---

### Q5. Statement: "A stitch in time saves nine." Conclusion: I. Timely action prevents bigger problems. II. We should not delay.
(a) Only I follows (b) Only II follows (c) Both follow (d) Neither follows

**Answer:** (c) Both follow
**Solution:** The proverb's meaning encompasses both.
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

---

### Q6. Statement: "The new model of the car has more features than the previous one." Assumption:
(a) Customers want more features
(b) The car is more expensive
(c) Old model is outdated (d) Features matter

**Answer:** (a) Customers want more features
**Solution:** Adding features assumes customer demand.
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

---

### Q7. Data Sufficiency: In which year was A born?
I. A is 25 years old now.
II. The current year is 2024.
(a) I alone is sufficient (b) II alone is sufficient (c) Both together are sufficient (d) Cannot be determined

**Answer:** (c) Both together are sufficient
**Solution:** Together: A born in 1999.
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

---

### Q8. Statement: "The library is open from 9 AM to 9 PM every day." Conclusion: I. The library is closed on holidays. II. The library has flexible hours.
(a) Only I follows (b) Only II follows (c) Both follow (d) Neither follows

**Answer:** (d) Neither follows
**Solution:** Neither conclusion is directly stated.
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

---

### Q9. Course of Action: "The crime rate in the city has increased by 30%."
(a) Lock down the city (b) Increase police patrolling and improve investigation
(c) Fine all citizens (d) Build more prisons

**Answer:** (b) Increase police patrolling and improve investigation
**Solution:** Practical.
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

---

### Q10. Data Sufficiency: How many apples did Ram buy?
I. Ram bought 5 kg of apples.
II. Each apple weighs 200 grams.
(a) I alone is sufficient (b) II alone is sufficient (c) Both together are sufficient (d) Cannot be determined

**Answer:** (c) Both together are sufficient
**Solution:** Together: 5000/200 = 25 apples.
**Time Target:** 30 sec |
