## Q1. Master gland (Easy)
The "master gland" of the human body is:
(a) Thyroid (b) Pituitary (c) Adrenal (d) Pancreas

**Answer:** (b) Pituitary
**Solution:** The pituitary gland (in the brain) controls other endocrine glands via its hormones (TSH, ACTH, FSH, LH, etc.). Hence called "master gland." Thyroid controls metabolism; adrenal → fight-or-flight; pancreas → blood sugar.
**Shortcut:** Pituitary = boss of all glands.
**Common Mistake:** Saying thyroid (it's controlled by pituitary via TSH).
**Time target:** 15 sec

## Q2. Insulin function (Easy)
Insulin hormone:
(a) Raises blood sugar (b) Lowers blood sugar (c) Causes fight-or-flight (d) Stimulates growth

**Answer:** (b) Lowers blood sugar
**Solution:** Insulin (from pancreas β-cells) lowers blood glucose by promoting its uptake into cells. Glucagon (from α-cells) raises blood sugar. Adrenaline causes fight-or-flight. Growth hormone (GH) stimulates growth.
**Shortcut:** Insulin = "IN" → sugar goes IN cells. Glucagon = "OUT" → sugar comes OUT.
**Common Mistake:** Saying raises (that's glucagon).
**Time target:** 15 sec

## Q3. Vitamin C deficiency (Easy)
Deficiency of Vitamin C causes:
(a) Beriberi (b) Scurvy (c) Rickets (d) Pellagra

**Answer:** (b) Scurvy
**Solution:** Vitamin C (ascorbic acid) deficiency → scurvy: bleeding gums, loose teeth, poor wound healing, anaemia. Vit A → night blindness. Vit B1 → Beriberi. Vit B3 → Pellagra. Vit D → Rickets (children)/Osteomalacia (adults).
**Shortcut:** Vit C → Scurvy. Source: citrus fruits.
**Common Mistake:** Confusing with other vitamin deficiencies.
**Time target:** 15 sec

## Q4. Iodine deficiency (Easy)
Deficiency of iodine causes:
(a) Anaemia (b) Goitre (c) Scurvy (d) Rickets

**Answer:** (b) Goitre
**Solution:** Iodine is essential for thyroxine (thyroid hormone). Iodine deficiency → goitre (enlarged thyroid). Common in hilly areas where soil/water is iodine-deficient. Iodised salt prevents this. Iron → anaemia. Vit C → scurvy. Vit D → rickets.
**Shortcut:** Iodine → Goitre (thyroid enlarges).
**Common Mistake:** Saying anaemia (that's iron deficiency).
**Time target:** 15 sec

## Q5. Cerebellum function (Medium)
The cerebellum in the brain is responsible for:
(a) Intelligence (b) Posture and balance (c) Breathing (d) Memory

**Answer:** (b) Posture and balance
**Solution:** Cerebellum (hindbrain) coordinates voluntary movements, posture, balance, and precision. Cerebrum (forebrain) = intelligence, memory, voluntary actions. Medulla = breathing, heartbeat. Pons = relay.
**Shortcut:** Cerebellum = balance. Cerebrum = thinking. Medulla = breathing.
**Common Mistake:** Saying intelligence (that's cerebrum).
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q6. Reflex arc (Medium)
A reflex action passes through the:
(a) Brain (b) Spinal cord (c) Heart (d) Stomach

**Answer:** (b) Spinal cord
**Solution:** Reflex arc: receptor → sensory neuron → spinal cord (interneuron) → motor neuron → effector (muscle). The signal goes through the spinal cord, not the brain — this makes reflexes very fast (no brain processing delay).
**Shortcut:** Reflex = spinal cord (fast, no brain needed).
**Common Mistake:** Saying brain (reflexes bypass the brain for speed).
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q7. Phototropism (Medium)
The plant hormone responsible for phototropism is:
(a) Gibberellin (b) Auxin (c) Cytokinin (d) Ethylene

**Answer:** (b) Auxin
**Solution:** Auxin (esp. IAA, indole-3-acetic acid) accumulates on the shaded side of a plant stem, causing those cells to elongate → stem bends toward light. Gibberellin = stem elongation, seed germination. Cytokinin = cell division. Ethylene = fruit ripening.
**Shortcut:** Auxin → phototropism. Ethylene → ripening.
**Common Mistake:** Saying gibberellin (also promotes growth but not phototropism).
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q8. Adrenaline function (Medium — Trap)
The "fight or flight" hormone is:
(a) Insulin (b) Thyroxine (c) Adrenaline (d) Oestrogen

**Answer:** (c) Adrenaline
**Solution:** Adrenaline (epinephrine) from adrenal medulla prepares the body for emergency: increases heart rate, blood pressure, blood sugar, breathing rate, dilates pupils. Insulin → blood sugar down. Thyroxine → metabolism. Oestrogen → female characteristics.
**Shortcut:** Adrenaline = alarm (fight/flight).
**Common Mistake:** Saying insulin (that lowers blood sugar).
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q9. AIDS pathogen (Medium)
AIDS is caused by:
(a) Bacteria (b) Virus (HIV) (c) Fungus (d) Protozoa

**Answer:** (b) Virus (HIV)
**Solution:** AIDS (Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome) is caused by HIV (Human Immunodeficiency Virus). The virus attacks CD4+ T cells (helper T cells), weakening the immune system. Spread via body fluids (sexual contact, blood transfusion, shared needles). No cure; ART can manage.
**Shortcut:** AIDS = HIV virus (attacks immune system).
**Common Mistake:** Saying bacteria (HIV is a virus, not bacteria).
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q10. Beriberi cause (Medium)
Beriberi is caused by deficiency of:
(a) Vitamin A (b) Vitamin B1 (Thiamine) (c) Vitamin C (d) Vitamin D

**Answer:** (b) Vitamin B1 (Thiamine)
**Solution:** Vitamin B1 (Thiamine) deficiency → Beriberi (weak muscles, nerve damage, heart issues). Vit A → night blindness. Vit C → scurvy. Vit D → rickets. B1 is found in whole grains, nuts, seeds.
**Shortcut:** B1 → Beriberi (B1-Beriberi, easy to remember).
**Common Mistake:** Confusing with other vitamins.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q11. TB pathogen (Medium)
Tuberculosis (TB) is caused by:
(a) Virus (b) Bacterium (Mycobacterium tuberculosis) (c) Fungus (d) Protozoa

**Answer:** (b) Bacterium (Mycobacterium tuberculosis)
**Solution:** TB is caused by Mycobacterium tuberculosis (a bacterium, not virus). Spread through droplets (cough/sneeze). Symptoms: persistent cough, weight loss, night sweats. Vaccine: BCG (Bacillus Calmette-Guérin).
**Shortcut:** TB = bacterium. AIDS/HIV = virus. Malaria = protozoa.
**Common Mistake:** Saying virus (TB is bacterial).
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q12. Rickets (Medium — Trap)
Rickets in children is caused by deficiency of:
(a) Vitamin A (b) Vitamin B (c) Vitamin C (d) Vitamin D

**Answer:** (d) Vitamin D
**Solution:** Vitamin D deficiency → rickets (children) or osteomalacia (adults). Vitamin D helps Ca absorption from the gut. Source: sunlight (skin synthesizes from cholesterol), fish, egg yolk, fortified milk. Vit A → night blindness. Vit B → Beriberi. Vit C → scurvy.
**Shortcut:** Vit D → rickets (think: "D for Development of bones").
**Common Mistake:** Confusing with Vitamin A (that causes night blindness).
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q13. Smallest bone (Hard)
The smallest bone in the human body is:
(a) Femur (b) Tibia (c) Stapes (d) Patella

**Answer:** (c) Stapes
**Solution:** Stapes (in the middle ear) is the smallest bone (~3 mm). Femur (thigh) is the longest and strongest bone. Tibia = shin bone. Patella = kneecap. The ear has 3 tiny bones: malleus (hammer), incus (anvil), stapes (stirrup).
**Shortcut:** Stapes = smallest (in ear). Femur = longest (thigh).
**Common Mistake:** Saying femur (that's largest, not smallest).
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q14. Number of bones in adult (Hard)
The adult human skeleton has:
(a) 206 bones (b) 270 bones (c) 300 bones (d) 350 bones

**Answer:** (a) 206 bones
**Solution:** Adult human skeleton has 206 bones (after many of the ~270 bones in newborns fuse together during growth). Babies have more bones; some fuse with age. The axial skeleton (skull, spine, ribs) = 80 bones; appendicular (limbs, girdles) = 126 bones.
**Shortcut:** Adult = 206 bones. Newborn = 270 bones (some fuse later).
**Common Mistake:** Saying 270 (that's newborn count).
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q15. Largest organ (Hard)
The largest organ in the human body is:
(a) Liver (b) Brain (c) Skin (d) Heart

**Answer:** (c) Skin
**Solution:** Skin is the largest organ (~2 m² surface area, ~3-4 kg). Largest internal organ = liver (largest gland too). The brain is the most complex organ. The heart is a muscular pump about the size of a fist.
**Shortcut:** Skin = largest external organ. Liver = largest internal organ/gland.
**Common Mistake:** Saying liver (that's largest internal organ; skin is overall largest).
**Time target:** 30 sec
