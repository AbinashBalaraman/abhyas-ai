### Q1. The highest civilian award of India is:
(a) Padma Vibhushan (b) Padma Bhushan (c) Padma Shri (d) Bharat Ratna

**Answer: (d) Bharat Ratna**

**Solution:** Bharat Ratna ("Jewel of India") is the highest civilian award of India, instituted on 2 January 1954. It is awarded for exceptional service in any field (art, literature, science, sports, public service). The first recipients (1954) were C. Rajagopalachari, S. Radhakrishnan, and C.V. Raman. Sachin Tendulkar (2014) is the first sportsperson to receive it.

### Q2. The first Indian to win the Nobel Prize was:
(a) C.V. Raman (b) Rabindranath Tagore (c) Mother Teresa (d) Amartya Sen

**Answer: (b) Rabindranath Tagore**

**Solution:** Rabindranath Tagore won the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1913 for his collection of poems "Gitanjali" — the first non-European to receive the Nobel. C.V. Raman won the Nobel in Physics in 1930 for the Raman Effect. Mother Teresa won Peace in 1979. Amartya Sen won Economics in 1998. Kailash Satyarthi won Peace in 2014. Abhijit Banerjee won Economics in 2019.

### Q3. The Param Vir Chakra is India's highest:
(a) Civilian Award (b) Wartime Military Gallantry Award (c) Peacetime Gallantry Award (d) Literary Award

**Answer: (b) Wartime Military Gallantry Award**

**Solution:** Param Vir Chakra (Pranavir Chakra) is India's highest wartime military gallantry award. Medal is bronze; replica has a monthly pension of ₹10,000. First recipient was Major Somnath Sharma (1947-48 Kashmir). 21 have been awarded. Ashoka Chakra is the highest peacetime gallantry award. Bharat Ratna is the highest civilian award.

### Q4. The National Anthem of India was written by:
(a) Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay (b) Rabindranath Tagore (c) Sarojini Naidu (d) Mahatma Gandhi

**Answer: (b) Rabindranath Tagore**

**Solution:** "Jana Gana Mana" (National Anthem) was composed by Rabindranath Tagore in 1911 (adopted 1950). "Vande Mataram" (National Song) was written by Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay in 1870 (from "Anandamath"). The duration of the National Anthem is approximately 52 seconds. National Calendar is based on Saka Era (Chaitra as 1st month).

### Q5. The National Game of India is:
(a) Cricket (b) Hockey (c) Kabaddi (d) Football

**Answer: (b) Hockey**

**Solution:** Although cricket is the most popular sport in India, **Hockey** is the official National Game of India. India has won 8 Olympic gold medals in field hockey (1928-1980). Major Dhyan Chand is the legendary Indian hockey player, called the "Wizard of Hockey." The National Sport Day is celebrated on 29 August (birth anniversary of Major Dhyan Chand).

### Q6. The National Flower of India is:
(a) Rose (b) Lotus (c) Jasmine (d) Marigold

**Answer: (b) Lotus**

**Solution:** The National Flower of India is the Lotus (Nelumbo nucifera). It symbolizes purity, beauty, and India's cultural heritage. The National Animal is the Bengal Tiger; National Bird is the Indian Peacock; National Tree is the Banyan; National Fruit is Mango; National River is the Ganga; National Sport is Hockey.

### Q7. The "Booker Prize" is awarded for:
(a) Best Science Book (b) Best Novel in English (c) Best Children's Book (d) Best Sports Biography

**Answer: (b) Best Novel in English**

**Solution:** The Booker Prize (Man Booker) is awarded annually for the best novel in English. Indian recipients include Arundhati Roy (1997, The God of Small Things), Standard Reference Desai (2006, The Inheritance of Loss), Aravind Adiga (2008, The White Tiger). In 2025, Karnataka-based writer Banu Mushtaq won for her short story anthology "Heart Lamp" — first Kannada language work.

### Q8. The "Magsaysay Award" is also known as:
(a) Nobel of Economics (b) Nobel of Literature (c) Asia's Nobel Prize (d) Nobel of Peace

**Answer: (c) Asia's Nobel Prize**

**Solution:** The Ramon Magsaysay Award is called "Asia's Nobel Prize." It is named after Ramon Magsaysay, the 7th President of the Philippines. It is awarded for various fields including public service, community leadership, journalism, literature, etc. Indian recipients include Mother Teresa, Vinoba Bhave, Jayaprakash Narayan, Standard Reference Bedi, Arvind Kejriwal.

### Q9. The "Dadasaheb Phalke Award" is given for contribution to:
(a) Literature (b) Sports (c) Cinema (d) Science

**Answer: (c) Cinema**

**Solution:** Dadasaheb Phalke Award is the highest award in Indian cinema, instituted in 1969. It is presented annually at the National Film Awards ceremony. It includes a Golden Lotus (Swarna Kamal), a cash prize, and a shawl. First recipient was Devika Rani (1969). Recent recipients include Mithun Chakraborty (2024), Waheeda Rehman (2021). The award is named after Dhundiraj Govind Phalke, the father of Indian cinema.

### Q10. The "Wimbledon" is associated with which sport?
(a) Football (b) Cricket (c) Tennis (d) Golf

**Answer: (c) Tennis**

**Solution:** Wimbledon is the oldest tennis tournament in the world, held annually in London, UK since 1877. It is one of the four Grand Slam tennis events. The other three are Australian Open (Melbourne), French Open (Roland Garros, Paris), and US Open (New York). The Wimbledon trophy for men is the Challenge Cup; for women, the Venus Rosewater Dish.

### Q11. "Bharat Ratna" is awarded for exceptional service in:
(a) Only military (b) Only science (c) Only arts (d) Any field

**Answer: (d) Any field**

**Solution:** Bharat Ratna is awarded for exceptional service in any field (art, literature, science, sports, public service). It carries no monetary grant. The medal is shaped like a peepal leaf, made of platinum. Recipients have included scientists (C.V. Raman, M.S. Swaminathan), politicians (Nehru, Patel), sportspersons (Sachin Tendulkar), and artists (M.S. Subbulakshmi).

### Q12. The "Khel Ratna Award" was renamed in 2021 as:
(a) Arjuna Award (b) Dronacharya Award (c) Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award (d) Dhyan Chand Award

**Answer: (c) Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award**

**Solution:** The Khel Ratna Award (instituted 1991-92) was renamed Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award in 2021. It is India's highest sporting honour. Recipients include Viswanathan Anand (chess), Abhinav Bindra (shooting), Mary Kom (boxing), Neeraj Chopra (javelin). The award carries ₹25 lakh cash prize, a medal, and a citation.

### Q13. The FIFA World Cup 2022 was won by:
(a) Brazil (b) Germany (c) Argentina (d) France

**Answer: (c) Argentina**

**Solution:** Argentina won the FIFA World Cup 2022 in Qatar, defeating France in the final (3-3, 4-2 on penalties). It was Lionel Messi's first World Cup win and his fifth. Argentina's previous World Cup win was in 1986 (Diego Maradona). France was the defending champion from 2018. Qatar 2022 was the first World Cup held in the Arab world.
