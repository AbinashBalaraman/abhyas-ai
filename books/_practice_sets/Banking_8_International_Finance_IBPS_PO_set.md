### Q1. The BIS headquarters is in:
(a) Paris (b) London (c) Basel (d) Zurich

**Answer: (c) Basel**

**Solution:** The BIS (Bank for International Settlements) headquarters is in Basel, Switzerland. BIS was established on 17 May 1930. It is the world's oldest international financial organisation. BIS is the "bank for central banks" — providing banking services to central banks and international financial institutions. BIS hosts the BCBS (Basel Committee on Banking Supervision), which issues the Basel Accords. BIS fosters international monetary and financial cooperation. The current BIS General Manager is Agustín Carstens. The Basel Accords (I, II, III, "Basel IV") shape global banking regulation.

### Q2. The SEBI (India) is the regulator for:
(a) Banking (b) Insurance (c) Securities Market (d) Pension

**Answer: (c) Securities Market**

**Solution:** SEBI (Securities and Exchange Board of India) regulates the securities market in India. SEBI was constituted in 1988 (non-statutory) and became statutory in 1992. SEBI's headquarters is in Mumbai. SEBI regulates stock exchanges (BSE, NSE), brokers, sub-brokers, mutual funds, and other intermediaries. SEBI's mission is to protect investor interests and promote the development of the securities market. RBI regulates banking. IRDAI regulates insurance. PFRDA regulates pension. SEBI is also called the "watchdog of the Indian securities market".

### Q3. The BSE was established in:
(a) 1850 (b) 1875 (c) 1900 (d) 1925

**Answer: (b) 1875**

**Solution:** BSE (Bombay Stock Exchange) was established in 1875. It is Asia's oldest stock exchange. BSE's benchmark index is Sensex (Sensitive Index) which comprises 30 stocks. BSE has over 5,500 listed companies. BSE was initially established as "The Native Share & Stock Brokers' Association". BSE is headquartered in Mumbai. BSE is regulated by SEBI. BSE obtained permanent recognition in 1956. BSE also has products like currency derivatives and interest rate derivatives. BSE has a trading platform called BOLT (BSE OnLine Trading).

### Q4. The SEBI was constituted in:
(a) 1988 (b) 1990 (c) 1992 (d) 1995

**Answer: (a) 1988**

**Solution:** SEBI was constituted as a non-statutory body on 12 April 1988 (Government resolution). It became a statutory body under the SEBI Act 1992 (provisions came into force on 30 January 1992). SEBI's preamble is "to protect the interests of investors in securities and to promote the development of, and to regulate the securities market". SEBI regulates stock exchanges, brokers, sub-brokers, mutual funds, and other intermediaries. SEBI's major regulations include ICDR (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements), LODR (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements), PIT (Prohibition of Insider Trading), SAST (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers).

### Q5. The "D-SIB" is associated with:
(a) A government scheme (b) A type of bank (c) An insurance product (d) A market regulator

**Answer: (b) A type of bank**

**Solution:** D-SIB (Domestic Systemically Important Bank) is a classification of banks by RBI based on their size, interconnectedness, and complexity. D-SIBs are banks whose failure would have a significant impact on the financial system. RBI's D-SIB framework is in line with the FSB (Financial Stability Board) guidelines. D-SIBs are required to maintain additional capital buffers (higher capital adequacy ratio). SBI, HDFC Bank, and ICICI Bank are some of the D-SIBs in India. D-SIBs are sometimes called "too big to fail" banks. The D-SIB framework helps to reduce the systemic risk in the banking system.

### Q6. The "World Bank" was founded in:
(a) 1940 (b) 1944 (c) 1950 (d) 1960

**Answer: (b) 1944**

**Solution:** The World Bank was founded in 1944 at the Bretton Woods Conference (along with the IMF). The World Bank Group has 5 institutions: IBRD, IDA, IFC, MIGA, ICSID. The World Bank's headquarters is in Washington, D.C. The current World Bank President is Ajay Banga (India-American, since June 2023). The World Bank provides loans, credits, and grants to developing countries. The World Bank's initial purpose was to help rebuild Europe after WWII. Now it focuses on poverty reduction and sustainable development. India is a member and recipient of World Bank funding.

### Q7. FEMA replaced which earlier act?
(a) FERA (b) RBI Act (c) BR Act (d) IBC

**Answer: (a) FERA**

**Solution:** FEMA (Foreign Exchange Management Act) 1999 replaced FERA (Foreign Exchange Regulation Act) 1973. FEMA is more liberal than FERA — FERA was very restrictive and had criminal penalties, while FEMA focuses on management of foreign exchange with civil penalties. FEMA is administered by RBI (Department of External Investments and Operations). FEMA applies to all foreign exchange transactions in India. The penalty for contravention can be up to 3x the sum involved. The Adjudicating Authority for serious cases is the Enforcement Directorate. FEMA was a major reform of the foreign exchange framework.

**Time Target:** 30s | **Chapter Reference:** Banking/08_International_Finance.md, p.262-270 | **VERIFY:** No

### Q8. The World Trade Organization (WTO) was established on:
(a) 1 January 1995 (b) 1 January 1948 (c) 1 January 1991 (d) 15 August 1947

**Answer: (a) 1 January 1995**

**Solution:** The World Trade Organization (WTO) was established on 1 January 1995. WTO is the successor to GATT (General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade, 1948). WTO's headquarters is in Geneva, Switzerland. WTO has 164 members. The current Director-General is Ngozi Okonjo-Iweala. WTO deals with global rules of trade between nations. The main functions: administering WTO trade agreements, providing a forum for trade negotiations, handling trade disputes, monitoring national trade policies. India is a founding member of WTO (1995). The GATT rounds of negotiations shaped global trade.

**Time Target:** 30s | **Chapter Reference:** Banking/08_International_Finance.md, p.165-172 | **VERIFY:** No

### Q9. The BSE Sensex has how many stocks?
(a) 30 (b) 50 (c) 100 (d) 500

**Answer: (a) 30**

**Solution:** The BSE Sensex has 30 stocks. It is the benchmark index of BSE (Bombay Stock Exchange), which was established in 1875. Sensex was first published in 1986. The 30 stocks in Sensex represent major sectors of the Indian economy. Sensex is a free-float market capitalization-weighted index. The base period for Sensex is 1978-79 = 100. Nifty 50 is the benchmark of NSE with 50 stocks. Other BSE indices: BSE 100, BSE 200, BSE 500, Bankex (banking). Sensex level crossed 80,000 in 2024 and 90,000+ in 2025.
