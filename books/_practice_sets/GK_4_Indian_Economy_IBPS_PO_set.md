### Q1. The 12th Five Year Plan covered which period?
(a) 2002-07 (b) 2007-12 (c) 2012-17 (d) 2017-22

**Answer: (c) 2012-17**

**Solution:** The 12th Five Year Plan covered 2012-17 and was the last Five Year Plan of India. Its theme was "Faster, Sustainable, and Inclusive Growth". The 11th Plan (2007-12) was "Faster, more inclusive growth". The 10th Plan (2002-07) had the theme "India Shining" with 8% growth target. After the 12th Plan, the Planning Commission was dissolved (Jan 2015) and replaced by NITI Aayog. There were 12 Five Year Plans in total (1st: 1951-56, 12th: 2012-17). Plan Holidays: 1966-69 and 1990-92.

### Q2. The corporate tax rate for new manufacturing companies in India is:
(a) 22% (b) 15% (c) 25% (d) 30%

**Answer: (b) 15%**

**Solution:** Under the Taxation Laws (Amendment) Act, 2019, the corporate tax for new manufacturing companies incorporated after 1 October 2019 was reduced to 15% (effective rate ~17.01% inclusive of surcharge and cess). Existing companies had a reduced rate of 22% (without exemptions). This was part of the "Make in India" initiative. CBDT (Central Board of Direct Taxes) administers direct taxes. CBIC (Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs) administers indirect taxes including GST.

### Q3. India overtook China as the world's most populous country in:
(a) 2020 (b) 2021 (c) April 2023 (d) 2024

**Answer: (c) April 2023**

**Solution:** India overtook China as the world's most populous country in April 2023, with a population of ~1.43 billion. China had been the most populous country for decades. This makes India crucial in terms of demographic dividend, market size, and labor. India's median age is around 28 years. India's HDI rank in 2023-24 was 134/193 (HDI value 0.644 - medium human development). India's per capita income (FY24-25) is approximately ₹1.99 lakh.

### Q4. The SARFAESI Act was enacted in:
(a) 1999 (b) 2002 (c) 2005 (d) 2016

**Answer: (b) 2002**

**Solution:** The SARFAESI Act (Securitisation and Reconstruction of Financial Assets and Enforcement of Security Interest Act) was enacted in 2002. It allows banks and financial institutions to recover NPAs (Non-Performing Assets) through three mechanisms: Securitisation, Asset Reconstruction (via ARCs), and Enforcement of Security Interest without court intervention. The Act requires 60-day notice to the borrower. It excludes unsecured loans and certain agricultural lands. The minimum loan threshold for SARFAESI is ₹2 lakh (raised to ₹20 lakh in some cases).

**Time Target:** 30s | **Chapter Reference:** GK/04_Indian_Economy.md, p.636, p.646 | **VERIFY:** No

### Q5. PMLA (Prevention of Money Laundering Act) was enacted in:
(a) 1999 (b) 2002 (c) 2005 (d) 2016

**Answer: (b) 2002**

**Solution:** The Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA) was enacted in 2002 and amended in 2005, 2009, and 2012. It defines the offence of money laundering under Section 3, with punishment of rigorous imprisonment for 3-7 years and fine (Section 4). The Adjudicating Authority is the PMLA Adjudicating Authority. The Appellate Tribunal is the PMLA Appellate Tribunal. The FIU-IND (Financial Intelligence Unit-India) was set up in 2004 under the Department of Revenue, Ministry of Finance. ED (Enforcement Directorate) investigates PMLA cases.

**Time Target:** 30s | **Chapter Reference:** GK/04_Indian_Economy.md, p.144-149, p.635 | **VERIFY:** No

### Q6. CBDT administers which type of tax in India?
(a) Indirect tax (b) Direct tax (c) GST (d) Customs duty

**Answer: (b) Direct tax**

**Solution:** CBDT (Central Board of Direct Taxes) administers Direct Taxes (Income Tax, Corporate Tax) under the Department of Revenue, Ministry of Finance. CBDT headquarters is in Delhi. CBIC (Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs) administers indirect taxes including GST, Customs Duty, and Excise Duty. The Income Tax Act was enacted in 1961. The PAN (Permanent Account Number) is issued by CBDT for assesses above the threshold. Direct taxes contribute about 50% of total tax revenue in India.

**Time Target:** 30s | **Chapter Reference:** GK/04_Indian_Economy.md, p.209-213, p.621 | **VERIFY:** No

### Q7. The HDI (Human Development Index) was created by:
(a) World Bank (b) IMF (c) UNDP (d) WHO

**Answer: (c) UNDP**

**Solution:** The HDI was created by the UNDP (United Nations Development Programme) and launched in 1990 by Pakistani economist Mahbub ul Haq and Indian economist Amartya Sen. It is a composite index based on three dimensions: Life expectancy, Education (Mean and Expected years of schooling), and Per capita income. India's HDI rank in 2023-24 was 134/193 (HDI value 0.644 - medium human development). HDI categories: Very High (≥0.800), High (0.700-0.799), Medium (0.550-0.699), Low (<0.550).

**Time Target:** 30s | **Chapter Reference:** GK/04_Indian_Economy.md, p.140-143, p.628 | **VERIFY:** No

### Q8. India's current account deficit (CAD) is approximately:
(a) 0.1% of GDP (b) 1% of GDP (c) 3% of GDP (d) 5% of GDP

**Answer: (b) 1% of GDP**

**Solution:** India's Current Account Deficit (CAD) is approximately 1% of GDP. The CAD is the difference between exports and imports of goods, services, and transfers. India's forex reserves are about $700 billion (4th largest in world, after China, Japan, Switzerland). The Capital Account includes FDI, FPI, ECBs, and banking capital. Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) inflows to India are approximately $80-90 billion per year. Services exports (especially IT) are a key strength of India's BoP.

**Time Target:** 30s | **Chapter Reference:** GK/04_Indian_Economy.md, p.65, p.495 | **VERIFY:** Yes - current CAD may vary

### Q9. SIDBI was established in:
(a) 1982 (b) 1990 (c) 1999 (d) 2010

**Answer: (b) 1990**

**Solution:** SIDBI (Small Industries Development Bank of India) was established on 2 April 1990 with headquarters in Lucknow. It is the principal financial institution for MSME (Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises) promotion, financing, and development. SIDBI provides refinance to banks, NBFCs, and MFIs. MUDRA Ltd (Micro Units Development & Refinance Agency) is also under SIDBI. The current Chairman is Manoj Mittal (verify). NABARD was established in 1982, EXIM Bank in 1982, and NHB in 1988.

**Time Target:** 30s | **Chapter Reference:** GK/04_Indian_Economy.md, p.629 | **VERIFY:** No

### Q10. The most recent Five Year Plan was the:
(a) 10th (b) 11th (c) 12th (d) 13th

**Answer: (c) 12th**

**Solution:** The 12th Five Year Plan (2012-17) was the last Five Year Plan of India. After it, the Planning Commission was dissolved on 1 January 2015 and replaced by NITI Aayog. The 12th Plan theme was "Faster, Sustainable, and Inclusive Growth". India then moved to a 3-year Action Agenda approach under NITI Aayog. There were 12 Five Year Plans in total. The first plan (1951-56) was based on the Harrod-Domar Model; the second (1956-61) on the Mahalanobis Model.
