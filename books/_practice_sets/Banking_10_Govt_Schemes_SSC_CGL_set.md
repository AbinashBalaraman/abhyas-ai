### Q1. PMJDY was launched on:
(a) 15 August 2014 (b) 28 August 2014 (c) 1 January 2015 (d) 26 January 2015

**Answer: (b) 28 August 2014**

**Solution:** PMJDY (Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana) was launched on 28 August 2014 by PM Narendra Modi (announced on Independence Day, 15 August 2014). The slogan is "Mera Khata, Bhagya Vidhata" (My account, fortune maker). It provides zero-balance bank accounts (BSBDA - Basic Savings Bank Deposit Account) with RuPay debit card. Features: Accident insurance up to ₹2 lakh, life insurance ₹30,000 (for accounts opened between 15 Aug 2014 - 31 Jan 2015), overdraft up to ₹10,000. Over 50 crore accounts have been opened. Guinness World Record: 15 million accounts opened on inauguration day. The scheme is part of the JAM Trinity (Jan Dhan + Aadhaar + Mobile).

### Q3. PMMY was launched in:
(a) 2014 (b) 2015 (c) 2016 (d) 2017

**Answer: (b) 2015**

**Solution:** PMMY (Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana) was launched on 8 April 2015 by PM Narendra Modi. The administrator is MUDRA Ltd (Micro Units Development & Refinance Agency). PMMY targets non-corporate, non-farm small/micro enterprises. The three categories are: Shishu (up to ₹50,000), Kishore (₹50,001 to ₹5 lakh), and Tarun (₹5,00,001 to ₹10 lakh). PMMY loans are collateral-free and available from banks, NBFCs, and MFIs. The scheme has helped millions of small entrepreneurs in India access formal credit. MUDRA Ltd provides refinance to these lenders.

### Q5. PMSBY premium is:
(a) ₹12/year (b) ₹20/year (c) ₹100/year (d) ₹330/year

**Answer: (b) ₹20/year**

**Solution:** PMSBY (Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana) premium is ₹20 per year (revised from ₹12 originally; revised on 1 June 2022). PMSBY was launched on 9 May 2015. The coverage: Accidental death and full disability: ₹2 lakh; Partial disability: ₹1 lakh. Available to savings bank account holders aged 18-70 years. The premium is auto-debited from the bank account. PMSBY is one of the 3 social security schemes launched on 9 May 2015 (the other 2 are PMJJBY and APY). PMSBY is part of India's social security framework. The scheme has provided affordable accident insurance to millions.

### Q9. APY was relaunched in:
(a) 2010 (b) 2014 (c) 2015 (d) 2016

**Answer: (c) 2015**

**Solution:** APY (Atal Pension Yojana) was relaunched (after the original Swavalamban Scheme of 2010-11) on 9 May 2015 by PM Modi in Kolkata. APY is named after former PM Atal Bihari Vajpayee. The scheme is targeted at the unorganized sector. The eligibility is 18-40 years. The pension range is ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 per month at age 60. APY had a government co-contribution of 50% of total contribution or ₹1,000 per annum (whichever is lower) for 5 years for subscribers enrolled between 1 June 2015 and 31 March 2016. APY has 7.64 crore active subscribers as of April 2026.

### Q10. SSY was launched on:
(a) 22 January 2015 (b) 28 August 2014 (c) 1 January 2015 (d) 1 April 2015

**Answer: (a) 22 January 2015**

**Solution:** SSY (Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana) was launched on 22 January 2015 by PM Modi as part of the "Beti Bachao, Beti Padhao" campaign. SSY is for the girl child below 10 years. The account is opened by the parent/guardian. The tenure is 21 years from opening. The current interest rate is 8.2% per annum (FY25-26). The maximum deposit is ₹1.5 lakh per year. SSY provides tax benefits under Section 80C on deposits; interest is tax-free. SSY is part of the government's initiative to promote girl child education and welfare.

### Q14. PM SVANidhi was launched in:
(a) 2018 (b) 2019 (c) 2020 (d) 2021

**Answer: (c) 2020**

**Solution:** PM SVANidhi (PM Street Vendor's AtmaNirbhar Nidhi) was launched on 1 June 2020 (during COVID-19). The scheme is for street vendors (urban). The loan amounts are in 3 tranches: ₹10,000 → ₹20,000 → ₹50,000. There is an interest subsidy of 7% on timely repayment. No collateral is required. The scheme aims to provide working capital to street vendors who were hit by the COVID-19 pandemic and lockdown. PM SVANidhi is part of the Aatmanirbhar Bharat Abhiyan. The scheme is implemented through banks and MFIs.
