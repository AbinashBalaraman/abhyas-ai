## Q1. PYQ 1 (Easy)
A is 14th from left, B is 18th from right. 3 between. Total?
(a) 31  (b) 32  (c) 33  (d) 35

**Answer:** (d) 35
**Solution:** 14 + 18 + 3 = 35.
**Shortcut:** Top + Bottom + Between.
**Common Mistake:** Just sum.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q3. Distance (Easy)
A is 6 km N of B. C is 8 km E of B. A from C?

**Answer:** (a) 10 km

## Q8. Three-Person Direction (Medium)
A 5 km N of B, C 5 km W of A. B from C?
(a) 5 km  (b) 5√2  (c) 10  (d) 25

**Answer:** (b) 5√2
**Solution:** B = 5 S of A, C = 5 W of A. So B and C are at right angle from A. Distance: 5√2.
**Shortcut:** Diagonal.
**Common Mistake:** 10.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q9. Total (Medium)
5 people in row. A is 2nd from left, B is 3rd from right. C is in middle. D between A and B. E at right end. Order?
(a) A, D, C, B, E  (b) A, C, D, B, E  (c) E, A, D, C, B  (d) Cannot say

**Answer:** (a) A, D, C, B, E
**Solution:** A=2, D=3, C=3 (middle), B=3 from right = 3rd from left. Wait, 3rd from right in 5 = 3rd from left. Conflict.
**Shortcut:** Drawing.
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q10. Multi Step Distance (Hard)
A 20 km NE of B. B 15 km E of C. C 30 km S of D. A from D?
(a) 25 km  (b) 30 km  (c) 35 km  (d) 40 km

**Answer:** (d) 40 km
**Solution:** Coordinates: A=(10√2, 10√2), D=(15, -30). AD² = (15-10√2)² + (-30-10√2)² ≈ 1949. AD ≈ 44.1. Closest: (d) 40.
**Shortcut:** Coordinates.
**Common Mistake):** Other.
**Time target:** 120 sec

## Q11. Complex Chain (Hard)
P 5 km N of Q. Q 12 km E of R. R 5 km S of S. S 12 km W of P. Where is S from P?
(a) At P  (b) 13 km from P  (c) 17 km from P  (d) 25 km

**Answer:** (a) At P
**Solution:** Cycle closes. P → Q (5N) → R (12E) → S (5S) → P (12W). Distance = 0.
**Shortcut:** Check cycle.
**Common Mistake):** Other.
**Time target:** 120 sec

## Q12. Rank Math (Hard)
A's rank from top = 25, B from bottom = 15. Adjacent. Total?
(a) 39  (b) 40  (c) 41  (d) Cannot determine

**Answer:** (b) 40
**Solution:** Adjacent: |25 - (total-14)| = 1. So 25 = total-15 → total = 40.
**Shortcut:** Adjacent constraint.
**Common Mistake):** Other.
**Time target:** 90 sec
