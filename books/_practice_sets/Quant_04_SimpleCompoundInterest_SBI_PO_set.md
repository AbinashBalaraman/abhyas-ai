## Q1. Basic SI (Easy)
SI on ₹20,000 at 8% for 3 years:
(a) ₹4,800  (b) ₹5,000  (c) ₹4,000  (d) ₹5,400

**Answer:** (a) ₹4,800
**Solution:** SI = 20000 × 8 × 3/100 = 4800. ✓
**Shortcut:** Direct.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting T.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q2. Basic CI (Easy)
CI on ₹5,000 at 20% for 2 years:
(a) ₹2,000  (b) ₹2,200  (c) ₹2,400  (d) ₹2,100

**Answer:** (b) ₹2,200
**Solution:** A = 5000 × 1.20² = 5000 × 1.44 = 7200. CI = 2200. ✓
**Shortcut:** 1.20² = 1.44.
**Common Mistake:** Using 1.20.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q3. Effective rate (Easy)
Effective rate when 20% compounded half-yearly:
(a) 20%  (b) 21%  (c) 22%  (d) 19%

**Answer:** (b) 21%
**Solution:** (1.10)² − 1 = 0.21 = 21%. ✓
**Shortcut:** Direct.
**Common Mistake:** Saying 20%.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q4. Doubles (Medium)
A sum doubles in 12 years at SI. R:
(a) 8%  (b) ~8.33%  (c) 10%  (d) 12%

**Answer:** (b) ~8.33%
**Solution:** R = 100/12 = 8.33%. ✓
**Shortcut:** R = 100/T for doubling in T years at SI.
**Common Mistake:** Rounding to 8%.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q5. Reverse P (Medium)
A sum of ₹1,00,000 at 10% SI for 5 years grows to:
(a) ₹1,50,000  (b) ₹1,40,000  (c) ₹1,60,000  (d) ₹1,45,000

**Answer:** (a) ₹1,50,000
**Solution:** A = 100000 + 100000·10·5/100 = 150,000. ✓
**Shortcut:** A = P + SI.
**Common Mistake:** Computing CI.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q6. CI-SI 3 yrs (Medium)
Difference CI and SI on ₹10,000 for 3 years at 10%:
(a) ₹310  (b) ₹300  (c) ₹320  (d) ₹330

**Answer:** (a) ₹310
**Solution:** 3-yr diff = P(R/100)²(3 + R/100) = 10000 × 0.01 × 3.10 = 310. ✓
**Shortcut:** Use 3-year formula.
**Common Mistake:** Using 2-year formula.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q7. Installment 2 yrs (Medium)
Sum of ₹60,000 at 10% SI for 2 years paid in 2 equal annual installments. Each:
(a) ₹33,000  (b) ₹30,000  (c) ₹35,000  (d) ₹28,000

**Answer:** (a) ₹33,000
**Solution:** I = P/(2 + R/100) = 60000/2.10 = 28,571. **Answer: 28,571** (closest: d) 28,000).
**Final Answer: ₹28,571** (closest: d)
**Shortcut:** I = P/(2 + R/100).
**Common Mistake:** Using P(200+R)/200.
**Time target:** 90 sec

## Q8. CI reverse (Hard)
P becomes 1.5x in 2 years at CI. R:
(a) ~22.5%  (b) 25%  (c) 20%  (d) 30%

**Answer:** (a) ~22.5%
**Solution:** 1.5 = (1 + R/100)². 1 + R/100 = √1.5 = 1.2247. R ≈ 22.5%. ✓
**Shortcut:** Square root of ratio.
**Common Mistake:** Confusing with simple interest.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q9. Multi-year growth (Hard)
Pop 1,00,000 grows 10%, 20%, 25% in 3 years. Final:
(a) 1,65,000  (b) 1,50,000  (c) 1,80,000  (d) 1,75,000

**Answer:** (a) 1,65,000
**Solution:** 100000 × 1.10 × 1.20 × 1.25 = 100000 × 1.65 = 165,000. ✓
**Shortcut:** Successive multipliers.
**Common Mistake:** Adding percentages.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q10. Population as CI (Hard — Trap)
Pop 80,000, declines 10% then grows 10% (in 2 years). Final:
(a) 79,200  (b) 80,000  (c) 78,000  (d) 80,800

**Answer:** (a) 79,200
**Solution:** 80000 × 0.90 × 1.10 = 80000 × 0.99 = 79,200. ✓
**Shortcut:** Same % down-up is never 0; always 1% decrease.
**Common Mistake:** Saying 80,000.
**Time target:** 60 sec
