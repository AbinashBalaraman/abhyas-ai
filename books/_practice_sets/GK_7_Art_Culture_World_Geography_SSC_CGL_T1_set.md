### Q1. How many classical dance forms are officially recognized by the Sangeet Natak Akademi in India?
(a) 6 (b) 7 (c) 8 (d) 9

**Answer: (c) 8**

**Solution:** India officially recognizes 8 classical dance forms by the Sangeet Natak Akademi: Bharatanatyam (TN), Kathak (UP), Kathakali (Kerala), Kuchipudi (AP), Odissi (Odisha), Mohiniyattam (Kerala), Manipuri (Manipur), and Sattriya (Assam, added 2000). The first 5 were recognized; Mohiniyattam, Kuchipudi, and Kathakali were added in 2000.

### Q2. Bharatanatyam is a classical dance form of which state?
(a) Kerala (b) Karnataka (c) Tamil Nadu (d) Andhra Pradesh

**Answer: (c) Tamil Nadu**

**Solution:** Bharatanatyam is the oldest classical dance form of India, originating in Tamil Nadu. It is a temple dance originally performed by Devadasis. The dance form involves balanced movements of Nritta (pure dance), Nritya (expressive), and Natya (drama). Rukmini Devi Arundale revived Bharatanatyam and founded Kalakshetra in Chennai.

### Q3. Kathak is a classical dance form associated with which state?
(a) Rajasthan (b) Uttar Pradesh (c) Bihar (d) Madhya Pradesh

**Answer: (b) Uttar Pradesh**

**Solution:** Kathak originated in North India (UP) and is associated with the Mughal court. It features ghungroo bells, spins (chakkars), and themes from Radha-Krishna. Famous gharanas (schools): Lucknow, Jaipur, Banaras, and Raigarh. Birju Maharaj is a legendary Kathak dancer. It developed through the Bhakti Movement.

### Q4. The Sitar is associated with which Indian classical music tradition?
(a) Carnatic (b) Hindustani (c) Folk (d) Both A and B

**Answer: (b) Hindustani**

**Solution:** The Sitar is associated with the Hindustani classical music tradition of North India. Pandit Ravi Shankar is the most famous sitar player globally, popularizing Indian music worldwide. Maihar Gharana (founded by Allauddin Khan, Ravi Shankar's guru) is the main sitar gharana. Veena, Mridangam, and Nadaswaram are typical Carnatic instruments.

### Q5. Which Indian music tradition uses the "72 Melakarta" system?
(a) Hindustani (b) Carnatic (c) Folk (d) Sufi

**Answer: (b) Carnatic**

**Solution:** The Carnatic music tradition of South India uses the "72 Melakarta" (parent) raga system, from which thousands of derived ragas (janya ragas) emerge. Hindustani music does not follow a strict Melakarta system. Carnatic compositions include Kriti, Varnam, Padam, and Tillana. Tyagaraja, Muthuswami Dikshitar, and Syama Sastri are the Trinity of Carnatic music.

### Q6. The "Raga" in Indian classical music refers to:
(a) A rhythmic cycle (b) A melodic framework (c) A type of instrument (d) A musical notation

**Answer: (b) A melodic framework**

**Solution:** Raga is a melodic framework in Indian classical music consisting of 5-7 notes (Swaras). A Raga has Aarohi (ascending) and Avarohi (descending) patterns. Famous ragas include Yaman, Bhairav, Kafi, Kalyan. Tala refers to the rhythmic cycle (e.g., Teentaal = 16 beats). Pakad is the signature phrase of a raga.

### Q7. The "Taj Mahal" is located in which city?
(a) Delhi (b) Jaipur (c) Agra (d) Lucknow

**Answer: (c) Agra**

**Solution:** The Taj Mahal is located in Agra, Uttar Pradesh, on the banks of the Yamuna River. It was built by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal (1632-1653). It is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the Seven Wonders of the World. It is made of white marble and combines Persian, Islamic, and Indian architectural styles.

### Q8. The "Ajanta and Ellora Caves" are located in which state?
(a) Madhya Pradesh (b) Karnataka (c) Maharashtra (d) Tamil Nadu

**Answer: (c) Maharashtra**

**Solution:** The Ajanta and Ellora Caves are located in the Aurangabad district of Maharashtra. Ajanta (30 caves, 2nd century BCE - 480 CE) is famous for Buddhist murals and sculptures. Ellora (34 caves, 600-1000 CE) has Buddhist, Hindu, and Jain caves, including the monolithic Kailasa Temple (Cave 16) carved from a single rock. Both are UNESCO World Heritage Sites.

**Time Target:** 30s | **Chapter Reference:** GK/07_Art_Culture_World_Geography.md, p.18 | **VERIFY:** No

### Q9. The "Kumbh Mela" is held at how many locations in India?
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 5

**Answer: (c) 4**

**Solution:** The Kumbh Mela is held at four locations: Prayagraj (Allahabad), Haridwar, Ujjain, and Nashik. It is based on the position of planets (Sun, Moon, Jupiter) and is the largest religious gathering in the world. The Prayagraj Kumbh is the largest. Nashik and Ujjain host the Simhastha Kumbh. It is a UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage.

### Q11. "Madhubani Painting" is traditionally associated with which state?
(a) Rajasthan (b) Gujarat (c) Bihar (d) West Bengal

**Answer: (c) Bihar**

**Solution:** Madhubani (or Mithila) Painting is a traditional folk art of Bihar. It is characterized by geometric patterns and natural dyes. Traditionally done by women on freshly plastered mud walls during festivals and weddings. Other folk arts: Warli (Maharashtra), Pattachitra (Odisha), Tanjore/Thanjavur (TN), Kalamkari (AP), Miniature (Rajasthan).

### Q13. The longest river in the world is:
(a) Amazon (b) Nile (c) Yangtze (d) Mississippi

**Answer: (b) Nile**

**Solution:** The Nile River (~6,650 km) is generally considered the longest river in the world, flowing through Egypt, Sudan, South Sudan, Ethiopia, Uganda, DR Congo, Kenya, Tanzania, Rwanda, and Burundi. It has two major tributaries: White Nile and Blue Nile. The Amazon (~6,400 km) is the largest by discharge. The Yangtze (~6,300 km) is the longest in Asia.

### Q14. The "Khajuraho Group of Monuments" is located in which state?
(a) Madhya Pradesh (b) Uttar Pradesh (c) Rajasthan (d) Gujarat

**Answer: (a) Madhya Pradesh**

**Solution:** The Khajuraho Group of Monuments is located in Chhatarpur district, Madhya Pradesh. Built between 950-1050 CE by the Chandela dynasty, these are Hindu and Jain temples known for their nagara-style architecture and intricate sculptures. There were originally 85 temples, of which only 25 survive. They are UNESCO World Heritage Sites.

### Q15. Which of the following is the largest desert in the world (by area)?
(a) Sahara (b) Arabian (c) Gobi (d) Antarctic Polar Desert

**Answer: (d) Antarctic Polar Desert**

**Solution:** The largest desert in the world is the Antarctic Polar Desert (~14 million km²). Hot deserts are the Sahara (~9 million km², largest hot desert). The Arabian Desert (~2.3 million km²) is the largest in Asia. The Gobi Desert (~1.3 million km²) is in Mongolia/China. Deserts are defined by low precipitation, not high temperature.
