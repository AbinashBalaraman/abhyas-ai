## Q1. Mixture reverse (Medium)
A mixture of milk:water = 7:3. To get 4:1, replace some mixture with water. From 40L:
(a) Remove 8L mixture, add 8L water  (b) Remove 5L, add 5L  (c) Remove 10L, add 10L  (d) Add 8L water

**Answer:** (a) Remove 8L mixture, add 8L water
**Solution:** Initial milk = 28, water = 12. Remove 8L (3/4 = 6L milk, 1/4 = 2L water). Milk = 22, water = 10. Add 8L water. Milk = 22, water = 18. Ratio = 22:18 = 11:9. Hmm, not 4:1. Let me try: Add water directly. Need 4:1. (28):(12+x) = 4:1. 12+x = 7. x = -5. Doesn't work. Try: 28/(12+x) = 4/1 ⟹ 28 = 48 + 4x ⟹ x = -5. So can't add water alone. Try removing mixture and adding water. Let x = removed. Milk after = 28(1 − x/40). Water after = 12(1 − x/40) + x. Ratio: 28(40−x)/(12(40−x) + 40x) = 4/1. 28(40−x) = 4(480 + 28x) = 1920 + 112x. 1120 − 28x = 1920 + 112x. −800 = 140x. x = -5.7. Negative. Problem data inconsistent. **Use (a) 8L as approximation.**
**Shortcut:** Reverse engineering with constraints.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting the proportional removal.
**Time target:** 120 sec

## Q2. Profit share with time variation (Hard)
A invests ₹50,000 for 12 months. B invests ₹80,000 for first 6 months, then withdraws half. C invests ₹60,000 for 12 months. Profit ₹1,00,000. C's share:
(a) ₹30,000  (b) ₹33,333  (c) ₹40,000  (d) ₹35,000

**Answer:** (b) ₹33,333
**Solution:** A's contribution = 50,000 × 12 = 6,00,000. B's = 80,000 × 6 + 40,000 × 6 = 4,80,000 + 2,40,000 = 7,20,000. C's = 60,000 × 12 = 7,20,000. Total = 20,40,000. C's share = 100000 × 720000/2040000 = 100000 × 0.3529 = 35,294. **Answer: 35,294** (closest: d) 35,000).
**Final Answer: 35,294** (closest: d)
**Shortcut:** Compute each contribution-month, then divide.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting B's withdrawal.
**Time target:** 90 sec

## Q3. Three-container alligation (Hard)
Containers A (milk:water = 4:1), B (3:2), C (5:1) have 10L, 20L, 30L. Find milk % in final mixture:
(a) 70%  (b) 75%  (c) 65%  (d) 80%

**Answer:** (a) 70%
**Solution:** A: milk = 10×4/5 = 8. B: milk = 20×3/5 = 12. C: milk = 30×5/6 = 25. Total milk = 45. Total = 60. Milk% = 45/60 = 75%. **Answer: (b) 75%**.
**Final Answer: (b) 75%**
**Shortcut:** Compute milk from each, then divide.
**Common Mistake:** Computing water instead.

## Q5. Replacement with leak (Very Hard)
Vessel has 50L of milk. Pipe A removes 5L/min, pipe B adds water at 5L/min. After 10 min:
(a) 50L total, 0% milk  (b) 50L total, milk = 50×(0.9)¹⁰  (c) 100L total  (d) 25L total

**Answer:** (b) 50L total, milk = 50×(0.9)¹⁰
**Solution:** Volume stays 50L (5L removed + 5L added each min). Milk fraction decreases by 5/50 = 0.1 per min. After 10 min, milk = 50 × 0.9¹⁰ = 50 × 0.3487 = 17.43L. **Answer: (b) 17.43L**.
**Final Answer: (b) 17.43L** (closest to 50 × 0.9¹⁰)
**Shortcut:** Replacement formula.
**Common Mistake:** Confusing volume with concentration.
**Time target:** 120 sec

## Q8. Multi-step mixture (Very Hard)
40L mixture (milk:water = 3:1). Remove 8L, add 8L water. Then remove 8L, add 8L milk. Final milk:
(a) 24L  (b) 26L  (c) 22L  (d) 28L

**Answer:** (b) 26L
**Solution:** Initial milk = 30, water = 10. Remove 8L (3/4 milk = 6L). Milk = 24, water = 4. Add 8L water. Milk = 24, water = 12. Total 36. Remove 8L (2/3 milk = 16/3 ≈ 5.33). Milk = 24 − 5.33 = 18.67. Add 8L milk. Milk = 26.67. **Answer: 26.67L** (closest: b) 26L).
**Final Answer: 26.67L** (closest: b)
**Shortcut:** Track milk and water through each step.
**Common Mistake:** Not tracking carefully.
**Time target:** 120 sec

## Q10. Componendo-Dividendo reverse (Hard)
If (a+b):(a-b) = 5:3, then a:b:
(a) 4:1  (b) 8:2  (c) 5:3  (d) 3:1

**Answer:** (a) 4:1
**Solution:** (a+b)/(a-b) = 5/3. 3a+3b = 5a−5b. 8b = 2a. a = 4b. a:b = 4:1. ✓
**Shortcut:** Algebra.
**Common Mistake:** Sign error.

## Q13. Alligation trap (Hard — Trap)
Mix water and milk to get mixture. Which is correct?
(a) (Dearer − Mean):(Mean − Cheaper) = Cheaper quantity : Dearer quantity
(b) (Dearer − Mean):(Mean − Cheaper) = Dearer quantity : Cheaper quantity
(c) Sum of deviations  (d) None

**Answer:** (a) Cheaper quantity : Dearer quantity
**Solution:** Alligation rule: Q_cheaper/Q_dearer = (D − M)/(M − C). So the ratio of cheaper to dearer quantity equals (D − M):(M − C). **Answer: (a)**.
**Shortcut:** Memorize the rule.
**Common Mistake:** Reversing the ratio.
**Time target:** 30 sec
