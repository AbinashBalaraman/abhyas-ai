## Q1. AP (Easy)
3, 6, 9, 12, ?
(a) 14  (b) 15  (c) 16  (d) 18

**Answer:** (b) 15
**Solution:** d=3. 12+3 = 15. ✓
**Shortcut:** AP.
**Common Mistake:** Setting = 14.
