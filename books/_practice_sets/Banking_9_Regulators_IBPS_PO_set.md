### Q1. The SEBI Act was passed in:
(a) 1988 (b) 1990 (c) 1992 (d) 1995

**Answer: (c) 1992**

**Solution:** The SEBI Act was passed in 1992. It made SEBI a statutory body (provisions came into force on 30 January 1992). SEBI was first constituted as a non-statutory body on 12 April 1988 (Government resolution). SEBI's preamble is "to protect the interests of investors in securities and to promote the development of, and to regulate the securities market". SEBI's headquarters is in Mumbai. SEBI regulates stock exchanges, brokers, mutual funds, and other intermediaries. Major SEBI regulations: ICDR (2018), LODR (2015), PIT (2015), SAST (2011).

### Q2. PFRDA was established as a statutory body in:
(a) 2003 (b) 2013 (c) 2014 (d) 2015

**Answer: (c) 2014**

**Solution:** PFRDA was established as a statutory body on 1 February 2014 (PFRDA Act was enacted on 19 September 2013). The interim PFRDA was set up on 23 August 2003. PFRDA is headquartered in New Delhi. PFRDA regulates NPS, APY, and other pension schemes. The current Chairman is Deepak Mohanty. PFRDA registers and regulates pension fund managers (PFMs), Points of Presence (POPs), and Central Recordkeeping Agencies (CRAs). NPS has 2.23 crore subscribers and AUM of ₹16.77 lakh crore as of June 2026.

### Q3. SIDBI headquarters is in:
(a) New Delhi (b) Mumbai (c) Lucknow (d) Hyderabad

**Answer: (c) Lucknow**

**Solution:** SIDBI (Small Industries Development Bank of India) is headquartered in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh. SIDBI was established on 2 April 1990 under the SIDBI Act, 1989. SIDBI is the principal financial institution for MSME (Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises) promotion, financing, and development. SIDBI provides refinance to banks, NBFCs, and MFIs for MSME lending. The current Chairman of SIDBI is Manoj Mittal (verify). MUDRA Ltd (Micro Units Development & Refinance Agency) is also under SIDBI. SIDBI plays a key role in MSME development and financing.

### Q4. The "FMC" (Forward Markets Commission) merged with SEBI in:
(a) 2013 (b) 2015 (c) 2017 (d) 2020

**Answer: (b) 2015**

**Solution:** FMC (Forward Markets Commission) merged with SEBI on 28 September 2015. The FMC was originally constituted under the Forward Contracts (Regulation) Act, 1952. It regulated commodity derivatives. After the merger, SEBI became the unified regulator for both securities and commodity derivatives markets. The merger was recommended by the Financial Sector Legislative Reforms Commission (FSLRC) headed by Justice B.N. Srikrishna. This brought all market regulation under one roof, avoiding regulatory gaps and improving efficiency. The merger is a major example of regulatory convergence in India.

### Q5. CCI regulates:
(a) Capital markets (b) Banking (c) Competition/Anti-trust (d) Insurance

**Answer: (c) Competition/Anti-trust**

**Solution:** CCI (Competition Commission of India) regulates competition and anti-trust matters in India. CCI was established in 2003 under the Competition Act, 2002. CCI is headquartered in New Delhi. CCI is a statutory body. CCI prevents anti-competitive agreements, abuse of dominant position, and regulates combinations (mergers, acquisitions, amalgamations) that may adversely affect competition in India. CCI's Chairperson is appointed by the Central Government. CCI has quasi-judicial powers. Other financial regulators: RBI (banking), SEBI (securities), IRDAI (insurance), PFRDA (pension). CCI works under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs.

**Time Target:** 30s | **Chapter Reference:** Banking/09_Regulators.md, p.190-195 | **VERIFY:** No

### Q6. IBBI was established under:
(a) RBI Act (b) IBC 2016 (c) Companies Act (d) SEBI Act

**Answer: (b) IBC 2016**

**Solution:** IBBI (Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India) was established in 2016 under the IBC (Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code), 2016. IBBI is headquartered in New Delhi. IBBI regulates Insolvency Professionals (IPs), Information Utilities (IUs), and Asset Reconstruction Companies (ARCs). IBBI writes rules for the Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP) and liquidation. The current Chairperson is Ravi Mital (verify). IBBI has 10 members. IBBI has played a key role in resolving corporate insolvency cases (Bhushan Steel, Videocon, etc.). The IBC is a major reform in India's insolvency framework.

### Q7. SEBI is the regulator for:
(a) Banking (b) Insurance (c) Securities Market (d) Pension

**Answer: (c) Securities Market**

**Solution:** SEBI is the regulator for the securities market in India. SEBI was constituted in 1988 (non-statutory) and became statutory in 1992. SEBI's headquarters is in Mumbai. SEBI regulates stock exchanges (BSE, NSE), brokers, sub-brokers, mutual funds, and other intermediaries. SEBI's mission is to protect investor interests and promote the development of the securities market. Other financial regulators and their focus: RBI (banking), IRDAI (insurance), PFRDA (pension), IFSCA (international financial services). SEBI is sometimes called the "watchdog of the Indian securities market".

**Time Target:** 30s | **Chapter Reference:** Banking/09_Regulators.md, p.55-70 | **VERIFY:** No

### Q8. FIMMDA is:
(a) An industry body for fixed income markets (b) A government body (c) A central bank (d) A pension fund

**Answer: (a) An industry body for fixed income markets**

**Solution:** FIMMDA (Fixed Income Money Market and Derivatives Association of India) is an industry body for the fixed income, money, and derivatives markets in India. FIMMDA represents the market participants in these segments. FIMMDA publishes FBIL (Financial Benchmarks India Pvt Ltd) benchmarks, including the Mumbai Inter-Bank Outright Rate (MIBOR), MIFOR (Mumbai Inter-Bank Forward Offer Rate), and other rates. FBIL was established in 2015 to manage these benchmark rates. FIMMDA is a self-regulatory organization (SRO). FIMMDA plays a key role in the development of the fixed income market in India.

**Time Target:** 30s | **Chapter Reference:** Banking/09_Regulators.md, p.225-230 | **VERIFY:** No

### Q9. The current RBI Governor (as of June 2026) chairs the:
(a) SEBI (b) FSDC Sub-Committee (c) NITI Aayog (d) PFRDA

**Answer: (b) FSDC Sub-Committee**

**Solution:** The current RBI Governor (Sanjay Malhotra, as of June 2026) chairs the FSDC Sub-Committee. The FSDC Sub-Committee is chaired by the RBI Governor and includes SEBI, IRDAI, PFRDA heads. The FSDC (Financial Stability and Development Council) is chaired by the Union Finance Minister. FSDC was set up in 2010 in the wake of the 2008 global financial crisis. The FSDC is an executive body for coordinating financial sector regulation. The FSDC Sub-Committee handles operational coordination among financial regulators.

**Time Target:** 30s | **Chapter Reference:** Banking/09_Regulators.md, p.180-185 | **VERIFY:** No

### Q10. NHB (National Housing Bank) is now under:
(a) RBI (b) SEBI (c) IRDAI (d) Government of India

**Answer: (a) RBI**

**Solution:** The NHB (National Housing Bank) is now under RBI. NHB was established in 1988 under the National Housing Bank Act, 1987. It was the regulator for housing finance companies. NHB was merged with RBI in 2019. NHB's headquarters is in Delhi. After the merger, the functions of NHB are now under RBI. The National Housing Bank was earlier a wholly-owned subsidiary of RBI. NHB supervises Housing Finance Companies (HFCs). The merger brought all housing finance regulation under RBI's umbrella.
