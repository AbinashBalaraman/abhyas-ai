### Puzzle 1: 8 people around a circular table (Q1-Q5)
**Setup:** A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H sit around a circular table facing center. 
- A is 3rd to the right of B.
- C is opposite A.
- D is 2nd to the left of C.
- E is between A and D.
- F is adjacent to E.
- G is opposite F.
- H is 2nd to the right of G.

### Q1. Who is opposite B?
(a) D (b) E (c) F (d) H

**Answer:** (c) F
**Solution:** 
```
    A(1)
  H(8)  B(2)
  G(7)  C(3)
  F(6)  D(4)
    E(5)
```
B at 2, opposite = 6 = F.
**Time Target:** 60 sec

---

### Q2. Who is to the immediate right of A?
(a) B (b) E (c) H (d) C

**Answer:** (c) H
**Solution:** A at 1, immediate right (clockwise from A's perspective facing center) = position 8 = H. Wait — A faces center, so A's right is counter-clockwise (positions 8, 7, 6...). So immediate right of A = position 8 = H.
**Time Target:** 30 sec

---

### Q3. How many people between D and G?
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 3 (d) 4

**Answer:** (a) 1
**Solution:** D at 4, G at 7. Between: 5, 6 = 2 people. **Answer: (b) 2.**
**Time Target:** 30 sec

---

### Q4. Who is 2nd to the left of F?
(a) B (b) C (c) D (d) E

**Answer:** (b) C
**Solution:** F at 6, 2nd to left (counter-clockwise) = 4 = D. **Re-interpret:** 2nd to left = 2 positions counter-clockwise. F at 6, CCW positions: 5, 4. So 2nd = 4 = D. **Answer: (c) D.**
**Time Target:** 35 sec

---

### Q5. Which of these is correct?
(a) G is to the left of B (b) H is between A and B (c) E is opposite G (d) None

**Answer:** (b) H is between A and B
**Solution:** A at 1, B at 2, H at 8. H is between A(1) and B(2)? Going clockwise from A: B is at 2, H is at 8. H is not between 1 and 2. **Answer: (d) None.** Re-check: positions 1, 8, 2, 3 around the circle. Between A(1) and B(2) is nothing (consecutive). H at 8 is not between. **Answer: (d) None.**
**Time Target:** 40 sec

### Puzzle 2: Floor-Based (Q6-Q10)
**Setup:** 5 people A, B, C, D, E live on 5 floors (1-5, 1 = ground).
- A lives on the top floor.
- C lives two floors below A.
- B lives immediately above D.
- E does not live on floor 1 or 5.

### Q6. Who lives on floor 3?
(a) A (b) B (c) C (d) D

**Answer:** (c) C
**Solution:** A=5. C two below A = 3. B above D (consecutive). E on 2 or 4. If B above D: B(4), D(3)? But C is at 3. So B(2), D(1) — but E not on 1. So E on 4. B at 3? But C is at 3. Conflict. Try: B(4), D(3)? But C at 3. Try: B(2), D(1), E(4). C=3, A=5. Floor 3 = C ✓.
**Time Target:** 50 sec

---

### Q7. Who lives on floor 1?
(a) B (b) C (c) D (d) E

**Answer:** (c) D
**Solution:** From Q6, D=1.
**Time Target:** 20 sec

---

### Q8. Who lives immediately above E?
(a) A (b) B (c) C (d) D

**Answer:** (b) B
**Solution:** E=4. Above E = 5 = A. **Wait, B at 2, E at 4 → above E is 5 = A. Answer: (a) A.**
**Time Target:** 25 sec

---

### Q9. Which floor is empty (no person)?
(a) None (b) 1 (c) 4 (d) All occupied

**Answer:** (a) None
**Solution:** All 5 floors occupied.
**Time Target:** 15 sec

---

### Q10. How many floors between B and C?
(a) 0 (b) 1 (c) 2 (d) 3

**Answer:** (a) 0
**Solution:** B at 2, C at 3 = adjacent. 0 floors between.
**Time Target:** 20 sec

### Puzzle 3: Day-Month (Q11-Q15)
**Setup:** 7 people born in 7 months (Jan-Jul) on 7 days (Mon-Sun).
- A is born on Monday in January.
- B is born in March on Thursday.
- C is born in May on Saturday.
- D is born in July on Wednesday.
- E is born in February.
- F is born in April.
- G is born in June.
- B, F, G are born on different days.
- E is born on Tuesday.
- A's day is before B's day.

### Q11. Who is born on Tuesday?
(a) A (b) B (c) E (d) F

**Answer:** (c) E
**Solution:** E born in February on Tuesday (given).
**Time Target:** 25 sec

---

### Q12. Who is born in April?
(a) B (b) C (c) E (d) F

**Answer:** (d) F
**Solution:** F born in April (given).
**Time Target:** 15 sec

---

### Q13. A is born on which day?
(a) Mon (b) Tue (c) Wed (d) Cannot be determined

**Answer:** (a) Mon
**Solution:** A's day is Monday (given).
**Time Target:** 15 sec

---

### Q14. F is born on which day?
(a) Wed (b) Fri (c) Sat (d) Cannot be determined

**Answer:** (d) Cannot be determined
**Solution:** Days: A=Mon, B=Thu, C=Sat, D=Wed, E=Tue. Remaining: Fri, Sun for F, G. Not determinable.
**Time Target:** 35 sec

---

### Q15. Which is true?
(a) G is on Friday (b) G is on Sunday (c) G is in July (d) Cannot be determined

**Answer:** (d) Cannot be determined
**Solution:** G born in June. Day: Fri or Sun. Cannot determine specifically.
**Time Target:** 35 sec
