## Q2. Painted 2×2 (Easy)
2×2×2 cube painted, cut. Cubes with 0 faces painted?
(a) 0  (b) 1  (c) 8  (d) None

**Answer:** (a) 0
**Solution:** (2-2)³ = 0.
**Shortcut:** (n-2)³.
**Common Mistake):** Other.
**Time target:** 20 sec

## Q3. Die Opposite (Easy)
Standard die: 3 on top, 4 on front, 5 on right. Bottom?
(a) 1  (b) 2  (c) 6  (d) Cannot say

**Answer:** (c) 6
**Solution:** 3 + 4 = 7? No, opposite pairs: 1-6, 2-5, 3-4. So 3's opposite is 4, not bottom.
**Shortcut:** For standard die, top+bottom=7.
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q6. Rectangles 3×3 (Medium)
Rectangles in 3×3 grid:
(a) 9  (b) 18  (c) 27  (d) 36

**Answer:** (d) 36
**Solution:** C(4,2)² = 6² = 36.
**Shortcut:** C(m+1,2)².
**Common Mistake):** Just 9.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q7. Die Three-Positions (Medium)
Die Pos 1: 1,2,3. Pos 2: 1,3,5. Opposite of 2 in Pos 1?
(a) 4  (b) 5  (c) 6  (d) 1

**Answer:** (c) 6
**Solution:** 2 adj to 1,3. 5 adj to 1,3 (pos 2). So 5 adj to 2. 2 opposite to 6.
**Shortcut:** Three-rule.
**Common Mistake):** Other.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q9. Painted 7×7 (Medium)
7×7×7 cube painted. At least 1 face painted?
(a) 218  (b) 296  (c) 343  (d) 125

**Answer:** (a) 218
**Solution:** 7³ = 343. Interior = 5³ = 125. Painted = 218.
**Shortcut:** Total - interior.
**Common Mistake):** Other.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q11. Painted Variation (Hard)
4×4×4 cube painted on 2 opposite faces only. Cubes with 1 face painted?
(a) 8  (b) 16  (c) 24  (d) 32

**Answer:** (a) 8
**Solution:** 2 × (4-2)² = 8.
**Shortcut:** 2(n-2)² for 2 opp faces.
**Common Mistake):** Standard formula.
**Time target:** 90 sec

## Q12. Squares 4×4 (Hard)
Squares in 4×4 grid:
(a) 16  (b) 25  (c) 30  (d) 36

**Answer:** (c) 30
**Solution:** 4×5×9/6 = 30.
**Shortcut:** n(n+1)(2n+1)/6.
**Common Mistake):** n².
**Time target:** 60 sec
