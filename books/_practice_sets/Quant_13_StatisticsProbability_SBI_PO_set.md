## Q1. Mean (Easy)
Mean of 5 numbers is 30. Sum:
(a) 100  (b) 150  (c) 200  (d) 6

**Answer:** (b) 150
**Solution:** Sum = 30·5 = 150. ✓
**Shortcut:** Sum = mean·n.
**Common Mistake:** Dividing.
**Time target:** 10 sec

---

## Q2. Range (Easy)
Range of 3, 7, 1, 9, 5:
(a) 1  (b) 5  (c) 8  (d) 9

**Answer:** (c) 8
**Solution:** 9 − 1 = 8. ✓
**Shortcut:** Max − min.
**Common Mistake:** Max value.
**Time target:** 10 sec

---

## Q3. P(odd die) (Easy — Trap)
(a) 1/2  (b) 1/3  (c) 1/6  (d) 5/6

**Answer:** (a) 1/2
**Solution:** 3 odd / 6 total = 1/2. ✓
**Shortcut:** Favorable/total.
**Common Mistake:** Setting = 1/3.
**Time target:** 15 sec

---

## Q4. Mean with replacement (Medium)
Mean of 8 numbers is 37. One excluded, mean reduced by 2. Excluded:
(a) 49  (b) 51  (c) 53  (d) 55

**Answer:** (b) 51
**Solution:** Sum = 8·37 = 296. New sum = 7·35 = 245. Excluded = 296−245 = 51. ✓
**Shortcut:** Old sum − new sum.
**Common Mistake:** Adding.
**Time target:** 60 sec

---

## Q5. P(prime die) (Medium)
P(2, 3, or 5):
(a) 1/2  (b) 1/3  (c) 1/6  (d) 2/3

**Answer:** (a) 1/2
**Solution:** 3 primes / 6 = 1/2. ✓
**Shortcut:** Favorable/total.
**Common Mistake:** Setting = 1/3.
**Time target:** 15 sec

---

## Q6. Median (Medium)
Median of 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12:
(a) 6  (b) 7  (c) 8  (d) 9

**Answer:** (b) 7
**Solution:** n=6, median = (6+8)/2 = 7. ✓
**Shortcut:** Average of 2 middle for even n.
**Common Mistake:** 4th value.
**Time target:** 15 sec

---

## Q7. P(no head in 3 coins) (Medium)
(a) 1/8  (b) 3/8  (c) 5/8  (d) 7/8

**Answer:** (a) 1/8
**Solution:** P(TTT) = 1/8. ✓
**Shortcut:** Direct.
**Common Mistake:** Setting = 1/4.
**Time target:** 15 sec

---

## Q8. Mean of first n natural (Medium)
Mean of first 100 natural numbers:
(a) 49  (b) 50  (c) 50.5  (d) 51

**Answer:** (c) 50.5
**Solution:** (100+1)/2 = 50.5. ✓
**Shortcut:** (n+1)/2.
**Common Mistake:** Setting = 50.
**Time target:** 15 sec

---

## Q9. P(A∪B) (Hard)
P(A) = 0.5, P(B) = 0.3, P(A∩B) = 0.2. P(A∪B):
(a) 0.6  (b) 0.8  (c) 1.0  (d) 0.1

**Answer:** (a) 0.6
**Solution:** 0.5 + 0.3 − 0.2 = 0.6. ✓
**Shortcut:** Addition rule.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting to subtract.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q10. P(face card) (Hard — Trap)
P(king, queen, or jack):
(a) 1/13  (b) 2/13  (c) 3/13  (d) 4/13

**Answer:** (c) 3/13
**Solution:** 12 face cards / 52 = 12/52 = 3/13. ✓
**Shortcut:** Favorable/total.
**Common Mistake:** Setting = 2/13.
**Time target:** 30 sec
