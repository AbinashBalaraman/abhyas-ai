## Q1. Rectangle area (Easy)
Rectangle 12 × 5. Area:
(a) 17  (b) 30  (c) 60  (d) 120

**Answer:** (c) 60
**Solution:** 12·5 = 60. ✓
**Shortcut:** Direct.
**Common Mistake:** Adding instead of multiplying.
**Time target:** 15 sec

---

## Q2. Cube volume (Easy)
Cube of side 5. Volume:
(a) 25  (b) 75  (c) 125  (d) 250

**Answer:** (c) 125
**Solution:** 5³ = 125. ✓
**Shortcut:** a³.
**Common Mistake:** 3a instead of a³.
**Time target:** 15 sec

---

## Q3. Circle circumference (Easy)
Circle radius 7. Circumference:
(a) 22  (b) 44  (c) 66  (d) 88

**Answer:** (b) 44
**Solution:** 2·(22/7)·7 = 44. ✓
**Shortcut:** 2πr with π=22/7.
**Common Mistake:** Using π=3.14.
**Time target:** 20 sec

---

## Q4. Cylinder CSA (Easy — Trap)
Cylinder r=7, h=10. CSA:
(a) 140π  (b) 220π  (c) 440π  (d) 700π

**Answer:** (a) 140π
**Solution:** CSA = 2πrh = 2π·7·10 = 140π. ✓
**Shortcut:** 2πrh.
**Common Mistake:** Computing TSA.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q5. Sphere TSA (Medium)
Sphere radius 7. TSA:
(a) 154π  (b) 196π  (c) 308π  (d) 616π

**Answer:** (b) 196π
**Solution:** TSA = 4πr² = 4π·49 = 196π. ✓
**Shortcut:** 4πr².
**Common Mistake:** Using (4/3)πr³.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q6. Cone slant (Medium)
Cone r=6, h=8. Slant height:
(a) 10  (b) 12  (c) 14  (d) 16

**Answer:** (a) 10
**Solution:** l = √(36+64) = √100 = 10. ✓
**Shortcut:** √(r²+h²).
**Common Mistake:** Adding r+h.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q7. Cuboid diagonal (Medium)
Cuboid 10×8×6. Diagonal:
(a) 10  (b) √200  (c) 12  (d) √250

**Answer:** (b) √200
**Solution:** d = √(100+64+36) = √200. ✓
**Shortcut:** √(l²+b²+h²).
**Common Mistake:** Summing sides.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q8. Square in circle (Medium)
Circle radius 7, square inscribed. Square side:
(a) 7  (b) 7√2  (c) 14  (d) 7√3

**Answer:** (b) 7√2
**Solution:** Diagonal = 14. Side = 14/√2 = 7√2. ✓
**Shortcut:** Diagonal = diameter.
**Common Mistake:** Side = radius.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q9. Path area (Medium)
Path 2 m wide outside 30×20 garden. Path area:
(a) 200  (b) 216  (c) 232  (d) 250

**Answer:** (b) 216
**Solution:** Outer = 34·24 = 816. Inner = 600. Path = 216. ✓
**Shortcut:** Outer − inner.
**Common Mistake:** Using 2w(L+B) only.
**Time target:** 45 sec

---

## Q10. Cylinder-cone volumes (Medium)
Cylinder and cone same base, same volume. Cylinder h=6. Cone h:
(a) 2  (b) 12  (c) 18  (d) 24

**Answer:** (c) 18
**Solution:** πr²·6 = (1/3)πr²·h → h = 18. ✓
**Shortcut:** Cone height = 3 × cylinder height.
**Common Mistake:** Dividing by 3.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q11. Sphere in cube (Medium)
Sphere radius r in cube. Cube side:
(a) r  (b) 2r  (c) r√2  (d) r√3

**Answer:** (b) 2r
**Solution:** Cube side = sphere diameter = 2r. ✓
**Shortcut:** Side = 2·radius.
**Common Mistake:** Side = radius.
**Time target:** 20 sec

## Q12. Triangle area (Medium)
Triangle base 12, height 8. Area:
(a) 24  (b) 48  (c) 96  (d) 20

**Answer:** (b) 48
**Solution:** ½·12·8 = 48. ✓
**Shortcut:** ½·b·h.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting ½.
**Time target:** 15 sec

## Q13. Hollow cylinder (Hard)
Hollow cylinder R=8, r=6, h=10. Material volume:
(a) 200π  (b) 280π  (c) 300π  (d) 400π

**Answer:** (b) 280π
**Solution:** V = πh(R²−r²) = π·10·(64−36) = 280π. ✓
**Shortcut:** πh(R²−r²).
**Common Mistake:** Adding instead of subtracting.
**Time target:** 45 sec

---

## Q14. Cone unfold (Hard)
Cone r=3, l=5 unfolded. Sector angle:
(a) 180°  (b) 216°  (c) 270°  (d) 360°

**Answer:** (b) 216°
**Solution:** Arc = 2π·3 = 6π. Sector angle = (6π/(2π·5))·360° = 0.6·360 = 216°. ✓
**Shortcut:** (r/l)·360°.
**Common Mistake:** Multiplying instead of ratio.
**Time target:** 60 sec

---

## Q15. Block with hole (Hard — Trap)
Block 8×6×4 with cylindrical hole r=2 through length 8. Remaining volume:
(a) 192−32π  (b) 192−16π  (c) 192−8π  (d) 192−4π

**Answer:** (a) 192−32π
**Solution:** Block = 192. Cylinder = π·4·8 = 32π. Remaining = 192−32π. ✓
**Shortcut:** Block − cylinder.
**Common Mistake:** Using r=1 for cylinder.
**Time target:** 60 sec
