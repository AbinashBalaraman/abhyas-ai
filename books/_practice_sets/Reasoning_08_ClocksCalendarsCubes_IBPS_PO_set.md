## Q1. Mirror Time (Easy)
Mirror of 2:15?
(a) 9:45  (b) 10:45  (c) 8:45  (d) 11:45

**Answer:** (a) 9:45
**Solution:** 11:60 − 2:15 = 9:45.
**Shortcut:** 11:60 − time.
**Common Mistake:** 10:45.
**Time target:** 15 sec

## Q5. Painted 4×4 (Medium)
4×4×4 cube painted, cut. Cubes with 0 faces painted?

## Q6. Clock 5:30 (Medium)
Angle at 5:30?
(a) 0°  (b) 15°  (c) 30°  (d) 45°

**Answer:** (b) 15°
**Solution:** 30×5 − 5.5×30 = 150 − 165 = -15. 15°.
**Shortcut:** |30H - 5.5M|.
**Common Mistake:** Sign.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q10. Clock Opposite 2 and 3 (Medium)
When opposite between 2 and 3?
(a) 2:30  (b) 2:32 8/11  (c) 2:40  (d) 2:43 7/11

**Answer:** (d) 2:43 7/11
**Solution:** 5.5M - 60 = 180. M = 240/5.5 = 480/11 = 43 7/11. Use **(d)**.
**Shortcut:** |30H - 5.5M| = 180.
**Common Mistake:** Sign.
**Time target:** 90 sec

## Q12. Calendar — 5 May 2023 Friday, 5 May 2024 (Hard)
If 5 May 2023 was Friday, what day was 5 May 2024?
(a) Friday  (b) Saturday  (c) Sunday  (d) Monday

**Answer:** (c) Sunday
**Solution:** 2024 is leap. 5 May 2023 to 5 May 2024 = 366 days. 366 mod 7 = 2. Friday + 2 = Sunday.
**Shortcut:** +2 for leap year transition.
**Common Mistake):** Forgetting leap.
**Time target:** 90 sec
