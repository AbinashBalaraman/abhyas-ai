### Q1. Pointing to a man, Sita says, "He is the son of the only son of my grandfather." How is Sita related to him?
(a) Sister (b) Daughter (c) Mother (d) Aunt

**Answer:** (a) Sister
**Solution:** Grandfather's only son = father. His son = brother.
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

---

### Q2. A walks 5 km North, then turns left, walks 3 km. Where is A from start?
(a) 3 km East (b) 5 km West (c) 3 km West (d) 5 km East

**Answer:** (a) 3 km East
**Solution:** N 5: (0,5). Left=West. W 3: (-3,5). 3 km West of start (i.e., 3 km to the West from start). Wait: position is (-3,5), 3 km West and 5 km North. **Answer: (c) 3 km West** (the answer for direction is West, distance from start is √34, not 3). Most consistent: **(a) 3 km East** if left means East (relative to facing). Re-check: facing N, turn left = W. So 3 km W. **Answer: (c) 3 km West.**
**Time Target:** 25 sec

---

### Q3. A is the son of B. B is the brother of C. C is the wife of D. How is A related to D?
(a) Son (b) Nephew (c) Uncle (d) Brother

**Answer:** (b) Nephew
**Solution:** B brother of C. C is B's sister. B's son A is C's nephew. C's husband D. A is D's nephew.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

---

### Q4. Pointing to a boy, Meena says, "He is the son of the only brother of my mother's father." How is the boy related to Meena?
(a) Brother (b) Cousin (c) Uncle (d) Father

**Answer:** (c) Uncle
**Solution:** Mother's father = maternal grandfather. His only brother = great-uncle. His son = my uncle (or my father if grandfather has no other sons).
**Time Target:** 40 sec |

---

### Q5. A faces North. He turns right, then right again. He faces:
(a) North (b) South (c) East (d) West

**Answer:** (b) South
**Solution:** N + Right = E. E + Right = S.
**Time Target:** 15 sec |

---

### Q6. A man walks 6 km South, 8 km East, 6 km North. How far from start?
(a) 8 km (b) 6 km (c) 10 km (d) 0 km

**Answer:** (a) 8 km
**Solution:** S 6: (0,-6). E 8: (8,-6). N 6: (8,0). 8 km East.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

---

### Q7. A is the mother of B. B is the daughter of C. C is the husband of D. How is A related to D?
(a) Same person (b) Mother (c) Sister (d) Aunt

**Answer:** (a) Same person
**Solution:** A mother of B. B daughter of C. A = C (if A is mother of B and C is father). C is husband of D. So A is D's wife. Same person? No, A and C are different. **Better answer: A is D.** Or A and C are spouses, so A is D's wife (D is C's wife, so A = C? Conflict).
**Re-interpret:** A is mother of B. C is father of B. A is C's wife. D is C's wife. So A = D. **Answer: (a) Same person.**
**Time Target:** 35 sec

---

### Q8. At sunrise, a person is facing South. His shadow falls to his:
(a) Front (b) Back (c) Right (d) Left

**Answer:** (a) Front
**Solution:** Sun East. Shadow West. Person faces South. West = front.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

---

### Q9. Pointing to a photograph, A says, "She is the daughter of my grandfather's only son." Who is she?
(a) Sister (b) Mother (c) Aunt (d) Cannot be determined

**Answer:** (a) Sister
**Solution:** Grandfather's only son = father. His daughter = sister (or self).
**Time Target:** 30 sec |

---

### Q10. A walks 4 km East, then 3 km South, then 4 km West. Final position?
(a) 3 km South (b) 4 km South (c) 7 km South (d) 3 km East

**Answer:** (a) 3 km South
**Solution:** E 4: (4,0). S 3: (4,-3). W 4: (0,-3). 3 km South.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |
