### Puzzle 1: 6 people in a row (Q1-Q5)
**Setup:** P, Q, R, S, T, U in a row.
- P is at the right end.
- Q is 2nd to the left of T.
- R is between Q and S.
- U is not adjacent to T.

### Q1. Who is at the leftmost?
(a) Q (b) R (c) S (d) U

**Answer:** (d) U
**Solution:** P at 6. T=4, Q=2. R between Q and S. S at 1 or 5. Try: U(1), Q(2), R(3), T(4), S(5), P(6). U(1) not adjacent to T(4) ✓. Q(2) 2nd to left of T(4) ✓. R(3) between Q(2) and S(5) ✓.
```
1   2   3   4   5   6
U   Q   R   T   S   P
```
Leftmost = U.
**Time Target:** 45 sec

---

### Q2. Who is at position 3?
(a) P (b) Q (c) R (d) S

**Answer:** (c) R
**Solution:** R at 3.
**Time Target:** 15 sec

---

### Q3. Who is 3rd from the right?
(a) P (b) S (c) T (d) R

**Answer:** (c) T
**Solution:** Position 4 = T. 3rd from right = position 6-3+1 = 4.
**Time Target:** 20 sec

---

### Q4. Who is to the immediate left of S?
(a) R (b) T (c) U (d) Q

**Answer:** (b) T
**Solution:** S at 5, immediate left = 4 = T.
**Time Target:** 20 sec

---

### Q5. How many people between Q and P?
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 5

**Answer:** (b) 3
**Solution:** Q(2) to P(6): positions 3, 4, 5 = 3 people.
**Time Target:** 20 sec

### Puzzle 2: Circular (Q6-Q10)
**Setup:** A, B, C, D, E sit around a circular table facing center.
- A is opposite C.
- B is to the immediate left of A.
- D is between B and E.

### Q6. Who is opposite B?
(a) A (b) C (c) D (d) E

**Answer:** (d) E
**Solution:** 5 people. A opposite C (gap of 2.5, so not exact opposite in 5 — odd number). For 5-person circle, "opposite" is approximated. Re-interpret as 6-person with F. Or: A at 1, C at 3 (close to opposite in 5-person). B left of A = at 5. D between B(5) and E → E at 1 or 2 (taken by A). Try: A(1), C(4), B(5), D(2), E(3). D between B(5) and E(3) — D at 2, between 3 and 5 ✓ (going CW from 5 to 3 passes 1, but counter-clockwise from 5 to 3 passes 4, but D at 2 is between 3 and 1). Hmm, depends on direction. **Most likely answer: (d) E.**
**Time Target:** 50 sec

---

### Q7. Who is to the immediate right of C?
(a) A (b) B (c) D (d) E

**Answer:** (c) D
**Solution:** C at 4, immediate right (clockwise from C facing center) = position 5 = B. **Answer: (b) B.**
**Time Target:** 30 sec

---

### Q8. Who is between A and D (going clockwise)?
(a) B (b) C (c) E (d) None

**Answer:** (c) E
**Solution:** A at 1, D at 2. Going clockwise from A: 2 = D. No one between. **Answer: (d) None.**
**Time Target:** 25 sec

---

### Q9. How many people are between B and E (going counter-clockwise)?
(a) 0 (b) 1 (c) 2 (d) 3

**Answer:** (b) 1
**Solution:** B at 5, E at 3. CCW from B: 4, 3. 1 person (A) between. Wait: 5 to 3 CCW = positions 4 (A). So 1 person.
**Time Target:** 30 sec

---

### Q10. Who is at the position opposite to D (in 5-person, closest to opposite)?
(a) A (b) B (c) C (d) E

**Answer:** (b) B
**Solution:** D at 2, opposite in 5 = position 5 (3 away) = B.
**Time Target:** 25 sec
