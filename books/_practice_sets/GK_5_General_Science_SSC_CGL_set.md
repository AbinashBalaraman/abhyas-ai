### Q1. The SI unit of force is:
(a) Joule (b) Newton (c) Watt (d) Pascal

**Answer: (b) Newton**

**Solution:** The SI unit of force is the Newton (N), named after Sir Isaac Newton. 1 N = 1 kg·m/s². Force is defined as the rate of change of momentum. SI unit of energy/work is Joule (J); SI unit of power is Watt (W); SI unit of pressure is Pascal (Pa). The 7 SI base units are: metre (length), kilogram (mass), second (time), ampere (current), kelvin (temperature), mole (amount), candela (luminous intensity). The SI system was adopted in 1960.

### Q2. The speed of light in vacuum is:
(a) 3 × 10⁶ m/s (b) 3 × 10⁸ m/s (c) 3 × 10⁷ m/s (d) 3 × 10⁵ m/s

**Answer: (b) 3 × 10⁸ m/s**

**Solution:** The speed of light in vacuum (c) is exactly 299,792,458 m/s (~3 × 10⁸ m/s). It is a fundamental physical constant. The speed of sound in air is ~343 m/s (at 20°C). Visible light has a wavelength of ~380-750 nm. The human hearing range is 20 Hz to 20,000 Hz. The escape velocity of Earth is 11.2 km/s, while for the Moon it is 2.4 km/s. The acceleration due to gravity (g) on Earth is 9.8 m/s².

### Q3. The pH of pure water is:
(a) 0 (b) 7 (c) 14 (d) 1

**Answer: (b) 7**

**Solution:** Pure water has a pH of 7 (neutral). The pH scale ranges from 0 (strongly acidic) to 14 (strongly alkaline). pH = -log[H⁺]. Acidic substances (pH < 7): HCl (0), lemon juice (2.2), vinegar (3), coffee (5). Basic substances (pH > 7): milk of magnesia (10.5), ammonia (11), bleach (13), NaOH 1M (14). Blood pH is 7.35-7.45 (slightly basic). Acid rain has pH < 5.6 (caused by SO₂ and NOₓ emissions).

### Q4. The gas mainly responsible for acid rain is:
(a) O₂ (b) CO₂ (c) SO₂ (d) H₂

**Answer: (c) SO₂**

**Solution:** SO₂ (Sulphur dioxide) and NOₓ (Nitrogen oxides) are the main gases responsible for acid rain. These gases react with water in the atmosphere to form sulphuric acid (H₂SO₄) and nitric acid (HNO₃). Acid rain has pH < 5.6. CO₂ is a greenhouse gas (causes global warming) but not the main cause of acid rain. O₂ is essential for respiration. H₂ is the lightest gas. Acid rain damages buildings, statues, and aquatic life.

### Q5. The "powerhouse of the cell" is:
(a) Nucleus (b) Ribosome (c) Mitochondria (d) Endoplasmic Reticulum

**Answer: (c) Mitochondria**

**Solution:** Mitochondria are called the "powerhouse of the cell" because they produce ATP (adenosine triphosphate) through cellular respiration, which is the energy currency of the cell. Other key cell organelles: Nucleus (control center; contains DNA), Ribosomes (protein synthesis), ER (rough — protein synthesis; smooth — lipid synthesis), Golgi apparatus (packaging, modification), Lysosomes (digestion — "suicide bags"), Vacuoles (storage), Chloroplasts (photosynthesis — only in plants). Cell theory was proposed by Schleiden and Schwann in 1839.

### Q6. The smallest bone in the human body is:
(a) Femur (b) Tibia (c) Stapes (d) Patella

**Answer: (c) Stapes**

**Solution:** The Stapes (in the middle ear) is the smallest bone in the human body. The Femur (thigh bone) is the longest and strongest bone. The Tibia is the shin bone. The Patella is the kneecap. The adult human body has 206 bones (270 in newborns; bones fuse as we grow). The skeletal system includes bone joints: hinge (knee), ball-and-socket (shoulder/hip), and pivot (neck). Normal human body temperature is 37°C (98.6°F).

### Q7. The universal blood donor is:
(a) A+ (b) B+ (c) AB+ (d) O negative

**Answer: (d) O negative**

**Solution:** O negative (O-) is the universal donor because it lacks A, B, and Rh antigens, so it can be transfused to any blood group. AB positive (AB+) is the universal recipient because it has all antigens and can accept blood from any group. Blood groups are determined by the ABO system (A, B, AB, O) and the Rh factor (+/-). Karl Landsteiner discovered blood groups. The ABO gene has multiple alleles (I^A, I^B, i). Blood clotting requires Vitamin K and Calcium.

### Q8. Vitamin C deficiency causes:
(a) Beriberi (b) Scurvy (c) Rickets (d) Pellagra

**Answer: (b) Scurvy**

**Solution:** Vitamin C deficiency causes Scurvy, characterized by bleeding gums, loose teeth, and poor wound healing. Other deficiency diseases: Vitamin A → Night blindness, Vitamin B1 (Thiamine) → Beriberi, Vitamin B3 (Niacin) → Pellagra, Vitamin B12 → Pernicious anemia, Vitamin D → Rickets (children) / Osteomalacia (adults), Vitamin E → Sterility, Vitamin K → Slow blood clotting, Iron → Anemia, Iodine → Goiter. Citrus fruits are rich in Vitamin C. Vitamin D is synthesized in skin using sunlight.

### Q9. The hardest natural substance is:
(a) Iron (b) Diamond (c) Quartz (d) Talc

**Answer: (b) Diamond**

**Solution:** Diamond is the hardest natural substance (10 on Mohs scale). It is an allotrope of carbon. Talc is the softest (1 on Mohs scale). Other Mohs scale minerals: Calcite (3), Fluorite (4), Apatite (5), Feldspar (6), Quartz (7), Topaz (8), Corundum (9). Gold is the most malleable and ductile metal. Silver is the best conductor of electricity. Mercury (Hg) is the only metal that is liquid at room temperature. Iron is the most abundant metal in Earth's crust (followed by Aluminum).

### Q10. The most abundant gas in Earth's atmosphere is:
(a) Oxygen (b) Nitrogen (c) CO₂ (d) Argon

**Answer: (b) Nitrogen**

**Solution:** Nitrogen (N₂) is the most abundant gas in Earth's atmosphere (~78%). Oxygen is the second most abundant (~21%). Argon is about 0.9%, and CO₂ is about 0.04% (rising). The atmospheric composition is: 78% Nitrogen, 21% Oxygen, 0.9% Argon, 0.04% CO₂, and trace gases. Atmospheric layers (bottom to top): Troposphere, Stratosphere (contains ozone layer), Mesosphere, Thermosphere, Exosphere. The ozone layer absorbs UV radiation. The ozone hole is largest over Antarctica.

### Q11. Newton's second law of motion is:
(a) F = ma (b) F = mg (c) F = mv (d) F = mc²

**Answer: (a) F = ma**

**Solution:** Newton's second law of motion is F = ma (Force = mass × acceleration), or equivalently F = dp/dt (rate of change of momentum). Newton's three laws: (1) First Law (Law of Inertia) — body at rest stays at rest, body in motion stays in motion, unless acted upon by external force; (2) Second Law (F = ma); (3) Third Law — for every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction. Mass is measured in kg; weight is measured in Newtons (N = m·g). The momentum p = m·v (vector).

### Q12. The largest organ in the human body is:
(a) Liver (b) Brain (c) Skin (d) Heart

**Answer: (c) Skin**

**Solution:** The Skin is the largest organ (external) in the human body. The Liver is the largest internal organ and the largest gland. The Brain is the most complex organ. The Heart is a muscular organ about the size of a fist that pumps blood. The Skin is part of the integumentary system and protects against infection, regulates temperature, and provides sensation. The adult human has 206 bones, 600+ muscles, and about 5 liters of blood.

### Q13. The chemical formula of Plaster of Paris is:
(a) CaSO₄·2H₂O (b) CaSO₄·½H₂O (c) CaCO₃ (d) Ca(OH)₂

**Answer: (b) CaSO₄·½H₂O**

**Solution:** Plaster of Paris (POP) has the chemical formula CaSO₄·½H₂O (calcium sulphate hemihydrate). It is made by heating gypsum (CaSO₄·2H₂O) at 373K. POP sets with water: CaSO₄·½H₂O + 1½H₂O → CaSO₄·2H₂O. It is used in setting bones, casts, and decorative work. Other important salts: NaCl (common salt), Na₂CO₃·10H₂O (washing soda), NaHCO₃ (baking soda), CaSO₄·2H₂O (gypsum), CaOCl₂ (bleaching powder), K₂SO₄·Al₂(SO₄)₃·24H₂O (potash alum).

### Q14. The SI unit of electric current is:
(a) Volt (b) Ohm (c) Ampere (d) Watt

**Answer: (c) Ampere**

**Solution:** The SI unit of electric current is the Ampere (A), named after André-Marie Ampère. 1 A = 1 Coulomb/second. Other SI electrical units: Volt (V) for potential difference, Ohm (Ω) for resistance, Watt (W) for power, Coulomb (C) for charge, Farad (F) for capacitance, Henry (H) for inductance, Hertz (Hz) for frequency. Ohm's Law: V = IR. Joule's Law of Heating: H = I²Rt. AC frequency in India is 50 Hz; voltage is 220V. Domestic supply is AC; batteries provide DC.

### Q15. Insulin is produced in which organ of the human body?
(a) Liver (b) Kidney (c) Pancreas (d) Thyroid

**Answer: (c) Pancreas**

**Solution:** Insulin is produced by the beta (β) cells of the Islets of Langerhans in the Pancreas. Insulin regulates blood sugar (glucose) levels. Lack of insulin causes Diabetes Mellitus. The Pancreas is both an endocrine and exocrine gland. Other endocrine glands: Pituitary ("Master Gland"), Thyroid (produces Thyroxine for metabolism), Adrenal (produces Adrenaline for "fight or flight"), Reproductive glands (Testes - testosterone; Ovaries - estrogen/progesterone). The Liver is the largest gland and produces bile.
