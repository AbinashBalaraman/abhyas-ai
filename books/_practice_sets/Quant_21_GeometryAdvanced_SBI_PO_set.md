## Q1. Cube Diagonal (Easy)
Cube side 4. Diagonal?
(a) 4  (b) 4√2  (c) 4√3  (d) 16

**Answer:** (c) 4√3
**Solution:** Diagonal of cube = $a\sqrt{3} = 4\sqrt{3}$
**Shortcut:** $a\sqrt{3}$.
**Common Mistake:** Using $a\sqrt{2}$.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q2. Rectangle Area (Easy)
Rectangle 8×6. Area?
(a) 14  (b) 28  (c) 48  (d) 96

**Answer:** (c) 48
**Solution:** $8 \times 6 = 48$
**Shortcut:** $l \times w$.
**Common Mistake:** Adding.
**Time target:** 15 sec

---

## Q3. Square Area (Easy)
Square with side 9. Area?
(a) 18  (b) 36  (c) 81  (d) 9

**Answer:** (c) 81
**Solution:** $9^2 = 81$
**Shortcut:** $a^2$.
**Common Mistake):** Just 9.
**Time target:** 15 sec

---

## Q4. Triangle Hypotenuse (Medium)
Right triangle with legs 3, 4. Hypotenuse?
(a) 5  (b) 6  (c) 7  (d) 25

**Answer:** (a) 5
**Solution:** $\sqrt{9+16} = 5$ (3-4-5).
**Shortcut:** 3-4-5 triple.
**Common Mistake:** Adding.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q5. Trapezium (Medium)
Parallel sides 6, 8; height 4. Area?
(a) 24  (b) 28  (c) 32  (d) 48

**Answer:** (b) 28
**Solution:** $\frac{1}{2}(6+8) \times 4 = 28$
**Shortcut:** $\frac{1}{2}(a+b)h$.
**Common Mistake):** Forgetting 1/2.
**Time target:** 60 sec

---

## Q6. Rhombus Diagonals (Medium)
Rhombus diagonals 6, 8. Area?
(a) 14  (b) 24  (c) 28  (d) 48

**Answer:** (b) 24
**Solution:** $\frac{1}{2} \times 6 \times 8 = 24$
**Shortcut:** $\frac{1}{2}d_1 d_2$.
**Common Mistake:** Adding.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q7. Parallelogram (Medium)
Parallelogram base 10, height 5. Area?
(a) 15  (b) 30  (c) 50  (d) 100

**Answer:** (c) 50
**Solution:** $b \times h = 10 \times 5 = 50$
**Shortcut:** $b \times h$.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting height.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q8. Circle Diameter (Medium)
Circle radius 5. Diameter?
(a) 5  (b) 10  (c) 15  (d) 25

**Answer:** (b) 10
**Solution:** $d = 2r = 10$
**Shortcut:** $d = 2r$.
**Common Mistake:** Just $r$.
**Time target:** 15 sec

---

## Q9. Sector Arc (Hard)
Sector angle 60°, radius 6. Arc length?
(a) 2π  (b) 4π  (c) 6π  (d) 12π

**Answer:** (a) 2π
**Solution:** $\frac{60}{360} \times 2\pi r = \frac{1}{6} \times 12\pi = 2\pi$
**Shortcut:** $\frac{\theta}{360} \times 2\pi r$.
**Common Mistake):** Using 360.
**Time target:** 60 sec

---

## Q10. Cube Surface Area (Hard)
Cube side 5. Total surface area?
(a) 25  (b) 100  (c) 150  (d) 125

**Answer:** (c) 150
**Solution:** $6a^2 = 6 \times 25 = 150$
**Shortcut:** $6a^2$.
**Common Mistake:** Just $a^2$ (one face).
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q11. Sphere SA (Hard)
Sphere radius 7. Surface area?
(a) 154  (b) 308  (c) 462  (d) 616

**Answer:** (d) 616
**Solution:** $4\pi r^2 = 4 \times 22/7 \times 49 = 616$
**Shortcut:** $4\pi r^2$.
**Common Mistake:** Using $\frac{4}{3}\pi r^3$ (volume).
**Time target:** 60 sec

---

## Q12. Cone Slant Height (Hard)
Cone r = 3, h = 4. Slant height?
(a) 5  (b) 6  (c) 7  (d) 25

**Answer:** (a) 5
**Solution:** $\sqrt{r^2 + h^2} = \sqrt{9+16} = 5$ (3-4-5).
**Shortcut:** Pythagorean.
**Common Mistake:** Adding.
**Time target:** 60 sec
