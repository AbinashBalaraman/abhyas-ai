### Q1. The highest civilian award in India is:
(a) Padma Vibhushan (b) Bharat Ratna (c) Padma Bhushan (d) Padma Shri

**Answer: (b) Bharat Ratna**

**Solution:** The Bharat Ratna (Jewel of India) is India's highest civilian award, instituted on 2 January 1954. The award is given for exceptional service in any field (art, literature, science, sports, public service). The first recipients (1954) were C. Rajagopalachari, S. Radhakrishnan, and C.V. Raman. The first sportsperson to receive was Sachin Tendulkar (2014). The first non-Indian (honorary) recipient was Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan (1987). Notable 2024 recipients include Karpoori Thakur, L.K. Advani, Charan Singh, P.V. Narasimha Rao, and M.S. Swaminathan.

### Q2. The Nobel Prize was first awarded in:
(a) 1896 (b) 1901 (c) 1910 (d) 1950

**Answer: (b) 1901**

**Solution:** The Nobel Prizes were first awarded in 1901 (5 years after Alfred Nobel's death in 1896). They are awarded annually on 10 December (Nobel Day - Alfred Nobel's death anniversary). The 6 categories are: Physics, Chemistry, Physiology or Medicine, Literature, Peace, and Economic Sciences. The Peace Prize is awarded in Oslo, Norway; the other 5 are awarded in Stockholm, Sweden. The Economic Sciences Prize was added in 1969. The first Indian Nobel laureate was Rabindranath Tagore (1913, Literature).

### Q3. The first Indian to win the Nobel Prize was:
(a) C.V. Raman (b) Rabindranath Tagore (c) Amartya Sen (d) Mother Teresa

**Answer: (b) Rabindranath Tagore**

**Solution:** Rabindranath Tagore (1861-1941) was the first Indian to win the Nobel Prize (1913, Literature) for his collection of poems "Gitanjali" (Song Offerings). He was also the first non-European to win the Nobel. He composed the Indian National Anthem "Jana Gana Mana". Other Indian Nobel laureates: C.V. Raman (1930, Physics - Raman Effect), Mother Teresa (1979, Peace - born in Macedonia, became Indian citizen), Amartya Sen (1998, Economics), Kailash Satyarthi (2014, Peace), Abhijit Banerjee (2019, Economics).

### Q4. The first Indian to win the Booker Prize was:

### Q5. The Indian National Flag was designed by:
(a) Mahatma Gandhi (b) Pingali Venkayya (c) Jawaharlal Nehru (d) Sarojini Naidu

**Answer: (b) Pingali Venkayya**

**Solution:** The Indian National Flag (Tiranga) was designed by Pingali Venkayya, a freedom fighter from Andhra Pradesh. The flag was adopted on 22 July 1947 by the Constituent Assembly. The tricolor has saffron (top, courage), white (middle, peace), and green (bottom, fertility). The navy-blue Ashoka Chakra (24 spokes) is in the center. "Satyameva Jayate" is the motto of the National Emblem. The Flag Code of India 2002 regulates the use of the flag.

### Q6. The national animal of India is:
(a) Lion (b) Tiger (c) Elephant (d) Peacock

**Answer: (b) Tiger**

**Solution:** The Royal Bengal Tiger is the National Animal of India. The Indian Peacock (Pavo cristatus) is the National Bird. The Indian Elephant is the National Heritage Animal (declared 2010). The Indian Roller is the state bird of several states. The Gangetic Dolphin is the National Aquatic Animal (declared 2011).

### Q7. India's first individual Olympic gold medalist is:
(a) Milkha Singh (b) Abhinav Bindra (c) Neeraj Chopra (d) Karnam Malleswari

**Answer: (b) Abhinav Bindra**

**Solution:** Abhinav Bindra won India's first individual Olympic gold medal in the 10m Air Rifle event at the 2008 Beijing Olympics. The first Indian Olympic gold was won in Hockey (1928 Amsterdam Olympics). India's first participation was in 1900 (Paris); Norman Pritchard won 2 silvers in athletics. The first Indian woman to win an Olympic medal was Karnam Malleswari (2000 Sydney, weightlifting, bronze). Neeraj Chopra won gold in 2020 Tokyo (javelin) and silver in 2024 Paris (javelin). PV Sindhu won silver in 2016 (badminton) and bronze in 2020.

### Q11. The "Flying Sikh" of India is:
(a) PT Usha (b) Milkha Singh (c) Harbhajan Singh (d) Mohinder Singh

**Answer: (b) Milkha Singh**

**Solution:** Milkha Singh (1929-2021) was known as the "Flying Sikh" of India. He was a sprinter who finished 4th in the 400m at the 1960 Rome Olympics (the closest any Indian had come to a track medal then). His life story inspired the film "Bhaag Milkha Bhaag". He was awarded the Padma Shri in 1959. PT Usha is called the "Payyoli Express" (1984 LA, 4th in 400m hurdles). Harbhajan Singh is a cricketer. Mohinder Singh (Mohinder Amarnath) is a former Indian cricketer.

### Q12. The National Anthem of India was composed by:
(a) Bankim Chandra (b) Rabindranath Tagore (c) Mahatma Gandhi (d) Sarojini Naidu

**Answer: (b) Rabindranath Tagore**

**Solution:** "Jana Gana Mana" was composed by Rabindranath Tagore in Sanskritized Bengali. It was first sung on 27 December 1911 at the Calcutta Session of the Indian National Congress. It was adopted as the National Anthem on 24 January 1950. The duration is ~52 seconds. The National Song "Vande Mataram" was composed by Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay (1870s, novel Anandamath) and adopted on 24 January 1950. Subramania Bharati was a Tamil poet, not the composer of the anthem.

### Q13. The motto of India's National Emblem is:
(a) Satyameva Jayate (b) Vande Mataram (c) Bharat Mata (d) Jai Hind

**Answer: (a) Satyameva Jayate**

**Solution:** The motto of India's National Emblem is "Satyameva Jayate" (Truth Alone Triumphs), taken from the Mundaka Upanishad. The National Emblem is the Lion Capital of Ashoka at Sarnath, adopted on 26 January 1950. The emblem has 4 Asiatic lions standing back-to-back (only 3 visible). Below the lions are a horse and a bull, with the dharma chakra in the center. The original Ashoka pillar dates to ~250 BCE (Mauryan period).

### Q14. The "Rocket Woman of India" is:
(a) Sunita Williams (b) Tessy Thomas (c) Kalpana Chawla (d) Ritu Karidhal

**Answer: (b) Tessy Thomas**

**Solution:** Dr. Tessy Thomas is known as the "Rocket Woman of India" for her contributions to the Agni missile programme. She is the first Indian woman scientist to head a missile project (Agni-IV, Agni-V). She was awarded the DRDO Scientist of the Year Award (2012) and the Padma Bhushan (2024). Sunita Williams and Kalpana Chawla are Indian-origin NASA astronauts (both born in the US; Kalpana died in the 2003 Columbia disaster). Ritu Karidhal is the "Rocket Woman" of ISRO (Mangalyaan/Chandrayaan missions).

### Q15. The most populous country in the world (since 2023) is:
(a) China (b) India (c) USA (d) Indonesia

**Answer: (b) India**

**Solution:** India overtook China as the most populous country in the world in April 2023, with a population of ~1.43 billion. India is also the most populous country in the world today. The most populous country before 2023 was China, which had ~1.42 billion. The next most populous countries are USA (~340 million), Indonesia (~280 million), Pakistan, Brazil, Nigeria, Bangladesh, Russia, Mexico, Ethiopia. India has 28 states and 8 UTs as of 2024-25.
