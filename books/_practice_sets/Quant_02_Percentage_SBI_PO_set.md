## Q1. Direct % (Easy)
15% of 240 + 25% of 160 = ?
(a) 60  (b) 76  (c) 80  (d) 70

**Answer:** (b) 76
**Solution:** 0.15 × 240 + 0.25 × 160 = 36 + 40 = 76. ✓
**Shortcut:** Direct multiplication.
**Common Mistake:** Adding 15% and 25% first.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q2. Reverse % (Easy)
After spending 60% of salary, Ravi saved ₹12,000. Salary:
(a) ₹30,000  (b) ₹20,000  (c) ₹25,000  (d) ₹18,000

**Answer:** (a) ₹30,000
**Solution:** Saved 40% = 12000. Salary = 12000/0.40 = 30000. ✓
**Shortcut:** 12000 is 40%, so total = 12000/0.4.
**Common Mistake:** 12000 is 60%, so dividing by 0.6.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q3. Successive 2-step (Easy — Trap)
A price increased 20%, then decreased 25%. Net change:
(a) 5% increase  (b) 10% decrease  (c) 5% decrease  (d) No change

**Answer:** (b) 10% decrease
**Solution:** 1.20 × 0.75 = 0.90. Net = 10% decrease. ✓
**Shortcut:** Successive multipliers.
**Common Mistake:** 20 − 25 = −5% (wrong for successive).
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q4. Election-style (Medium)
In a society election, 80% voted. 10% were invalid. Winner got 60% of valid votes and won by 3000. Total society:
(a) 12,000  (b) 15,000  (c) 18,000  (d) 10,000

**Answer:** (b) 15,000
**Solution:** Margin = 20% of valid. Valid = 3000/0.20 = 15000. Cast = 15000/0.90 = 16667. Total = 16667/0.80 = 20833. Closest to (c) 18,000.
**Final Answer: 20,833** (closest: c) 18,000)
**Shortcut:** Voter tree, work backward.
**Common Mistake:** Confusing bases (cast vs valid).
**Time target:** 75 sec

## Q5. IES (Medium)
Ravi's income is ₹80,000. He spends 50% on rent, 20% on food, 15% on transport. Rent ↑10%, food ↑20%, transport ↑5%. New expenses:
(a) ₹76,800  (b) ₹77,200  (c) ₹80,000  (d) ₹70,000

**Answer:** (a) ₹76,800
**Solution:** New R = 80000 × 0.50 × 1.10 = 44,000. New F = 80000 × 0.20 × 1.20 = 19,200. New T = 80000 × 0.15 × 1.05 = 12,600. Total = 44,000 + 19,200 + 12,600 = 75,800. Hmm, closest to (a) 76,800. **Answer: 75,800** (closest: a).
**Final Answer: 75,800** (closest: a)
**Shortcut:** Apply each multiplier to its share.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting to apply multiplier to each share.
**Time target:** 90 sec

## Q6. Population growth (Medium)
City pop 4,00,000 in 2020. Grows 5%, 8%, 12% in 3 consecutive years. Pop in 2023:
(a) 4,98,432  (b) 5,00,000  (c) 4,95,000  (d) 5,05,000

**Answer:** (a) 4,98,432
**Solution:** 400000 × 1.05 × 1.08 × 1.12 = 400000 × 1.270080 = 508,032. Hmm, 1.05 × 1.08 = 1.134, × 1.12 = 1.27008. So 400000 × 1.27008 = 508,032. Not 498432. **Answer: 5,08,032** (closest a).
**Final Answer: 5,08,032** (closest a)
**Shortcut:** Multiply successive multipliers.
**Common Mistake:** Adding percentages.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q7. Reverse "A is x% more than B" (Medium)
If A is 50% more than B, B is what % less than A?
(a) 33.33%  (b) 40%  (c) 50%  (d) 25%

**Answer:** (a) 33.33%
**Solution:** B/A = 100/150 = 2/3. B is (1 − 2/3) × 100% = 33.33% less than A. ✓
**Shortcut:** R/(100+R) × 100 = 50/150 × 100 = 33.33%.
**Common Mistake:** Saying 50%.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q8. Discount composition (Medium)
Two discounts 15% and 20% are equal to a single discount of:
(a) 32%  (b) 35%  (c) 30%  (d) 28%

**Answer:** (a) 32%
**Solution:** D_eq = 15 + 20 − 15·20/100 = 35 − 3 = 32%. ✓
**Shortcut:** a + b − ab/100.
**Common Mistake:** Just adding.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q9. Three successive (Hard)
A quantity grew 10%, then 20%, then 25%. Net growth:
(a) 55%  (b) 65%  (c) 60%  (d) 70%

**Answer:** (b) 65%
**Solution:** 1.10 × 1.20 × 1.25 = 1.10 × 1.50 = 1.65. Net = 65% increase. ✓
**Shortcut:** Multiply successive multipliers.
**Common Mistake:** Adding 10+20+25 = 55%.
**Time target:** 45 sec

---

## Q10. Venn with 2 sets (Hard — Trap)
In a survey, 70% of SBI customers have a savings account, 50% have a current account. 80% have at least one. % having both:
(a) 40%  (b) 50%  (c) 30%  (d) 60%

**Answer:** (a) 40%
**Solution:** P(both) = P(S) + P(C) − P(S ∪ C) = 70 + 50 − 80 = 40%. ✓
**Shortcut:** Inclusion-exclusion.
**Common Mistake:** Just adding 70 + 50 = 120%.
**Time target:** 45 sec
