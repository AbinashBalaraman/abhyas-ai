## Q1. Basic TSD (Easy)
180 km in 3 hrs. Speed:
(a) 50 km/h  (b) 60 km/h  (c) 70 km/h  (d) 80 km/h

**Answer:** (b) 60 km/h
**Solution:** S = 180/3 = 60. ✓
**Shortcut:** Direct.
**Common Mistake:** Wrong unit.
**Time target:** 15 sec

---

## Q2. Unit conversion (Easy)
36 km/h = ? m/s
(a) 8  (b) 10  (c) 12  (d) 15

**Answer:** (b) 10
**Solution:** 36 × 5/18 = 10. ✓
**Shortcut:** km/h × 5/18.
**Common Mistake:** Multiplying by 18/5.
**Time target:** 15 sec

---

## Q3. Train (Easy — Trap)
Train 100 m at 54 km/h. Time to pass a pole:
(a) 5 sec  (b) 6.67 sec  (c) 7.5 sec  (d) 8 sec

**Answer:** (b) 6.67 sec
**Solution:** 54 km/h = 15 m/s. T = 100/15 = 6.67 sec. ✓
**Shortcut:** Convert to m/s.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting conversion.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q4. Opposite direction (Medium)
A at 40 km/h, B at 60 km/h opposite, 100 km apart. Meet:
(a) 1 hr  (b) 1.5 hrs  (c) 2 hrs  (d) 0.5 hr

**Answer:** (a) 1 hr
**Solution:** v_rel = 100. T = 100/100 = 1 hr. ✓
**Shortcut:** Sum for opposite.
**Common Mistake:** Subtracting.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q5. Average speed (Medium)
Half at 20 km/h, half at 30 km/h. Average:
(a) 24 km/h  (b) 25 km/h  (c) 26 km/h  (d) 28 km/h

**Answer:** (a) 24 km/h
**Solution:** Harmonic = 2·20·30/50 = 24. ✓
**Shortcut:** Harmonic mean.
**Common Mistake:** Arithmetic (25).
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q6. Boat upstream (Medium)
Boat 18 km/h, stream 2 km/h. Time for 32 km upstream:
(a) 1.5 hrs  (b) 2 hrs  (c) 2.5 hrs  (d) 1.78 hrs

**Answer:** (b) 2 hrs
**Solution:** v_u = 16. T = 32/16 = 2 hrs. ✓
**Shortcut:** v_b − v_s.
**Common Mistake:** Adding.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q7. Race (Medium)
A beats B by 20 m in 200 m race. Speed ratio:
(a) 10:9  (b) 200:180  (c) 9:10  (d) 11:9

**Answer:** (a) 10:9
**Solution:** A:B = 200:180 = 10:9. ✓
**Shortcut:** D:(D−d).
**Common Mistake:** Reversing.
**Time target:** 15 sec

---

## Q8. Same direction (Hard)
A at 50 km/h, B at 30 km/h same direction. A starts 1 hr earlier. When does B catch A:
(a) 1.5 hrs  (b) 2.5 hrs  (c) 1 hr  (d) 2 hrs

**Answer:** (b) 2.5 hrs
**Solution:** A's head start = 50 km. v_rel = 20. T = 2.5 hrs. ✓
**Shortcut:** Head start / v_rel.
**Common Mistake:** Adding speeds.
**Time target:** 60 sec

---

## Q9. Train platform (Hard)
Train 200 m at 72 km/h. Platform 100 m. Time:
(a) 15 sec  (b) 10 sec  (c) 12 sec  (d) 18 sec

**Answer:** (a) 15 sec
**Solution:** 72 km/h = 20 m/s. T = 300/20 = 15 sec. ✓
**Shortcut:** (L+l)/v.
**Common Mistake:** Using only train length.
**Time target:** 45 sec

---

## Q10. Circular trap (Hard — Trap)
2 runners on 200 m track at 4 and 6 m/s opposite. Meeting time:
(a) 20 sec  (b) 25 sec  (c) 100 sec  (d) 10 sec

**Answer:** (a) 20 sec
**Solution:** v_rel = 10 m/s. T = 200/10 = 20 sec. ✓
**Shortcut:** L/v_rel.
**Common Mistake:** Using difference for opposite.
**Time target:** 30 sec
