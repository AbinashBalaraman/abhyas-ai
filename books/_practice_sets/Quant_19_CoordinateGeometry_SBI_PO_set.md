## Q1. ATM Locations (Easy)
Two ATMs at (0, 0) and (3, 4). Distance?
(a) 3  (b) 4  (c) 5  (d) 7

**Answer:** (c) 5
**Solution:** $\sqrt{9+16} = 5$
**Shortcut:** 3-4-5.
**Common Mistake:** Just $3+4 = 7$.
**Time target:** 20 sec

## Q2. Origin Distance (Easy)
Distance from (0,0) to (8, 6)?
(a) 10  (b) 14  (c) 100  (d) 48

**Answer:** (a) 10
**Solution:** $\sqrt{64+36} = 10$ (6-8-10 triple).
**Shortcut:** 6-8-10.
**Common Mistake:** Adding.
**Time target:** 20 sec

## Q3. Slope of Diagonal (Easy)
Diagonal from (0, 0) to (5, 5). Slope?
(a) 0  (b) 1  (c) 5  (d) Undefined

**Answer:** (b) 1
**Solution:** $5/5 = 1$
**Shortcut:** $y = x$ has slope 1.
**Common Mistake:** Using 5/0.
**Time target:** 20 sec

## Q4. Customer Distance (Medium)
Customers at (1, 1) and (4, 5). Distance?
(a) 3  (b) 4  (c) 5  (d) 25

**Answer:** (c) 5
**Solution:** $\sqrt{9 + 16} = 5$
**Shortcut:** 3-4-5.
**Common Mistake:** Adding $\sqrt{9} + \sqrt{16} = 7$.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q5. Midpoint of Customers (Medium)
Average location of customers at (2, 4) and (6, 8)?

## Q6. Circle — Through Origin (Medium)
$x^2 + y^2 + 6x - 8y = 0$. Radius?
(a) 5  (b) 7  (c) 10  (d) 25

**Answer:** (a) 5
**Solution:** $g = 3, f = -4, c = 0$. $r = \sqrt{9 + 16 - 0} = 5$.
**Shortcut:** $r = \sqrt{g^2 + f^2 - c}$.
**Common Mistake:** Missing -c.
**Time target:** 60 sec

## Q7. Centroid — Sales (Medium)
Sales data points (0, 0), (10, 20), (20, 40). Centroid?
(a) (10, 20)  (b) (15, 30)  (c) (5, 10)  (d) (10, 30)

**Answer:** (a) (10, 20)
**Solution:** $(\frac{0+10+20}{3}, \frac{0+20+40}{3}) = (10, 20)$
**Shortcut:** Average all points.
**Common Mistake:** Median instead of mean.
**Time target:** 45 sec

## Q8. Point on Line (Medium)
Is (2, 3) on line $y = 2x - 1$?
(a) Yes  (b) No  (c) Cannot say  (d) On axis

**Answer:** (a) Yes
**Solution:** $y = 2(2) - 1 = 3$. ✓
**Shortcut:** Substitute x.
**Common Mistake:** Computing $2x + 1$ instead of $2x - 1$.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q9. Triangle Vertices (Medium)
Triangle (0, 0), (3, 0), (0, 4). Type of triangle?
(a) Equilateral  (b) Right  (c) Obtuse  (d) Acute

**Answer:** (b) Right
**Solution:** $3^2 + 4^2 = 5^2$ → right triangle.
**Shortcut:** Pythagorean test.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting to check 3-4-5.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q10. Locus of Points (Hard)
Locus of point equidistant from x-axis and y-axis?
(a) y = x  (b) y = -x  (c) y = ±x  (d) y = 0

**Answer:** (c) y = ±x
**Solution:** Distance from x-axis = |y|. From y-axis = |x|. Equal: |x| = |y|, so y = ±x.
**Shortcut:** Set distances equal.
**Common Mistake:** Just y = x.
**Time target:** 90 sec

---

## Q11. Parabola Locus (Hard)
Locus of points equidistant from (0, 0) and (0, 2)?
(a) y = 1  (b) x = 0  (c) y = 0  (d) y = 2

**Answer:** (a) y = 1
**Solution:** Perpendicular bisector y = 1.
**Shortcut:** Midpoint of (0,0) and (0,2) = (0, 1). Horizontal bisector.
**Common Mistake:** Computing x = 1.
**Time target:** 60 sec

---

## Q12. Distance from Origin (Hard)
Distance from origin to line $3x + 4y + 5 = 0$?
(a) 1  (b) 5  (c) 1/5  (d) 5/√5

**Answer:** (a) 1
**Solution:** $\frac{|5|}{\sqrt{9+16}} = 5/5 = 1$
**Shortcut:** $\frac{|c|}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2}}$ from origin.
**Common Mistake:** Forgetting to take |c|.
**Time target:** 45 sec
