### Q1. Statements: A ≥ B > C = D ≤ E < F. Conclusion: A > F?
(a) Yes (b) No (c) Cannot be determined (d) A < F

**Answer:** (c) Cannot be determined
**Solution:** A ≥ B > C = D ≤ E < F. A in one segment, F at end. A and F not directly related.
**Time Target:** 40 sec |

### Q2. Statements: P < Q ≥ R = S > T. Conclusion: P > T?
(a) Yes (b) No (c) Cannot be determined (d) P ≤ T

**Answer:** (c) Cannot be determined
**Solution:** P < Q ≥ R = S > T. P and T in different segments.
**Time Target:** 35 sec |

### Q3. Statements: A < B ≤ C, C > D, D ≥ E. Conclusion: A < E?
(a) Yes (b) No (c) Cannot be determined (d) A ≥ E

**Answer:** (c) Cannot be determined
**Solution:** A < B ≤ C > D ≥ E. A < C, C > D, D ≥ E. A and E in different segments (C > D breaks the chain).
**Time Target:** 40 sec |

### Q4. Statements: X = Y ≤ Z > W = V. Conclusion: Y ≥ V?
(a) Yes (b) No (c) Cannot be determined (d) Y = V

**Answer:** (c) Cannot be determined
**Solution:** Y = X, X ≤ Z > W = V. Y and V not directly comparable.
**Time Target:** 35 sec |

### Q5. Coded: A # B means A > B; A $ B means A ≤ B; A @ B means A = B. A # B $ C @ D means?
(a) A > B ≤ C = D (b) A > B < C > D (c) A < B ≤ C = D (d) A > B ≤ C < D

**Answer:** (a) A > B ≤ C = D
**Solution:** A > B ≤ C = D.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

### Q6. Input-Output: 
Input: 84 27 56 13 49 70 32 61
Step I: 84 27 56 13 49 32 61 70
Step II: 84 27 56 13 32 49 61 70
Step III: 84 27 32 13 49 56 61 70
Step IV = ?
(a) 84 27 32 13 49 56 61 70 (b) 32 27 13 49 56 61 70 84 (c) 84 32 27 13 49 56 61 70 (d) 84 27 13 32 49 56 61 70

**Answer:** (a) 84 27 32 13 49 56 61 70
**Solution:** Hmm, comparing: Step I swaps 70 with 49. Step II swaps 49 with 32. Step III swaps 56 with 32. Step IV: swap 49 with 13? Pattern: place numbers in ascending order at the right end. Step IV: 84 27 32 13 49 56 61 70 — already that. **Answer: (a).**
**Time Target:** 60 sec |

### Q7. Input-Output: 
Input: 12 45 78 23 56 89
Step I: 89 12 45 78 23 56
Step II: 89 56 12 45 78 23
Step III: 89 56 23 12 45 78
Step IV = ?
(a) 89 56 23 12 45 78 (b) 89 56 23 45 12 78 (c) 89 56 45 12 23 78 (d) 89 56 23 12 78 45

**Answer:** (a) 89 56 23 12 45 78
**Solution:** Pattern: largest to position 1, then 2nd largest to position 2, then 3rd largest to position 3, then 4th largest to position 4. Order: 89, 56, 23, 12, 45, 78. Step IV should have 4th largest (12) at position 4: 89 56 23 12 45 78. ✓
**Time Target:** 60 sec |

### Q10. Statements: X ≥ Y, Y > Z, Z < W, W = V. Conclusion: X > V?
(a) Yes (b) No (c) Cannot be determined (d) X = V

### Q11. Coded: P @ Q = P < Q; P $ Q = P ≥ Q; P # Q = P ≠ Q. P @ Q $ R means?
(a) P < Q ≥ R (b) P < Q ≤ R (c) P > Q ≥ R (d) P < Q < R

**Answer:** (a) P < Q ≥ R
**Solution:** @ = <, $ = ≥. P < Q ≥ R.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

### Q12. Input: 32 84 19 67 45 23. Step I: 84 32 19 67 45 23. Step II: 84 67 32 19 45 23. Step III: 84 67 45 32 19 23. Step IV = ?
(a) 84 67 45 32 19 23 (b) 84 67 45 32 23 19 (c) 84 67 45 23 32 19 (d) 84 67 32 45 19 23

**Answer:** (b) 84 67 45 32 23 19
**Solution:** Descending order from left. After 84, 67, 45, 32, the next is 23 at position 5, then 19 at position 6.
**Time Target:** 60 sec |

### Q13. Statements: A ≤ B, B > C, C ≥ D, D = E. Conclusion I: A < E. Conclusion II: B > D.

**Solution:** B > C ≥ D = E → B > D ✓. I: A ≤ B, but A and E not directly linked. Cannot determine.

### Q14. Input-Output: 
Input: RAM SIT BAN GET
Step I: GET RAM SIT BAN
Step II: GET RAM BAN SIT
Step III = ?
(a) GET RAM BAN SIT (b) GET BAN RAM SIT (c) GET SIT RAM BAN (d) BAN GET RAM SIT

**Answer:** (b) GET BAN RAM SIT
**Solution:** Step I moves first word to front (alphabetical: GET first). Step II reorders remaining. Step III: BAN next alphabetically. 89 67 45 32 = wait, this is for words. Words sorted: BAN, GET, RAM, SIT. Step III: GET BAN RAM SIT.
**Time Target:** 40 sec |
