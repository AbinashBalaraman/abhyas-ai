# Practice Set: Blood Relations & Direction — SBI PO Prelims (Set 4)

**Source:** Reasoning Standard Reference Ch.16 & Ch.9, Standard Reference Reasoning SSC Ch.2 & Ch.5
**Pattern:** 10 Questions | 45 seconds each | Total 7.5 minutes
**Difficulty:** Easy-Medium (SBI PO Prelims Standard)

---

### Q1. Pointing to a man in a photo, Anil says, "He is the son of the only son of my mother." How is the man related to Anil?
(a) Brother (b) Son (c) Father (d) Nephew

**Answer:** (a) Brother
**Solution:** My mother's only son = me (or my brother). The man is Anil's brother or Anil himself.
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

---

### Q2. At sunset, a person is facing North. Shadow falls to his:
(a) Front (b) Back (c) Right (d) Left

**Answer:** (c) Right
**Solution:** Sun in West. Shadow = East. Person faces North. East is to his right.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

---

### Q3. A is father of B. C is daughter of B. D is brother of B. E is son of C. How is E related to A?
(a) Grandson (b) Son (c) Nephew (d) Brother

**Answer:** (a) Grandson
**Solution:** A father of B. C is B's daughter. E is C's son. E is A's grandson.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

---

### Q4. A is facing West. He turns 180°. Now faces:
(a) North (b) South (c) East (d) West

**Answer:** (c) East
**Solution:** 180° turn = reverse direction. West → East.
**Time Target:** 10 sec |

---

### Q5. A walks 7 km East, then 24 km South. Distance from start?
(a) 25 km (b) 26 km (c) 31 km (d) 17 km

**Answer:** (a) 25 km
**Solution:** √(7² + 24²) = √(49+576) = √625 = 25 km.
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

---

### Q6. Pointing to a girl, Raju says, "She is the daughter of the woman who is the mother of the husband of my mother." Who is she to Raju?
(a) Sister (b) Mother (c) Aunt (d) Wife

**Answer:** (c) Aunt
**Solution:** My mother's husband = my father. The mother of my father = my paternal grandmother. The daughter of my paternal grandmother = my father's sister = my aunt.
**Time Target:** 35 sec |

---

### Q7. A man walks 5 km South, 5 km West, 5 km North. Where is he from start?
(a) 5 km West (b) 5 km East (c) At start (d) 5 km North

**Answer:** (a) 5 km West
**Solution:** S 5: (0,-5). W 5: (-5,-5). N 5: (-5,0). 5 km West.
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

---

### Q8. A is the brother of B. B is the wife of C. C is the son of D. How is A related to D?
(a) Son (b) Son-in-law (c) Brother (d) Grandson

**Answer:** (a) Son
**Solution:** C is D's son. B is C's wife. A is B's brother. A is D's daughter-in-law's brother = D's son.
**Time Target:** 30 sec |

---

### Q9. At 9 AM, a person faces East. Where is his shadow?
(a) East (b) West (c) North (d) South

**Answer:** (b) West
**Solution:** 9 AM = sun East. Shadow = West.
**Time Target:** 15 sec |

---

### Q10. A is the son of B. C, the daughter of A, is married to D. E is the son of D. How is E related to B?
(a) Son (b) Grandson (c) Great-grandson (d) Nephew

**Answer:** (c) Great-grandson
**Solution:** A is B's son. C is A's daughter. E is C's son. E is B's great-grandson.
**Time Target:** 30 sec |
