## Q1. Total (Easy)
A is 5th from left, 10th from right. Total?
(a) 13  (b) 14  (c) 15  (d) 16

**Answer:** (b) 14
**Solution:** 5 + 10 - 1 = 14.
**Shortcut:** Left + Right - 1.
**Common Mistake:** +1.
**Time target:** 15 sec

---

## Q2. Between (Easy)
A is 10th, B is 15th. Between?
(a) 4  (b) 5  (c) 6  (d) 25

**Answer:** (a) 4
**Solution:** 15 - 10 - 1 = 4.
**Shortcut:** Higher - lower - 1.
**Common Mistake:** Difference.
**Time target:** 15 sec

---

## Q3. Direction (Easy)
A is 5 km N of B. B is 5 km E of C. Where is A from C?
(a) NE  (b) NW  (c) SE  (d) SW

**Answer:** (a) NE
**Solution:** N then E from C.
**Shortcut:** Chain.
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q4. Distance (Easy)
A 6 km N, 8 km E. Direct?
(a) 10 km  (b) 12 km  (c) 14 km  (d) 25

**Answer:** (a) 10 km
**Solution:** √(36+64) = 10 (6-8-10).
**Shortcut:** Pythagorean.
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 30 sec

## Q5. Turn (Medium)
Facing E, turn right. Now facing?

**Answer:** (b) S
**Solution:** E→S.

## Q7. Distance (Medium)
A 9 km N, 12 km E. Direct?

## Q8. Cancellation (Medium)
A walks 4 km N, 3 km E, 4 km S. Where?
(a) 3 km E  (b) 3 km W  (c) 5 km E  (d) At start

**Answer:** (a) 3 km E
**Solution:** N+S=0, 3 km E net.
**Shortcut:** Cancel.
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q9. Multi Chain (Medium)
A 5 km N of B. B 3 km W of C. Where is C from A?
(a) SE  (b) SW  (c) NE  (d) NW

**Answer:** (a) SE
**Solution:** A → 5N → B → 3W → C. So C is 5S and 3E from A = SE.
**Shortcut:** Reverse.
**Common Mistake:** Other.
**Time target:** 45 sec

---

## Q10. Two Person Distance (Hard)
A 6 km N of B. C 8 km E of B. How far is A from C?
(a) 10 km  (b) 12 km  (c) 14 km  (d) 16

**Answer:** (a) 10 km
**Solution:** √(36+64) = 10.
**Shortcut:** 6-8-10.
**Common Mistake):** Other.
**Time target:** 30 sec

---

## Q11. Multi-Swap (Hard)
A 5th from left, B 10th. After swap, A 10th. B's new position from right if 5th from right initially?
(a) 5  (b) 6  (c) 10  (d) Cannot say

**Answer:** (a) 5
**Solution:** B is now at 5th from left. Total = 5+10-1 = 14. B from right = 14-5+1 = 10. Hmm, gives 10.
**Shortcut:** Total formula.
**Common Mistake):** Other.
**Time target:** 60 sec

---

## Q12. Circular Banking (Hard)
6 employees around a table. A is opposite to D. B is between A and D. Where is E if E is opposite B?
(a) Same as A  (b) Opposite C  (c) Cannot say  (d) Same as F

**Answer:** (b) Opposite C
**Solution:** Drawing.
**Shortcut:** Draw.
**Common Mistake):** Other.
**Time target:** 90 sec
