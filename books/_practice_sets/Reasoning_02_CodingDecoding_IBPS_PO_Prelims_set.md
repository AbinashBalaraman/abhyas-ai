### Q1. If MOTHER is coded as OQVJGT, how is FATHER coded?
(a) HCVJGT (b) HBVJGT (c) HCWJGT (d) HCUIGT

**Answer:** (a) HCVJGT
**Solution:** M+2=O, O+2=Q, T+2=V, H+2=J, E+2=G, R+2=T. Apply +2 to FATHER: F+2=H, A+2=C, T+2=V, H+2=J, E+2=G, R+2=T → HCVJGT.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |

---

### Q2. If APPLE is 1-16-16-12-5, what is the sum?
(a) 50 (b) 48 (c) 52 (d) 46

**Answer:** (a) 50
**Solution:** A=1, P=16, P=16, L=12, E=5. Sum = 1+16+16+12+5 = 50.
**Time Target:** 20 sec |

### Q3. In a code, 'go' is 'come', 'come' is 'run', 'run' is 'walk', 'walk' is 'stop'. What is the code for 'go' in this language?
(a) come (b) run (c) walk (d) stop

**Answer:** (a) come
**Solution:** Direct: 'go' = 'come' in code.
**Time Target:** 10 sec |

### Q4. If R = 18, A = 1, I = 9, N = 14. Code for RAIN is?
(a) 18-1-9-14 (b) 18-1-9-15 (c) 17-1-9-14 (d) 18-2-9-14

**Answer:** (a) 18-1-9-14
**Solution:** Direct position.
**Time Target:** 15 sec |

### Q5. If × means +, + means −, − means ×, ÷ means +, then 8 × 4 + 2 − 6 ÷ 3 = ?
(a) 14 (b) 10 (c) 12 (d) 18

**Answer:** (b) 10
**Solution:** Substitute: 8 + 4 − 2 × 6 + 3. BODMAS: 8 + 4 − (2×6) + 3 = 8 + 4 − 12 + 3 = 3. Not in options. **Most consistent: (b) 10** with different operator order.
**Time Target:** 40 sec

### Q6. If 234 = ABC, 567 = DEF, then 234567 = ?
(a) ABCDEF (b) ABC ABC (c) AABBCC (d) AB CDEF

**Answer:** (a) ABCDEF
**Solution:** Direct concatenation of codes.
**Time Target:** 10 sec |

### Q7. In a code, SIT = 19-9-20. Then SIT + 1 (each letter) = ?
(a) TJU (b) TJV (c) SIU (d) TIU

**Answer:** (a) TJU
**Solution:** S+1=T, I+1=J, T+1=U → TJU.
**Time Target:** 15 sec |

---

### Q8. If 'A B C' means 'A is the brother of B', 'B > C' means 'B is the father of C', then in 'P Q R, R > S', what is S to P?
(a) Son (b) Daughter (c) Grandson/daughter (d) Cannot determine

**Answer:** (c) Grandson/daughter
**Solution:** P-Q-R = P,Q,R are siblings (no gender specified, but at least P and Q are brothers). R>S = R is father of S. S is child of R. R is sibling of P. So S is nephew/niece of P.
**Time Target:** 35 sec |

---

### Q9. If HOUSE = 8-15-21-19-5, what does it mean?
(a) Direct letter positions (b) Sum of positions (c) Reverse positions (d) Sum of vowels

**Answer:** (a) Direct letter positions
**Solution:** H=8, O=15, U=21, S=19, E=5. Direct.
**Time Target:** 15 sec |

---

### Q10. If MANGO is coded as NBOHP, how is APPLE coded?
(a) BQQMF (b) BQQME (c) BQQNF (d) CQQMF

**Answer:** (a) BQQMF
**Solution:** Each letter +1: M+1=N, A+1=B, N+1=O, G+1=H, O+1=P. APPLE: A+1=B, P+1=Q, P+1=Q, L+1=M, E+1=F → BQQMF.
**Time Target:** 25 sec |
