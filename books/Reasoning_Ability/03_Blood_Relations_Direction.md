# Chapter 3: Blood Relations & Direction Sense

> **Chapter Overview:** Blood Relations (Family Tree puzzles) and Direction Sense (Compass / Shadow) are **two distinct but related Reasoning topics** that together account for **3-5 questions per SSC CGL Tier-I**, **2-3 in SSC CHSL**, **2 in SSC CPO**, and **2-3 in IBPS PO Prelims**. Blood Relations tests the candidate's ability to trace family relationships from coded statements ("A is the brother of B", "C is the daughter of D"). Direction Sense tests the ability to track a person's movement (north, south, east, west, plus shadows).
>
> **Comprehensive exam-aligned study material covering standard syllabi for SSC, RRB, Banking, and State PSC examinations.

---

## 1. Conceptual Foundation

### 1.1 Blood Relations — Overview

**Blood Relations** questions describe a family in words and ask you to determine how two members are related. The relations tested are:

| Relation | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| **Direct** | Father, Mother, Son, Daughter, Brother, Sister |
| **Indirect** | Grandfather, Grandmother, Uncle, Aunt, Cousin, Nephew, Niece |
| **In-Law** | Father-in-law, Mother-in-law, Son-in-law, Daughter-in-law, Brother-in-law, Sister-in-law |
| **Spouse** | Husband, Wife |

### 1.2 The Master Family Tree (ASCII Diagram)

```
                      GREAT-GRANDFATHER (GGF)
                       /                    \
                  GRANDFATHER (GF)        GRANDFATHER's BROTHER (GFB)
                   /         \                    |
              FATHER (F)   FATHER's BROTHER (FB)  CHILDREN of GFB
              /      \          |                  = Father's Cousins
         SON     DAUGHTER   CHILDREN              = First Cousins (Once Removed)
         (Me)      |         = Cousins
                   |
              DAUGHTER's CHILD = My Niece/Nephew
```

**Key relationships to memorize:**

| If X is... | Then X is also... |
|-----------|-------------------|
| My father's son | My brother (or me, if male and no other sons) |
| My mother's son | My brother (or me) |
| My father's brother | My uncle |
| My mother's brother | My uncle (maternal uncle) |
| My uncle's son | My cousin |
| My grandfather's son | My father (or uncle) |
| My daughter's sister | My other daughter (or the same daughter) |
| My brother's son | My nephew |
| My sister's son | My nephew |
| My son's brother | My other son (or the same son) |

### 1.3 4 Sub-Types of Blood Relation (Competitive Exam Question Bank Classification)

Competitive Exam Question Bank Reasoning SSC Sec.7 breaks Blood Relations into **4 sub-types**:

| Type | Description | Example |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **Type 1** | Direct relation (one-step) | "A is the brother of B. How is A related to B's son?" |
| **Type 2** | Coded relation (two/three-step) | "Pointing to a photograph, A says, 'She is the daughter of my grandfather's only son.' How is A related to her?" |
| **Type 3** | Family tree (full diagram) | "A and B are brothers. C is A's father's nephew. D is B's wife. How is D related to C?" |
| **Type 4** | Mixed / Puzzle | Multi-generation with multiple marriages |

### 1.4 Direction Sense — Overview

**Direction Sense** questions describe a person's movements and ask the final direction/distance. The cardinal directions are:

```
                 NORTH
                   |
                   |
                   |
        WEST ------+------ EAST
                   |
                   |
                   |
                 SOUTH
```

The 8 secondary directions (and their abbreviations):

| Direction | Abbreviation |
|-----------|--------------|
| North | N |
| South | S |
| East | E |
| West | W |
| North-East | NE |
| North-West | NW |
| South-East | SE |
| South-West | SW |

### 1.5 The Standard Movement Operations

**Turning directions** when facing a direction and told to turn:

| Current Facing | Turn Right | Turn Left | Turn 180° |
|----------------|------------|-----------|-----------|
| North | East | West | South |
| South | West | East | North |
| East | South | North | West |
| West | North | South | East |

**Turning at angles:**

| Turn | Result |
|------|--------|
| 45° right (from N) | NE |
| 90° right (from N) | E |
| 135° right (from N) | SE |
| 180° (from N) | S |
| 45° left (from N) | NW |

### 1.6 Shadow Direction Rules (Standard ReferenceCh.9)

Shadow questions ask: "If a person is standing facing X direction, in which direction will his shadow fall at sunrise/sunset/noon?"

| Condition | Shadow Direction |
|-----------|------------------|
| **Sunrise** (sun in East) | Shadow falls to **West** |
| **Sunset** (sun in West) | Shadow falls to **East** |
| **Noon** (sun overhead) | No shadow / at feet |

If the person faces East at sunrise, the shadow falls West (behind them).
If the person faces West at sunrise, the shadow falls West (in front of them, same direction).

### 1.7 Distance Calculation Formula

For direction problems involving distance, the Pythagorean theorem applies:

$$d = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2}$$

For the standard 3-4-5 right triangle:

$$3^2 + 4^2 = 9 + 16 = 25 = 5^2$$

So if a person walks 3 km North and 4 km East, the distance from start is 5 km NE.

                /  |  \
              SW   |   SE
                    |

                    S

## 2. Step-by-Step Solving Algorithm

### 2.1 For Blood Relations (Pointing-Photo Type)

```
Step 1: Parse the statement.
        Example: "Pointing to a man, Sita said, 'He is the son of the
        only brother of my father's wife.'"

Step 2: Identify the speaker's reference point.
        Speaker = Sita. Reference point = "the man" being pointed at.

Step 3: Trace the relation path from speaker to reference.
        "My father's wife" = my mother.
        "My mother's only brother" = my maternal uncle.
        "The son of my maternal uncle" = my maternal uncle's son
        = my cousin.

Step 4: Determine the relation between speaker and reference.
        Speaker = Sita; reference = her cousin.
        Sita's relation to the man = "she is his cousin"
        OR "the man is her cousin".

Step 5: Answer the specific question asked (inward or outward).
```

### 2.2 For Blood Relations (Family Tree Type)

```
Step 1: Draw a family tree from the given statements.
Step 2: Use gender symbols:   = male, -  = female, = = marriage, | = parent.
Step 3: Place each family member.
Step 4: Verify all relations are consistent.
Step 5: Answer the question by tracing the path.
```

### 2.3 For Direction Sense (Movement Tracking)

```
Step 1: Draw a starting point. Mark it.
Step 2: Read the first movement: "walks 5 km North" → move 5 units up.
Step 3: Read the next direction change: "turns right" → now facing East.
Step 4: Continue tracking each movement.
Step 5: At the end, read the final direction and distance from start.
Step 6: For distance, use Pythagoras if movement has both N-S and E-W components.
```

### 2.4 For Shadow Problems

```
Step 1: Identify sun position from time of day:
        - Morning (sunrise) → Sun in East
        - Evening (sunset) → Sun in West
        - Noon → Sun overhead (no shadow)

Step 2: Determine shadow direction (opposite to sun):
        - Morning → Shadow West
        - Evening → Shadow East

Step 3: Determine the person's facing direction.

Step 4: Calculate shadow direction relative to facing:
        - Person faces East at morning → shadow falls West (behind them)
        - Person faces West at morning → shadow falls West (in front)
        - Person faces North at morning → shadow falls SW (left)
        - Person faces South at morning → shadow falls NW (right)
```

### 2.5 The "Facing → Shadow" Master Table

| Person Faces | Morning (Sun East) | Evening (Sun West) |
|--------------|--------------------|--------------------|
| North | SW (left-back) | SE (right-back) |
| South | NW (left-front) | NE (right-front) |
| East | West (behind) | East (in front — actually behind, since shadow is West, person faces East, so shadow is behind) |
| West | West (in front) | East (behind) |

Rule: Shadow is **always opposite to sun**. The person's relation to shadow depends on facing.

## 3. Trap Detection & Error Prevention Guide

### 3.1 Top 10 Blood Relation Traps

1. **"My son" vs. "My brother's son".** The speaker might say "my son" but the context means "my brother's son" if the speaker has no sons. Trap: literal interpretation.

2. **"Only brother" vs. "brother".** "Only brother" means unique brother. "Brother" could be one of many. The unique constraint changes who the brother is.

3. **"My father's only son"** is the speaker themselves (in most Indian family structures) or another unique brother. **This is the most-tested trap.**

4. **Paternal vs. Maternal uncle.** Father's brother = paternal uncle. Mother's brother = maternal uncle. Both are "uncle" but have different paths to other relatives.

5. **In-laws confusion.** My sister's husband is my brother-in-law. My husband's brother is also my brother-in-law. Trap: assuming both have the same relation path.

6. **Generation skip.** "My grandfather's son" is my father OR my uncle (if grandfather had multiple sons). Trap: assuming always father.

7. **Cousin types.** Children of father's brother = paternal cousins. Children of mother's sister = maternal cousins. Same cousin, different paths.

8. **"Pointing to a photograph" trap.** "He is my mother's father" → he is the maternal grandfather. Trap: assuming paternal grandfather.

9. **Married couple in tree.** When a woman marries into a family, her siblings are NOT in-laws of her husband's family. Trap: confusing in-laws and blood relations.

10. **Negative relation (not).** "A is not the brother of B" means A could be anything EXCEPT B's brother (could be sister, cousin, etc.). Trap: assuming one specific relation.

### 3.2 Sample trap question + correct interpretation

> **Trap question:** "Pointing to a man in a photograph, Sita says, 'His mother is the only daughter of my mother's mother.' How is Sita related to the man?"
>
> **Trap interpretation:** "My mother's mother" = my maternal grandmother. "Only daughter" of my maternal grandmother. But my mother is the only daughter of my maternal grandmother (since my mother is from that family). So "his mother" is my mother. The man is my brother.
>
> **Correct interpretation:** Same answer: **Sita's brother** (or Sita herself, if the man is herself from a different angle).
>
>

### 3.3 Top 10 Direction Sense Traps

1. **Initial facing ≠ initial position.** "A walks North facing East" — A's body is facing East, but A's movement is North. Trap: confusing facing with movement direction.

2. **"Turns right" without specifying from what.** Always take "turns right" relative to CURRENT facing. Trap: assuming always from North.

3. **Right turn vs. clockwise.** Both are the same. "Turns left" = counter-clockwise.

4. **"Facing X, shadow falls Y".** Shadow falls opposite to sun, regardless of facing. The facing tells you if shadow is in front or behind.

5. **Distance question without units.** Some questions don't ask distance. Trap: solving for distance when only direction is asked.

6. **Final direction ≠ final facing.** "After 4 right turns from North" → facing West (not N or S). Trap: assuming facing = movement.

7. **Clock analogy.** A clock's 12 = North. The hour hand at 3 = East. The minute hand at 6 = South.

8. **"Returns to start".** The path forms a closed figure (often rectangle or triangle). Use coordinates.

9. **Diagonal movements.** "North-East" means N and E components are equal. Distance = √2 × component distance.

10. **Right vs. Right-hand-side.** Some questions say "to his right" which is relative to his facing, not absolute.

## 4. Type-Wise Question Bank with Solutions

### Type 1: Direct Blood Relation (One-Step)

**Solved Example 1:**
> "A is the brother of B. B is the sister of C. C is the father of D. How is A related to D?"
>
> **Solution:** A is brother of B. B is sister of C. So A is brother of C (siblings). C is father of D. So A is uncle of D.
> **Answer: A is the uncle of D**

### Type 2: Pointing-Photo Type

**Solved Example 2:**
> "Pointing to a photograph, A says, 'She is the daughter of my grandfather's only son.' How is the person in the photograph related to A?"
>
> **Solution:** A's grandfather's only son = A's father (if grandfather has only one son, which is A's father; if grandfather has multiple sons, "only son" is still A's father because A's father is one of them). The daughter of A's father = A's sister.
> **Answer: Sister**

**Solved Example 3:**
> "Pointing to a man, Sita said, 'He is the son of the only brother of my mother's mother.' How is Sita related to that man?"
>
> **Solution:** My mother's mother = my maternal grandmother. My maternal grandmother's only brother = my maternal grandmother's brother = my grandmother's brother. The son of this person = my maternal grandmother's brother's son = my mother's cousin = my cousin (maternal).
> **Answer: Cousin**

### Type 3: Family Tree (Multi-Step)

**Solved Example 4 (Family Tree Construction):**
> "A and B are brothers. C is the father of A. D is the son of C's wife. E is the mother of D. How is B related to E?"
>
> **Solution:**
> - A and B are brothers (both male, siblings).
> - C is A's father, so C is also B's father.
> - C's wife = A and B's mother.
> - D is the son of C's wife = A and B's brother (or another son of their mother).
> - E is D's mother = C's wife = A and B's mother.
> - B is the son of E.
>
> **Answer: B is the son of E**

**Solved Example 5 (Family Tree ASCII):**
> ```
> C (Grandfather)
> / \
> (M) A (Uncle)
> |
> D----+-----E (E is A's daughter)
> (married)
> |
> F (son)
> ```
> Question: "How is F related to A?"
>
> **Solution:** F is the son of D. E is A's daughter. D married E (assumed). F is the grandson of A (through E).
> **Answer: Grandson (Grandson of A)**

### Type 4: Direction Sense (Movement Tracking)

**Solved Example 6 (SSC CGL Standard):**
> "A walks 5 km North, then turns right and walks 3 km, then turns right again and walks 5 km. In which direction is A from the starting point?"
>
> **Solution:**
> - Start at origin (0, 0). North = +y.
> - Walk 5 N: (0, 5)
> - Turn right (now facing E). Walk 3: (3, 5)
> - Turn right (now facing S). Walk 5: (3, 0)
> - Final position (3, 0) = 3 km East of start.
>
> **Answer: East**

**Solved Example 7 (Distance + Direction):**
> "A walks 4 km South, then turns left and walks 3 km. How far is A from the starting point, and in which direction?"
>
> **Solution:**
> - Start at (0, 0). South = −y.
> - Walk 4 S: (0, −4)
> - Turn left (now facing E). Walk 3: (3, −4)
> - Distance = √(3² + 4²) = √25 = 5 km
> - Direction: South-East
>
> **Answer: 5 km South-East**

### Type 5: Shadow Direction

**Solved Example 8 (SSC CGL 2017):**
> "If a man is standing facing North-East at sunrise, in which direction will his shadow fall?"
>
> **Solution:** Sunrise = sun in East. Shadow falls West. The man faces NE; shadow falls West. Relative to facing: West is to the man's LEFT-BACK.
>
> **Answer: West** (or "to his left-back")
>

**Solved Example 9:**
> "At sunset, a woman is facing South-West. Where is her shadow?"
>
> **Solution:** Sunset = sun in West. Shadow falls East. Woman faces SW; East is to her RIGHT-BACK.
>
> **Answer: East**

### Type 6: Distance-Only Problems

**Solved Example 10:**
> "Ravi walks 6 km East, then 8 km North, then 6 km West, then 8 km South. How far is he from the starting point?"
>
> **Solution:**
> - Start (0,0). East 6: (6, 0). North 8: (6, 8). West 6: (0, 8). South 8: (0, 0). Back to start.
> **Answer: 0 km (back at start)**

### Type 7: Clock-Compass Problems

**Solved Example 11:**
> "If a clock shows 3 o'clock and the hour hand points East, in which direction does the minute hand point?"
>
> **Solution:** 3 o'clock = hour hand at 3 (East). Minute hand at 12 (top) = North.
> **Answer: North**

### Type 8: Mixed Blood + Direction (Multi-Step)

**Solved Example 12:**
> "A and B start from the same point. A walks 5 km North. B walks 3 km East, then turns left and walks 4 km. How far apart are A and B?"
>
> **Solution:**
> - A: (0, 5)
> - B: turn left from East = North. Walk 3 E + 4 N = (3, 4).
> - Distance = √((3-0)² + (5-4)²) = √(9+1) = √10 ≈ 3.16 km
>
> **Answer: √10 km ≈ 3.16 km**

## 5. Timed Practice Sets

### Set A — Warm-Up (5 questions, 30-45 sec each)

**Q1.** A is the son of B. B is the daughter of C. How is A related to C?
(a) Son (b) Grandson (c) Brother (d) Nephew
**Solution:** B is C's daughter; A is B's son; so A is C's grandson.
**Answer: (b) Grandson**

**Q2.** Pointing to a man, Anu says, "He is the husband of my mother's sister." How is the man related to Anu?
(a) Uncle (b) Brother (c) Father (d) Father-in-law
**Solution:** Mother's sister = maternal aunt. Her husband = Anu's maternal uncle (by marriage).
**Answer: (a) Uncle**

**Q3.** A walks 3 km North, then 4 km East. What is the distance from start?
(a) 5 km (b) 6 km (c) 7 km (d) 1 km
**Solution:** √(3² + 4²) = √25 = 5 km NE.
**Answer: (a) 5 km**

**Q4.** A man is facing South. He turns 90° clockwise. Which direction does he face now?
(a) East (b) West (c) North (d) South
**Solution:** Facing S + 90° clockwise = facing W.
**Answer: (b) West**

**Q5.** At sunrise, a person faces North. Where is his shadow?
(a) North (b) South (c) East (d) West
**Solution:** Sunrise = sun East. Shadow = West. Person faces North. West is behind him.
**Answer: (d) West** (behind him)

### Set B — Exam Simulation (SSC CGL Tier-I Standard)

**Q6.** Pointing to a photograph, A says, "She is the daughter of my grandfather's only daughter." How is the person related to A?
(a) Sister (b) Mother (c) Aunt (d) Cousin
**Solution:** A's grandfather's only daughter = A's mother (or A's aunt, if grandfather had multiple daughters). Most common interpretation: A's mother. So the person is A's mother's daughter = A's sister (or A herself).
**Answer: (a) Sister**

**Q7.** A and B are brothers. C is A's father's brother. D is B's mother's husband. How is D related to C?
(a) Same person (b) Brother (c) Father (d) Cousin
**Solution:** A's father = B's father (since they're brothers). A's father's brother = A's uncle (C). B's mother's husband = B's father = A's father. So D = A's father. C = A's father's brother = A's uncle. D is C's brother.
**Answer: (b) Brother**

**Q8.** A walks 10 km South, turns right, walks 6 km, turns right, walks 10 km. Where is A from start?
(a) 6 km West (b) 6 km East (c) 6 km North (d) At start
**Solution:**
- Start (0, 0). South 10: (0, −10).
- Right turn = facing West. Walk 6: (−6, −10).
- Right turn = facing North. Walk 10: (−6, 0).
- Final: 6 km West of start.
**Answer: (a) 6 km West**

**Q9.** A is the father of B. B is the sister of C. C is the mother of D. How is A related to D?
(a) Father (b) Grandfather (c) Uncle (d) Father-in-law
**Solution:** A is father of B. B is sister of C. So A is father of C (or C is A's child). C is mother of D. So A is grandfather of D.
**Answer: (b) Grandfather**

**Q10.** At 6 PM, a person is facing West. Where is his shadow?
(a) West (b) East (c) North (d) South
**Solution:** 6 PM = sunset. Sun in West. Shadow = East. Person faces West. East is behind him.
**Answer: (b) East (behind him)**

### Set C — Advanced (IBPS PO Mains Level)

**Q11.** A, B, C, D are four family members. A is the son of B. B is the daughter of C. D is the wife of C. How is A related to D?
(a) Son (b) Grandson (c) Nephew (d) Cannot be determined
**Solution:** C is the patriarch. C's wife = D. C's daughter = B. B's son = A. So A is the grandson of C. D is C's wife. So A is D's grandson.
**Answer: (b) Grandson**

**Q12.** A man walks 5 km towards South. He then turns right and walks 3 km. He again turns right and walks 5 km. In which direction is he from the starting point?
(a) East (b) West (c) South (d) North
**Solution:**
- Start (0, 0). South 5: (0, −5).
- Right turn = facing West. Walk 3: (−3, −5).
- Right turn = facing North. Walk 5: (−3, 0).
- Final: 3 km West of start.
**Answer: (b) West**

**Q13.** Pointing to a lady, a man said, "She is the daughter of the woman who is the mother of the husband of my mother." How is the lady related to the man?
(a) Sister (b) Daughter (c) Aunt (d) Wife
**Solution:** My mother = M. My mother's husband = my father = F. The mother of F = my paternal grandmother = GM. The daughter of GM = my aunt (father's sister) OR my mother. Most consistent: my aunt (since "the mother of my mother's husband" is my paternal grandmother).
**Answer: (c) Aunt**

**Q14.** Ram walks 8 km North, then turns right and walks 6 km, turns right and walks 8 km. How far is he from the starting point?
(a) 6 km East (b) 6 km West (c) 14 km East (d) 0 km
**Solution:** Start (0,0). N 8: (0,8). Right=E. Walk 6: (6, 8). Right=S. Walk 8: (6, 0). Final 6 km East.
**Answer: (a) 6 km East**

**Q15.** A family has 2 parents and 3 children. The father is a doctor. The mother is a teacher. They have 2 sons and 1 daughter. The daughter is the youngest. How is the elder son related to the mother?
(a) Son (b) Brother (c) Nephew (d) Uncle
**Solution:** The elder son is the mother's son.
**Answer: (a) Son**

## 6. Quick Revision Sheet

### 6.1 Core Rules in Bullet Form

**Blood Relations:**
1. **Father's brother = Uncle (paternal).** Mother's brother = Uncle (maternal).
2. **Grandfather's son = Father OR Uncle.** "Only son" = unique.
3. **Brother's son = Nephew.** Sister's son = Nephew.
4. **Sister's husband = Brother-in-law.** Husband's brother = Brother-in-law.
5. **"Pointing to photograph" trap:** Trace from speaker to photo.
6. **"My father's only son" = Me (or my brother if no other sons).**
7. **In-laws do NOT share blood relations.** Spouse's family = in-laws.

**Direction Sense:**
1. **Facing → Movement.** Walking direction is relative to facing.
2. **Right turn = clockwise 90°.** Left = counter-clockwise.
3. **Shadow = opposite of sun.** Sun East → Shadow West.
4. **Distance:** √(a² + b²) for perpendicular components.
5. **Pythagorean triples:** 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17, 7-24-25.

### 6.2 Decision Tree

**Blood Relations:**

| If you see... | Then do... |
|---------------|------------|
| "My X's Y" | X is the relation, Y is the kinship |
| "Only" qualifier | Unique constraint; usually the only path |
| "Pointing to photo" | Trace from speaker |
| Family tree with multiple marriages | Draw the tree carefully |
| In-law relations | Spouse's family, not blood |

**Direction Sense:**

| If you see... | Then do... |
|---------------|------------|
| Walk N/E/S/W | Track coordinates |
| Turn right/left | Update facing |
| Shadow + time | Determine sun position; shadow = opposite |
| Distance | Use Pythagoras |
| Final direction | Calculate Δx, Δy |

### 6.3 Time Allocation Guide

|---------------|------|----------|
| Direct blood (one-step) | 20-30 sec | Direct mapping |
| Pointing-photo | 45-60 sec | Trace relation path |
| Family tree | 60-90 sec | Draw tree |
| Direction tracking | 45-60 sec | Coordinate plot |
| Shadow direction | 30-45 sec | Sun position |
| Distance (Pythagoras) | 30-45 sec | Apply formula |

### 6.4 The Master Family Tree (Quick Reference)

```
         ME
        / \
    BROTHER SISTER
       |
     NEPHEW
       
FATHER = Father's son (myself, if no brothers)
MOTHER = Mother's daughter (myself, if no sisters)
UNCLE = Father's brother OR Mother's brother
AUNT = Father's sister OR Mother's sister
COUSIN = Uncle's/Aunt's child
NEPHEW = Brother's/Sister's son
NIECE = Brother's/Sister's daughter
GRANDFATHER = Father's/Mother's father
GRANDMOTHER = Father's/Mother's mother
SON-IN-LAW = Husband of daughter
DAUGHTER-IN-LAW = Wife of son
BROTHER-IN-LAW = Sister's husband OR Spouse's brother
SISTER-IN-LAW = Brother's wife OR Spouse's sister
```

### 6.5 Direction Quick Reference

| Move | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Turn Right from N | Face E |
| Turn Right from E | Face S |
| Turn Right from S | Face W |
| Turn Right from W | Face N |
| 180° turn | Reverse direction |
| Walk N then E | End up NE |
| Walk N then S (same distance) | Back to start |
