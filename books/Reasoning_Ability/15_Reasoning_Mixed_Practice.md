## 2. The 50-Question Mixed Practice Set

### Set A — Analogy, Classification, Series, Coding (Q1–Q10)

**Q1. (Analogy - Number)** 4: 67:: 5:?
(a) 124 (b) 126 (c) 128 (d) 130
**Solution:** 4: 4³+3 = 67. 5³+3 = 128. **Answer: (c).** *Time: 30 sec.* [See Ch.1]

**Q2. (Analogy - Word)** Doctor: Hospital:: Teacher:?
(a) Student (b) School (c) Book (d) Class
**Solution:** Doctor works in Hospital. Teacher works in School. **Answer: (b).** *Time: 15 sec.* [See Ch.1]

**Q3. (Classification - Word)** Find odd one: Mango, Apple, Banana, Potato
(a) Mango (b) Apple (c) Banana (d) Potato
**Solution:** Potato is a vegetable; others are fruits. **Answer: (d).** *Time: 10 sec.* [See Ch.1]

**Q4. (Classification - Number)** Find odd one: 8, 27, 64, 100, 125
(a) 8 (b) 27 (c) 64 (d) 100
**Solution:** 100 is not a perfect cube (others are). **Answer: (d).** *Time: 30 sec.* [See Ch.1]

**Q5. (Series - Number)** 2, 6, 12, 20, 30,?
(a) 36 (b) 40 (c) 42 (d) 44
**Solution:** Pattern: n²+n = 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, 42. **Answer: (c) 42.** *Time: 30 sec.* [See Ch.1]

**Q6. (Series - Letter)** A, C, F, J,?
(a) N (b) O (c) P (d) Q
**Solution:** Differences: 2, 3, 4, 5. A+2=C, C+3=F, F+4=J, J+5=O. **Answer: (b) O.** *Time: 30 sec.* [See Ch.1]

**Q7. (Coding - Direct)** If HOUSE is coded as 12345, then USE =?
(a) 345 (b) 345 (c) 345 (d) 345
**Solution:** U=4, S=3, E=5. So 435. **Answer: 435.** *Time: 15 sec.* [See Ch.2]

**Q8. (Coding - Letter Shift)** If CAT = DBU, then DOG =?
(a) EPH (b) EPG (c) EPH (d) EOG
**Solution:** Each letter +1. D+1=E, O+1=P, G+1=H. **Answer: EPH.** *Time: 30 sec.* [See Ch.2]

**Q9. (Analogy - Letter-Cluster)** AB: YZ:: BC:?
(a) XY (b) XW (c) WX (d) YW
**Solution:** AB ↔ YZ (opposite). BC ↔ YX. **Answer: YX or (a) XY.** *Time: 30 sec.* [See Ch.1]

**Q10. (Series - Alpha-Numeric)** A1, B2, C3, D4,?
(a) E5 (b) E6 (c) F5 (d) E4
**Solution:** Letter+1, Number+1. **Answer: (a) E5.** *Time: 15 sec.* [See Ch.1]

### Set B — Blood Relations, Direction, Syllogism (Q11–Q20)

**Q11. (Blood Relation)** A is B's father. B is C's sister. How is A related to C?
(a) Father (b) Mother (c) Brother (d) Cannot say
**Solution:** B is C's sister, so A is C's father (or mother, but father stated). **Answer: (a) Father.** *Time: 30 sec.* [See Ch.3]

**Q12. (Blood Relation)** Pointing to a man, Sita said, "He is the son of my mother's only son." How is the man related to Sita?
(a) Brother (b) Nephew (c) Son (d) Cousin
**Solution:** Mother's only son = Sita's brother. His son = Sita's nephew. **Answer: (b) Nephew.** *Time: 60 sec.* [See Ch.3]

**Q13. (Direction)** A walks 5 km N, then 3 km E, then 5 km S. Where is A from start?
(a) 3 km E (b) 3 km W (c) 5 km E (d) 5 km N
**Solution:** N+S=0, net 3 km E. **Answer: (a) 3 km E.** *Time: 30 sec.* [See Ch.11]

**Q14. (Direction)** Facing N, turn left, right, left. Now facing?
(a) N (b) S (c) E (d) W
**Solution:** N→W→N→W. **Answer: (d) W.** *Time: 30 sec.* [See Ch.11]

**Q15. (Syllogism)** Statements: All cats are animals. All animals are living. Conclusion: All cats are living.
(a) Follows (b) Does not follow (c) Cannot say (d) Both A and B
**Solution:** Transitive: All cats ⊂ Animals ⊂ Living. So All cats are living. **Answer: (a) Follows.** *Time: 30 sec.* [See Ch.4]

**Q16. (Syllogism)** Statements: Some A are B. All B are C. Conclusion: Some A are C.
(a) Follows (b) Does not follow (c) Cannot say (d) Only if all A are C
**Solution:** Some A are B (subset). All B are C. So some A are C. **Answer: (a) Follows.** *Time: 30 sec.* [See Ch.4]

**Q17. (Syllogism)** Statements: No A is B. Some B are C. Conclusion: Some A are C.
(a) Follows (b) Does not follow (c) Cannot say (d) Only if all
**Solution:** No A is B; A and B are disjoint. So no overlap. Cannot conclude. **Answer: (b) Does not follow.** *Time: 60 sec.* [See Ch.4]

**Q18. (Blood Relation)** A and B are brothers. C is A's father. D is C's father. How is B related to D?
(a) Son (b) Grandson (c) Brother (d) Father
**Solution:** B is C's son. C is D's son. So B is D's grandson. **Answer: (b) Grandson.** *Time: 60 sec.* [See Ch.3]

**Q19. (Direction - Distance)** A is 6 km N of B. C is 8 km E of B. Distance A to C?
(a) 10 km (b) 12 km (c) 14 km (d) 16 km
**Solution:** √(36+64) = √100 = 10. **Answer: (a) 10 km.** *Time: 30 sec.* [See Ch.11]

**Q20. (Syllogism - Negative)** Statements: All P are Q. No Q is R. Conclusion: No P is R.
(a) Follows (b) Does not follow (c) Cannot say (d) Some
**Solution:** All P ⊂ Q. No Q = R (disjoint). So No P = R. **Answer: (a) Follows.** *Time: 60 sec.* [See Ch.4]

### Set C — Seating, Coding-Decoding, Order (Q21–Q30)

**Q21. (Linear Seating)** 5 people A, B, C, D, E sit in a row. A is left of B. C is right of A. D is at the right end. E is left of A. Order?
(a) E, A, C, B, D (b) D, B, C, A, E (c) A, B, C, D, E (d) E, C, A, B, D
**Solution:** D right end. E left of A. So E, A or E, A, C,?, D. B must be between A and D. So E, A, C, B, D or E, A, B, C, D. C is right of A, so E, A, C, B, D. **Answer: (a).** *Time: 90 sec.* [See Ch.5]

**Q22. (Circular Seating)** 6 people around a circle, A opposite to B, C next to A, D opposite to C, E next to B. Where is F?
(a) Between D and E (b) Between A and C (c) Opposite to A (d) Cannot determine
**Solution:** Place A. B opposite. C next to A. D opposite to C. E next to B. F must be the remaining. **Answer: (a) Between D and E.** *Time: 90 sec.* [See Ch.5]

**Q23. (Coding)** In a code, PEN = 123, NIB = 345, BAG = 567. What is ING?
(a) 358 (b) 358 (c) 358 (d) 358
**Solution:** P=1, E=2, N=3. I=4? Let me check NIB = 345. N=3, I=4, B=5. BAG = 567. B=5, A=6, G=7. ING = 4, 3? Wait, I-N-G. I=4, N=3, G=7. So 437. **Answer: 437.** *Time: 60 sec.* [See Ch.2]

**Q24. (Inequality)** A > B ≥ C < D = E > F. True: A > F?
(a) Yes (b) No (c) Cannot say (d) Only if all
**Solution:** A > B ≥ C. D = E > F. No link between A and F. **Answer: (c) Cannot say.** *Time: 60 sec.* [See Ch.6]

**Q25. (Ranking)** A is 5th from top. B is 10th from top. C is between A and B. Positions?
(a) 5, 6, 7 (b) 5, 7, 10 (c) 5, 8, 10 (d) 5, 9, 10
**Solution:** A=5, B=10, C between (6, 7, 8, or 9). All options. Closest: 5, 7, 10 (c is 8). **Answer: (b) 5, 7, 10.** *Time: 60 sec.* [See Ch.11]

**Q26. (Input-Output)** Input: cat dog bat ant. Step 1: cat bat dog ant. Step 2: cat bat ant dog. Step 3:?
(a) ant bat cat dog (b) cat bat ant dog (c) bat cat ant dog (d) cat ant bat dog
**Solution:** Pattern: smallest at end, then second smallest, etc. Step 3: cat bat ant dog (already). Hmm. The output is "cat bat ant dog" — smallest to largest alphabetically? No, dog is largest. Confused.

Let me re-examine: cat, dog, bat, ant → cat, bat, dog, ant → cat, bat, ant, dog. 
Step 1: 1st and 3rd swapped.
Step 2: 3rd and 4th swapped.
Step 3: maybe 2nd and 4th? bat, cat, ant, dog? **Answer: (c).** *Time: 90 sec.* [See Ch.6]

**Q27. (Number Series)** 3, 9, 27, 81,?
(a) 162 (b) 200 (c) 243 (d) 256
**Solution:** ×3 each time. 81×3 = 243. **Answer: (c) 243.** *Time: 15 sec.* [See Ch.1]

**Q28. (Letter Series)** AZ, BY, CX, DW,?
(a) EU (b) EV (c) FW (d) FV
**Solution:** First letter increases (A, B, C, D, E), second decreases (Z, Y, X, W, V). **Answer: (b) EV.** *Time: 30 sec.* [See Ch.1]

**Q29. (Square Seating)** 4 people A, B, C, D at corners of a square. A opposite C, B opposite D. A faces North. Where does B face?
(a) North (b) South (c) East (d) West
**Solution:** A North, C South, B East or West, D the other. **Answer: (c) East or (d) West.** *Time: 60 sec.* [See Ch.5]

**Q30. (Data Sufficiency)** Q: What is the value of x? Stmt 1: x + 5 = 10. Stmt 2: x = 5.
(a) Stmt 1 alone (b) Stmt 2 alone (c) Both (d) Each alone
**Solution:** Stmt 1: x = 5. Stmt 2: x = 5. Both alone sufficient. **Answer: (d).** *Time: 60 sec.* [See Ch.14]

### Set D — Non-Verbal, Math Operations, Venn, Word (Q31–Q40)

**Q31. (Mirror Image)** Mirror image of "b":
(a) d (b) q (c) p (d) b
**Solution:** b's mirror = d. **Answer: (a) d.** *Time: 5 sec.* [See Ch.9]

**Q32. (Water Image)** Water image of "T":
(a) ⊥ (b) T (c) ⊢ (d) Different
**Solution:** T top-bottom = ⊥. **Answer: (a).** *Time: 5 sec.* [See Ch.9]

**Q33. (Figure Counting)** Triangles in a 5-pointed star:
(a) 5 (b) 10 (c) 25 (d) 35
**Solution:** 35. **Answer: (d) 35.** *Time: 30 sec.* [See Ch.13]

**Q34. (Painted Cube)** 4×4×4 cube painted. Cubes with 2 faces painted?
(a) 12 (b) 24 (c) 36 (d) 48
**Solution:** 12(4-2) = 24. **Answer: (b) 24.** *Time: 30 sec.* [See Ch.13]

**Q35. (BODMAS)** Evaluate: 6 + 4 × 3 − 8 ÷ 2
(a) 14 (b) 16 (c) 18 (d) 20
**Solution:** 4×3=12, 8÷2=4. 6+12-4 = 14. **Answer: (a) 14.** *Time: 30 sec.* [See Ch.12]

**Q36. (Symbol Substitution)** If + = ×, × = +, then 5 + 3 × 2 =?
(a) 11 (b) 13 (c) 16 (d) 17
**Solution:** 5 × 3 + 2 = 15 + 2 = 17. **Answer: (d) 17.** *Time: 60 sec.* [See Ch.12]

**Q37. (Number Analogy)** 6: 35:: 7:?
(a) 48 (b) 49 (c) 50 (d) 51
**Solution:** 6: 6×5+5 = 35. 7: 7×6+6 = 48. **Answer: (a) 48.** *Time: 30 sec.* [See Ch.12]

**Q38. (Venn Diagram)** Venn for "Mammals, Cows, Birds":
(a) Two disjoint inside one (b) Three overlapping (c) Three disjoint (d) All inside one
**Solution:** Cows are Mammals. Birds separate from Mammals. **Answer: (a) — Cows inside Mammals, Birds separate.** *Time: 30 sec.* [See Ch.14]

**Q39. (Dictionary Order)** First in dictionary: EGG, EGO, EAT, EIGHT
(a) EAT (b) EGG (c) EGO (d) EIGHT
**Solution:** E-A-T, E-G-G, E-G-O, E-I-G-H-T. A < G < I. **First: EAT.** **Answer: (a).** *Time: 30 sec.* [See Ch.14]

**Q40. (Word Formation)** Which cannot be formed from "PROBLEM"?
(a) PROBE (b) LEMON (c) PROBLEM (d) BLOOM
**Solution:** BLOOM: B-L-O-O-M. PROBLEM has only 1 O, 1 B. BLOOM needs 2 O's. Cannot form. **Answer: (d) BLOOM.** *Time: 60 sec.* [See Ch.14]

### Set E — Statement, Calendar, Clock, Cubes (Q41–Q50)

**Q41. (Statement & Conclusion)** Statement: "The company has decided to increase salaries by 10%." Conclusion: Employees will get more money.
(a) Follows (b) Does not follow
**Solution:** Direct conclusion. **Answer: (a) Follows.** *Time: 15 sec.* [See Ch.10]

**Q42. (Statement & Assumption)** Statement: "Please use the back entrance." Assumption: The front entrance is not available.
(a) Valid (b) Invalid
**Solution:** Implicit assumption. **Answer: (a) Valid.** *Time: 30 sec.* [See Ch.10]

**Q43. (Cause & Effect)** 1: Sales decreased. 2: Product quality declined.
(a) 1 is cause, 2 is effect (b) 2 is cause, 1 is effect (c) Independent (d) Both are effects
**Solution:** Quality decline → sales decrease. **Answer: (b) 2 is cause, 1 is effect.** *Time: 60 sec.* [See Ch.10]

**Q44. (Calendar)** What day was 1 Jan 2020?
(a) Monday (b) Tuesday (c) Wednesday (d) Friday
**Solution:** Standard answer: Wednesday. **Answer: (c) Wednesday.** *Time: 30 sec.* [See Ch.8]

**Q45. (Clock Angle)** Angle at 4:00?
(a) 90° (b) 100° (c) 110° (d) 120°
**Solution:** 30(4) = 120°. **Answer: (d) 120°.** *Time: 15 sec.* [See Ch.8]

**Q46. (Clock Coincide)** When do hands coincide between 3 and 4?
(a) 3:16 4/11 (b) 3:15 (c) 3:18 (d) 3:20
**Solution:** M = 60(3)/11 = 180/11 = 16 4/11. **Answer: (a) 3:16 4/11.** *Time: 60 sec.* [See Ch.8]

**Q47. (Dice Opposite)** A die shows 2 on top, 3 on front, 6 on right. Bottom?
(a) 1 (b) 4 (c) 5 (d) 6
**Solution:** Standard die: 1-6, 2-5, 3-4. Top 2 → bottom 5. **Answer: (c) 5.** *Time: 15 sec.* [See Ch.8]

**Q48. (Painted Cube Total)** 5×5×5 cube painted. Total small cubes?
(a) 100 (b) 115 (c) 125 (d) 150
**Solution:** 5³ = 125. **Answer: (c) 125.** *Time: 10 sec.* [See Ch.13]

**Q49. (Embedded Figure)** Is a square embedded in a 5-pointed star?
(a) Yes (b) No (c) Cannot say (d) Only if rotated
**Solution:** Depends on the figure. Star contains triangles, not typically squares. **Answer: (b) No (typically).** *Time: 30 sec.* [See Ch.13]

**Q50. (Assertion-Reason)** A: "India is a democracy." R: "Citizens have the right to vote."
(a) Both true, R explains A (b) Both true, R doesn't explain A (c) A true, R false (d) A false, R true
**Solution:** Both true, R explains A. **Answer: (a).** *Time: 30 sec.** [See Ch.10]

## 6. Common Mistakes to Avoid in Exam

### 6.1 Calculation Errors

- **Double-check** BODMAS problems.
- **Verify** Pythagorean triples (6-8-10 = 10, not 14).
- **Re-verify** opposite faces in dice (1-6, 2-5, 3-4).

[NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

### 6.2 Misreading

- **Read the question twice** — especially for "not" type questions.
- **Check options** before computing — sometimes the answer is in the options.

[NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

### 6.3 Time Wastage

- Don't spend 5 minutes on one question. Move on.
- Don't change answers unless you have a clear reason.

[NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

### 6.4 Careless Syllogism

- Always **draw the Venn** for syllogism.
- Don't assume "some" implies "all" or vice versa.

[See Chapter 4 for full syllogism treatment]

### 6.5 Mirror/Water Confusion

- **Mirror**: left-right flip.
- **Water**: top-bottom flip.
- Don't mix up.

[See Chapter 9]

- **Sleep well** the night before.
