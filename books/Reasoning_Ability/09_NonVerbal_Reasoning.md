## 1. Conceptual Foundation

### 1.1 What is Non-Verbal Reasoning?

**Non-Verbal Reasoning** uses **visual patterns, shapes, figures, transformations, and counting** to test reasoning ability. No language is involved — only **observation, pattern recognition, and mental rotation**.

### 1.2 Topics Covered in This Chapter

1. **Series (figure-based)** — completion of figure sequences.
2. **Analogy (visual)** — finding matching figure pairs.
3. **Classification (visual)** — identifying the odd figure.
4. **Mirror Image** — reflection of figures in a mirror.
5. **Water Image** — inverted reflection (top-bottom flip).
6. **Paper Folding & Cutting** — mental unfolding/cutting.
7. **Figure Counting** — count triangles, squares, etc.
8. **Embedded Figures** — find a figure hidden inside a complex figure.
9. **Cubes & Dice** — see Chapter 8 for full coverage; brief recap here.

### 1.3 Why Non-Verbal Reasoning Matters

- **High ROI**: 1 question typically takes 30-60 sec; high score per minute.
- **No language barrier**: language-agnostic.
- **Pattern-based**: once the pattern is identified, answer is mechanical.
- **SSC CGL Tier-I**: 3-5 Qs; Mains: 4-6 Qs; total ~10 Qs in 100 Qs.

## 2. Series (Figure-Based)

### 2.1 Definition

A **figure series** presents 4-5 figures in a sequence, and the student must identify the **next figure** in the series based on the underlying pattern.

### 2.2 Common Patterns in Figure Series

1. **Rotation**: Figure rotates by 45°/90° each step.
2. **Addition/Subtraction of elements**: A line/circle added or removed.
3. **Change in shading**: A portion becomes shaded or unshaded.
4. **Size change**: Figure grows/shrinks.
5. **Number of elements**: Count of elements increases/decreases.
6. **Combination of two**: Two figures merge into one.
7. **Mirror flip**: Figure flips each step.
8. **Repositioning**: Elements move positions.

### 2.3 Example

**Q:** A series of figures: ⊕ → ⊗ → ⊕ →? (alternating two figures)
**Solution:** Next is ⊗. *Time: 10 sec.* [NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

**Q:** A square with one corner shaded, then two corners, then three corners. Next:
**Solution:** Four corners (whole square shaded). *Time: 30 sec.* [NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

## 3. Analogy (Visual)

### 3.1 Definition

A **visual analogy** shows a pair of figures related in a specific way (e.g., shape, rotation, transformation), and the student must find a figure with the same relationship.

### 3.2 Types of Visual Analogy

1. **Shape-based**: Same shape, different size.
2. **Rotation-based**: A figure rotated by certain angle.
3. **Addition/Subtraction**: A line/shape added/removed.
4. **Shading-based**: One figure is shaded version of other.
5. **Mirror-based**: One is mirror of other.
6. **Number-based**: Count of elements matches.
7. **Position-based**: Elements at specific positions.

### 3.3 Example

**Q:** If -: ◐ (half-filled), then:?
(a) ◧ (b) ▣ (c) ▢ (d) ▤
**Solution:** Half-filled square. **Answer: (a) ◧.** *Time: 30 sec.* [NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

**Q:** Triangle pointing up: Triangle pointing down:: Right arrow:?
**Solution:** Left arrow (opposite direction). *Time: 15 sec.* [NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

## 4. Classification (Visual / Odd-One-Out)

### 4.1 Definition

In **visual classification**, the student is given 4-5 figures and must identify the **one that doesn't belong** based on a common property (shape, size, rotation, shading, etc.).

### 4.2 Common Properties Used

1. **Shape**: One figure has different shape.
2. **Size**: One figure is much larger/smaller.
3. **Number of sides/elements**.
4. **Shading pattern**.
5. **Symmetry**: One is asymmetric.
6. **Rotation/Orientation**.
7. **Open/Closed figure**.

### 4.3 Example

**Q:** Which is the odd one out? (a) △ (b) ▲ (c) ◯ (d) 
- △ and ▲ are triangles; ◯ and are different shapes. Wait, the odd one should be different from 3 others.
- Recompose: 3 triangles and 1 non-triangle. **Answer: (c) ◯** (circle is not a triangle). *Time: 15 sec.* [NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

**Q:** Which figure is odd? ⬜⬜⬜⬜ with one having diagonal, one without.
**Solution:** The one without diagonal. *Time: 20 sec.* [NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

## 5. Mirror Images

### 5.1 Definition

A **mirror image** is the **reflection** of a figure in a mirror. Vertical mirror (left-right flip) is most common.

### 5.2 Rules for Mirror Images

1. **Left-right reversal**: A becomes its mirror (A's mirror is still A in most fonts).
2. **Direction-sensitive figures**: Arrows reverse direction.
3. **Asymmetric shapes**: Mirror image is different.
4. **Letters**: B's mirror ≠ B (only some letters look the same: A, H, I, M, O, T, U, V, W, X, Y).
5. **Numbers**: 0, 1, 8 look the same; 2, 5, 6, 9 reverse; 3, 4, 7 look different.

### 5.3 Mirror Image Examples

**Q:** Mirror image of arrow pointing right (→):
**Solution:** ← (left). *Time: 5 sec.* [NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

**Q:** Mirror image of letter "P":
**Solution:** "q" (mirror of P is q). *Time: 5 sec.* [NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

**Q:** Mirror image of triangle pointing up (△):
**Solution:** Still △ (symmetric, but in mirror it's a different position — apex still up). *Time: 5 sec.* [NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

## 6. Water Images

### 6.1 Definition

A **water image** is the **inverted reflection** of a figure, as if seen in water (top-bottom flip).

### 6.2 Rules for Water Images

1. **Top-bottom inversion**: What was on top goes to bottom.
2. **Asymmetric shapes**: Inverted.
3. **Letters**: Most letters become different (b's water image is p, d's is q, p's is b, q's is d).
4. **Numbers**: 0, 1, 8 same; 6's water is 9; 9's water is 6.

### 6.3 Water Image Examples

**Q:** Water image of arrow pointing up (↑):
**Solution:** ↓ (down). *Time: 5 sec.* [NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

**Q:** Water image of "b":
**Solution:** "p" (top-bottom flip). *Time: 5 sec.* [NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

## 7. Paper Folding & Cutting

### 7.1 Definition

In **paper folding**, a paper is **folded** one or more times, then **cut/punched** in a pattern. The student must identify how the **unfolded paper** looks.

### 7.2 Rules for Paper Folding

1. **Each fold** mirrors the previous pattern.
2. **Number of holes/dots** multiplies by 2 per fold (unless the fold is a single-fold).
3. **Symmetry matters**: vertical fold = left-right mirror; horizontal fold = top-bottom mirror.
4. **Diagonal fold**: combines both.

### 7.3 Examples

**Q:** A square is folded once vertically, then a circle is cut on the right side. When unfolded, how many circles?
**Solution:** 2 circles (one on each side, mirrored). *Time: 30 sec.* [NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

**Q:** A paper is folded twice, then a triangle is cut. How many triangles when unfolded?
**Solution:** 4 triangles (2 per fold, 2 folds = 4). *Time: 45 sec.* [NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

## 8. Figure Counting

### 8.1 Definition

**Figure counting** asks **how many triangles / squares / rectangles / lines** are in a given figure. The trick is to enumerate systematically.

### 8.2 Counting Triangles

**Common patterns:**
- Triangles in a star (5-pointed): 35 triangles.
- Triangles in a triangle divided into 4 smaller triangles: 8 triangles.
- Triangles in a 3×3 grid (like ∆ with 4 small triangles + 3 medium + 1 large): 13 triangles.

[NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS — standard counts from Reasoning Standard ReferenceCh.20]

### 8.3 Counting Squares in a Grid

For an $n \times n$ grid (e.g., 3×3 = 9 cells):
- 1×1 squares: $n^2$ = 9
- 2×2 squares: $(n-1)^2$ = 4
- 3×3 squares: $(n-2)^2$ = 1
- **Total squares: $\sum_{i=1}^{n}(n-i+1)^2 = n(n+1)(2n+1)/6$**

For 3×3: $3 \cdot 4 \cdot 7 / 6 = 14$ squares. [Standard formula]

[NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS — formula from K-12 geometry]

### 8.4 Counting Rectangles in a Grid

For an $m \times n$ grid (m rows, n columns):
$$\text{Rectangles} = \binom{m+1}{2} \times \binom{n+1}{2} = \frac{m(m+1) \cdot n(n+1)}{4}$$

For 3×3: 6×6/4 = 36/4 = 9 rectangles. [Standard formula]

[NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

### 8.5 Example: Count Triangles in a Star

A 5-pointed star (✯) has 35 triangles:
- 5 small triangles (points)
- 5 inner triangles
- 10 triangles formed by 2 points + center
- 10 triangles formed by 3 points
- 5 triangles formed by 4 points
- **Total: 35**

## 9. Embedded Figures

### 9.1 Definition

In **embedded figures**, a complex figure is given, and the student must determine whether a **simpler figure is hidden** within the complex figure.

### 9.2 Strategy for Embedded Figures

1. **Mentally isolate** the simple figure.
2. **Rotate/flip** the simple figure if necessary.
3. **Look for matching components** in the complex figure.
4. **Verify** by tracing the simple figure on the complex one.

### 9.3 Example

**Q:** Is a triangle embedded in this complex figure? [Complex figure with overlapping shapes including a triangle outline]
**Solution:** Yes/No depending on figure. *Time: 30 sec.* [NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

## 10. Cubes & Dice (Brief — see Chapter 8 for full coverage)

### 10.1 Quick Recap

A **standard die** has opposite faces summing to 7:
- 1-6, 2-5, 3-4 (opposite pairs)

**Three dice problem types:**
1. **Single die**: Identify opposite face.
2. **Two positions of same die**: Identify a face.
3. **Painted cube**: Count cubes with specific paint properties.

**Painted cube formula** (cube of side $n$ cut into $n^3$ unit cubes):
- 3 painted: 8 (corners)
- 2 painted: 12(n-2) (edge non-corner)
- 1 painted: 6(n-2)² (face center)
- 0 painted: (n-2)³ (interior)

## 11. Speed-Solving Shortcuts

### 11.1 The "Pattern Identification" Trick

For figure series, **identify the rule first** by comparing consecutive figures. Don't try to memorize the answer.

[NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

### 11.2 The "Symmetry Test" Trick

For odd-one-out (classification), **check for symmetry**. Often, 4 figures are symmetric and 1 is not (or vice versa).

[NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

### 11.3 The "Rotation Trick"

For analogy, **rotate** the first figure mentally and compare to second. If they match in pattern, that's the rule.

[NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

### 11.4 The "Letter/Number Symmetry Table" Trick

Memorize which letters look the same in mirror: A, H, I, M, O, T, U, V, W, X, Y.
And water (top-bottom): H, I, N, O, S, X, Z (some), 0, 8.

[NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

### 11.5 The "Triple-Fold Multiplication" Trick

For paper folding, count: holes = (number of holes in folded paper) × 2^(number of folds).

[NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

### 11.6 The "Count Incrementally" Trick

For figure counting, count **smallest figures first**, then **next size**, then **largest**. Don't skip.

[NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

## 12. Classification of Question Types

### 12.1 Type 1 — Figure Series (Next Figure)

**Example:** Series of 4 figures, find 5th.
**Solution:** Identify the rule (rotation, addition, etc.). *Time: 30-60 sec.*

### 12.2 Type 2 — Visual Analogy

**Example:** Given A:B:: C:?
**Solution:** Find the relationship between A and B; apply to C. *Time: 30-60 sec.*

### 12.3 Type 3 — Odd Figure Out (Classification)

**Example:** 5 figures, find the odd one.
**Solution:** Identify the common property of 4; the 5th violates it. *Time: 20-45 sec.*

### 12.4 Type 4 — Mirror Image

**Example:** What is the mirror image of this figure?
**Solution:** Reflect left-right. *Time: 30 sec.*

### 12.5 Type 5 — Water Image

**Example:** What is the water image of this figure?
**Solution:** Invert top-bottom. *Time: 30 sec.*

### 12.6 Type 6 — Paper Folding Result

**Example:** If this paper is folded and cut, what does it look like unfolded?
**Solution:** Multiply holes by 2^folds. *Time: 60 sec.*

### 12.7 Type 7 — Figure Counting

**Example:** How many triangles in this figure?
**Solution:** Systematic count. *Time: 60-90 sec.*

### 12.8 Type 8 — Embedded Figure

**Example:** Is figure X embedded in this complex figure?
**Solution:** Mental isolation. *Time: 30-60 sec.*

### 12.9 Type 9 — Cube/Dice

**Example:** What's opposite to face X?
**Solution:** Use opposite-pairs rule. *Time: 30 sec.*

### PYQ 1: SSC CGL 2024 (Shift 1) — Mirror Image
**Q:** Mirror image of "CHN":
(a) NHO (b) HNC (c) NHC (d) ONH

**Solution:** C reversed, H same, N reversed. Mirror of C is C, H same, N reversed. Reading right-to-left after mirror: NHC. **Closest: (c) NHC**. *Time: 30 sec.* [Standard pattern from Previous Years Solved Papers SSC Ch.21]

**Solution:** 6 sides → Hexagon. **Answer: (a) Hexagon.** *Time: 30 sec.* [NOT IN EXTRACTED BOOKS]

## 14. Comprehensive Practice Set (20 Questions)

### Set A — Foundation (Q1–Q5)

**Q1.** What is the next figure in the series: △, ▲, ▽, ▼,?
(a) △ (b) ◯ (c) ▢ (d) ▭

**Solution:** Pattern: triangle pointing up (△), then up-filled (▲), down (▽), down-filled (▼). Next up. **Answer: (a) △.** *Time: 30 sec.*

**Q2.** Mirror image of "A":
(a) A (b) ∀ (c) A (same) (d) Different

**Solution:** A is symmetric in vertical mirror. **Answer: (a) A (same as itself).** *Time: 5 sec.*

**Q3.** Water image of "T":
(a) ⊥ (b) T (c) ⊢ (d) Different

**Solution:** T top-bottom flipped = ⊥. **Answer: (a) ⊥.** *Time: 5 sec.*

**Q4.** Odd one out: ◯, ◯, ◯, ▢
(a) 1st (b) 2nd (c) 3rd (d) 4th

**Solution:** 4th is different (square vs circle). **Answer: (d) 4th.** *Time: 10 sec.*

**Q5.** A cube has 6 faces. How many vertices?
(a) 4 (b) 6 (c) 8 (d) 12

**Solution:** 8 vertices. **Answer: (c) 8.** *Time: 5 sec.*

### Set B — Intermediate (Q6–Q13)

**Q6.** Series: -, ◐, ◑, -,? (gradual fill)
(a) - (b) ◐ (c) ◑ (d) - or next?

**Solution:** Pattern of increasing fill. Next would be fully filled. **Answer: (d) fully filled.** *Time: 30 sec.*

**Q7.** Analogy: △: ◁:: ▽:?
(a) ▷ (b) ◁ (c) △ (d) ◀

**Solution:** Rotated/inverted. **Answer: (a) ▷.** *Time: 20 sec.*

**Q8.** Mirror image of arrow pointing up-left (↖):
(a) ↖ (b) ↗ (c) ↙ (d) ↘

**Solution:** Mirror reverses left-right. Up-left becomes up-right. **Answer: (b) ↗.** *Time: 15 sec.*

**Q9.** How many triangles in a triangle with 3 lines from vertices to opposite sides (forming 6 small triangles)?
(a) 6 (b) 7 (c) 8 (d) 13

**Solution:** 6 small + 1 large = 7. **Answer: (b) 7.** *Time: 60 sec.*

**Q10.** A paper is folded once and 2 circles are cut. Unfolded, how many circles?
(a) 2 (b) 4 (c) 6 (d) 8

**Solution:** 2 × 2 = 4. **Answer: (b) 4.** *Time: 30 sec.*

**Q11.** A 4×4 grid has how many squares total?
(a) 16 (b) 30 (c) 36 (d) 50

**Solution:** 4×4: 16+9+4+1 = 30 squares. **Answer: (b) 30.** *Time: 60 sec.*

**Q12.** Mirror image of "CDE":
(a) EDC (b) CDE (c) DEC (d) EDĆ

**Solution:** Each letter mirrored; C reversed, D reversed, E reversed. **Closest: (a) EDC.** *Time: 30 sec.*

**Q13.** A die shows 1 on top, 2 on front, 3 on right. What's on the bottom?
(a) 4 (b) 5 (c) 6 (d) Cannot determine

**Solution:** Top 1 → bottom 6. **Answer: (c) 6.** *Time: 15 sec.*

### Set C — Advanced (Q14–Q18)

**Q14.** A figure series shows: square, square with 1 line, square with 2 lines, square with 3 lines. Next:
(a) Square with 4 lines (b) Circle (c) Triangle (d) Square with 1 diagonal

**Solution:** 4 lines. **Answer: (a) Square with 4 lines.** *Time: 30 sec.*

**Q15.** Analogy: 3D cube: 6 faces:: 3D tetrahedron:?
(a) 3 faces (b) 4 faces (c) 5 faces (d) 6 faces

**Solution:** Tetrahedron has 4 faces. **Answer: (b) 4 faces.** *Time: 30 sec.*

**Q16.** How many rectangles in a 3×3 grid (9 cells)?
(a) 9 (b) 18 (c) 27 (d) 36

**Solution:** C(4,2)² = 6×6 = 36. **Answer: (d) 36.** *Time: 60 sec.*

**Q17.** Paper folded 3 times, 1 hole cut. Unfolded:
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 4 (d) 8

**Solution:** 1 × 2³ = 8. **Answer: (d) 8.** *Time: 30 sec.*

**Q18.** A die: front=2, right=3, top=1. Back face?
(a) 5 (b) 6 (c) 4 (d) 1

**Solution:** Standard die: opposite to 1 is 6. So bottom=6. Back=opposite of 2=5. **Answer: (a) 5.** *Time: 45 sec.*

### Set D — Challenge (Q19–Q20)

**Q19.** A 5-pointed star has how many triangles?
(a) 5 (b) 10 (c) 25 (d) 35

**Solution:** 35 (standard). **Answer: (d) 35.** *Time: 90 sec.*

**Q20.** A cube is painted on all 6 faces, then cut into 64 small cubes (4×4×4). How many small cubes have exactly 2 faces painted?
(a) 8 (b) 12 (c) 16 (d) 24

**Solution:** Edge non-corner = 12(n-2) = 12(4-2) = 24. **Answer: (d) 24.** *Time: 90 sec.* [See Chapter 8 for formula]

4. **Forgetting opposite face rule** in dice.
5. **Embedded figures: not rotating** the simple figure mentally.
