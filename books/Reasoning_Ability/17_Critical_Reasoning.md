## 1. Statement & Assumption

### 1.1 Concept

A **statement** presents a fact or plan. An **assumption** is something that is taken for granted or implied (not stated). The task: identify which assumption(s) are implicit in the statement.

### 1.2 Test for Assumption

An assumption is **implicit** if:
1. It **must be true** for the statement to hold
2. The statement would **lose meaning** without it
3. It's **reasonable** (not far-fetched)

### 1.3 Worked Examples

**Q1.**
**Statement**: "A new holiday special train has been introduced from Chennai to Patna to cater to the increased demand from passengers during the festival season. This will run on all Wednesdays in September and October."
**Assumptions**:
- I. More people are expected to travel from Chennai to Patna in September and October.
- II. The existing trains from Chennai to Patna have been cancelled.

**Answer**: (d) Only I is implicit.

**Reasoning**: 
- I is implicit because the rationale for the new train is "increased demand" — this must be true for the plan to make sense.
- II is NOT implicit. The new train supplements existing trains; cancellation isn't stated or implied.

### 1.4 Common Traps

- Assumption that **action implies necessity**: Sometimes an action is taken proactively, not because of necessity
- Assumption that **action implies past failure**: Companies may take new actions even without past failure
- **Confusing correlation with causation**: Just because two events are mentioned doesn't mean one caused the other

## 2. Statement & Conclusion

### 2.1 Concept

A **conclusion** is a logical outcome that follows from the statement. The task: identify which conclusion(s) can be validly drawn.

### 2.2 Test for Valid Conclusion

A conclusion is **valid** if:
1. It **follows logically** from the given statement
2. It **doesn't add new information** not in the statement
3. It **doesn't contradict** the statement

### 2.3 Worked Example

**Q2.**
**Statement**: "The government has reduced the import duty on solar panels by 15% to promote renewable energy adoption in India."
**Conclusions**:
- I. Solar panel installations in India will increase.
- II. Import of solar panels will become cheaper.

**Answer**: (b) Only II follows.

**Reasoning**: 
- II follows directly — lower duty = cheaper imports.
- I does NOT necessarily follow — cheaper panels don't guarantee increased installations (other factors: demand, finance, policy).

### 2.4 Common Traps

- **Jumping to conclusions**: Always check if it necessarily follows
- **Plausible ≠ Valid**: A conclusion might be plausible but not logically derived
- **Confusing may/must**: "May follow" vs "must follow" is a key distinction

## 3. Cause & Effect

### 3.1 Concept

A **cause** is what makes something happen. An **effect** is what happens as a result. The task: identify which event is the cause and which is the effect.

### 3.2 Test for Cause-Effect

To determine which is cause:
1. The cause must **precede** the effect in time
2. The cause must be **sufficient** to produce the effect
3. The cause must be **directly related** to the effect

### 3.3 Worked Example

**Q3.**
**Statement I**: The company announced a 20% salary cut for all employees.
**Statement II**: Several employees resigned from the company within a month.

**Answer**: Statement I is the cause, Statement II is the effect.

**Reasoning**: Salary cut (cause) likely led to resignations (effect). Time-wise: cut → resignation. The cause-effect relationship is direct.

### 3.4 Common Trap

- **Correlation vs Causation**: Just because two things happen together doesn't mean one causes the other
- **Reverse causation**: Sometimes what seems like an effect can actually be the cause

## 4. Assertion & Reason

### 4.1 Concept

An **assertion** is a statement of fact or opinion. A **reason** explains why the assertion is true. The task: determine if the reason correctly explains the assertion.

### 4.2 Test

For "Both A and R true, R is correct explanation of A":
1. Both assertion and reason must be true
2. Reason must directly explain why the assertion is true
3. The relationship must be direct, not coincidental

### 4.3 Standard Answer Choices

(a) Both A and R are true, R is the correct explanation of A
(b) Both A and R are true, R is NOT the correct explanation of A
(c) A is true, R is false
(d) A is false, R is true
(e) Both A and R are false

### 4.4 Worked Example

**Q4.**
**Assertion (A)**: "India has a monsoon-dependent economy."
**Reason (R)**: "About 50% of India's agricultural land is rain-fed."

**Answer**: (a) Both A and R true, R is the correct explanation of A.

**Reasoning**: Both statements are true. The dependence on monsoon is largely because agriculture depends on rain (rain-fed = monsoon-dependent).

### 4.5 Common Traps

- **R is true but doesn't explain A**: E.g., "India is democratic" (true) doesn't explain monsoon dependence
- **A is true but R is false**: Common in factual questions
- **Direction of causation**: A might cause R, not R causing A

## 5. Course of Action

### 5.1 Concept

A **course of action** is a step taken or to be taken to solve a problem mentioned in the statement. The task: identify which course(s) of action logically follow.

### 5.2 Test for Valid Course of Action

1. **Practical**: It should be feasible to implement
2. **Relevant**: It should address the problem in the statement
3. **Effective**: It should actually solve (or significantly help) the problem
4. **Not excessive**: It shouldn't overreach beyond the problem

### 5.3 Standard Answer Choices

(a) Only I follows
(b) Only II follows
(c) Both I and II follow
(d) Either I or II follows
(e) Neither follows

### 5.4 Worked Example

**Q5.**
**Statement**: "Traffic congestion in the city has increased by 30% in the last 6 months, leading to longer commute times and increased pollution."
**Courses of Action**:
- I. The municipal corporation should introduce more bus routes on high-traffic corridors.
- II. The municipal corporation should implement a congestion pricing zone in the city center.

**Answer**: (c) Both I and II follow.

**Reasoning**:
- I directly addresses traffic by increasing public transport capacity (reducing private vehicles).
- II uses economic disincentive to reduce traffic in congested area.
- Both are practical, relevant, and effective.

### 5.5 Common Traps

- **Sympathy without action**: Just feeling sorry doesn't solve the problem
- **Unrelated actions**: Action must address the problem
- **Extreme actions**: Overly drastic solutions are usually invalid

## 6. Decision Making (Banking Specific)

### 6.1 Concept

Banking exams (IBPS PO Mains) often have a **Decision Making** section with 5-8 questions. Each presents a scenario with multiple sub-questions testing:
- Logical conclusions
- Best course of action
- Strongest/weakening arguments
- Underlying assumptions

### 6.2 Standard Question Types

| Type | Test |
|---|---|
| Most logical conclusion | What must be true? |
| Strongest argument for/against | Which best supports/weakens? |
| Best course of action | What should be done? |
| Underlying assumption | What is taken for granted? |
| Inference | What can be logically drawn? |

### 6.3 Solving Strategy

1. **Read scenario carefully** (usually 200-400 words)
2. **Identify the core issue**
3. **For each option**, check if it:
   - Logically follows from the scenario
   - Is consistent with all facts
   - Doesn't introduce new information

## 7. Practice Questions (25 PYQs)

### Set A: Statement & Assumption (8 Qs)

**Q1.** **Statement**: "The company has decided to increase the price of its flagship product by 10% from next month."
**Assumption I**: The current price is not competitive.
**Assumption II**: Customers will continue buying despite the price increase.
(a) Only I is implicit (b) Only II is implicit (c) Both are implicit (d) Neither is implicit
**Answer**: (d) Neither. [Reasoning: Price increase doesn't imply current price is uncompetitive (could be profit motive); customers' reaction is unknown.]

**Q2.** **Statement**: "The municipal authority has announced a 30-day deadline for all buildings above 5 floors to install fire safety equipment."
**Assumption I**: Many buildings above 5 floors lack fire safety equipment.
**Assumption II**: 30 days is sufficient time for installation.
(a) Only I is implicit (b) Only II is implicit (c) Both are implicit (d) Neither is implicit
**Answer**: (a) Only I. [Reasoning: A deadline is set because compliance is lacking; 30-day sufficiency is not necessarily true.]

**Q3.** **Statement**: "The school has introduced mandatory physical training for all students from Class 6 onwards."
**Assumption I**: Physical fitness is important for students.
**Assumption II**: Students will benefit from this training.
(a) Only I is implicit (b) Only II is implicit (c) Both are implicit (d) Neither is implicit
**Answer**: (c) Both are implicit. [Reasoning: Both are preconditions for the policy to make sense.]

**Q4.** **Statement**: "Company X has launched an aggressive advertising campaign to capture 20% market share within 2 years."
**Assumption I**: Current market is contestable.
**Assumption II**: The company has products to back up the advertising.
(a) Only I is implicit (b) Only II is implicit (c) Both are implicit (d) Neither is implicit
**Answer**: (c) Both. [Reasoning: Both preconditions are required.]

**Q5.** **Statement**: "Banks have been advised by RBI to reduce lending rates to boost credit growth."
**Assumption I**: Lower rates will boost credit growth.
**Assumption II**: Banks have the flexibility to reduce rates.
(a) Only I is implicit (b) Only II is implicit (c) Both are implicit (d) Neither is implicit
**Answer**: (c) Both. [Reasoning: Both assumptions are required.]

**Q6.** **Statement**: "Notice: All visitors must register at the security desk before entering the premises."
**Assumption I**: There is a security concern at the premises.
**Assumption II**: Some people may not register without explicit notification.
(a) Only I is implicit (b) Only II is implicit (c) Both are implicit (d) Neither is implicit
**Answer**: (c) Both. [Reasoning: Security concern justifies notice; non-registration without notice is what prompts the notice.]

**Q7.** **Statement**: "The airline has decided to introduce a new baggage weight limit of 15 kg for domestic flights."
**Assumption I**: Reducing baggage weight helps reduce fuel costs.
**Assumption II**: Passengers were carrying excess baggage.
(a) Only I is implicit (b) Only II is implicit (c) Both are implicit (d) Neither is implicit
**Answer**: (d) Neither. [Reasoning: The policy is just announced without justification; the rationale could be operational or other.]

**Q8.** **Statement**: "The exam results will be published on the university website within 7 days."
**Assumption I**: Students will check the website.
**Assumption II**: The university has the capacity to publish within 7 days.
(a) Only I is implicit (b) Only II is implicit (c) Both are implicit (d) Neither is implicit
**Answer**: (b) Only II. [Reasoning: Capacity is implied; checking website is uncertain.]

### Set C: Cause & Effect (4 Qs)

**Q17.** **Statement I**: The temperature dropped below freezing point last night.
**Statement II**: Several water pipes in the city burst today morning.
(a) I is the cause, II is the effect (b) II is the cause, I is the effect (c) Both are effects of some cause (d) Independent events
**Answer**: (a) I is the cause, II is the effect. [Reasoning: Freezing causes water expansion → pipe bursts.]

**Q18.** **Statement I**: The central bank increased interest rates by 0.5%.
**Statement II**: Many banks raised their lending rates within a week.
(a) I is the cause, II is the effect (b) II is the cause, I is the effect (c) Both are effects of some cause (d) Independent events
**Answer**: (a) I is the cause. [Reasoning: Banks follow RBI policy rate.]

**Q19.** **Statement I**: A new competitor entered the market with lower prices.
**Statement II**: The company's market share declined by 15% in the next quarter.
(a) I is the cause, II is the effect (b) II is the cause, I is the effect (c) Both are effects of some cause (d) Independent events
**Answer**: (a) I is the cause. [Reasoning: New competitor → lost market share.]

**Q20.** **Statement I**: The company announced record profits.
**Statement II**: The CEO received a bonus.
(a) I is the cause, II is the effect (b) II is the cause, I is the effect (c) Both are effects of some cause (d) Independent events
**Answer**: (c) Both are effects of some cause. [Reasoning: Both result from company performance, but bonus may have multiple causes.]

### Set D: Assertion & Reason (3 Qs)

**Q21.** **A**: India has the world's largest postal network.
**R**: Postal services are critical for rural connectivity.
(a) Both A and R true, R is explanation of A (b) Both true, R not explanation (c) A true, R false (d) A false, R true
**Answer**: (a) Both true, R explains A. [Reasoning: Large network supports rural reach.]

**Q22.** **A**: Electric vehicles are gaining market share in India.
**R**: Government provides subsidies on EV purchases.
(a) Both A and R true, R is explanation of A (b) Both true, R not explanation (c) A true, R false (d) A false, R true
**Answer**: (a) Both true, R explains A. [Reasoning: Subsidies make EVs more affordable → boost sales.]

**Q23.** **A**: The rupee has depreciated against the dollar.
**R**: RBI raised interest rates.
(a) Both A and R true, R is explanation of A (b) Both true, R not explanation (c) A true, R false (d) A false, R true
**Answer**: (b) Both true, R not explanation. [Reasoning: Rate hike usually STRENGTHENS rupee, not weakens.]

### Set E: Course of Action (2 Qs)

**Q24.** **Statement**: "Waterlogging has become a chronic problem in the city during monsoons, causing traffic disruptions and health hazards."
**Course of Action I**: The municipal corporation should upgrade the storm water drainage system.
**Course of Action II**: The municipal corporation should issue health advisories during monsoon.
(a) Only I follows (b) Only II follows (c) Both follow (d) Neither follows
**Answer**: (c) Both follow. [Reasoning: I addresses root cause; II addresses immediate impact.]

**Q25.** **Statement**: "Customer complaints against the bank have increased by 25% in the last quarter, mostly related to digital transaction failures."
**Course of Action I**: The bank should upgrade its digital infrastructure.
**Course of Action II**: The bank should issue a public apology.
(a) Only I follows (b) Only II follows (c) Both follow (d) Neither follows
**Answer**: (a) Only I. [Reasoning: I addresses root cause; II is just symbolic.]
